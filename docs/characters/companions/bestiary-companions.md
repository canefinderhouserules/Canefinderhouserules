# Bestiary Animal Companions

The Bestiary books present many animals beyond those in the Core Rulebook. The entries below give the starting statistics and advancement for each as an animal companion. The rules for choosing and progressing an animal companion are in the [druid](../classes/druid.md) class description; the companions from Ultimate Wilderness are on the [Animal Companions](animal-companions.md) page.
