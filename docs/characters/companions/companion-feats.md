# Companion Feats

!!! note "Source"
    Rules from *Pathfinder Roleplaying Game: Ultimate Wilderness*.

As they grow in strength and experience, animal companions and mounts develop mutations, personality quirks, and tricks that grant them new abilities unlike those seen in typical animals of their kind. The following feats can be chosen by characters with the animal companion or by companions themselves, as indicated in each feat’s prerequisite line.

An animal companion or mount can select from the feats listed below that include “animal companion” as a prerequisite as if it appeared on the list of animal feats in the *Core Rulebook*.

**Table: Companion and Familiar Feats**

| Feat | Prerequisites | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| [Boon Companion](../../rules/feats/boon-companion.md) | Animal companion class feature or familiar class feature | Companion or familiar abilities are treated as if you were a higher level |
| [Changeling Familiar](../../rules/feats/changeling-familiar.md) | Familiar, caster level 9th, change shape ability | Familiar can take a humanoid form |
| [Critical Conduit](../../rules/feats/critical-conduit.md) | Deliver touch spells ability, familiar | Double the threat range of touch attacks made to deliver your master's spells |
| [Curious Companion](../../rules/feats/curious-companion.md) | Animal companion | Select a plant or vermin companion as an animal companion |
| [Devotion against the Unnatural](../../rules/feats/devotion-against-the-unnatural.md) | Animal companion, devotion special ability | Gain additional resistance to certain creatures’ abilities |
| [Disruptive Companion](../../rules/feats/disruptive-companion.md)† | Animal companion | Companion hinders spellcasting |
| [Extra Item Slot](../../rules/feats/extra-item-slot.md) | Non-humanoid body shape | Use magic items in one additional slot |
| [Familiar Focus](../../rules/feats/familiar-focus.md) | Familiar | Master's caster level is higher for harmless spells targeting you |
| [Familiar Spell](../../rules/feats/familiar-spell.md) | Spellcaster with familiar class feature | Master can store a spell in you for later casting |
| [Favored Animal Focus](../../rules/feats/favored-animal-focus.md) | Animal companion, animal focus | Animal focus is stronger when applied to your companion |
| [Feral Grace](../../rules/feats/feral-grace.md)† | Weapon Finesse, base attack bonus +3, animal companion | Gain bonus to damage from fighting with finesse |
| [Ferocious Beast](../../rules/feats/ferocious-beast.md)† | Intimidate 1 rank, animal companion | Gain bonuses on Intimidate checks |
| [Ferocious Feint](../../rules/feats/ferocious-feint.md)† | Bluff 1 rank, animal companion | Gain bonuses to feint |

| [Greater Tenacious Hunter](../../rules/feats/greater-tenacious-hunter.md) | Combat Reflexes, Tenacious Hunter, Perception 3 ranks, Stealth 3 ranks, animal companion | Deny Dexterity bonus to creatures pinpointed |

| [Improved Intercept Blow](../../rules/feats/improved-intercept-blow.md)† | Intercept Blow, base attack bonus +6, animal companion | Further reduce damage while using Intercept Blow |
| [Intercept Blow](../../rules/feats/intercept-blow.md)† | Base attack bonus +1, animal companion | Shield master from attacks |
| [Jumper](../../rules/feats/jumper.md) | Acrobatics 1 rank, creature type other than humanoid or outsider | Always have a running start for Acrobatics jump checks |
| [Lithe Attacker](../../rules/feats/lithe-attacker.md) | Narrow Frame, Escape Artist 5 ranks, animal or magical beast | Attack and keep Dexterity bonus while squeezing |
| [Master of Your Kind](../../rules/feats/master-of-your-kind.md) | Familiar, speak with animals of its kind ability | Use animal messenger, animal trance or charm animal on animals of your kind |
| [Narrow Frame](../../rules/feats/narrow-frame.md) | Escape Artist 1 rank, animal or magical beast | No penalties for squeezing through spaces at least half as wide |
| [Reflexive Interception](../../rules/feats/reflexive-interception.md)† | Intercept Blow, base attack bonus +3, animal companion, evasion | Provide evasion to master |
| [Shapeless Familiar](../../rules/feats/shapeless-familiar.md) | Familiar, caster level 7th | Familiar gains an alternate animal form |

| [Spark of the Uncanny](../../rules/feats/spark-of-the-uncanny.md) | Familiar | Familiar speaks one of your languages |
| [Spell Sponge](../../rules/feats/spell-sponge.md) | Animal companion or familiar, share spells ability | Harmless spells on you with a target of "you" last twice as long |
| [Stable Gallop](../../rules/feats/stable-gallop.md) | Animal or magical beast | Halved charge AC penalty, steadier rider |

| [Valiant Steed](../../rules/feats/valiant-steed.md) | Animal or magical beast | Bonus against fear and emotion; easier to push |

† This is a combat feat, and it can be selected as a bonus feat by companions with levels in brawler, fighter, gunslinger, swashbuckler, or warpriest gained via feats or another special ability. In addition, the animal companion counts as a fighter with a number of class levels equal to its Hit Dice for the purposes of qualifying for the Disruptive feat, as well as for any feat that lists the Disruptive feat as a prerequisite. The animal companion also adds those feats to the list of animal feats that it can choose from when gaining a new feat.
