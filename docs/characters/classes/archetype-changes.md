# Archetype and Class Changes

House-rule changes to specific archetypes and class features. Changes to the core classes themselves (barbarian rage powers, monk anti-dip and unarmed scaling, ranger favored terrain, draconic bloodline) are written directly into the class pages.

## Alchemist

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Vivisectionist:** sneak attack works like it does for any other class, and does not force other classes to work like the vivisectionist. One level of vivisectionist gives only 1d6 sneak attack; it does not turn every other class with sneak attack into a way to get 10d6 with full BAB.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Legendary Alchemist:** can no longer trade poison resistance for evasion. Has bomb uses per day like a normal alchemist.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Any archetype that grants mutagen:** requires Intelligence 15.

## Barbarian and Bloodrager

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Invulnerable Rager:** gains damage reduction from 1st level, 1 point at every level.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Bloodrager, Primalist:** may swap 1 bloodline power for 1 rage power (instead of 2), and cannot take the powers exclusive to the unchained barbarian (see stances).

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Bloodrager, Prowler at World's End:** Spirit Bonus counts as Medium –2 to determine when improvements are gained, minimum 1.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Blood Arcanist:** banned.

## Bard

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Duettist:** can no longer be combined with Sound Striker.

## Druid

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Kraken Caller:** Tentacle Wild Shape starts at 2nd level with only 1 tentacle, and gains a new tentacle every level until 12th. It is not affected by the eidolon's natural attack limit.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Feyspeaker:** wizard spells are not raised by 1 level; they are cast as the level they are for the wizard. Wild Shape lets the druid take Fey forms and use their spell-like abilities by spending slots of the spell-like ability's appropriate level (no need to prepare it).

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Green Scourge:** may use Wisdom instead of Charisma for feats and anything that uses or requires Flame Blade.

## Gunslinger

!!! warning "House rule"
    The Legendary Gunslinger may be used. Firearms still do not automatically target touch AC, but grit is now Wisdom modifier + gunslinger level instead of just Wisdom modifier.

## Rogue

!!! warning "House rule"
    A rogue archetype that replaces Dexterity-to-damage (gained at 3rd, 11th and 19th level) instead gains 1 combat bonus feat, chosen from the normal list plus two unique options:

    - **Combat Deceiver:** sacrifice one of your attacks during a full attack to feint. On a successful feint, the second attack gains sneak attack; with Greater Feint, all other attacks do.
    - **Ranged Sneak Attacker:** whenever you are within Point-Blank Shot range and the enemy is flanked by you or by two of your allies, you may make sneak attacks with ranged weapons.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Rogue's Edge:** at 5th level a rogue gains the Skill Unlock powers of any 5 skills of their choice. At 10th level a rogue gains the Skill Unlock powers for all skills in which they have sufficient ranks.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Glory Rogue:** may use Intelligence instead of Charisma (one or the other).

## Samurai

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Warrior Poet:**

    - **Chrysanthemum's Blooming:** minimum level 5. Can be taken again to gain Improved Vital Strike at 9th level and again for Greater Vital Strike at 13th. With Improved Vital Strike, you may use Improved Spring Attack with Vital Strike; with Greater Vital Strike, Greater Spring Attack with Improved Vital Strike.
    - **Harmony of the Tranquil Garden:** raised to 10 ft. (base and increase). Taken 3 times it becomes blindsight; taken 5 times it becomes a free action.
    - **Wrath of the Heavens:** the warrior poet gains Deific Obedience as a bonus feat, ignoring its prerequisites.
    - **New feat, Extra Flourish:** grants one extra flourish of your choice. Minimum level 5, maximum one per 5 levels.
    - **Graceful Strike:** adds your full level.
    - **Battle Dance:** gained at levels 4, 8 and 12.

## Sorcerer

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Orc bloodline:** no longer has the light sensitivity penalty.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Blood Intensity:** need not replace the 3rd power; it may replace the 1st or 2nd if you prefer.

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Nine-Tailed Heir:** gains tails each time a bloodline spell would be gained, instead of at the levels listed in the archetype. Tail spells may be swapped for other appropriately themed spells (a permanent change), up to the highest spell level the character can cast when receiving it (if the second tail arrives at 5th level, its spell can be swapped for another kitsune-themed spell of level 2 or lower, which is what the character can cast at that level).

## Vigilante

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Mystic Bolts (Warlock archetype):** now deal 1d6 + half Intelligence modifier damage; everything else is unchanged.

## Witch

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Slumber hex:** can be used twice per target per 24 hours instead of once, and the accursed hex lets you use it twice more instead of once more.

## Wizard

!!! warning "House rule"
    **Exploiter Wizard:** also sacrifices the wizard's bonus feats.
