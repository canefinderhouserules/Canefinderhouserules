# Archetype and Class Changes

House-rule changes to specific archetypes and class features. Changes to the core classes themselves ([barbarian rage powers](barbarian.md#rage-powers-ex), monk anti-dip and unarmed scaling, [ranger favored terrain](ranger.md#favored-terrain-ex), draconic bloodline) are written directly into the class pages. The changes below are also written into each archetype’s own page, with the book version in a closed box.

## Alchemist

**[Vivisectionist](alchemist-archetypes.md#vivisectionist):** sneak attack works like it does for any other class, and does not force other classes to work like the vivisectionist. One level of vivisectionist gives only 1d6 sneak attack; it does not turn every other class with sneak attack into a way to get 10d6 with full BAB.

**[Legendary Alchemist](legendary/legendary-alchemist.md):** can no longer trade poison resistance for evasion. Has bomb uses per day like a normal alchemist.

**[Mutagen](alchemist.md):** requires Intelligence 15, for the alchemist and for any class or archetype that grants it.

## Barbarian and Bloodrager

**[Invulnerable Rager](barbarian-archetypes.md#invulnerable-rager):** gains damage reduction from 1st level, 1 point at every level.

**[Bloodrager, Primalist](bloodrager-archetypes.md#primalist):** may swap 1 bloodline power for 1 rage power (instead of 2), and cannot take the powers exclusive to the unchained barbarian (see stances).

**[Bloodrager, Prowler at World’s End](bloodrager-archetypes.md#prowler-at-worlds-end):** Spirit Bonus counts as Medium –2 to determine when improvements are gained, minimum 1.

**[Blood Arcanist](arcanist-archetypes.md#blood-arcanist):** banned.

## Bard

**[Duettist](bard-archetypes.md#duettist):** can no longer be combined with Sound Striker.

## Druid

**[Kraken Caller](druid-archetypes.md#kraken-caller):** Tentacle Wild Shape starts at 2nd level with only 1 tentacle, and gains a new tentacle every level until 12th. It is not affected by the eidolon's natural attack limit.

**[Feyspeaker](druid-archetypes.md#feyspeaker):** wizard spells are not raised by 1 level; they are cast as the level they are for the wizard. Wild Shape lets the druid take Fey forms and use their spell-like abilities by spending slots of the spell-like ability's appropriate level (no need to prepare it).

**[Green Scourge](druid-archetypes.md#green-scourge):** may use Wisdom instead of Charisma for feats and anything that uses or requires Flame Blade.

## Gunslinger

The Legendary Gunslinger may be used. Firearms still do not automatically target touch AC, but grit is now Wisdom modifier + gunslinger level instead of just Wisdom modifier.

## Rogue

These changes are written into the [Unchained Rogue](unchained-rogue.md).

**Finesse Training:** instead of Dexterity to damage at 3rd, 11th and 19th level, the unchained rogue gains 1 bonus combat feat, chosen from the normal list or two unique options:

- **Combat Deceiver:** sacrifice one of your attacks during a full attack to feint. On a successful feint, the second attack gains sneak attack; with Greater Feint, all other attacks do.
- **Ranged Sneak Attacker:** whenever you are within Point-Blank Shot range and the enemy is flanked by you or by two of your allies, you may make sneak attacks with ranged weapons.

**Rogue's Edge:** at 5th level a rogue gains the Skill Unlock powers of any 5 skills of their choice. At 10th level a rogue gains the Skill Unlock powers for all skills in which they have sufficient ranks.

**Glory Rogue:** its class features (extra weapon proficiencies, guile pool and ambush) are part of the unchained rogue, and the guile pool may use Intelligence instead of Charisma (one or the other).

## Samurai

**[Warrior Poet](samurai-archetypes.md#warrior-poet):**

- **Chrysanthemum's Blooming:** minimum level 5. Can be taken again to gain Improved Vital Strike at 9th level and again for Greater Vital Strike at 13th. With Improved Vital Strike, you may use Improved Spring Attack with Vital Strike; with Greater Vital Strike, Greater Spring Attack with Improved Vital Strike.
- **Harmony of the Tranquil Garden:** raised to 10 ft. (base and increase). Taken 3 times it becomes blindsight; taken 5 times it becomes a free action.
- **Wrath of the Heavens:** the warrior poet gains Deific Obedience as a bonus feat, ignoring its prerequisites.
- **New feat, [Extra Flourish](../../rules/feats/extra-flourish.md):** grants one extra flourish of your choice. Minimum level 5, maximum one per 5 levels.
- **Graceful Strike:** adds your full level.
- **Battle Dance:** gained at levels 4, 8 and 12.

## Sorcerer

**[Orc bloodline](sorcerer-archetypes.md#orc):** no longer has the light sensitivity penalty, and its damage per die is an enhancement bonus.

**[Blood Intensity](bloodline-mutations.md):** need not replace the 3rd power; it may replace the 1st or 2nd if you prefer.

**[Nine-Tailed Heir](sorcerer-archetypes.md#nine-tailed-heir):** gains tails each time a bloodline spell would be gained, instead of at the levels listed in the archetype. Tail spells may be swapped for other appropriately themed spells (a permanent change), up to the highest spell level the character can cast when receiving it (if the second tail arrives at 5th level, its spell can be swapped for another kitsune-themed spell of level 2 or lower, which is what the character can cast at that level).

## Vigilante

**[Mystic Bolts (Warlock archetype)](vigilante-archetypes.md#warlock):** now deal 1d6 + half Intelligence modifier damage; everything else is unchanged.

## Witch

**[Slumber hex](witch.md):** can be used twice per target per 24 hours instead of once, and the accursed hex lets you use it twice more instead of once more.

## Wizard

**[Exploiter Wizard](wizard-archetypes.md#exploiter-wizard):** also sacrifices the [wizard's bonus feats](wizard.md#bonus-feats).
