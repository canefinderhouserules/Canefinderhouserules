# Damage-Per-Die Bonuses

Bonuses of the form "+X damage per die rolled" (such as those granted by the Sorcerer Draconic and Orc bloodlines, Blood Havoc, and the Flumefire Rage feat) are **enhancement bonuses**. They follow the usual rule for bonuses of the same type: they do not stack, and you only use the highest one.

**Exception**: A raging character with the [Flumefire Rage](../../rules/feats/flumefire-rage.md) feat may benefit from two such bonuses.
