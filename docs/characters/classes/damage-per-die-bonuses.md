# Damage-Per-Die Bonuses

!!! warning "House rule"
    Per-die damage bonuses are no longer untyped. They do not stack: you only benefit from one.

Bonuses of the form "+X damage per die" (such as those granted by the Sorcerer Draconic and Orc bloodlines, Blood Havoc, and Flumefire) are **no longer untyped bonuses that stack**.

- You can only benefit from **one** such bonus at a time.
- If you have more than one, you use the **highest** one.
- Exception: a character with **Flumefire** who is also raging may benefit from **two**.
