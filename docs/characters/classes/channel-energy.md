# Channel Energy

!!! warning "House rule"
    Channel energy is no longer a choice between healing and harming. It is a choice between **positive** and **negative** energy, and it uses **d8s**.

When you use channel energy you choose whether it is **positive** or **negative** energy. Either way, it affects **every creature in the area** appropriately:

- **Positive energy** heals living creatures and damages undead.
- **Negative energy** heals undead and damages living creatures.

You can no longer choose to affect only the creatures you want to heal or only the creatures you want to harm.

The damage or healing dice are **d8s** (instead of d6s), gained at the usual rate of one die per two levels.

Creatures that are damaged by your channel energy can attempt a **Will save for half damage**, as normal.
