# Shifter

This class uses the [Legendary Shifter](legendary/legendary-shifter.md), with the following changes. Its [original version](shifter-ultimate-wilderness.md) is kept for reference.

## Class Features

**Shifter Claws (Su)**: At will, a shifter in her natural form can extend her claws as a swift action to use as a weapon. This magical transformation is fueled as much by the shifter’s faith in the natural world as it is by inborn talent. The claws on each hand can be used as a primary natural attack, dealing 1d4 points of piercing and slashing damage (1d3 if she is Small). If she uses one of her claw attacks in concert with a weapon held in the other hand, the claw acts as a secondary natural attack instead.

As the shifter gains levels, the power of her claws increases. At 3rd level, her claws ignore DR/cold iron and DR/silver. At 7th level, her claw damage increases to 1d6 (1d4 if Small). At 11th level, her claw damage increases to 1d8 (1d6 if Small). At 13th level, her claw damage increases to 1d10 (1d8 if Small). At 17th level, the damage die does not increase, but the critical multiplier becomes ×3. Lastly, at 19th level, the claws ignore DR/adamantine and DR/—.

While a shifter uses wild shape to assume her aspect’s major form, her natural attacks gain the same benefits granted by her shifter claws ability. If the form she takes has claw attacks, she can use either the base damage of her shifter claws or the damage of the form’s claws, whichever is greater. If the form does not have claw attacks, she can choose up to two natural attacks that would deal less damage than her shifter claw damage and have those attacks instead deal the same damage as her shifter claws.

Instead of the Legendary Shifter’s shifter evolution, a shifter uses shifter claws. If her wild shape form has only 1 attack, she can choose to have 2 natural attacks in that form, as long as they make sense (claws in most cases), instead of increasing the damage of that attack with this class feature. These two attacks are always secondary.

**Shifter’s Fury (Ex)**: At 6th level, a shifter gains the ability to make several ferocious attacks with the same natural weapon. Instead of attacking with all her natural weapons, the shifter can choose a single natural weapon and make a full attack with that natural weapon, gaining a second iterative attack at a –5 as if it was a manufactured weapon. When she does so, all her other natural attacks count as secondary attacks and don’t benefit from shifter’s claws. At 11th level, she gains a third iterative attack at a –10, and at 16th level, she gains a fourth iterative attack at –15. This replaces speed shift.

**Speed Shift, Lightning Shift, and Instant Shift**: A shifter doesn’t have these class features. She can use wild shape faster with [Quick Wild Shape](../../rules/feats/quick-wild-shape.md).

## Shifter Aspects

These aspects replace the minor forms of the aspects with the same names. Their major forms don’t change. See the [Swarm Shifter](shifter-archetypes.md#swarm-shifter) for the vermin aspect.

### Bat

**Minor Form**: You gain blindsense 10ft and +1 to Perception skill checks. At 8th level, the bonuses increase to 20ft and +2, and at 13th level it increases to 30ft and +3. Blindsense stacks with the major form.

### Bear

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Constitution score. At 8th level, the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Boar

**Minor Form**: You gain Improved Initiative as a bonus feat. Any wildshape level allows for Powerful Charge if the form you take has it. At 8th level, you gain a +2 to Charge Attacks. At 13th level it increases to +4.

### Bull

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Strength score. At 8th level, the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Crocodile

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Swim checks and grapple combat maneuver checks. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Deinonychus

**Minor Form**: You gain a +6 bonus on Initiative checks. This bonus increases to +8 at 8th level and +10 at 13th level.

### Dolphin

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Knowledge checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Dragonfly

**Minor Form**: Reduces the penalty to maintain a grapple without gaining the grappled condition by -5. At 8th level decreases to -7, and at 13th level decreases to -9.

### Electric Eel

**Minor Form**: Your natural attacks deal 1d6 point of electricity damage in addition to their normal damage. This additional electricity damage increases to 2d6 at 8th level and 3d6 at 13th level.

### Elephant

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 enhancement bonus on bull rush and overrun combat maneuver checks. Any wildshape level allows for Trample if the form you take has it. At 8th level, this bonus increases to +6, and at 13th level it increases to +8.

### Falcon

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Frog

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on CMD against grapple. Any wildshape level allows for Pull and Swallow Whole if the form you take has it. These bonuses increase to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Giant Wasp

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting affects. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Horse

**Minor Form**: You gain the Endurance feat as well as an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed. At 8th level, you gain the Run feat. If you already have the Run feat, you instead can run at 6 times your base speed when using the Run feat. At 13th level, your enhancement bonus to your base speed increases to +15.

### Lion

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Intimidate checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Lizard

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Acrobatics checks. Any wildshape level allows for Fast Healing if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Mantis

**Minor Form**: You gain Lunge as a bonus feat. At 10th level, your reach increases by 5 feet.

### Monkey

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Climb and acrobatics checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Mouse

**Minor Form**: You gain evasion, as per the rogue class feature. At 10th level, this benefit becomes improved evasion, as per the rogue advanced talent.

### Octopus

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Escape Artist and Grapple checks. It increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Owl

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Stealth checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Peafowl

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Charisma score. At 8th level the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Scorpion

**Minor Form**: You gain a +1 competence bonus on any poison DC you use. Any wildshape level allows for Poison if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +2 at 8th level and +3 at 13th level.

### Snake

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls when making attacks of opportunity and a +2 dodge bonus to your AC against attacks of opportunity. Any wildshape level allows for Constrict if the form you take has it. These bonuses increase to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Snapping Turtle

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to Wisdom. At 8th level, this bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level, it increases to +6.

### Spider

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Climb checks, Stealth checks, saving throws against web effects (magical or otherwise), and checks to break free of webs. Any wildshape level allows for Web if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Stag

**Minor Form**: You gain an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed. This bonus increases to +10 feet at 8th level and +20 feet at 15th level.

### Tiger

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Dexterity score. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.

### Wolf

**Minor Form**: You gain Outflank. You gain the scent ability with a range of 10 feet, or, if you have the scent ability, the range increases by 10 feet. Any wildshape level allows for Trip if the form you take has it. The range of this scent or the increase to your scent increases to 20 feet at 8th level and 30 feet at 13th level. The range doubles if the opponent is upwind and is halved if the opponent is downwind.

### Wolverine

**Minor Form**: You gain 1 additional hit point per Hit Die you have, and treat your Constitution score as 4 points higher than it is for the purpose of determining when you die at negative hit points. At 8th level, you gain Diehard as a bonus feat. Any wildshape level allows for Rage if the form you take has it. At 13th level, you treat your Constitution as being 8 points higher for the purpose of determining when you die at negative hit points.
