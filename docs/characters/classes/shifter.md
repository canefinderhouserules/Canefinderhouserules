# Canefinder Shifter

Shifters are masters of the wild who focus their supernatural powers inward to gain control over their own forms, taking on the traits of the animals to which they are attuned. The [Legendary Shifter](legendary/legendary-shifter.md) and [Ultimate Wilderness shifter](shifter-ultimate-wilderness.md) this class is based on are kept for reference.

**Alignment**: Any.

**Hit Die**: d10.

**Starting Wealth**: 3d6 × 10 gp (average 105 gp.) In addition, each character begins play with an outfit worth 10 gp or less.

### Class Skills

The shifter’s class skills are Acrobatics (Dex), Climb (Str), Craft (Int), Fly (Dex), Handle Animal (Cha). Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (nature) (Int), Perception (Wis). Profession (Wis), Ride (Dex), Stealth (Dex), Survival (Wis), and Swim (Str).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 4 + Int Modifier

### Class Features

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiencies

A shifter is proficient with all simple and [martial weapons](../../equipment/weapons-martial.md) and the natural attacks (claw, bite, and so forth) from the shifter claws class feature and of forms they assume with shifter shape.

Shifters are proficient with light and [medium armor](../../equipment/armor.md) and [shields](../../equipment/armor.md) (except [tower shields](../../equipment/armor.md)).

#### Bonus Languages

A shifter’s bonus language options include Sylvan, the language of woodland creatures, and Aklo, the tongue spoken by certain sinister fey or otherworldly denizens of reaches of the wild rarely explored by humanity. These choices are in addition to the bonus languages available to the character because of their race.

A shifter speaks Druidic, a secret language known only to druids and shifters, which they learn upon becoming a 1st-level shifter. Druidic is a free language for a shifter; that is, they know it in addition to their regular allotment of languages, and it doesn’t take up a language slot. Shifters are forbidden to teach this language to non-druids or non-shifters, although they do not suffer any consequences for doing so.

#### Shifter Aspect (Su)

At 1st level, a shifter gains their first aspect—a category of animal to which their body and soul have become supernaturally attuned. They can shift into their aspect’s form (see Aspects) for a number of minutes per day equal to 3 + their shifter level. The duration need not be consecutive but must be spent in 1-minute increments. Shifting into a form is a swift action, while ending the effect is a free action that can be taken only on the shifter’s turn. A shifter may switch which aspect’s form they are taking as a swift action.

Until a shifter reaches 9th level and gains the chimeric aspect class feature, they can assume only one form at a time. Shifting to a new aspect (or aspects, in the case of chimeric aspects or greater chimeric aspects) ends all forms currently manifested. The forms of aspects are not polymorph effects, and the shifter does not lose the benefits of a form while affected by polymorph effects.

As the shifter gains levels, they gain more aspects; they gain their second aspect at 5th level and an additional aspect every four levels afterwards, to a maximum number of 5 at 17th level.

#### Shifter Shape (Su)

At 1st level, a shifter gains the ability to turn herself into any small or Medium animal and back again, although they is limited to the following forms; badger, bird, camel, cat (small), dire rat, dog, dolphin, horse, manta ray, pony, snake (viper or constrictor), or wolf. This ability functions like the beast shape I spell, except as noted here. The effect lasts for a number of hours equal to 1/2 their shifter level + their Wisdom modifier (minimum 1), and they may split the duration of this ability however they want. Each use of this ability uses up at least an hour of its duration, regardless of how long they stay in it. A shifter can change form while in their shifter shape but doing so reduces their total duration by 1 hour. Shifter shape is treated as wild shape for the purpose of feats which would affect it.

A shifter loses their ability to speak while in animal form because they are limited to the sounds that a normal, untrained animal can make, but they can communicate normally with other animals of the same general grouping as their new form. (The normal sound a wild parrot makes is a squawk, so changing to this form does not permit speech.)

In addition, a shifter can change their form while in shifter shape as a standard action a number of times per day equal to 1/2 their character level + their Wisdom modifier (minimum 1); doing so does not consume any duration of their shifter shape ability.

At 3rd level, a shifter is no longer limited in which forms they can take from the options provided by beast shape I.

At 6th level, a shifter can use shifter shape to change into a Large or Tiny animal. When taking the form of an animal, a shifter’s shifter shape now functions as beast shape II.

At 8th level, a shifter can use shifter shape to change into a Huge or Diminutive animal or a small or medium magical beast. When taking the form of animals or magical beasts, a shifter’s shifter shape now functions as beast shape III.

At 12th level, a shifter can use shifter shape to transform into a Tiny or Large magical beast. When taking the form of magical beasts, a shifter’s shifter shape now functions as beast shape IV.

At 16th level, a shifter can use shifter shape to transform into a Diminutive or Huge magical beast. When taking the form of magical beasts, a shifter’s shifter shape now functions as magical beast shape.

#### Shifter Claws (Su)

At will, a shifter in her natural form can extend her claws as a swift action to use as a weapon. This magical transformation is fueled as much by the shifter’s faith in the natural world as it is by inborn talent. The claws on each hand can be used as a primary natural attack, dealing 1d4 points of piercing and slashing damage (1d3 if she is Small). If she uses one of her claw attacks in concert with a weapon held in the other hand, the claw acts as a secondary natural attack instead.

As the shifter gains levels, the power of her claws increases. At 3rd level, her claws ignore DR/cold iron and DR/silver. At 7th level, her claw damage increases to 1d6 (1d4 if Small). At 11th level, her claw damage increases to 1d8 (1d6 if Small). At 13th level, her claw damage increases to 1d10 (1d8 if Small). At 17th level, the damage die does not increase, but the critical multiplier becomes ×3. Lastly, at 19th level, the claws ignore DR/adamantine and DR/—.

While a shifter uses shifter shape to assume another form, her natural attacks gain the same benefits granted by her shifter claws ability. If the form she takes has claw attacks, she can use either the base damage of her shifter claws or the damage of the form’s claws, whichever is greater. If the form does not have claw attacks, she can choose up to two natural attacks that would deal less damage than her shifter claw damage and have those attacks instead deal the same damage as her shifter claws.

If her shifter shape form has only 1 attack, she can choose to have 2 natural attacks in that form, as long as they make sense (claws in most cases), instead of increasing the damage of that attack with this class feature. These two attacks are always secondary.

#### Wild Empathy (Ex)

A shifter can improve the attitude of an animal. This ability functions as a [Diplomacy](../../rules/skills/diplomacy.md) check to improve the attitude of a person. The shifter rolls 1d20 and adds their shifter level and Charisma modifier to determine the wild empathy check result. The typical domestic animal has a starting attitude of indifferent, while wild animals are usually unfriendly.

To use wild empathy, the shifter and the animal must be within 30 feet of one another and under normal conditions. Generally, influencing an animal in this way takes 1 minute but, as with influencing people, it might take more or less time.

The shifter can also use this ability to influence a magical beast with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2, but they take a –4 penalty on the check.

#### Defensive Instinct (Ex)

At 2nd level, when unarmored, not using a shield, unencumbered, and conscious, the shifter adds their Wisdom bonus (if any) to their AC and CMD. If they are wearing armor or using a shield, they instead add half their Wisdom bonus to their AC (minimum 0). In addition, the shifter gains a +1 bonus to their AC and CMD at 4th level. This bonus increases by 1 for every 4 shifter levels thereafter (up to a maximum of +5 at 20th level).

These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks and when the shifter is [flat-footed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed), they lose these bonuses when they are immobilized or [helpless](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#helpless). These bonuses do not stack with the [monk AC bonus](monk.md#ac-bonus-ex) class feature.

#### Track (Ex)

At 2nd level, a shifter adds half their level as a bonus on [Survival](../../rules/skills/survival.md) checks to follow tracks.

#### Woodland Stride (Ex)

At 3rd level, a shifter can move through any sort of undergrowth (such as briars, natural thorns, overgrown areas, and similar terrain) at their normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other impairment. Briars, thorns, and overgrown areas that are enchanted or magically manipulated to impede motion still affect the shifter.

#### Bonus Feat (Ex)

At 4th level, and every four levels thereafter, a shifter gains a bonus feat in addition to those gained by normal advancement. These bonus feats must be combat feats or feats which have wild shape as a prerequisite. They treat their shifter shape class feature as the Wild Shape class feature, and use their shifter level as their druid level for qualifying for such feats.

#### Trackless Step (Ex)

At 5th level, a shifter leaves no trail in natural surroundings and cannot be tracked, they can choose to leave a trail if they so desire.

#### Shifter’s Fury (Ex)

At 6th level, a shifter gains the ability to make several ferocious attacks with the same natural weapon. Instead of attacking with all her natural weapons, the shifter can choose a single natural weapon and make a full attack with that natural weapon, gaining a second iterative attack at a –5 as if it was a manufactured weapon. When she does so, all her other natural attacks count as secondary attacks and don’t benefit from shifter’s claws. At 11th level, she gains a third iterative attack at a –10, and at 16th level, she gains a fourth iterative attack at –15.

#### Chimeric Aspect (Su)

At 9th level, when a shifter uses their shifter aspect ability to take on a form, they can choose two aspects and assume the form of each aspect.

#### Greater Chimeric Aspect (Su)

At 14th level, when a shifter uses their shifter aspect ability to take on a form, they can choose up to three aspects and assume the form of each aspect.

#### Master of the Many Forms (Ex)

At 20th level, a shifter may use shifter shape at will and gains the shapechanger subtype, becoming immune to any transmutation effect unless they are willing to accept it.

## Shifter Aspects

These aspects are the minor forms a Canefinder shifter can take with shifter aspect. See the [Swarm Shifter](canefinder-shifter-archetypes.md#swarm-shifter) for the vermin aspect.

### Bat

**Minor Form**: You gain blindsense 10ft and +1 to [Perception](../../rules/skills/perception.md) skill checks. At 8th level, the bonuses increase to 20ft and +2, and at 13th level it increases to 30ft and +3. Blindsense stacks with the major form.

### Bear

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Constitution score. At 8th level, the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Boar

**Minor Form**: You gain [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md) as a bonus feat. Any wildshape level allows for Powerful Charge if the form you take has it. At 8th level, you gain a +2 to Charge Attacks. At 13th level it increases to +4.

### Bull

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Strength score. At 8th level, the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Crocodile

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on [Swim](../../rules/skills/swim.md) checks and grapple combat maneuver checks. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Deinonychus

**Minor Form**: You gain a +6 bonus on Initiative checks. This bonus increases to +8 at 8th level and +10 at 13th level.

### Dolphin

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [Knowledge](../../rules/skills/knowledge.md) checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Dragonfly

**Minor Form**: Reduces the penalty to maintain a grapple without gaining the [grappled](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#grappled) condition by -5. At 8th level decreases to -7, and at 13th level decreases to -9.

### Electric Eel

**Minor Form**: Your natural attacks deal 1d6 point of electricity damage in addition to their normal damage. This additional electricity damage increases to 2d6 at 8th level and 3d6 at 13th level.

### Elephant

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 enhancement bonus on bull rush and overrun combat maneuver checks. Any wildshape level allows for Trample if the form you take has it. At 8th level, this bonus increases to +6, and at 13th level it increases to +8.

### Falcon

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Perception checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Frog

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on CMD against grapple. Any wildshape level allows for Pull and Swallow Whole if the form you take has it. These bonuses increase to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Giant Wasp

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 bonus on Will saves against mind-affecting affects. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Horse

**Minor Form**: You gain the Endurance feat as well as an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed. At 8th level, you gain the Run feat. If you already have the Run feat, you instead can run at 6 times your base speed when using the Run feat. At 13th level, your enhancement bonus to your base speed increases to +15.

### Lion

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [Intimidate](../../rules/skills/intimidate.md) checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Lizard

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](../../rules/skills/acrobatics.md) checks. Any wildshape level allows for Fast Healing if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Mantis

**Minor Form**: You gain Lunge as a bonus feat. At 10th level, your reach increases by 5 feet.

### Monkey

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [Climb](../../rules/skills/climb.md) and acrobatics checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Mouse

**Minor Form**: You gain evasion, as per the rogue class feature. At 10th level, this benefit becomes improved evasion, as per the rogue advanced talent.

### Octopus

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on [Escape Artist](../../rules/skills/escape-artist.md) and Grapple checks. It increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Owl

**Minor Form**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on [Stealth](../../rules/skills/stealth.md) checks. This bonus increases to +6 at 8th level and +8 at 13th level.

### Peafowl

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Charisma score. At 8th level the bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level it increases to +6.

### Scorpion

**Minor Form**: You gain a +1 competence bonus on any poison DC you use. Any wildshape level allows for Poison if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +2 at 8th level and +3 at 13th level.

### Shark

**Minor Form**: You deal an additional +2 damage on attacks made against foes whose hit points are lower than their maximum. This bonus increases to +3 at 8th level and +4 at 15th level.

### Snake

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls when making attacks of opportunity and a +2 dodge bonus to your AC against attacks of opportunity. Any wildshape level allows for Constrict if the form you take has it. These bonuses increase to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Snapping Turtle

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to Wisdom. At 8th level, this bonus increases to +4, and at 13th level, it increases to +6.

### Spider

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 competence bonus on Climb checks, Stealth checks, saving throws against web effects (magical or otherwise), and checks to break free of webs. Any wildshape level allows for Web if the form you take has it. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 13th level.

### Stag

**Minor Form**: You gain an enhancement bonus of +5 feet to your base speed. This bonus increases to +10 feet at 8th level and +20 feet at 15th level.

### Tiger

**Minor Form**: You gain a +2 enhancement bonus to your Dexterity score. This bonus increases to +4 at 8th level and +6 at 15th level.

### Wolf

**Minor Form**: You gain Outflank. You gain the scent ability with a range of 10 feet, or, if you have the scent ability, the range increases by 10 feet. Any wildshape level allows for Trip if the form you take has it. The range of this scent or the increase to your scent increases to 20 feet at 8th level and 30 feet at 13th level. The range doubles if the opponent is upwind and is halved if the opponent is downwind.

### Wolverine

**Minor Form**: You gain 1 additional hit point per Hit Die you have, and treat your Constitution score as 4 points higher than it is for the purpose of determining when you die at negative hit points. At 8th level, you gain Diehard as a bonus feat. Any wildshape level allows for Rage if the form you take has it. At 13th level, you treat your Constitution as being 8 points higher for the purpose of determining when you die at negative hit points.
