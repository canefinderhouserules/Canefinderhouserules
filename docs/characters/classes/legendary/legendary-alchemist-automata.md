# Automata

The automaton of a [tinker](legendary-alchemist.md#automaton-ex) legendary alchemist. Its statistics depend on the alchemist’s level and its customizations.

An automaton’s abilities are determined by the alchemist’s level and by the choices made using its customizations. Automata are mindless constructs with the automaton subtype. As such, it has no Constitution or Intelligence scores.

**Class Level**

This is the alchemist’s class level.

**HD**

This is the total number of 10-sided (d10) Hit Dice the automaton possesses. Since it it is a construct, the automaton does not gain bonus hit points from a high Constitution score, and does not gain bonus hit points from its size as normal (but see bonus HP).

**BAB**

This is the automaton’s base attack bonus. A automaton’s base attack bonus is equal to its Hit Dice. Automata do not gain additional attacks using their natural weapons at higher base attack bonuses.

**Good/Bad Saves**

These are the automaton’s base saving throw bonuses. An automaton possesses two good saving throws and one bad saving throw, determined by the automaton’s configuration.

**Armor Bonus**

The number noted here is the automaton’s base total armor bonus. This bonus may be split between an armor bonus and a natural armor bonus, as decided by the alchemist. This number is modified by the automaton’s base form and some options available through its customizations. Automata may not wear armor of any kind as it interferes with the alchemical processes that animate it.

**Customizations**

These are the number of customizations the automaton has. Automata start with two minor customizations and their creator can add new ones as they grow in power. At 8th level they may also give their automaton major and at 16th level they may give their automaton fantastical customizations. Many customizations also grant a customization benefit when selected.

(Automata Customizations can be found here)

**Special**

This includes a number of abilities gained by all automata as they increase in power. Each of these bonuses is described below.

**Bonus HP**

The automaton gains bonus hit points equal to its creator’s Intelligence modifier for each hit die it has. These are not modified by any bonuses (other than an inherent bonus) to Intelligence the creator might have. For example, an automaton belonging to a 7th-level alchemist with an Intelligence of 18 would have 28 bonus hit points. If its creator put on a Headband of Intellect, its hit points would not change, but if they read a Tome of Clear Thought +2, these bonus hit points would increase to 35.

**Darkvision (Ex)**

The automaton has darkvision out to a range of 60 feet.

**Low-light Vision (Ex)**

The automaton has low-light vision.

**Magical Attacks (Ex)**

The automaton treats its slam attacks as if they were magic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. If the alchemist is 10th level or higher, all of the automaton’s weapons are treated as adamantine for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**Damage Reduction (Ex)**

An automaton gains damage reduction overcome by adamantine at the indicated levels.

## Automaton Base Forms

Each automaton has one of three base forms that determines its starting size, speed, AC, attacks, and ability scores. Alternatively, any one of these base forms can be used to make a Small automaton. If the automaton is Small, it gains a +2 bonus to its Dexterity score and takes a –4 penalty to its Strength. It also gains a +1 size bonus to its AC and attack rolls, a –1 penalty to its CMB and CMD scores, a +2 bonus on its Fly skill checks, and a +4 bonus on its Stealth skill checks. Its slam attack deals the same damage as if it were Medium. If this choice is made, the automaton can be made Medium whenever its creator gains a new alchemist level, while a Medium automaton may be made Small whenever its creator gains a new alchemist level.

**Stable (Ex)**: A multiped automaton has anywhere from 6 to 750 legs, making it especially stable, and the DC to perform an overrun or trip combat maneuver on it is increased by +6. This replaces the normal increase for having multiple legs.

!!! note "Note"
    Fiction is full of constructed servants that display a personality despite not actually having any ability to reason for themselves or disobey their owners. Even real computer programs are sometimes given personalities— consider the alternately deferential and sarcastic responses of some digital assistants (possibly mimicking the portrayal of butlers in fiction). So too can automata be created and portrayed as having a personality. While an automaton never speaks and still does precisely what their master orders, the way in which they do so may differ. A quasi-biological automaton with an appetite might gaze longingly at a morsel of (what it perceives to be) food and look dejected when told to do something that pulls it away from its desire, while a bloodthirsty construction of metal and wheels might jump giddily when ordered to kill its creator’s enemies.   An automaton’s personality might reflect its creator’s or not; often the latter is the more interesting character choice because it offers opportunities for conflict even though the automaton can’t do anything without orders. Is the unsavory nature of the automaton actually a reflection of the mild-mannered chirurgeon’s dark desires? Does the fact that the depraved vivisector’s amoeboid seems reluctant to attack the heroes indicate that she can be redeemed? These can be questions asked not only by the GM or the alchemist’s player, but by the alchemist themselves, adding inner richness to their character.   This is not to say that an automaton must have a character to be interesting. Sometimes there is something delightful about an uncomplicated robot butler with axes for hands.

## Automaton Customizations

An automaton gains minor, major and fantastical customizations as shown on the table above.

## Minor Customizations

### Accelerated

The automaton’s base land speed (along with any other speeds granted by its base form) increases by 10 feet.

**Special**: This customization may be chosen multiple times.

*Customization benefit*: +1 Dexterity

### Advanced Movement

The automaton gains a swim speed equal to its land speed and a burrow speed equal to half its land speed.

*Customization benefit*: +1 Dexterity

### Alchemical Spray

The automaton gains the ability to create and spray large quantities of alchemical substances at foes. This takes the form of a 30-foot cone and deals damage equal to 1d6 + 1d6 per three hit die. A successful Reflex save (DC 10 + ½ the automaton’s HD + its creator’s Intelligence modifier) halves this damage. The type of damage is chosen when the automaton gains this customization and may be acid, cold, electricity, or fire. The automaton’s creator may change the damage type by infusing a dose of an alchemical substance into the automaton—acid for acid, liquid ice for cold, alchemist’s fire for fire, or liquid lightning for electricity.

*Customization benefit*: +5 HP

### Exude Substance

The automaton gains the ability to apply alchemical substances to its slam attack, as if by the alchemical weapon ability of a 2nd-level alchemist. Rather than needing to hold the alchemical items to apply them, its creator infuses a number of them up to their Intelligence modifier into the automaton’s body. This requires a full-round action (3 AP) that provokes attacks of opportunity and requires that the alchemist have the alchemical substance and be able to reach the automaton. Both slams are treated as a single weapon, so the substance affects the next creature struck with either attack and only one substance may affect both slams at a time.

*Customization benefit*: +5 HP

### Graft Weapon

The automaton’s slam attack can deal either slashing, piercing or bludgeoning damage. In addition, the critical range multiplier for its slam attack becomes 18-20/x2, 19-20/ x3, or x4. The choice of damage type and critical range and multiplier may be changed with 1 hour of work.

*Customization benefit*: +1 Strength or Dexterity

### Hulk

The automaton increases to large size. It gains a +4 bonus to Strength and a +2 bonus to its natural armor. It takes a -2 penalty to its Dexterity. This size change also gives it a -1 size penalty to its AC and on attack rolls, a +1 bonus to its CMB and CMD, a -2 penalty on Fly skill checks, and a -4 penalty on Stealth skill checks. If the automaton has the biped base form, it also gains 10-foot reach. Its slam damage does not change.

### Menacing

The automaton may use the aid another action to assist its creator in making Intimidate checks, merely by looming within 30 feet of the creature the creator is attempting to affect. It gains a bonus to the check to aid its creator equal to its HD, and if it is successful its creator treats Intimidate as a class skill if it isn’t already in addition to gaining the normal +2 bonus.

*Customization benefit*: +1 Strength

### Retort

The automaton may make a ranged attack as an attack action. This deals the same damage as its slam attack and counts as one for any special abilities, and has a range increment of 30 feet. It does not provoke attacks of opportunity for using this attack.

*Customization benefit*: +1 Dexterity

### Saddle

The automaton has a built-in perch for a rider and may serve as a combat-trained mount. In addition, it provides its rider a +2 circumstance bonus on Ride checks related to staying in the saddle and a 75% chance to stay in the saddle if they are knocked unconscious, as if they were using a military saddle. (This does not stack with any bonuses from an additional saddle.) The automaton must be at least one size category larger than its rider.

If the automaton has a burrow speed, it may burrow while being ridden with no ill effect for its rider, though this does not give the rider the ability to breathe underground.

*Customization benefit*: +1 armor bonus

### Sensory Unit, Olfactory

The automaton gains the scent special ability and may make Survival checks to follow tracks with a bonus to its check equal to its HD and using its creator’s Intelligence modifier in place of its Wisdom modifier. If it also has either the Seismic Sensory Unit or Proximity Sensory Unit customizations, the distance at which it can detect and pinpoint creatures using scent is doubled.

*Customization benefit*: +5 HP

### Wrecker

The automaton’s natural attacks deal full damage to objects and ignore hardness up to 5 + its HD. It also gains a +10 bonus to Strength checks to break or burst objects.

Customization benefit: +1 Strength

## Major Customizations

### Agile Attacker

As a full-round action (3 AP), the automaton can move up to its speed and make a single attack action without provoking any attacks of opportunity from the target of its attack for its movement. It can move both before and after the attack, but it must move at least 10 feet before the attack and the total distance that it moves cannot be greater than its speed.

*Customization benefit*: +2 Dexterity

### Barrage

*Prerequisite(s)*: Retort.

As a full-round action (3 AP) the automaton may make one ranged attack, as the retort customization, for every 3 HD it has. No more than two such attacks may target a single creature.

*Customization benefit*: +2 Dexterity

### Colossus

*Prerequisite(s)*: Hulk.

The automaton increases to Huge size. It gains a +6 bonus to Strength and a +4 bonus to its natural armor. It takes a

-4 penalty to its Dexterity. This size change also gives the creature a -2 size penalty to its AC and attack rolls, a +2 bonus to its CMB and CMD, 10-foot reach, a -4 penalty on Fly skill checks, and a -8 penalty on Stealth skill checks. If the automaton has the biped base form, its reach increases to 15 feet (10 feet for all other base forms). These bonuses and penalties replace, and do not stack with, those gained from becoming Large. Its slam damage does not change.

### Demolisher

*Prerequisite(s)*: Wrecker.

Once per turn when the automaton’s movement (including a charge) is blocked by an object it may make a single slam attack against that object as a free action. If it successfully destroys the object it may continue moving. If it fails, it immediately stops.

### Feat

The automaton gains the benefit of one of the following feats without meeting its prerequisites, even though it is mindless: Agile Maneuvers, Blind-Fight, Combat Reflexes, Improved Bull Rush, Improved Critical, Improved Natural Attack, Improved Overrun, Improved Sunder, Improved Trip, Power Attack, Toughness, Weapon Finesse.

*Special*: This customization may be chosen multiple times, each time selecting a different feat.

*Customization benefit*: +2 Dexterity or Strength

### Flight

The automaton gains a flight speed equal to its land speed with average maneuverability.

*Customization benefit*: +2 Dexterity

### Hemophage

The automaton’s slam attacks deal 1d4 bleed damage, +1 per 3 Hit Dice. In addition, when it begins its turn with a creature taking bleed damage within its reach it gains 5 temporary HP and a +2 alchemical bonus to Strength until the beginning of its next turn.

*Customization benefit*: +2 Strength

### Nanites

The automaton gains the ability to disassemble itself into a cloud of microscopic fragments that move together. This functions as the [*gaseous form*](../../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md) spell, except that it is non- magical in nature, the automaton keeps any damage reduction it may have, and it lasts for 10 minutes. The automaton may enter its cloud form as a standard action (1 AP) and leave it as a free action on its turn. Once it has returned to its normal form it may not enter its cloud form for 1 hour.

*Customization benefit*: +10 HP

### Repair Drones

The automaton gains fast healing 5, but this cannot bring its hit points above 75% of its maximum (rounded down).

*Customization benefit*: +2 Dexterity or Strength

### Sensory Unit, Seismic

The automaton gains tremorsense 30 feet. If it has the Proximity Sensory Unit customization the range increases to 60 feet.

*Customization benefit*: +10 HP

### Size Change

The automaton may transform between medium and small size, gaining the modifiers to ability scores, AC, attack rolls, CMB and CMD, reach, Fly skill checks, and Stealth skill checks appropriate to its new size. If it has the hulk customization, it may also transform between large size. If it has the colossus customization, it may also transform between huge size. If it has the behemoth customization, it may also transform between gargantuan size.

## Fantastical Customizations

### Behemoth

*Prerequisite(s)*: Colossus.

The automaton increases to Gargantuan size. It gains a +8 bonus to Strength and a +6 bonus to its natural armor. It takes a -6 penalty to its Dexterity. This size change also gives the creature a -4 size penalty to its AC and attack rolls, a +4 bonus to its CMB and CMD, 15-foot reach, a -6 penalty on Fly

skill checks, and a -12 penalty on Stealth skill checks. If the automaton has the biped base form, its reach increases to 20 feet (15 feet for all other base forms). These bonuses and penalties replace, and do not stack with, those gained from becoming huge. Its slam damage does not change.

In addition, as a standard action (1 AP) the automaton can stomp the ground, creating a shockwave. It makes a single trip check, comparing its roll to all valid targets within 30 feet. Any enemies that fall prone take damage as if struck by the automaton’s slam attack. Enemies not knocked prone cannot attempt to trip the automaton in return.

### Eternal Reconstruction

*Prerequisite(s)*: Repair drones.

The automaton’s fast healing becomes regeneration, overcome by any two of acid, cold, electricity, and fire. (If it has immunity to either of these two energy types, it loses it.) It is no longer limited to restoring to 75% of the automaton’s maximum hit points.

### Hurtling Flight

*Prerequisite(s)*: Flight.

The automaton’s flight speed increases to 100 feet (perfect). In addition, as a full-round action (3 AP) it may move up to ten times its speed. While moving overland this is equivalent to 115 miles per hour. Since it has no Constitution score, it may continue this pace indefinitely, covering 2725 miles in 24 hours.

The automaton also gains the benefit of one of the following feats, even though it is mindless: Flyby Attack, Hover, or Wingover.

### Invade Host

*Prerequisite(s)*: Nanites.

While in its cloud form, the automaton may spend a full round action (3 AP) to attempt to perfuse another creature and control it. This functions as the magic jar spell, except that a successful Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + ½ the automaton’s

HD + its creator’s Intelligence modifier) negates the effect instead of a Will saving throw. This requires no focus; the automaton attempts to possess the target immediately upon using the ability and if it fails the automaton simply remains in its nanite form. A controlled creature remains in its body, fully aware of its surroundings. It may make a new Fortitude saving throw each hour; if successful, the automaton is expelled. The automaton’s control

While in control of another creature, the automaton is still limited to one move action (1 AP) per round unless commanded by its creator.

### Orichalcum Shell

The automaton’s damage reduction is no longer overcome by adamantine (becoming DR/-) and it becomes immune to acid, cold, and electricity damage.

### Sensory Unit, Proximity

The automaton gains blindsense 30 feet and blindsight 15 feet.
