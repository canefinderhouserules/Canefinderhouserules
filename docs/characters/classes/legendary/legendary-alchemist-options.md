# Legendary Alchemist Options

## Feats

### Alchemical Archetype Specialist

*Your talents are unique in ways that others could only dream about.*

**Prerequisites:** Legendary Alchemist 1st, an archetype which replaces poison resist +2, +4, +6, +8, and poison immunity.

**Benefit:** You can immediately apply a second archetype to your class which replaces or alters poison resist +2, +4, +6, +8, and poison immunity, regardless if it has already been replaced or altered. If the applied archetype would replace or alter other class features, it must still be a legal choice, replacing or altering other class features as normal. (A 2nd level alienist/red headed merchant would gain dimensional stability and renowned seller at that level).

**Special:** This feat must be selected at 1st level.

**Normal:** You cannot select two archetypes which replace or alter the same class feature.

### Alchemical Launching Crossbow

This weapon can only be loaded with splash weapons, such as Alchemist’s fire, Acid, Holy water, and bombs (from the alchemist class feature), although it can only be fired as an attack action. If this weapon is enhanced, it grants the splash weapon used as ammunition bonus damage equal to that enhancement bonus as a ranged weapon normally would for its ammunition. If the alchemical launching crossbow confirms a critical hit, the splash weapon damage is doubled. Loading an alchemical launching crossbow requires a standard action, although it is reduced by 1 step (from standard to move or from move to free) if the wielder has either the alchemy class feature or the Master Alchemist feat (having both does not allow the weapon to be reloaded any faster).

### Alchemical Weapon Master

*The weapons of alchemy are but playthings to you.*

**Benefit:** You gain proficiency with all 'alchemical' weapons as well as a +1 bonus to attack rolls with those weapons.

**Special:** This feat is treated as Weapon Focus (alchemical weapons) for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites of feats, prestige classes, and other such things.

### Blackened Alchemist

*You are willing to sacrifice even more for your power than others.*

**Prerequisite:** Legendary Alchemist 1st.

**Benefit:** You gain the burn class feature of the kineticist, being able to accept burn in place of spending quintessence. You can only accept an amount of burn equal to your Intelligence modifier.

### Extra Quintessence

*You have far more personal reserves of life than others.*

**Prerequisite:** Quintessence class feature.

**Benefit:** You gain 2 additional quintessence.

**Special:** You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects stack.

### Formulae Familiarity

*Your understanding of certain formulae is second nature.*

**Prerequisite:** Extract class feature.

**Benefit:** Choose a number of extracts equal to your Intelligence modifier; you can prepare these extracts without referring to a formulae book. Whenever you gain a level, you can change the formulae you selected with this feat.

**Normal:** Without this feat, you must use a formulae book to prepare all your extracts.

### Formulae Mastery

*You have surpassed the need for a formulae book.*

**Prerequisites:** Formulae Familiarity, alchemist 9th.

You no longer need to refer to a formulae book to prepare any extract, having exact knowledge of all extracts in your formulae book as long as it is on the same plane as you.

**Special:** As part of this feat, you must select 1 or more formulae books to bond with yourself, becoming instantly aware of all extracts contained within. You can bond with additional formulae books, although doing this takes 1 week of study.

### Launching Crossbow Flurry

*Your expertise with the launching crossbow allows you to rain devastation on others.*

**Prerequisites:** Proficiency with alchemical launching crossbow, multi bomb class feature.

**Benefit:** You treat attacks with the alchemical launching crossbow as normal attacks with bombs, allowing you to load the additional bomb(s) you can throw with the multi bomb or flurry bomb class feature into your alchemical launching crossbow and fire it during the same attack action.

**Special:** If you have an ability which would increase the range increment of your bombs, you can also have it increase the range increment of your alchemical launching crossbow.

### Practiced Alchemist

*You have continued to improve your alchemical research while pursuing other lines of inquiry.*

**Prerequisite:** Extract class feature.

**Benefit:** Your caster level for your extracts gains a +4 bonus as long as this bonus doesn’t raise your caster level above your current Hit Dice.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Its effects stack.

### Practiced Bomber

*You have continued to improve your explosives knowledge while pursuing other lines of inquiry.*

**Prerequisite:** Bomb class feature.

**Benefit:** The damage and saving throws of your bombs are calculated as though your alchemist level were 4 levels higher, to a maximum effective alchemist level equal to your character level.

**Special:** You can select this feat multiple times. Its effects stack.

### Practiced Discipline

*You have continued to improve your disciplines while pursuing other lines of inquiry.*

**Prerequisite:** Alchemical discipline class feature.

**Benefit:** The abilities of your alchemical discipline are calculated as though your alchemist level were 4 levels higher, to a maximum effective alchemist level equal to your character level.

**Special:** If you possess two alchemical disciplines, you must select one of them of which to apply this feat’s benefits. In that situation, you can select this feat a second time to apply its benefits to both of your alchemical disciplines.

**Special:** This feat does not affect alchemical disciplines gained through corruptions.

### Signature Bomb

*Your practice with a specific bomb formula has yielded incredible results.*

**Prerequisites:** Bomb class feature, discovery class feature.

**Benefit:** Select one Bomb discovery which requires a creature to make a saving throw; increase that saving throw by 2.

### Smooth Drinker

*You are able to imbibe magical liquids without drawing attention.*

**Prerequisite:** Sleight of Hand 1 rank.

**Benefit:** Whenever you imbibe a potion, extract, elixir, or similar item, you can make a Sleight of Hand check opposed by all observer’s Perception check. If they fail to exceed your check, they do not notice you imbibe your chosen liquid. For each additional round you use this ability to imbibe a liquid, they gain a +5 circumstance bonus to their Perception check.

**Special:** In a location where constantly drinking would be considered normal, such as at a bar or while eating, you gain a +5 circumstance bonus to your Sleight of Hand check.

### Sneaky Splash

*Even your splash weapons are precise enough to cause precise damage.*

**Prerequisite:** Alchemy class feature.

**Benefit:** Whenever you land a direct hit with a splash weapon (but not bombs from the bomb class feature), your attack can deal precision damage against the creature your attack landed against (but not dealt splash damage) if it would normally qualify for it, such as by attacking an unaware creature while possessing the sneak attack class feature. This precision damage deals the same type of damage as the splash weapon.

**Normal:** You cannot deal precision damage with ranged touch attacks.

### Toxin Focus

*Your study into the field of toxicology has made your toxins far more deadly.*

**Prerequisite:** Toxicologist alchemical discipline class feature.

**Benefit:** The DC of your toxins increases by 2.

### Unique Splash

*Your talent with bombs also extends to other alchemical items.*

**Prerequisites:** Alchemy class feature, 1 or more [shape] discoveries.

**Benefit:** Whenever you spend 1 quintessence to increase the damage of an alchemical splash weapon, you can also apply a [shape] discovery to it as though it was a bomb, paying any associated quintessence cost the discovery may have.

### Virulent Toxins

*Your study into the field of toxicology has made your toxins ignore common defenses.*

**Prerequisites:** Toxicologist alchemical discipline class feature, alchemist 7th.

**Benefit:** Whenever a creature must make a saving throw against one of your toxins, that creature cannot benefit from racial or circumstance bonuses to poison effects (this does not interact with poison immunity).

## Weapons

### Alchemical Bastard Sword

This weapon functions as a Bastard sword with the same qualities as the alchemical blade, and is treated as a bastard sword for the purposes of feats and other features.

### Alchemical Blade

This [short sword](../../../equipment/weapons-martial.md#martial-light-melee-weapons) is intentional hollow, from pommel to the groves along the blade. The pommel can be removed, allowing the contents of one splash weapon (Alchemist’s fire, Acid, Holy water, but not bombs) to be fed into the tube within the grip. Doing so is a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity. While it can be wielded as a short sword and qualifies as such for feats and other abilities, it requires the Exotic Weapon Proficiency feat to use it the way it was intended. So long as the Alchemical Blade is filled with one splash weapon, a successful hit also delivers the contents of the splash weapon to the target. No other creatures are affected by this splash weapon and if the Alchemical Blade confirms a critical hit, the splash weapon damage is not doubled. Once the splash weapon has been delivered, the Alchemical Blade must be refilled to be used again. If an Alchemy Blade gains the broken condition, any splash weapon stored inside is lost. Crafting an Alchemical Blade requires a single Craft (alchemy) check, DC 20, in addition to the normal crafting checks.

### Alchemical Gauntlet

This weapon functions as a Cestus with the same qualities as the alchemical blade and is treated as a cestus for the purposes of feats and other features.

### Alchemical Long Scythe

This weapon functions as a Scythe with the same qualities as the alchemical blade, and is treated as a scythe for the purpose of feats and other features.

### Alchemical Spear

This weapon functions as a Spear with the same qualities as the alchemical blade and is treated as a spear for the purposes of feats and other features.

### Battle Wrench

*This object appears to be an oversized wrench, but is weighted for swinging.*

The battle wrench is not simply a single tool, but is equipped with many foldable parts that allow it to double as a set of thieves' tools when performing Disable Device checks. A masterwork battle wrench counts as a set of masterwork thieves' tools.

The battle wrench’s slim design makes it possible to hide with the Sleight of Hand skill.

### Mercurial Greatsword

This huge blade hides a secret reservoir of mercury that runs along the interior of the blade in a slender channel. When the blade is vertical, the mercury swiftly fills an interior bulb in the haft, but when swung, the heavy liquid flows out into the blade, making it far more impactful. Successful attacks made against objects (including sunder checks) with this weapon deal 4 additional damage. This weapon is treated as a greatsword for the purpose of feats and other features.

### Piston Maul

An alchemically fired piston in the head of this two-handed hammer strikes targets with astounding force. Successful attacks made against objects (including sunder checks) with this weapon deal 4 additional damage.

### Weaponized Syringe

This syringe has been altered to work as a weapon, designed to be thrown or used in close quarters combat. Weaponized syringes have the same qualities as syringes (see new equipment).
