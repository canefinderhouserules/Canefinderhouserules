# Legendary Alchemist Archetypes

## Alienist

*Alchemist tend to stick to earthly knowledge, leaving the forbidden lore of the stars to others with more curiosity than bravery, and yet alienists not only seek this knowledge, but its most horrid teachers. Forming bonds with eldritch horrors, alienists give these waking nightmares form through forbidden rituals, allowing them to exist in our reality.*

**Nightmare Summoner (Su):** The alienist adds *summon monster I*-VI to their formulae list, being able to prepare them without their formulae book. Rather than imbibing this extract, the alienist tosses it as a standard action to a square within 30 feet of themselves, the extract bubbling up before taking the form of a summoned monster of the alienist’s choice known as a summoned horror. The summoned horror only vaguely resembles the creature it is intended to be, looking somehow wrong to all observers except for the alienist and is treated as an aberration for the purpose of effects which would benefit it. Preparing this extract is in effect forming a pact with a specific unique creature from another dimension, causing its soul to temporarily inhabit a body made of alchemical slurry of the alienist’s choice, the creature’s form normally being one that could not exist within reality. Due to this reason, the alienist cannot choose to summon multiple creatures with an extract, even if it would normally be allowed (such as being able to use summon monster II to summon 1d3 creatures from the summon monster I list). A creature summoned through this ability lasts for 1 minute per alienist level. The square (or squares if the creature is larger than Medium) that the monster was summoned to becomes difficult terrain and blocks line of sight for all creatures except the summoned horror and the alienist for a number of minutes equal to the alienist’s intelligence modifier. An alienist cannot summon more than one creature at a time, attempting to do so will cause the extract to fail.

At 5th level, the summoned horror can be 1 level higher than the extract’s level (a 5th level alienist who used a summon monster II extract could instead summon a creature as though using summon monster III). At 11th level, the summoned monster can be 2 levels higher than the extract’s level. At 17th level, summoned monster can be 3 levels higher than the extract’s level.

*This ability alters extract and replaces discipline breakthrough.*

**Godless Research (Ex):** Whenever an alienist throws a summon monster extract, they can spend 1 quintessence while doing so to further modify their summoned horror, pulling it from the darkest depths of the outer reaches. The summoned horror instead takes on a far more disturbing form, inimical to the world into which it was summoned and gains a +4 alchemical bonus to its Strength and 2 additional hit points per hit die it possesses. In addition, the summoned horror is immune to mind-affecting effects. At 10th level, this alchemical bonus increases to +6, the summoned horror gains 6 additional hit points per level, and whenever the summoned horror damages a creature with its natural attacks, that creature must make a successful Will saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 the alienist’s level + their Intelligence modifier) or take 2 Wisdom damage; if a creature takes Wisdom damage from this ability, the summoned horror gains temporary hit points equal to the alienist’s level which last for 1 hour (these hit points do not stack with each other). At 16th level, this alchemical bonus increases to +8, the summoned horror gains 8 additional hit points per level, and the summoned horror instead deals Wisdom drain, the summoned horror gaining temporary hit points equal to two times the alienist’s level.

*This ability replaces alchemical discipline.*

**Altered Research (Ex):** The alienist can select [evolution] talents as though they had the mutagenic alchemical specialization, being able to apply them to their summoned horror. As normal, a summoned horror can only have a single [evolution] talent applied to it, but the alienist can select the dual evolution discovery.

**Dimensional Stability (Ex):** At 2nd level, the alienist and their summoned horror gain a +2 bonus on effects which would force them to a different plane (such as banishment or plane shift). At 4th, 6th, and 8th level, this bonus increases by +2.

*This ability replaces poison resistance.*

**Horrific Exposure (Ex):** At 3rd level, whenever the alienist uses a summon monster extract, all enemies within 30 feet of the summoned horror except those of the aberration type must make a successful Will saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 the alienist’s level + their Intelligence modifier) or become [confused](../../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#confused) for 1 round. At 13th level, this effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the alienist’s Intelligence modifier, and enemies must also make a successful Will save to avoid becoming confused whenever the summoned horror reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points.

*This ability replaces swift alchemy and instant alchemy.*

**Shared Essence (Su):** An alienist’s summoned horror can use quintessence surge as long as the alienist is not currently affected by that ability, using the alchemist’s quintessence as though it was their own. If they do, the alchemist heals an amount of hit points equal to 1/2 their class level and cannot use their quintessence surge until its effects on their summoned horror ends. In addition, as long as either the alienist or their summoned creature is under the effects of quintessence surge and within 120 feet of the square the summoned horror was summoned, they can teleport to that square as a move action.

*This alters quintessence surge.*

**Adamant Horrors (Ex):** At 9th level, the alienist’s summoned horror gain DR 5/orichalcum. At 19th level, this increases to DR 10/orichalcum.

*This ability replaces discipline defense.*

**Unbound Step (Su):** At 10th level, whenever an alienist or their summoned horror is under the effects of quintessence surge, they can teleport up to 30 feet per round as a move action.

*This ability replaces poison immunity.*

**Reality Rip (Su):** At 20th level, an alienist gains 2 discoveries and can spend 2 points of quintessence to use the [*gate*](../../../magic/spells/gate.md) spell for planar travel. They can instead spend 5 points of quintessence to use gate to use the call creature effect, treating any creature summoned as though it was summoned by their nightmare summoner ability (thus allowing them to spend quintessence to enhance it).

*This ability replaces grand discovery.*

## Archaic Philosopher

*Science moves on, but that doesn’t mean that old theories become worthless. The archaic philosopher uses old ideas of how the natural elements work to power their alchemy and gain the service of a powerful, mutable elemental.*

**Natural Philosophy (Ex):** The archaic philosopher gains all [Knowledge](../../../rules/skills/knowledge.md) skills as class skills. Upon taking the first level in this class, they learn Aquan, Ignan, Sylvan, and Terran. If they already speak one of these languages they do not gain a replacement.

**Bound Elemental (Ex):** Starting at 1st level, an archaic philosopher retains the service of an elemental being that grows and mutates as its master’s knowledge of natural forces grows. This functions similarly to a tinker alchemist’s automaton, with the following exceptions.

It is an outsider with the elemental subtype and is not mindless. It speaks one of Aquan, Ignan, Sylvan, or Terran (typically the one associated with its element).

It gains bonus hit points based on its Constitution score and does not gain the Bonus HP ability. Its Constitution increases by 1 at 4 HD and every 4 HD thereafter.

It gains one feat at 1st level and every odd HD thereafter.

Instead of the elemental’s slam being treated as adamantine for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction when the archaic philosopher attains 10th level, the elemental’s slam gains the following effect, depending on the base form:

- *Earth:* push 5 feet, +5 feet for every 5 levels above 10th

- *Fire:* burn 2d6, DC 10 + 1/2 the elemental’s level + the archaic philosopher’s Intelligence modifier

- *Metal:* treated as adamantine for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction

- *Water:* trip

- *Wood*: grab

The alchemical binding of elemental to alchemist limits the elemental’s freedom, and so it is limited to taking a single move action per round unless commanded, just as an automaton.

If an archaic philosopher releases their bound elemental, they may gain a new one by performing a ceremony requiring 24 uninterrupted hours of meditation and alchemical experimentation in an environment infused with that element (e.g. a forge for a metal elemental, a river for a water elemental, or a laboratory filled with candles, braziers, and open flames). The ceremony requires rare reagents with a cost of 100 gp. This ceremony can also replace a bound elemental that has perished.

*This ability replaces alchemical discipline, but this archetype may still be combined with the recondite mechanist archetype. The changes it applies to the tinker alchemist’s automaton instead apply to the bound elemental.*

**Archaic Breakthrough (Ex):** At 5th level and every six levels afterwards, an alchemist experiences a mystic fusion with their bound elemental, gaining the following benefits:

*Assimilating Command (Ex):* At 5th level, when an archaic philosopher spends quintessence to command their bound elemental, they gain 10 temporary hit points and a +2 bonus to saving throws against paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning. These temporary hit points and bonuses last for a number of rounds equal to the tinker alchemist’s Intelligence modifier. They increase to 20 temporary hit points and +4 at 10th level and 30 temporary hit points and +6 at 20th level.

*Life Link (Ex):* At 11th level, as an immediate action when either the archaic philosopher or their bound elemental would take damage, the archaic philosopher may divide the damage taken between the two in any way they wish. This occurs before the damage is taken and so may prevent either from dying due to damage taken.

*Gentle Fetters (Ex):* At 17th level, an archaic philosopher no longer needs to spend quintessence to command their bound elemental as a free action. If they have Assimilating Command, they gain the bonuses from that breakthrough whenever they command their automaton.

*This alters discipline breakthrough, but this archetype may still be combined with the subtle alchemist archetype. The archaic alchemist cannot also choose the tinker discipline with the subtle alchemist archetype.*

**Elemental Resistance (Ex):** At 9th level and again at 19th level, the archaic philosopher gains a defense depending on their bound elemental.

9th level

- *Earth:* Resist acid 10

- *Fire:* Resist fire 10

- *Metal:* DR 5/bludgeoning and magic

- *Water:* DR 5/piercing and magic

- *Wood:* DR 5/slashing and magic

19th level

- *Earth:* Immunity to acid

- *Fire:* Immunity to fire

- *Earth:* DR 20/bludgeoning and magic

- *Water:* DR 20/piercing and magic

- *Wood:* DR 20/slashing and magic

If the archaic philosopher retains this defense if they lose or dismiss their bound elemental, but if they later gain a bound elemental of a different element their defense changes to match that element.

*This alters discipline defense.*

**Bound Elemental Base Forms**

Each elemental has one of five elements, which determines its starting size, speed, AC, attacks, and ability scores. Alternatively, any one of these base forms can be used to make a Small elemental. If the automaton is Small, it gains a +2 bonus to its Dexterity score. It also takes a –4 penalty to its Strength and a –2 penalty to its Constitution. It also gains a +1 size bonus to its AC and attack rolls, a –1 penalty to its CMB and CMD scores, a +2 bonus on its [Fly](../../../rules/skills/fly.md) skill checks, and a +4 bonus on its [Stealth](../../../rules/skills/stealth.md) skill checks. Its slam attack deals the same damage as if it were medium. If this choice is made, the bound elemental can be made Medium whenever its master gains a new alchemist level, while a Medium bound elemental may be made Small whenever its master gains a new alchemist level.

Earth

*Starting Statistics*

Size Medium; Speed 20 ft., burrow 5 ft.; AC +2 natural armor; Attack slam (1d6); Saves Fort (good), Ref (bad), Will (good); Ability Scores Str 14, Dex 12, Con 16, Int 3, Wis 10, Cha 11

Fire

*Starting Statistics*

Size Medium; Speed 30 ft.; AC +2 natural armor; Attack slam (1d6); Saves Fort (bad), Ref (good), Will (good); Ability Scores Str 14, Dex 16, Con 12, Int 3, Wis 10, Cha 11

Metal

*Starting Statistics*

Size Medium; Speed 30 ft.; AC +2 natural armor; Attack slam (1d6); Saves Fort (good), Ref (good), Will (bad); Ability Scores Str 16, Dex 12, Con 14, Int 3, Wis 10, Cha 11

Water

*Starting Statistics*

Size Medium; Speed 20 ft., swim 20 ft.; AC +2 natural armor; Attack slam (1d6); Saves Fort (good), Ref (bad), Will (good); Ability Scores Str 12, Dex 14, Con 16, Int 3, Wis 10, Cha 11

Wood

*Starting Statistics*

Size Medium; Speed 30 ft.; AC +2 natural armor; Attack slam (1d6); Saves Fort (bad), Ref (good), Will (good); Ability Scores Str 14, Dex 14, Con 14, Int 3, Wis 10, Cha 11

## Beastborn Shifter

*More attuned to nature than the more abstract forms other alchemists take, beastborn shifters focus on more traditional forms, instead trying to perfect their own evolution.*

**Bestial Transformation (Su):** At 1st level, beastborn shifters gain the ability to turn themselves into any small or Medium animal and back again, although they are limited to the following forms; badger, bird, camel, cat (small), dire rat, dog, dolphin, horse, manta ray, pony, snake (viper or constrictor), or wolf. This ability functions like the *beast shape I* spell, except as noted here. The effect lasts for a number of hours equal to their beastborn shifter class level + their Intelligence modifier (minimum 1), and they split the duration of this ability however they want. Each use of this ability uses up at least an hour of its duration, regardless of how long they stay in it. A beastborn shifter can change form while in their bestial transformation but doing so reduces their total duration by 1 hour. Bestial transformation is treated as wild shape for the purpose of feats which would affect it.
A beastborn shifter loses their ability to speak while in animal form because they are limited to the sounds that a normal, untrained animal can make, but they can communicate normally with other animals of the same general grouping as their new form. (The normal sound a wild parrot makes is a squawk, so changing to this form does not permit speech.) In addition, a beastborn shifter can change their form while in bestial transformation as a standard action a number of times per day equal to 1/2 their character level + their Intelligence modifier (minimum 1); doing so does not consume any duration of their bestial transformation ability. At 3rd level, a beastborn shifter is no longer limited in which forms they can take from the options provided by beast shape I.

At 6th level, a beastborn shifter can use bestial transformation to change into a Large or Tiny animal. When taking the form of an animal, a beastborn shifter’s bestial transformation now functions as beast shape II. At 8th level, a beastborn shifter can use bestial transformation to change into a Huge or Diminutive animal or a small or medium magical beast. When taking the form of animals or magical beasts, a beastborn shifter’s shifter shape now functions as beast shape III. At 12th level, a beastborn shifter can use bestial transformation to transform into a Tiny or Large magical beast. When taking the form of magical beasts, a beastborn shifter’s bestial transformation now functions as beast shape IV. At 16th level, a beastborn shifter can use bestial transformation to transform into a Diminutive or Huge magical beast. When taking the form of magical beasts, a beastborn shifter’s bestial transformation now functions as magical beast shape I.

*This ability replaces alchemical discipline and discipline breakthrough.*

**Lycanthropic Defense (Ex):** At 9th level, the beastborn shifter gains DR 5/silver. At 19th level, this increases to DR 10/silver.

*This ability replaces discipline defense.*

## Black Powder Researcher

*The battlefield is alive with the sound of explosives, and none are louder than the roar of firearms. Already a master of explosives, black powder researchers take things a step further with their study of firearms. Intrigued and excited by the potential of these unique weapons, black powder researchers live for the moment of explosions, never satisfied until something blows up.*

**Weapon and Armor proficiencies:** Black powder researchers are proficient with all [simple weapons](../../../equipment/weapons-simple.md) and [firearms](../../../equipment/weapons-firearms.md). They are also proficient with [light armor](../../../equipment/armor.md) and [bucklers](../../../equipment/weapons-exotic.md#exotic-ranged-weapons-part-3).

*This alters the alchemist’s weapon and armor proficiencies.*

**Gunsmith (Ex):** At 1st level, a black powder researcher gains one of the following firearms of their choice: blunderbuss, musket, or pistol. Their starting weapon is battered, and only they know how to use it properly. All other creatures treat their gun as if it had the broken condition. If the weapon already has the broken condition, it does not work at all for anyone else trying to use it. This starting weapon can only be sold for scrap (it’s worth 4d10 gp when sold). The black powder researcher also gains Gunsmithing as a bonus feat, although they can craft bullets, pellets, and black powder for a cost in raw materials equal to 1% of the price rather than 10%.

**Quintessential Grit (Ex):** A black powder researcher can spend quintessence in place of grit.

*This ability alters quintessence.*

**Alchemical Deeds (Ex):** At each listed level, the black powder research gains the listed deeds:

*Gunslinger’s Reflexes:* At 1st level, the black powder researcher gains an uncanny knack for knowing how to avoid attacks. As long as the black powder researcher has at least 1 point of quintessence, they do not provoke attacks of opportunity for firing or reloading a firearm.

*Quick Clear*: At 1st level, as a standard action, the black powder researcher can remove the broken condition from a single firearm they are currently wielding, as long as that condition was gained by a firearm misfire. The black powder researcher must have at least 1 point of quintessence to perform this deed. Alternatively, if the black powder researcher spends 1 quintessence to perform this deed, they can perform quick clear as a move action instead of a standard action.

*Ricochet Shot:* At 5th level, the black powder researcher can make a surprise attack, being able fire a shot at a wall or piece of solid terrain, and have it ricochet off. When they do, they use the square immediately in front of the wall or piece of solid terrain to determine line of sight to a target, and this square is considered the new origin square of the attack, although their attack roll takes a -2 penalty. The attack uses that square to determine the effects of cover, and the black powder researcher’s own square to determine the effects of concealment. The black powder researcher can make this shot as long as they have at least 1 grit point. When making this shot, they can spend 1 quintessence to ignore the effects of all cover or concealment. The black powder researcher must choose to spend the quintessence before they make the attack roll.

*Speedloader:* At 3rd level, as long as the black powder researcher has at least 1 quintessence, they reduce the action needed to load a firearm as long as it would be able to be loaded as a full action or quicker; from a full round to a standard, standard to move, and move to free action.

In addition, the black powder researcher can select [[Legendary Gunslinger Deed |]] or gun masteries (legendary gunslinger) in place of discoveries, treating their black powder researcher level as their gunslinger level for the purpose of meeting the prerequisites.

*This ability replaces throw anything and poison resistance +4*

**Boomstick (Ex):** At 2nd level, the black powder researcher must select the explosive weapon discovery and can only use it on ammunition which can be loaded into a firearm. When using this discover, their attacks deal full damage to swarms.

**Chemist’s Trigger (Ex):** At 2nd level and every four levels afterwards, the black powder researcher reduces the misfire rate of any firearm they wield by 1 (to a minimum of 0).

*This ability replaces poison resistance +2 and +6.*

**Gritty Research (Ex):** At 3rd level, whenever a black powder researcher rolls initiative, they gain 1 point of gritty quintessence which can only be spent on a deed or quintessence surge. Gritty quintessence lasts for 1 minute or until spent. Once a black powder researcher has gained gritty quintessence from this class feature, they cannot do so again for 5 minutes. At 6th level and every six levels thereafter, this ability grants an additional gritty quintessence upon rolling initiative.

*This ability replaces swift alchemy and instant alchemy.*

## Chirurgeon

*While many alchemists revel in the destruction their creations can cause, the chirurgeon pursues a more subtle art. By studying anatomy and medicine, they not only gain skill at healing, but an adeptness at striking the most vulnerable parts of their enemies.*

**Sneak Attack (Ex):** At 1st level, a chirurgeon gains the sneak attack ability as a rogue of the same level. If a character already has sneak attack from another class, the levels from the classes that grant sneak attack stack to determine the effective rogue level for the sneak attack’s extra damage dice (so an alchemist 1/rogue 1 has a +1d6 sneak attack like a 2ndlevel rogue, an alchemist 2/rogue 1 has a +2d6 sneak attack like a 3rd-level rogue, and so on).

*This ability replaces bomb. The chirurgeon cannot select bomb discoveries.*

**Disease Resistance (Ex):** At 2nd level, a chirurgeon gains a +2 bonus on all saving throws against disease. This bonus increases to +4 at 5th level, and then again to +6 at 8th level. At 10th level, an alchemist becomes completely immune to disease.

*This ability replaces poison resistance and poison immunity.*

**Skilled Hands (Ex):** The chirurgeon may use their Intelligence modifier when making [Heal](../../../rules/skills/heal.md) checks instead of their Wisdom modifier. At 2nd level, a chirurgeon adds half their level on Heal skill checks. At 5th level they gain the skill unlockPU for Heal.

**Makeshift Bandages (Ex):** Starting at 3rd level, when the chirurgeon uses the last use of a healer’s kit there is a 20% chance that they are able to stretch the medicaments and plasters, leaving the kit with one use only they or another chirurgeon can use. This remaining use can potentially be stretched in the same manner. At 7th level, and every 4 levels thereafter, this chance increases by 20%, to a maximum of 100% at 19th level.

*This ability replaces multi bomb, empowered bomb, flurry bomb, and ultimate bomb.*

**Rogue Talents:** A chirurgeon can select a rogue talent that adds effects to sneak attack in place of a discovery. At 10th level they can select an advanced rogue talent that adds effects to sneak attack in place of a discovery.

## Esotericist

*Most alchemists must make careful note of the laws of nature, bending and shaping them to achieve fantastic results. The esotericist, on the other hand, needs exert no such effort. When they formulate a hypothesis, the world bends itself to accommodate it. Because of this supernatural ability to warp reality, esotericists do not train as rigorously in mixing chemicals to create wondrous effects and instead produce them directly through magic.*

**Class Skills:** The esotericist gains [Bluff](../../../rules/skills/bluff.md), [Diplomacy](../../../rules/skills/diplomacy.md), and Intimidate as class skills. This modifies class skills but may be combined with other archetypes that add new class skills.

**Spells:** An esotericist casts psychic spells drawn from the psychic spell list plus the legendary alchemist’s extract list. Only spells from the psychic class spell list of 6th level or lower and esotericist spells are considered to be part of the esotericist’s spell list. If a spell appears on both the psychic class spell list and the legendary alchemist extract list, the esotericist uses the spell level from the legendary alchemist extract list. They can cast any spell she knows without preparing it ahead of time. To learn or cast a spell, an esotericist must have a Charisma score equal to at least 10 + the spell’s level. The saving throw DC against a esotericist’s spell is 10 + the spell’s level + the esotericist’s Charisma modifier.

Like other spellcasters, an esotericist can cast only a certain number of spells of each spell level per day. They know the same number of spells and receive the same number of spells slots per day as a bard of her legendary alchemist level and knows and uses 0-level knacks as a bard uses cantrips. In addition, they receive bonus spells per day if they have a high Charisma score.

At 5th level and every 3 levels thereafter, an esotericist can choose to learn a new spell in place of one they already know, using the same rules as a bard. In effect, the esotericist loses the old spell in exchange for the new one. The new spell’s level must be the same as that of the spell being exchanged, and it must be at least 1 level lower than the highest-level spell the esotericist can cast.

A esotericist need not prepare her spells in advance. They can cast any esotericist spell they know at any time, assuming they have not yet used up their allotment of spells per day for the spell’s level.

*This ability replaces extracts, but this archetype may still be combined with other archetypes that reduce the number of extracts per day. In this case, reduce the esotericist’s number of spell slots per day for each level by the same number.*

**Reality Impressor (Ex):** The esotericist uses Charisma for all alchemist class features that would normally use Intelligence.

**Magnetic Surge (Su):** At 4th level, the esotericist can spend 1 quintessence as a swift action. If they do, they gain a +1 alchemical bonus to the caster level of the next spell they cast before the end of their next turn. At 8th level and every four levels afterwards, this alchemical bonus increases by 1 (to a maximum of +5 at 20th level).

*This ability replaces quintessence surge.*

## Gourmand

*The greatest masters of a craft devote their lives to perfecting it, whether that be alchemy, swordplay, or woodworking. For no one is this as true as the gourmand, who dedicates themselves to the study of both alchemy and the culinary arts. By blending the science of alchemical formulas with the art of flavor, the gourmand strives towards creating masterpieces of food and drink with incredible side effects and a dash of spice.*

**Culinary Alchemy (Su):** A gourmand can use Craft (alchemy) to create food and beverages as though it were the relevant [Craft](../../../rules/skills/craft.md) or [Profession](../../../rules/skills/profession.md) skill and treats food and drink as alchemical items for the purpose of crafting them. Alcoholic beverages created by a gourmand can never inflict addiction.

*This alters alchemy.*

**Gourmet Meal (Su):** At 1st level, a gourmand can spend 10 minutes and 1 point of quintessence to turn a prepared extract into a meal for themselves and their allies. This meal can feed up to four Small or Medium creatures plus one for every five alchemist levels. A Large creature counts as two Medium creatures, a Huge creature counts as two Large creatures, and so on. Eating enough of the meal to gain the benefits takes 10 minutes. Any creatures who consume this meal gain the benefits of the extract used with its duration increased by one step; from rounds per level to minutes, minutes to ten minutes, ten minutes to hours, and hours to 24 hours. The effect ends as soon as the gourmand uses this ability again.

*This replaces discipline.*

**Alchemical Distillery (Su):** When brewing a potion, a gourmand can instead distil it into an alcoholic beverage. Potions created with this ability increase all their numerical effects by 50% as though affected by the [Empower Spell](../../../rules/feats/empower-spell.md) metamagic feat and grant an alchemical bonus to Charisma based skill and ability checks equal to the potion’s caster level for one hour, but impart a -2 penalty to Intelligence, Wisdom and Dexterity based checks for the same duration. This penalty stacks if multiple potions made with this ability are consumed. Effects that remove poison will negate both the bonus and the penalty.

*This alters brew potion.*

**Perfect Pairing (Su):** Every good gourmand pairs their meals with a good drink. At 5th level, a gourmand can add an alcoholic beverage to their gourmet meal, granting anyone who consumes both a number of temporary hit points equal to double the gourmand’s level. These temporary hit points last until the next time the gourmand uses their gourmet meal ability, and if lost are regained at a rate of 1 per minute.

*This replaces the discipline breakthrough at 5th level.*

**Culinary Defense (Ex):** At 9th level, the gourmand’s culinary failures have helped them develop a strong stomach, granting them a bonus equal to 1/2 their class level to saving throws which inflict the [sickened](../../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened) and [nauseated](../../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#nauseated) condition and against poisons.

*This replaces the discipline defence at 9th level.*

**Alchemical Snacks (Su):** At 11th level, when a gourmand creates an extract, they can infuse it into a snack or beverage they’ve already prepared, such as a cookie or a small bottle of wine. When a creature consumes this alchemical snack while under the benefits of a gourmet meal, they can gain the benefit of that extract for the remaining duration of the meal. If they do this, they lose the benefits of the previous extract extended by gourmet meal.

*This replaces the discipline breakthrough at 11th level.*

**Lavish Meals (Su):** At 16th level, when creating a gourmet meal, a gourmand can expend an amount of quintessence up to half their maximum and an equal number of extracts. Creatures who consume this meal gain the benefits of as many of these extracts as they want, dividing the duration of the effect by the number of extracts they accept.

*This replaces the discipline breakthrough at 16th level.*

**Perfect Palette (Ex):** At 19th level, a gourmand’s palette becomes so refined, they can identify the properties of anything that touches their tongue. The gourmand automatically identifies any alchemical substance, potion, or the properties of any food or beverage they taste. In addition, as an immediate action they can spit out any such substance, such as an ingested poison or diseased food.

*This replaces the discipline defence at 19th level.*

## Legendary Alchemist Alternate Class Features

Alternate class features are small, modular archetypes. They swap out a single class feature (or a few related class features) for new abilities. By affecting only small parts of the class at a time, a player is able to build the legendary alchemist that best fits their ideas. With these alternate class features, the legendary alchemist is more flexible than ever before. If an archetype or alternate class replaces one of the below class features, the replacement class feature may not be exchanged for an alternate class feature. Mix and match these alternate class features to build your perfect legendary alchemist!

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**

The following options can be taken in place of the legendary alchemist’s normal weapon and armor proficiencies:

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies**: Alchemists are proficient with all [simple weapons](../../../equipment/weapons-simple.md), bows, and weapons with 'alchemical' in their name. They are also proficient with [light armor](../../../equipment/armor.md) and [bucklers](../../../equipment/weapons-exotic.md#exotic-ranged-weapons-part-3).

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies** Alchemists are proficient with all simple and [martial weapons](../../../equipment/weapons-martial.md). They are also proficient with [light armor](../../../equipment/armor.md), [medium armor](../../../equipment/armor.md), [bucklers](../../../equipment/weapons-exotic.md#exotic-ranged-weapons-part-3), and [light shields](../../../equipment/weapons-martial.md#martial-light-melee-weapons).

**Weapon and Armor Proficiencies** Alchemists are proficient with all [simple weapons](../../../equipment/weapons-simple.md), crossbows, and weapons with 'alchemical' in their name. They are not proficient in any armor or [shields](../../../equipment/armor.md). In addition, they gain the following class feature:

**AC Bonus (Ex)** When unarmored and unencumbered, the alchemist adds their Intelligence bonus (if any) to their AC and their CMD. In addition, an alchemist gains a +1 bonus to AC and CMD at 4th level. This bonus increases by 1 for every four alchemist levels thereafter, up to a maximum of +5 at 20th level. These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the alchemist is [flat-footed](../../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed). They lose these bonuses when they are immobilized or [helpless](../../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#helpless), when they wear any armor, when they carry a shield, or when they carry a medium or heavy load.

**Throw Anything**

These alternative class features can be taken in place of throw anything.

**Polymath (Ex)**: The legendary alchemist gains 2 additional skill points per level.

**Healer (Ex)**: When the legendary alchemist restores a creature’s hit points with an alchemist extract, alchemist spell, or alchemical item, they add their Intelligence modifier to the hit points restored. This does not apply to effects that grant fast healing.

**Weapon Focus (Ex)** The legendary alchemist gains the Weapon Focus feat, even if they do not meet the prerequisites. In addition, they are treated as fighters of half their class level for the purpose of selecting feats which require levels in the fighter class.

**Poison Resistance**

These alternative class features can be taken in place of poison resistance.

**Mithridatic Healer (Ex)**: Starting at 2nd level, whenever the legendary alchemist succeeds on a saving throw against poison (but not their own toxins, if they have the toxicologist alchemical discipline), they heal 2 hit points. This increases to 4 hit points at 5th level and 6 hit points at 8th level. At 10th level, whenever the legendary alchemist succeeds on a saving throw against poison they also heal one point of ability damage to an ability score of their choice.

**Toughness (Ex)** At 2nd level, the legendary alchemist gains the Toughness feat.

**Alchemical Weapon**

These alternative class features can be taken in place of alchemical weapon.

**Alchemical Weapon Transformation (Ex)** At 3rd level, a legendary alchemist can spend 1 minute to transform an alchemist fire into a melee weapon of their choice which is sized for them. This weapon only deals fire damage equal to the weapon’s normal damage, but does not gain the alchemist’s Strength modifier to damage, instead gaining the legendary alchemist’s Intelligence modifier to damage (minimum 1). After a number of minutes equal to the legendary alchemist’s Intelligence modifier, this weapon melts into inert alchemical slime.

**Firebreather (Ex)**: At 3rd level, the legendary alchemist can ingest a dose of a single splash weapon as a move action. Once within a number of rounds equal to their Intelligence modifier (minimum 1), they may breathe it out in a 15-foot cone as a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity.

All within the area are affected as if struck with the splash weapon. If a target succeeds on a Reflex saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 the legendary alchemist’s level + their Intelligence modifier) they are instead affected as if by the splash damage (if any). The legendary alchemist may add their Intelligence modifier to the damage if they have the throw anything class feature.

**Vigilante Talent (Ex)** At 3rd level, the legendary alchemist can select a vigilante talent as though they were a vigilante of their class level (they do not have a vigilante specialization). Each time they gain a level, they can choose to change this talent.

**Alchemical Fusion**

These alternative class features can be taken in place of alchemical fusion.

**Advanced Study (Ex)** At 3rd level and every level thereafter, the legendary alchemist adds 1 formula of every level they know free of cost (at 4th level, the legendary alchemist would add a 1st and 2nd level formulae to their formulae book).

**Magical Weapon (Su)** At 3rd level, the legendary alchemist can spend 1 point of quintessence as a free action to increase the enhancement bonus of any weapon they wield by +1 (to a maximum of +5) for a number of minutes equal to their Intelligence modifier.

**Potion Grenade (Su)**: At 3rd level, a alchemist can spend 1 point of quintessence to infuse explosives into a potion; doing so takes 1 minute. The alchemist may throw a potion prepared in such a manner as a splash weapon. Any target hit with the potion takes 1d6 piercing and slashing damage and is affected by the potion as if they had drunk it. This preparation lasts 24 hours, after which it reverts to a normal potion.

## Martial Analyst

*The combination of raw power and scientific discovery is a rare one, but martial analysts manage to mix both into the sweetest of sciences. Unorthodox competitors, martial analysts know that the only thing that they can rely upon is their fist, doing their best to make their attacks as explosive as possible.*

**Weapon and Armor proficiency:** Martial analysts are proficient with all [simple weapons](../../../equipment/weapons-simple.md) and [light armor](../../../equipment/armor.md) but not [shields](../../../equipment/armor.md).

*This alters the [alchemist’s weapon and armor proficiency](../alchemist.md#weapon-and-armor-proficiency).*

**Educated Fist (Ex):** The martial analyst gains the [Improved Unarmed Strike](../../../rules/feats/improved-unarmed-strike.md) feat, dealing damage equal to a monk of their class level. In addition, the martial analyst gains the [Stunning Fist](../../../rules/feats/stunning-fist.md) feat, although they use their Intelligence in place of their Wisdom to determine its saving throw and is treated as a monk for how often they can use it. A martial analyst also can spend 1 point of quintessence to use their Stunning Fist feat.

*This ability replaces throw anything.*

**Erudite Defense (Ex):** At 1st level, the martial analyst gains a bonus to their armor class equal to their Intelligence modifier while wearing light or no armor and not carrying a shield. In addition, a martial analyst gains a +1 bonus to AC and CMD at 4th level. This bonus increases by 1 for every four alchemist levels thereafter, up to a maximum of +5 at 20th level. These bonuses to AC apply even against touch attacks or when the alchemist is flat-footed. They lose these bonuses when they are immobilized or helpless, when they wear any armor heavier than light, when they carry a shield, or when they carry a medium or heavy load.

This bonus cannot exceed the martial analyst’s class level.

*This ability replaces poison resistance +2 and poison resistance +6.*

**Power Gauntlet (Ex):** At 2nd level, the martial analyst gains the ability to create a unique alchemical gauntlet known as a power gauntlet, gaining proficiency with this weapon. No other creature is proficient in its use and treats it as a gauntlet. This unique item is treated as a masterwork alchemical gauntlet; however, it deals damage equal to the martial analyst’s unarmed damage. This gauntlet can be enhanced as a normal item, and the martial analyst can do so by sacrificing gold or valuable items equal to the cost of the enhancement and spending 1 day in study, successfully enhancing her weapon without fail. If the gauntlet is destroyed, the martial analyst can spend 1 day and gp equal to her level x 50 to create a new one.

A power gauntlet also has a unique reservoir that can be filled as a move action, allowing it to be filled with a liquid or powdered alchemical item (including a toxin); the alchemical item takes full effect on the next creature struck by the weapon, but does not splash, spread, affect additional targets, or benefit from any other effects that specifically affect splash weapons. Any extra damage added is not doubled on a critical hit. At 6th level and every six levels afterwards, the martial analyst can add an additional reservoir to their power gauntlet, allowing them to store an additional alchemical item. At 6th level, this reservoir can be filled as a swift action.

*This ability replaces alchemical weapon, poison resistance +4, and poison immunity.*

**Studied Quintessence (Ex):** At 4th level, whenever a martial analyst is under the effects of their quintessence surge, if they deal damage with an alchemical item using their power gauntlet, they can reroll any damage dice from that alchemical item if they come up as a 1 or 2. The martial analyst must accept the result of the reroll, even if it would be lower.

*This ability alters quintessence surge.*

**Bomb Knuckle (Ex):** At 5th level, the martial analyst’s gauntlet gains an additional reservoir which can only be filled with a bomb as a free action once per turn (which remains active, even if the martial analyst does not have the delayed detonation discovery), although they cannot apply a [shape] discovery to a bomb placed in that reservoir, and it does not deal splash damage. When the martial analyst uses an attack action to attack, they can detonate this bomb as a part of a successful attack. Any creature damaged by this bomb is treated as though they took a direct hit from that bomb for the purpose of its effects.

*This ability replaces multi bomb.*

**Full Metal Fist (Ex):** At 5th level, the martial analyst’s power gauntlet is treated as all metals for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

*This ability replaces alchemical study, but the martial analyst can take Skill Unlock: Craft (alchemy) as a discovery; this does not count as taking the Signature Skill feat.*

**Dynamite Punch (Ex):** At 15th level, the martial analyst’s power gauntlet gains an additional bomb reservoir (both of which can be filled with the same free action) and may detonate both bombs during a single attack action.

*This ability replaces flurry bomb.*

## Reanimator

*The study of life, death, and what lies between is as much a scientific topic as it is a magical one, and the reanimator’s studies blur the lines between. For some it is a dedication to intellectual study of undeath, for others the pursuit of a more unsavory immortality, and for an unsavory few merely an appreciation for the unique manner in which zombies rip skulls open to devour the brains within. Whatever their reasons, reanimators have a deep understanding of undeath in all its facets.*

**Biological Concentration:** While a reanimator’s heavy focus on biology gives the reanimator plenty of options for a discipline, it leaves little room for engineering. A reanimator cannot select the tinker discipline.

*This alters alchemical discipline.*

**Zombie Virus:** At 1st level, a reanimator’s primary source of undead is through a virus. By spending 1 point of quintessence and 10 minutes, the reanimator can create a number of viral agents equal to the reanimators Intelligence modifier. The reanimator can use a viral agent as though it were an injury poison and is immune to its effects. A creature that is exposed to a viral agent must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10+ 1/2 the reanimator’s class level + the reanimator’s Intelligence modifier). On a failed save, the target is afflicted with zombie virus.

*Zombie Virus:* injury; save special (see above); onset immediate; frequency 1/hour; effect 1d3 Con damage; cure 1 save; special a creature who dies while afflicted with the zombie virus immediately rises as a zombie under the control of the reanimator who created the zombie virus. After 1 minute, this zombie collapses, inert, but may later be reanimated by other means.

At 5th level the Constitution damage form zombie virus increases to 1d6; at 9th level, the frequency increases to 1/ minute; at 13th it also deals 1d6 Cha damage; and at 17th the frequency increases to 1/round.

*This replaces bomb.*

**Alchemical Necromancy:** At 6th level, a reanimator gains the alchemical zombie discovery as a bonus discovery. The reanimator can use this ability to create skeletons as well as zombies and can give their skeletons the bloody template or their zombies the fast template by using an additional 50 gp worth of alchemical reagents when creating them.

*This replaces multi bomb.*

**Improved Alchemical Necromancy:** At 11th level, a reanimator unlocks the secrets of greater undead creation. When creating an undead with alchemical necromancy, the reanimator can spend an additional 100 gp in alchemical reagents per HD of the corpse being animated to create a more powerful undead as though casting create undead, using their alchemist level as their caster level to determine what they can create. Undead created by this effect are under the reanimator’s control as normal but are treated as 1.5x their HD for the purpose of determining how many undead the reanimator can control at once.

*This replaces empowered bomb.*

**Viral Carrier:** At 15th level, when a reanimator creates an undead creature, they can make that creature a carrier for their zombie virus. That creature inflicts the zombie virus with its natural attacks.

*This replaces flurry bomb.*

**Virulent Contagion:** At 19th level, the reanimator cultivates a strain of the zombie virus capable of spreading from victim to victim. Any zombie created by the zombie virus ability can spread the virus as if they were a carrier.

*This replaces ultimate bomb.*

**Zombie Plague:** At 20th level, zombies created by a reanimator’s zombie plague no longer fall inert after one minute

*This replaces grand discovery.*

*With the vast quantities of alchemical items that most alchemists have available to them, it’s no wonder that some decide to go into business for themselves. They gain the Renown [vigilante social talent](../vigilante.md#social-talent), treating their red-headed merchant level as their vigilante level for this ability. This community can be an entire settlement or portions of a settlement (such as a particular district or neighborhood), depending on the population as allowed by the renown social talent. For the purposes of this class feature, the red-headed merchant does not have a vigilante identity and is always considered to be in their social identity. In addition, the red-headed merchant gains Bluff, Diplomacy, and Sense Motive as class skills and can attempt checks with these skills using their Intelligence modifier instead of their Charisma or Wisdom modifier.

The red-headed merchant can also select the following social talents in place of a discovery, treating their red-headed merchant level as their vigilante level for the purpose of meeting prerequisites and their effects: Celebrity Discount, Celebrity Perks, Feign Innocence, Gossip Collector, Great Renown, Incredible Renown, Instant Recognition, Loyal Aid, Safe House, Social Grace, Triumphant Return, or Well-Known Expert. The redheaded merchant can also choose to gain an additional identity in place of a discovery, treating it as another social identity.

*This ability replaces poison resistance +2 and +6.*

**Item Crafter (Ex):** At 3rd level, a red-headed merchant gains the [Craft Wondrous Item](../../../rules/feats/craft-wondrous-item.md) feat and is always treated as though they knew a spell for the purpose of being able to construct an item with it.

*This ability replaces swift alchemy.*

**Efficient Construction (Ex):** At 5th level, a red-headed merchant reduces the cost of making a magical or alchemical item by 5% (this stacks with other such reductions). At 8th level, this increases to 10%.

*This ability replaces poison resistance +4 and +8.*

**Incredible Efficiency (Ex):** At 10th level, a red-headed merchant can create alchemical items, potions, and wondrous items in 1/2 the normal amount of time and gains a +1 circumstance bonus to their attack rolls while using a weapon they personally created or enhanced and a +1 dodge bonus to armor class when wearing armor they personally created or enhanced.

*This ability replaces poison immunity*

## Teratogenic Beastmaster

*Not all with animal companions are shining knights or nature lovers. Some are experimentalists whose companions are half pet, half test subject. The teratogenic beastmaster gains an animal companion and the ability to produce mutagens that can affect both of them at the expense of some of their own combat prowess.*

**Base Attack Bonus:** A teratogenic beastmaster gains a base attack bonus equal to a sorcerer of their class level.

*This modifies base attack bonus.*

**Hit Dice:** A teratogenic beastmaster uses d6 for their Hit Die.

**Class Skills:** The teratogenic beastmaster gains [Handle Animal](../../../rules/skills/handle-animal.md) and Ride as class skills.

*This modifies class skills, but may be combined with other archetypes that add new class skills.*

**Animal Companion (Ex):** At 1st level, a teratogenic beastmaster forms a bond with an animal companion. A teratogenic beastmaster may begin play with any of the animals available to a druid. This animal is a loyal companion that accompanies the teratogenic beastmaster on their adventures. This ability functions like the druid animal companion ability (which is part of the nature bond class feature). The teratogenic beastmaster’s effective druid level is equal to their alchemist level. If a character receives an animal companion from more than one source, their effective druid levels stack for the purposes of determining the companion’s statistics and abilities.

If a teratogenic beastmaster releases their companion from service or their animal companion perishes, they may gain a new one by performing a ceremony requiring 24 uninterrupted hours of alchemical dabbling, producing potent pheromones to attract the new companion in the environment where it typically lives.

**Teratogen:** The teratogenic beastmaster must select the mutagenic discipline at 1st level. The mutagens they create can affect their animal companion in exactly the same way they affect their animal companion, including special effects of [evolution] discoveries. Applying a mutagen to their animal companion companion is a standard action and requires the teratogenic beastmaster to be adjacent to it.

*This modifies alchemical discipline. A teratogenic beastmaster cannot also gain an automaton in any way.*

**Bomb-Proof Companion (Ex):** At 2nd level, a teratogenic beastmaster’s animal companion gains a +2 bonus on all saving throws against fear effects and the DC to intimidate it increases by 2. This bonus and increase to DC increase to +4 at 5th level, and then again to +6 at 8th level. At 10th level, the teratogenic beastmaster’s animal companion becomes completely immune to fear effects.

*This replaces poison resistance.*

**Monstrous Link (Ex):** At 5th level, in any round where the teratogenic beastmaster applies a mutagen to their animal companion, they may spend a swift action to allow the animal companion to immediately take a move action to move, even if it isn’t the animal companion’s turn.

*This ability replaces the discipline breakthrough gained at 5th level.*

**Extended Mutagen:** At 11th level, the teratogenic beastmaster can spend 1 additional point of quintessence when creating a mutagen to cause its effects to last for 1 hour per alchemist level. This mutagen can only be applied to their animal companion. In addition, the mutagenic alchemist’s animal companion may spend a full-round action to suppress the effects of their mutagen. While suppressed, the animal companion can spend an immediate action to regain the benefits of their mutagen. Both of these actions requires the teratogenic beastmaster to command their animal companion, as though it were a trick. (The animal companion need not spend a trick known to do this.)

*This ability replaces the discipline breakthrough gained at 11th level.*

**Anomalous Companion:** At 17th level, the teratogenic beastmaster’s animal companion is considered to permanently be under the effect of one of its master’s mutagens. As a standard action the teratogenic beastmaster may touch their animal companion and spend one point of quintessence to change the [evolution] discoveries the animal companion benefits from and which of their physical ability scores gains a +8 bonus, which gains a +6 bonus, and which gains a +4 bonus. This activates monstrous link class feature as if the teratogenic beastmaster were applying a mutagen to their animal companion. In addition, the teratogenic beastmaster may now benefit from mutagens created with the extended mutagen class feature, though it requires 1 minute for them to suppress the effect and a full-round action to regain it. The animal companion may still suppress and regain the benefits of their mutagen as normal.

*This ability replaces the discipline breakthrough gained at 17th level.*

**Fortified Companion:** The teratogenic beastmaster does not gain the benefit of the mutagenic discipline defense. Their animal companion does instead.

*This modifies discipline defense.*

## Urban Adept

*To the urban adept, alchemy is a tool and only one of many they have available to them. Giving up some facility with alchemy itself, they gain more mundane skills and use quintessence to gain bursts of insight into the foes they track through crowded squares and dirty alleys.*

**Smart and Street-Smart:** The urban adept gains 2 additional skill points each level. In addition, they gain all skills as class skills.

*This modifies the legendary alchemist’s skill points per level and class skills.*

**Alchemical Dilettante (Ex):** an urban adept does not increase the DCs of alchemical items they use, nor may they spend quintessence to increase the damage done by an alchemical item.

*This modifies alchemy.*

**Flash of Inspiration (Ex):** As a free action, the urban adept can expend one point of quintessence to add 1d6 to the result of any skill check, including any on which they take 10 or 20. This choice is made after the check is rolled and before the results are revealed. An urban adept investigator can only use this ability once per check or roll. The urban adept can use this ability on any Craft, Knowledge, [Linguistics](../../../rules/skills/linguistics.md), or [Spellcraft](../../../rules/skills/spellcraft.md) skill checks without expending a point of quintessence, provided they’re trained in the skill.

In addition, at 1st level, and again at each odd level thereafter, the urban adept gains a bonus point of quintessence.

**Tactical Analysis (Ex):** As a move action, an urban adept can target one creature within 60 feet, making a Knowledge check as though they were attempting to identify them. If this check is successful, the urban adept analyzes the creature and grants all allies a +1 circumstance bonus to attack and damage rolls against the analyzed creature for a number of rounds equal to the urban adept’s Intelligence modifier + 1/2 their level (minimum 1). At 5th level and every 4 levels thereafter, this bonus increases by +1 (to a maximum of +5 at 17th level). An ally must be able to either see or hear the urban adept when they analyze a creature to gain this bonus.

The type of knowledge check is dependent on the type of creature being analyzed:

- *Arcana:* constructs, dragons, magical beast

- *Dungeoneering:* aberrations, oozes

- *Local:* humanoids

- *Nature:* animal, fey, monstrous humanoids, plants, vermin

- *Planes:* outsiders

- *Religion:* undead

At 5th level, whenever the urban adept uses their tactical analysis class feature, they can select two creatures to analyze and they can analyze as a move or swift action. At 11th level, they can analyze three creatures and they can analyze as a free action once per round. At 17th level, they can analyze a number of creatures equal to their Intelligence modifier (minimum 4).

*This ability replaces discipline.*

**Specialized Knowledge (Ex):** At 5th level, whenever the urban adept successfully analyzes a creature with their tactical analysis class feature, they can select one of the following benefits, each effect lasting for as long as the creature is analyzed:

- The analyzed creature cannot make a 5-foot step or use the withdraw action while threatened by the urban adept or their allies

- The circumstance bonus to attack and damage rolls provided by their tactical analysis increases by +1 against the analyzed creature

- The urban adept and their allies know the analyzed creature’s exact hit points

- The analyzed creature cannot make attacks of opportunity against the urban adept or their allies

- The urban adept and their allies can ignore an amount of damage reduction the creature possesses equal to the urban adept’s Intelligence modifier (minimum 1)

- The urban adept and their allies gain a +2 circumstance bonus to saving throws against spells, spell-like abilities, supernatural abilities, and extraordinary abilities possessed by the analyzed creature

- The urban adept and their allies gain a +2 circumstance bonus to armor class against attacks made by the analyzed creature

- The analyzed creature takes a -1 penalty on all saving throws against spells, spell-like abilities, supernatural abilities, and extraordinary abilities possessed by the urban adept and their allies

At 11th level and again at 17th, they can select an additional benefit upon successfully analyzing a creature.

*This ability replaces discipline breakthrough.*

**Ratiocination (Ex):** Starting at 9th level an urban adept’s ability to draw conclusions from the smallest of evidence borders on the supernatural. They may spend 1 hour in consideration and reach an answer to a single question, as the spell [*divination*](../../../magic/spells/divination.md) with a caster level equal to their legendary alchemist level. The question they consider must be one they have some small bit of evidence. For example, they could not determine whether a suspect committed a crime unless they examined the crime scene, questioned the suspect, or in some other way procured some slight bit of evidence. The GM is the final arbiter of whether information counts as evidence but should err on the side of allowing it.

At 19th level the urban adept may use this ability at will in only 10 minutes, and once per day may spend 1 hour in consideration, gaining an effect similar to the spell [*contact other plane*](../../../magic/spells/contact-other-plane.md) with a caster level equal to their legendary alchemist level, contacting a greater deity. They have no risk of suffering Intelligence or Charisma damage, but still must have some evidence relating to each question they ask.

Despite replicating a divination spell, this effect is nonmagical and not a divination effect, so is not blocked by mind blank or similar effects.

*This ability replaces discipline defense.*

**Investigator Talents:** The urban adept may select an investigator talent in place of a discovery using their legendary alchemist level as their investigator level and may use quintessence to activate them in place of inspiration. They may not choose a talent that modifies or requires studied combat or studied strike.

## Void Demolitionist

*Masters of explosions, normal alchemist are already prime examples of those who can destroy an object’s structural stability in the blink of an eye, but void demolitionists take it one step further. Not content with simply destroying physical objects, they revel in using unique explosives to also destroy the tenuous bound of reality, leaving the fabric of dimensions in tatters with their specialized quantum charges.*

**Dimensional Charges (Su):** A void demolitionist can not only blow holes in solid objects, but reality itself. As a standard action, a void demolitionist can spend 1 point of quintessence to create and throw up to two quantum charges which have a range increment of 30 feet; quantum charges can only target empty squares which do not contain hazardous terrain. These quantum charges, once hitting their targeted square, leave a rip in the fabric of reality for 1 minute per void demolitionist level. They can also choose to create and throw quantum charges as a full-round action without needing to spend quintessence.

The range increment of these quantum charges increases by 5 feet per void demolitionist level they possess past 1st level. These two-dimensional rips stand 5 feet tall and wide, must be placed vertically upon solid ground, and can be identified by a DC 15 Knowledge (planes) as a dimensional rip. Dimensional rips block line of sight. A rip cannot be created in any space where extra dimensional travel is blocked. A void demolitionist can close one or more rips as a free action. If a rip is closed while a creature is inside of it, they are shunted off to the nearest empty square and take 1d6 damage.

A void demolitionist’s rips are connected only to each other, making them independent of other rips, and if a void demolitionist is ever further than 100 feet. +10 feet per void demolitionist level they possess from a rip, it instantly closes. A Large or smaller creature can step into one rip, exiting on the other side of any rip to which they have line of sight. If they cannot see another rip, the rip from which they exit is decided randomly. If a void demolitionist creates only 1 rip, anything entering it will exit through the same rip. If a void demolitionist’s placement of rips would create an infinite loop, both rips will instantly close after a creature enters the same rip a third time. Objects and spells that enter a rip (including attacks) used by a non-void demolitionist that enter a rip exit through a random rip (or the rip they entered if there is only one rip in existence), while a void demolitionist can choose from which rip they exit.

A void demolitionist can throw their bombs into one rip, causing it to exit from another rip of their choice and treats the second rip as the point of origin for the attack, although the maximum distance traveled cannot exceed that of their bomb’s range increment. For each rip a bomb travels through, increase its range increment by 10 feet, up to a maximum of 10 feet per three void demolitionist levels they possess.

A void demolitionist can maintain a number of rips equal to twice the amount they can create with a single use of this ability. If a void demolitionist attempts to create another rip while they already have their maximum in effect, the oldest rip instantly closes. At 7th level and every four levels thereafter, the void demolitionist can create and throw an additional quantum charge with this ability.

*This ability replaces alchemical discipline.*

**Aerial Rips (Ex):** At 5h level, a void demolitionist can cause target a square no further from the ground than half the maximum distance of the rip from themselves, causing the quantum charge to blow up in the square as normal and create a rip (a 7th level void demolitionist could place a rip 45 foot into the air), although it cannot be placed over hazardous terrain (if the terrain later becomes hazardous, the rip remains).

*This ability replaces alchemical study.*

**Phase Step (Su):** At 9th level, the void demolitionist can teleport up to 30 feet as a move action. At 19th level, this increases to 100 feet.

*This ability replaces discipline defense.*

**Phase Bomb (Ex):** At 11th level, if the alchemist throws a bomb through a rip, its damage die increases to 1d8.

*This ability replaces empowered bomb.*

**Infinite Explosion (Ex):** At 19th level, a void demolitionist can split their assault by using their attacks from parallel dimensions, spending 3 points of quintessence to make an attack action through one of their rips. If they do so, the bomb emerges from each rip except for the one being used to make the attack, dealing damage as an alchemist of half their class level (a 19th level void demolitionist’s bombs would deal 5d6+ Intelligence modifier damage). If the void demolitionist applies a [substance] discovery to this bomb, for every two additional bombs that hit a creature using this ability, the DC of the saving throw associated with this bomb increases by +1.

*This ability replaces ultimate bomb.*

**Dimensional Discoveries**

The void demolitionist can select the following discoveries:

**Corporeal Rip**

**Prerequisite(s):** Void demolitionist 8th.

*Quintessence Cost:* 1.

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist is able to create a dimensional rip inside of a corporeal creature, using it as though it was a dimensional rip of the creature’s size. The creature is allowed a Fortitude save to negate being treated as a rip, and if the void demolitionist chooses to have a bomb exit through a corporeal rip, the creature is made into the target and is the only creature affected by it (it does not deal splash damage), the bomb ignoring the effect of any [shape] discoveries applied to it. If a creature chooses to exit through a corporeal rip, they can decide to deal damage equal to two times the void demolitionist’s class level to the creature (Fortitude for half ), although a creature forced to exit through a rip always deals damage.

**Deceptive Rip**

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist is capable of laying a rip across a solid surface, disguising it as normal terrain. If a creature steps into a deceptive rip, they are transported to another rip of your choice. Creatures exiting from a deceptive rip may choose which space adjacent to the selected exit rip to occupy. The [Perception](../../../rules/skills/perception.md) check to locate this rip is equal to the void demolitionist’s class level + 10.

**Distant Rip**

**Prerequisite(s):** Void demolitionist 6th.

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can be 1,000 feet. +100 feet per void demolitionist level they possess away from a rip before it instantly closes.

**Enlarged Rip**

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can create large rips, large rips are 10 feet tall and wide, and can be traveled through by Huge or smaller creatures, being able to exit out of normal sized rips. At 10th level, they can also create huge rips which are 15 feet tall and wide and can be traveled through by Gargantuan or smaller creatures. At 20th level, they can create gargantuan rips which are 20 feet large and wide and can be traveled through by all creatures (GMs may rule that some creatures or objects are too large to pass through these rips).

**Fast Quantum Charges**

**Prerequisite(s):** Void demolitionist 6th.

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can create and throw quantum charges as a move action. If they instead create and throw quantum charges as a standard action, it does not cost quintessence. At 12th level, the void demolitionist can create and throw quantum charges as a swift action. If they instead create and throw quantum charges as a move action, it does not cost quintessence.

**Lingering Rips**

**Prerequisite(s):** Void demolitionist 6th.

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can choose to have their rips to last for a number of hours equal to their void demolitionist level.

**Mobile Rips**

**Prerequisite(s):** Void demolitionist 6th.

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can move any number of rips they create up to 30 feet as a move action, although two or more rips cannot occupy the same square. As an immediate action, a void demolitionist can spend 1 point of quintessence to move a rip in the pathway of a charging enemy, forcing them to make a Reflex save or be forced to enter the rip, the void demolitionist choosing through which rip they exit.

**Rip Bomb**

**Benefit:** When the alchemist creates a bomb, they can choose for it to create a rip as though it was a quantum charge. Creatures that take a direct hit from a must make a successful Reflex save or be transported to another rip of the void demolition’s choice. If this save is successful, the creature is moved an adjacent space. If this bomb does not land a direct hit against a creature, no rip is formed.

*Special:* The rip from this bomb does not count towards the limit of rips a void demolitionist can have in existence at any time, although only a single rip can be caused by this discovery at a single time. If it is used again, any previously created rip automatically closes, even if a new rip is not formed.

**Universal Discoveries**

At 20th level, a void demolitionist can select one of the following grand discoveries:

**Spatial Collapse**

*Quintessence Cost:* 3

**Benefit:** As a standard action, a void demolitionist can move two or more rips into the same square and collapse them in on each other. This forces all creatures within 15 feet of the square to make a Reflex save or be caught in the blast, disintegrating them instantly as per the spell. Those who make their saving throw instead take 10d6 damage. For each two additional rips used in a spatial collapse beyond the second, increase the DC by 1 and the damage taken on a failed save by 2d6.

**Planar Breach**

*Quintessence Cost:* 1 or 2 (see text)

**Benefit:** The void demolitionist can spend 1 point of quintessence to use plane shift or 2 points of quintessence to use either *gate* (planar travel only) or [*interplanetary teleport*](../../../magic/spells/interplanetary-teleport.md).
