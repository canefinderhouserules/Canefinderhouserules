# Backgrounds and Anti-Backgrounds

Use your starting experience to buy background points.

**Background point:** 125/250 XP (ask the GM, depending on the role).

## Backgrounds

**Money:** 1,000 gp per point. Maximum 6 points. 0 means you start with nothing but your starting clothes.

**Fame:** you are famous, which gives some social advantages decided by the GM according to the situation. 10 is roughly a global superstar.

**Influence:** you are influential in whatever field or with whomever you choose; discuss it with the GM. Maximum 10, roughly equivalent to being a king. 0 means you are the new kid on the scene or have simply never stood out.

**Contacts:** you have one or more powerful contacts; discuss it with the GM. Maximum 10, roughly equivalent to a god casually reaching out to you. Contacts can supply teachers for anything in the experience system that requires one, allies for shady dealings, or merchants of rare goods (firearms). 0 means you have no relevant contacts who give you an advantage in the circles the game moves in (you can of course still have ordinary acquaintances, but nothing advantageous).

**Allies:** 1 = a minor ally of level 1 or 2, as it comes up. 2 = a regular ally of your own experience. More points = more allies. 0 = you play a single character, as is traditional.

## Anti-Backgrounds

Each point gives 250 XP.

**Debt:** 2,500 gp per point. Maximum 4. You owe money to a dangerous group that will try to collect by any means. Debt grants no XP, but it does give you gold to spend.

**Infamy:** maximum 3 points. For example, 1 is a former prisoner, 2 an escaped slave, 3 an exile. People react negatively to your presence or on learning this fact, which will inevitably come out sooner or later for it to matter.

**Enemies:** maximum 4 points. Some people in the world want to see you dead or suffering. One point is a rival character, two a small gang, three an influential character or noble, and four someone of enormous power who would love to kill you or throw you in a dungeon.

**Rivals:** maximum 3 points. The GM spawns rival characters who act as anti-allies, hounding you and making your life difficult. The points are either the number of rivals, or the number of levels by which a single rival outclasses you (your choice).
