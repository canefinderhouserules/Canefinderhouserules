## More Familiars

**Keulia**: This small, frilled lizard covers itself in debris and shiny detritus to blend in with its surroundings. Its master gains a +3 bonus on Disguise checks. (Tiny animal; speed 20 ft., burrow 5 ft.; Str 5, Dex 12, Con 11, Int 1, Wis 12, Cha 7; custom camouflage: after 1 hour adhering debris to its scales, +2 racial bonus on Stealth checks in that environment.)

**Tauhoti**: This fluffy trading squirrel has a round body and long tufts atop its ears, and never forgets a debt or a slight. Its master gains a +3 bonus on one Profession check of her choice, selected when gaining the familiar. (Diminutive animal; speed 20 ft., burrow 5 ft.; Str 1, Dex 16, Con 8, Int 2, Wis 15, Cha 9; excellent memory: +4 bonus on Intelligence checks to remember facts, +2 racial bonus on saves against effects that damage, drain or reduce Intelligence or Wisdom.)

**Ceru**: This pint-sized creature resembles a tiny blue elephant with clawed, spiked feet, tail and ears. A spellcaster of 7th level or higher can bond with a ceru via the Improved Familiar feat. (NG Tiny magical beast; speed 20 ft.; constant *detect magic*, at will *mage hand*; luckbringer: as a standard action, once per day per 4 Hit Dice, grant one creature within 30 feet good or bad luck for 1 round, rolling twice and taking the better or worse result; DC 13 Will negates bad luck.)

**Hellfire Ignis**: This small fey has mottled red skin and smells of brimstone. A corrupted arsonist, it makes an excellent familiar for pyromancers who can keep it in check. (NE Small fey (fire); speed 30 ft.; burn 1d3; at will *flare* and *spark*, 3/day *pyrotechnics*; molten flesh: 3/day hurl a chunk of itself within 20 feet.)

**Mockingfey**: This tiny avian has the head of a humanoid and the body of a brilliantly colored parrot. (CN Tiny fey; speed 20 ft., fly 60 ft.; at will *daze*; mock: as a standard action, take on the appearance of any creature it can see, as *veil*.)

**Smokeshade**: This tube of roiling mist writhes through the air. A spellcaster of at least 7th level with an alignment within one step of neutral evil who has the Improved Familiar feat can select a smokeshade as a familiar. (NE Tiny aberration (incorporeal); fly 60 ft.; tendril 2d6 negative energy plus cloying shade; hide in plain sight.)
