# Spell Changes

!!! warning "House rules"
    Changes to existing spells, followed by new spells. Spells not listed here work as written.

## Changed Spells

**Haste:** if you have 1 or 2 attacks, it grants the extra attack. If you have 3 or more attacks, it increases the attack bonus and grants the same bonus to damage, based on your number of attacks (+3 attack and damage with 3 attacks, +4 with 4 attacks, and so on). It also lets you cast one spell of 2 levels lower as if it were quickened (under the same rules as Quicken Spell), once per turn, and it does not stack with quicken. It is now a single-target 3rd-level spell; the normal 30-foot-area version, with its usual rules, is a 4th-level spell.

**Instant Enemy:** now lasts 1 round/level. It is a 1st-level spell exclusive to rangers, and a 2nd-level spell for other classes with 6th-level spellbooks that somehow steal favored enemy.

**Shield:** +3 instead of +4. Lasts 10 minutes/level instead of 1.

**Bless:** the +1 is now a divine bonus, which is much rarer and easier to stack with other classes.

**Charm Person:** no longer requires a verbal component, and does not work on people who see you as an enemy. Much easier to use outside combat, and less ridiculous to use on enemies you have just brutally killed.

**Call Lightning / Call Lightning Storm:** gain 1 extra die of damage per 2 caster levels above 5th/10th, to a maximum of 6d6/9d6.

**Magic Missile:** now deals 1d4+2.

**Barkskin:** also grants DR/magic equal to twice the AC bonus it gives, so that it has real value.

**Lead Blades:** now available to the cleric, oracle, warpriest and druid. Clerics and oracles get it as a touch spell, but at 2nd level.

**Divine Favor:** can now be cast on creatures within the alignment range your deity (or whoever grants your powers) accepts. In that case it is a touch spell.

**Effortless Armor:** now 1st level, and lasts 1 hour/level.

**Magic Weapon / Greater Magic Weapon:** now an area effect if you are a divine caster. Uses the same range rules as Haste.

**Save-or-die spells:** the target now rolls the saving throw twice and keeps the higher result. If something lets you make the enemy roll twice and take the worse, it rolls only once instead. This includes all direct death spells.

**Align Weapon:** now an area effect.

**Divine Trident / Flame Blade:** last 1 hour/level. Both can be used with Weapon Finesse. They gain +1 damage per caster level instead of per 2 levels (the maximum is still 10).

**Gravity Bow:** divine classes can now cast it too.

**Cure and Inflict spells:** you can cast them as if they were 1 spell level higher, making them ranged touch attacks instead of touch, with Close range (25 ft. + 5 ft. per 2 caster levels). For example, *cure light wounds* becomes a 2nd-level spell that you can cast at range. A prepared caster who cannot cast it spontaneously must prepare it that way in his spellbook (it works as if it had a metamagic feat applied, without the feat cost).

**Fiery Shuriken:** at caster level 6 the shuriken increase to 2d8 each.

**Hedging Weapons:** gain an additional weapon every 4 levels, to a maximum of 4. Throwing them is a touch attack, and can be a full-round action to throw more than one if you can attack more than once (rapid shot, iterative attacks, and so on). They have the returning property, and you lose the bonus only while attacking. Each weapon thrown reduces the duration by 1 minute.

## New Spells

**Divine Bolt** *(level 1: cleric, oracle)*: ranged touch attack. 1d5 positive energy damage per caster level, maximum 5d5. Cannot heal.

**Necrotic Bolt** *(level 1: cleric, oracle)*: ranged touch attack. 1d5 negative energy damage per caster level, maximum 5d5. Cannot heal.

**Divine Smite** *(level 2: cleric, oracle, paladin; level 3: warpriest, inquisitor)*: 1d5 sacred/profane damage per caster level, maximum 10d5. The damage type depends on the source of your power (good = sacred, evil = profane). Medium range. Will save halves. You can split the damage in half to affect two targets at once, as long as they are no more than 30 feet apart.

### Summon Monster I–IX (rework)

**Casting Time** 1 round; **Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels); **Effect** one summoned creature; **Duration** 1 round/level (D); **Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

Summons one or more creatures. There is no longer a fixed list: you can choose any monster from the Bestiary (if it has a proper name, the GM is very likely to trick you, so be careful). The creature has its own desires and ambitions, so be careful with that too (though it is predisposed to obey you, because you can banish it with a thought).

The creature's CR equals the spell's level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. For example, a 2nd-level character using *summon monster I* can summon one CR 2 creature, or two CR 1 creatures.

More specific spells, or feats that expand the list in some way, will most likely be treated as +1 CR for that type of creature and –1 CR for everything else (for example, *summon nature's ally* gives +1 CR if the creature is an animal and –1 CR for all others; a spell that gives you devils gives +1 CR for devils and –1 CR for all others).

The Superior Summoning feat increases the maximum CR you can summon by 1.

### Mark and Recall

**School** conjuration (teleportation); **Level** shaman 2, druid 3, unchained summoner 2, witch 3; **Domain** travel 3; **Elemental School** void 3
**Casting Time** 1 round; **Components** V, S
**Range** personal; **Target** you; **Duration** instantaneous; **Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

Cast it once to mark a place; cast it a second time to return to the marked place. It has a maximum distance of 100 miles per caster level. The mark lasts forever or until you place it somewhere else, but it can be detected with *detect magic* and altered or erased.
