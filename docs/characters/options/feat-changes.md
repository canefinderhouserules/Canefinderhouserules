# Feat Changes

!!! warning "House rules"
    A quick summary of this table’s feat changes. Each feat’s own page already shows the changed rule, with the book version in a closed “Original rule” box. A feat not listed here works as written in the book.

## Combat Feats

**[Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md):** not a feat. It is a feature available to any character with BAB +1 and Str 13.

**[Piranha Strike](../../rules/feats/piranha-strike.md):** not a feat. It is a feature available to any character with BAB +1, Dex 13 and Weapon Finesse.

**[Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md):** not a feat. It is a feature available to any character with BAB +1 and Dex 13.

**[Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md):** choose one of two options: ×3 the weapon damage dice, or ×2 the static damage bonuses. Neither extra damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

**[Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md):** ×4 the weapon damage dice, or ×3 the static damage bonuses.

**[Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md):** ×5 the weapon damage dice, or ×4 the static damage bonuses.

**[Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md)** *(total rework; prerequisites: Str 13, BAB +1)*: as a standard action (1 AP), you make a single melee attack against 2 adjacent squares (adjacent to you and to each other), attacking whatever is in them with that one attack. With a two-handed weapon you affect 3 adjacent squares. With a reach weapon you affect 2 or 3 adjacent squares and the squares directly behind them. You can use Cleave as a full-round action (3 AP), in which case instead of an attack it becomes a Reflex save for half, with a DC of 10 + half your level + your Strength modifier. On a turn in which you use either method of Cleave, you take –2 AC.

**[Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md)** *(rework; prerequisites: Cleave, BAB +4)*: using Cleave no longer costs you 2 AC.

**[Cleaving Finish](../../rules/feats/cleaving-finish.md)** *(rework; prerequisites: Cleave, BAB +6)*: landing the final melee blow on a target with Cleave grants you another standard action (1 AP), which must be used strictly for a nonmagical offensive action, once per round.

**[Improved Cleaving Finish](../../rules/feats/improved-cleaving-finish.md)** *(rework; prerequisites: Cleaving Finish, BAB +8)*: twice per round.

**[Cleave Through](../../rules/feats/cleave-through.md)** *(rework; prerequisites: Cleaving Finish, BAB +6)*: when you trigger the effects of Cleaving Finish, you can take a 5-foot move before making the new attack.

**[Cleaver](../../rules/feats/goblin-cleaver.md)** *(Goblin Cleaver line rework; prerequisites: Cleave, BAB +4)*: one of the squares you choose to attack may be non-adjacent to the others you choose, as long as it is no more than 1 square away from them. You gain an extra +2 on attack rolls if you have a racial hatred ability against a specific race matching the race you attack with Cleave (for example, +2 against orcs if you have the dwarven hatred ability against orcs).

**[Weapon Focus](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md):** applies to a weapon group instead of a single weapon, and grants +1 BAB instead of +1 to attack rolls. It counts for prerequisites of strictly offensive feats and for gaining more attacks, but if you are not using that weapon group you cannot use those feats or that extra attack.

**[Skill Focus](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md):** the bonus it grants also counts as skill ranks for prerequisites, and lets you take 10 with the chosen skill even in stressful or combat situations.

**[Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md):** includes Dexterity to damage. Add Agile Maneuvers (use combat maneuvers with Dexterity instead of Strength).

**[Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md):** it is no longer a prerequisite for maneuver feats or other clearly offensive feats, and those feats also lose its Int 13 requirement.

**[Whirlwind Attack](../../rules/feats/whirlwind-attack.md):** if made with a two-handed weapon, you gain reach while doing so (so with a reach weapon, a full 15 feet).

**[Dodge and Mobility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md):** merged into one feat ("Dodgility"). Dodge grants the effects of Mobility.

**[Precise Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md):** merged with Point-Blank Shot.

**[Toughness, Endurance and Diehard](../../rules/feats/toughness.md):** merged into a single feat (Toughness's +1 hit point per level, plus the effects of Endurance and Diehard).

**[Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md):** prerequisite is now Dex 14. Otherwise unchanged.

**[Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md):** prerequisites: Dex 15, Two-Weapon Fighting, BAB +6. In addition to the standard single extra attack you get with an off-hand weapon, you get a second attack with it, at a –5 penalty. Once your BAB reaches +11, you also gain a third attack with your off-hand weapon, at a –10 penalty.

**[Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md):** is now a move action (1 AP).

**[Arcane Strike](../../rules/feats/arcane-strike.md):** is now a free action.

**[Throw Anything and Catch Off-Guard](../../rules/feats/throw-anything.md):** merged into one feat.

**[Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md):** +4 to initiative, and you can take 10 on initiative checks.

**[Improved Coup de Grace](../../rules/feats/improved-coup-de-grace.md):** no longer requires [Heal](../../rules/skills/heal.md) or Combat Expertise; it now requires level 9. This feat is what grants the death save: the base coup de grace rule has no death save, so you need this feat to have one.

**[Deft Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/deft-maneuvers.md)** *(prerequisite: Int 10)*: you do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a trip, disarm, dirty trick, feint, reposition, or steal combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks with these combat maneuvers. It is now a prerequisite for the relevant greater combat maneuver feats.

**[Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md)** *(prerequisites: Str 13, BAB +1)*: you do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a grapple, bull rush, drag, overrun, or sunder combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks with these combat maneuvers. It is now a prerequisite for the relevant greater combat maneuver feats.

**[Powerful Nature](../../rules/feats/powerful-nature.md)** *(prerequisite: BAB +6)*: if you change shape or your natural form has only one natural attack, you gain iterative attacks with that single natural attack every 5 points of BAB, like any martial character.

## New Feats

**[Pinpoint Attack](../../rules/feats/pinpoint-attack.md)** *(prerequisite: BAB +1; free feat, like Power Attack)*: you may attempt a pinpoint attack on a specific part of the enemy's body in place of an attack. It works as a normal attack, requiring an attack roll and dealing the damage due, but with a small bonus or penalty to yourself and sometimes to the enemy if it hits. The negative effects always last 1 round, or 2 on a critical hit.

- Ranged attacks always take a –1 penalty to attack.
- Both the benefits and the penalties increase every 4 points of BAB (like Power Attack).
- If the damage dealt is 50% or more of the enemy's total hit points, double the penalty inflicted.

| Target | Effect |
|---|---|
| Head | –1 attack, +4 to confirm critical hits. |
| Arm | –2 damage; if it hits, –2 to attack with whatever the target holds in that arm, and 5% arcane spell failure. |
| Shield arm | –1 attack; if it hits, –2 to the shield's AC bonus. |
| Legs | –2 damage; if it hits, –2 to Dex/dodge AC. |
| Wings | –2 damage; if it hits, the target has disadvantage on Fly (rolled each time it is hit) and a –4 penalty on Fly checks. |

**[Pinpoint Mastery](../../rules/feats/pinpoint-mastery.md)** *(prerequisite: BAB +4)*: you count as 4 BAB higher, only for the positive effects of pinpoint attack (or the negative effects on the enemy). Removes the –1 attack on ranged attacks. Increases the duration of the penalties by 1 (so they last 2 rounds).

## Obedience

[Deific](../../rules/feats/deific-obedience.md), [demonic](../../rules/feats/demonic-obedience.md), [fiendish](../../rules/feats/fiendish-obedience.md), [fey](../../rules/feats/fey-obedience.md), [monitor](../../rules/feats/monitor-obedience.md) or any other Obedience feat now grants its boons at levels 6, 10 and 14 (see [Deific Obediences](deific-obediences.md), [Celestial Obediences](celestial-obediences.md), [Demonic Obediences](demonic-obediences.md), [Fiendish Obediences](fiendish-obediences.md), [Fey Obediences](fey-obediences.md) and [Monitor Obediences](monitor-obediences.md)). All of them work as one feat: you simply choose which of your patron’s boon lists you want (Exalted, Sentinel, Evangelist, Demoniac, Mystery Cultist or any other it offers), but the decision is absolute: once made, it cannot be changed.

## [Eclectic Training](../../rules/feats/eclectic-training.md)

**Prerequisites:** ability to cast spells, Knowledge (arcana or religion) 3 ranks, Spellcraft 3 ranks.

**Benefit:** choose one spellcasting class you have at least one level in. You increase your effective caster level in that class (including the number of spells you know and can cast per day) by one, to a maximum caster level equal to your total Hit Dice.

**Extra:** may not be used for min-maxing; use it only to make prestige classes or less efficient combinations easier to play.

## Metamagic and Teamwork Feat Categories

Metamagic feats and teamwork feats are sorted into categories (Powerful, Common and Trait). These feats grant picks from a category:

- **Powerful Metamagic:** gives you 1 metamagic feat of the Powerful category.
- **Common Metamagic:** gives you 2 metamagic feats of the Common category.
- **Powerful Teamwork Feat:** gives you 1 teamwork feat of the Powerful category.
- **Common Teamwork Feat:** gives you 2 teamwork feats of the Common category.

See [Metamagic Feats](metamagic.md) and [Teamwork Feats](teamwork-feats.md).

## Other Feats

**[Angel Wings](../../rules/feats/angel-wings.md):** no longer requires Angelic Blood; the two are merged. It requires only level 9 and being an aasimar.

**[Shaping Focus](../../rules/feats/shaping-focus.md):** requires level 4 of Legendary Shifter to meet this feat's prerequisite.

**[Animal Ally](../../rules/feats/animal-ally.md):** no longer requires Nature Soul. Ask the GM if you want a pet that is not on that list.

**[Fireball Tricks](../../magic/mastering-magic/spell-magic-tricks.md#fireball-tricks):** you can no longer choose more than 1 trick at a time.

**[Superior Summoning](../../rules/feats/superior-summoning.md)** *(rework of Summon Monster)*: increases the maximum CR you can summon by 1.

**Weapon Trick (Dual Strike):** clearly does not require Vital Strike; it exists to give a pseudo-pounce when dual wielding.

**Shikigami Style:** the first and second styles grant +1 size, but the third no longer does.

**[Improvised Weapon Mastery](../../rules/feats/improvised-weapon-mastery.md):** stacks with Shikigami Style.

**Surprise Weapon** *(trait)*: the +2 becomes +1 to attack with improvised weapons.

**Magical Lineage:** no longer exists.
