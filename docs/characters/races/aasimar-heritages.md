# Aasimar Heritages

Most aasimars do not know exactly where their celestial powers came from, and the similar qualities of many such celestially touched beings hint at a relatively indistinct or all-encompassing heavenly force in their lineage. Some, however, possess more unique traits and abilities inherited from their supernal forebears, attributes that hint at the precise type of celestial being that affected their ancestors. Any good outsider can introduce its blessing into a humanoid bloodline, and many families forget or never learn the true nature of the outsider that touched them.

Players may choose one of the following six heritages for their aasimar characters in place of the traditional [aasimar](aasimars.md) racial features. Each heritage presents new ability modifiers, a spell-like ability, and skill modifiers that replace the default aasimar ability modifiers, *daylight* spell-like ability, and skilled racial trait, as well as a pair of custom race traits. A heritage is not a straitjacket: variation exists within heritages as much as between them, and many aasimars go through their lives without knowing what their heritage truly is. If you choose a specific heritage, work with your GM to ensure that your character's appearance reflects that bloodline.

All other aasimar racial traits (native outsider, darkvision, celestial resistance, languages, and so on) are unchanged. Spell-like abilities from a heritage are usable once per day with a caster level equal to the aasimar's character level.

## Agathion-Blooded (Idyllkin)

Idyllkin possess bestial aspects and calm dispositions, and often act as peaceful intermediaries between lawful and chaotic agents of good.

- **Ancestry**: Agathion. **Typical Alignment**: NG.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Constitution, +2 Charisma.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Handle Animal and Survival checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *summon nature's ally II* 1/day.

Like their agathion ancestors, idyllkin seek to spread good in its purest form, unconcerned with the trivialities of chaos and law. They often travel from place to place to spread their righteous wisdom, and recruit both intelligent creatures and animals to their cause. Idyllkin often possess bestial qualities such as dragon scales, fish scales, fur, manes, or talons; slit pupils, pronounced canines, and furry ears are all common indicators of an agathion background. Many feel called to walk the path of the druid. They are usually found in wild reaches, prefer villages to cities, and often work as hunters, herbalists, or herders.

### Idyllkin Traits

**Enlightened Warrior**: You have always found it easy to maintain inner peace and enlightenment that translate well to the battlefield. You may take levels in monk even while maintaining a neutral or neutral good alignment.

**Speech of the Wilds**: Like your agathion ancestor, you have a knack for communicating with other creatures, and you are adept at breaking down language barriers. You can speak one extra language.

## Angel-Blooded (Angelkin)

Angelkin possess exceptional beauty, and they serve as mortal exemplars of good and light regardless of the myriad forms they may take.

- **Ancestry**: Angel. **Typical Alignment**: Any good.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Strength, +2 Charisma.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Heal and Knowledge (planes) checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *alter self* 1/day.

Angel-blooded aasimars follow the righteous paths their celestial ancestors walked, and are as varied as angels in how they go about their virtuous duties, split evenly among those who embrace law, neutrality, or chaos. Of all the aasimars, angelkin are the type who perhaps most often clash with tieflings: they have difficulty embracing the idea of harmony with their corrupt cousins, and most see tieflings as embodiments of evil that can never be trusted. Many feel called to the life of a crusader or paladin.

### Angelkin Traits

**Celestial Tracker**: You share the same talent for tracking evildoers that your angelic ancestor possessed. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Survival checks made to follow tracks. In addition, you treat trails as being up to 24 hours fresher than they actually are when using the Survival skill to follow tracks.

**Planetar's Visions**: Ever since you were a child, you've experienced vivid dreams of cutting down hordes of shrieking, twisted demons. Whenever you succeed at a critical hit with a melee weapon against an evil outsider, you ignore an amount of the outsider's damage reduction (if any) equal to the critical multiplier of your weapon (this cannot reduce a creature's damage reduction to below 0).

## Archon-Blooded (Lawbringers)

Lawbringers are champions of justice and of doling out punishment to the wicked, and often believe chaos is the cause of all mortal suffering.

- **Ancestry**: Archon. **Typical Alignment**: LG.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Dexterity, +2 Charisma.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Intimidate and Sense Motive checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *continual flame* 1/day.

Lawbringers reflect the patience of their archon ancestors in their daily lives; they understand the need for rules and routines, and use their inherent discipline to train in their chosen field as they seek to do good. They develop their talents faster than most of their peers and show exceptional skill at whatever they focus their attention on. They feel most comfortable in regions where a clear hierarchy and system of justice exists, and tyrannical regimes that keep their people in line through repression infuriate them; they seek the most legitimate and least destructive ways to neutralize such governments.

### Lawbringer Traits

**Good Influence**: You possess an innate ability to motivate and inspire others to do the right thing. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Diplomacy checks when attempting to persuade a nongood creature to make a decision that benefits the cause of good, and when persuading a nonlawful creature to make a decision in line with the laws of the region. These bonuses stack if you attempt to persuade a nongood, nonlawful creature to take a lawful good action.

**Lantern Spirit**: Your celestial ancestor was a lantern archon for centuries before ascending to a higher rank. You feel a strange sense of nostalgia whenever you use your *continual flame* spell-like ability, and can use it as a move action instead of a standard action.

## Azata-Blooded (Musetouched)

The musetouched epitomize freedom and joy, and travel the world to liberate less fortunate individuals.

- **Ancestry**: Azata. **Typical Alignment**: CG.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy and Perform checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *glitterdust* 1/day.

The capricious aasimars known as musetouched are the most likely of their kind to be found adventuring throughout lands both civilized and remote. Imbued with an undeniable spirit of wanderlust, they feel the constant urge to explore new places, meet new people, and ferret out evil and corruption wherever it lurks. Musetouched tend to be among the most beautiful of aasimars by most humans' standards, with features such as long, thick hair of a seemingly impossible hue and limpid, jewel-toned eyes. They are commonly possessed of innate musical talent, and many train as bards while they wander.

### Musetouched Traits

**Bralani's Step**: Your ancestor was a bralani, an azata known for its untamed speed in battle. When in combat, you feel a fierce surge of energy through your limbs. Once per day, you may move an additional 5 feet as part of a move action.

**Lillend's Harp**: Your ancestor was a lillend, a being of exceptional grace and singular musical talent. You gain a +1 trait bonus on Perform (string) checks. This bonus increases to +2 when you make a Perform check as part of a bardic performance.

## Garuda-Blooded (Plumekith)

Their shimmering avian features make plumekith instantly recognizable. Though they can act rashly, plumekith never shirk their duty after making a commitment.

- **Ancestry**: Garuda. **Typical Alignment**: CG.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Dexterity, +2 Wisdom.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Acrobatics and Fly checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *see invisibility* 1/day.

Garudas are noble but impetuous birdlike celestials, and most garuda-blooded aasimars grow graceful feathers during puberty. The majority have shimmering wings of virtually any shade, ranging from metallic colors to muted hues to pure white, or rarely, glossy black. Plumekith are sometimes born with taloned fingers or toes, and occasionally eyes like those of an eagle. Like their celestial ancestors, they take action at the spur of the moment when their abilities seem needed, rushing into the heat of battle to face evildoers and only stopping to ask questions afterward. Plumekith often have an extreme hatred for nagas and other snakelike creatures, and are drawn to high, mountainous regions.

### Plumekith Traits

**Snake Hater**: You've always loathed serpents and other slithering monsters, and have studied them extensively. You gain a +2 trait bonus on Knowledge (dungeoneering) checks and Knowledge (dungeoneering) is always a class skill for you.

**Toxophilite**: You've inherited some of your celestial ancestor's prowess with the bow, and gain a +2 trait bonus on attack rolls made to confirm critical hits with bows.

## Peri-Blooded (Emberkin)

Masters of fire magic, emberkin feel the dual pull of their peri forebear as well as that of a fallen angel further down their ancestry, and wrestle with their urges to do both good and evil.

- **Ancestry**: Peri. **Typical Alignment**: NG.
- **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Intelligence, +2 Charisma.
- **Skill Modifiers**: +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (planes) and Spellcraft checks.
- **Spell-Like Ability**: *pyrotechnics* 1/day.

Emberkin reveal their divine heritage through their skill with fire-based magic and their desire to repent for others' sins. Many suffer from frequent nightmares and strange impulses to commit spontaneous acts of evil. They typically have igneous features such as wispy auburn hair and bright amber eyes, and a rare few are born with albino skin. Emberkin often feel an insatiable need to continually perform acts of good, though perhaps more so than most of their celestial kith they have a propensity to revolt against their benevolent predisposition and follow a darker path. Such wicked individuals are dangerous, and are both pitied and hunted down by their aasimar brethren.

### Emberkin Traits

**Burnished Skin**: Sometime during your adolescence, you suffered severe burns in a devastating fire. Along with your scars, you developed the uncanny ability to tell what is and isn't real, gaining a +2 trait bonus on saving throws made to disbelieve illusions.

**Pyromancer**: You're especially adept at casting flame-based spells. You gain a +1 trait bonus on damage rolls for any spell you cast with the fire descriptor. Spells that do not deal damage do not benefit from this trait.

## Random Aasimar Features

Dozens of features aasimars might possess. None of these grant any special powers in excess of their usual abilities. Roll d% for each feature, or choose.

**Arms**: appear sculpted from marble; extra long; feathered forearms; scaled forearms; seemingly boneless.

**Build**: always slender; beautifully proportioned; graceful; unusually light; well-muscled.

**Digits**: extra long; metallic nails; odd number; shining talons; unusually colored nails.

**Ears**: catlike; feathered; long-lobed; pivoting; pointed.

**Eyes**: catlike; glowing; iridescent; jewel-like; multicolored.

**Face**: baby-faced; metallic lips; perfectly symmetrical; unearthly beauty; white scar.

**Hair**: animated; feathers; heatless flames; metallic; turns silver in moonlight.

**Hands**: always cool and dry; blackened knuckles; glowing palms; leave contrails; fingerprints look like holy symbols.

**Head**: animal features; bald; draconic features; halo; unusually shaped.

**Legs**: clawed feet; extra long; feathered shins; metallic scaled shins; unnaturally small feet.

**Shadow**: animated; bright; metallic; prismatic; winged.

**Skin**: ashen; feathered; furred; glittering; glowing; iridescent; metallic scales; metallic sheen; prismatic scales; unusual hue.

**Voice**: echoes dramatically; musical; unusually high; unusually low; words you speak aloud seem to be heard mentally.

**Wings**: butterfly; feathered; light; metallic dragon; prismatic.

**Other**: always look clean; always well lit; androgynous; breathing sounds like ocean waves; clothing billows even without wind; covered in freckles; don't sweat; floral breath; fox tail; melodic laugh; multicolored tears; nearby bells ring when you pass by; no body hair; pearlescent teeth; random choral sounds surround you; sacred birthmark; stigmata; sweet scent; sweet taste; trancelike sleep; unicorn horn; unusual footprints; unusual temperature.
