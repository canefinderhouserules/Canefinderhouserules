# Geniekin Heritages

Geniekin trace their lineage to beings from the various Elemental Planes. Though their progenitors are typically genies, this ancestry can derive from any number of elemental creatures. Furthermore, even geniekin whose ancestry includes only genies can manifest their elemental heritage in uncommon ways.

The following heritages represent geniekin variants who have an affinity for different aspects of the elements, with new ability modifiers, spell-like abilities, and elemental affinities that replace the default [ifrit](ifrits.md), [oread](oreads.md), [sylph](sylphs.md), and [undine](undines.md) racial traits. Each affinity functions as and can be replaced as the base race's affinity does, except for the benefits listed.

## Gemsoul (Crystal Oread)

With scintillating personalities to match their captivating appearances, gemsoul oreads command attention—and prefer it that way. They gravitate toward artistic pursuits, most frequently of a performative nature, and typically have dazzling, faceted skin, along with a cluster of crystalline formations instead of hair and pupil-less eyes of vivid colors.

- **Typical Alignment**: N. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Strength, +2 Charisma, –2 Wisdom.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *color spray* 1/day.
- **Crystal Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the deep earth bloodline and spellcasters with the Charm domain.

## Ironsoul (Metal Oread)

Stolid and indomitable, ironsoul oreads undertake great works, both physical and conceptual, with unwavering determination. Possessed of patience and a sense of self-refinement through struggle, they operate best within existing formal structures, and most have some amount of metallic sheen to their skin, in colors ranging from gold and silver to copper-green and dull, oxide gray.

- **Typical Alignment**: LN. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom, –2 Dexterity.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *unerring weapon* 1/day.
- **Metal Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the shaitan bloodline and spellcasters with the Metal subdomain.

## Lavasoul (Magma Ifrit)

Like the primal fires of creation, lavasoul ifrits are both fluid and fixed, alternating between stability and violent alteration. Mutable and intense, they are unafraid to pursue new paradigms when the old ones no longer suit them. Many are dark-skinned but riven with blazing lines, and they tend to embrace body modification.

- **Typical Alignment**: CN. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Constitution, +2 Intelligence, –2 Dexterity.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *burning sands* 1/day.
- **Magma Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the efreeti bloodline and spellcasters with the Ash subdomain.

## Mistsoul (Vapor Undine)

Soothing and insightful, mistsoul undines move through the world with airy resilience and earnest action. With gentle spirits and contemplative minds, they are drawn to the more mystic aspects of their element, and their ephemeral, vaporous natures can allow them to lull others into a dreamlike state, or facilitate a peaceful transition into the next life.

- **Typical Alignment**: NG. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Constitution, +2 Wisdom, –2 Intelligence.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *obscuring mist* 1/day.
- **Mist Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the dreamspun bloodline and spellcasters with the Repose domain.

## Rimesoul (Frost Undine)

Rimesoul undines are distant and imperious, aloof in their intellect and hesitant to change their ways. Like their ice-coated ancestors, they represent the solidification of change, with precise, ordered minds that take well to individual pursuits. They are frequently pale, sometimes with translucent or blue-tinged skin.

- **Typical Alignment**: LN. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Dexterity, +2 Intelligence, –2 Charisma.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *chill touch* 1/day.
- **Ice Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the boreal bloodline and spellcasters with the Ice subdomain.

## Smokesoul (Fume Sylph)

Veiled and surreptitious, smokesoul sylphs are natural deceivers, always concealing their true intentions. They are experts at concealing both their bodies and motivations, and at making the insubstantial seem as real as any physical object. With few distinguishing features save ashen skin and dark hair, smokesouls can be mistaken for any number of other races.

- **Typical Alignment**: CE. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Dexterity, +2 Charisma, –2 Constitution.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *blurred movement* 1/day.
- **Smoke Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the shadow bloodline and spellcasters with the Smoke subdomain.

## Stormsoul (Lightning Sylph)

With an abundance of confidence and a shortage of patience, stormsoul sylphs are personalities who cannot be ignored. Unafraid to speak their minds, they are both direct and unsubtle, and they make fierce allies. A stormsoul's hair always seems to move, even when there is no breeze, and their eyes are the color of stormy water or lightning-lit skies.

- **Typical Alignment**: CN. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Dexterity, +2 Charisma, –2 Wisdom.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *shocking grasp* 1/day.
- **Lightning Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the stormborn bloodline and spellcasters with the Weather domain.

## Sunsoul (Solar Ifrit)

Strong in character and in body, sunsoul ifrits are incurably optimistic agents of positive change. With fire comes light, and the brightness of the sunsouls comes both from their affinity for positive energy and their own luminous attitudes. Most have fair hair, often nearly white, and eyes that glow with a twinkle all their own.

- **Typical Alignment**: LG. **Ability Modifiers**: +2 Strength, +2 Charisma, –2 Wisdom.
- **Alternate Spell-Like Ability**: *sun metal* 1/day.
- **Solar Affinity**: This racial trait benefits sorcerers with the celestial bloodline and spellcasters with the Sun domain.
