# Sahuagin

Sahuagin are cruel, vicious piscine predators who see all other creatures as either competitors or prey. The sole exception is the shark: sahuagin are rarely found without semidomesticated sharks nearby, and the two hunt ruthlessly alongside each other. An individual sahuagin might decide to pursue glory on her own, perhaps seeking a powerful artifact or a richer hunting ground. Sahuagin should be played only with your GM's permission.

## Sahuagin Racial Traits (22 RP)

Sahuagin normally have racial Hit Dice, skills, and other abilities, but PCs calculate these benefits based solely on their class. Statistics for PCs using these rules may not match the monster's statistics exactly.

**+2 Strength, +2 Wisdom, –2 Charisma**: While sahuagin are powerful and cunning, they are repulsive creatures.

**Monstrous Humanoid**: Sahuagin are monstrous humanoids with the aquatic subtype.

**Medium**: Sahuagin are Medium creatures and have no bonuses or penalties due to their size.

**Blindsense**: Sahuagin have blindsense with a range of 30 feet.

**Darkvision**: Sahuagin have darkvision with a range of 60 feet.

**Light Blindness**: Sahuagin have the light blindness trait.

**Natural Armor**: Sahuagin have a +3 natural armor bonus.

**Normal Speed**: Sahuagin have a base speed of 30 feet.

**Fast Swimmer (5 RP)**: Sahuagin have a swim speed of 60 feet.

**Bite**: Sahuagin have a natural bite attack that deals 1d4 points of damage. This bite is a primary attack, or a secondary attack if the creature is wielding manufactured weapons.

**Claws**: Sahuagin have two claw attacks that deal 1d4 points of damage. These are primary natural attacks.

**Blood Frenzy**: Once per day, whenever a sahuagin takes damage, it can fly into a frenzy for 1 minute, gaining a +2 racial bonus to Constitution and Strength but a –2 penalty to AC.

**Speak with Sharks (1 RP)**: Sahuagin can communicate telepathically with sharks to a distance of 100 feet. This communication is limited to simple concepts, such as "attack," "come," and "defend."

**Languages**: Sahuagin begin play speaking Aquan and Common. Sahuagin with high Intelligence scores can choose from the following: Aboleth, Abyssal, Aklo, Draconic, Giant, Goblin, and Infernal.

## Alternate Racial Traits

The following racial traits may be selected instead of existing sahuagin racial traits, typically by sahuagin who live peacefully alongside merfolk and other races. Consult your GM before selecting any of these options.

**Sahuagin Song**: Sahuagin with this racial trait have learned the merfolk love of song. They gain a +2 racial bonus on Perform (sing) checks and a +1 racial bonus on saves against language-dependent effects. This replaces speak with sharks.

**Stoic Defender**: Sahuagin who have learned to control their aggression stand as reliable guards of the city they are proud to call home. Once per day when a sahuagin with this racial trait takes damage, he can focus on remaining steadfast. For 1 minute, the sahuagin gains a +2 racial bonus to AC and a +2 racial bonus on Will saves. This replaces blood frenzy.
