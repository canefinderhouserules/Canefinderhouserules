# Sword Duelist

Sword duelists follow no singular path to dueling mastery—some of those who take up the blade only dabble in its use, while others explore swordplay with the dueling weapon in unconventional ways. Those who seek to most closely emulate the traditions of a famed school of dueling usually do so by taking levels in this prestige class. A sword duelist traveling the back roads, confronting bandits and defeating braggarts by challenging them to single combat, has very likely pursued this path.

Due to this prestige class’s emphasis on mastering numerous feats, sword duelists often have levels in the fighter class. Rangers, rogues, slayers, and swashbucklers also seek to master this signature swordfighting art, even though, in most cases, they must wait longer to qualify for the class than fighters. Many of a sword duelist’s abilities rely or build on the duel rules in *Ultimate Combat*. This prestige class also works well in conjunction with the Sword Duelist Defender archetype, though neither requires the other.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a sword duelist, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Feats**: [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus (heavy blades).

**Skills**: Acrobatics 2 ranks, Intimidate 2 ranks, Knowledge (nobility) 2 ranks, Sense Motive 2 ranks.

??? note "Original requirements"

    **Feats**: Dazzling Display, Exotic Weapon Proficiency ([dueling sword](../../equipment/weapons-exotic.md#exotic-one-handed-melee-weapons)), Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (dueling sword).

    **Skills**: Acrobatics 3 ranks, Intimidate 5 ranks, Knowledge (nobility) 3 ranks, Sense Motive 3 ranks.

### Class Skills

The sword duelist's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Acrobatics (Dex), Bluff (Cha), Diplomacy (Cha), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (nobility) (Int), Perform (Cha), and Sense Motive (Wis).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the sword duelist prestige class.

Unless stated otherwise, the following benefits apply only when a sword duelist is wielding a [dueling sword](../../equipment/gear-ag.md) and carrying nothing in his other hand.

**Bonus Proficiency and Feat**: At 1st level, a sword duelist gains proficiency with the dueling sword and [Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md) as a bonus feat.

**Deft Strike (Ex)**: A sword duelist can add his Dexterity bonus (if any) to damage rolls made with a dueling sword instead of his Strength bonus, or 1-1/2 times his Dexterity bonus if he wields it two-handed. He also adds his sword duelist level to these damage rolls, whether he wields the dueling sword one-handed or two-handed. A sword duelist cannot use this ability if he is wielding a shield or using an off-hand weapon (including armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).

??? note "Original rule"

    **Deft Strike (Ex)**: A sword duelist can add his Dexterity bonus (if any) to damage rolls made with a dueling sword instead of his Strength bonus. This bonus on damage rolls applies whether the duelist is wielding a dueling sword one-handed or two-handed. A sword duelist cannot use this ability if he is wielding a shield or using an off-hand weapon (including armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).

**Bonus Feat**: At 1st level, a sword duelist gains one combat feat of his choice as a bonus feat. He must meet its prerequisites.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Quick Draw**: A sword duelist gains Quick Draw as a bonus feat. If the character already has this feat, he instead gains Duelist Mastery. If he already has both feats, he instead gains a combat feat of his choice as a bonus feat. The sword duelist must meet all prerequisites of the selected combat feat.

**Display Weapon Prowess (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a sword duelist adds a bonus equal to half his class level on [Intimidate](../../rules/skills/intimidate.md) checks while using [Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md) with a dueling sword. He also gains an additional +1 bonus on these Intimidate checks for each of the following feats he has with the dueling sword: [Greater Weapon Focus](../../rules/feats/greater-weapon-focus.md), [Greater Weapon Specialization](../../rules/feats/greater-weapon-specialization.md), [Improved Critical](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), Weapon Focus, or [Weapon Specialization](../../rules/feats/weapon-specialization.md). The bonuses stack with each other (the total bonus is considered a competence bonus).

When involved in a duel, a sword duelist’s mastery of elaborate strikes and stances grants him a bonus equal to half his class level on performance combat checks (*Ultimate Combat*). These tricks can also be of great help in countering an enemy during a duel, granting him a bonus equal to half his class level on rolls to attempt a dueling parry or to use dueling resolve.

**Defensive Parry (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a sword duelist gains a +1 dodge bonus to his AC when making a full attack with a dueling sword. This AC bonus increases to +2 at 7th level. If a sword duelist is also a fighter with the Sword Duelist Defender archetype, levels in this class stack with his fighter levels when determining the AC bonus from this ability.

**Panache (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a sword duelist gains panache, as a swashbuckler of his sword duelist level. More than just a lightly armored warrior, a sword duelist is a daring combatant. She fights with panache: a fluctuating measure of a sword duelist’s ability to perform amazing actions in combat. At the start of each day, a sword duelist gains a number of panache points equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Her panache goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot go higher than her Charisma modifier (minimum 1), though feats and magic items can affect this maximum. A sword duelist spends panache to accomplish deeds (see below), and regains panache in the following ways.

- **Critical Hit with a Light or One-Handed Piercing Melee Weapon**: Each time the sword duelist confirms a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, she regains 1 panache point. Confirming a critical hit on a [helpless](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#helpless) or unaware creature or a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than half the sword duelist’s character level doesn't restore panache.

- **Killing Blow with a Light or One-Handed Piercing Melee Weapon**: When the sword duelist reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon attack while in combat, she regains 1 panache point. Destroying an unattended object, reducing a helpless or unaware creature to 0 or fewer hit points, or reducing a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than half the sword duelist’s character level to 0 or fewer hit points doesn't restore any panache.

**Opportune Parry and Riposte (Ex)**: At 3rd level, when an opponent makes a melee attack against the sword duelist, she can spend 1 panache point and expend a use of an attack of opportunity to attempt to parry that attack. The sword duelist makes an attack roll as if she were making an attack of opportunity; for each size category the attacking creature is larger than the sword duelist, the sword duelist takes a -2 penalty on this roll. If her result is greater than the attacking creature's result, the creature's attack automatically misses. The sword duelist must declare the use of this ability after the creature's attack is announced, but before its attack roll is made. Upon performing a successful parry and if she has at least 1 panache point, the sword duelist can as an immediate action make an attack against the creature whose attack she parried, provided that creature is within her reach.

**Adaptive Tactics (Ex)**: At 4th level, a sword duelist learns to adapt his fighting style to counter his enemy’s strengths. He reduces the attack roll penalty for fighting defensively or using [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md) by 1 at 4th level and by 2 at 8th level. In addition, after he uses his dueling sword to attack a creature he attacked during the previous round, as a swift action he can attempt a [Sense Motive](../../rules/skills/sense-motive.md) check (DC = 10 + the opponent’s base attack bonus). If successful, he gains a +2 circumstance bonus either on attack rolls or to his AC against that creature until the beginning of his next turn.

**Shatter Confidence (Ex)**: At 5th level, a sword duelist can attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize his target as a swift action after he confirms a critical hit or succeeds at a disarm, reposition, or sunder combat maneuver with a dueling sword. If his target is already [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken), the duelist can attempt an Intimidate check to demoralize the target as a swift action after any successful melee attack or combat maneuver. If he succeeds at this check, any morale bonuses the target has are suppressed for a number of rounds equal to the sword duelist’s class level. Suppressed effects are not dispelled, and they resume after this time elapses if their duration has not expired. Suppressed morale bonuses include those gained from a [barbarian’s rage](../classes/barbarian.md#rage-ex) ability; however, this ability doesn’t end rage, nor does it suppress other effects of rage or rage powers that do not provide morale bonuses (thus, it doesn’t cause a barbarian to become [fatigued](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fatigued) unless the barbarian chooses to end the rage during the suppressed duration, in which case fatigue from rage affects the barbarian normally).

**Saving Slash (Ex)**: At 6th level, when wielding a dueling sword, a sword duelist can use an immediate action to try deflecting a melee critical hit targeting him, reducing its damage to that of a normal hit, with a 25% chance of success. This does not stack with the fortification special ability of magical armor or similar effects.

**Dexterous Duelist (Ex)**: At 7th level, a sword duelist with at least one free hand is not denied his Dexterity bonus when attempting [Acrobatics](../../rules/skills/acrobatics.md) or [Climb](../../rules/skills/climb.md) checks, nor does he provoke attacks of opportunity when standing up from [prone](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#prone).

**Greater Saving Slash**: At 9th level, a sword duelist’s saving slash has a 50% chance of reducing a melee critical hit to a normal hit. In addition, he can attempt a saving slash against ranged critical hits, including those from rays and other ranged touch effects, with a 25% chance of success.

**Confounding Duelist (Ex)**: At 10th level, a sword duelist who successfully uses shatter confidence also suppresses the target’s competence and insight bonuses. He can use shatter confidence with any hit, even if the enemy is not shaken. He also gains +1 to the critical range or critical multiplier of the dueling sword, which stacks with Improved Critical (making it 18–20/×2 or 19–20/×3, his choice).

??? note "Original rule"

    **Confounding Duelist (Ex)**: At 10th level, a sword duelist who successfully uses shatter confidence also suppresses the target’s competence and insight bonuses.
