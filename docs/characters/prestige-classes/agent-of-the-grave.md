# Agent of the Grave

Agents of the grave are spellcasters who serve undead masters and study the secrets of undeath.

**Hit Die**: d8.

**Table: Agent of the Grave**

| Level | Base Attack Bonus | Fort Save | Ref Save | Will Save | Special | Spells per Day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | +0 | +1 | +0 | +1 | Inspired necromancy, lich’s touch, unholy fortitude | +1 level of existing class |
| 2nd | +1 | +1 | +1 | +1 | Undead manipulator, aligned class | +1 level of existing class |
| 3rd | +1 | +2 | +1 | +2 | Negative energy conduit | +1 level of existing class |
| 4th | +2 | +2 | +1 | +2 | Death’s shroud, negative energy affinity, aligned class | +1 level of existing class |

### Requirements

To qualify to become an agent of the grave, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any evil.

**Skills**: Knowledge (arcana) 5 ranks, Knowledge (religion) 5 ranks.

**Spells**: Able to cast [*animate dead*](../../magic/spells/animate-dead.md).

**Special**: Member of an undead-worshipping cult for 1 year.

### Class Skills

The agent of the grave’s class skills are Bluff (Cha), Heal (Wis), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (arcana) (Int), Knowledge (history) (Int), Knowledge (religion) (Int), Linguistics (Int), Spellcraft (Int), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: An agent of the grave gains no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

**Spells per Day**: At every level, an agent of the grave gains new spells per day (and spells known, if applicable) and an increased effective caster level as if they had also gained a level in a spellcasting class they belonged to before adding the prestige class. They gain no other benefit of that class from this ability.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Spells per Day**: This ability did not apply at 1st level.

**Inspired Necromancy (Ex)**: Agent of the grave levels count double when determining the total Hit Dice of undead you can control with spells such as *animate dead*. This does not change how many undead one casting creates.

**Lich’s Touch (Su)**: As a melee touch attack, you deal 2d6 points of negative energy damage per agent of the grave level. This can heal your undead minions, and also you once you gain negative energy affinity. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Charisma bonus.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Lich’s Touch (Su)**: The touch deals 1d6 points of negative energy damage per level.

**Unholy Fortitude (Su)**: Starting with the hit points you roll for your first agent of the grave level, and for each level in any class after that, you can add either your Constitution bonus or your Charisma bonus to the hit points gained that level.

**Aligned Class (Ex)**: At 2nd and 4th level, an agent of the grave gains the class features of an additional level of a class they belonged to before adding the prestige class.

**Undead Manipulator (Ex)**: Your mind-affecting spells and spell-like abilities treat undead as their original type (for example, [*charm person*](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md) works on a humanoid zombie).

**Negative Energy Conduit (Ex)**: Once per day, as a standard action, you can create an aura identical to [*desecrate*](../../magic/spells/desecrate.md) that lasts 10 minutes per level. Undead you create are treated as created within a *desecrate* area.

**Death’s Shroud (Su)**: Once per day, you can use [*nondetection*](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md) or [*undetectable alignment*](../../magic/spells/undetectable-alignment.md), with a caster level equal to your highest caster level.

**Negative Energy Affinity (Ex)**: You are healed by negative energy and harmed by positive energy, as if you were undead. This gives no added benefit if you are already undead.

**Secrets of Death (Ex)**: Add a number of necromancy spells that are not on your class spell list equal to your Intelligence modifier. You can add spells above your current casting level, which become usable when you reach the required level.

**Undeath Initiate (Ex)**: You gain a +5 bonus on ability checks, skill checks, and saving throws related to transforming into an undead creature (such as a lich). If you are slain by a creature with create spawn, you keep your Intelligence score and free will, and you are never controlled by the creature that killed you. Unless the create spawn ability states otherwise, the new undead loses all class levels.
