# Scar Soldier

Scar soldiers are the elite of a band of heavily indoctrinated, deliberately scarred female soldiers who serve a tyrant. Many of them have turned to worship infernal patrons who reward them with devilish powers. Drive and will, not race or origin, make a scar soldier. Scar soldiers wear distinctive scar soldier plate and drill relentlessly in shield-and-sword tactics.

**Hit Die**: d10.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a scar soldier, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Lawful evil, lawful neutral, or neutral evil.

**Armor Proficiency**: Proficient with heavy armor.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +5.

**Feats**: Improved Shield Bash, Iron Will, Weapon Focus (longsword).

**Gender**: Female.

**Language**: Infernal.

### Class Skills

The scar soldier's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Diplomacy (Cha), Fly (Dex), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (local) (Int), Knowledge (nobility) (Int), Perception (Wis), and Sense Motive (Wis).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the scar soldier prestige class.

**Hardened Heart (Ex)**: A scar soldier adds twice her class level to the DC of any attempt to Intimidate her.

**Maiden’s Shield (Ex)**: A scar soldier is treated as if she had the Two-Weapon Fighting feat as long as she is using a longsword and a shield. This allows her to take feats that require Two-Weapon Fighting as a prerequisite without meeting the Two-Weapon Fighting or Dexterity prerequisites, but she benefits from such feats only when she’s wielding a longsword and shield.

**Tyrant’s Discipline (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a scar soldier draws upon her indoctrination, tactical training, and devotion to tyranny to develop potent talents. A scar soldier selects a discipline from the following list at 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter.

- **Castling**: When the scar soldier takes a 5-foot step, she can step into the space of a willing, adjacent ally. If she does, that ally immediately takes a 5-foot step into the scar soldier’s former space without spending an action to do so.
- **Driving Assault**: When making a full attack, the scar soldier can take a –2 penalty on melee attack rolls for 1 round. If she does, the first time in that round that she hits a creature of her size or smaller with a melee attack, she can attempt a bull rush combat maneuver check to push the creature back 5 feet. She gains a +2 bonus on this combat maneuver check and does not provoke attacks of opportunity when performing the bull rush, but regardless of the result, she cannot push the target further than 5 feet. If the scar soldier pushes a creature in this way, she can immediately move into its space without provoking attacks of opportunity.
- **Dispassionate Fury**: The scar soldier can enter a dispassionate, murderous state. This functions as the dreadnought barbarian’s dead calm ability and is usable for a number of rounds each day equal to twice her class level. If the scar soldier has levels in barbarian and has the dreadnought archetype, her scar soldier levels instead stack with her barbarian levels to determine the duration of her rage.
- **Furious Huntress**: The scar soldier adds her Strength bonus, rather than her Dexterity bonus, to attack rolls made with bows.
- **Kinslayer**: The scar soldier gains favored enemy (humanoids [humans] +2), as per the ranger ability. If the scar soldier already has the same favored enemy bonus from another class, her existing bonus instead increases by 2.
- **Merciless Massacre**: When the scar soldier hits a cowering or panicked creature with a melee attack or reduces a creature to negative hit points with a melee attack, as a swift action she can deal additional damage to that creature equal to 1d8 + her class level.
- **Tyrant’s Sword**: The scar soldier’s weapon training bonus with heavy blades increases by 1. The scar soldier must have at least 4 levels in this prestige class and have the weapon training (heavy blades) class feature to select this discipline. The scar soldier can select this discipline a second time when her class level is 8th level or higher.
- **Unyielding**: While wearing [scar soldier plate](../../equipment/gear-ag.md), the scar soldier gains DR 1/—. The character must have at least 6 levels in this prestige class to select this discipline. The damage reduction increases to DR 2/— at 8th level and to DR 3/— at 10th level. This damage reduction does not stack with damage reduction granted by other sources.

**Armored Angel (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a scar soldier gains the armor training class feature, treating her class level as her fighter level while wearing scar soldier plate. If the scar soldier has levels in another class that grants the armor training class feature, the levels stack to determine its benefits, but only when she is wearing scar soldier plate.

**Infernal Eye (Su)**: At 5th level, a scar soldier gains Alertness as a bonus feat and gains a +4 profane bonus on Will saves against illusions. In addition, once per day as a swift action, the scar soldier can take 1d4 points of Charisma damage to gain the benefits of the *true seeing* spell for a number of rounds equal to her class level.

**Infernal Mystique (Ex and Sp)**: At 7th level, a scar soldier gains a +4 bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks against targets of the same creature type as the scar soldier. Once per day, the scar soldier can use *dominate person* as a spell-like ability (CL = her character level).

**Hollow Heart (Ex)**: At 9th level, a scar soldier gains Diehard as a bonus feat. In addition, she gains a +4 profane bonus on saving throws against emotion and pain effects, and she is immune to fear effects.

**Infernal Ascension (Su)**: At 10th level, a scar soldier’s type changes to outsider (evil, extraplanar, lawful), and Hell becomes her native plane. She gains fire resistance 30, telepathy with a range of 50 feet, and the see in darkness universal monster ability. Ebon-feathered wings emerge from the scar soldier’s back, granting her a fly speed of 50 feet with good maneuverability.
