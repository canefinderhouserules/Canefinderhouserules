# Devoted Muse

A cultural tradition of passionate muses trains and dispatches the faithful of a deity of art, beauty, love, and music to study, recover, and propagate dying art forms abandoned and despoiled in turbulent times. To protect themselves and the valuable works they preserve, each muse trains extensively in self-defense. Their unique fighting styles are an eclectic tribute to lost martial schools, fending off foes as much with fascinating form as with actual harm.

*Note: the original class was tied to a specific deity. Here the deity requirement is replaced with a requirement to worship any deity with the listed portfolio, and the deity-specific details have been made generic.*

### Requirements

To qualify to become a devoted muse, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +3.

**Alignment**: Lawful good, neutral good, or chaotic good.

**Deity**: Must worship a deity whose portfolio includes art, beauty, love, or music.

**Skills**: Bluff 3 ranks, Knowledge (history) 3 ranks, Perform (dance) 5 ranks.

**Feats**: [Improved Feint](../../rules/feats/improved-feint.md) and [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md).

### Class Skills

The devoted muse's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Acrobatics (Dex), Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Craft (Int), Escape Artist (Dex), Knowledge (history) (Int), Knowledge (local) (Int), Knowledge (nobility) (Int), Knowledge (religion) (Int), Perception (Wis), Perform (all) (Cha), Sense Motive (Wis), Sleight of Hand (Dex), and Swim (Str).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the devoted muse prestige class.

**Combat Expertise**: At 1st level, a devoted muse gains [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md) as a bonus feat, ignoring its prerequisites.

**Artful Defense (Ex)**: When wearing light or no armor and not using a shield, a devoted muse adds 1 point of her Charisma bonus (if any) per class level as a dodge bonus to her Armor Class. If she is caught [flat-footed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) or otherwise denied her Dexterity bonus, she also loses this bonus.

**Panache (Ex)**: More than just a lightly armored warrior, a devoted muse is a daring combatant. She fights with panache: a fluctuating measure of a devoted muse’s ability to perform amazing actions in combat. At the start of each day, a devoted muse gains a number of panache points equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 1). Her panache goes up or down throughout the day, but usually cannot go higher than her Charisma modifier (minimum 1), though feats and magic items can affect this maximum. A devoted muse spends panache to accomplish deeds (see below), and regains panache in the following ways.

- **Critical Hit with a Light or One-Handed Piercing Melee Weapon**: Each time the devoted muse confirms a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, she regains 1 panache point. Confirming a critical hit on a [helpless](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#helpless) or unaware creature or a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than half the devoted muse’s character level doesn't restore panache.

- **Killing Blow with a Light or One-Handed Piercing Melee Weapon**: When the devoted muse reduces a creature to 0 or fewer hit points with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon attack while in combat, she regains 1 panache point. Destroying an unattended object, reducing a helpless or unaware creature to 0 or fewer hit points, or reducing a creature that has fewer Hit Dice than half the devoted muse’s character level to 0 or fewer hit points doesn't restore any panache.

For the purpose of this ability, a devoted muse’s class levels stack with levels in any other class that grants the panache ability.

**Deeds (Ex)**: Devoted muses spend panache points to accomplish deeds. Most deeds grant the devoted muse a momentary bonus or effect, but some provide longer-lasting effects. Some deeds remain in effect while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, but do not require expending panache to be maintained. A devoted muse can only perform deeds of her level or lower. Unless otherwise noted, a deed can be performed multiple successive times, as long as the devoted muse has or spends the required number of panache points to perform the deed. A devoted muse qualifies for and benefits from deeds as if she were a swashbuckler of her class level. For the purpose of this ability, her class levels stack with levels in any other class that grants the deeds ability, which is how she can reach the deeds above 10th level.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Deeds (Ex)**: This ability functions exactly as the [swashbuckler ability](../classes/swashbuckler.md#deeds) of the same name with two exceptions. First, the devoted muse qualifies for and benefits from deeds as if she were a swashbuckler 3 levels lower than her class level (minimum 1). Second, the devoted muse doesn't gain the opportune parry and riposte deed until 4th level. For the purpose of this ability, a devoted muse's class levels otherwise stack with levels in any other class that grants the deeds ability.

- **Derring-Do (Ex)**: At 1st level, a devoted muse can spend 1 panache point when she makes an [Acrobatics](../../rules/skills/acrobatics.md), [Climb](../../rules/skills/climb.md), [Escape Artist](../../rules/skills/escape-artist.md), [Fly](../../rules/skills/fly.md), [Ride](../../rules/skills/ride.md), or [Swim](../../rules/skills/swim.md) check to roll 1d6 and add the result to the check. She can do this after she makes the check but before the result is revealed. If the result of the d6 roll is a natural 6, she rolls another 1d6 and adds it to the check. She can continue to do this as long as she rolls natural 6s, up to a number of times equal to her Dexterity modifier (minimum 1).

- **Dodging Panache (Ex)**: At 1st level, when an opponent attempts a melee attack against the devoted muse, the devoted muse can as an immediate action spend 1 panache point to move 5 feet; doing so grants the devoted muse a dodge bonus to AC equal to her Charisma modifier (minimum 0) against the triggering attack. This movement doesn't negate the attack, which is still resolved as if the devoted muse had not moved from the original square. This movement is not a 5-foot step; it provokes attacks of opportunity from creatures other than the one who triggered this deed. The devoted muse can only perform this deed while wearing light or no armor, and while carrying no heavier than a light load.

- **Opportune Parry and Riposte (Ex)**: At 1st level, when an opponent makes a melee attack against the devoted muse, she can spend 1 panache point and expend a use of an attack of opportunity to attempt to parry that attack. The devoted muse makes an attack roll as if she were making an attack of opportunity; for each size category the attacking creature is larger than the devoted muse, the devoted muse takes a -2 penalty on this roll. If her result is greater than the attacking creature's result, the creature's attack automatically misses. The devoted muse must declare the use of this ability after the creature's attack is announced, but before its attack roll is made. Upon performing a successful parry and if she has at least 1 panache point, the devoted muse can as an immediate action make an attack against the creature whose attack she parried, provided that creature is within her reach.

- **Kip-Up (Ex)**: At 3rd level, while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she can kip-up from [prone](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#prone) as a move action without provoking an attack of opportunity. She can kip-up as a swift action instead by spending 1 panache point.

- **Menacing Swordplay (Ex)**: At 3rd level, while she has at least 1 panache point, when a devoted muse hits an opponent with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, she can choose to use Intimidate to demoralize that opponent as a swift action instead of a standard action.

- **Precise Strike (Ex)**: At 3rd level, while she has at least 1 panache point, a devoted muse gains the ability to strike precisely with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon (though not natural weapon attacks), adding her devoted muse level to the damage dealt. To use this deed, a devoted muse cannot attack with a weapon in her other hand or use a shield other than a buckler. She can even use this ability with thrown light or one-handed piercing melee weapons, so long as the target is within 30 feet of her. Any creature that is immune to sneak attacks is immune to the additional damage granted by precise strike, and any item or ability that protects a creature from critical hits also protects a creature from the additional damage of a precise strike. This additional damage is precision damage, and isn't multiplied on a critical hit.

    As a swift action, a devoted muse can spend 1 panache point to double her precise strike's damage bonus on the next attack. This benefit must be used before the end of her turn, or it is lost. This deed's cost cannot be reduced by any ability or effect that reduces the amount of panache points a deed costs (such as the Signature Deed feat).

- **Swashbuckler Initiative (Ex)**: At 3rd level, while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks. In addition, if she has the [Quick Draw](../../rules/feats/quick-draw.md) feat, her hands are free and unrestrained, and she has any single light or one-handed piercing melee weapon that isn't hidden, she can draw that weapon as part of the initiative check.

- **Swashbuckler's Grace (Ex)**: At 7th level, while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she takes no penalty for moving at full speed when she uses Acrobatics to attempt to move through a threatened area or an enemy's space.

- **Superior Feint (Ex)**: At 7th level, a devoted muse with at least 1 panache point can, as a standard action, purposefully miss a creature she could make a melee attack against with a wielded light or one-handed piercing weapon. When she does, the creature is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC until the start of the devoted muse’s next turn.

- **Targeted Strike (Ex)**: At 7th level, as a full-round action the devoted muse can spend 1 panache point to make an attack with a single light or one-handed piercing melee weapon that cripples part of a foe's body. The devoted muse chooses a part of the body to target. If the attack succeeds, in addition to the attack's normal damage, the target suffers one of the following effects based on the part of the body targeted. If a creature doesn't have one of the listed body locations, that body part cannot be targeted. Creatures that are immune to sneak attacks are also immune to targeted strikes. Items or abilities that protect a creature from critical hits also protect a creature from targeted strikes.

    - Arms: The target takes no damage from the attack, but it drops one carried item of the devoted muse’s choice, even if the item is wielded with two hands. Items held in a locked gauntlet cannot be chosen.
    - Head: The target is confused for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect.
    - Legs: The target is knocked prone. Creatures with four or more legs or that are immune to trip attacks are immune to this effect.
    - Torso or Wings: The target is staggered for 1 round.

- **Bleeding Wound (Ex)**: At 11th level, when the devoted muse hits a living creature with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon attack, as a free action she can spend 1 panache point to have that attack deal additional bleed damage. The amount of bleed damage dealt is equal to the devoted muse’s Dexterity modifier (minimum 1). Alternatively, the devoted muse can spend 2 panache points to deal 1 point of Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution bleed damage instead (swashbuckler's choice). Creatures that are immune to sneak attacks are also immune to these types of bleed damage.

- **Evasive (Ex)**: At 11th level, while a devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she gains the benefits of the evasion, uncanny dodge, and improved uncanny dodge rogue class features. She uses her devoted muse level as her rogue level for improved uncanny dodge.

- **Subtle Blade (Ex)**: At 11th level, while a devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she is immune to disarm, steal, and sunder combat maneuvers made against a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon she is wielding.

- **Dizzying Defense (Ex)**: At 15th level, while wielding a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon in one hand, the devoted muse can spend 1 panache point to take the fighting defensively action as a swift action instead of a standard action. When fighting defensively in this manner, the dodge bonus to AC gained from that action increases to +4, and the penalty to attack rolls is reduced to -2.

- **Perfect Thrust (Ex)**: At 15th level, while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she can as a full-round action make a perfect thrust, pooling all of her attack potential into a single melee attack made with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon. When she does, she makes the attack against the target's touch AC, and ignores all damage reduction.

- **Swashbuckler's Edge (Ex)**: At 15th level, while the devoted muse has at least 1 panache point, she can take 10 on any Acrobatics, Climb, Escape Artist, Fly, Ride, or Swim check, even while distracted or in immediate danger. She can use this ability in conjunction with the derring-do deed.

- **Cheat Death (Ex)**: At 19th level, whenever the devoted muse is reduced to 0 hit points or fewer, she can spend all of her remaining panache to instead be reduced to 1 hit point. She must have at least 1 panache point to spend. Effects that kill the devoted muse outright without dealing hit point damage are not affected by this ability.

- **Deadly Stab (Ex)**: At 19th level, when the devoted muse confirms a critical hit with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, in addition to the normal damage, she can spend 1 panache point to inflict a deadly stab. The target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or die. The DC of this save is 10 + 1/2 the devoted muse’s level + the devoted muse’s Dexterity modifier. This is a death attack. Performing this deed does not grant the devoted muse a panache point.

- **Stunning Stab (Ex)**: At 19th level, when a devoted muse hits a creature with a light or one-handed piercing melee weapon, she can spend 2 panache points to stun the creature for 1 round. The creature must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw (DC = 10 + 1/2 the devoted muse’s level + the devoted muse’s Dexterity modifier) or [stunned](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#stunned) for 1 round. Creatures that are immune to critical hits are also immune to this effect.

**Aligned Class (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a devoted muse must choose a class they belonged to before adding the prestige class to be their aligned class. At 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter, they gain the class features of an additional level of their aligned class. They still retain the Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saving throw bonuses, and skill ranks of the prestige class.

**Artistic Flourish (Su)**: At 2nd level, when a devoted muse has at least 1 panache point and successfully feints against an opponent in combat, in addition to denying the opponent its Dexterity bonus, she can create one of the following effects (all of which are mind-affecting effects). A creature can be affected by only one such effect at a time, and imposing a second effect immediately ends the first. The DC of any saving throws to resist these effects is equal to 10 + the devoted muse's class level + her Charisma modifier.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Artistic Flourish (Su)**: At 2nd level, when a devoted muse has at least 1 panache point and successfully feints against an opponent in combat, instead of denying the opponent its Dexterity bonus, she can instead create one of the following effects (all of which are mind-affecting effects). A creature can be affected by only one such effect at a time, and imposing a second effect immediately ends the first. The DC of any saving throws to resist these effects is equal to 10 + the devoted muse's class level + her Charisma modifier.

- *Distracted*: The target treats all other creatures as though they had concealment, suffering a 20% miss chance on all attack rolls (even if the creature has blindsight). This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to the devoted muse's Charisma modifier, though a successful Will save reduces the duration to 1 round.
- *Confused*: At 5th level, the devoted muse can cause the target to be [confused](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#confused) for 1 round (Will negates). Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this artistic flourish again for 1 day.
- *Staggered*: At 5th level, the devoted muse can cause the target to be [staggered](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round (Will negates). Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this artistic flourish again for 1 day.
- *Dazed*: At 8th level, the devoted muse can spend 1 panache point to cause the target to be [dazed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazed) for 1 round (Will negates). Whether or not the save is successful, a creature cannot be the target of this artistic flourish again for 1 day.

**Harmonious Strike (Su)**: At 2nd level, when a devoted muse hits a creature affected by her artistic flourish ability, she can spend 1 panache point to perform an especially elegant attack. She selects one of the effects below to modify this harmonious strike.

- *Circling Strike*: After the attack, the devoted muse can move 5 feet as a free action as long as she ends her movement within the target's reach (or former reach, if the attack incapacitated it). This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity and does not count as a 5-foot step. If she attacked with a weapon whose critical multiplier is ×3 or higher, she can instead move 10 feet in this way.
- *Deadly Strike*: The attack deals an additional 1d4 points of damage. This extra damage increases by 1d4 every 2 devoted muse levels after 2nd level (maximum 5d4). This is precision damage and isn't multiplied on a critical hit. If her weapon's critical multiplier is ×3, the size of these bonus damage dice increases by one step to 1d6. If her weapon's critical hit multiplier is ×4, the die size increases to 1d8.
- *Inspirational Strike*: At 6th level, the devoted muse can inspire her comrades with her attack, granting her allies (including herself) within 30 feet a competence bonus on attack and weapon damage rolls until the end of her next turn. This bonus is equal to her weapon's critical modifier (i.e., +2 for ×2, +3 for ×3, etc.). If her attack was a critical hit, these benefits last a number of rounds equal to her Charisma modifier.
- *Masterful Strike*: At 10th level, the devoted muse can feint against one creature within her reach as a free action after resolving the attack. If she performs an artistic flourish with this feint against a distracted target, the target's miss chance against the devoted muse increases by an amount equal to 5 × her weapon's critical multiplier.

**Feinting Feat**: At 3rd level and every 3 class levels thereafter, a devoted muse gains one of the following as a bonus feat: [Disengaging Feint](../../rules/feats/disengaging-feint.md), [Disengaging Flourish](../../rules/feats/disengaging-flourish.md), [Feint Partner](../../rules/feats/feint-partner.md), [Greater Feint](../../rules/feats/greater-feint.md), [Improved Feint Partner](../../rules/feats/improved-feint-partner.md), [Passing Trick](../../rules/feats/passing-trick.md), Skill Focus ([Bluff](../../rules/skills/bluff.md)), [Twinned Feint](../../rules/feats/twinned-feint.md), or [Wave Strike](../../rules/feats/wave-strike.md). The devoted muse must meet the prerequisites for the feat selected.

**Limitless Art (Ex)**: At 4th level, a devoted muse starts each day with 1 additional panache point, and her maximum number of panache points increases by 1. At 7th level and again at 10th level, the number of additional panache points increases by 1.
