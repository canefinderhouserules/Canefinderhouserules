# Other Prestige Class Changes

!!! warning "House rules"
    Changes to prestige classes outside the Core Rulebook, which are published elsewhere (look them up by name on d20pfsrd or Archives of Nethys for the base class). Changes to the Core Rulebook prestige classes are written into their own pages. "Aligned Class" refers to the house ability that lets a class advance an existing class as if it had gained a level in it (the Evangelist version is the full one; "half" works at half rate, as the Mortal Usher).

## Agent of the Grave

- Grants caster level at every level.
- Every 2 levels (at levels 2 and 4) it advances the class features of another class.
- Lich's Touch is 2d6 per level.

## Anchorite of Dawn

- Skill requirements drop to 3; the spell level requirement drops to 1.
- Solar Invocation changes to +1 at level 1, +2 at level 3, +3 at level 6, and +4 at level 9.

## Arclord of the Eye

- Skill requirements drop to 3. The special requirement no longer asks for arcane sight. The feat requirement is only the class's own entry feat.
- Arcane Sight is a 1st-level spell for them.
- From 2nd level onward it counts as gaining a level in another class of your choice as well (Aligned Class; see Evangelist for details).

## Argent Dramaturge

- Perform and Spellcraft requirements drop to 4.
- Argent Dramaturge levels stack with bard levels to improve bardic performances.
- If you spend double the rounds, you can add Song of Silver to any other song you want to use, letting you have 2 at once.
- Starting songs as a swift action is, naturally, a level 7 ability.

## Nomad Horse Rider

- Handle Animal and Ride requirements are 3 instead of 5; the BAB requirement also drops to 3.
- Efreeti Blessing is gained at level 5.
- Its pet is +3 instead of +2 (which saves you the feat entirely).

## Ashavic Dancer

- No longer requires Ghost Whisperer; it is granted at level 1. Requires Perform 3 and spell level 1.
- Deathwaking Dance is 1d6 per 2 levels + Charisma, and the save halves the damage instead of negating it.
- Untethering Steps automatically targets everything within 30 feet.
- Exorcist's Waltz requires 1 full round instead of 4 rounds.
- Ghostly Grace grants its ally effect at level 7 instead of 9.
- Levels of this class add to bard levels for bardic performance.

## Battle Herald

- Skill requirements drop to 2 and the BAB requirement drops to 2.
- Any song that grants its inspiring command bonus can be activated at the same time as any other bardic performance if you pay double the rounds (letting you have 2 bardic performances at once: one of those and, for example, inspire courage, or two of those if you like).
- Instead of Improved Leadership, you gain a bonus equal to half your level on social checks with people below you, whether socially or militarily.

## Blood Bloatmage

- The spell level requirement drops to 2.
- Its rage makes you lose 10 hit points per round it lasts, instead of killing you instantly when it ends.
- Its Bloat lets you choose between 1d4, 1d8 or 1d12 (as you unlock them).
- The +1 natural armor scales every 2 levels (3, 5, 7, 9).

## Brewkeeper

- Requirements drop to caster level 1 and 3 ranks in each skill.

## Brother of the Seal

- Requirements drop to Knowledge (arcana) 3 and it no longer requires Flurry of Blows.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate.

## Champion of the Enlightened

- Skill requirements drop to 3.
- Now requires Improved Unarmed Strike instead of Still Mind.
- You choose whether to use Charisma or Wisdom for all the class features this prestige class advances (for example Charisma to AC, as the monk, even if you are not a scaled monk). It also gives you those class features even if you have not gained them yet, counting levels of this class as monk levels only to improve and unlock the class features mentioned there.
- Special: you can ignore all requirements and replace them with 4 levels of the matching order of paladin, in which case you gain Aligned Class (which only works with that paladin order).

## Chevalier

- Requirements change to: BAB +3, Diplomacy 3 and Knowledge (local) 3. The rest stays the same.
- This class stacks with any class that gives you a mount or animal companion.
- Recklessness can be used once per day per level (2 times per day at level 2, 3 times per day at level 3).

## Crimson Templar

- No longer requires Vital Strike. The BAB requirement drops to 4.
- Heavenly Fire: the save halves instead of negating.
- Immolate is optional, so you do not harm an ally who uses pieces of enemies.

## Script Mage

- Skill requirements drop to 2.
- Gains the Evangelist's Aligned Class.
- Every class feature that has 1 use per 3 levels becomes 1 use per 2 levels, minimum 1 (so at level 2 you already have 2 uses).
- Swift Scrivener reduces the cost of scrolls to 25%.

## Daivrat

- Skill requirements drop to 3. The caster level requirement drops to 1. Instead of requiring Spell Focus (conjuration), it gives it to you at level 1.
- Raises caster level at every level, without skipping level 1.
- Spell Fetch does not increase the spell's level.

## Darechaser

- Requirements drop to BAB +2 and 3 ranks in every skill it asks for.
- No longer requires Athletic; it is granted at level 1.
- Adrenaline Rush also gives a +2 morale bonus to all physical ability scores, improving to +4 at level 6.

## Darkfire Adept

- Requirements drop to *summon monster II* and skills 3.
- Grants caster level at every level.
- Darkfire Taint is a move action.

## Death Slayer

- No longer requires Great Fortitude, skill requirements drop to 3, and the spell requirement drops to 1st-level spells. (Its weapon may be negotiable for another one that reasonably suits some other fitting deity.)
- Its channel rises to 1d10 (but keeps the same restrictions and inability to heal).

## Demoniac / Diabolist

- Skill requirements drop to 5, and it no longer requires Iron Will.
- The spell requirement drops to being able to cast 1st-level spells.
- Gains full Aligned Class (as the Evangelist).

## Devoted Muse

- BAB requirement drops to +3. No longer requires Combat Expertise; it is given to you at level 1, ignoring prerequisites.
- Its Deeds class feature is no longer level –3, and they have Parry and Riposte at level 1.
- Artistic Flourish no longer removes the enemy's denied Dexterity bonus when using feint.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate.

## Dissident of Dawn

- Skill requirements drop to 3.
- The Daylight requirement changes to "being a divine caster with at least 2nd-level spells".
- Divine Deception counts for its Secret Caster roll.
- Secret Caster lets you take an extra –5 on the roll to avoid it being a full-round action.
- Burning Blade lasts 1 minute instead of 1 round per die of channel/inquisitor level/etc.

## Divine Assessor

- Gains caster level at level 1 as well.
- Instead of Open Locks, it is of course Disable Device.
- Instead of 3rd-level spells, requires 2nd-level spells.
- Appraising Eye has no penalty.

## Divine Scion

- No longer requires Iron Will.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate.

## Enchanting Courtesan

- Skill requirements drop to 3; the spell requirement is generalized to 1st-level spells.
- Grants spells per day at every level (it no longer takes it away at the first level you take).
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate (caster level for determining which spells you know has a maximum of your Hit Dice).

## Envoy of Balance

- The spell level requirement drops to 1.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate.
- Can swap endowments for channel feats, metamagic feats and summoning feats.

## Esoteric Knight

- BAB requirement drops to 3, and instead of advancing spells every other level, it advances at every level except the first, like the Eldritch Knight.

## Exalted / Sentinel / Evangelist

- The skill requirements change to 5 and BAB +1 (Exalted), the ability to cast 1st-level spells (Evangelist), and 5 ranks in Religion (Sentinel). Alignment and deity requirements stay the same, as does the Obedience feat; ignore the rest.

## Forest Warden

- Skill and BAB requirements drop to 3.
- Gains full Aligned Class (currently half; use the Evangelist's).

## Genie Binder

- Skill requirements drop to 2 and 6 respectively.
- The spell requirement drops to "being able to cast *summon monster II*", whether as a spell or a spell-like ability.
- No longer requires Persuasive; it is granted at level 1.

## Golden Knight

- BAB requirement drops to 2.
- Both skill requirements drop to 2.
- Authoritative Command starts as a swift action and improves to a free action at level 8.

## Gray Knight

- Skill requirements drop to 3.
- No longer requires Sea Legs; it is granted at level 1.
- Instead of Gray Focus, gains full Aligned Class as the Evangelist.

## Gray Warden

- All skill requirements drop to 2.
- The spell requirement drops to 1.
- No longer requires Combat Reflexes.
- No longer loses caster level at levels 5 and 9 (it works like the Eldritch Knight, which only loses the first level).

## Green Faith Acolyte

- Requirements drop to 2 for skills and 1 for spell level.
- Companion Bond directly gives you a pet equal to your level, or scales any pet you already had, as any class with a pet does.
- Wild Link directly gives you the wild shape class feature, or scales the one you already have if you have druid levels or any class/archetype with wild shape, adding the levels to unlock more forms.

## Guild Agent

- Gains Aligned Class at level 2.
- Its requirements drop to 2 in each of the listed skills.

## Guild Poisoner

- Skill requirements drop to 3 ranks.
- The DC of its abilities scales with character level, not class level.
- Increases the DC of any poison it applies by its class level.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate (as the Mortal Usher).

## Halfling Opportunist

- No longer requires the feat; it is granted at level 1.
- Skill requirements drop to 3.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate (only at levels 2 and 4).

## Harvester Tiller

- Crop Guardian gives an extra +2 to aid another instead of just +1.
- Scarecrow gives +1 to attack and damage against anyone threatening an ally (or rather, a crop).
- At level 6 they gain tactician as a cavalier of their level +5, counting the teamwork feats the class already gives as part of this class feature (and adding 1 extra for the normal tactician effects).

## Infernal Knight

- Requirements drop to BAB +3 and Intimidate 2; the rest stays the same.
- Force of Will changes to directly give Iron Will at level 3, Improved Iron Will at level 6 and Mythic Iron Will at level 9.
- Smite Chaos is not ×2 to EVERYTHING against chaotic outsiders; it is only an explanation of which creature types the normal ×2 of smite evil works against (chaotic subtype, chaotic aberrations and fey). The damage added per level is still ×2, or stacks with other smite classes you have, up to a maximum of your Hit Dice.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate (as the Mortal Usher).
- If you have Infernal Knight Obedience, you may choose to gain the boons at levels 3, 6 and 9 instead of gaining a discipline.

## Infernal Knight Signifer

- The spell level requirement drops to 1. Skill requirements drop to 3.
- No longer requires Warrior Priest or Arcane Armor Training. It directly gives you them at level 1, depending on whether you are a divine or arcane caster, respectively.
- Gains Aligned Class at half rate (as the Mortal Usher).
- Catechesis changes to full Aligned Class (–1 as the Evangelist) for divine casters only.

## Stargazer

- Skill requirements drop to 3, and the caster level requirement drops to 1.

## Sword Duelist

- Instead of Quick Draw, it simply grants an extra feat.
- Deft Strike gives 1.5× Dexterity damage and level to damage.
- Skill requirements are 2 instead of 5 or 3, and it requires only Weapon Focus (heavy blades) and Weapon Finesse.
- Gains proficiency with the order's dueling sword at level 1, and Dazzling Display.
- Defensive Parry also gives Parry and Riposte and Panache as if you were a swashbuckler of your level.
- Confounding Duelist lets you use Shatter Confidence with any hit even if the enemy is not shaken, and gives +1 critical range or critical multiplier, which stacks with Improved Critical (that is, it makes the dueling sword 18–20/×2 or 19–20/×3, your choice).

## Sworn of the Fae

- Gains Aligned Class at half rate.

## Winter Witch

- The spell level requirement drops to 1.
