# Arclord of the Eye

The arclords trace their lineage to the personal retinue of a long-vanished archmage, and though the demiurge has been missing for millennia, they still seek to enact his will, following in his footsteps through his diary entries, workbooks, and scrolls. Seizing political power in the years after his disappearance cost them their homeland, and in the centuries since, most arclords have pursued less radical methods. They pioneered and refined the practical application of magical constructs, conjurations, and education. Their goal is as subtle as it is simple: to make themselves and their magic an indispensable and irreplaceable foundation of communities and societies, achieving positions of influence and leadership in courts across the known world.

### Requirements

To qualify to become an arclord of the eye, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Feats**: [Eye of the Arclord](../../rules/feats/eye-of-the-arclord.md).

**Skills**: Diplomacy 3 ranks, Knowledge (arcana) 3 ranks, Knowledge (engineering) 3 ranks, Spellcraft 3 ranks.

**Special**: You must have at least two spells from every school of magic scribed in your spellbook.

**Special**: Arcane school class feature. You must have the ability to use hand of the apprentice or a different arcane school power that you can use a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier. For arclord abilities that require you to expend a number of uses of hand of the apprentice, if you can’t use hand of the apprentice you can substitute an equivalent arcane school power you can use a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Intelligence modifier. If you do, you must expend one additional use.

### Class Skills

The arclord of the eye's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Diplomacy (Cha), Linguistics (Int), Sense Motive (Wis), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the arclord of the eye prestige class.

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiency

An arclord gains no additional weapon or armor proficiencies.

#### Mystic Pedagogue (Ex)

An arclord adds a bonus equal to 1/2 his class level on [Spellcraft](../../rules/skills/spellcraft.md) checks to learn a wizard spell or craft a magical item.

#### Third Eye (Su)

An arclord can use his Eye of the Arclord feat one additional time per day, plus one additional time per day for every two levels beyond 1st (three times per day at 3rd level, and so on). In addition, while the eye is open, the arclord can use the aid another action to grant an adjacent wizard a +1 bonus to her caster level and a +2 circumstance bonus on concentration checks for the next wizard spell she casts before the beginning of the arclord’s next turn.

#### Aligned Class (Ex)

At 2nd level, a arclord of the eye must choose a class they belonged to before adding the prestige class to be their aligned class. They gain all the class features of this class, essentially adding every arclord of the eye level beyond 1st to their aligned class to determine what class features they gain. They still retain the Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saving throw bonuses, and skill ranks of the prestige class, but gain all other class features of their aligned class as well as those of this prestige class.

##### Arcane Sight

*Arcane sight* is a 1st-level spell for an arclord of the eye.

#### Artificer’s Touch (Sp)

At 2nd level, an arclord can expend one use of his hand of the apprentice to use the Artifice domain’s artificer’s touch power, using his caster level as his cleric level.

#### Scholiast (Sp)

At 3rd level, an arclord can access the wizard school powers of another school of magic. He chooses a single arcane school spell-like ability that has a number of uses per day equal to 3 + his Intelligence modifier. Henceforth, he may use that ability (with a wizard level equal to his caster level) by expending two uses of his hand of the apprentice ability. A specialist wizard needs to expend two uses of his equivalent ability if using scholiast for hand of the apprentice, but still has to expend one additional daily use (for a total of three) to use it for a power of a different school. At 6th level and again at 9th level, an arclord can choose an additional arcane school spell-like ability to use with this ability.

#### Arcane Architect (Ex)

At 4th level, an arclord can create constructs with the Craft Construct feat in only half the time it would normally take. In addition, he can apply the [Extend Spell](../../rules/feats/extend-spell.md) and/or [Widen Spell](../../rules/feats/widen-spell.md) metamagic feats to conjuration (creation) spells he prepares, even if he doesn’t possess those feats. If he does possess them, the level increase for applying Extend Spell to a conjuration (creation) spell is eliminated, and the level increase for applying Widen Spell to a conjuration (creation) spell is reduced to 2.

#### Improved Third Eye (Sp)

At 5th level, an arclord can activate his Eye of the Arclord feat as a move action (1 AP). An arclord can expend two uses of his hand of the apprentice ability to add the effect of [*arcane sight*](../../magic/spells/arcane-sight.md) or [*see invisibility*](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md) to the benefits of his Eye of the Arclord for 1 minute (or until the duration of the Eye ends, whichever is sooner). He can add both effects by expending four uses of this ability.

#### Extradimensional Extension (Su)

At 6th level, the duration of any spell cast by an arclord that creates an extradimensional space (or demiplane) or moves creatures or objects through the Ethereal Plane is doubled as though affected by the Extend Spell feat, without altering the level of the spell. Applications of Extend Spell do not stack with this ability.

#### Call for Aid (Su)

At 8th level, as a standard action (1 AP), an arclord can sacrifice a prepared spell of 2nd level or higher to call a creature bound to him, including an animal companion, eidolon, familiar, personally controlled construct or undead creature, creature telepathically linked to him (including by [*telepathic bond*](../../magic/spells/telepathic-bond.md), [*dominate person*](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md), or [*dominate monster*](../../magic/spells/dominate-monster.md)), or an outsider bound with a [*planar binding*](../../magic/spells/planar-binding.md) spell. The named creature appears adjacent to the arclord, or in the nearest open space if all adjacent squares are occupied. This ability functions like *greater teleport*, except the range limit is determined by the level of the sacrificed spell: short (2nd–3rd), medium (4th–5th), long (6th–7th), unlimited (same plane) (8th), or unlimited (any plane) (9th).

#### Greater Third Eye (Sp)

At 10th level, an arclord can activate his Eye of the Arclord as a swift action (1 AP). In addition, he can expend four uses of his hand of the apprentice while opening his third eye to gain *greater arcane sight* or [*true seeing*](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md) until the beginning of his next turn. He gains both effects if he expends eight uses of this ability.
