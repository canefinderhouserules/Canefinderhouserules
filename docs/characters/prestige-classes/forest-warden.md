# Forest Warden

Forest warden is a title that few can reasonably hope to earn. Among the freedom fighters, wardens have proven themselves again and again, taking on enemies that threaten the fate of their free forest land. With their unmatched martial prowess and tactical skills, forest wardens are masters of the forest. They specialize in neutralizing enemies without being seen, but they are not a threat to be taken lightly even in close-quarters combat.

This title is earned only by those who have overcome insurmountable odds and survived unimaginable danger. There are just a handful of wardens alive at any given time, as it is a position that places one in constant peril. Wardens set themselves apart by wearing crests or armor fashioned from trophies won in battle. Traditionally, these were made from the horns or hide of great beasts of the deep forest, but in recent years, regalia of high-ranking enemy officials has become more common. Only the forest marshal commands more respect than a warden among the forest rangers.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a forest warden, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any nonevil and nonlawful.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +3.

**Skills**: Stealth 3 ranks, Survival 3 ranks.

**Special**: You must defeat an evil enemy of a CR 5 levels higher than your character level. This victory must be witnessed by a forest ranger.

### Class Skills

The Forest Warden’s class skills are Acrobatics (Dex), Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Disable Device (Dex), Disguise (Cha), Escape Artist (Dex), Handle Animal (Cha), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (nature) (Int), Perception (Wis), Sense Motive (Wis), Spellcraft (Int), Stealth (Dex), and Survival (Wis).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 6 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

All of the following are class features of the forest warden prestige class.

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiency

Forest wardens are proficient with all simple and [martial weapons](../../equipment/weapons-martial.md), and [light armor](../../equipment/armor.md) and [bucklers](../../equipment/weapons-exotic.md#exotic-ranged-weapons-part-3).

#### Forest Magic (Sp)

A forest warden is one with the forest and gains a number of spell-like abilities due to this arboreal affinity. She uses her forest warden level as her caster level. She can select one of the following spells to gain as a spell-like ability at 1st level, usable once per day: [*alarm*](../../magic/spells/alarm.md), [*detect snares and pits*](../../magic/spells/detect-snares-and-pits.md), [*endure elements*](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md), [*entangle*](../../magic/spells/entangle.md), [*jump*](../../magic/spells/jump.md), [*longstrider*](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md), or [*pass without trace*](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md). Spell-like abilities selected from this list can be used three times per day at 3rd level and five times per day at 5th level.

At 3rd level she gains a second spell-like ability and adds the following spells to her available choices: [*fog cloud*](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md), [*snare*](../../magic/spells/snare.md), [*spider climb*](../../magic/spells/spider-climb.md), and [*spike growth*](../../magic/spells/spike-growth.md). Spell-like abilities selected from this list can be used once per day at 3rd level, three times per day at 5th level, and five times per day at 7th level.

At 5th level she gains a third spell-like ability and adds the following spells to her available choices: [*barkskin*](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md), [*cure light wounds*](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md), [*darkvision*](../../magic/spells/darkvision.md), [*gravity bow*](../../magic/spells/gravity-bow.md), [*lead blades*](../../magic/spells/lead-blades.md), [*plant growth*](../../magic/spells/plant-growth.md), and [*spectral scout*](../../magic/spells/spectral-scout.md). Spell-like abilities from this list can be used once per day at 5th level, three times per day at 7th level, and five times per day at 9th level.

At 7th level she gains a fourth spell-like ability and adds the following spells to her available choices: [*commune with nature*](../../magic/spells/commune-with-nature.md), [*cure moderate wounds*](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md), [*nondetection*](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md), [*sylvan hideaway*](../../magic/spells/sylvan-hideaway.md), and [*tree shape*](../../magic/spells/tree-shape.md). Spell-like abilities selected from this list can be used once per day at 7th level and three times per day at 9th level.

At 9th level, she gains a fifth spell-like ability and adds the following spells to her available choices: [*cure serious wounds*](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md), [*discern lies*](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md), [*freedom of movement*](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md), and [*tree stride*](../../magic/spells/tree-stride.md). Spell-like abilities selected from this list can be used once per day.

#### Unchained Heart (Ex)

A forest warden’s free spirit goes far beyond simple stubbornness. At 1st level, she gains a +2 bonus on Will saving throws made against compulsions and mind-affecting effects. If she succeeds at this saving throw, the forest warden gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls against the source of the spell or effect for 1d4 rounds. The bonus on saving throws increases to +3 at 4th level and +4 at 7th level.

#### Aligned Class (Ex)

Forest wardens come from all walks of life, each offering a unique perspective and skill set. At 2nd level, a forest warden must choose a class she belonged to before adding the prestige class to be her aligned class. She gains all the class features of this class, essentially adding every forest warden level beyond 1st to her aligned class to determine what class features she gains. She still retains the Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saving throw bonuses, and skill ranks of the prestige class.

##### Guerrilla Tactics (Ex)

A forest warden is an expert at hit-and-run attacks, sabotage, and subterfuge. At 3rd level, she can use the following tactics in combat. She can gain the bonus of only a single tactic at a time, but can switch between them as a swift action. If the forest warden becomes [confused](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#confused), [frightened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#frightened), [panicked](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#panicked), or [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken), she cannot gain any bonuses from guerrilla tactics for as long as the condition persists.

*Exploit Weakness*: When flanking an enemy, a forest warden can forgo the +2 bonus on attack rolls from flanking until the start of her next turn in order to further punish the foe. On a successful hit against a flanked enemy while forgoing the attack bonus, the forest warden can perform a disarm, sunder, or trip combat maneuver against the target as a swift action at her full base attack bonus. This action still provokes attacks of opportunity as normal.

*Sneak Attack*: A forest warden gains sneak attack +1d6. This increases to +2d6 at 6th level and +3d6 at 9th level.

*Sniping Prowess*: The forest warden takes advantage of any available cover. Her penalty on [Stealth](../../rules/skills/stealth.md) checks to snipe changes to –16. The penalty changes to –12 at 6th level and –8 at 9th level.

*Sprite Step*: When moving through wooded terrain, the forest warden gains a +4 competence bonus on [Acrobatics](../../rules/skills/acrobatics.md) and [Climb](../../rules/skills/climb.md) checks. While climbing, she does not lose her Dexterity bonus to AC and does not take a penalty for climbing quickly.

#### Advanced Tactics (Ex)

At 6th level, a forest warden adds the following tactics to her list of choices.

*Preemptive Strike*: The forest warden strikes before her targets can react. When acting during a surprise round, she can take one move action and one standard action. She cannot take a full-round action during a surprise round, as normal.

*Trick Shot*: This tactic allows the forest warden to attempt Dirty Trick combat maneuver checks with a ranged weapon at a –4 penalty. At 9th level, this penalty changes to –2.

#### Second Tactic (Ex)

At 9th level, a forest warden is able to benefit from two tactics at a time. She can change one of these at a time as a swift action.

#### Phantom Strike (Ex)

At 10th level, a forest warden becomes a true master of the battlefield. She can always move at her full speed across nonmagical difficult terrain and does not take movement penalties due to poor visibility.

A number of times per day equal to her Intelligence modifier, when the forest warden deals weapon damage to an opponent who is unaware of her presence, she can deal an additional 2d6 points of precision damage or cause the target to cower for 2d6 rounds. The target receives a Will saving throw to negate the [cowering](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#cowering) effect (DC = 10 + half the forest warden’s character level + her Intelligence modifier).
