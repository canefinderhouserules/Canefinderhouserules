# Hearth Warden

Guards patrol great cities and druids protect the deep forests, but what of the boundaries between them? The hinterlands comprise the farmlands and woodland villages of the world, a liminal space between humanity and the wild. Some of the rugged men and women who defend these pockets of civilization are the hearth wardens. Followers of a deity of family, farming, hunting, and trade, hearth wardens shield rural folk from dangerous creatures, extraplanar interlopers, and marauding undead. A hearth warden is skilled with a bow and in tune with the wild, yet capable of harnessing natural resources to nourish mortal communities. They are often touched by the divine, sworn to guard the borders of towns and villages against the forces that threaten to snuff out the hearth fires.

*Note: the original class was tied to a specific deity. Here the deity requirement is replaced with a requirement to worship any deity with the listed portfolio, and the deity-specific details have been made generic.*

### Requirements

To qualify to become a hearth warden, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Lawful good or neutral good.

**Deity**: Must worship a deity whose portfolio includes family, farming, hunting, or trade.

**Skills**: Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, Survival 5 ranks.

**Feats**: Endurance, Weapon Focus (longbow).

**Spellcasting**: Able to cast 1st-level spells.

### Class Skills

The hearth warden's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Climb (Str), Escape Artist (Dex), Handle Animal (Cha), Heal (Wis), Knowledge (geography) (Int), Knowledge (local) (Int), Knowledge (nature) (Int), Knowledge (religion) (Int), Perception (Wis), Ride (Dex), Survival (Wis), Stealth (Dex), and Swim (Str).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the hearth warden prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A hearth warden is proficient with all simple weapons, longbows, light armor, medium armor, and shields (except tower shields).

**Favored Enemy (Ex)**: At 1st level, a hearth warden chooses a favored enemy from the list below. This functions like the ranger class feature of the same name, except that the hearth warden gains an additional ability corresponding with the favored enemy chosen. Additionally, at 8th level, he can select a new favored enemy and the bonus for one such enemy increases by 2 (rather than at 5th and 10th levels).

- *Aberrations*: The hearth warden gains a +2 bonus on all saves against spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities used by aberrations.
- *Constructs*: The hearth warden's ranged attacks ignore the first 10 points of a construct's DR or hardness.
- *Evil Outsiders*: The hearth warden's ranged attacks are treated as good weapons for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.
- *Magical Beasts*: The hearth warden can improve the attitude of a magical beast regardless of its Intelligence score. This ability functions like wild empathy, except the hearth warden takes no penalty for attempting this check.
- *Monstrous Humanoids*: The hearth warden gains a +2 bonus on all saves against spells, spell-like abilities, and supernatural abilities used by monstrous humanoids.
- *Undead*: Arrows loosed from a hearth warden's bow are infused with divine power and deal full damage to any undead that are incorporeal, as if the arrows had the ghost touch weapon special ability.

**Master Archer (Ex)**: At 1st level, and again at 3rd and 5th levels, a hearth warden gains a bonus feat from the following list, even if he doesn't meet the prerequisites: Archer's Blessing, Far Shot, Focused Shot, Point Blank Shot, and Rapid Shot. At 3rd level, he adds Improved Precise Shot, Parting Shot, Point Blank Master, and Manyshot to the list. At 5th level, he adds Pinpoint Targeting and Shot on the Run to the list. The benefits of these feats apply only when the hearth warden wears light, medium, or no armor.

**Fast Movement (Ex)**: At 2nd level, a hearth warden's base land speed increases by 10 feet.

**Favored Terrain (Hinterland) (Ex)**: At 2nd level, when a hearth warden is within a 10-mile radius of a settlement with a population of 2,000 or fewer individuals, he gains a +2 bonus on initiative checks and Knowledge (geography), Perception, Stealth, and Survival checks. A hearth warden traveling through this terrain leaves no trail and can't be tracked (although he can leave a trail if he so desires). At 9th level, these initiative and skill bonuses increase to +4, and the bonuses function as long as the hearth warden is within 20 miles of a settlement with a population of 2,000 or fewer.

**Hinterland Stride (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a hearth warden moves with ease through natural and agrarian environments. This functions exactly like woodland stride, except that a hearth warden also moves normally through any nonmagical difficult terrain in settled areas with a population of 2,000 or fewer individuals, such as a farm or village.

**Chosen Kin (Su)**: At 4th level, as a standard action, a hearth warden can designate a number of allies up to 3 + his Wisdom modifier to be his chosen kin. Allies so designated gain half the hearth warden's favored terrain (hinterland) bonus and half of the bonuses granted by defended hearth. Members of his chosen kin must remain within 30 feet of the hearth warden in order to gain these benefits. At 7th level, this range increases to 60 feet.

**Defended Hearth (Su)**: At 6th level, a hearth warden can designate a settlement as a defended hearth, allowing him to sense the presence of unnatural creatures within 5 miles of it. Aberrations, constructs, dragons, magical beasts, monstrous humanoids, outsiders, and undead constitute unnatural presences for this purpose. Though the hearth warden is aware of potential danger, he doesn't know the exact nature or location of the creature unless he can perceive it. The defended hearth must be a settled area with a population of 2,000 or fewer individuals, and the hearth warden must spend 24 hours carefully studying the entire area to activate this ability, after which he gains a +2 favored terrain bonus in that settlement, which stacks with any other favored terrain bonus he receives in that area. This bonus increases to +4 at 10th level. A hearth warden can have a number of defended hearths equal to his Wisdom bonus (minimum 1). When he reaches his maximum number of defended hearths, he can select a new defended hearth by abandoning an existing one and performing the ritual for a new area.

**Imbue Arrow (Su)**: At 7th level, a hearth warden gains the ability to place a spell that affects an area upon an arrow. This functions exactly like the arcane archer ability of the same name.

**Waymarker Travel (Sp)**: At 10th level, a hearth warden gains the ability to create a waymarker anywhere within the area of a defended hearth. The hearth warden can maintain up to five waymarkers at a time—if the hearth warden creates a sixth waymarker, he chooses which of the previous five deactivates. Once per day as a move action, the hearth warden can cast *word of recall* as a spell-like ability to teleport to any of his active waymarkers.

**Spells per Day**: At the indicated levels, the character gains new spells per day as if he had also gained a level in a spellcasting class he belonged to before adding the prestige class. He doesn't, however, gain other benefits a character of that class would have gained, except for additional spells per day, spells known (if he is a spontaneous spellcaster), and an increased effective level of spellcasting. If a character had more than one spellcasting class before gaining this prestige class ability, he must decide to which class he adds the new level for purposes of determining spells per day.
