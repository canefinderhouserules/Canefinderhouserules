# Blood Bloatmage

Blood bloatmages swell their bodies with magically charged blood, spending it to fuel their spells.

**Hit Die**: d6.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a blood bloatmage, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Feats**: [Bloatmage Initiate](../../rules/feats/bloatmage-initiate.md), Spell Focus (any school).

**Spells**: Able to cast 2nd-level arcane spells.

??? note "Original requirements"

    **Spells**: Able to cast 3rd-level arcane spells.

### Class Skills

The blood bloatmage’s class skills are Fly (Dex), Heal (Wis), Knowledge (all, taken individually) (Int), Spellcraft (Int), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A blood bloatmage gains no proficiency with any weapon or armor.

**Spells per Day**: Each level adds to the spellcasting progression of an arcane class you already had: spells per day, spells known (for spontaneous casters), and effective caster level only. If you have several arcane classes, choose one to receive each level. Characters who use spellbooks gain no free spellbook entries.

**Blood Pool**: Your blood pool maximum equals your blood bloatmage level. You can spend blood points when casting a spell to cast it without using a spell slot: a prepared caster recalls the spell as if it had not been cast, and a spontaneous caster keeps the slot unused. Retaining a slot costs blood points equal to the spell’s level, paid at the time of casting. Blood points cannot teach you spells you do not know or have prepared.

*Overextension*: Bloat can push you past your normal limits. If your current blood points are above your class level but no more than twice it, you are [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened). If they exceed twice your class level, you enter a homicidal rage for 1d6 rounds or until your pool reaches 0, whichever comes first. You lose 10 hit points per round the rage lasts. Afterward, your blood points drop to 0.

??? note "Original rule"

    *Overextension*: After the rage, your blood points drop to 0, your hit points drop to –1, and you begin dying. You do not lose hit points during the rage.

*Daily recovery*: When you rest to regain spells, your blood points return to your class level, but never higher. Excess points remain until spent.

*Regulation rituals*: Daily rituals during spell preparation let you subtract 1 point from a bloat roll after the result is known. The cost is a noncumulative –2 penalty to Constitution, which lasts until you skip the rituals for one day.

**Bloat**: A blood bloatmage can exert herself as a free action to instantly gain extra blood points, but doing so puts her at risk of a dangerous collapse. At 1st level, she can bloat once per day to gain 1d4 points. At 4th level, she can bloat twice per day, and each time she chooses to gain 1d4 or 1d8 points. At 8th level, she can bloat three times per day, and each time she chooses to gain 1d4, 1d8, or 1d12 points.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Bloat**: A bloatmage can exert herself as a free action to instantly gain extra blood points, but doing so puts her at risk of a dangerous collapse. At 1st level, the bloatmage can bloat once per day to gain 1d4 points. At 4th, she can bloat twice per day, and the die roll becomes 1d8. At 8th, she can bloat three times per day, and the roll becomes 1d12.

**Hemophilia**: The DC of [Heal](../../rules/skills/heal.md) checks to stop your bleed effects is 5 higher than normal. Each bleeding wound inflicted on you costs 1 blood point. Multiple bleed attacks stack this loss, but one attack that deals damage repeatedly does not cost extra points each time.

**Corpulence**: At 3rd level, a blood bloatmage becomes so massive that her rolls of fatty, blood-laden flesh grant her a +1 natural armor bonus. At 5th level, this bonus increases to +2. At 7th level, this bonus increases to +3 but reduces her speed by 10 feet. This penalty stacks with the penalty from the [Bloatmage Initiate](../../rules/feats/bloatmage-initiate.md) feat. This reduction in speed can never reduce her speed below 5 feet, nor does it affect magical flying effects. At 9th level, this bonus increases to +4.

??? note "Original rule"

    **Corpulence**: +1 natural armor at 3rd level; +2 at 7th level, when your speed also drops by 10 feet.

**Absorb Bloodline**: At 10th level, once per day, you can drink 1 pint of fresh blood from a sorcerer of a given bloodline, or from a creature affiliated with it. This deals 1 point of Constitution damage to the source and takes a full minute. For 1 hour, you gain that bloodline’s powers as a sorcerer whose level equals your total arcane caster level. You do not gain its bonus spells or proficiencies. With [Brew Potion](../../rules/feats/brew-potion.md), you can distill the blood into a potion-sized draught usable as a standard action. Absorbed bloodlines stack with any original [sorcerer bloodline](../classes/sorcerer.md#bloodline).
