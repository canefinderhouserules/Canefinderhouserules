# Chevalier

Chevaliers are daring heroes who ride into danger with little regard for their own safety.

*Note: this class was originally published for the 3.5 rules. It is legal here, with the 3.5 skills adapted.*

**Hit Die**: d10.

**Table: Chevalier**

| Level | Base Attack Bonus | Fort Save | Ref Save | Will Save | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1st | +1 | +2 | +0 | +2 | Aura of courage, recklessness |
| 2nd | +2 | +3 | +0 | +3 | Controlled charge, stubborn mind |
| 3rd | +3 | +3 | +1 | +3 | Poison immunity, smite evil |

### Requirements

To qualify to become a chevalier, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any good.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +3.

**Skills**: Diplomacy 3 ranks, Knowledge (local) 3 ranks.

**Special**: The character must have succeeded at a challenge requiring great heroism, such as an encounter with an EL at least 3 higher than their level. Being carried through an encounter by more experienced heroes does not count.

??? note "Original requirements"

    **Base Attack Bonus**: +6.

    **Skills**: Gather Information 4 ranks, Knowledge (local) 4 ranks.

### Class Skills

The chevalier’s class skills are Acrobatics (Dex), Bluff (Cha), Craft (Int), Diplomacy (Cha), Intimidate (Cha), Perform (Cha), Profession (Wis), and Survival (Wis).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 4 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

#### Mounts and Companions

Chevalier levels stack with any class that gives you a mount or animal companion, but only to determine the level of that mount or companion.

#### Aura of Courage (Su)

You have an aura of courage equivalent to that of a 3rd-level paladin.

#### Recklessness (Ex)

On the round you enter battle, you gain a morale bonus equal to your class level on attack and damage rolls. You can use this ability once per day per chevalier level (twice per day at 2nd level, and three times per day at 3rd level).

??? note "Original rule"

    **Recklessness (Ex)**: This applies only once per day against a given opponent or group, so re-entering the same battle does not grant it again.

#### Controlled Charge (Ex)

At 2nd level, you no longer take the –2 AC penalty when charging.

#### Stubborn Mind (Ex)

At 2nd level, if you fail a saving throw against an enchantment spell or effect, you can retry it one round later against the same DC. This is your only extra chance, though other means of breaking the effect still work.

#### Poison Immunity (Ex)

At 3rd level, you become immune to poison.

#### Smite Evil (Su)

At 3rd level, once per day, you can smite evil as if you were a paladin of your character level.
