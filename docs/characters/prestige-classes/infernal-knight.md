# Infernal Knight

Infernal knights are lawful warriors who enforce order against chaos, feared and respected wherever they maintain a presence. Each belongs to a knightly order with its own ideals and a specific form of disorder it seeks to combat. To join, a candidate must pass the Test: single combat against a devil. Most are lawful neutral and proudly avoid being “tainted” by distractions such as good or evil, though some who rise in power shift toward tyranny and cruelty.

### Requirements

To qualify to become an infernal knight, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any lawful.

**Armor Proficiency**: Must be proficient with heavy armor.

**Base Attack Bonus**: +5.

**Skills**: Intimidate 5 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 2 ranks.

**Special**: The character must slay a devil with Hit Dice greater than his character level. This victory must be witnessed by an infernal knight.

### Class Skills

The infernal knight's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (local) (Int), Perception (Wis), Ride (Dex), and Sense Motive (Wis).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the infernal knight prestige class.

**Aura of Law (Ex)**: An infernal knight’s lawful aura (see the *detect law* spell) functions as that of a cleric whose level is equal to the infernal knight’s character level.

**Detect Chaos (Sp)**: This ability functions like the paladin’s detect evil class feature, save that it grants the use of *detect chaos* instead.

**Order**: A character must choose one knightly order to join at 1st level: the order of the Chain (bounty hunters who seek escaped convicts and fugitives and often jail politically inconvenient prisoners), Gate (spellcasting signifers who use infernal magic to learn of catastrophic crimes before they occur), Godclaw (faith-driven knights devoted to a pantheon of lawful deities), Nail (knights who crusade against “uncivilized” peoples), Pyre (hunters of shamans, witches, occult practitioners, and disciples of less-established faiths), Rack (destroyers of dangerous knowledge and revolutionary philosophies), or Scourge (opponents of criminal activity and watchers for corruption among authorities). The choice of order determines which disciplines the character later gains access to.

**Smite Chaos (Su)**: This ability functions as the paladin’s smite evil class feature but against chaotic-aligned creatures. This ability is twice as effective against outsiders with the chaotic subtype, chaotic-aligned aberrations, and fey.

**Discern Lies (Sp)**: At 2nd level, an infernal knight can use *discern lies* as a spell-like ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + his Charisma modifier with a caster level equal to his character level.

**Infernal Knight Armor (Ex)**: At 2nd level, an infernal knight earns the right to wear [infernal knight plate](../../equipment/gear-ag.md), which does not reduce his speed. While wearing this armor, the infernal knight reduces its armor check penalty by 1 and increases the maximum Dexterity bonus allowed by 1. At 5th level, these adjustments increase to 2. At 8th level, these adjustments increase to 3.

**Disciplines**: At 3rd level, an infernal knight gains access to his first discipline, choosing one associated with his specific order. At 6th level, he gains a second discipline, choosing from any listed as being available to “any order.” At 9th level, he gains his third discipline, choosing this one from any of the disciplines listed below, even those from another order, with the exception of pentamic faith. When applicable, the save DC to resist a discipline’s effect is equal to 10 + the infernal knight’s level + his Charisma modifier.

The infernal knight can use any one discipline a number of times per day equal to the total number of disciplines he has access to, so at 3rd level he can use his discipline once per day. At 6th level, he can use both disciplines twice per day each. At 9th level, he can use all three of his disciplines three times per day.

- **Brand (Sp; Order of the Pyre)**: The infernal knight can use *mark of justice* as a spell-like ability.
- **Censor (Su; Order of the Rack)**: When the infernal knight uses smite chaos on a creature, he can strike the creature mute for 1d4 rounds unless it succeeds at a Will save. A mute creature cannot speak, cast spells with verbal components, or use language-dependent effects.
- **Fearsomeness (Ex; any order)**: An infernal knight who successfully uses the Intimidate skill to cause a creature within 10 feet to become shaken can instead cause that creature to become frightened.
- **Onslaught (Su; Order of the Nail)**: As a free action, an infernal knight increases his base speed by 10 feet and gains a +4 bonus to his Strength for 1 round. If the infernal knight is mounted, these bonuses also apply to his mount.
- **Pentamic Faith (Ex; Order of the Godclaw only)**: This ability allows the infernal knight to select one of the following domains: Artifice, Glory, Knowledge, Law, Magic, Nobility, Protection, Rune, Strength, Travel, or War. The infernal knight gains the granted powers (but not domain spells) of that domain, treating his infernal knight levels as cleric levels to determine what domain abilities he has access to.
- **Shackle (Su; Order of the Chain)**: When the infernal knight uses smite chaos on a creature, he can impede its mobility. The creature can negate this effect with a successful Will save; otherwise, it is affected as if by *slow* for 1d4 rounds.
- **Summon Devil (Sp; Order of the Gate)**: The infernal knight can use *summon monster V* as a spell-like ability to summon 1 bearded devil. At 6th level, this spell-like ability is replaced by *summon monster VI*, allowing him to summon 1d3 bearded devils or 1 erinyes. At 9th level, this spell-like ability is replaced by *summon monster VII*, allowing him to summon 1d4+1 bearded devils, 1d3 erinyes, or 1 bone devil.
- **Tracker (Sp; any order)**: The infernal knight can summon a creature to aid him, either in battle or to track an enemy, as if using a *summon monster* spell, save that the summoned creature lingers for 1 hour before vanishing. A 6th-level infernal knight can summon either an eagle, a riding dog, a wolf, or a leopard. A 9th-level infernal knight can instead opt to summon a dire wolf or a hell hound.
- **Vigilance (Su; Order of the Scourge)**: The infernal knight gains low-light vision (this effect is constant). In addition, as a full-round action, he can see through up to 5 feet of stone, wood, or similar barriers as if they didn’t exist. Metal and denser barriers block this effect. Each use of this ability to see through solid objects lasts as long as the infernal knight concentrates, up to a number of rounds equal to the infernal knight’s level.
- **Wrack (Su; any order)**: The infernal knight can make a touch attack as a standard action to cause a creature to suffer incredible pain. The creature touched takes damage equal to 1d6 + the infernal knight’s Charisma modifier, and must succeed at a Will save or become staggered for 1d4 rounds.

**Force of Will (Ex)**: At 3rd level, an infernal knight gains a +2 bonus on Will saves against spells with any one of the following subschools or descriptors: charm, compulsion, glamer, fear, figment, pattern, or phantasm. At 6th level, the infernal knight chooses another subschool or descriptor from the list and gains a +2 bonus on his Will saves against spells of that type, and the bonus provided by his first selection increases to +4. At 9th level, he chooses from the list again, and both previous selections increase by 2, so that he has a +6, a +4, and a +2 bonus, respectively, against spells of three different groups.

**Lawbringer (Ex)**: At 7th level, an infernal knight’s attacks are treated as being lawful for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**Infernal Armor (Su)**: As long as he wears infernal knight armor, a 9th-level infernal knight gains a +2 bonus on all Charisma-related checks made while interacting with lawful, nongood creatures. In addition, while wearing the armor, he gains the ability to see perfectly in darkness of any kind, resistance to fire 30, resistance to acid 10, and resistance to cold 10.

**Hell’s Knight (Su)**: At 10th level, an infernal knight can grant a weapon he wields or touches the axiomatic, flaming burst, or unholy weapon special ability. This weapon has this special ability as long as the infernal knight remains within 100 feet of the weapon, but an infernal knight can maintain only a single weapon’s granted special ability at a time. The infernal knight also becomes immune to fire while wearing infernal knight plate.
