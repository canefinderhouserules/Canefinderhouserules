# Fate Reader

One of the most mysterious and mystical nomad traditions, and certainly one that has captured the imaginations and curiosity of many peoples, is the fate reading. Using a special deck of cards known as a fate deck, one can perform a fate reading by laying the cards out in a three-by-three grid that reveals secrets of the target’s past, present, and future.

Whether or not the revelations and divinations of a typical fate reading are trustworthy, none can deny that the strange powers wielded by the fate reader are real. The fate reader uses the ancient art of fortune-telling to harness destiny and thus augment her spellcasting abilities, infusing them with power by drawing cards from her fate deck and letting fate decide what elements of her magic need augmentation.

Several of the fate reader’s powers require the drawing of cards from a fate deck. You can use an actual fate deck for this act, but you can also simulate a draw as detailed on the [Fate Deck](../../equipment/fate-deck.md) page. For most of the fate reader’s class features, you need only roll 1d6 to determine the suit of each card drawn for a deck casting. Only the blessing of the deck and spirit deck class features pay attention to a card’s alignment.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a fate reader, a character must fulfill all the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Must be chaotic, evil, good, or lawful (cannot be true neutral). Most fate readers represent alignment extremes: lawful good, chaotic good, lawful evil, or chaotic evil.

**Feats**: Fate-Touched.

**Skills**: Knowledge (arcana or religion) 5 ranks, Perform (any) 5 ranks.

**Special**: Ability to cast 3rd-level spells. Must be able to cast at least three divination spells.

**Special**: Must own a fate deck.

### Class Skills

The Fate Reader’s class skills are Bluff (Cha), Craft (Int), Diplomacy (Cha), Knowledge (arcana) (Int), Knowledge (local) (Int), Knowledge (planes) (Int), Knowledge (religion) (Int), Perform (Cha), Profession (Wis), Spellcraft (Int), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks Per Level**: 2 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the fate reader.

#### Weapon and Armor Proficiency

A fate reader gains no additional weapon or armor proficiencies.

#### Spells

When a fate reader gains a level, she gains new spells per day as if she had also gained a level in a spellcasting class she belonged to before she added the prestige class. She does not, however, gain any other benefits a character of that class would have gained. This essentially means that she adds the level of fate reader to the level of whatever other spellcasting class she has. If the character had more than one spellcasting class before she became a fate reader, she must choose which class she adds each fate reader level to for the purposes of determining spells per day.

A fate reader adds the [*fate reading*](../../magic/spells/fate-reading.md) spell (see [fate reading](../../magic/spells/fate-reading.md)) to all of her spell lists as a 3rd-level spell if it is not already on a spell list.

#### Blessing of the Deck (Su)

Once per day, a fate reader may perform a fate reading for herself and all allies within 20 feet of her. This fate reading takes 10 minutes, and allies to be affected by it must remain within 20 feet of the fate reader for the entire time. At the conclusion of the fate reading, count up the number of cards from each suit that were used in the reading. This fate reading provides a bonus based upon the suit with the most cards showing. In case of a tie, choose one suit. The bonus lasts for 24 hours. The suits grant insight bonuses as follows. Strength: +1 on attack rolls; Dexterity: +1 to AC; Constitution: +1 on weapon damage rolls; Intelligence: +1 on all skill checks; Wisdom: +1 on all saving throws; Charisma: +1 on caster level and concentration checks.

#### Deck Casting (Su)

Beginning at 2nd level, a fate reader may, as she casts a spell, draw three cards from her fate deck. This adds both a somatic component (if the spell does not already have one) and a focus component (the fate deck) to the spell, but does not increase the spell’s casting time. Depending on the fate reader’s level, the cards she draws might change the parameters of her spell or grant her some other benefit, as described in each tower ability. The fate reader gains all of the different tower abilities available to her. If she draws cards that she has not yet gained the use of, those cards provide no benefit. Each card the fate reader draws that exactly matches her alignment counts as two cards of that suit. A spell may not be affected by both deck casting and a metamagic feat. The fate reader may use this ability a number of times per day equal to her class level.

#### Tower of Intelligence (Su)

Beginning at 2nd level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability, for each card she draws from the suit of books (Intelligence), she gains a +1 bonus on caster level checks made to penetrate Spell Resistance.

#### Tower of Strength (Su)

Beginning at 3rd level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability to augment a spell that deals damage to hit points, the spell deals +1 point of damage per die for each card from the suit of hammers (Strength) she draws.

#### Tower of Charisma (Su)

Beginning at 4th level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability, for each card she draws from the suit of crowns (Charisma), the save DC of the spell increases by +1.

#### Spirit Deck (Su)

A 5th-level fate reader may, as a standard action, summon a shimmering, translucent fate deck made of force that flies through the air and engulfs a target within 30 feet in a whirling cloud of knife-edged cards. The fate reader then draws a number of fate cards equal to her fate reader level from her personal fate deck, and the spirit deck deals damage based on the number of drawn cards that match her alignment, as shown on the table above. Each exact match deals 5 points of damage, each partial match deals 3 points, each non-matched card deals 1 point, and each opposite match deals 0 points. The fate reader may use this ability a number of times per day equal to 1 + her Charisma modifier (minimum 1/day).

#### Divination (Sp)

A 6th-level fate reader gains the ability to cast [*divination*](../../magic/spells/divination.md) once per day as a spell-like ability. Her caster level equals her character level.

#### Tower of Constitution (Ex)

Beginning at 7th level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability, for each card she draws from the suit of shields (Constitution), she heals 1d6 points of damage.

#### Tower of Dexterity (Su)

At 8th level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability, for each card she draws from the suit of keys (Dexterity), she gains a +1 bonus on Reflex saves and to AC until the beginning of her next turn.

#### Tower of Wisdom (Su)

At 9th level, whenever a fate reader uses her deck casting ability, for each card she draws from the suit of stars (Wisdom), she increases the spell’s effective caster level by +1.

#### Reading the Signs (Ex)

When a 10th-level fate reader draws cards from any deck of cards in order to either activate a class ability (such as deck casting) or to activate a magic item function, she may draw an extra card and choose one to ignore and shuffle back into the deck. The fate reader may not use this ability when performing a fate reading, but may use it when performing her deck casting or spirit deck abilities. She can also use this ability when drawing cards from magical decks, such as a *deck of many things*. She may use this ability at will, but must wait 1d4 rounds between each use.
