# Prestige Classes

Prestige classes allow characters to become truly exceptional, gaining powers beyond the ken of their peers. Unlike the core classes, characters must meet specific requirements before they can take their first level of a prestige class. If a character does not meet the requirements for a prestige class before gaining any benefits of that level, that character cannot take that prestige class. Characters that take levels in prestige classes do not gain any favored class bonuses for those levels.

This chapter presents 10 prestige classes for you to choose from, and other prestige classes appear in other Pathfinder products. Some prestige classes are quite focused and heavy on flavor that might not be compatible with your campaign—consult with your GM before you start to work toward qualifying for a prestige class to make sure that the class is allowed.

The prestige classes presented in this chapter are summarized below.

**[Arcane Archer](arcane-archer.md)**: An arcane spellcaster who draws upon ancient elven traditions to infuse his arrows with potent magical power.

**[Arcane Trickster](arcane-trickster.md)**: A troublemaker and a scoundrel who uses arcane magic to enhance her thievery and trickery.

**[Assassin](assassin.md)**: A remorseless murderer who kills for money and the sheer thrill of death-dealing.

**[Dragon Disciple](dragon-disciple.md)**: An arcane spellcaster who has embraced his latent draconic heritage and, over the course of training and devotion, undergoes a partial transformation into a dragon.

**[Duelist](duelist.md)**: A swashbuckling swordfighter who relies upon grace, poise, and acrobatics to win the day.

**[Eldritch Knight](eldritch-knight.md)**: An arcane spellcaster who augments his magical skills with combat to create a deadly combination of weapons and magic.

**[Loremaster](loremaster.md)**: A spellcaster who devotes his life to research and rumination upon the mysteries of the world.

**[Mystic Theurge](mystic-theurge.md)**: Equally devoted to divine and arcane magic, the mystic theurge combines both magical traditions into one incredibly diverse class.

**[Pathfinder Chronicler](pathfinder-chronicler.md)**: An explorer at heart, the Pathfinder chronicler travels to distant, exotic lands to expand her knowledge of the world.

**[Shadowdancer](shadowdancer.md)**: A mysterious adventurer who walks the boundaries between the real world and the realm of shadows, and who can command shadows to do her bidding.

**[Battle Herald](battle-herald.md)**: A veteran whose masterful tactics and air of command inspire allies to greater feats of heroism.

**[Cyphermage](cyphermage.md)**: An arcane scholar of runes and written magic who masters scrolls, glyphs, and ancient lore.

**[Divine Scion](divine-scion.md)**: A spellcaster chosen by her deity, whose domain and alignment-opposed foes shape her power.

**[Faithless Enforcer](faithless-enforcer.md)**: A lawful investigator who hunts hidden cults and purges divine influence from lands that outlaw religion.

**[Genie Binder](genie-binder.md)**: A conjurer who seals and commands genies, either as an eidolon-like minion or as a source of spellcasting power.

**[Holy Vindicator](holy-vindicator.md)**: A pious warrior who spreads religion at the edge of a sword.

**[Horizon Walker](horizon-walker.md)**: A scout and wanderer comfortable in even the strangest terrain.

**[Master Chymist](master-chymist.md)**: An alchemist whose mutagens create an alternate, brutish personality.

**[Master Spy](master-spy.md)**: An espionage expert specializing in disguises and striking swiftly from the shadows.

**[Nature Warden](nature-warden.md)**: A master of the wilderness bonded spiritually to a fearsome animal companion.

**[Rage Prophet](rage-prophet.md)**: A bold and barbaric champion who embraces otherworldly powers in order to perfect the art of combat.

**[Stalwart Defender](stalwart-defender.md)**: A master of defending territory and holding the line at all costs.

**[Sworn Guard](sworn-guard.md)**: A barbarian bodyguard whose rage and fierce loyalty protect a chosen ally.

### Definitions of Terms

Here are definitions of some terms used in this section.

**Core Class**: One of the standard eleven classes found in Chapter 3.

**Caster Level**: Generally equal to the number of class levels (see below) in a spellcasting class. Some prestige classes add caster levels to an existing class.

**Character Level**: The total level of the character, which is the sum of all class levels held by that character.

**Class Level**: The level of a character in a particular class. For a character with levels in only one class, class level and character level are the same.
