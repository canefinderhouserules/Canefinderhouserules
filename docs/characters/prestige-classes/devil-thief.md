# Devil Thief

The premier agents of a great thieves’ guild—called “devils” as an ironic nod to the infernal rulers of their land—are steeped in the lore and mystery of the guild’s home city. They draw on the spiritual power of the city’s ancient patrons to open locks, vanish through crowds, and slip past the city guard.

**Hit Die**: d8.

### Requirements

To qualify to become a devil thief, a character must fulfill all of the following criteria.

**Alignment**: Any nonlawful.

**Feats**: Weapon Finesse plus any one of the following: Alertness, Athletic, Deceitful, Deft Hands, Magical Aptitude, Persuasive, or Stealthy.

**Skills**: Knowledge (local) 5 ranks, 5 ranks each in any three of the following skills: Acrobatics, Bluff, Climb, Disable Device, Knowledge (history), Knowledge (nobility), Sense Motive, Sleight of Hand, Stealth, or Use Magic Device.

### Class Skills

The devil thief's class skills (and the key ability for each skill) are Acrobatics (Dex), Appraise (Int), Bluff (Cha), Climb (Str), Diplomacy (Cha), Disable Device (Dex), Disguise (Cha), Escape Artist (Dex), Intimidate (Cha), Knowledge (arcana) (Int), Knowledge (history) (Int), Knowledge (local) (Int), Knowledge (nobility) (Int), Linguistics (Int), Perception (Wis), Perform (Cha), Sense Motive (Wis), Sleight of Hand (Dex), Spellcraft (Int), Stealth (Dex), Swim (Str), and Use Magic Device (Cha).

**Skill Ranks at Each Level**: 6 + Int modifier.

### Class Features

The following are class features of the devil thief prestige class.

**Weapon and Armor Proficiency**: A devil thief gains proficiency with longswords.

**Classically Trained (Ex)**: The guild trains its members with the longsword—a weapon its legendary patron used to great effect by eschewing strength for finesse and expertise. A devil thief can use Weapon Finesse with a longsword sized for him when he wields it one-handed, even though it is not a light weapon.

**Patrons’ Favor (Su)**: The patrons of the city are spiritual guardians whose traditions are so strong that the city’s current rulers have not effaced their mark. A devil thief learns to call upon the patrons’ spiritual power, enjoying a mastery of the city matched by few.

At 1st level, a devil thief gains a pool of favor points equal to his class level + his Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (whichever is highest). In addition to the powers inspired by specific patrons (see below), the devil thief can spend one favor point to add a bonus equal to half his class level (minimum +1) on a skill check without spending an action. He must use this ability before learning the result of the check, and he can add this additional bonus only once per skill check. The pool of favor is replenished each morning after 8 hours of rest.

As the devil thief gains levels, he learns to call upon powers inspired by specific patrons. The caster level of spell-like abilities used this way is equal to the character’s class level. Effects (including spell-like abilities) that allow a saving throw have a DC equal to 10 + the devil thief’s class level + the devil thief’s Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma modifier (whichever is highest).

*Eye of the Glassmaker (Su)*: The patron of glassmakers allows the devil thief to turn walls to glass. At 1st level, as a full-round action that costs 1 favor point, the devil thief can see the internal workings of a lock, trap, or similar device that he touches. He gains a +4 bonus on Disable Device checks to disable this device. At 4th level, he can use this ability to see through a section of any door or wall he is adjacent to and touches, as though a section of that wall or door no larger than 5 square feet were transparent. This effect works only on walls or doors that are no thicker than 6 inches. This effect lasts 1 round per class level.

*Navigator’s Guidance (Su)*: The patron of navigation guides the devil thief while hindering any pursuit. At 3rd level, when in an urban environment, the devil thief can spend 1 favor point as a swift action to gain a +10-foot enhancement bonus to his base speed and a +4 insight bonus on Acrobatics and Stealth checks for 1 minute. Whenever the devil thief spends favor to improve a skill check as part of a chase or pursuit, he adds 2d6 to his check instead of 1d6.

*Woodcrafter’s Craftsmanship (Sp)*: The patron of woodcraft imparts mastery over such creations. At 5th level, as a standard action, the devil thief can spend 1 favor point to use *warp wood* or *meld into wood* (as per *meld into stone*, except that the wood can be of any size or dimensions). If the devil thief is 8th level or higher, he can use this ability as *passwall* through wooden barriers or statue to assume the form of a statue made of wood (hardness 5) instead.

*Deep Water’s Bounty (Sp)*: Calling upon the patron of the bounty of deep waters allows for safe navigation of underwater regions. At 7th level, the devil thief can spend 1 favor point as an immediate action to grant the benefits of *water breathing* for 24 hours. When he activates this ability, he can affect other creatures he can touch by spending 1 additional favor point for each added creature.

*Renewer’s Recovery (Sp)*: The patron of renewal grants access to powerful healing. At 8th level, as a standard action, a devil thief can spend 1 favor point to use *cure serious wounds* or *lesser restoration*. He can spend 3 favor points in a 1-minute ritual to use *break enchantment* or *restoration* (requiring expensive material components as appropriate).

*Guardsman’s Shroud (Su)*: Experienced devil thieves gain the favor of the patron of the city guard. At 9th level, the devil thief gains a +5 insight bonus on all skill checks opposed by or targeting city guards, a +2 dodge bonus to his AC against attacks from city guards, and a +2 insight bonus on all saving throws against effects created by city guards. This ability is constant and applies only while the devil thief has at least 1 favor point remaining.

*Monument’s Avatar (Su or Sp)*: The city’s greatest monument symbolizes a dead god of humanity, and the devil thief can draw inspiration from this towering statue to invoke that god’s authority or mastery of the blade. At 10th level, the devil thief can use his favor ability to improve attack rolls with longswords as if they were skill checks. In addition, as a full-round action, the devil thief can spend 2 favor points to issue a *suggestion*, or spend 5 favor points to issue a *mass suggestion*. These function as the spells of the same name except that they affect only humans.

**Guild Secret**: At 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter, a devil thief gains a rogue talent, selected from those available to rogues. The devil thief treats his character level as his rogue level for the purpose of determining the talents’ effects. At 6th level, whenever the devil thief would select a rogue talent, he can select an advanced talent instead.

Alternatively, the devil thief can focus on occult studies instead. If he chooses this option, when he would gain a talent via this ability, his spellcasting ability in a psychic spellcasting class he has increases by 1 level instead. The devil thief can gain a level of spellcasting in this way once, plus one additional time at 6th and 10th levels.

**Sneak Attack (Ex)**: At 3rd level, a devil thief gains sneak attack as per the rogue class feature. The extra damage is 1d6 at 3rd level, and it increases to 2d6 at 6th level, and to 3d6 at 9th level.
