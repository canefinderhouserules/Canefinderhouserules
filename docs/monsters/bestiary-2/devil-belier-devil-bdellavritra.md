## Belier Devil (Bdellavritra) (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
LE Large outsider (devil, evil, extraplanar, lawful)  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., see in darkness; Perception +28

**Defense**

**AC** 33, touch 15, flat-footed 27 (+6 Dex, +18 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 212 (17d10+119)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +11, **Will** +20  
**DR** 10/good and silver; **Immune** fire, poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10; **SR** 28

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** bite +23 (4d8+7), 3 tongues +23 (2d6+7/19–20 plus grab)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (20 ft. with tongues)  
**Special Attacks** blood drain (1d4 Con damage), constrict (1d6+7), possession, strangle  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +24)  
At will—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 pounds of objects only), *[persistent image](../../magic/spells/persistent-image.md)* (DC 22)  
3/day—*[acid fog](../../magic/spells/acid-fog.md)*, *[dimensional anchor](../../magic/spells/dimensional-anchor.md)*, *[dominate person](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md)* (DC 22), *[greater scrying](../../magic/spells/scrying-greater.md)* (DC 24), *[mass suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion-mass.md)* (DC 23)  
1/day—*[blasphemy](../../magic/spells/blasphemy.md)* (DC 24), *[demand](../../magic/spells/demand.md)* (DC 25), *[magic jar](../../magic/spells/magic-jar.md)* (DC 22), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (DC 22), summon (level 6, 3 barbed devils 45%), *[waves of exhaustion](../../magic/spells/waves-of-exhaustion.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 24, **Dex** 23, **Con** 25, **Int** 25, **Wis** 26, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +25 (+29 grapple); **CMD** 41 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Greater Disarm](../../rules/feats/greater-disarm.md), [Greater Trip](../../rules/feats/greater-trip.md), [Improved Critical (tongue)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Trip](../../rules/feats/improved-trip.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md)  
**Skills** Appraise +24, Bluff +27, Diplomacy +27, Fly +32, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (arcana, local, nobility, and planes) +27, Perception +28, Sense Motive +28, Spellcraft +24, Stealth +22  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Hell)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** double

**Special Abilities**

**Possession (Su)** When a belier devil uses its *magic jar* spell-like ability, it can identify and pinpoint life forces accurately, allowing it to select its victims with ease. It does not require a receptacle to use *magic jar*. If it uses *magic jar* on a host while on the Material Plane, its own body becomes ethereal for the duration of the possession and the *magic jar* effect lasts until the effect is dispelled, the devil ends the effect, it is forced out of its host body, or it or its host body is slain.
