## Movanic Deva (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
NG Medium outsider (angel, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*, low-light vision; Perception +26  
**Aura** protective aura

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 13, flat-footed 21 (+3 Dex, +11 natural; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 180 (12d10+60)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +11, **Will** +9; +4 vs. poison, +4 resistance vs. evil  
**Defensive Abilities** nature’s pacifism, protected life force; **DR** 10/evil; **Immune** acid, cold, electricity, fire, death effects, energy drain, petrification; **SR** 21

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+1 flaming greatsword* +18/+13/+8 (2d6+7/19–20 plus 1d6 fire)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +12)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
At will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[discern lies](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md)* (DC 18), *[dispel evil](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)* (DC 19), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[holy smite](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)* (DC 18), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)* (self only), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (DC 19), *[remove curse](../../magic/spells/remove-curse.md)*, *[remove disease](../../magic/spells/remove-disease.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*  
7/day—*[cure serious wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md)*  
1/day—*[antimagic field](../../magic/spells/antimagic-field.md)*, *[awaken](../../magic/spells/awaken.md)*, *[holy aura](../../magic/spells/holy-aura.md)* (DC 22)

**Statistics**

**Str** 19, **Dex** 17, **Con** 18, **Int** 17, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 29  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (greatsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greatsword).*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +19, Fly +22, Intimidate +19, Knowledge (planes) +18, Knowledge (religion) +18, Perception +26, Sense Motive +22, Stealth +18, Survival +18; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech

**Ecology**

**Environment** any good-aligned plane  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or squad (3–6)  
**Treasure** double (*+1 flaming greatsword*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Nature’s Pacifism (Ex)** Animals and plant creatures do not willingly attack a movanic deva, though they can be forced to do so by magic. If the deva attacks a plant or animal, its protection against that creature ends.

**Protected Life Force (Ex)** Movanic devas are never harmed by positive-dominant or negative-dominant planar traits.
