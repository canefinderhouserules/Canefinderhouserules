## Immolation Devil (Puragaus) (CR 19)

**XP** 204,800  
LE Large outsider (devil, evil, extraplanar, lawful)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *true seeing*; Perception +27

**Defense**

**AC** 36, touch 17, flat-footed 28 (+8 Dex, +19 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 396 (18d10+216); regeneration 5 (good weapons or good spells)  
**Fort** +23, **Ref** +19, **Will** +14  
**DR** 15/good and silver; **Immune** fire, poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10; **SR** 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 80 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +30 (2d6+12 plus burn), 2 claw +29 (1d8+12 plus burn), gore +29 (2d8+12 plus burn), 2 wings +27 (1d8+6)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** burn (2d6, DC 31), hellfire  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +24)  
Constant—*[fire shield](../../magic/spells/fire-shield.md)*, *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
At will—*[fireball](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)* (DC 20), *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[persistent image](../../magic/spells/persistent-image.md)* (DC 22), *[wall of fire](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md)*  
3/day—*[dictum](../../magic/spells/dictum.md)* (DC 24), *firestorm* (DC 25), *[mass charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster-mass.md)* (DC 25)  
1/day—summon (level 9, any 2d4 devils of CR 10 or lower, 90%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 34, **Dex** 26, **Con** 35, **Int** 24, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +31; **CMD** 49  
**Feats** [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Blinding Critical](../../rules/feats/blinding-critical.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, [Stand Still](../../rules/feats/stand-still.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Bluff +28, Diplomacy +28, Disable Device +26, Fly +31, Intimidate +28, Knowledge (arcana, nobility) +25, Knowledge (engineering, planes) +28, Perception +27, Sense Motive +27, Spellcraft +28, Stealth +25  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Hell)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or council (3–6)  
**Treasure** double

**Special Abilities**

**Hellfire (Su)** Any fire damage caused by an immolation devil’s abilities and spells is half fire damage, half unholy damage.
