## Scylla (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CE Huge aberration (aquatic)  
**Init** +15; **Senses** all-around vision, blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, *see invisibility*; Perception +29  
**Aura** frightful presence (30 ft., DC 26),

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 20, flat-footed 18 (+11 Dex, +1 dodge, +10 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 340 (20d8+180); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +14, **Ref** +17, **Will** +18  
**Defensive Abilities** *freedom of movement*, improved evasion; **DR** 10/cold iron and lawful; **Immune** cold, charm effects, confusion and insanity effects; **Resist** acid 20, fire 20; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 50 ft.  
**Melee** 4 bites +25 (1d8+11/19–20 plus bleed), 4 tentacles +23 (1d6+7 plus grab)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** bleed (1d6), constrict (1d6+8)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +22)  
Constant—*[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[nondetection](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[control water](../../magic/spells/control-water.md)*, *[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[major image* ](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)(DC 19)  
3/day—*[black tentacles](../../magic/spells/black-tentacles.md)*, *[charm monster* ](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)(DC 20), *[insanity* ](../../magic/spells/insanity.md)(DC 23), *[mirage arcana* ](../../magic/spells/mirage-arcana.md)(DC 21), *[solid fog](../../magic/spells/solid-fog.md)*  
1/day—*[control weather](../../magic/spells/control-weather.md)*, *[power word stun](../../magic/spells/power-word-stun.md)*, *[project image* ](../../magic/spells/project-image.md)(DC 23), summon (level 8, 1 charybdis)

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 32, **Con** 27, **Int** 20, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +28 (+32 grapple); **CMD** 47 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), Multiattack, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (tentacles)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Acrobatics +34, Bluff +26, Knowledge (nature) +25, Intimidate +29, Perception +29, Sense Motive +26, Stealth +26, Swim +39, Use Magic Device +26
