## Mu Spore (CR 21)

**XP** 409,600  
CN Colossal plant  
**Init** +3; **Senses** blindsight 240 ft., low-light vision; Perception +43

**Defense**

**AC** 37, touch 1, flat-footed 37 (–1 Dex, +36 natural, –8 size)  
**hp** 449 (31d8+310); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +26, **Ref** +11, **Will** +19  
**Defensive Abilities** grasping tendrils; **DR** 10/epic; **Immune** plant traits; **Resist** acid 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 30 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** bite +32 (6d6+16/19–20 plus grab), 4 tentacles +27 (3d8+8/19–20 plus grab)  
**Space** 30 ft., **Reach** 30 ft. (60 ft. with tentacle)  
**Special Attacks** spore cough, constrict (3d8+16), swallow whole (20d8 acid, AC 28, 41 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 42, **Dex** 9, **Con** 29, **Int** 18, **Wis** 28, **Cha** 29  
**Base Atk** +23; **CMB** +47 (+51 grapple); **CMD** 56 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Greater Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/greater-bull-rush.md), [Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Critical (tentacles)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/improved-lightning-reflexes.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (tentacles)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Toughness.*  
**Skills** Fly +33, Knowledge (dungeoneering, geography, nature) +35, Perception +43, Sense Motive +40  
**Languages** Aklo, Common, Terran, Undercommon

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary or pair  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Grasping Tendrils (Ex)** Sticky, arm-length tendrils cover a mu spore. A mu spore can use these tendrils to attempt a grab as an immediate action when an adjacent creature hits it with a melee attack. As it is only using the tendrils (instead of conducting the grapple normally), it takes a –20 penalty to its CMB to make and maintain the grapple (+31 CMB with tendrils). The mu spore does not gain the grappled condition while grappling a creature with its tendrils.

**Spore Cough (Su)** Once every 1d4 rounds as a standard action, a mu spore can release a cloud of burrowing spores in a 100-foot cone. The burrowing spores deal 20d8 points of damage to all creatures and wooden structures in the area, or half damage to any creatures that make a DC 34 Reflex save. Plants and plant creatures are immune to this damage. The save DC is Constitution-based.
