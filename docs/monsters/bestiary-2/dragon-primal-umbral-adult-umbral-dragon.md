## Adult Umbral Dragon (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
CE Huge dragon (extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +25  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 8, flat-footed 29 (+21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 289 (17d12+85)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +10, **Will** +15  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** cold, death effects, energy drain, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +24 (2d8+12/19–20), 2 claws +23 (2d6+8), tail slap +21 (2d6+12), 2 wings +21 (1d8+4)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, DC 23, 12d8 negative energy, DC 23), crush, shadow breath (6 Str)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +22)

3rd (5/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[inflict serious wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-serious-wounds.md)* (DC 18)  
2nd (7/day)—*[command undead](../../magic/spells/command-undead.md)* (DC 17), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[web* ](../../magic/spells/web.md)(DC 17)  
1st (8/day)—*[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)* (DC 16),[ *inflict light wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-light-wounds.md)* (DC 16), *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[reduce person](../../magic/spells/reduce-person.md)* (DC 16), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 21, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +27; **CMD** 37 (41 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Hover, Imp. Critical (bite), Imp. Initiative, Imp. Vital Strike, Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), Snatch, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Bluff +25, Diplomacy +25, Fly +16, Knowledge (arcana, local, planes) +25, Perception +25, Sense Motive +25, Spellcraft +25, Stealth +18, Survival +25  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Draconic, Undercommon, 2 more  
**SQ** ghost bane, umbral scion

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Cruel and sadistic, umbral dragons prefer the taste of undead flesh or ghostly ectoplasm, yet never turn down opportunities to consume living flesh.
