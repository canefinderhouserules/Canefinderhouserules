## Nightwalker (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CE Huge undead (extraplanar, nightshade)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darksense, darkvision 60 ft., *detect magic*, low-light vision; Perception +29  
**Aura** desecrating aura (30 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 31, touch 10, flat-footed 29 (+2 Dex, +21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 241 (21d8+147)  
**Fort** +14, **Ref** +11, **Will** +19  
**DR** 15/good and silver; **Immune** cold, undead traits; **SR** 27  
**Weaknesses** light aversion

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +29 (3d6+15/19–20 plus 4d6 cold)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** channel energy (8d6, DC 29, 8/day), fear gaze, swift sundering  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +21)  
Constant—*[air walk](../../magic/spells/air-walk.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[magic fang](../../magic/spells/magic-fang.md)*  
At will—*[contagion* ](../../magic/spells/contagion.md)(DC 19), *[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[unholy blight* ](../../magic/spells/unholy-blight.md)(DC 19)  
3/day—*[confusion* ](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)(DC 19), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[hold monster* ](../../magic/spells/hold-monster.md)(DC 20), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, quickened *[unholy blight* ](../../magic/spells/unholy-blight.md)(DC 19)  
1/day—*[cone of cold* ](../../magic/spells/cone-of-cold.md)(DC 20), *[finger of death* ](../../magic/spells/finger-of-death.md)(DC 22), *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 22), summon (level 7, 4 greater shadows)

**Statistics**

**Str** 35, **Dex** 14, **Con** —, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +29; **CMD** 41  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Command Undead](../../rules/feats/command-undead.md), [Greater Sunder](../../rules/feats/greater-sunder.md), [Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md), [Improved Critical (claws)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*unholy blight*), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw).*  
**Skills** Intimidate +29, Knowledge (arcana) +29, Knowledge (planes) +26, Knowledge (religion) +29, Perception +29, Sense Motive +29, Spellcraft +29, Stealth +18 (+26 in darkness), Swim +33; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Stealth in dim light and darkness  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Negative Energy Plane)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or gang (3–4)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Fear Gaze (Su)** Cower in fear for 1 round, 30 feet, Will DC 25 negates. This is a mind-affecting fear effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Swift Sundering (Su)** A nightwalker can make a sunder attempt as a swift action with one of its claws.
