## Zelekhut (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
LN Large outsider (extraplanar, inevitable, lawful)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, *true seeing*; Perception +20

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 15, flat-footed 18 (+5 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 125 (10d10+70); regeneration 5 (chaotic)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +8, **Will** +10  
**Defensive Abilities** constructed; **DR** 10/chaotic; **SR** 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., fly 60 ft. (average)  
**Melee** 2 chains +17 (2d6+7 plus 1d6 electricity and trip)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  
Constant—*[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
At will—*[clairaudience/clairvoyance](../../magic/spells/clairaudience-clairvoyance.md)*, *[dimensional anchor](../../magic/spells/dimensional-anchor.md)*, *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fear* ](../../magic/spells/fear.md)(DC 17), *[hold person* ](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)(DC 16), *[locate creature](../../magic/spells/locate-creature.md)*  
3/day—*[hold monster* ](../../magic/spells/hold-monster.md)(DC 18), *[mark of justice](../../magic/spells/mark-of-justice.md)*  
1/week—*[lesser geas* ](../../magic/spells/geas-lesser.md)(DC 17)

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 20, **Con** 16, **Int** 10, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 34 (38 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Weapon Focus (chain)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)

**Skills** Acrobatics +18 (+26 jump), Diplomacy +16, Fly +16, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Survival +16; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception, +4 Sense Motive  
**Languages** truespeech  
**SQ** chains

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land (lawful plane)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Chains (Ex)** A zelekhut’s arms end in long lengths of barbed metal. These chains deal slashing damage and 1d6 points of electricity damage with each hit.
