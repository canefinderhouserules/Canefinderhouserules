## Leprechaun (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CN Small fey  
**Init** +7; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +17

**Defense**

**AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 11 (+3 Dex, +1 size)  
**hp** 28 (4d6+4)  
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +7, **Will** +6  
**DR** 5/cold iron; **SR** 13

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 club* +7 (1d8–1)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +7)  
Constant—*[shillelagh](../../magic/spells/shillelagh.md)*  
At will—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 13), *[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[major image* ](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)(visual and auditory elements only, DC 16), *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[ventriloquism* ](../../magic/spells/ventriloquism.md)(DC 14)  
3/day—*[color spray* ](../../magic/spells/color-spray.md)(DC 14), *[fabricate* ](../../magic/spells/fabricate.md)(1 cubic foot of material only)  
1/day—*[major creation](../../magic/spells/major-creation.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 7, **Dex** 16, **Con** 13, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +2; **CMB** –1; **CMD** 12  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +10, Escape Artist +10, Knowledge (nature) +9, Perception +17, Perform (comedy) +8, Perform (dance) +8, Sense Motive +9, Sleight of Hand +14, Stealth +14; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Perception, +4 Sleight of Hand  
**Languages** Common, Elven, Halfling, Sylvan  
**SQ** leprechaun magic

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** solitary, pair, band (3–6), or family (7–10)  
**Treasure** standard (club, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Leprechaun Magic (Sp)** When a leprechaun uses any of its spell-like abilities to deceive, trick, or humiliate a creature (at the GM’s discretion), the spell-like ability resolves at caster level 8th rather than 4th. If a leprechaun uses its spell-like abilities in this manner, it has a bonus of +11 on concentration checks.
