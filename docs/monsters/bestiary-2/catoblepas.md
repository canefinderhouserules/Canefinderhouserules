## Catoblepas (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
N Large magical beast  
**Init** –1; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +25  
**Aura** stench (30 ft., DC 23, 10 rounds)

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 8, flat-footed 27 (–1 Dex, +19 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 175 (14d10+98)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +8, **Will** +8  
**SR** 23

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., swim 20 ft.  
**Melee** bite +19 (3d6+5), gore +18 (2d8+5/19–20), 2 hooves +16 (1d8+2)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, poison, Fortitude DC 23, usable every 1d4 rounds), trample (4d6+7, DC 22)

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 8, **Con** 22, **Int** 5, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +20; **CMD** 29 (33 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Critical (gore)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Perception +25, Swim +13  
**Languages** Aklo

**Ecology**

**Environment** any swamps  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or herd (3–6)  
**Treasure** incidental

**Special Abilities**

**Poison Breath (Ex)** A catoblepas’s horrid, stinking breath is 60-foot cone of poison gas. Breath—contact; *save* Fort DC 23; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d6 Con damage; *cure* 3 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
