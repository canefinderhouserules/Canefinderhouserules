## Voidworm (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CN Tiny outsider (chaotic, extraplanar, protean, shapechanger)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** blindsense 30 ft., darkvision 30 ft., *detect law*; Perception +8

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 15, flat-footed 12 (+3 Dex, +2 size)  
**hp** 30 (3d10); fast healing 2  
**Fort** +1, **Ref** +6, **Will** +2  
**Defensive Abilities** amorphous anatomy, freedom of movement; **Immune** acid; **Resist** electricity 10, sonic 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 50 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** bite +8 (1d3+3), tail slap +3 (1d3 plus confusion)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +7)  
Constant—*[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*  
At will—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 11), *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*  
3/day—*[blur* ](../../magic/spells/blur.md)(self only), *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*  
1/week—*[commune* ](../../magic/spells/commune.md)(CL 12th, 6 questions)

**Statistics**

**Str** 7, **Dex** 17, **Con** 10, **Int** 8, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +9; **CMD** 12 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +9 (+5 jump), Bluff +7, Escape Artist +7, Fly +19, Knowledge (arcana) +5, Perception +8, Stealth +15  
**Languages** Common, Protean  
**SQ** change shape (2 forms, both of which must be Tiny animals; *beast shape II*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Limbo)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or school (3–18)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Confusion (Su)** A creature struck by a voidworm’s tail slap must make a DC 12 Will save or become confused for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.
