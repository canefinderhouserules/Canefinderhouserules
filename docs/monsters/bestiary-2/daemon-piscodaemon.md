## Piscodaemon (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
NE Medium outsider (aquatic, daemon, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*, *see invisibility*; Perception +16

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 14, flat-footed 20 (+4 Dex, +10 natural)  
**hp** 187 (11d10+77)  
**Fort** +14, **Ref** +7, **Will** +9  
**DR** 10/good; **Immune** acid, death effects, disease, poison; **Resist** cold 10, electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 21

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 50 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +19 (2d6+7/18-20/×3 plus grab and 1d6 bleed), tentacles +16 (1d10+3 plus poison)  
**Special Attacks** constrict (2d6+10)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 11th; concentration +14)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only)  
3/day—*[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[stinking cloud](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)* (DC 16)  
1/day—summon (level 4, 1d3 hydrodaemons 35%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 18, **Con** 24, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +18 (+22 grapple); **CMD** 32  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Multiattack, [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw).*  
**Skills** Escape Artist +18, Intimidate +17, Knowledge (planes) +16, Perception +16, Sense Motive +16, Stealth +18, Survival +16, Swim +29  
**Languages** Abyssal, Draconic, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** amphibious, augmented critical

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abaddon)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or knot (3–5)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Augmented Critical (Ex)** A piscodaemon’s claws threaten a critical hit on an 18–20 and inflict ×3 damage on a successful critical hit.

**Poison (Ex)** Tentacles—injury; *save* Fort DC 22; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Con plus staggered for 1 round; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.
