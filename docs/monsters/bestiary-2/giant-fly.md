## Giant Fly (CR 1)

**XP** 400  
N Medium vermin  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +2

**Defense**

**AC** 13, touch 13, flat-footed 10 (+3 Dex)  
**hp** 22 (2d8+6)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +3, **Will** –2  
**Immune** disease, mind-affecting effects

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., climb 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +2 (1d6+1 plus disease)

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 17, **Con** 16, **Int** —, **Wis** 7, **Cha** 2  
**Base Atk** +1; **CMB** +2; **CMD** 15 (21 vs. trip)  
**Skills** Climb +9, Fly +7, Perception +2; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception

**Ecology**

**Environment** any temperate or tropical  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or swarm (3–12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Disease (Ex)** *Filth Fever*: Bite—injury; *save* Fortitude DC 14; *onset* 1d3 days; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Con damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. Some flies might carry other diseases, at the GM’s discretion. The save DC is Constitution-based.
