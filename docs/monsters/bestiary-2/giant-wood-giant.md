## Wood Giant (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
CG Large humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +11

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 15, flat-footed 15 (+1 dodge, +2 armor, +5 Dex, +4 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 99 (9d8+27)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +8, **Will** +7  
**Defensive Abilities** rock catching

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** longsword +11/+6 (2d6+5/19–20) or 2 slams +10 (1d6+5)  
**Ranged**  mwk composite longbow +9/+9/+4 (2d6+5/×3)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +8)  
Constant—*[pass without trace](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
3/day—*[charm animal* ](../../magic/spells/charm-animal.md)(DC 12), *[quench](../../magic/spells/quench.md)*, *[tree shape](../../magic/spells/tree-shape.md)*  
1/day—*[enlarge person* ](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)(self only), *[spike growth](../../magic/spells/spike-growth.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 21, **Con** 17, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +12; **CMD** 28  
**Feats** [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (longsword), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +11 (+15 when jumping), Climb +14, Knowledge (nature) +8, Perception +11, Profession (farmer) +8, Stealth +7 (+11 in forests), Survival +8; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth in forests  
**Languages** Common, Giant, Sylvan; *speak with animals*

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** solitary, gang (2–4), hunting party (5–9, plus 1–4 dire wolves), or clan (10–40, plus 35% noncombatants, 1–3 druids or witches of 2nd–4th level, 1 ranger chieftain of 3rd–7th level, 4–10 dire wolves, and 2–8 giant eagles)  
**Treasure** standard (leather armor, longsword, masterwork composite longbow with 20 arrows, other treasure)
