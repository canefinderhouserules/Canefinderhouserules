## Monadic Deva (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
NG Medium outsider (angel, aquatic, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*, low-light vision; Perception +29  
**Aura** protective aura

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 14, flat-footed 23 (+4 Dex, +13 natural; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 147 (14d10+70)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +13, **Will** +10; +4 vs. poison  
**DR** 10/evil; **Immune** acid, cold, electricity, fire, death effects, energy drain, petrification; **SR** 23

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 90 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+3 morningstar* +23/+18/+13 (1d8+10 plus solid blow)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +14)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
At will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 18, elementals only), *[discern lies](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md)* (DC 18), *[dispel evil](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)* (DC 19), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[holy smite](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)* (DC 18), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)* (self only), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (DC 19), *[remove curse](../../magic/spells/remove-curse.md)*, *[remove disease](../../magic/spells/remove-disease.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*  
3/day—*[cure serious wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md)*, *[holy word](../../magic/spells/holy-word.md)* (DC 21), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*  
1/day—*[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*, *[hold monster](../../magic/spells/hold-monster.md)* (DC 19), *[holy aura](../../magic/spells/holy-aura.md)* (DC 22)

**Statistics**

**Str** 21, **Dex** 19, **Con** 18, **Int** 19, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +19; **CMD** 33  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (morningstar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (morningstar).*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +21, Fly +25, Intimidate +21, Knowledge (planes) +21, Knowledge (religion) +21, Perception +29, Sense Motive +25, Stealth +21, Survival +27, Swim +27; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech  
**SQ** amphibious

**Ecology**

**Environment** any good-aligned plane  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or squad (3–6)  
**Treasure** double (*+3 morningstar*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Solid Blow (Su)** If a monadic deva strikes an opponent twice in 1 round with its mace, that creature takes an extra 1d8+10 points of damage.
