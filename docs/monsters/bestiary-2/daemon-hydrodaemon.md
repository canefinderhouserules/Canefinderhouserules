## Hydrodaemon (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
NE Large outsider (aquatic, daemon, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect magic*; Perception +15

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 12, flat-footed 18 (+1 dodge, +2 Dex, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 95 (10d10+40)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +9, **Will** +3  
**DR** 10/cold iron or silver; **Immune** acid, death effects, disease, poison, waters of the River Styx; **Resist** cold 10, electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 19

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (average; see glide, below), swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +14 (1d8+4 plus grab), 2 claws +13 (1d6+4)  
**Ranged**  sleep spittle +11 (sleep)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** rake (2 claws +13, 1d6+4)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +11)  
Constant—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[water walk](../../magic/spells/water-walk.md)*  
At will—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*  
3/day—*[control water](../../magic/spells/control-water.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[summon monster V](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-v.md)* (Large water elemental only)  
1/day—*[desecrate](../../magic/spells/desecrate.md)*, summon (level 3, 1 hydrodaemon 50%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 15, **Con** 18, **Int** 9, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +15 (+9 grapple); **CMD** 28  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Fly +0, Intimidate +14, Knowledge (planes) +10, Perception +15, Sense Motive +12, Stealth +10, Swim +21  
**Languages** Abyssal, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** amphibious, glide

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abaddon)  
**Organization** solitary, gang (2–5), or mob (6–12)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Glide (Ex)** A hydrodaemon can launch itself into the air and glide along for 1 minute, gaining a fly speed of 40 feet with average maneuverability. While gliding, the hydrodaemon gains the pounce ability.

**Sleep Spittle (Su)** A hydrodaemon can spit at a single target within 20 feet, making a ranged touch attack as a standard action. A target hit by this spittle must succeed on a DC 19 Will save or fall asleep for 6 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.
