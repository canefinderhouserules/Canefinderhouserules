## Draugr (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CE Medium undead (water)  
**Init** +0; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., Perception +6

**Defense**

**AC** 14, touch 10, flat-footed 14 (+2 armor, +2 natural)  
**hp** 30 (3d8+6)  
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +1, **Will** +3  
**DR** 5/bludgeoning or slashing; **Immune** undead traits; **Resist** fire 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.  
**Melee** greataxe +6 (1d12+4/×3 plus nausea) or slam +5 (1d10+4 plus nausea)

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 10, **Con** —, **Int** 8, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +5; **CMD** 15  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (greataxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greataxe).*  
**Skills** Climb +9, Perception +6, Stealth +6, Swim +11  
**Languages** Common (cannot speak)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any coastal  
**Organization** solitary or crew (2–8)  
**Treasure** standard (greataxe, leather armor, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Nausea (Su)** A creature that is damaged by a draugr must make a DC 12 Fortitude save or be nauseated 1 for 1 round. The save DC is Charisma-based.
