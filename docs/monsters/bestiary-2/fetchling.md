## Fetchling (CR 1/2)

**XP** 200  
Fetchling rogue 1  
N Medium outsider (native)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +3

**Defense**

**AC** 17, touch 13, flat-footed 14 (+4 armor, +3 Dex)  
**hp** 10 (1d8+2)  
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +5, **Will** –1  
**Defensive Abilities** shadow bending; **Resist** cold 5, electricity 5

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** dagger +3 (1d4+3/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** sneak attack +1d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 1st; concentration +3)  
1/day—*[disguise self* ](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)(humanoid only)

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 17, **Con** 14, **Int** 8, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +0; **CMB** +3; **CMD** 14  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Appraise +3, Bluff +6, Diplomacy +6, Knowledge (local) +3, Knowledge (planes) +2, Perception +3, Sense Motive +3, Stealth +7; **Racial Modifiers** +2 Knowledge (planes), +2 Stealth  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** trapfinding +1

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Plane of Shadow)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, guild (3–12), or enclave (13–30 plus 1–4 2nd–4th level rogue spies, 1–4 2nd–4th level sorcerers, and 1 3rd–6th level fighter/rogue leader)  
**Treasure** NPC gear (chain shirt, dagger, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Shadow Blending (Su)** Attacks against a fetchling in dim light have a 50% miss chance instead of the normal 20% miss chance. This ability does not grant total concealment; it just increases the miss chance.
