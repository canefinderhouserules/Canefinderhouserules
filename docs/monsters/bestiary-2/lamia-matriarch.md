## Lamia Matriarch (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
CE Large monstrous humanoid (shapechanger)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +3

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 13, flat-footed 17 (+4 Dex, +8 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 156 (12d10+36)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +12, **Will** +11  
**Immune** mind-affecting effects; **SR** 19

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 scimitars* +14/+14/+9/+9/+4 (1d6+6/15–20 plus 1 Wisdom drain on first hit each round) or touch +16 (1d4 Wisdom drain)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** Wisdom drain  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +17)  
At will—*[charm monster* ](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)(DC 19), *[ventriloquism* ](../../magic/spells/ventriloquism.md)(DC 16)  
3/day—*[deep slumber* ](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)(DC 18), *[dream](../../magic/spells/dream.md)*, *[major image* ](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)(DC 18), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[suggestion* ](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)(DC 18)  
**Spells Known** (CL 6th; concentration +11)  
3rd (4/day)—*[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*  
2nd (6/day)—*[death knell* ](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)(DC 17), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 15), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 15), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 19, **Con** 17, **Int** 16, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 32 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Double Slice](../../rules/feats/double-slice.md), [Extend Spell](../../rules/feats/extend-spell.md), [Improved Critical (scimitar)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (scimitar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +8 (+12 jump), Bluff +21, Climb +13, Diplomacy +11, Disguise +11, Intimidate +20, Knowledge (any one) +15, Knowledge (arcana) +15, Spellcraft +15, Swim +13, Use Magic Device +21; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Acrobatics, +4 Bluff, +4 Use Magic Device  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Draconic  
**SQ** change shape (fixed Medium humanoid form, *alter self*), undersized weapons

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cult (3–6)  
**Treasure** double (two *+1 scimitars*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Spells** A lamia matriarch casts spells as a 6th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the cleric list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a lamia matriarch.

**Wisdom Drain (Su)** A lamia matriarch drains 1d4 points of Wisdom each time she hits with her melee touch attack. The first time each round that she strikes a foe with a melee weapon, she also drains 1 point of Wisdom. A DC 21 Will save negates the Wisdom drain. Unlike with other kinds of ability drain attacks, a lamia matriarch does not heal damage when she uses her Wisdom drain. The save DC is Charisma-based.
