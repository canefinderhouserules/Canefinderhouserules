## Adult Magma Dragon (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
CN Large dragon (extraplanar, fire)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +22  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 20)

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 10, flat-footed 28 (+1 Dex, +19 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 172 (15d12+75)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +10, **Will** +15  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 23  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +23 (2d6+12/19–20 plus 6 fire), 2 claws +22 (1d8+8/19–20), tail slap +17 (1d8+12), 2 wings +17 (1d6+4)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (40-ft. cone, 12d6 fire, DC 22)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +18)  
At will—*[burning hands](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)* (DC 14), *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*, *[wall of fire](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md)*  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
3rd (5/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fireball* ](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)(DC 16)  
2nd (7/day)—*[dust of twilight](../../magic/spells/dust-of-twilight.md)*, *[flaming sphere](../../magic/spells/flaming-sphere.md)* (DC 15), *[glitterdust* ](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)(DC 15), *[pyrotechnics* ](../../magic/spells/pyrotechnics.md)(DC 15)  
1st (7/day)—*[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[flare burst](../../magic/spells/flare-burst.md)* (DC 14), *[grease* ](../../magic/spells/grease.md)(DC 14), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[spark](../../magic/spells/spark.md)*,[ *touch of fatigue](../../magic/spells/touch-of-fatigue.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 12, **Con** 21, **Int** 18, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +24; **CMD** 35 (39 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Critical (claws)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +16 (+20 jump), Climb +26, Escape Artist +16, Fly +13, Intimidate +21, Perception +22, Sense Motive +22, Sleight of Hand +16, Stealth +15, Swim +26  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, Ignan  
**SQ** superheated

**Ecology**

**Environment** any mountains or underground (Plane of Fire)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Temperamental and prone to violent outbursts, magma dragons are regarded by most other dragons as dangerously insane—an assumption that, more often than not, proves correct. One can rarely predict a magma dragon’s state of mind until it either attacks or attempts to engage in conversation. For their part, magma dragons can justify all of their actions—they just rarely feel the need to do so.
