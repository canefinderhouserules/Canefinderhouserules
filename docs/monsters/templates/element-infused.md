# Element-Infused Creature

Sometimes unpredictable elemental events cause planar energies to infuse mundane creatures with otherworldly power, transforming them into element-infused creatures.

"Element-infused" is an inherited or acquired template that can be added to any living, corporeal creature. An element-infused creature retains the base creature's statistics and special abilities except as noted here.

An element-infused creature has a strong connection to one or two of the Elemental Planes. Unlike most templates, it grants few universal abilities; rather, it grants the creature a choice of several defensive, offensive, and movement abilities that match its element or elements, so not all water-infused creatures are necessarily identical. To select an ability, at least one of the creature's elements must match one of those listed in parentheses after the ability's name. For abilities that reference an energy type, the type chosen must be associated with one of the creature's elements: acid (earth), cold (water), electricity (air), or fire (fire).

An element-infused creature selects a number of movement, defensive, and offensive abilities based on its Hit Dice, as shown below. In addition, a creature with 4 or more Hit Dice gains one or more bonus abilities that it can use to select additional abilities from any of these three categories. A creature infused with only one element gains one additional bonus ability.

| HD | Movement | Defensive | Offensive | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| 4–6 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 7–9 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 10–12 | 1 | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| 13–15 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 16–18 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| 19+ | 1 | 3 | 2 | 3 |

**CR**: If 9 HD or fewer, base creature's CR + 1; if 10 HD or more, base creature's CR + 2.

**Type**: The creature's type changes to outsider (native). It also gains the air, earth, fire, or water subtypes corresponding to its element or elements. Do not recalculate HD, BAB, or saves.

**Senses**: Darkvision with a range of up to 60 feet.

## Defensive Abilities

The creature can choose one or more of the following extraordinary abilities.

- **Damage Reduction (any)**: DR 2/—. If the creature has 10 or more HD, this can be selected one additional time, increasing the damage reduction to 5/—.
- **Energy Immunity (any)**: Immunity to one of its associated energy types, chosen when it gains this ability. The creature must already have energy resistance 10 or higher for that energy to select this ability.
- **Energy Resistance (any)**: The creature ignores the first 10 points of damage it takes from one of its associated energy types, chosen when it gains this ability. A fire-infused creature that selects this ability also ignores the damage dealt by the fire-dominant planar trait.
- **Evasion (air or fire)**: As the rogue's evasion class feature.
- **Fiery Blood (fire)**: Anyone who damages the creature with a slashing or piercing melee weapon is sprayed with boiling blood that deals 1d4 points of fire damage, increasing by 1d4 for every 5 HD the creature has. Creatures using reach weapons are not subject to this damage.
- **Fortification (any)**: A 50% chance to negate a critical hit or sneak attack, taking only the normal amount of damage.
- **Improved Natural Armor (earth)**: The natural armor bonus improves by 2 (3 if the creature has 10 or more HD).
- **Indistinct Form (air or water)**: A 20% miss chance, as if it had concealment.
- **Stability (earth or water)**: A +4 racial bonus to CMD against bull rush, drag, overrun, and trip combat maneuvers.

## Speed

The creature can choose one or more of the following abilities (extraordinary unless noted).

- **Burrow (earth)**: A burrow speed equal to half its highest speed. Tunnels it creates always collapse behind it and never leave a usable passage.
- **Earth Glide (earth)**: The earth glide ability of an earth elemental, usable for only 1 minute per HD (not necessarily consecutive, in 1-minute increments). The creature must have a burrow speed. Selecting this ability a second time allows its use at will.
- **Flight (air)**: A fly speed equal to its highest speed with average maneuverability (maximum fly speed of 10 feet per HD).
- **Improved Quickness (Su, fire)**: Three times per day as a swift action, the creature can take an extra move action. It must already have quickness.
- **Puddle Form (Su, water)**: Once per day, the creature can turn into an animate puddle of water for up to 10 minutes. This functions as *gaseous form*, but the creature instead has both a base speed and swim speed of 10 feet.
- **Quickness (fire)**: Base movement speed increases by 10 and a +1 bonus on initiative checks. For every 5 HD, speed increases by an additional 10 feet (maximum double its base speed) and the initiative bonus increases by 1 (maximum +5).
- **Swim (water)**: A swim speed equal to its highest speed (maximum 100 feet). The creature also gains the amphibious and aquatic subtypes.

## Special Attacks

The creature can choose one or more of the following abilities.

- **Breath Weapon (Su, any)**: A breath weapon (15-foot cone or 30-foot line) usable as a standard action once every minute. It deals 1d6 points of damage of one of its associated energy types (Reflex half, DC = 10 + 1/2 the creature's HD + its Constitution modifier). The damage increases by 1d6 for every 3 HD. If the creature has 10 or more HD, the breath weapon's size doubles.
- **Burn (Ex, fire)**: The creature can set creatures it strikes on fire, as per the burn ability, but only with attacks augmented by its energy attacks ability.
- **Energy Attacks (Ex, any)**: One of the creature's natural attacks deals an additional 1d6 points of its corresponding energy damage, selected when it gains this ability. The damage increases by 1d6 for every 10 HD. The creature can select this ability multiple times, applying it to two additional natural attacks each time.
- **Gusting Strike (Ex, air)**: Once per round when the creature hits with a melee attack, it can attempt a bull rush against the target as a free action, without provoking an attack of opportunity.
- **Toppling Strike (Ex, earth or water)**: The trip special ability with one of its natural attacks.

## Other Changes

**Abilities**: +4 bonus to two ability scores of its choice, +2 to two others, and –2 to one ability score of its choice.

**Skills**: A creature with racial Hit Dice has 6 + Intelligence modifier skill points per racial Hit Die. Racial class skills are unchanged, and class level skill ranks are unaffected.

**Languages**: If its Intelligence is 3 or higher, the creature gains an elemental language associated with its element: Aquan (water), Auran (air), Ignan (fire), or Terran (earth). If the base creature cannot speak, it instead understands the acquired languages.
