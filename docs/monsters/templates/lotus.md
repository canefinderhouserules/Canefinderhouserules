# Lotus Creature Template

### Leshy

Lotus leshys grow best in warm ponds and lakes in quiet areas. To grow a lotus leshy, the maker must plant a lotus seed by the water’s edge, and sit in quiet mediation by the seed until it sprouts. LOTUS LESHY CL 12th; Price 9,000 gp RITUAL Requirements Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, Knowledge (religion) 5 ranks, lily pad stride, plant growth, summon nature’s ally V; Skill Knowledge (nature or religion) DC 19; Cost 4,500 gp
