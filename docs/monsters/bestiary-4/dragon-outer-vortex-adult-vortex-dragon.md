## Adult Vortex Dragon (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
LN Huge dragon (fire)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** dragon senses, see in darkness; Perception +24  
**Aura** alien presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 9, flat-footed 29 (+1 Dex, +21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 195 (17d12+85)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +13, **Will** +14  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 25  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +24 (2d8+12/19–20), 2 claws +23 (2d6+8), 2 wings +21 (1d8+4), tail slap +21 (2d6+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d8 fire, DC 23), crush, fragmented strike  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +22)  
At will—*[anticipate peril](../../magic/spells/anticipate-peril.md)*, *[entropic shield](../../magic/spells/entropic-shield.md)*  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (5/day)—*[displacement](../../magic/spells/displacement.md)*, *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*, *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[comprehend languages](../../magic/spells/comprehend-languages.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, 3 more

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 12, **Con** 21, **Int** 20, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +27; **CMD** 38 (42 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, Snatch, Wingover, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Bluff +25, Diplomacy +25, Fly +21, Intimidate +25, Knowledge (arcana, geography, planes, religion) +25, Perception +24, Sense Motive +24, Spellcraft +25  
**Languages** Abyssal, Auran, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal  
**SQ** agile, galactic emissary, no breath, starflight

**Ecology**

**Environment** vacuum  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Vortex dragons are the undisputed masters of travel across the galaxies, serving as messengers and observers for the greatest outer dragons and godlike entities in the great beyond.
