## Broken Soul Lillend (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
CE Large outsider (azata, chaotic, extraplanar, evil)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 13, flat-footed 21 (+1 dodge, +3 Dex, +12 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 133 (7d10+63)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +10, **Will** +6  
**DR** 5/—; **Immune** electricity, petrification, poison; **Resist** acid 5, cold 10, fire 10, sonic 5

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (average)  
**Melee** *+1 longsword* +13/+8 (2d6+8/19–20), tail slap +6 (2d6+2 plus grab) or torturous touch +11 touch (2d6 plus 1d6 Dex damage and convulsions), tail slap +6 (2d6+2 plus grab)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** agonized wail (DC 18), baleful gaze (DC 18), bardic performance (21 rounds/day), constrict (2d6+5)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +2)  
3/day—*[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[hallucinatory terrain](../../magic/spells/hallucinatory-terrain.md)* (DC 19), *[knock](../../magic/spells/knock.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*  
1/day—*[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 16), *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*, *[speak with plants](../../magic/spells/speak-with-plants.md)*  
**Bard Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +2)  
3rd (2/day)—*[crushing despair](../../magic/spells/crushing-despair.md)* (DC 18), *[terrible remorse](../../magic/spells/terrible-remorse.md)* (DC 18)  
2nd (4/day)—*[blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)* (DC 17), *[piercing shriek](../../magic/spells/piercing-shriek.md)* (DC 17), *[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)*, *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 17)  
1st (6/day)—*[cause fear](../../magic/spells/cause-fear.md)* (DC 16), *[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 16), *[ear-piercing scream](../../magic/spells/ear-piercing-scream.md)* (DC 16), *[hideous laughter](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)* (DC 16), *[lesser confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion-lesser.md)* (DC 16)  
0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)* (DC 15), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 15), *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 17, **Con** 27, **Int** 14, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +13 (+17 grapple); **CMD** 27 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), Hover, [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance and Toughness are one feat, Toughness; in their place: Weapon Focus (longsword), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Bluff +15, Fly +11, Intimidate +13, Knowledge (nature) +9, Perception +9, Perform (string) +17, Sense Motive +9, Stealth +9, Survival +10; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Intimidate, +4 Survival  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard (*+1 longsword*, masterwork harp, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Bardic Performance** A lillend has the bardic performance ability of a 7th-level bard, granting her access to the countersong, fascinate, inspire courage, inspire competence, and *suggestion* aspects of bardic performance.

**Spells** A lillend casts spells as a 7th-level bard.
