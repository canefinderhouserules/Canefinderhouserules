## Soulbound Shell (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
N Medium construct  
**Init** +1; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +15

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 11, flat-footed 25 (+4 armor, +1 Dex, +11 natural)  
**hp** 132 (15d10+50)  
**Fort** +5, **Ref** +6, **Will** +7  
**DR** 10/adamantine and magic; **Immune** construct traits; **SR** 23  
**Weaknesses** cemented mind, susceptible to mind-affecting effects

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk dagger +17/+12/+7 (1d4+1/19–20)  
**Wizard Spells Prepared** (CL 12th; concentration +17)  
6th—*[beast shape IV](../../magic/spells/beast-shape-iv.md)*, *[disintegrate](../../magic/spells/disintegrate.md)* (DC 21)  
5th—*[cloudkill](../../magic/spells/cloudkill.md)* (2, DC 22), *[dominate person](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md)* (DC 21), *[passwall](../../magic/spells/passwall.md)*  
4th—*[black tentacles](../../magic/spells/black-tentacles.md)* (2), *[fire shield](../../magic/spells/fire-shield.md)*, *[shout](../../magic/spells/shout.md)* (DC 19)  
3rd—*[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[lightning bolt](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)* (2, DC 18), *[stinking cloud](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)* (2, DC 20)  
2nd—*[false life](../../magic/spells/false-life.md)* (already cast), *[glitterdust](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)* (DC 19), *[hideous laughter](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)* (DC 18), *[make whole](../../magic/spells/make-whole.md)*, *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*  
1st—*[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (2, DC 17), *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)* (DC 18), *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)* (already cast), *[unseen servant](../../magic/spells/unseen-servant.md)*  
0—*[acid splash](../../magic/spells/acid-splash.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 13, **Con** —, **Int** 20, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 27  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), Craft ConstructB, [Greater Spell Focus (conjuration)](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-focus.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Skill Focus (Spellcraft)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Spell Focus (conjuration)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Spell Focus (enchantment)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**Skills** Craft (sculptures) +20, Disguise +16, Knowledge (arcana) +20, Knowledge (engineering) +20, Perception +15, Spellcraft +26, Use Magic Device +16  
**Languages** Aklo, Common, Draconic  
**SQ** soul focus

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land  
**Organization** solitary or workshop (1 plus 2–8 other constructs)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk dagger, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Cemented Mind (Ex)** A soulbound shell can never change its known or prepared spells.

**Soul Focus (Su)** The soul bound to the shell lives within a gem inside its chest. As long as this soul focus remains intact, it can be used to animate another shell, at the same cost as creating a new soulbound shell, and retains its personality and memories. A soul focus has hardness 8, 12 hit points, and a break DC of 20.

**Spells** A spellbound shell casts spells as a 12th-level cleric, witch, or wizard, but does not gain any other class abilities.

**Susceptible to Mind-Affecting Effects (Ex)** A soulbound shell is not immune to mind-affecting effects.
