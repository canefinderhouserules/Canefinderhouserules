## Hamadryad (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
CG Medium fey  
**Init** +6; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +31

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 17, flat-footed 22 (+6 Dex, +1 dodge, +12 natural)  
**hp** 240 (20d6+120); fast healing 5  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +18, **Will** +18  
**DR** 10/cold iron; **Resist** acid 30, cold 30, electricity 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk dagger +16/+11 (1d4+10/19–20)  
**Ranged**  *+2 speed composite longbow* +18/+18/+13 (1d8+7/×3)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +26)  
Constant—*[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*, *[speak with plants](../../magic/spells/speak-with-plants.md)*  
At will—*[entangle](../../magic/spells/entangle.md)* (DC 17), *[hold animal](../../magic/spells/hold-animal.md)* (DC 18), *[rusting grasp](../../magic/spells/rusting-grasp.md)*, *[tree shape](../../magic/spells/tree-shape.md)*, *[wood shape](../../magic/spells/wood-shape.md)* (10 lbs. only)  
5/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 20), *[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 17), *[confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)* (DC 19), *[deep slumber](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)* (DC 19), *[neutralize poison](../../magic/spells/neutralize-poison.md)*, *[sending](../../magic/spells/sending.md)* (dryads only), *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 19), *[tree stride](../../magic/spells/tree-stride.md)*  
1/day—*[call lightning storm](../../magic/spells/call-lightning-storm.md)* (DC 21), *[changestaff](../../magic/spells/changestaff.md)*, *[greater heroism](../../magic/spells/heroism-greater.md)*, *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*, *[liveoak](../../magic/spells/liveoak.md)*, *[summon nature’s ally VIII](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-viii.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 23, **Con** 23, **Int** 18, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 28  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Mounted Combat](../../rules/feats/mounted-combat.md), [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Bluff +29, Climb +14, Craft (wood) +12, Diplomacy +29, Escape Artist +19, Handle Animal +16, Heal +9, Intimidate +16, Knowledge (geography) +17, Knowledge (nature) +27, Knowledge (nobility) +9, Perception +31, Perform (any one) +19, Ride +16, Sense Motive +19, Spellcraft +14, Stealth +19, Survival +14; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Craft (wood)  
**Languages** Common, Elven, Sylvan; *speak with animals*, *speak with plants*, *tongues*  
**SQ** druid magic, tree meld, wild empathy, woodcraft

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** retinue (1 plus 2–5 dryads) or court (1 plus 2–12 centaurs, 2–12 dryads, 2–8 satyrs, and 1–2 treants)  
**Treasure** triple (*+2 speed composite longbow* [+5] with 20 arrows, masterwork dagger, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Druid Magic (Su)** A hamadryad can use any magic item as if she were a 20th-level druid.

**Tree Meld (Su)** A hamadryad can meld with any tree (similar to meld into stone) and can remain melded with a tree as long as she wishes.

**Woodcraft (Ex)** A hamadryad has a +8 racial bonus on Craft checks involving wood, and is always treated as if she had masterwork artisan’s woodworking tools when attempting such checks.
