## Ancient Vortex Dragon (CR 19)

**XP** 204,800  
LN Gargantuan dragon (fire)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses, see in darkness; Perception +34  
**Aura** alien presence (300 ft., DC 29)

**Defense**

**AC** 39, touch 6, flat-footed 39 (+33 natural, –4 size)  
**hp** 337 (25d12+175)  
**Fort** +23, **Ref** +16, **Will** +20  
**DR** 15/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 30  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 250 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +33 (4d6+18/19–20), 2 claws +33 (2d8+12/19–20), 2 wings +31 (2d6+6), tail slap +31 (2d8+18)

**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 20d8 fire, DC 29), collapsing breath (DC 29), crush, fragmented strike, tail sweep

At will—*anticipate peril*, *dimension door*, *entropic shield*, *greater teleport*  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 15th; concentration +22)  
7th (5/day)—*forcecage* (DC 24), *spell turning*

2nd (8/day)—*cat’s grace*, *mirror image*, *obscure object*, *resist energy*, *scorching ray*  
1st (8/day)—*feather fall*, *identify*, *mage armor*, *magic missile*, *true strike*  
0 (at will)—*detect magic*, *light*, *message*, *read magic*, 5 more

**Statistics**

**Str** 35, **Dex** 10, **Con** 25, **Int** 24, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +25; **CMB** +41; **CMD** 51 (55 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), Flyby Attack, [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Critical (bite](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), claw), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Quicken Spell](../../rules/feats/quicken-spell.md), Snatch, [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), Wingover

**Languages** Abyssal, Auran, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Elven, Infernal

**Ecology**

**Environment** vacuum  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Vortex dragons are the undisputed masters of travel across the galaxies, serving as messengers and observers for the greatest outer dragons and godlike entities in the great beyond.
