## Adult Lunar Dragon (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
CN Huge dragon  
**Init** +3; **Senses** dragon senses, see in darkness; Perception +25  
**Aura** alien presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 7, flat-footed 28 (–1 Dex, +21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 288 (16d12+96)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +9, **Will** +16  
**Defensive Abilities** reflected light; **DR** 5/magic; **Immune** cold, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 20 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +23 (2d8+12), 2 claws +22 (2d6+8), 2 wings +20 (2d6+4), tail slap +20 (2d8+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** bewildering breath, breath weapon (100-ft. line, 12d8 cold, DC 23), crush, moonsilver  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +21)  
At will—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[life bubble](../../magic/spells/life-bubble.md)*, *[moonstruck](../../magic/spells/moonstruck.md)*  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (3/day)—*[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[heroism](../../magic/spells/heroism.md)*  
2nd (5/day)—*[blur](../../magic/spells/blur.md)*, *[hypnotic pattern* ](../../magic/spells/hypnotic-pattern.md)(DC 17), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[comprehend languages](../../magic/spells/comprehend-languages.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[protection from evil](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md)*, *[shocking grasp](../../magic/spells/shocking-grasp.md)*, *[vanish](../../magic/spells/vanish.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[arcane mark](../../magic/spells/arcane-mark.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 9, **Con** 20, **Int** 20, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 35 (39 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Arcane Strike](../../rules/feats/arcane-strike.md), [Dazing Assault](../../rules/feats/dazing-assault.md), Flyby Attack, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Multiattack, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Wingover, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Bluff +12, Diplomacy +24, Fly +10, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (arcana, geography, history, local, nature) +20, Perception +25, Sense Motive +25, Spellcraft +24, Use Magic Device +24  
**Languages** Aklo, Aquan, Auran, Common, Draconic, Ignan  
**SQ** no breath, starflight

**Ecology**

**Environment** vacuum  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Lunar dragons frequently interact with mortals, spending long hours watching the activities occurring on planets that interest them.
