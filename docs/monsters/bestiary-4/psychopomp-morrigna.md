## Morrigna (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
N Medium outsider (extraplanar, psychopomp)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, spiritsense; Perception +28

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 13, flat-footed 25 (+8 armor, +3 Dex, +5 natural, +2 shield)  
**hp** 252 (18d10+72); regeneration 5 (acid or fire)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +15, **Will** +16  
**DR** 10/adamantine; **Immune** death effects, disease, poison; **Resist** cold 10, electricity 10; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft. (30 ft. in armor), climb 15 ft.  
**Melee** 2 slams +24 (2d6+6), 2 wrappings +19 (1d6+3 plus grab)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with wrappings)  
**Special Attacks** wrappings  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +15)  
At will—*[detect undead](../../magic/spells/detect-undead.md)*, *[stone tell](../../magic/spells/stone-tell.md)*  
5/day—*[speak with dead](../../magic/spells/speak-with-dead.md)*  
3/day—summon  
**Inquisitor Spells Known** (CL 12th; concentration +15)  
4th (3)—*[cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds.md)*, *[divination](../../magic/spells/divination.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[spell immunity](../../magic/spells/spell-immunity.md)*  
3rd (5)—*[blood biography](../../magic/spells/blood-biography.md)* (DC 16), *[dimensional anchor](../../magic/spells/dimensional-anchor.md)*, *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[halt undead](../../magic/spells/halt-undead.md)* (DC 16)  
2nd (6)—*[confess](../../magic/spells/confess.md)* (DC 15), *[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)* (DC 15), *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 15), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
1st (6)—*[bane](../../magic/spells/bane.md)* (DC 14), *[command](../../magic/spells/command.md)* (DC 14), *[comprehend languages](../../magic/spells/comprehend-languages.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[sanctuary](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md)* (DC 14), *[wrath](../../magic/spells/wrath.md)*  
0—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[disrupt undead](../../magic/spells/disrupt-undead.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[sift](../../magic/spells/sift.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 22, **Dex** 19, **Con** 18, **Int** 13, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +24 (+28 grapple); **CMD** 38  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Deflect Arrows](../../rules/feats/deflect-arrows.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Following Step](../../rules/feats/following-step.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Persuasive](../../rules/feats/persuasive.md), [Step Up](../../rules/feats/step-up.md), [Step Up and Strike](../../rules/feats/step-up-and-strike.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +15, Climb +11, Diplomacy +25, Disguise +15, Intimidate +17, Knowledge (planes) +13, Perception +28, Sense Motive +25, Sleight of Hand +10, Stealth +22, Survival +15, Swim +6  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Infernal; *speak with animals* (including vermin), *tongues*  
**SQ** change shape (any animal or humanoid), spirit touch

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Purgatory)  
**Organization** solitary of group (3–15)  
**Treasure** standard (*+2 glamered breastplate*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Spells** A morrigna casts spells as a 12th-level inquisitor.

**Spider Sight (Su)** A morrigna can see through the eyes of a spider swarm she summons as though it were the sensor of an *arcane eye* spell. She does not have to concentrate to use this ability.

**Wrappings (Su)** A morrigna’s web wrappings grant her a +2 shield bonus to AC and can make secondary natural attacks.
