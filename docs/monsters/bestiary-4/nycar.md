## Nycar (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CN Tiny dragon (aquatic)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 16, touch 15, flat-footed 13 (+3 Dex, +1 natural, +2 size)  
**hp** 39 (3d12+3); regeneration 1 (cold iron)  
**Fort** +4, **Ref** +6, **Will** +3  
**Defensive Abilities** ferocity; **DR** 5/cold iron; **Immune** paralysis, sleep; **Resist** acid 5

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +8 (1d4+3 plus poison)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.  
**Special Attacks** poison

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 16, **Con** 12, **Int** 5, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 9  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 15 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Escape Artist +13, Fly +11, Perception +5, Stealth +20, Swim +13; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Escape Artist  
**Languages** Draconic (can’t speak)  
**SQ** amphibious, elusive

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold marshes  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or clutch (3–8)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Elusive (Ex)** A nycar can make an Escape Artist check as a swift action.

**Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 12; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 acid and 1 Con damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.
