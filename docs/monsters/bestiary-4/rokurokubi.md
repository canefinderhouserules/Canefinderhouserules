## Rokurokubi (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
LE Medium monstrous humanoid  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +21  
**Aura** nightfall (15 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 16, flat-footed 22 (+6 Dex, +12 natural)  
**hp** 272 (17d10+102)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +16, **Will** +13  
**DR** 5/cold iron; **Immune** sleep; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +24 (2d6+4/19–20 plus rokurokubi’s curse), 2 claws +23 (1d6+4)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (20 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** elongate neck, rokurokubi’s curse  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 12th; concentration +17)

5th (6)—*[feeblemind](../../magic/spells/feeblemind.md)* (DC 20), *[nightmare](../../magic/spells/nightmare.md)* (DC 20)  
4th (7)—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 19), *[confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)* (DC 19), *[locate creature](../../magic/spells/locate-creature.md)*  
3rd (7)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[keen edge](../../magic/spells/keen-edge.md)*, *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*

0 (at will)—*[arcane mark](../../magic/spells/arcane-mark.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 15), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 23, **Con** 22, **Int** 15, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +21; **CMD** 37  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Deceitful](../../rules/feats/deceitful.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +26, Climb +24, Disguise +26, Intimidate +25, Perception +21, Stealth +26  
**Languages** Aklo, Common  
**SQ** no sleep

**Ecology**

**Environment** any ruins  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or harem (3–6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Elongate Neck (Ex)** A rokurokubi can stretch her neck from a normal human length up to 20 feet, giving her reach with her bite attack and threatening all squares within that range.

**Nightfall Aura (Su)** A rokurokubi can create an aura of *darkness* and *silence* (CL 12th) with a radius of 15 feet. The rokurokubi is immune to the effects of her aura, and can activate or suppress this ability as a free action. These aura effects can be dispelled as if they were spells.

**No Sleep (Ex)** A rokurokubi does not need to sleep and is immune to all sleep effects.

**Rokurokubi’s Curse (Su)** Bite—injury; *save* Will DC 23; *frequency* 1 day; *effect* 1 Cha damage and permanently silenced after 4 Cha damage. Fully healing the Cha damage cures the silence. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Spells** A rokurokubi casts spells as a 12th-level sorcerer.
