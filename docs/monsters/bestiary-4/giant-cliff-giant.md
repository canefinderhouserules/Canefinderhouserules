## Cliff Giant (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
NG Large humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** low-light vision, tremorsense 30 ft.; Perception +15

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 11, flat-footed 22 (+1 dodge, +4 armor, +1 Dex, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 182 (14d8+70)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +7, **Will** +11  
**Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **Resist** acid 10, fire 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** greatclub +17/+12 (2d8+12) or 2 slams +17 (1d8+8)  
**Ranged**  rock +12 (1d8+8)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** rock throwing (120 ft.)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +15)  
At will—*[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[know direction](../../magic/spells/know-direction.md)*  
3/day—*[animal messenger](../../magic/spells/animal-messenger.md)*, *[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[detect animals or plants](../../magic/spells/detect-animals-or-plants.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*, *[stone shape](../../magic/spells/stone-shape.md)*  
1/day—*[commune with nature](../../magic/spells/commune-with-nature.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 26, **Dex** 13, **Con** 21, **Int** 13, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +19; **CMD** 31  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Martial Weapon Proficiency (greatclub)](../../rules/feats/martial-weapon-proficiency.md), [Skill Focus (Climb)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (rock)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Climb +18, Diplomacy +8, Handle Animal +11, Knowledge (geography) +5, Knowledge (nature) +11, Perception +15, Survival +12; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Knowledge (geography)  
**Languages** Common, Giant, Terran  
**SQ** earth attunement

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm deserts or plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair or family (3–5 plus 2–3 non–combatants)  
**Treasure** standard (hide armor, greatclub, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Earth Attunement (Su)** A cliff giant has tremorsense 30 feet when standing on unworked stone or natural earth. Knowledge (nature) is a class skill for cliff giants.
