## Ved (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
NE Large humanoid (giant)  
**Init** -1; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +6

**Defense**

**AC** 16, touch 8, flat-footed 16 (–1 Dex, +8 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 57 (6d8+30)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +1, **Will** +6

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** club +11 (1d8+7)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** gale breath

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 8, **Con** 20, **Int** 7, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 9  
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +12 (+14 bull rush); **CMD** 22 (24 vs. bull rush)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Weapon Focus (club)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (club).*  
**Skills** Climb +7 (+11 when climbing rocks), Intimidate +4,
Perception +6, Survival +6 (+10 in snow); **Racial Modifiers**
+4 Climb when climbing rocks, +4 Survival in snow  
**Languages** Giant

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold hills or mountains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, gang (3–5), or clan (6–16)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Gale Breath (Su)** Every 1d4 rounds as a fullround
action, a ved can draw in a mighty
breath and expel it outward in a 30-foot cone.
Targets within the first 10-foot segment of this
cone must succeed at a DC 18 Strength check or be
knocked prone. Those who succeed at this check
and all other targets in the cone treat the effects
of the gale breath as a *gust of wind* spell. The DC
is Constitution-based.
