## Adult Astral Dragon (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
N Huge dragon (extraplanar)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** dragon senses, *see invisibility*; Perception +24  
**Aura** mental static (30 ft., DC 21), frightful presence (180 ft., DC 21)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 9, flat-footed 27 (+1 Dex, +19 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 184 (16d12+80)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +11, **Will** +17; +4 vs. psychic spells  
**Defensive Abilities** psychic resilience; **DR** 5/magic; **Immune** paralysis, sleep; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +23 (2d8+12), 2 claws +22 (2d6+8), 2 wings +20 (1d8+4), tail slap +20 (2d6+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (100-ft. line, DC 23, 12d6 force), crush  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 16th; concentration +21)  
11 PE—*[analyze aura](../../magic/spells/analyze-aura.md)* (3 PE), *[aversion](../../magic/spells/aversion.md)* (2 PE, DC 17), *[deja vu](../../magic/spells/deja-vu.md)* (1 PE), *[id insinuation I](../../magic/spells/id-insinuation-i.md)* (3 PE, DC 18), *[telekinetic projectile](../../magic/spells/telekinetic-projectile.md)* (0 PE)  
 **Psychic Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
 3rd (5/day)—*[ego whip I](../../magic/spells/ego-whip-i.md)*, *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*  
 2nd (7/day)—*[blur](../../magic/spells/blur.md)*, *[hideous laughter](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)* (DC 17), *[locate object](../../magic/spells/locate-object.md)*  
 1st (8/day)—*[burst of insight](../../magic/spells/burst-of-insight.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[sundering shards](../../magic/spells/sundering-shards.md)* (DC 16), *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*, *[unwitting ally](../../magic/spells/unwitting-ally.md)* (DC 16)  
 0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)* (DC 15), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 12, **Con** 20, **Int** 21, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 37 (41 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), Flyby Attack, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lunge](../../rules/feats/lunge.md), Multiattack, [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Appraise +24, Diplomacy +22, Fly +12, Intimidate +22, Knowledge (arcana, history, planes, religion) +24, Perception +24, Sense Motive +24, Stealth +12  
**Languages** Aklo, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal  
**SQ** change shape

Reserved and haughty, these dragons roam the Astral Plane, seeking to expand their knowledge of its esoteric secrets. They hunt to eat, and use psychic magic to fight one another.
