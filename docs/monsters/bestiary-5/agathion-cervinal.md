## Cervinal (CR 17)

**XP** 102,400  
NG Large outsider (agathion, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect scrying*, low-light vision, *see invisibility*; Perception +31

**Defense**

**AC** 32, touch 15, flat-footed 26 (+5 Dex, +1 dodge, +17 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 399 (21d10+189)  
**Fort** +20, **Ref** +14, **Will** +21; +4 vs. poison  
**DR** 10/evil and silver; **Immune** electricity, petrification; **Resist** cold 10, sonic 10; **SR** 28

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft.; gallop  
**Melee** 2 slams +30 (1d6+10), 2 hooves +25 (2d6+5)  
**Ranged**  *+3 composite longbow* +29/+24/+19/+14 (1d8+13/×3)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** powerful charge (gore, 2d8+15 plus stagger)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +24)  
Constant—*[detect scrying](../../magic/spells/detect-scrying.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
At will—*[discern lies](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[invisibility purge](../../magic/spells/invisibility-purge.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*  
5/day—*[clairaudience/clairvoyance](../../magic/spells/clairaudience-clairvoyance.md)*, *[cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds.md)*, *[dismissal* ](../../magic/spells/dismissal.md)(DC 18), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*  
3/day—*[breath of life](../../magic/spells/breath-of-life.md)*, *[mass bull’s strength](../../magic/spells/bulls-strength-mass.md)*, *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 19)  
1/day—*[discern location](../../magic/spells/discern-location.md)*, *[greater scrying* ](../../magic/spells/scrying-greater.md)(DC 21)

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 20, **Con** 26, **Int** 19, **Wis** 24, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +21; **CMB** +32; **CMD** 48 (52 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Manyshot](../../rules/feats/manyshot.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Weapon Focus (composite longbow)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative, Toughness, Iron Will.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +29, Climb +31, Diplomacy +28, Intimidate +25, Knowledge (arcana, nature, planes) +28, Perception +31, Stealth +25, Survival +28  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; *speak with animals*; truespeech  
**SQ** lay on hands (10d6, 14/day, as a 20th-level paladin), undersized weapons

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land (Nirvana)  
**Organization** solitary, collective (2-3), or herd (4-6)  
**Treasure** double (*+3 composite longbow* [+10 Str], other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Gallop (Ex)** When a cervinal uses a full-round action to run, it can move up to six times its speed.

**Stagger (Ex)** Any creature that takes damage from a cervinal’s powerful charge attack must succeed at a DC 28 Fortitude save or be staggered for 1 round. The save DC is Constitution-based.
