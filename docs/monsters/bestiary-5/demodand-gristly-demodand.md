## Gristly Demodand (CR 17)

**XP** 102,400  
CE Medium outsider (chaotic, demodand, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*, *see invisibility*; Perception +25

**Defense**

**AC** 32, touch 16, flat-footed 26 (+1 armor, +6 Dex, +15 natural)  
**hp** 368 (23d10+138); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +15, **Will** +15; +4 vs. divine spells  
**Defensive Abilities** fortification (50%); **DR** 15/good and slashing; **Immune** acid, poison; **Resist** cold 10, fire 10; **SR** 28

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 50 ft. (average)  
**Melee** +1 vicious maul +35/+30/+25/+20 (2d6+16/19-20/×3 plus 2d6) or 2 claws +33 (2d6+10)  
**Special Attacks** faith-stealing strike (DC 25), sacrilegious spittle  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +21)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[dimensional anchor](../../magic/spells/dimensional-anchor.md)*, *[fear](../../magic/spells/fear.md)* (DC 18), *[protection from good](../../magic/spells/protection-from-good.md)*, *[seek thoughts](../../magic/spells/seek-thoughts.md)* (DC 17)  
3/day—quickened *[excruciating deformation](../../magic/spells/excruciating-deformation.md)* (DC 17), *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, quickened *[pain strike](../../magic/spells/pain-strike.md)* (DC 17)  
1/day—*[mass pain strike](../../magic/spells/pain-strike-mass.md)* (DC 19), summon (level 6, 1d4 tarry demodands or 1d2 stringy demodands 55%), *[waves of exhaustion](../../magic/spells/waves-of-exhaustion.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 22, **Con** 23, **Int** 17, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 18  
**Base Atk** +23; **CMB** +33; **CMD** 49  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Critical (maul)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Intimidating Prowess](../../rules/feats/intimidating-prowess.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Lunge](../../rules/feats/lunge.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*excruciating deformation*), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*pain strike*), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Stunning Critical](../../rules/feats/stunning-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (maul)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (maul).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +21, Bluff +22, Climb +25, Fly +29, Intimidate +40, Knowledge (arcana, planes, religion) +14, Perception +25, Sense Motive +20, Spellcraft +23, Stealth +29, Survival +25  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common  
**SQ** heretical soul

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  
**Organization** solitary or hunting party (1 gristly demodand plus 2-5 tarry demodands)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk armored kilt, +1 *vicious maul*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Sacrilegious Spittle (Ex)** As a standard action once every 1d4 rounds, a gristly demodand can spit acidic mucus in a 30-foot cone. Creatures in the area take 15d6 points of acid damage (DC 27 Reflex half), and are entangled on a failed save. Entangled creatures take 5d6 points of acid damage each round at the end of their turns. Creatures that take acid damage from sacrilegious spittle are also subject to the demodand’s faith-stealing strike. Each round on its turn, an entangled creature may attempt a new saving throw to end the effect, as a full-round action that provokes attacks of opportunity. The save DC is Constitution-based.
