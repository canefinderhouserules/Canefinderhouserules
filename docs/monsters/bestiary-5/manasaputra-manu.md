## Manu (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
LG Medium outsider (extraplanar, good, lawful, manasaputra)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +25  
**Aura** unity (30 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 27, flat-footed 22 (+3 deflection, +4 Dex, +1 dodge,
+9 soul armor)  
**hp** 161 (17d10+68)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +14, **Will** +15; +2 vs. enchantment  
**Immune** calling, disease, poison; **Resist** adaptive 10; **SR** 23

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** 4 slams +18 (1d10+1)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +17; Wis-based)  
At will—*[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self only), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (self only)  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 12th; concentration +17; Wis-based)  
25 PE—*[analyze aura](../../magic/spells/analyze-aura.md)* (3 PE), *[aversion](../../magic/spells/aversion.md)* (2 PE, DC 17),
*[enshroud thoughts](../../magic/spells/enshroud-thoughts.md)* (2 PE), *[mind thrust VI](../../magic/spells/mind-thrust-vi.md)* (6 PE, DC 21),
*[psychic asylum](../../magic/spells/psychic-asylum.md)* (5 PE), *[psychic crush I](../../magic/spells/psychic-crush-i.md)* (5 PE, DC 20),
*[quintessence](../../magic/spells/quintessence.md)* (1 PE)

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 19, **Con** 18, **Int** 14, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 45  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodge](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Mobility](../../rules/feats/mobility.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), Spring
Attack, [Whirlwind Attack](../../rules/feats/whirlwind-attack.md), [Wind Stance](../../rules/feats/wind-stance.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +18, Diplomacy +23, Fly +12,
Intimidate +17, Knowledge (any one) +20,
Knowledge (history, local, planes) +20, Perception
+25, Sense Motive +22  
**Languages** Celestial, Common; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** aura of unity, creature bond, formless, no breath,
positive energy affinity, subjective appearance

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Material Plane or Positive Energy Plane)  
**Organization** solitary or round (2–8)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Aura of Unity (Su)** All allies that belong to the manu’s
creature bond work toward a unified goal while
within 30 feet of the manu. As an immediate action,
the manu can attempt an attack roll, saving throw,
or skill check to replace the roll just made by an ally
benefitting from the manu’s aura of unity.  

**Creature Bond (Ex)** Each manu is associated with a
single race or kind of creature, such as humans or
basilisks (not humanoids or magical beasts); this bond never
changes. The manu is treated as both a manasaputra and as
the race, creature type, and subtypes of its bonded creature
for the purposes of effects that target a specific race or kind
of creature.
