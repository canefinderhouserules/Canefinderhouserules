## Adult Nightmare Dragon (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
NE Large dragon (extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses, dreamsight, see in darkness; Perception +20  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 20)

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 9, flat-footed 26 (+17 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 175 (13d12+91)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +8, **Will** +14  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** mind-affecting effects, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 21

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +22 (2d6+13), 2 claws +21 (1d8+9), 2 wings +19 (1d6+4), tail slap +19 (1d8+13)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (40-ft. cone, DC 23, 12d6 acid), nightmare talons  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 13th; concentration +17)  
8 PE—*[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (0 PE), *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)* (1 PE)  
 **Psychic Spells Known** (CL 5th; concentration +8)  
 2nd (5/day)—*[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[scare* ](../../magic/spells/scare.md)(DC 15)  
 1st (7/day)—*[command* ](../../magic/spells/command.md)(DC 14), *[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)*, *[ill omen](../../magic/spells/ill-omen.md)*, *[unwitting ally* ](../../magic/spells/unwitting-ally.md)(DC 14)  
 0 (at will)—*[bleed* ](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)(DC 13), *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 13), *[detect psychic significance](../../magic/spells/detect-psychic-significance.md)*, *[lullaby* ](../../magic/spells/lullaby.md)(DC 13), *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 29, **Dex** 11, **Con** 24, **Int** 16, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +23; **CMD** 33 (37 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +20, Fly +10, Intimidate +20, Knowledge (arcana) +19, Knowledge (planes) +19, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Stealth +18, Survival +20  
**Languages** Aklo, Common, Draconic  
**SQ** change shape

These hunters of the Dimension of Dreams seek to wreak nightmares on sleepers and make existing bad dreams even more terrifying. Nightmare dragons often work with night hags in their grim collection of sleeping souls.
