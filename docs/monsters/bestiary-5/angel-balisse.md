## Balisse (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
NG Medium outsider (angel, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*; Perception +16  
**Aura** protective aura

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 13, flat-footed 18 (+2 Dex, +1 dodge, +8 natural; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 150 (10d10+50)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +7, **Will** +10; +4 vs. poison, +4 resistance vs. evil  
**DR** 10/evil; **Immune** acid, cold, petrification; **Resist** electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 19

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** +1 flaming heavy mace +15/+10 (1d8+4 plus 1d6 fire)  
**Special Attacks** brand of the impenitent  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +14)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
At will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[dispel evil](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)* (DC 19), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[holy smite](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)* (DC 18), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)* (self only), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (self only),[ *remove curse](../../magic/spells/remove-curse.md)*, *[remove disease](../../magic/spells/remove-disease.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*  
3/day—*[cure serious wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md)*, *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 16)  
1/day—*[atonement](../../magic/spells/atonement.md)*, *[mark of justice](../../magic/spells/mark-of-justice.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 15, **Con** 18, **Int** 13, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 18  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +13; **CMD** 26  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Weapon Focus (heavy mace)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (heavy mace), Toughness.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +15, Diplomacy +17, Fly +19, Knowledge (planes) +14, Knowledge (religion) +14, Perception +16, Sense Motive +16  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech

**Ecology**

**Environment** any good-aligned planes  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** double (+1 *flaming heavy mace*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Brand of the Impenitent (Su)** Three times per day, a balisse can brand a target within 30 feet (Will DC 19 negates) with a painless, glowing icon on its chest—usually the holy symbol of the deity or empyreal lord the balisse serves. This brand lasts for a number of days equal to the balisse’s Hit Dice (10 days for most balisses). Anyone who attacks the branded target gains a +2 sacred bonus on weapon attack and damage rolls and a +2 sacred bonus on caster level checks to overcome the target’s spell resistance. The save DC is Charisma-based.
