## Ancient Dream Dragon (CR 19)

**XP** 204,800  
N Gargantuan dragon (extraplanar)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** dragon senses, dreamsight; Perception +44  
**Aura** frightful presence (300 ft., DC 30)

**Defense**

**AC** 38, touch 5, flat-footed 38 (–1 Dex, +33 natural, –4 size)  
**hp** 337 (25d12+175)  
**Fort** +21, **Ref** +15, **Will** +22  
**Defensive Abilities** dream fortress; **DR** 15/magic; **Immune** paralysis, sleep; **SR** 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 250 ft. (clumsy)  
**Melee** bite +33 (4d6+16), 2 claws +32 (2d8+11), 2 wings +30 (2d6+5), tail slap +30 (2d8+16)  
**Space** 20 ft., **Reach** 15 ft. (20 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, DC 29, 20d6), crush, sleeping breath 3/day, tail sweep  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 25th; concentration +33)  
19 PE—*dream* (4 PE), *dream travel* (5 PE), *euphoric cloud* (2 PE, DC 21), *invisibility* (2 PE), *lullaby* (0 PE, DC 19), *mirror strike* (1 PE), *thought shield II*

 0 (at will)—*arcane mark*, *dancing light*, *detect magic*, *daze* (DC 17), *ghost sound* (DC 17), *mage hand*, *mending*, *message*, *resistance*

**Statistics**

**Str** 33, **Dex** 9, **Con** 24, **Int** 24, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 26  
**Base Atk** +25; **CMB** +40; **CMD** 49 (53 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), Snatch, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), Wingover
