## Young Astral Dragon (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
N Large dragon (extraplanar)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** dragon senses, *see invisibility*; Perception +16  
**Aura** mental static (30 ft., DC 16)

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 12, flat-footed 19 (+1 dodge, +2 Dex, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 150 (10d12+30)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +9, **Will** +10; +4 vs. psychic spells  
**Defensive Abilities** psychic resilience; **Immune** paralysis, sleep; **SR** 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +15 (2d6+7), 2 claws +14 (1d8+5), 2 wings +12 (1d6+2), tail slap +12 (1d8+7)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (80-ft. line, DC 18, 6d6 force)  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  
5 PE—*[deja vu](../../magic/spells/deja-vu.md)* (1 PE), *[telekinetic projectile](../../magic/spells/telekinetic-projectile.md)* (0 PE)  
**Psychic Spells Known** (CL 1st; concentration +4)  
 1st (4/day)— *[burst of insight](../../magic/spells/burst-of-insight.md)*, *[sundering shards](../../magic/spells/sundering-shards.md)* (DC 14)  
0 (at will)—*[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)* (DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 21, **Dex** 14, **Con** 16, **Int** 17, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 29 (33 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Multiattack, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Appraise +16, Diplomacy +14, Fly +9, Intimidate +14, Knowledge (arcana, history, planes) +16, Perception +16, Sense Motive +16  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic
