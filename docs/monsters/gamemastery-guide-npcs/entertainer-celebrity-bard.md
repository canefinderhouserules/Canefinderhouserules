## Entertainer (Celebrity Bard) (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
Human bard 11  
N Medium humanoid  
**Init** +2; **Senses** Perception +10

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 17 (+5 armor, +2 Dex, +2 shield)  
**hp** 118 (11d8+30)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +10, **Will** +8; +4 vs. bardic performance, language–dependent, and sonic

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** dagger +10/+5 (1d4+2/19–20)  
**Ranged**  mwk light crossbow +11 (1d8/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** bardic music 30 rounds/day (countersong, dirge of doom, distraction, fascinate, inspire competence +4, inspire courage +3, inspire greatness, suggestion)  
**Bard Spells Known** (CL 11th; concentration +17)  
4th (3/day)—*[dominate person](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md)* (DC 20), *[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*, *[rainbow pattern](../../magic/spells/rainbow-pattern.md)* (DC 20)  
3rd (5/day)—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 19), *[crushing despair](../../magic/spells/crushing-despair.md)* (DC 19), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)* (DC 19)  
2nd (6/day)—*[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 18), *[minor image](../../magic/spells/minor-image.md)* (DC 18), *[silence](../../magic/spells/silence.md)* (DC 18), *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 17), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[disguise self](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[hideous laughter](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)* (DC 17), *[identify](../../magic/spells/identify.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 8, **Dex** 14, **Con** 14, **Int** 12, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 19  
**Feats** [Arcane Strike](../../rules/feats/arcane-strike.md), [Craft Wondrous Item](../../rules/feats/craft-wondrous-item.md), [Greater Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-penetration.md), [Magical Aptitude](../../rules/feats/magical-aptitude.md), [Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/spell-penetration.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Linguistics +5, Perception +10, Perform (act) +20, Perform (dance) +20, Perform (sing) +20, Sense Motive +5, Sleight of Hand +10, Spellcraft +19, Stealth +15, Use Magic Device +24  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Elven  
**SQ** bardic knowledge +5, jack-of-all-trades (use any skill), lore master 2/day, versatile performance (act, dance, sing)  
**Combat Gear**  *scrolls of align weapon*, *see invisibility*, *mount*, *wand of shocking grasp* (CL 4, 50 charges); **Other Gear** *+1 chain shirt*, *+1 buckler*, masterwork light crossbow with 10 bolts, daggers (2), *cloak of resistance +1*, *headband of alluring charisma +4*, 2,500 gp in jewelry

**Boon** Knowing a celebrity bard can give PCs a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy checks in a region for 1 month.

A celebrity bard is a legendary performer, and the wealthy and powerful clamor for her to grace them with a performance. Celebrity bards often entertain at the request of a queen or general (CR 12).
