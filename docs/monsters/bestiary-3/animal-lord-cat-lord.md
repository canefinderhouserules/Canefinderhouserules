## Cat Lord (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
Human animal lord ranger 10  
NG Medium outsider (native, shapechanger)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +17

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 18, flat-footed 18 (+3 armor, +1 deflection, +6 Dex, +1 dodge, +4 natural)  
**hp** 139 (10d10+80)  
**Fort** +13, **Ref** +14, **Will** +9  
**Defensive Abilities** evasion; **DR** 10/silver

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.; leap  
**Melee** bite +16 (1d6+5 plus grab), 2 claws +15 (1d3+5)  
**Ranged**  *+1 seeking composite longbow* +18/+13 (1d8+6/×3)  
**Special Attacks** favored enemy (evil outsiders +6, giants +2, undead +2), pounce, rake (2 claws +15, 1d3+5)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +12)  
At will—*[charm animal* ](../../magic/spells/charm-animal.md)(cats only, DC 13)  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +11)  
3rd—*[greater magic fang](../../magic/spells/magic-fang-greater.md)*  
2nd—*[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[hold animal* ](../../magic/spells/hold-animal.md)(DC 16)  
1st—*[charm animal* ](../../magic/spells/charm-animal.md)(DC 15), *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, *[pass without trace](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 24, **Con** 22, **Int** 12, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +17 (+21 grapple); **CMD** 34  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Shot on the Run](../../rules/feats/shot-on-the-run.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Endurance and Toughness are one feat, Toughness; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Agile Maneuvers is part of Weapon Finesse; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite), Improved Initiative, Iron Will.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +17 (+22 when jumping), Climb +26, Handle Animal +15, Knowledge (nature) +14, Perception +17, Sense Motive +14, Stealth +20 (+24 in undergrowth), Survival +17; **Racial Modifiers** +5 Acrobatics when jumping, +4 Stealth in undergrowth  
**Languages** Common, Sylvan; *speak with animals* (cats only)  
**SQ** change shape (leopard; *shapechange*), favored terrain (jungle +4, plains +2), hunter’s bond (leopard), swift tracker, track +5, wild empathy +12, woodland stride

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm jungles  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** NPC gear (*+1 leather armor*, *+1 seeking composite longbow* [+5 Str] with 20 arrows, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *ring of protection +1*, other treasure)
