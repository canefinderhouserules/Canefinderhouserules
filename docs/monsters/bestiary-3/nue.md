## Nue (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
NE Large magical beast  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +10

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 15, flat-footed 18 (+5 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 126 (12d10+60)  
**Fort** +13, **Ref** +13, **Will** +7

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +18 (2d6+6 plus energy drain), bite +18 (1d4+6 plus poison), 2 claws +17 (1d6+6)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** energy drain (2 levels, DC 16), pounce  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +12)  
3/day—*[contagion* ](../../magic/spells/contagion.md)(DC 14), *[hold person* ](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)(DC 13)  
1/day—*[nightmare* ](../../magic/spells/nightmare.md)(DC 15), *[shout* ](../../magic/spells/shout.md)(DC 14), *[waves of fatigue](../../magic/spells/waves-of-fatigue.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 22, **Dex** 21, **Con** 20, **Int** 7, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +19; **CMD** 35 (39 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Climb +13, Perception +10, Stealth +11  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** cloud form

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm forests or mountains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or ambush (3–6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Cloud Form (Su)** A nue can change into the form of a 10-foot black cloud or back to its normal form as a standard action. A nue in cloud form is otherwise treated as if under the effects of *gaseous form*, except that it obscures vision like *fog cloud*.

**Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 21; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 Strength damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
