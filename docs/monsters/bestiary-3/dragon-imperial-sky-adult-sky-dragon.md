## Adult Sky Dragon (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
LG Huge dragon (air)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** cloud sight, dragon senses; Perception +30  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 8, flat-footed 29 (+21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 272 (16d12+80)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +12, **Will** +17  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** electricity, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +22 (2d8+12/19–20), 2 claws +22 (2d6+8), gore +22 (2d6+12), tail slap +20 (2d6+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite and gore)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d8 electricity damage, DC 23), crush (DC 23, 2d8+12)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +21)  
At will—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[gust of wind* ](../../magic/spells/gust-of-wind.md)(DC 17)  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (5/day)—*[blink](../../magic/spells/blink.md)*, *[lightning bolt* ](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)(DC 18)  
2nd (7/day)—*[glitterdust* ](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)(DC 17), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[shocking grasp](../../magic/spells/shocking-grasp.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[disrupt undead](../../magic/spells/disrupt-undead.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 21, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 36 (40 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +16 (+20 when jumping), Diplomacy +24, Fly +19, Heal +24, Knowledge (arcana, geography, religion) +24, Perception +30, Perform (sing) +21, Sense Motive +24, Spellcraft +24  
**Languages** Auran, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal, Sylvan  
**SQ** borne aloft, change shape

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm mountains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Benevolent and noble, sky dragons, or tienlungs, are fearsome champions of good and protectors of those in need. They are often sought out for their wise council, which they grant only to the deserving and true.
