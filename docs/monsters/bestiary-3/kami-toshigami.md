## Toshigami (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
NG Medium outsider (kami, native)  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., deathwatch, true seeing; Perception +25

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 20, flat-footed 22 (+6 Dex, +2 dodge, +2 insight, +10 natural)  
**hp** 230 (20d10+120); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +23, **Will** +16  
**DR** 10/cold iron and evil; **Immune** bleed, mind-affecting effects, petrification, polymorph; **Resist** acid 10, electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 26

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft. (30 ft. without *haste*), fly 90 ft. (good, 60 ft. without *haste*)  
**Melee** *+2 quarterstaff* +24/+24/+19/+14/+9 (1d6+4 plus touch of ages), *+2 quarterstaff* +24/+19/+14 (1d6+3 plus touch of ages) or touch +23 (touch of ages)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +27)  
Constant—*[deathwatch](../../magic/spells/deathwatch.md)*, *[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[foresight](../../magic/spells/foresight.md)*, *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[speak with plants](../../magic/spells/speak-with-plants.md)*, *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
At will—*[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[tree shape* ](../../magic/spells/tree-shape.md)(cherry tree only), *[tree stride* ](../../magic/spells/tree-stride.md)(self plus 50 lbs. of objects only)  
3/day—*[greater restoration](../../magic/spells/restoration-greater.md)*, *[slow* ](../../magic/spells/slow.md)(DC 20)  
1/day—*[finger of death* ](../../magic/spells/finger-of-death.md)(DC 24), *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)*, *[waves of exhaustion](../../magic/spells/waves-of-exhaustion.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 15, **Dex** 22, **Con** 20, **Int** 15, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 25  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 42  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (quarterstaff)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Agile Maneuvers is part of Weapon Finesse; Greater Two-Weapon Fighting and Improved Two-Weapon Fighting are one feat, Improved Two-Weapon Fighting; in their place: Toughness.*  
**Skills** Bluff +30, Diplomacy +30, Fly +20, Heal +25, Knowledge (nature) +25, Perception +25, Sense Motive +25, Stealth +29, Survival +25  
**Languages** Common; *speak with plants*, telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** merge with ward, ward (cherry tree)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard (*+2 quarterstaff*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Touch of Ages (Su)** When a toshigami hits a target with a touch attack or any melee weapon, the target must succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save to avoid being magically aged. When a toshigami strikes a foe, it must decide whether it wishes to physically increase the target’s age or mentally reduce the target’s age. If it increases the target’s physical age, the target takes 1 point of Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution drain. If it decreases the target’s mental age, the target takes 1 point of Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma drain. Combined with the toshigami’s numerous attacks, this can quickly devastate a target, but once a target succeeds at its saving throw against the touch of ages, it can no longer be affected by this ability for 1 round. This is a magical aging effect, but does not actually alter the victim’s true age—it merely simulates the creeping effect of age on the flesh or the reversion to an increasingly infantile state of mind. The save DC is Charisma-based.
