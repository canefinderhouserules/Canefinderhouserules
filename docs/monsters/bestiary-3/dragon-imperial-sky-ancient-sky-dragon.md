**Statistics**

**Str** 35, **Dex** 8, **Con** 25, **Int** 24, **Wis** 25, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +24; **CMB** +40; **CMD** 49 (53 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Lunge](../../rules/feats/lunge.md), Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)

**Languages** Auran, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Elven, Gnome, Infernal, Sylvan  
**SQ** borne aloft, change shape

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm mountains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Benevolent and noble, sky dragons, or tienlungs, are fearsome champions of good and protectors of those in need. They are often sought out for their wise council, which they grant only to the deserving and true.
