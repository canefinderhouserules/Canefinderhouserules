## Skunk (CR 1/4)

**XP** 100  
N Tiny animal  
**Init** +2; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 12 (+2 Dex, +2 size)  
**hp** 7 (1d8–1)  
**Fort** +1, **Ref** +4, **Will** +1

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +4 (1d3+2), 2 claws +4 (1d2+2)  
**Ranged**  spray +4 touch (musk)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 2-1/2 ft.  
**Special Attacks** musk

**Statistics**

**Str** 3, **Dex** 15, **Con** 9, **Int** 2, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 6  
**Base Atk** +0; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 6  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Perception +5

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or surfeit (3–5)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Musk (Ex)** Up to twice per day, a skunk can spray a stream of noxious musk at a single target within 10 feet as a standard action. With a successful ranged touch attack, the creature struck by this spray must make a DC 11 Fortitude save or be nauseated 1 for 1d4 rounds and then sickened for 1d4 minutes by the horrific stench. A successful save reduces the effect to only 1d4 rounds of being sickened. A creature cannot use the scent ability as long as it is affected by this musk. The save DC is Constitution-based, and includes a +2 racial bonus.
