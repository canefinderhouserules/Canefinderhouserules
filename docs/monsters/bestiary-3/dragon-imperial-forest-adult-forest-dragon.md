## Adult Forest Dragon (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
CE Huge dragon (earth)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses, tremorsense 60 ft.; Perception +23  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 21)

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 8, flat-footed 30 (+22 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 229 (17d12+119)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +12, **Will** +15  
**DR** 2/adamantine; **Immune** paralysis, poison, sleep; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 20 ft., climb 30 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +25 (2d8+13), 2 claws +24 (2d6+9), gore +24 (2d6+13), tail slap +22 (2d6+13)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite and gore)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d6 piercing damage, DC 24), crush (DC 24, 2d8+13)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +20)  
At will—*[blight* ](../../magic/spells/blight.md)(DC 18), *[entangle* ](../../magic/spells/entangle.md)(DC 14), *[pass without trace](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md)*  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
3rd (5/day)—*[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*, *[stinking cloud* ](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)(DC 16)  
2nd (7/day)—*[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*, *[hideous laughter* ](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)(DC 15), *[touch of idiocy](../../magic/spells/touch-of-idiocy.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[hypnotism* ](../../magic/spells/hypnotism.md)(DC 14), *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)(DC 14), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*  
0 (at-will)—*[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*, *[touch of fatigue](../../magic/spells/touch-of-fatigue.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 29, **Dex** 10, **Con** 22, **Int** 16, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +28; **CMD** 38 (42 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Improved Natural Armor, [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Lightning Reflexes.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +8 (+11 when jumping), Bluff +23, Climb +37, Escape Artist +2, Fly +12, Intimidate +23, Knowledge (arcana, nature) +14, Perception +23, Spellcraft +23, Stealth +22, Survival +16  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Sylvan  
**SQ** change shape, sound imitation, woodland stride

**Ecology**

**Environment** any forest  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple

Forest dragons, or dilung, are fickle and malevolent dragons that dwell in deep, rugged woodlands. While a forest dragon can fly, it prefers to stalk the earth, flying only to pursue objects of its wrath.
