## Tarry Demodand (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
CE Medium outsider (chaotic, demodand, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*; Perception +22

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 13, flat-footed 23 (+6 armor, +3 Dex, +7 natural)  
**hp** 171 (18d10+72)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +11, **Will** +12; +4 vs. divine spells  
**DR** 10/good and magic; **Immune** acid, poison; **Resist** cold 10, fire 10; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 40 ft. (average)  
**Melee** mwk short sword +27/+22/+17/+12 (1d6+9/19–20), mwk short sword +27/+22/+17 (1d6+4/19–20), bite +22 (1d8+4)  
**Special Attacks** adhesion, faith-stealing strike (DC 22)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th; concentration +16)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*  
3/day—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*  
1/day—*[chaos hammer* ](../../magic/spells/chaos-hammer.md)(DC 17), summon (level 4, 1d2 tarry demodands 40%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 28, **Dex** 21, **Con** 18, **Int** 12, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +27; **CMD** 42 (46 vs. disarm)  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Greater Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/greater-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Lunge](../../rules/feats/lunge.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (short sword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +16, Climb +19, Fly +23, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (arcana) +14, Knowledge (planes) +14, Perception +22, Sense Motive +14, Stealth +17, Survival +14  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common  
**SQ** heretical soul

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or patrol (3–8)  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork breastplate, 2 masterwork short swords, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Adhesion (Ex)** A creature striking a tarry demodand with a manufactured weapon must make a DC 23 Reflex save; failure means the weapon sticks to the demodand and cannot be used to make attacks until freed. Freeing a stuck weapon requires a successful grapple check. This adhesion grants a +4 racial bonus to CMD against disarm attempts.
