## Slimy Demodand (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CE Medium outsider (chaotic, demodand, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*, *see invisibility*; Perception +2  
**Aura** stench (DC 26, 1d6 rounds)

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 13, flat-footed 27 (+6 armor, +3 Dex, +11 natural)  
**hp** 336 (21d10+126)  
**Fort** +18, **Ref** +13, **Will** +14; +4 vs. divine spells  
**DR** 10/good and magic; **Immune** acid, poison; **Resist** cold 10, fire 10; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 40 ft. (average)  
**Melee** bite +31 (1d10+9 plus 2d6 acid), 2 claws +30 (2d6+13/19–20 plus 2d6 acid and grab)  
**Special Attacks** acid, dread claws, faith-stealing strike (DC 23)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +19)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[detect thoughts* ](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)(DC 15), *[fear* ](../../magic/spells/fear.md)(DC 17)  
3/day—*[acid fog](../../magic/spells/acid-fog.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*  
1/day—summon (level 6, 1d4 tarry demodands 50%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 28, **Dex** 18, **Con** 23, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +21; **CMB** +30 (+34 grapple); **CMD** 44  
**Feats** [Bleeding Critical](../../rules/feats/bleeding-critical.md), [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), Flyby Attack, [Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md), [Improved Critical (claw)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Intimidating Prowess](../../rules/feats/intimidating-prowess.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +8, Bluff +27, Climb +16, Fly +25, Intimidate +36, Knowledge (arcana) +13, Knowledge (planes) +13, Sense Motive +26, Spellcraft +20, Stealth +25, Survival +20  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common  
**SQ** heretical soul

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  
**Organization** solitary or slaving party (2 slimy demodands and 2–5 tarry demodands)  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork breastplate, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Acid (Su)** A slimy demodand is coated in an ever-dripping layer of acid that deals an extra 2d6 points of acid damage on a successful natural attack. In addition, opponents that successfully strike a slimy demodand with an unarmed strike or natural attack take 2d6 points of acid damage.

**Dread Claws (Ex)** A slimy demodand adds 1-1/2 times its strength bonus on all attack rolls made when using its claws.
