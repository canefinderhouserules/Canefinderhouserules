## Great Cyclops (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
CE Huge humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +1; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +22

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 10, flat-footed 25 (+1 dodge, +4 armor, +1 Dex, +13 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 272 (17d8+136)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +6, **Will** +14

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft. (35 ft. in armor)  
**Melee** mwk greatclub +25/+20/+15 (3d8+19), gore +18 (1d8+6) or gore +23 (1d8+13), 2 slams +23 (2d6+13)  
**Ranged**  rock +12 (2d6+19)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** powerful charge (gore, 4d8+24), rock throwing (120 ft.)

**Statistics**

**Str** 36, **Dex** 13, **Con** 25, **Int** 7, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +27; **CMD** 39  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (greatclub)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greatclub), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Perception +22  
**Languages** Common, Cyclops, Giant  
**SQ** flash of brutality

**Ecology**

**Environment** any temperate or tropical  
**Organization** solitary, colony (2–5), or tribe (6–14)  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork greatclub, hide armor, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Flash of Brutality (Su)** Once per day as a swift action, a great cyclops can gain a burst of savage of inspiration. When it does, it doubles the threat range of all weapons, natural attacks, and rock attacks it makes until the start of its next turn. Furthermore, once per day, when the great cyclops reaches 0 or fewer hit points and is conscious because of its Diehard feat, this ability recharges, allowing it to use the ability a second time that same day.
