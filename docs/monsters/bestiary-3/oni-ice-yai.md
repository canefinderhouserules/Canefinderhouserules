## Ice Yai (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
CE Large outsider (cold, oni, giant, native, shapechanger)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +22

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 12, flat-footed 24 (+4 armor, +2 Dex, +1 dodge, +11 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 272 (16d10+112); regeneration 5 (fire or acid)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +7, **Will** +12  
**Immune** cold; **SR** 25  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to fire

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** 4 slams +25 (2d8+9)  
**Ranged**  icy missile +17 touch (4d6 cold)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** staggering strikes  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 18th; concentration +22)  
Constant—*[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*  
At will—*[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only)  
3/day— *[charm monster* ](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)(DC 18), *[cone of cold* ](../../magic/spells/cone-of-cold.md)(DC 19), *[deep slumber* ](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)(DC 17), *[gaseous form* ](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)(self only)  
1/day—*[polar ray](../../magic/spells/polar-ray.md)*, *[solid fog](../../magic/spells/solid-fog.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 29, **Dex** 14, **Con** 25, **Int** 12, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 18  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 39  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (slam)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (slam), Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Bluff +23, Disguise +14, Fly +25, Intimidate +23, Knowledge (arcana) +11, Perception +22, Sense Motive +13, Spellcraft +8, Stealth +17 (+21 in snow), Use Magic Device +14; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth in snow  
**Languages** Common, Giant  
**SQ** change shape (Medium or Large humanoid; *alter self* or *giant form I*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold mountains  
**Organization** solitary or gang (1 plus 4–16 frost giants)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Icy Missile (Su)** As a swift action, the ice yai can fire a dart of ice from its third eye. This dart is a ranged touch attack (+20 attack bonus), dealing 4d6 points of cold damage on a hit. This attack has a range of 180 feet with no range increment.

**Staggering Strikes (Ex)** An ice yai can strike twice per round with its two slam attacks. A creature struck by more than two of these slam attacks in a round must make a DC 27 Fortitude save or be staggered for 1 round. The save DC is Strength-based.
