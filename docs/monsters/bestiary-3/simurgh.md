## Simurgh (CR 18)

**XP** 153,600  
NG Gargantuan magical beast  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*, *detect magic*, low-light vision; Perception +27  
**Aura** peace (50 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 34, touch 10, flat-footed 30 (+3 Dex, +1 dodge, +24 natural, –4 size)  
**hp** 432 (24d10+192)  
**Fort** +22, **Ref** +17, **Will** +14  
**Immune** ability damage, ability drain, disease, fire, negative energy, petrification, poison, sleep; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10, electricity 10; **SR** 29

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 120 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +33 (2d8+12 plus 2d6 fire), 2 claws +32 (2d8+12 plus 2d6 fire), tail slap +30 (1d4+6 plus banishing swipe)  
**Ranged** glaring ray +23 touch (20d6 fire)  
**Space** 20 ft., **Reach** 20 ft.  
**Special Attacks** banishing swipe, glaring ray, radiant feathers  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +22)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
At will—*[daylight](../../magic/spells/daylight.md)*, *[zone of truth* ](../../magic/spells/zone-of-truth.md)(DC 18)  
3/day—*[flame strike* ](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)(DC 21), *[mass cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds-mass.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 34, **Dex** 16, **Con** 27, **Int** 16, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 23  
**Base Atk** +24; **CMB** +40; **CMD** 54  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, Snatch, Wingover, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +26, Fly +7, Heal +14, Knowledge (arcana) +33, Knowledge (dungeoneering) +21, Knowledge (all others) +15, Perception +27, Perform (sing) +19, Survival +14; **Racial Modifiers** +10 Knowledge (all)  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic; tongues

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm deserts or mountains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Aura of Peace (Su)** Creatures within a 50-foot spread from a simurgh feel a sensation of peace wash over them, as if affected by *calm emotions*, except the simurgh can choose which creatures are affected. A DC 28 Will save negates the effects of this aura for 1 round, but a new save must be made each round to continue to resist the effects. The saving throw is Charisma-based.

**Banishing Swipe (Su)** A simurgh can use its radiant tail to return creatures to their native planes. In addition to taking damage, any extraplanar creature touched by a simurgh’s tail must succeed at a DC 28 Will save or be affected as if by *banishment*. A creature that makes this save cannot be affected by the same simurgh’s banishing swipe for the next 24 hours. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Glaring Ray (Su)** A simurgh can blast a fiery ray of brilliant light from its eyes as a standard action to a range of 100 feet.

**Radiant Feathers (Su)** Once per day as a standard action, a simurgh can fan out its glimmering tail feathers and blast its foes with a 100-foot cone of radiant light from its tail. Aside from its size, this attack is identical to a *prismatic spray* (DC 28). The save is Charisma-based.
