# Monsters by CR

Each Challenge Rating has its own page listing every creature and NPC on the wiki at that CR, from all the bestiaries, codexes, other sources and third-party books. Mythic creatures are listed under their CR with their mythic rank (MR) next to the name. Templates and creatures without a fixed CR are left out.
