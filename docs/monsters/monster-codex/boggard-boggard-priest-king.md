**XP** 12,800  
Boggard cleric 10

**hp** 166 (13d8+62)

**Speed** 15 ft., swim 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 morningstar* +15/+10 (1d8+5), tongue +8 touch (sticky tongue)
