## Frost Giant Ice Mage (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Frost giant sorcerer 6  
CE Large humanoid (cold, giant)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +25

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 10, flat-footed 26 (+2 armor, +1 deflection, +10 natural, +4 shield, –1 size)  
**hp** 288 (20 HD; 14d8+6d6+120)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +8, **Will** +11  
**Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **Immune** cold  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to fire

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** mwk morningstar +22/+17/+12 (2d6+12) or 2 slams +20 (1d8+8 plus 1d6 cold)  
**Ranged**  rock +12 (1d8+12 plus 1d6 cold)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** rock throwing (120 ft.)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +9)  
7/day—cold steel (grant weapon or 50 ammunition *frost* weapon special ability for 3 rounds)  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 6th; concentration +9)  
3rd (4/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*  
2nd (6/day)—*[frigid touch](../../magic/spells/frigid-touch.md)*, *[ice slick](../../magic/spells/ice-slick.md)*, *[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)* (DC 16)  
1st (7/day)—*[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 13), *[flare](../../magic/spells/flare.md)* (DC 13), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[touch of fatigue](../../magic/spells/touch-of-fatigue.md)* (DC 13)  
**Bloodline** boreal

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** A frost giant ice mage casts *shield* and *expeditious retreat*.  
 **During Combat** A frost giant ice mage uses his *wand of solid fog* to trap opponents, then blasts them at range with his *wand of ice storm* or *wand of lightning bolt*. He casts *ice slick* and uses his icewalker bloodline ability to outmaneuver his opponents in icy and snowy areas.

**Statistics**

**Str** 26, **Dex** 11, **Con** 22, **Int** 14, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +22; **CMD** 33  
**Feats** [Arcane Strike](../../rules/feats/arcane-strike.md), [Born of Frost](../../rules/feats/born-of-frost.md), [Chilled Rock](../../rules/feats/chilled-rock.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Extend Spell](../../rules/feats/extend-spell.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/improved-lightning-reflexes.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (morningstar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (morningstar).*  
**Skills** Bluff +12, Climb +21, Craft (alchemy) +15, Intimidate +16, Knowledge (arcana) +15, Perception +25, Spellcraft +15, Stealth +12 (+16 in snow); **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth in snow  
**Languages** Abyssal, Auran, Common, Giant  
**SQ** icewalker  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of cure serious wounds* (2), *potion of invisibility*, *potion of protection from energy* (fire), *wand of ice storm* (10 charges), *wand of lightning bolt* (10 charges), *wand of solid fog* (5 charges), alchemist’s fire (4); **Other Gear** mwk morningstar, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *bracers of armor +2*, *ring of protection +1*, 362 gp

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold mountains
