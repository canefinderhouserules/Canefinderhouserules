## Juggernaut Beetle (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
N Huge magical beast  
**Init** –1; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., low-light vision; Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 7, flat-footed 29 (–1 Dex, +22 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 210 (20d10+100)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +11, **Will** +7  
**Defensive Abilities** selective mind; **Immune** acid, mind-affecting effects  
**Weaknesses** light sensitivity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +29 (4d10+15/19–20)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 8d6 acid damage, Reflex DC 25 half, usable once every 1d4 rounds), trample (2d8+15, DC 30)

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 9, **Con** 20, **Int** 2, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +32 (+34 bull rush); **CMD** 41 (43 vs. bull rush or trip)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Stunning Critical](../../rules/feats/stunning-critical.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Climb +23, Perception +14

**Ecology**

**Environment** any underground  
**Organization** single or pair  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Selective Mind (Su)** To anyone but duergar, these beetles count as mindless. Duergar can affect them with mind-affecting effects, and train and control them as if the beetles were animals.
