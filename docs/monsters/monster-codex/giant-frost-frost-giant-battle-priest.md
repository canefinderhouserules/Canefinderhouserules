## Frost Giant Battle Priest (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
Frost giant cleric 8  
CE Large humanoid (cold, giant)  
**Init** –1; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +30

**Defense**

**AC** 27, touch 9, flat-footed 27 (+8 armor, +1 deflection, –1 Dex, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 272 (22d8+96)  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +7, **Will** +16  
**Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **Immune** cold  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to fire

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 battleaxe* +29/+24/+19/+14 (2d6+13/×3)  
**Ranged**  rock +14 (1d8+18)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** channel negative energy 5/day (DC 16, 4d6), might of the gods (+8, 8 rounds/day), rock throwing (120 ft.), weapon master (8 rounds/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +14)  
9/day—strength surge (+4)  
9/day—battle rage (+4)  
**Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +14)  
4th—*[blessing of fervor](../../magic/spells/blessing-of-fervor.md)*, *[communal protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy-communal.md)*, *[divine power](../../magic/spells/divine-power.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[unholy blight](../../magic/spells/unholy-blight.md)* (DC 20)  
3rd—*[enter image](../../magic/spells/enter-image.md)*, *[invisibility purge](../../magic/spells/invisibility-purge.md)*, *[magic vestment](../../magic/spells/magic-vestment.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*, *[sacred bond](../../magic/spells/sacred-bond.md)*  
2nd—*[align weapon](../../magic/spells/align-weapon.md)*, *[death knell](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)* (DC 18), *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 18), *[magic boulder](../../magic/spells/magic-boulder.md)*, *[spiritual weapon](../../magic/spells/spiritual-weapon.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[weapon of awe](../../magic/spells/weapon-of-awe.md)*  
1st—*[bane](../../magic/spells/bane.md)* (DC 17), *[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[command](../../magic/spells/command.md)* (DC 17), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)*<sup>D</sup> (DC 17), *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  
**D** domain spell; **Domains** Strength, War

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** A battle priest casts *bull’s strength*, *magic vestment*, and *protection from energy* (fire) before combat, and sacred bond if her jarl is present.  
 **During Combat** The battle priest opens combat by casting *divine power*, then uses spells like *blessing of fervor* to support allies and *unholy blight* to harm opponents.

**Statistics**

**Str** 34, **Dex** 8, **Con** 18, **Int** 13, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +29 (+31 sunder); **CMD** 39 (41 vs. sunder)  
**Feats** [Brew Potion](../../rules/feats/brew-potion.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](../../rules/feats/craft-magic-arms-and-armor.md), [Craft Wand](../../rules/feats/craft-wand.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Martial Weapon Proficiency (battleaxe)](../../rules/feats/martial-weapon-proficiency.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (battleaxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (battleaxe).*  
**Skills** Climb +17, Craft (armor) +10, Intimidate +15, Knowledge (religion) +14, Perception +30, Spellcraft +7, Stealth +1 (+5 in snow); **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth in snow  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Giant  
**SQ** icicle 9/day (1d6+3)  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of bull’s strength* (2), *potions of cure serious wounds* (4), *potions of protection from energy* (2), *potion of water breathing*, *wand of cure critical wounds* (15 charges), *wand of cure moderate wounds* (15 charges); **Other Gear** mwk chainmail, *+1 battleaxe*, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *boots of the winterlands*, *headband of inspired wisdom +2*, *ring of protection +1*, *ring of sustenance*, golden bracelets for sacred bond (2), silver unholy symbol, spell component pouch, 4,410 gp

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold mountains
