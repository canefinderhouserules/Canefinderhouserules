## Bugbear Cultist (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Bugbear oracle 6  
CE Medium humanoid (goblinoid)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., scent; Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 14, flat-footed 18 (+5 armor, +3 Dex, +1 dodge, +3 natural)  
**hp** 96 (9d8+24)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +9, **Will** +6

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 morningstar* +10/+5 (1d8+4)  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 6th; concentration +8)  
3rd (3/day)—*[bestow curse](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)* (DC 16), *[inflict serious wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-serious-wounds.md)* (DC 15), *[magic vestment](../../magic/spells/magic-vestment.md)*  
 2nd (6/day)—*[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*, *[inflict moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-moderate-wounds.md)* (DC 14), *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*, *[summon monster II](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-ii.md)*  
 1st (7/day)—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)*, *[inflict light wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-light-wounds.md)* (DC 13), *[protection from good](../../magic/spells/protection-from-good.md)*, *[ray of sickening](../../magic/spells/ray-of-sickening.md)* (DC 14), *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)* (DC 13)  
 0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 13), *[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[spark](../../magic/spells/spark.md)*, *[virtue](../../magic/spells/virtue.md)*  
 **Mystery** battle

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The bugbear casts *magic vestment* on her chain shirt.  
 **During Combat** The bugbear casts *shield of faith* on herself and *enlarge person* on either herself or a tough ally. She then uses *bestow curse* and *ray of sickening* to harry her enemies and cause them misery.  
 **Base Statistics** Without *magic vestment*, the bugbear’s statistics are **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 16.

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 16, **Con** 15, **Int** 12, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +9; **CMD** 21  
**Feats** [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Spell Focus (necromancy)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Intimidate +18, Knowledge (religion) +7, Perception +14, Spellcraft +7, Stealth +18, Survival +5  
**Languages** Common, Giant, Goblin, Infernal  
**SQ** oracle’s curse (tongues), revelations (battlefield clarity 1/day, surprising charge 1/day), stalker  
**Combat Gear**  *wand of cure moderate wounds* (25 charges); **Other Gear** mwk chain shirt, mwk morningstar, *cloak of resistance +1*, spell component pouch, 186 gp

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate mountains
