## Kobold Devilspeaker (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Kobold cleric of Asmodeus 8  
LE Small humanoid (reptilian)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 14, flat-footed 19 (+4 armor, +3 Dex, +1 natural, +3 shield, +1 size)  
**hp** 72 (8d8+8)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +5, **Will** +9  
**Weaknesses** light sensitivity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk dagger +5/+0 (1d3–3/19–20)  
**Ranged**  dagger +10 (1d3–3/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** channel negative energy 5/day (DC 16, 4d6), staff of order (4 rounds, 1/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +11)  
6/day—*copycat* (8 rounds), *touch of law*  
At will—master’s illusion (8 rounds/day)  
**Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +11)  
4th—*[aura of doom](../../magic/spells/aura-of-doom.md)* (DC 17), *[confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)*<sup>D</sup> (DC 17), *[cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds.md)*  
3rd—*[animate dead](../../magic/spells/animate-dead.md)*, *[blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)* (DC 16), *[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*, *[magic circle against chaos](../../magic/spells/magic-circle-against-chaos.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[summon monster III](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-iii.md)*  
2nd—*[death knell](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)* (DC 15), *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 15), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[lesser animate dead](../../magic/spells/animate-dead-lesser.md)*, *[protective penumbra](../../magic/spells/protective-penumbra.md)*  
1st—*[bane](../../magic/spells/bane.md)* (DC 14), *[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[disguise self](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[moment of greatness](../../magic/spells/moment-of-greatness.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[sanctuary* ](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md)(DC 14)  
0 (at will)—*[bleed* ](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)(DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*  
**D** domain spell; **Domains** Law, Trickery

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The devilspeaker casts *invisibility* on herself.  
 **During Combat** The devilspeaker stays invisible as long as possible, casting spells such as *animate dead*, *aura of doom*, *bless*, *cure critical wounds*, *deeper darkness*, *lesser animate dead*, and *summon monster III* as well as using her *wand of cure light wounds* or channeling negative energy to heal undead.

**Statistics**

**Str** 4, **Dex** 16, **Con** 10, **Int** 10, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +2; **CMD** 15  
**Feats** [Channeled Shield Wall](../../rules/feats/channeled-shield-wall.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Selective Channeling](../../rules/feats/selective-channeling.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**Skills** Craft (trapmaking) +2, Heal +14, Perception +5, Profession (miner) +5, Spellcraft +11, Stealth +18; **Racial Modifiers** +2 Craft (trapmaking), +2 Perception, +2 Profession (miner)  
**Languages** Draconic  
**SQ** crafty  
**Combat Gear**  *feather token* (whip), *pearl of power* (2nd level), *wand of cure light wounds*; **Other Gear** *+1 studded leather*, *+1 light wooden shield*, dagger, mwk dagger, various onyx gems worth 200 gp

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate underground or deep forest
