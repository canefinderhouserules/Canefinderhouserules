## Bugbear Bloodmage (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
Bugbear sorcerer 10  
CE Medium humanoid (goblinoid)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., scent; Perception +0

**Defense**

**AC** 18, touch 13, flat-footed 15 (+2 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural)  
**hp** 146 (13 HD; 3d8+10d6+49)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +11, **Will** +9  
**Resist** fire 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 quarterstaff* +13/+8 (1d6+7)  
**Ranged**  mwk shortbow +10 (1d6/×3)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +12)  
6/day—*elemental ray* (1d6+5 fire)  
1/day—*elemental blast* (10d6 fire, DC 18)  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  
5th (3/day)—*[fire snake](../../magic/spells/fire-snake.md)* (DC 18)  
4th (5/day)—*[black tentacles](../../magic/spells/black-tentacles.md)*, *[elemental body I](../../magic/spells/elemental-body-i.md)*, *[fire shield](../../magic/spells/fire-shield.md)*  
3rd (6/day)—*[fireball](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)* (DC 16), *[howling agony](../../magic/spells/howling-agony.md)* (DC 16), *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*, *[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)*, *[stinking cloud](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)* (DC 16)  
2nd (7/day)—*[alter self](../../magic/spells/alter-self.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[levitate](../../magic/spells/levitate.md)*, *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*, *[touch of idiocy](../../magic/spells/touch-of-idiocy.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[burning hands](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)* (DC 14), *[color spray](../../magic/spells/color-spray.md)* (DC 14), *[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)* (DC 14), *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)* (DC 14), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[acid splash](../../magic/spells/acid-splash.md)*, *[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)* (DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 13), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*, *[touch of fatigue](../../magic/spells/touch-of-fatigue.md)* (DC 13)  
**Bloodline** elemental (fire)

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The bloodmage casts *invisibility* and positions herself where she can hurl spells with impunity.  
 **During Combat** At the start of the battle, the bloodmage casts *fire snake* and *fireball* on her foes. She reserves spells such as *fire shield* and *shield* to protect herself when she becomes the target of attacks.

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 15, **Con** 16, **Int** 8, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 24  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Empower Spell](../../rules/feats/empower-spell.md), [Enlarge Spell](../../rules/feats/enlarge-spell.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Weapon Focus (quarterstaff)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; in their place: Weapon Focus (quarterstaff).*  
**Skills** Intimidate +6, Spellcraft +12, Stealth +9  
**Languages** Common, Goblin  
**SQ** bloodline arcana (change energy damage spells to fire), stalker  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*; **Other Gear** *+1 quarterstaff*, mwk dagger, mwk shortbow with 20 arrows, *amulet of natural armor +2*, *cloak of resistance +1*, *headband of alluring charisma +2*, 117 gp

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate mountains
