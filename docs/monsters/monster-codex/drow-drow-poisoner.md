**Defense**

**AC** 23, touch 16, flat-footed 18 (+6 armor, +1 deflection, +5 Dex, +1 natural)  
**hp** 81 (12d8+24)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +14, **Will** +5; +2 vs. enchantment  
**Immune** sleep, poison; **SR** 18  
**Weaknesses** light blindness

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk rapier +10/+5 (1d6/18–20 plus poison)
