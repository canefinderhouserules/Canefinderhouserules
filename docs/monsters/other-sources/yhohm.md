## Yhohm (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
NG Tiny outsider (extraplanar, fire, good)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, *detect poison*; Perception +10  
**Aura** shroud of flame (10 ft., DC 14, 10 rounds)

**Defense**

**AC** 18, touch 15, flat-footed 15 (+2 Dex, +1 dodge, +3 natural, +2 size)  
**hp** 60 (5d10+10); regeneration 1 (cold or evil)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +3, **Will** +6; +4 vs. poison  
**Defensive Abilities** self-resurrection; **DR** 5/evil; **Immune** fire, petrification; **Resist** electricity 10; **SR** 15  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 10 ft., fly 60 ft. (average)  
**Melee** 2 talons +9 (1d2 plus 1d6 fire), bite +9 (1d3 plus 1d6 fire)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.  
**Special Attacks** holy fire  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +7)  
Constant—detect poison  
At will—*[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[purify food and drink](../../magic/spells/purify-food-and-drink.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*, *[virtue](../../magic/spells/virtue.md)*  
3/day—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[daylight](../../magic/spells/daylight.md)*, *[flaming sphere* ](../../magic/spells/flaming-sphere.md)(DC 14)  
1/day—*[dimension door* ](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)(self only), *[lesser restoration](../../magic/spells/restoration-lesser.md)*, *see invisibiliity*

**Statistics**

**Str** 10, **Dex** 15, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +5; **CMB** +5; **CMD** 18  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Fly +14, Heal +10, Knowledge (religion) +8, Perception +10, Perform (sing) +10, Stealth +18  
**Languages** Celestial

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Nirvana)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or flight (3–5)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Holy Fire (Su)** Like a *flame strike*, half the fire damage from a yhohm’s fire attacks is fire damage; the other half is divine power and is not subject to fire immunity or resistance.

**Self-Resurrection (Su)** A slain yhohm remains dead for only 1d4 rounds unless its body is completely destroyed by an effect such as *disintegrate*. Otherwise, a fully healed yhohm emerges from the remains 1d4 rounds after death, as if brought back to life via *resurrection*. The yhohm gains 1 permanent negative level when this occurs. A yhohm can self-resurrect only once per year. If a yhohm dies a second time before that year passes, its death is permanent. A yhohm that dies within the area of a *desecrate* spell cannot self-resurrect until the *desecrate* effect ends, at which point the yhohm immediately resurrects. A yhohm brought back to life by other means never gains negative levels as a result.

**Shroud of Flame (Su)** A yhohm can cause its feathers to burst into fire as a free action. As long as its feathers are burning, it deals an additional 1d6 points of fire damage with each natural attack, and any creature within 5 feet must attempt a DC 14 Reflex save each round or take 1d6 points of fire damage at the start of its turn. A creature that attacks the yhohm with a natural or non-reach melee weapon takes 1d6 points of fire damage (no save) with each successful hit. The save DC is Constitution-based.
