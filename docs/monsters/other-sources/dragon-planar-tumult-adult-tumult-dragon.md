## Adult Tumult Dragon (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CN Huge dragon (chaotic, extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +28  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 25)

**Defense**

**AC** 31, touch 8, flat-footed 31 (+23 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 400 (20d12+160)  
**Fort** +20, **Ref** +12, **Will** +17  
**Defensive Abilities** adaptive form; **DR** 5/lawful; **Immune** paralysis, sleep, warpproof; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., climb 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor), swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +29 (2d8+15), 2 claws +28 (2d6+10), tail slap +26 (2d6+15), 2 wings +27 (1d8+5)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d8 damage, DC 28), crush, entropic breath, flexible breath, vexing wings  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (5/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[sands of time](../../magic/spells/sands-of-time.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[eagle’s splendor](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md)*, *[grace](../../magic/spells/grace.md)*, *[shatter](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)* (DC 17)  
1st (8/day)—*[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[entropic shield](../../magic/spells/entropic-shield.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[ray of sickening](../../magic/spells/ray-of-sickening.md)* (DC 16), *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 15), *[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*, *[spark](../../magic/spells/spark.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 10, **Con** 27, **Int** 18, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +32 (+36 dirty trick); **CMD** 42 (44 vs. dirty trick, 46 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Greater Dirty Trick](../../rules/feats/greater-dirty-trick.md), Hover, [Improved Dirty Trick](../../rules/feats/improved-dirty-trick.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Multiattack, [Weapon Focus (bite](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), wing)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +20, Bluff +28, Climb +18, Fly +15, Knowledge (local, nature, planes) +27, Perception +28, Perform (dance) +25, Spellcraft +27, Swim +18, Use Magic Device +28  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Protean  
**SQ** planar infusion (180 ft.)
