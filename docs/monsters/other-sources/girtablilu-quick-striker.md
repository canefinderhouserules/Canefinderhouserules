## Quick Striker (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Girtablilu rogue (scout) 8  
N Large monstrous humanoid  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 30 ft.; Perception +25

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 15, flat-footed 22 (+3 armor, +5 Dex, +1 dodge, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 280 (18 HD; 8d8+10d10+98)

**Melee** *+1 spear* +21/+16/+11/+6 (1d8+7/×3 plus poison), 2 claws +17 (1d6+2 plus grab), sting +17 (1d6+2 plus poison)  
**Ranged**  *+1 composite shortbow* +22/+17/+12/+7 (1d6+5/×3 plus poison) or *+1 spear* +23/+18/+13/+8 (1d8+7/×3 plus poison)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** constrict (1d6+4), poison (DC 24), scout’s charge, skirmisher, sneak attack +4d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 18th, concentration +19)  
1/day—*[summon nature’s ally V](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-v.md)* (1d3 giant scorpions)

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 23, **Con** 21, **Int** 14, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +21 (+25 grapple); **CMD** 38 (50 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Shot on the Run](../../rules/feats/shot-on-the-run.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Weapon Focus (spear)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Acrobatics +22 (+30 when jumping), Bluff +9, Climb +20, Craft (alchemy) +16, Disable Device +15, Escape Artist +14, Heal +13, Knowledge (geography, history) +7, Knowledge (local) +15, Perception +25, Sense Motive +16, Stealth +26, Survival +24; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Climb, +4 Perception, +4 Stealth  
**Languages** Common, Girtablilu, Gnoll, Kelish

**Combat Gear**  *potions of cure serious wounds* (2), *potion of haste*, *potions of invisibility* (3), alchemist’s fire (2), antitoxin (3), smokesticks (5), thunderstones (3); **Other Gear** studded leather, *+1 spear*, *+1 composite shortbow* with 30 arrows, *amulet of natural armor +2*, *belt of incredible dexterity +2*, *elixirs of hiding* (2), portable alchemist’s lab, tindertwigs (10)
