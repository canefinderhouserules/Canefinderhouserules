## Savage Great Cyclops (CR 18)

**XP** 153,600  
Barbarian 6  
CE Huge humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +23

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 9, flat-footed 23 (+1 dodge, +4 armor, +2 Dex, +13 natural, –2 rage, –2 size)  
**hp** 368 (23 HD; 17d8+6d12+253)  
**Fort** +21, **Ref** +11, **Will** +19  
**Defensive Abilities** improved uncanny dodge, trap sense +2

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft.  
**Melee** mwk Huge greatclub +35/+30/+25/+20 (3d8+25), gore +33 (1d8+8) or gore +33 (1d8+17), 2 slams +33 (2d6+17)  
**Ranged**  rock +18 (2d6+19)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** powerful charge (gore, 4d8+24), rage (23 rounds/day), rage powers (knockback, night vision, quick reflexes), rock throwing (120 ft.)

**Tactics**

**Base Statistics** When not raging, the barbarian’s statistics are **AC** 27, touch 10, flat-footed 25; **hp** 322; **Fort** +19, **Will** +17; **Str** 40, **Con** 29; **CMB** 35, **CMD** 48; **Skills** Climb +21.

**Statistics**

**Str** 44, **Dex** 15, **Con** 33, **Int** 6, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +37; **CMD** 47  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (greatclub)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility, Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Climb +23, Intimidate +14, Perception +23  
**SQ** fast movement, flash of brutality

**Ecology**

**Environment** any temperate or tropical  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork greatclub, hide armor, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Flash of Brutality (Su)** Once per day as a swift action, a great cyclops can gain a burst of savage inspiration. When it does, it doubles the threat range of all attacks using weapons, natural attacks, and rock attacks it makes until the start of its next turn. Furthermore, once per day when the great cyclops reaches 0 or fewer hit points and is conscious because of its Diehard feat, this ability recharges, allowing the great cyclops to use the ability a second time that day.
