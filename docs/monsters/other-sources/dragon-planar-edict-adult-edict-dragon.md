## Adult Edict Dragon (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
LN Huge dragon (extraplanar, lawful)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +30  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 25)

**Defense**

**AC** 32, touch 7, flat-footed 32 (–1 Dex, +23 natural, +2 shield, –2 size)  
**hp** 270 (20d10+140)  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +13, **Will** +19  
**Defensive Abilities** parapet wings; **DR** 5/chaotic; **Immune** disease, fear, paralysis, poison, sleep; **SR** 29

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +28 (2d8+15/19–20), 2 claws +28 (2d6+10), tail slap +23 (scrivener), 2 wings +29 (1d8+10)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (100-foot line, 12d10 slashing damage, Reflex DC 27 half), crush, hindering shards, powerful wings, scrivener, tail sweep  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (5/day)—*[bestow curse](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)* (DC 18), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[calm emotions](../../magic/spells/calm-emotions.md)* (DC 17), *[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[command](../../magic/spells/command.md)* (DC 16), *[comprehend languages](../../magic/spells/comprehend-languages.md)*, *[detect chaos](../../magic/spells/detect-chaos.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*, *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[purify food and drink](../../magic/spells/purify-food-and-drink.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 8, **Con** 25, **Int** 20, **Wis** 25, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +32 (+34 reposition); **CMD** 45 (47 vs. reposition, 49 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), Hover, [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Reposition](../../rules/feats/improved-reposition.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Repositioning Strike](../../rules/feats/repositioning-strike.md), [Stand Still](../../rules/feats/stand-still.md), [Weapon Focus (wing)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Diplomacy +28, Fly +14, Knowledge (engineering, history, local, nobility, planes) +28, Linguistics +28, Perception +30, Sense Motive +30, Spellcraft +28  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, plus any 20 others; truespeech  
**SQ** planar infusion (180 ft.)
