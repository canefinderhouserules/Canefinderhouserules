## Onyx Scourge (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CE Huge outsider (earth, elemental, extraplanar)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., see in darkness, tremorsense 60 ft.; Perception +27

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 10, flat-footed 28 (+2 Dex, +20 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 231 (22d10+110)  
**Fort** +18, **Ref** +11, **Will** +15  
**DR** 10/—; **Immune** death effects, elemental traits, energy drain, negative energy

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 20 ft.; earth glide  
**Melee** 2 slams +32 (2d12+12/19–20 plus 3d6 negative energy)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** channel negative energy (8d6, DC 19, 4/day), entropic strikes, negative energy capacitance  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +17)  
3/day—*waves of exhaustion* (DC 18)

**Statistics**

**Str** 34, **Dex** 14, **Con** 21, **Int** 12, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +22; **CMB** +36 (+40 bull rush, +38 sunder); **CMD** 48 (50 vs. bull rush or sunder)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Greater Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/greater-bull-rush.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (slam)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Intimidating Prowess](../../rules/feats/intimidating-prowess.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Sundering Strike](../../rules/feats/sundering-strike.md)  
**Skills** Appraise +26, Climb +37, Intimidate +38, Knowledge (dungeoneering, planes) +26, Perception +27, Stealth +19  
**Languages** Terran

**Ecology**

**Environment** any underground (Plane of Earth)  
**Organization** solitary or pair  
**Treasure** incidental

**Special Abilities**

**Channel Negative Energy (Su)** As a standard action, an onyx scourge can channel negative energy in a 30-foot burst as a 16th-level evil cleric. This ability requires no divine focus. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Entropic Strikes (Ex)** An onyx scourge’s slam attack deals an additional 3d6 points of negative energy damage. When used against an object, its slam attack ignores the first 5 points of hardness.

**Negative Energy Capacitance (Su)** An onyx scourge’s body stores negative energy, making it immune to death effects, energy drain, and negative energy. Additionally, if an onyx scourge is targeted by a negative energy attack, the next time it channels negative energy within 1 minute, it deals 50% more damage, as though this were an empowered spell.
