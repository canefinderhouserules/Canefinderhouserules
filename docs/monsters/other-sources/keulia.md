## Keulia (CR 1/4)

**XP** 100  
N Tiny animal  
**Init** +1; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 13, touch 13, flat-footed 12 (+1 Dex, +2 size)  
**hp** 4 (1d8)  
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +3, **Will** +1

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., burrow 5 ft.  
**Melee** gore –1 (1d3–3)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.

**Statistics**

**Str** 5, **Dex** 12, **Con** 11, **Int** 1, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 7  
**Base Atk** 0; **CMB** -1; **CMD** 6 (10 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md)  
**Skills** Escape Artist +3, Perception +5, Stealth +11  
**SQ** custom camouflage

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm forests  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or masquerade (3–30)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Custom Camouflage (Ex)** While in an area with ample debris, a keulia can spend 1 hour adhering pieces of debris to its scales. This grants the keulia a +2 racial bonus on Stealth checks while in that environment. A keulia can shed its current camouflage with 1 hour of work; otherwise, it sheds it gradually over the course of 1d6 weeks.
