## Litanu (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
CE Medium undead (aquatic)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +20

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 15, flat-footed 20 (+4 Dex, +1 dodge, +10 natural)  
**hp** 147 (14d8+84)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +8, **Will** +12  
**Defensive Abilities** channel resistance +4, deep dweller; **Immune** undead traits; **Resist** cold 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 10 ft., swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** mwk trident +20/+15 (1d8+8), 4 tentacles +16 (1d4+4 plus paralysis)  
**Special Attacks** paralysis (1d4+1 rounds, DC 19), seclusion empowerment, sequestering paralysis

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 17, **Con** —, **Int** 15, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 33  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), Multiattack, [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (trident)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (trident).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +18 (+10 when jumping), Craft (traps) +16, Intimidate +22, Perception +20, Sense Motive +13, Stealth +27, Swim +26  
**Languages** Aquan, Common  
**SQ** amphibious

**Ecology**

**Environment** any oceans  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or school (3-8)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk trident, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Deep Dweller (Ex)** Litanus are immune to damage from water pressure.

**Seclusion Empowerment (Su)** Litanus draw strength from other creatures’ feelings of isolation and loneliness. A litanu gains a +1 profane bonus on saving throws, attack rolls, and weapon damage rolls for each non-litanu creature within 60 feet that does not have line of sight to an ally, including any creature affected by the litanu’s sequestering paralysis. These profane bonuses stack, up to a maximum equal to the litanu’s Charisma modifier (maximum +5 for a normal litanu).

**Sequestering Paralysis (Su)** Creatures affected by a litanu’s paralysis cannot breathe and begin to suffocate, and they are blinded and deafened. Although effects such as *[remove blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/remove-blindness-deafness.md)* can remove the blinded or deafened condition from a creature paralyzed by a litanu, this does not remove the paralysis. This ability is a necromancy and paralysis effect.
