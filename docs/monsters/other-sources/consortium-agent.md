**Treasure** NPC gear (chain shirt, *+1 rapier*, *potions of cure light wounds* [2], antitoxin, oil of taggit [2], sassone leaf residue [2], other treasure)

The city of monuments’s relatively loose laws and the affluence of the Vista district bring members of the mercenary consortium to the city in droves. Their primary base of operations in the frontier is located in this district, a bronze-faced building with heavy doors emblazoned with sigils of three intertwined asps. The mercenary consortium has had a presence in the city of monuments for nearly a century, though agents have had to proceed more cautiously of late, following the recent founding of a Pathfinder lodge in the city.

Consortium agents in the city of monuments favor no particular class, and agents from all walks of life can be found throughout the city. Some are rogues or fighters, while others practice the arcane arts as sorcerers or wizards. Quite a few, however, come from the ranks of bards, utilizing their various skills, social graces, and musical talents to covertly extort buyers, to beat Pathfinders to major finds, or simply to steal such treasures out from under Pathfinders’ very noses.

As with Consortium headquarters in other parts of the known world, the leadership of the city of monuments’s Consortium is broken up into a strict hierarchy of power. The lowest and most common members are paid mercenaries who partake in the various odd jobs assigned by true Consortium agents, whether such tasks involve pillaging Rune Empire ruins or taking care of pesky competitors who would seek to hinder the Consortium’s endeavors. Bronze agents are made up of the city of monuments’s dockworkers, freelancers, and lowly adventurers seeking money and improved status, while those agents wielding silver emblems organize the activities of their underlings throughout the regions surrounding the city. Only a couple gold agents operate in the city of monuments, though their influence and power extends to nearly all the corners of the frontier, and their word is law as far as lesser agents are concerned.

While the NPC depicted here represents a city-based agent who excels at espionage, deceit, and other urbane tactics, those working for the mercenary consortium in the city of monuments take myriad forms. The company has enlisted warlords and wizards alike, and even some adventuring groups find their way onto the Consortium’s payroll—all it takes is the gumption and means to find lost treasures and bring them back to the auction house, as well as a certain lack of ethics and a strong sense of cupidity for those who wish to rise in rank.
