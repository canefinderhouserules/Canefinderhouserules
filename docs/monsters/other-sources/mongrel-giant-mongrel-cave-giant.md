## Mongrel Cave Giant (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
CE Large humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., low-light vision; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 9, flat-footed 19 (+4 armor, +6 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 99 (9d8+27)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +3, **Will** +3  
**Defensive Abilities** ferocity, improved rock catching  
**Weaknesses** light sensitivity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** battleaxe +12/+7 (2d6+9/×3) or 2 slams +11 (1d8+6)  
**Ranged**  rock +6 (1d8+9)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** improved rock throwing (140 ft.)

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 10, **Con** 17, **Int** 6, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 7  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +13 (+15 sunder); **CMD** 23 (25 vs. sunder)  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (battleaxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Climb +7, Intimidate +7, Perception +5, Stealth –7 (–3 in rocky terrain); **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth in rocky terrain  
**Languages** Giant  
**SQ** axe wielder, stone giant ancestry (improved rock catching, improved rock throwing)

**Special Abilities**

**Axe Wielder (Ex)** All cave giants are proficient with handaxes, battleaxes, and greataxes.
