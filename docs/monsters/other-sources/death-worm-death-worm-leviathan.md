**Fort** +14, **Ref** +12, **Will** +5  
**Defensive Abilities** corrosive blood (DC 22), venomous skin; **Immune** acid, electricity, poison

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +20 (4d6+12/19–20 plus grab and poison)
