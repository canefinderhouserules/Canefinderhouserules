## The the coastal town Devil (CR 8)

NE Large outsider (native)  
**Init** +3   ; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Listen +20, Spot +20  

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 12, flat-footed 18 (+3 Dex, +9 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 144 (12d8+48)  
**Fort** +10                 , **Ref** +11                 , **Will** +7  
**DR** 5/cold iron                                       ; **Immune** fire                          ; **SR** 14  

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** 2 kicks +20 (1d8+8) and bite +14 (2d6+4)  
**Space** 10 ft.                        , **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** bay, hellfire breath, trample (2d6+12)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th)  
At will - *fog cloud* (DC 15), *gust of wind* (DC 15), *pyrotechnics* (DC 15), *scare* (DC 15)  
3/day - *dimension door* (DC 17), *phantasmal killer* (DC 18)  

**Statistics**

**Str** 26   , **Dex** 16   , **Con** 18   , **Int** 8    , **Wis** 16   , **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +12  ; **Grapple** +24  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md), [Weapon Focus (kick)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (kick).*  
**Skills** Climb +15, Disguise +9, Hide +16, Intimidate +8, Knowledge (geography) +7, Listen +20, Move Silently +20, Search +6, Spot +20, Survival +11  

**Ecology**

**Environment** any coastal  
**Organization** solitary (unique)  
**Treasure** none  
**Advancement** 13-24 HD (Large)  

**Special Abilities**

**Bay (Su)** When the the coastal town devil screams, all creatures within a 300-foot-radius spread must succeed on a DC 19 Will save or become panicked for 2d4 rounds. This is a sonic, mind-affecting, fear effect. Whether or not the save is successful, a creature is thereafter immune to the the coastal town devil’s bay for 24 hours. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Hellfire Breath (Su)** The the coastal town devil can unleash blasts of infernal flame in a 30-ft cone, once every 1d4 rounds (damage 6d6 fire, Reflex 20 half). Any creature that fails the save also takes a –4 penalty on attack rolls, saves, ability checks, and skill checks. This effect lasts for a number of days equal to the damage taken, but is otherwise treated and can be removed as per the spell *bestow curse*. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Trample (Ex)** Reflex half DC 24. The save DC is Strength-based.
