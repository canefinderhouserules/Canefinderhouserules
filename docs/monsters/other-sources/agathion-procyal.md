## Procyal (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
NG Medium outsider (agathion, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +16

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 16, flat-footed 18 (+2 Dex, +8 natural)  
**hp** 140 (10d10+40)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +9, **Will** +6; +4 vs. poison  
**Immune** electricity, petrification; **Resist** cold 10, sonic 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 short sword* +12/+7 (1d6+2/19–20) or 2 claws +11 (1d4+1)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  
Constant—*[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
At will—*[calm emotions](../../magic/spells/calm-emotions.md)* (DC 15), *[magic aura](../../magic/spells/magic-aura.md)* (DC 14), *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 16)  
3/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 17), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[heroism](../../magic/spells/heroism.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[shadow conjuration](../../magic/spells/shadow-conjuration.md)* (DC 17)  
1/day—*[blood biography](../../magic/spells/blood-biography.md)*(DC 16), *[dominate person](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md)* (DC 18), *[spiritual ally](../../magic/spells/spiritual-ally.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 15, **Con** 18, **Int** 18, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +11 (+15 dirty trick); **CMD** 23 (25 vs. dirty trick)  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Deceitful](../../rules/feats/deceitful.md), [Greater Dirty Trick](../../rules/feats/greater-dirty-trick.md), [Improved Dirty Trick](../../rules/feats/improved-dirty-trick.md), [Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/spell-penetration.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +20, Diplomacy +16, Disguise +20, Knowledge (local) +14, Knowledge (planes) +17, Perception +16, Perform (act) +16, Sense Motive +16, Sleight of Hand +15, Stealth +15  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic, Halfling; *speak with animals*, truespeech  
**SQ** fabricated forgery, imitator

**Ecology**

**Environment** any urban (Nirvana)  
**Organization** solitary or party (3–4)  
**Treasure** standard (*+1 short sword*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Fabricated Forgery (Su)** Procyal agathions are able to flawlessly forge signatures and legal documents even if they have never seen them before. They can use Bluff in place of Linguistics when forging the signature of or a document written by the creature whose form they are currently assuming.

**Imitator (Su)** Procyal agathions can use the change shape special quality as per the *alter self* spell, but they can assume only the forms of any specific individual they have met at least once. They are unable to assume any other form beyond their natural shape.
