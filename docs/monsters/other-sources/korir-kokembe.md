## Korir-Kokembe (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
N Huge dragon  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +18  
**Aura** frightful presence (30 ft., DC 18)

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 10, flat-footed 22 (+2 Dex, +14 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 136 (13d12+52)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +12, **Will** +10  
**Immune** disease, dragon traits, magic paralysis and sleep

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., fly 80 ft. (good), swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** bite +18 (2d6+10 plus disease and grab), 2 claws +18 (2d6+7/19–20 plus grab), tail slap +13 (2d6+3 plus grab)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft. (20 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** constrict (2d6+7), grab (Gargantuan), rake (2 claws +18, 2d6+7/19–20)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th; concentration +15)  
Constant—*freedom of movement*  
At will—*repel vermin* (DC 16), *vomit swarm*<sup>APG</sup> (must wait 1d4 rounds before using this ability again)  
3/day—*entangle* (DC 13)  
1/day—*creeping doom* (DC 19), *insect plague*

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 15, **Con** 18, **Int** 10, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +22 (+26 grapple); **CMD** 34 (46 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Critical (claw)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), Wingover  
**Skills** Climb +21, Fly +15, Intimidate +15, Knowledge (nature) +13, Perception +18, Spellcraft +10, Stealth +10 (+18 in jungles), Survival +10, Swim +25; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Stealth in jungles  
**Languages** Draconic  
**SQ** compression

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm jungles (Jungle Expanse)  
**Organization** solitary or nest (2–5)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Disease (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 20; *onset* 1 round; *frequency* 1 day; *effect* 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Str damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.
