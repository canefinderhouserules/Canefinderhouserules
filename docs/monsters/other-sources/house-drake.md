## House Drake (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CG Tiny dragon  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*; Perception +7

**Defense**

**AC** 16, touch 14, flat-footed 14 (+2 Dex, +2 natural, +2 size)  
**hp** 30 (2d12+6)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +5, **Will** +7  
**Defensive Abilities** ferocious will; **Immune** paralysis, sleep

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 50 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +6 (1d4+2), sting +6 (1d4+2)  
**Space** 0 ft., **Reach** 0 ft. (5 ft. with tail)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon, silver strike  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 3rd; concentration +5)  
Constant—*[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
3/day—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*  
1/day—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)* (CL 12th), *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 11, **Dex** 15, **Con** 17, **Int** 12, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 12  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +4, Fly +15, Knowledge (local) +6, Perception +7, Sense Motive +7, Stealth +15, Survival +7  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Infernal

**Ecology**

**Environment** urban (the walled city)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or clutch (3-9)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Breath Weapon (Su)** Up to three times per day and no more than once every 1d4 rounds, a house drake can breathe out a small cloud of silver mist in a 10-foot cone. All creatures in this area must succeed at a DC 14 Will save or be confused for 1 round. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Ferocious Will (Ex)** House drakes have a stubborn streak of fierce independence and gain a +2 racial bonus on Will saves (this bonus is included in the stats above). In addition, the first time in a day that a house drake successfully saves against a mind-affecting spell or spelllike ability, the caster of the spell must succeed at a DC 13 Will save or be staggered for 1 round by a blast of magical, mental feedback. This feedback is a mind-affecting effect, and the save DC is Charisma-based.

**Silver Strike (Ex)** All of a house drake’s natural attacks are treated as being silver for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction.
