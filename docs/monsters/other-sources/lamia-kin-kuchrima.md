## Kuchrima (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
CE Medium monstrous humanoid  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 23, touch 17, flat-footed 16 (+1 dodge, +6 Dex, +6 natural)  
**hp** 154 (11d10+44)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +13, **Will** +6  
**Immune** disease, *magic missile*

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +17 (1d6+5 plus disease), 2 talons +16 (1d4+5)  
**Ranged**  mwk Large composite longbow +18/+13/+8 (2d6+5/×3)  
**Special Attacks** catastrophic shot, disease

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 22, **Con** 19, **Int** 7, **Wis** 9, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +16 (+20 grapple); **CMD** 33  
**Feats** Improved Initiative, [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), Skill Focus (Perception), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Fly +24, Perception +19  
**Languages** Common, Giant, Rune Empire  
**SQ** oversized weapon

**Ecology**

**Environment** any mountains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, flight (3–7), or gluttony (8–12)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Catastrophic Shot (Ex)** Whenever a kuchrima
 makes only a single attack in a round with a bow, it
 increases the critical threat range of that shot to 18–20.

**Disease (Ex)** Bite—Filth Fever; *save* Fort DC 19; *onset* 1d3
 days; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1d3 Dex damage and 1d3 Con
 damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.

**Oversized Weapon (Ex)** A kuchrima can wield an oversized
 longbow in its talons while flying without incurring any
 penalty—this longbow can be one size category larger
 than normal (Large for most kuchrimas).
