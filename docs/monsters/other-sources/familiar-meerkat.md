## Meerkat (CR 1/4)

**XP** 100  
N Tiny animal  
**Init** +2; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +1

**Defense**

**AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 12 (+2 Dex, +2 size)  
**hp** 9 (1d8+1)  
**Fort** +3, **Ref** +4, **Will** +1  
**Immune** scorpion venom

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 5 ft.  
**Melee** bite +4 (1d3+2)  
**Space** 2-1/2 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.  
**Special Attacks** mob

**Statistics**

**Str** 5, **Dex** 14, **Con** 13, **Int** 2, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +0; **CMB** +5; **CMD** 7 (11 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Stealth +14

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, mob (3–16), or clan (17–30)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Immunity to Scorpion Venom (Ex)** Meerkats are immune to the poison of all scorpions.

**Mob (Ex)** If two meerkats in the same square attack the same foe, they are considered to be flanking that foe as if they were in two opposite squares.
