## Crimson Whale (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
N Gargantuan animal  
**Init** +2; **Senses** blindsense 120 ft., low-light vision; Perception +21

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 4, flat-footed 22 (–2 Dex, +18 natural, –4 size)  
**hp** 140 (10d8+60)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +7, **Will** +6

**Offense**

**Speed** swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** bite +17 (2d8+26 plus grab)  
**Space** 20 ft., **Reach** 20 ft.  
**Special Attacks** swallow whole (4d6 damage, AC 19, 10 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 36, **Dex** 7, **Con** 21, **Int** 2, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 6  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +24 (+28 grapple); **CMD** 32 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Perception +21, Swim +29; **Racial Modifiers** +12 Perception  
**SQ** hold breath, powerful bite

**Ecology**

**Environment** any ocean  
**Organization** single, pair, or pod (3–8)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Hold Breath (Ex)** A leviathan can hold its breath for a number of rounds equal to 6 times its Constitution score before it risks drowning.

**Powerful Bite (Ex)** A leviathan applies twice its Strength modifier to bite damage.
