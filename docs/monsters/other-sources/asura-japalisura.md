## Japalisura (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
LE Medium outsider (asura, evil, extraplanar, lawful)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*; Perception +25  
**Aura** elusive (75 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 18, flat-footed 20 (+1 dodge, +7 Dex, +10 natural)  
**hp** 210 (14d10+70); regeneration 5 (good spells, good weapons)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +16, **Will** +13; +2 vs. enchantment  
**DR** 10/good; **Immune** curse effects, disease, poison; **Resist** acid 10, electricty 10; **SR** 23

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk handaxe +17/+12/+7 (1d6+3/×3), mwk handaxe +17/+12 (1d6+1/×3)  
**Ranged** *+1 composite shortbow* +22/+17/+12 (1d6+4/×3) or *+1 composite shortbows* +20/+20/+15/+15/+10 (1d6+4/×3)  
**Special Attacks** dual bows  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +17)  
Constant—*[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[augury](../../magic/spells/augury.md)*, *[flame arrow](../../magic/spells/flame-arrow.md)*  
3/day—*[arcane sight](../../magic/spells/arcane-sight.md)*, *[divination](../../magic/spells/divination.md)*  
1/day—summon (level 5, 1d3 adhukaits 50% or 1 upasunda 30%), *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*, *[vision](../../magic/spells/vision.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 24, **Con** 21, **Int** 14, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 35  
**Feats** [Deflect Arrows](../../rules/feats/deflect-arrows.md), [Improved Precise Shot](../../rules/feats/improved-precise-shot.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Snatch Arrows](../../rules/feats/snatch-arrows.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (handaxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (handaxe), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +24 (+28 when jumping), Bluff +20, Climb +21, Escape Artist +23, Knowledge (planes) +12, Perception +25, Profession (soothsayer) +21, Sense Motive +21, Spellcraft +9, Stealth +24; **Racial Modifiers** +6 Escape Artist, +4 Perception  
**Languages** Common, Infernal; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** hateful prophecy, infernal arrows, selective *[nondetection](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md)*

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Hell)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or foreboding (3-5)  
**Treasure** standard (2 *+1 composite shortbows* [+3 Str], 2 mwk handaxes, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Dual Bows (Ex)** A japalisura can simultaneously wield two bows of any type. When it makes a full attack with both bows, the penalties for fighting with two weapons apply and can be offset with feats such as [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md). The japalisura can apply extra attacks from other sources, such as [Manyshot](../../rules/feats/manyshot.md) or [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), to only one of its bows per round. For the purpose of calculating the penalties for fighting with two weapons, a japalisura treats shortbows and composite shortbows as light weapons, and it treats other bows as one-handed weapons.

**Hateful Prophecy (Sp)** A japalisura can cast *[augury](../../magic/spells/augury.md)* or *[divination](../../magic/spells/divination.md)* for a willing creature, in which case the spell provides information that pertains to that creature’s future rather than that of the asura. Upon completing the casting of the spell-like ability, the japalisura can attempt a Profession (soothsayer) check opposed by the creature’s Sense Motive check. If successful, the japalisura twists the prophecy in such a way to convey impending violence or betrayal, subjecting the creature to the effects of *[vengeful outrage](../../magic/spells/vengeful-outrage.md)* with an indicated enemy of the asura’s choice. The creature can resist the vengeful outrage effect with a successful DC 20 Will saving throw. The save DC is Charisma-based. **Infernal Arrows (Su)** As a swift action, a japalisura with at least one free hand can create 10 mundane arrows. It can have no more than 20 such arrows in existence at a time, and its arrows are always destroyed when fired, whether they hit their target or not. **Selective Nondetection (Su)** A japalisura automatically succeeds at caster level checks to bypass its elusive aura.
