## Spyglass Archon (CR 3)

**XP** 800  
LG Medium outsider (archon, extraplanar, good, lawful)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*, hone senses, lowlight vision; Perception +7  
**Aura** aura of menace (DC 13), *magic circle against evil*

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 12, flat-footed 13 (+2 armor, +2 Dex, +1 natural)  
**hp** 44 (4d10+4)  
**Fort** +2, **Ref** +6, **Will** +4; +4 vs. poison  
**DR** 5/evil; **Immune** electricity, petrification; **SR** 14

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 40 ft. (good)  
**Melee** mwk longsword +5 (1d8+2/19–20), mwk short sword +5 (1d6+1/19–20)  
**Ranged**  shortbow +6 (1d6/×3)  
**Special Attacks** sneak attack +1d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +3)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[magic circle against evil](../../magic/spells/magic-circle-against-evil.md)*  
At will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[continual flame](../../magic/spells/continual-flame.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*  
3/day—*[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[entropic shield](../../magic/spells/entropic-shield.md)*, *[undetectable alignment](../../magic/spells/undetectable-alignment.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 15, **Con** 12, **Int** 13, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 9  
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 18  
**Feats** [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Bluff +6, Fly +13, Knowledge (planes) +8, Perception +7, Sense Motive +7, Stealth +12  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; *truespeech*

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Heaven)  
**Organization** solitary or pair  
**Treasure** standard (mwk longsword, mwk short sword, shortbow, leather armor, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Hone Senses (Su)** Three times per day, a spyglass archon can concentrate its attention as a swift action. For a number of rounds equal to its Hit Dice (4 for most spyglass archons), the range of the spyglass archon’s darkvision is doubled, it gains a +6 bonus on Perception checks, and it gains blindsense out to a range of 15 feet.
