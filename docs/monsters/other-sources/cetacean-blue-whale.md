## Blue Whale (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
N Colossal animal  
**Init** +3; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +27

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 1, flat-footed 26 (–1 Dex, +25 natural, –8 size)  
**hp** 200 (16d8+128)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +11, **Will** +8

**Offense**

**Speed** swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** tail slap +21 (8d6+24 plus stun)  
**Space** 30 ft., **Reach** 30 ft.  
**Special Attacks** capsize, swallow whole (4d6+16 damage, AC 22, 18 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 42, **Dex** 8, **Con** 25, **Int** 2, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 6  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +36; **CMD** 45 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (tail slap)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes.*  
**Skills** Perception +27, Swim +35; **Racial Modifiers** +12 Perception  
**SQ** hold breath

**Ecology**

**Environment** any ocean  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or pod (3–18)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Hold Breath (Ex)** A blue whale can hold its breath a number of rounds equal to 10 times its Constitution score before it risks drowning.

**Powerful Tail (Ex)** A blue whale’s massive tail deals more damage than a normal tail slap. The blue whale’s tail slap is a primary attack and applies 1-1/2 times its Strength bonus to its tail slap damage.

**Stun (Ex)** A blue whale’s fluke can deliver a powerful stunning blow. A creature struck by this attack must succeed at a DC 34 Fortitude save or be dazed for 1 round. If the strike is a critical hit and the target fails its save, it is instead stunned 1 for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.
