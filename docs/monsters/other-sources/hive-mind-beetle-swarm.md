## Hive Mind Beetle Swarm (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
LN Diminutive magical beast (swarm)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +28

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 25, flat-footed 14 (+3 Dex, +8 dodge, +4 size)  
**hp** 260 (20d10+60)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +15, **Will** +9  
**Defensive Abilities** swarm traits; **Immune** electricity, weapon damage  
**Weaknesses** exposed nerves

**Offense**

**Speed** 5 ft., fly 30 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** swarm (8d6 electricity plus distraction)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 0 ft.  
**Special Attacks** distraction (DC 24)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +11)  
Constant—*[thought echo](../../magic/spells/thought-echo.md)*  
At will—*[shocking grasp](../../magic/spells/shocking-grasp.md)*  
**Psychic Magic** (CL 14th, concentration +16)  
15 PE—*[explode head](../../magic/spells/explode-head.md)* (5 PE, DC 18), *[greater synaptic pulse](../../magic/spells/synaptic-pulse-greater.md)* (5 PE, DC 18), *[lightning bolt](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)* (3 PE, DC 19), *[mental barrier III](../../magic/spells/mental-barrier-iii.md)* (4 PE), *[mind probe](../../magic/spells/mind-probe.md)* (4 PE, DC 16), *[mind thrust IV](../../magic/spells/mind-thrust-iv.md)* (4 PE, DC 16), *[synapse overload](../../magic/spells/synapse-overload.md)* (5 PE, DC 17), *[synaptic scramble](../../magic/spells/synaptic-scramble.md)* (4 PE, DC 17), *[thought shield IV](../../magic/spells/thought-shield-iv.md)* (5 PE)

**Statistics**

**Str** 1, **Dex** 16, **Con** 14, **Int** 15, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 5  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** —; **CMD** —  
**Feats** Ability Focus (distraction), Flyby Attack, [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Greater Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-penetration.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Spell Focus (enchantment)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Spell Focus (evocation)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/spell-penetration.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**Skills** Fly +40, Knowledge (nature) +22, Perception +28, Spellcraft +22; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception  
**Languages** thought echo (can’t speak)  
**SQ** reflexive dodge

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** solitary or colony (2–4)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Exposed Nerves (Ex)** Whenever a hive mind beetle swarm is at or below half its full hit points, its Intelligence score drops to 9 and it loses its spellcasting ability. When at or below one-quarter its full hit points, it becomes effectively mindless and can only use feats that provide a static bonus.

**Reflexive Dodge (Ex)** The constituent creatures of a hive mind beetle swarm can dodge attacks, forming holes in the swarm. It gains a +8 dodge bonus to its AC.
