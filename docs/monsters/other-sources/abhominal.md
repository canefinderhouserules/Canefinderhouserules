## Abhominal (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
NE Medium undead  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., scent; Perception +13

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 13, flat-footed 16 (+3 Dex, +6 natural)  
**hp** 99 (9d8+27)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +6, **Will** +9  
**Immune** acid, cold, undead traits

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft., swim 30 ft.  
**Melee** barbed tentacles +11 (3d6+6 plus bleed and grab and pull)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** bleed (1d4), constrict (3d6+6 plus 1d4 Str damage), pull (barbed tentacles, 15 ft.), tear

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 17, **Con** —, **Int** 7, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +10 (+14 grapple); **CMD** 23  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Step Up](../../rules/feats/step-up.md), [Weapon Focus (barbed tentacles)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +7 (+11 when jumping), Climb +16, Perception +13, Stealth +14, Swim +13  
**Languages** Aboleth (can’t speak); telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** grip, hive hunt

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cluster (3-6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Barbed Tentacles (Ex)** An abhominal attacks with all of its barbed tentacles at once, grappling and raking its target. This attack is a primary attack that deals both bludgeoning and piercing damage.

**Grip (Ex)** The hands and feet of an abhominal adhere to surfaces so well that its 40-foot climb speed applies even to perfectly sheer surfaces and ceilings, with no chance of the abhominal falling off unless it is actively pinned and peeled away as part of a grapple.

**Hive Hunt (Su)** When an abhominal pinpoints the location of a creature, that knowledge is instantly shared with other abhominals within 100 feet.

**Tear (Ex)** When an abhominal attacks a creature that is already grappled by another abhominal, it can attempt to tear the creature between them with a successful pull combat maneuver check. The creature is not moved by the pull, but it takes an additional 3d6+6 points of bludgeoning and piercing damage instead.

**Telepathy (Su)** An abhominal can communicate telepathically with other abhominals and alghollthus that are within 100 feet.
