## Wayward Crusader (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
Human paladin of the goddess of valor 5  
LG Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** –1; **Senses** Perception +1  
**Aura** courage (10 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 9, flat-footed 20 (+9 armor, –1 Dex, +2 shield)  
**hp** 42 (5d10+10)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +3, **Will** +8  
**Immune** disease, fear

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** mwk cold iron longsword +9 (1d8+3/19–20) or mwk lance +9 (1d8+4/x3)  
**Ranged**  javelin +4 (1d6+3)  
**Special Attacks** channel positive energy (DC 15, 3d6), smite evil 2/day (+3 attack and AC, +5 damage)  
**Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +5)  
1st—*[bless weapon](../../magic/spells/bless-weapon.md)*, *[lesser restoration](../../magic/spells/restoration-lesser.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 8, **Con** 13, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +5; **CMB** +8; **CMD** 17  
**Feats** [Mounted Combat](../../rules/feats/mounted-combat.md), [Ride-By Attack](../../rules/feats/ride-by-attack.md), [Skill Focus (Ride)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Spirited Charge](../../rules/feats/spirited-charge.md)  
**Skills** Diplomacy +11, Heal +9, Ride +4  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** divine bond (mount), lay on hands 5/day (2d6), mercy (sickened)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** NPC gear (*wand of cure light wounds* [9 charges], holy water [2], mwk full plate, mwk heavy steel shield, dagger, javelins [4], mwk cold iron longsword, mwk lance, everburning torch, silver holy symbol of the goddess of valor, spell component pouch, 83 gp)

This crusader believed her destiny to be in the crusader land, as a sword and shield against the cancer of the demon-ravaged land. Yet the long road from the old empire took her through the wasteland of the fallen starship, and here she found a cause perhaps as great, and with far fewer ready to stand with her on the side of compassion and righteousness. Now she quests to bring justice to Sovereign’s Reach and mercy to the Felldales, and is always in search of stout hearts to stand beside her. She preaches the glory of the goddess of valor to those in search of meaning and answers, but recognizes the universal truths behind all good faiths.

PCs adventuring in the wasteland of the fallen starship may run across or even be rescued by this wayward crusader, and she doesn’t hesitate to enlist them in her cause. For low-level PCs she can serve as a powerful ally and mentor, while companions who surpass her in power could even claim her crusade for their own.
