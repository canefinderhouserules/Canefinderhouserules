## Duergar Ironclad (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
Duergar fighter 7/stalwart defender 4  
LE Medium humanoid (dwarf)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft.; Perception +10

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 15, flat-footed 24 (+11 armor, +2 Dex, +3 dodge, +3 shield)  
**hp** 180 (11 HD; 7d10+4d12+51)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +8, **Will** +7 (+2 vs. fear); +2 vs. spells  
**Defensive Abilities** uncanny dodge; **Immune** paralysis, phantasms, poison  
**Weaknesses** light sensitivity

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 bastard sword* +17/+12/+7 (1d10+7/19–20) or mwk light hammer +15/+10/+5 (1d4+3)  
**Ranged**  javelin +14/+9/+4 (1d6+3)  
**Special Attacks** weapon training (heavy blades +1)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 11th; concentration +9)  
1/day—*[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)* (self only), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)* (self only)

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 14, **Con** 16, **Int** 8, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 6  
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 29 (33 vs. bull rush, 33 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Exotic Weapon Proficiency (bastard sword)](../../rules/feats/exotic-weapon-proficiency.md), [Quick Draw](../../rules/feats/quick-draw.md), [Stalwart](../../rules/feats/stalwart.md), [Weapon Focus (bastard sword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Specialization (bastard sword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-specialization.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Diehard and Endurance and Toughness are one feat, Toughness; in their place: Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes.*  
**Skills** Intimidate +7, Perception +10, Stealth +2; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth  
**Languages** Common, Dwarven, Undercommon  
**SQ** armor training 2, defensive powers (bulwark, halting blow), defensive stance  
**Combat Gear**  *brooch of shielding*, *potion of cure moderate wounds*, *potion of heroism*; **Other Gear** *+2 full plate*, *+1 heavy steel shield*, *+1 bastard sword*, *javelins of lightning* (2), javelins (3), mwk javelin, mwk light hammer, *cloak of resistance +1*, iron chain (10 ft.), iron spikes (5), 5 pp, 7 gp

Bred in the steam vents of Diepkamer, ironclads are as unfeeling as the metallic casings that entomb them. These duergar are known to chain themselves to one another and anchor their iron boots to the very structures they defend.
