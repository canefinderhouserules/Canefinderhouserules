## Mix Couatl (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
CG Large outsider (native)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *[detect chaos/evil/good/law](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, see in darkness; Perception +20

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 13, flat-footed 17 (+4 Dex, +8 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 140 (10d10+40)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +11, **Will** +12

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +14 (1d8+6 plus grab and poison)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (15-ft. cone, 8d6 fire, Reflex DC 19 half, usable every 1d6 rounds), constrict (1d8+6), poison  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +10)  
Constant—*[detect chaos](../../magic/spells/detect-chaos.md)*, *[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*  
At will—*[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)* (DC 15), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only)  
1/day—*[modify memory](../../magic/spells/modify-memory.md)* (DC 17)  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
4th—*[flame strike](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)* (DC 18), *[summon nature’s ally IV](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-iv.md)*  
3rd—*[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*, *[speak with plants](../../magic/spells/speak-with-plants.md)*  
2nd—*[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[flaming sphere](../../magic/spells/flaming-sphere.md)* (DC 15), *[lesser restoration](../../magic/spells/restoration-lesser.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*  
1st—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[faerie fire](../../magic/spells/faerie-fire.md)* (DC 14), *[pass without trace](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md)*, *[produce flame](../../magic/spells/produce-flame.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[flare](../../magic/spells/flare.md)* (DC 13), *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*, *[virtue](../../magic/spells/virtue.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 19, **Con** 18, **Int** 15, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +15 (+19 grapple); **CMD** 29 (can't be tripped)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), Hover, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Diplomacy +16, Fly +19, Knowledge (geography) +15, Knowledge (nature) +15, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Stealth +0 (+20 in the night sky)  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** gift of knowledge, star child

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or flight (3-4)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Gift of Knowledge (Ex)** When a mix couatl uses its *[modify memory](../../magic/spells/modify-memory.md)* ability, it can implant a memory of up to 1 hour in length. In addition to its normal modifications, it can use *[modify memory](../../magic/spells/modify-memory.md)* to grant the knowledge of a particular skill to a creature. When using the ability in this way, the mix couatl chooses one of the following skills: Craft (any), Handle Animal, Heal, Knowledge (engineering), Knowledge (geography), Knowledge (nature), Profession (any), Ride, Spellcraft, Survival, or Use Magic Device. The target of the spell automatically gains a number of ranks in that skill equal to its Hit Dice (maximum 5 ranks) and treats that skill as a class skill. The target creature retains the skill ranks for up to 1 month, after which the creature loses the skill ranks but still treats the chosen skill as a class skill. A creature can receive the gift of skills in this way only once; it can never receive this gift again, even from a different mix couatl.

**Poison (Ex)** Injury—bite; *save* Fortitude DC 19; *frequency* 1/ minute for 10 minutes; *effect* 1d3 Wis damage; *cure* 1 save. The DC is Constitution-based.

**Spells** A mix couatl casts spells as a 7th-level druid. Druid spells are considered arcane spells for a mix couatl, meaning that it doesn’t need a divine focus to cast them.

**Star Child (Ex)** A mix couatl gains power from the night sky, granting it a +20 bonus on Stealth checks to hide against the darkness of the sky. In addition, its fly speed increases to 80 feet (perfect) at night.
