## Nephlei (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
CN Large outsider (air, extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +16

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 17, flat-footed 16 (+4 deflection, +4 Dex, +3 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 115 (11d10+55)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +15, **Will** +13  
**Immune** cold, electricity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+1 shocking burst scorpion whip* +13/+8/+3 (1d6+2 plus 1d6 electricity) or slam +14 (1d6+1)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** lifeburst  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +14)  
5/day—*[alter winds](../../magic/spells/alter-winds.md)* (DC 16), *[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*, *[frigid touch](../../magic/spells/frigid-touch.md)* (DC 17), *[gust of wind](../../magic/spells/gust-of-wind.md)* (DC 17), *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*  
 3/day—*[cloak of winds](../../magic/spells/cloak-of-winds.md)*, *[lightning bolt](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)* (DC 18), *[river of wind](../../magic/spells/river-of-wind.md)* (DC 19), *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)* (DC 18)  
1/day—*[ball lightning](../../magic/spells/ball-lightning.md)* (DC 19), *[control winds](../../magic/spells/control-winds.md)* (DC 20), *[lightning arc](../../magic/spells/lightning-arc.md)* (DC 20)

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 19, **Con** 20, **Int** 16, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 31  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), Flyby Attack, [Greater Spell Focus (evocation)](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-focus.md), [Spell Focus (evocation)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (whip)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Agile Maneuvers and Weapon Finesse are one feat, Weapon Finesse; in their place: Weapon Focus (whip).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +18, Bluff +19, Fly +20, Intimidate +19, Knowledge (nature) +17, Knowledge (planes) +17, Perception +16, Sense Motive +16, Stealth +14  
**Languages** Auran, Common, Draconic  
**SQ** crackling whip, unearthly grace

**Ecology**

**Environment** any sky (Plane of Air)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Crackling Whip (Su)** A nephlei uses a magical whip fashioned from the essence of the air around her. This functions as a *+1 shocking burst scorpion whip*, but if it ever leaves the nephlei’s possession, it immediately dissipates back into the air. A nephlei can make a new crackling whip with 10 minutes of work.

 **Lifeburst (Sp)** When a nephlei dies, she releases the electrical energies that she has built up in her body in the form of a lightning bolt. As soon as a nephlei’s hit points are reduced to a negative amount equal to her Constitution score, her Constitution drops to 0, or she is killed outright by a spell or effect, she releases a lightning bolt that deals 9d6 points of damage (Reflex DC 20 half). The nephlei can choose where to aim the bolt as her final act. The bolt disintegrates the nephlei’s body, leaving behind a trace of fine dust. The saving throw DC is Charisma-based.

 **Unearthly Grace (Su)** A nephlei adds her Charisma modifier as a racial bonus on saving throws, and as a deflection bonus to her Armor Class.
