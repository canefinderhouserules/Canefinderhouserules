## Vault Keeper (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
LN Medium outsider (earth, elemental, extraplanar)  
**Init** +12; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 120 ft.; Perception +23

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 19, flat-footed 19 (+8 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural)  
**hp** 272 (16d10+112); fast healing 5  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +18, **Will** +14  
**DR** 10/adamantine and bludgeoning; **Immune** elemental traits; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.  
**Melee** 4 claws +25 (2d4+6/19–20 plus crystallization)  
**Special Attacks** crystal burst (DC 25), crystallization (DC 25), rend (2 claws, 2d4+9)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +23)  
At will—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[shatter* ](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)(DC 19), *[statue* ](../../magic/spells/statue.md)(DC 24), *[stone shape](../../magic/spells/stone-shape.md)*, *[stone tell](../../magic/spells/stone-tell.md)*  
3/day—*command stone* (DC 26), *[flesh to stone](../../magic/spells/flesh-to-stone.md)* (DC 23), *[spike stones](../../magic/spells/spike-stones.md)* (DC 21), *[wall of stone](../../magic/spells/wall-of-stone.md)*  
1/day—*[summon monster VII](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-vii.md)* (earth elementals only), *[symbol of scrying](../../magic/spells/symbol-of-scrying.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 22, **Dex** 27, **Con** 25, **Int** 27, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +22 (+24 sunder); **CMD** 41 (43 vs. sunder)  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Critical (claw)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw), Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +20, Climb +30, Craft (sculpture) +13, Disable Device +22, Heal +20, Knowledge (arcana) +27, Knowledge (dungeoneering) +24, Knowledge (geography) +24, Knowledge (nature) +24, Knowledge (planes) +27, Perception +23, Sense Motive +23, Spellcraft +27, Stealth +27, Use Magic Device +26  
**Languages** Terran; telepathy 300 ft.  
**SQ** item mastery

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cabal (3–6 plus 4–12 pechs and 2–6 earth elementals)  
**Treasure** double

**Special Abilities**

**Crystal Burst (Su)** A Vault Keeper’s crystal burst deal 12d6 points of damage.

**Crystallization (Su)** A creature struck by a Vault Keeper’s claw must succeed at a DC 25 Fortitude save or take 1 point of Dexterity drain. On a critical hit, a target that fails its save instead takes 4 points of Dexterity drain. This ability otherwise functions as the Vault Builder version.

**Item Mastery (Ex)** A Vault Keeper can always activate spell trigger and spell completion items as if the spell were on its class list.

**Item Shaper (Ex)** For the purposes of crafting magic items or constructs, a Vault Keeper is treated as though it had all item creation feats.
