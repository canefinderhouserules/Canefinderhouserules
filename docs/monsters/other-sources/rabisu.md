## Rabisu (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
CE Medium fey  
**Init** +7; **Senses** low-light vision, scent, see in darkness; Perception +22

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 18, flat-footed 17 (+8 Dex, +1 Dodge, +7 natural)  
**hp** 182 (14d6+98)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +16, **Will** +14  
**DR** 10/cold iron and magic; **Immune** fire, bleed, fear; **Resist** cold 10, electricity 10  
**Weaknesses** suceptible to salt

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +17 (1d6+10 plus blood drain), 4 claws +18 (1d4+10 plus grab)  
**Special Attacks** blood drain (1d2 Constitution)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +18)  
Constant—*[greater magic fang](../../magic/spells/magic-fang-greater.md)* (self only)  
 At will—*[command undead](../../magic/spells/command-undead.md)* (DC 18), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)* (DC 19)  
 3/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 20), quickened *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, quickened *[ray of enfeeblement](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)* (DC 19), *[ray of exhaustion](../../magic/spells/ray-of-exhaustion.md)*, *[vampiric touch](../../magic/spells/vampiric-touch.md)*  
 1/day—*[mirage arcana](../../magic/spells/mirage-arcana.md)* (DC 21), *[veil](../../magic/spells/veil.md)* (self only), *[waves of fatigue](../../magic/spells/waves-of-fatigue.md)*, *[wind walk](../../magic/spells/wind-walk.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 21, **Dex** 24, **Con** 24, **Int** 19, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +14 (+18 grapple); **CMD** 30 (34 vs. grapple)  
**Feats** Ability Focus (*ray of enfeeblement*), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*invisibility*, *ray of enfeeblement*), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +24, Disguise +23, Escape Artist +24, Fly +28, Intimidate +20, Knowledge (nature) +21, Perception +22, Sense Motive +22, Stealth +38, Survival +19; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Stealth  
**Languages** Aklo, Auran, Common  
**SQ** blood frenzy

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm deserts, hills, and mountains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or gang (3-6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Blood Frenzy (Ex)** When a rabisu successfully drains blood from a creature, it becomes frenzied. For 1d4 rounds after it drains blood, it acts as if affected by haste and gains fast healing 5.

 **Susceptible to Salt (Ex)** A handful of salt burns a rabisu as a flask of acid would, causing 1d6 points of damage per use. Ground sprinkled with salt functions as difficult terrain for a rabisu. A line of salt placed across a doorway, window, or other aperture can largely prevent a rabisu from passing through that opening. It can attempt to go through the opening as a move action, but unless it succeeds at a DC 20 Fortitude save, the salt prevents it from completing the movement, and it becomes nauseated 1 for 1 round.
