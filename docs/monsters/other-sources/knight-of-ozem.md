## Knight of Ozem (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Human paladin of the goddess of valor 5/Knight of Ozem 3  
LG Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** Perception +6  
**Aura** courage (10 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 10, flat-footed 24 (+10 armor, +4 shield)  
**hp** 101 (8d10+21)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +6, **Will** +10  
**Immune** disease

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 longsword* +13/+8 (1d8+4/19–20)  
**Ranged**  javelin +8 (1d6+3)  
**Special Attacks** channel positive energy (DC 15, 3d6), favored enemy (undead +2), smite evil 2/day (+3 attack and AC, +5 damage)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +8)  
At will—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
**Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +5)  
1st—*[bless weapon](../../magic/spells/bless-weapon.md)*, *[detect undead](../../magic/spells/detect-undead.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 10, **Con** 14, **Int** 8, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 21  
**Feats** [Extra Mercy](../../rules/feats/extra-mercy.md), [Improved Shield Bash](../../rules/feats/improved-shield-bash.md), [Shield Focus](../../rules/feats/shield-focus.md), [Shield Wall](../../rules/feats/shield-wall.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +14, Heal +5, Knowledge (religion) +7, Perception +6, Sense Motive +7  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** aura, code of conduct, divine bond (weapon +1, 1/day), lay on hands (2d6, 5/day), mercy (shaken, sickened), sentinel against undeath +1  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of eagle’s splendor*, *potion of lesser restoration*, holy water; **Other Gear** *+1 full plate*, *+1 heavy steel shield*, *+1 longsword*, javelins (4), *cloak of resistance +1*, silver holy symbol of the goddess of valor, sunrods (2), 7 gp

**Special Abilities**

**Favored Enemy (Ex)** A Knight of Ozem gains a favored enemy bonus of +2 against undead. This ability functions as the ranger class ability.

**Sentinel Against Undeath (Ex)** A Knight of Ozem gains a bonus equal to 1/3 his Knight of Ozem level on Perception and Sense Motive checks against undead. He also gains this bonus as a dodge bonus to his AC and CMD against attacks made by undead.

Formed to battle the threat of the lich-king, the Knights of Ozem stand in unflinching defense of the fallen bastion and against undead wherever they may rise. Once they owed allegiance to the betrayed goddess, but with her fall and the demise of the last emperor-god, the order has transferred its allegiance to the goddess of valor, herself a leader of the knights during her mortal days.

Though much of the attention of the fallen bastion has turned to the orc lands, the knights argue for eternal vigilance against the possibility of the lich-king’s escape from Gallowspire or the rise of one of his many lieutenants. Besides this unending watch, the Knights of Ozem search for the bloodstones of the betrayed goddess, canopic jars said to contain the organs of the Harlot Queen’s mortal frame. With or without the bloodstones, they seek the final destruction of their lost patron, that her soul might at last find eternal rest. If anything can move a Knight of Ozem to bend his oaths, it is the hope of freeing the betrayed goddess from the undead kingdom’s sinister control.
