## Tletli (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
NG Huge outsider (native)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., *[detect chaos/evil/good/ law](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*; Perception +30

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 13, flat-footed 25 (+4 Dex, +1 dodge, +17 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 200 (16d10+112)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +16, **Will** +17  
**Immune** fire; **Resist** acid 15, cold 15, electricity 15; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 120 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +25 (2d8+11/19–20 plus grab and poison), 2 wings +20 (1d8+5 plus burn and stoke fire)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d6 fire, Reflex DC 25 half, usable every 1d4 rounds), burn (2d6 fire, DC 25), constrict (2d8+11 plus burn)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +18)  
Constant—*[detect chaos](../../magic/spells/detect-chaos.md)*, *[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*  
 At will—*[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)* (DC 17), *[ethereal jaunt](../../magic/spells/ethereal-jaunt.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (DC 22)  
 1/day—*[sunburst](../../magic/spells/sunburst.md)* (DC 24)  
**Spells Known** (CL 12th; concentration +18)  
6th (4/day)—*[holy word](../../magic/spells/holy-word.md)* (DC 22)  
 5th (6/day)—*[dismissal](../../magic/spells/dismissal.md)* (DC 21), *[flame strike](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)*  
4th (7/day)—*[dispel evil](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)* (DC 20), *[holy smite](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)*, *[wall of fire](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md)*  
3rd (7/day)—*[daylight](../../magic/spells/daylight.md)*, *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fireball](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)* (DC 19), *[searing light](../../magic/spells/searing-light.md)* (DC 19)  
2nd (8/day)—*[castigate](../../magic/spells/castigate.md)* (DC 18), *[eagle’s splendor](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md)*, *[flaming sphere](../../magic/spells/flaming-sphere.md)* (DC 18), *[gust of wind](../../magic/spells/gust-of-wind.md)* (DC 18), *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[burning hands](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)* (DC 17), *[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 17), *[comprehend languages](../../magic/spells/comprehend-languages.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[protection from evil](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 32, **Dex** 19, **Con** 24, **Int** 17, **Wis** 24, **Cha** 23  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +29 (+33 grapple); **CMD** 44 (can't be tripped)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Dodge](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Eschew MaterialsB, Hover, [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +25, Fly +15, Intimidate +25, Knowledge (arcana) +22, Knowledge (planes) +22, Perception +30, Sense Motive +30, Spellcraft +19, Stealth +15  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** holy fire

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or revelation (3–6 plus 1–2 angels and 1 chicome couatl)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Holy Fire (Su)** Fire damage dealt by a tletli, including that of its breath weapon and spells, is infused with divine energy. Half the damage of such attacks is fire damage, but the other half is the result of holy power and thus not subject to being reduced by resistance to fire-based attacks. Fire damage dealt by a tletli is considered good-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.

**Poison (Ex)** Injury—bite; *save* Fort DC 25; *frequency* 1/minute for 10 minutes; *effect* 1d6 Str; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.

**Spells** A tletli casts spells as a 12th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the inquisitor and paladin spell lists as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. These divine spells are considered arcane spells for the tletli, meaning that the creature does not need a divine focus to cast them.

**Stoke Flames (Ex)** Once per round when a tletli attacks with its wings, it can cause areas of active fire within 30 feet to spread as a free action. An affected 5-foot square of fire spreads to one adjacent square that is not on fire, possibly burning objects or creatures in that space. The tletli determines which adjacent squares are set on fire.
