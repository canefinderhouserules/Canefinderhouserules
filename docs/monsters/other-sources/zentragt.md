## Zentragt (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
CN Large outsider (chaotic, extraplanar)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 10, flat-footed 18 (+1 Dex, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 72 (6d10+12)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +3, **Will** +5  
**DR** 5/lawful; **Immune** fear; **Resist** cold 5, electricity 5, fire 5; **SR** 15

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +8 (1d6+3), 2 claws +9 (1d6+3 plus grab)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** adamantine attacks  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +6)  
3/day—*[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
1/day—*[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 12, **Con** 13, **Int** 8, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +10 (+14 grapple, +12 sunder); **CMD** 21 (23 vs. sunder, 25 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Weapon Focus (claws)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Toughness.*  
**Skills** Climb +12, Intimidate +9, Knowledge (planes) +5, Knowledge (religion) +5, Perception +9, Swim +16; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Swim  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial; *speak with animals* (bears only)  
**SQ** eat metal

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Elysium)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or gang (3–5)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Adamantine Attacks (Ex)** A zentragt’s bite and claw attacks count as adamantine, chaotic, and magic for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction and bypassing hardness.

**Eat Metal (Su)** A zentragt can eat metal to heal itself. For every 5 pounds of iron or steel it consumes, it heals 5 hit points. Adamantine, cold iron, mithral, or metals with an enhancement bonus of +1 or higher heal it double this amount. It takes a zentragt 1 minute to eat 5 pounds of metal.
