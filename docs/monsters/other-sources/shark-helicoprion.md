## Helicoprion (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
N Large animal (aquatic)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** blindsense 30 ft., keen scent; Perception +12

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 13, flat-footed 15 (+4 Dex, +6 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 104 (8d8+40)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +12, **Will** +3

**Offense**

**Speed** swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +12 (1d8+10 plus 1d4 bleed)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** bleed (1d4), whorled jaw

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 18, **Con** 19, **Int** 3, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 4  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +14 (+18 grapple); **CMD** 28  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md)  
*House rules: Endurance is part of Toughness.*  
**Skills** Perception +12, Swim +15

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate oceans  
**Organization** solitary, pair, school (3–  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Whorled Jaw (Ex)** When a helicoprion hits a Medium or smaller creature with its bite it can, as a free action, attempt to grapple and then pin that creature should the initial grapple be successful. Once the target is pinned, the helicoprion continues to do its bite damage (including bleed) for as long as it maintains the pin.
