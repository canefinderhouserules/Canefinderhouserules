## Faceless Whale (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
N Colossal magical beast  
**Init** +2; **Senses** blindsight 150 ft.; Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 0, flat-footed 30 (–2 Dex, +30 natural, –8 size)  
**hp** 264 (16d10+176)  
**Fort** +22, **Ref** +10, **Will** +7  
**Immune** sonic, gaze attacks, sight-based attacks, visual effects and illusions  
**Weaknesses** blind

**Offense**

**Speed** 0 ft., swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** bite +26 (6d6+17), tail slap +20 (4d6+8)  
**Space** 30 ft., **Reach** 30 ft.  
**Special Attacks** capsize, resonant song, swallow whole (4d6 acid damage, AC 25, 24 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 45, **Dex** 6, **Con** 30, **Int** 4, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 5  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +41; **CMD** 49  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; Improved Bull Rush, Improved Overrun are covered by Powerful Maneuvers; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite), Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes.*  
**Skills** Perception +19, Swim +25  
**Languages** Aklo (can’t speak)  
**SQ** blind, hold breath

**Ecology**

**Environment** any water  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or pod (3–16)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Blind (Ex)** A faceless whale sees and senses exclusively through its blindsight ability, which is based on sound and movement—beyond 150 feet, the whale is considered blind. A deafened faceless whale is effectively blinded as well. It is invulnerable to all sight-based effects and attacks, including gaze attacks.

**Resonant Song (Su)** As a standard action, a faceless whale can focus a 60-foot ray of sonic energy on a single target that deals 8d6 points of damage. This ray deals normal damage to objects, but is still subject to hardness. Alternatively, a faceless whale can blast this resonant energy in a 60- foot cone. Creatures in this area must succeed at a DC 28 Fortitude or be stunned 1 for 1d4 rounds. Any submerged creature holding its breath must succeed at a second Fortitude saving throw at the same DC or it has its breath knocked out of it and instantly begins drowning.
