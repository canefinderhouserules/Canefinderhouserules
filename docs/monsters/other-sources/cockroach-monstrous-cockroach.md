## Monstrous Cockroach (CR 1/2)

Always N Small vermin  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft.; Listen +0, Spot +4

**Defense**

**AC** 14, touch 12, flat-footed 13 (+1 Dex, +2 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 11 (1d8+7)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +3, **Will** +1  
**Defensive Abilities** vermin traits

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +1 (1d4)

**Statistics**

**Str** 10, **Dex** 12, **Con** 19, **Int** &mdash;, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 2  
**Base Atk** +0; **Grapple** -4  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md)  
*House rules: Diehard and Endurance are one feat, Toughness; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Climb +9, Hide +9, Spot +4; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Hide, +4 Spot  
**SQ** hold breath, light sensitivity

**Ecology**

**Environment** any temperate, warm, or urban  
**Organization** solitary, intrustion (2-40)  
**Treasure** none  
**Advancement** 2-5 HD (Small), 6-10 HD (Medium), 11-20 HD (Large), 21-34 HD (Huge), 35-51 HD (Gargantuan)

**Special Abilities**

**Hold Breath (Ex)** A monstrous cockroach can hold its breath for a number of rounds equal to four times its Constitution score before it risks drowning (see page 304 of the DMG).

**Light Sensitivity (Ex)** A monstrous cockroaches is dazzled in bright sunlight or within the radius of a *daylight* spell.
