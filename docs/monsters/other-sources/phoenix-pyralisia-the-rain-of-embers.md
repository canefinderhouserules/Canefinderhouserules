## Pyralisia, the Rain of Embers (CR 18)

**XP** 153,600  
Female unique phoenix  
NE Gargantuan magical beast (fire)  
**Init** +10; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*, low-light vision, *see invisibility*; Perception +39  
**Aura** shroud of flame (20 ft., DC 28)

**Defense**

**AC** 33, touch 13, flat-footed 26 (+6 Dex, +1 dodge, +20 natural, –4 size)  
**hp** 414 (23d10+184); regeneration 10 (cold or good)  
**Fort** +20, **Ref** +19, **Will** +14  
**Defensive Abilities** self-resurrection; **DR** 15/good; **Immune** fire; **SR** 29  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 90 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +29 (2d8+9 plus 4d6 fire and 4d6 electricity), 2 talons +28 (2d6+9/19–20 plus 4d6 fire)  
**Space** 20 ft., **Reach** 20 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +27)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
At will—*[bestow curse* ](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)(DC 21), *[chain lightning* ](../../magic/spells/chain-lightning.md)(DC 23), *[continual flame](../../magic/spells/continual-flame.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[wall of fire](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md)*  
3/day—*[fire storm* ](../../magic/spells/fire-storm.md)(DC 25), *[mass inflict critical wounds* ](../../magic/spells/inflict-critical-wounds-mass.md)(DC 25), quickened *[wall of fire](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md)*  
1/day—*[destruction* ](../../magic/spells/destruction.md)(DC 24), *[meteor swarm* ](../../magic/spells/meteor-swarm.md)(DC 26)

**Statistics**

**Str** 29, **Dex** 23, **Con** 24, **Int** 23, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +23; **CMB** +36; **CMD** 53  
**Feats** [Blinding Critical](../../rules/feats/blinding-critical.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Flyby Attack, [Improved Critical (talon)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Quicken Spell- Like Ability (*wall of fire*), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Bluff +30, Fly +30, Intimidate +30, Knowledge (arcana) +29, Knowledge (nature) +29, Perception +39, Sense Motive +28, Stealth +20; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Perception
