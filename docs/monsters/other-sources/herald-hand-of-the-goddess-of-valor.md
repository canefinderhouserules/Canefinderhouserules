1/day—*[blade barrier* ](../../magic/spells/blade-barrier.md)(DC 22), *[death ward](../../magic/spells/death-ward.md)*, *[dispel evil* ](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)(DC 21), *[greater magic weapon](../../magic/spells/magic-weapon-greater.md)*, *[greater restoration](../../magic/spells/restoration-greater.md)*, *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 21, **Con** 22, **Int** 22, **Wis** 24, **Cha** 23  
**Base Atk** +18; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 42  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Shield Bash](../../rules/feats/improved-shield-bash.md), [Shield Master](../../rules/feats/shield-master.md), [Shield Slam](../../rules/feats/shield-slam.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (heavy shield)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Heaven)  
**Organization** solitary or squad (the Hand plus 2–5 astral devas)  
**Treasure** double (*+2 holy longsword*, *+2 heavy steel shield*)

**Special Abilities**

**Aura of Courage (Su)** The Hand of the goddess of valor radiates an aura of courage identical to that of an 18th-level paladin.

**Divine Trigger (Su)** The Hand of the goddess of valor can activate any paladin spell from a spell trigger item as if he were an 18thlevel paladin.
