## Time Dimensional (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
N Large outsider (extraplanar)  
**Init** +9; omniobservant; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft.; Perception +26  
**Aura** temporal dilation (30 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 20, flat-footed 24 (+5 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 insight, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 285 (19d10+95)  
**Fort** +13, **Ref** +18, **Will** +17  
**Defensive Abilities** all-around vision, time mastery, timestream knowledge; **DR** 10/—; **Immune** aging, bleed, critical hits, paralysis, poison, sleep, stunning; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10, electricity 10, fire 10, sonic 10; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** 2 slams +25 (6d6+6 plus dilating strike)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** dilating strike  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 19th; concentration +22)  
At will—*[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[memory lapse](../../magic/spells/memory-lapse.md)* (DC 14), *[modify memory](../../magic/spells/modify-memory.md)* (DC 17), *[plane shift](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)* (self only)  
3/day—*[disintegrate](../../magic/spells/disintegrate.md)* (DC 19), *[sands of time](../../magic/spells/sands-of-time.md)* (DC 16)  
1/day—*[temporal stasis](../../magic/spells/temporal-stasis.md)* (DC 21), *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 21, **Con** 20, **Int** 16, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +19; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 47  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/improved-great-fortitude.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Improved Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/improved-lightning-reflexes.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Weapon Focus (slam)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (slam).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +27, Diplomacy +25, Fly +33, Knowledge (history) +35, Knowledge (all other) +13, Perception +26, Sense Motive +26, Spellcraft +25, Stealth +23, Use Magic Device +25; **Racial Modifiers** +10 Knowledge  
**Languages** Abyssal, Aklo, Common; telepathy 100 ft., *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Dimension of Time)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or chronology (3-12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Dilating Strike (Ex)** A time dimensional’s attacks slow the flow of local time around its target. A creature hit by a time dimensional’s slam attack must succeed at a DC 22 Will save or be affected by slow for 1d4 rounds. Whether or not it succeeds at the saving throw, a creature cannot be affected again by this ability for 1 minute. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Omniobservant (Ex)** A time dimensional has nearly perfect information about the passage of time in its vicinity. It always acts in the surprise round and its initiative roll is automatically a natural 20.

**Temporal Dilation (Ex)** The area around a time dimensional slows the temporal passage of projectiles and spells. Creatures in this aura have concealment against ranged attacks. A time dimensional can dismiss or resume this aura as a free action.

**Time Mastery (Ex)** As a living embodiment of time, a time dimensional is free from the typical constraints of time’s flow. It is immune to *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)*, and *[temporal stasis](../../magic/spells/temporal-stasis.md)* and can use any actions remaining on its turn after using *dimension door*. Whenever a creature within 60 feet of a time dimensional casts *time stop*, the time dimensional can act normally as if it had cast *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)* itself for the same effective duration. As neither the time dimensional nor the caster is frozen in time with respect to the other, each can target and affect the other with attacks, spells, or other effects they create, though neither can affect frozen creatures for the duration of the *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)*. This ability functions even if the time dimensional is flat-footed or unaware of its opponent.

**Timestream Knowledge (Ex)** A time dimensional shares its own knowledge from all points in its personal timeline. This knowledge grants it a +5 insight bonus to AC and all-around vision. It treats all skills as class skills, gains a +10 insight bonus on all Knowledge checks, and can attempt any Knowledge check untrained.
