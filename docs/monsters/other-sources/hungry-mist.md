## Hungry Mist (CR 14)

NE Fine undead (incorporeal, swarm)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft.; Listen +26, Spot +26

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 15, flat-footed 11 (+1 Dex, +4 deflection)  
**hp** 139 (20d12+9)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +9, **Will** +13  
**Immune** incorporeal immunities, swarm immunities, undead immunities

**Offense**

**Speed** fly 30 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** swarm (5d6 plus blood drain)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 0 ft. (shapeable)

**Statistics**

**Str** 1, **Dex** 12, **Con** &mdash;, **Int** 6, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 18  
**Base Atk** +10; **Grapple** &mdash;  
**Feats** Ability Focus (distraction), [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Toughness (3)](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**SQ** blood drain, distraction, hive mind, immune to weapon damage, scent, swarm traits

**Special Abilities**

**Blood Drain (Ex)** Whenever the hungry mist damages a victim with its swarm attack, it inflicts 1 point of Constitution drain. With each successful attack, it gains 5 temporary hit points.

**Distraction (Ex)** A creature that begins its turn in the same square as the hungry mist must make a Fortitude save (DC 21) or be nauseated 1 for 1 round. In order to cast a spell while within the hungry mist, a character must succeed on a Spellcraft check (DC 21 + spell level).

**Hive Mind (Ex)** Any hungry mist with at least 20 hit points forms a hive mind, giving it an intelligence of 6. When a hungry mist is reduced below this hit point threshold, it becomes mindless.

There is no description for this monster.
