**hp** 117 (7 HD; 3d8+4d10+46)

**SR** 18  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to sunlight

**Melee** *+1 aklys* +8/+3 (1d6+3), mwk spiked gauntlet +8 (1d3+1)

**Statistics**

**Str** 15, **Dex** 17, **Con** 22, **Int** 13, **Wis** 5, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +7 (+11 trip); **CMD** 20 (22 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Greater Trip](../../rules/feats/greater-trip.md), [Improved Trip](../../rules/feats/improved-trip.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md)

**SQ** favored terrain (urban +2), madness, poison use, slayer talents (slowing strike, terrain mastery), track +2  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of bull’s strength*, *potion of enlarge person*, *potion of invisibility* (2), medium spider venom (4 doses); **Other Gear** chain shirt, *+1 aklys*, darts (10), mwk spiked gauntlet, *hat of disguise*

**Ecology**

**Environment** any underground
