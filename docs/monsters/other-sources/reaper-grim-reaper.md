## Grim Reaper (CR 20)

**XP** 307,200  
NE Large undead (evil, extraplanar, incorporeal)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +35  
**Aura** fear aura (40 ft., DC 36)

**Defense**

**AC** 33, touch 21, flat-footed 30 (+1 dodge, +9 deflection, +2 Dex, +12 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 476 (28d8+252)  
**Fort** +18, **Ref** +11, **Will** +20  
**Defensive Abilities** channel resistance +4, incorporeal; **DR** 15/cold iron and good; **Immune** undead traits; **SR** 31

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** *+3 scythe* +27/+22/+17/+12 (2d6+13/19–20/×4 plus death touch)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +29)  
Constant—*[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[foresight](../../magic/spells/foresight.md)*, *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
At will—*[circle of death* ](../../magic/spells/circle-of-death.md)(DC 25), *[control undead* ](../../magic/spells/control-undead.md)(DC 26), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 26), *[polymorph](../../magic/spells/polymorph.md)*  
3/day—*[energy drain* ](../../magic/spells/energy-drain.md)(DC 28), *[finger of death* ](../../magic/spells/finger-of-death.md)(DC 26), *[soul bind* ](../../magic/spells/soul-bind.md)(DC 28), summon minor reapers (level 8, 1d4 minor reapers), *[unwilling shield* ](../../magic/spells/unwilling-shield.md)(DC 25)  
1/day—quickened *[destruction* ](../../magic/spells/destruction.md)(DC 26), *[wail of the banshee* ](../../magic/spells/wail-of-the-banshee.md)(DC 28)

**Statistics**

**Str** 24, **Dex** 15, **Con** —, **Int** 16, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 29  
**Base Atk** +21; **CMB** +24; **CMD** 51 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Greater Weapon Focus (scythe)](../../rules/feats/greater-weapon-focus.md), [Improved Critical (scythe)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*destruction*), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (scythe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +37, Disguise +40, Fly +39, Knowledge (planes) +31, Perception +35, Sense Motive +35, Stealth +29  
**Languages** Common, Celestial, Infernal; truespeech

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** double (*+3 scythe*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Death Touch (Su)** Creatures hit by either a grim reaper’s touch attack or by a weapon wielded by a grim reaper must succeed at a DC 33 Fortitude save or gain 2d4 negative levels. The save DC is Charisma-based. A grim reaper can channel this ability through any weapon it wields. A humanoid slain by a reaper’s death touch is consumed in unholy fire and has its remains destroyed as the *destruction* spell. This is a death effect.

**Summon Minor Reapers (Sp)** Three times per day, a reaper can summon 1d4 minor reapers as a standard action. Each of these minor reapers is assigned a single creature to attack, and the targeted creature must battle the minor reaper by itself. The target that the grim reaper assigns to its minor reapers need not be in sight, but it must be on the same plane on which the minor reaper was summoned. A grim reaper may only assign one minor reaper to any creature at a given time, even if it uses this ability multiple times.
