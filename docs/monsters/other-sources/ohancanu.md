## Ohancanu (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
CE Large fey  
**Init** +6; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +12

**Defense**

**AC** 18, touch 12, flat-footed 15 (+1 dodge, +2 Dex, +6 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 70 (7d6+28)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +7, **Will** +7  
**DR** 5/cold iron  
**Weaknesses** white hairs

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk greataxe +10 (3d6+9/×3)  
**Ranged**  rock +5 (1d8+6)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** rock throwing (100 ft.)

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 14, **Con** 19, **Int** 7, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 23  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Intimidating Prowess](../../rules/feats/intimidating-prowess.md), [Weapon Focus (greataxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Climb +16, Intimidate +12, Perception +12, Swim +16  
**Languages** Aklo, Common, Sylvan

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests  
**Organization** solitary or hunting party (2–6)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk greataxe, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**White Hairs (Su)** An ohancanu has 2d4 white hairs that connect not only to its head, but to its essence. If one of these hairs is plucked with a successful steal combat maneuver, the ohancanu gains 2 negative levels. The ohancanu automatically regrows the lost white hair and recovers the negative levels after 24 hours.
