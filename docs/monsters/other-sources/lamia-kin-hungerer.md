## Hungerer (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
CE Huge monstrous humanoid  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 90 ft., low-light vision; Perception +28  
**Aura** stench (30 ft., DC 25)

**Defense**

**AC** 31, touch 10, flat-footed 29 (+1 Dex, +1 dodge, +21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 220 (21d10+105)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +15, **Will** +18  
**DR** 10/cold iron and piercing; **Immune** acid, poison; **Resist**  electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 26

**Offense**

**Speed** 10 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +29 (2d8+10/19–20/×4 plus 2d6 acid damage and 2 Wisdom drain), 2 claws +29 (1d8+10 plus 2 Wisdom drain)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** devastating bite, vile spew  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +19)  
Constant—*fly*  
At will—*grease* (DC 15), *major image* (DC 17), *ventriloquism* (DC 15)  
3/day—*charm monster* (DC 18), *gust of wind* (DC 16), quickened *stinking cloud* (DC 17), *suggestion* (DC 17)  
1/day—*deep slumber* (DC 17), *mass charm monster* (DC 22), *mirror image*

**Statistics**

**Str** 30, **Dex** 13, **Con** 20, **Int** 13, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +21; **CMB** +33; **CMD** 45 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Dodge](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), Improved Initiative, [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Mobility](../../rules/feats/mobility.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*stinking cloud*), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md)  
**Skills** Fly +32, Intimidate +28, Perception +28, Sense Motive +25, Stealth +17  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Giant, Rune Empire

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold mountains  
**Organization** solitary or feast (2–5)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Devastating Bite (Ex)** A hungerer’s bite deals ×4 damage on a successful critical hit. If this damage is enough to reduce a victim to negative hit points, the victim must succeed at a DC 30 Fortitude save to avoid being decapitated, bitten in half, or otherwise instantly killed by the horrific wound. The save DC is Strength-based.

**Vile Spew (Su)** Whenever a hungerer takes damage, the resulting wound spews a great gout of vile blood and acid. Any creature adjacent to a hungerer when it is wounded takes 2d6 points of acid damage (Reflex DC 25 negates). The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Wisdom Drain (Su)** A hungerer drains 2 points of Wisdom each time it strikes a foe with its bite or claw attacks. Unlike with other kinds of ability drain attacks, a hungerer does not heal any damage when it uses its Wisdom drain.
