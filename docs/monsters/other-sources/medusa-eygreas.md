## Eygreas (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
Female brazen medusa ranger 5  
LE Large monstrous humanoid  
**Init** +8; **Senses** all-around vision, darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +21

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 14, flat-footed 24 (+6 armor, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 213 (13 HD; 8d10+5d10+70)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +16, **Will** +8  
**DR** 5/adamantine and magic; **Immune** poison

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +17 (1d6+4), snake bite +16 (1d6+4 plus poison), tail slap +11 (1d8+2 plus grab) or mwk scimitar +15/+10/+5 (1d8+2/18–20)  
**Ranged**  *+2 seeking composite longbow* +18/+13/+8 (2d6+4/x3)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** constrict (1d8+1), favored enemy (humans +2, monstrous humanoids +4), petrifying gaze  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +3)  
1st—*[entangle* ](../../magic/spells/entangle.md)(DC 12), *[pass without trace](../../magic/spells/pass-without-trace.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 15, **Dex** 19, **Con** 20, **Int** 10, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +18 (+22 grapple); **CMD** 31  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Far Shot](../../rules/feats/far-shot.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Endurance is part of Toughness; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw); Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Bluff +16, Intimidate +16, Perception +21, Stealth +15, Survival +17; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**SQ** favored terrain (jungle +2), hunter’s bond (constrictor snake), track +2, wild empathy +8  
**Gear** *+2 chain shirt*, *+2 seeking composite longbow* (+2 Str) with 40 arrows, masterwork scimitar, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *bracers of armor +1*

**Special Abilities**

**Petrifying Gaze (Su)** Turn to stone permanently, 30 feet, Fortitude DC 19 negates. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Poison (Ex)** Snake bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 19; *frequency* 1/ round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d3 Str; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
