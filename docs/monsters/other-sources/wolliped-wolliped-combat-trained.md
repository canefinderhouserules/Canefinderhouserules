## Wolliped (Combat Trained) (CR 3)

**XP** 800  
N Large animal  
**Init** +2; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +6

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 11, flat-footed 13 (+2 Dex, +4 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 34 (4d8+16)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +6, **Will** +1

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft.  
**Melee** gore +7 (1d8+6)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** trample (1d6+6, DC 16)

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 14, **Con** 17, **Int** 2, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 3  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +8 (+10 bull rush); **CMD** 20 (22 vs. bull rush, 32 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Weapon Focus (gore)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Endurance is part of Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (gore).*  
**Skills** Climb +8, Perception +6

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate hills  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cavalry (3–12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Spit (Ex)** Once per hour, a wolliped can regurgitate the contents of its stomach, spitting the foul material at a single target within 10 feet. The target must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or be sickened for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.
