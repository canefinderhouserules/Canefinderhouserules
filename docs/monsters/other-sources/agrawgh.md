## Agrawgh (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
N Large outsider (earth, extraplanar)  
**Init** +0; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 100 ft.; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 18, touch 9, flat-footed 18 (+9 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 59 (7d10+21)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +5, **Will** +1  
**Immune** acid

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., burrow 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +11 (2d6+6 plus 2d6 acid)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-ft. line, 6d4 acid damage, Reflex DC 16 half, usable every 1d4 rounds)

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 11, **Con** 16, **Int** 6, **Wis** 9, **Cha** 7  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +12 (+14 bull rush); **CMD** 22 (24 vs. bullrush, can't be tripped)  
**Feats** [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Lunge](../../rules/feats/lunge.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Intimidate +8, Perception +9, Stealth +6, Survival +9  
**Languages** Common, Terran

**Ecology**

**Environment** any underground (Plane of Earth)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or crew (3-12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Burrow (Ex)** An agrawgh can burrow through solid stone at half its normal burrow rate. When traveling using its burrow speed, as a free action an agrawgh can choose whether to leave a tunnel in its wake that is 10 feet tall by 10 feet wide. As long as the agrawgh does not encounter any non-dirt or non-stone substances along its way, it digests dirt and stone with its stomach acids at a rate equal to its burrow movement speed. An agrawgh can’t run or perform combat maneuvers while burrowing. If an agrawgh encounters any creature while burrowing (such as one using *meld with stone*), it can’t enter that square.
