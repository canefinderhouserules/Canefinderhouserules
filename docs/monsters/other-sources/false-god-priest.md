False God priests serve the same role as the clergy of most religions: spreading their faith and punishing heresy. The false god’s priests are quick to unleash their power on any who deny his divinity, or worse, who actually have evidence that would expose his ruse. Though they are primarily isolated in the nation of False God itself, priests of the Living God have begun travelling throughout the northern continent, particularly the nations on Lake Encarthan, with increasing regularity, leading some to believe that the false god has some sinister plan in the works beyond simply converting more followers into the fold through intimidation and force.
