## Genie-Touched Horse (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
N Large magical beast  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 11, flat-footed 17 (+2 Dex, +8 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 73 (7d10+35)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +7, **Will** +5  
**Resist** energy 10 (see genie-touched abilities)

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +12 (1d8+6), 2 hooves +11 (1d6+3)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.

**Statistics**

**Str** 22, **Dex** 15, **Con** 20, **Int** 4, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 11  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 26  
**Feats** [Endurance](../../rules/feats/endurance.md), Multiattack, [Run](../../rules/feats/run.md), [Weapon Focus (hooves)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +7, Perception +9, Survival +5  
**Languages** see below  
**SQ** genie-touched abilities

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or herd (3-10)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Genie-Touched Abilities** Each of the four breeds of genie-touched horse (istaheq, misayyah, tuanu, and zefaheen) has three unique genie-touched abilities. In addition, each breed has energy resistance to a specific form of energy and can understand (but cannot speak) one elemental language.
