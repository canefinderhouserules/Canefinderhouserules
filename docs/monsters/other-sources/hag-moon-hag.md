## Moon Hag (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
CE Medium monstrous humanoid (extraplanar)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +17

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 15, flat-footed 19 (+1 Dex, +4 insight, +5 natural)  
**hp** 126 (9d10+36)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +7, **Will** +9  
**Defensive Abilities** ferocity, lunatic insight; **Immune** *confusion*, *fear*, *insanity*; **SR** 18

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** bite +15 (1d6+5), 2 claws +14 (1d6+5)  
**Special Attacks** dreadful ravings, rend (2 claws, 1d6+3+7)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +11)  
At will—*[augury](../../magic/spells/augury.md)*, *[deathwatch](../../magic/spells/deathwatch.md)*, *[doom* ](../../magic/spells/doom.md)(DC 15)  
3/day—*[death knell* ](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)(DC 16), *[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)*, *[speak with dead* ](../../magic/spells/speak-with-dead.md)(DC 17)  
1/day—*[bestow curse* ](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)(DC 18), *[confusion* ](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)(DC 18), *[divination](../../magic/spells/divination.md)*, *[fear* ](../../magic/spells/fear.md)(DC 18), *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 12, **Con** 19, **Int** 14, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 29  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Skill Focus (Intimidate)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Intimidate +19, Knowledge (planes) +11, Knowledge (religion) +11, Perception +17, Sense Motive +14, Spellcraft +11  
**Languages** Aklo, Common

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Boneyard)  
**Organization** solitary or coven (three hags of any kind)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Dreadful Ravings (Su)** Three times per day as a standard action, the moon hag can howl a litany of dreadful prognostications at a creature within 30 feet. The predictions afflict the victim, gnawing away at its sanity. This is a language-dependent curse effect; if a moon (including the god of the end times) is visible to the victim, it takes a –2 penalty on the save against the effect.

 *Dreadful Ravings*—curse; *save* Will DC 18; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d3 Wisdom damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Lunatic Insight (Su)** A moon hag adds her Charisma modifier as an insight bonus to AC and on initiative checks. These bonuses increase by 2 when a moon (including the god of the end times) is visible above (this increase is not included in the statistics above).
