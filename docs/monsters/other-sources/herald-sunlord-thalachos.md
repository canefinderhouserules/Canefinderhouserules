## Sunlord Thalachos (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
NG Large outsider (angel, extraplanar, good, herald, shapechanger)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +24  
**Aura** protective aura (20 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 14, flat-footed 25 (+1 dodge, +4 Dex, +16 natural, –1 size; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 255 (15d10+105); regeneration 10 (evil effects or weapons)  
**Fort** +18, **Ref** +13, **Will** +13; +4 vs. poison, +4 resistance vs. evil  
**Defensive Abilities** uncanny dodge; **DR** 10/evil; **Immune** acid, cold, fire, petrification; **Resist** electricity 10; **SR** 26

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., fly 100 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+1 flaming holy scimitars* +19/+19/+14/+14/+9/+9 (1d8+8/18–20 plus 1d6 fire plus stun)  
**Ranged**  *+1 flaming holy composite longbow* +19/+14/+9 (2d6+8/×3 plus 1d6 fire)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** stun  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +21)  
At will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[continual flame](../../magic/spells/continual-flame.md)*, *[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[discern lies* ](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md)(DC 20), *[dispel evil* ](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)(DC 21), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[holy aura* ](../../magic/spells/holy-aura.md)(DC 24), *[holy smite* ](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)(DC 20), *[holy word* ](../../magic/spells/holy-word.md)(DC 23), *[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only), *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 21), *[remove curse](../../magic/spells/remove-curse.md)*, *[remove disease](../../magic/spells/remove-disease.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*  
7/day—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[daylight](../../magic/spells/daylight.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
1/day—*[blade barrier* ](../../magic/spells/blade-barrier.md)(DC 22), *[fire shield](../../magic/spells/fire-shield.md)*, *[flame strike* ](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)(DC 21), *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*, *[sunburst* ](../../magic/spells/sunburst.md)(DC 24)

**Statistics**

**Str** 24, **Dex** 19, **Con** 24, **Int** 19, **Wis** 23, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +23; **CMD** 38  
**Feats** [Double Slice](../../rules/feats/double-slice.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (scimitar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md)  
*House rules: Greater Two-Weapon Fighting and Improved Two-Weapon Fighting are one feat, Improved Two-Weapon Fighting; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +19, Diplomacy +24, Fly +6, Heal +21, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (history, planes, religion) +22, Perception +24, Sense Motive +24, Stealth +18  
**Languages** Celestial, Common; truespeech  
**SQ** divine trigger, change shape (Small or Medium humanoid or elemental, *alter self* or *elemental body II*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (extraplanar)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple (2 *+1 flaming holy scimitars*, *+1 flaming holy composite longbow* with 40 arrows)

**Special Abilities**

**Divine Trigger (Su)** Thalachos can activate any divine spell from a spell trigger item as if he were a 15th-level caster.

**Protective Aura (Su)** In addition to the normal effects of an angel’s protective aura, Thalachos’s aura grants the benefits of the *endure elements* spell.

**Stun (Su)** If Thalachos strikes a creature twice in the same round with his scimitars (whether two strikes with one scimitar or one strike with each), that creature must succeed at a DC 24 Fortitude save or be stunned 1 for 1d6 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.
