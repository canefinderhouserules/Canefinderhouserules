## Adult Bliss Dragon (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
NG Huge dragon (extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +32  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 27)

**Defense**

**AC** 32, touch 8, flat-footed 32 (+24 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 380 (20d12+140)  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +12, **Will** +17  
**DR** 5/evil; **Immune** ability damage, ability drain, electricity, paralysis, petrification, sleep; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +27 (2d8+13), 2 claws +28 (2d6+9/19–20), tail slap +22 (2d6+13), 2 wings +22 (1d8+4)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (100-ft. line, 12d10 electricity, DC 27), crush, peacemaker, subduing strikes  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +14)  
3rd (6/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*

1st (8/day)—*[deathwatch](../../magic/spells/deathwatch.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*, *[sanctuary](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md)* (DC 18), *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[detect poison](../../magic/spells/detect-poison.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 29, **Dex** 10, **Con** 25, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +31 (+35 disarm); **CMD** 41 (43 vs. disarm, 45 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Greater Disarm](../../rules/feats/greater-disarm.md), [Improved Critical (claw)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Self-Sufficient](../../rules/feats/self-sufficient.md), [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)
