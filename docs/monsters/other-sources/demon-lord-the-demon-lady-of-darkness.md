## the demon lady of darkness (CR 30)

**XP** 9,830,400  
CE Medium outsider (chaotic, demon, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +13; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect good*, *detect law*, see in darkness, *true seeing*; Perception +58  
**Aura** seductive presence (180 ft., DC 43), *unholy aura* (DC 33)

**Defense**

**AC** 49, touch 42, flat-footed 35 (+1 dodge, +4 deflection, +13 Dex, +7 natural, +14 profane)  
**hp** 936 (36d10+576); regeneration 30 (deific or mythic)  
**Fort** +32, **Ref** +37, **Will** +35  
**Defensive Abilities** Abyssal resurrection, *freedom of movement*; **DR** 20/cold iron, epic, and lawful; **Immune** ability damage and drain, charm and compulsion effects, death effects, electricity, energy drain, fire, petrification, poison; **Resist** acid 30, cold 30; **SR** 41

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 90 ft. (good)  
**Melee** 2 claws +49 (1d8+12 plus 1d4 Cha drain), 3 stings +48 (2d6+12/19–20 plus poison), 2 hooves +43 (1d4+6 plus 1d6 fire and burn), 2 wings +43 (1d4+6)  
**Ranged**  *Shadowkiss* +54/+49/+44/+39 (1d4+20/17–20 plus poison)  
**Special Attacks** burn (3d6 fire, DC 44), cruel shot, domination, energy drain, poison, profane ascension, sneak attack +4d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 30th)  
Constant—*[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*, *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*, *[unholy aura](../../magic/spells/unholy-aura.md)* (DC 33)  
At will—*[astral projection](../../magic/spells/astral-projection.md)*, *[blasphemy](../../magic/spells/blasphemy.md)* (DC 32), *[chaos hammer](../../magic/spells/chaos-hammer.md)* (DC 29), *[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*, *[desecrate](../../magic/spells/desecrate.md)*, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)*, *[power word blind](../../magic/spells/power-word-blind.md)*, *[telekinesis](../../magic/spells/telekinesis.md)* (DC 30), *[shapechange](../../magic/spells/shapechange.md)*, *[unhallow](../../magic/spells/unhallow.md)*  
3/day—*[finger of death](../../magic/spells/finger-of-death.md)* (DC 32), quickened *[mass suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion-mass.md)* (DC 31), summon demons, *[symbol of death](../../magic/spells/symbol-of-death.md)* (DC 33)  
1/day—*[soul bind](../../magic/spells/soul-bind.md)* (DC 34), *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)*, *[wail of the banshee](../../magic/spells/wail-of-the-banshee.md)* (DC 34)

**Statistics**

**Str** 34, **Dex** 36, **Con** 42, **Int** 35, **Wis** 32, **Cha** 40  
**Base Atk** +36; **CMB** +48; **CMD** 90  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), Craft Construct, [Craft Magic Arms and Armor](../../rules/feats/craft-magic-arms-and-armor.md), [Craft Wondrous Item](../../rules/feats/craft-wondrous-item.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), Flyby Attack, [Greater Feint](../../rules/feats/greater-feint.md), [Improved Critical (hand crossbow)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Critical (sting)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Feint](../../rules/feats/improved-feint.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), Quicken Spell-Like Ability (*mass suggestion*), [Rapid Reload (hand crossbow)](../../rules/feats/rapid-reload.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +52, Bluff +62, Diplomacy +54, Disguise +51, Fly +56, Intimidate +51, Knowledge (arcana, local, nobility) +48, Knowledge (planes, religion) +51, Perception +58, Perform (dance) +51, Sense Motive +50, Sleight of Hand +49, Spellcraft +48, Stealth +52, Use Magic Device +54; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Bluff, +8 Perception  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Undercommon; telepathy 300 ft., *tongues*  
**SQ** change shape (any humanoid; *alter self*), swift transformation

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  
**Organization** solitary (unique)  
**Treasure** triple (*Shadowkiss*, 100,000 gp in various jewelry, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Cruel Shot (Ex)** the demon lady of darkness is adept at making ranged attacks to strike cruel shots that deal significant and humiliating damage. She adds her Charisma bonus to all damage dealt by ranged weapons.

**Domination (Su)** As a standard action, the demon lady of darkness can crush a foe’s will. The target must be visible to the demon lady of darkness and within 120 feet. It must succeed at a DC 43 Will save or fall instantly under the demon lady of darkness’s influence as *dominate monster* (CL 30th). If she uses domination against a humanoid creature, she may instead choose to use the ability as a swift action, and it functions as *dominate person*. As long as the target is under this effect, it gains a +4 profane bonus on all saving throws against targets other than the demon lady of darkness.

**Energy Drain (Su)** the demon lady of darkness’s energy drain functions identically to that of a succubus, except that she drains 2 levels when she uses this ability against mythic creatures, or 1d6+4 levels against non-mythic creatures.

**Poison (Ex)** Sting or hand crossbow—injury; *save* Fort DC 44; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 Wisdom drain plus paralysis for 1 round. Anyone who fails two consecutive saves against this poison is permanently blinded. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Profane Ascension (Su)** As a swift action while in an act of passion with a willing mortal, the demon lady of darkness may grant a profane ascension. The target’s name appears in glowing Abyssal runes on the demon lady of darkness’s wings, and a crimson mark manifests somewhere on the target’s body. The target immediately gains a +6 profane bonus to any one ability score of its choice, a +4 profane bonus to any other ability score of its choice, and the see in darkness ability. A single creature may have only one profane ascension in effect at any one time. As long as the effect persists, the demon lady of darkness can communicate telepathically with the target across any distance and may use any of her spell-like abilities through the target, manifesting them as if the target were using them. A profane ascension may be removed by a *miracle* or *wish*. The demon lady of darkness can remove it as a free action, dealing 4d6 points of Charisma drain and imparting 1d10+10 permanent negative levels to the victim.

**Seductive Presence (Su)** Unlike most demon lords, the demon lady of darkness does not possess a frightful presence ability. Rather, she has a seductive presence that she can activate as a free action whenever she speaks or uses a spell-like ability. Anyone within 180 feet who fails a DC 43 Fortitude save loses any immunity to mind-affecting effects, charm effects, and compulsion effects, and becomes fascinated by the demon lady of darkness for 5d4 rounds. A creature affected by a mind-affecting effect while within this aura remains affected even after leaving the demon lady of darkness’s seductive presence. Creatures that succeed at this saving throw are immune to this ability for 24 hours. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Shadowkiss** the demon lady of darkness’s favored weapon is Shadowkiss, a *+5 unholy hand crossbow* that magically creates ammunition as it fires. Once a target is damaged by a bolt fired from *Shadowkiss*, the hand crossbow gains the *bane* weapon special ability against that target’s creature type on all further attacks. *Shadowkiss* may only have one bane effect in place at one time. Bolts fired from *Shadowkiss* gain the *ghost touch* ability (an effect not normally available to ranged weapons).

**Swift Transformation (Ex)** the demon lady of darkness can use her change shape ability as a free action.
