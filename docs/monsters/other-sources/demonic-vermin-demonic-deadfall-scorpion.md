## Demonic Deadfall Scorpion (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
CE Huge magical beast (augmented vermin)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense 60 ft.; Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 11, flat-footed 21 (+2 Dex, +1 dodge, +13 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 150 (10d8+70)  
**Fort** +13, **Ref** +7, **Will** +7  
**DR** 5/cold iron; **Immune** electricity, poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10, fire 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +16 (1d8+10 plus grab), sting +15 (1d8+10 plus poison)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** death throes (DC 21, 9d6 cold damage), constrict (1d8+10), sudden strike  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +11)  
1/day—*[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[insect plague](../../magic/spells/insect-plague.md)*, *[vomit swarm](../../magic/spells/vomit-swarm.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 15, **Con** 22, **Int** 10, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +19 (+23 grapple); **CMD** 32 (44 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +15, Climb +23, Perception +19, Stealth +21 (+29 in forests); **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception, +8 Stealth (+16 in forests)  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or colony (3–10)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Poison (Ex)** Sting—injury; *save* Fort DC 21; *frequency* 1/ round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 Str damage; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.

**Sudden Strike (Ex)** During a surprise round, a demonic deadfall scorpion may act as if it had a full round to act, rather than just one standard action.
