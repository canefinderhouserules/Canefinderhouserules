## Unchosen (CR 4)

Usually CE Large humanoid (gnoll)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., deathwatch, scent; Listen +4, Spot +4

**Defense**

**AC** 17, touch 11, flat-footed 17 (+2 insight, +6 natural, -1 size)  
**hp** 42 (5d8+20)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +1, **Will** +1  
**Defensive Abilities** foresighted; **Immune** charm, fear

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +8 (1d6+6) and bite +3 (1d8+3)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** ferocity

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 10, **Con** 19, **Int** 4, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +3; **Grapple** +13  
**Feats** [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md)  
**Skills** Climb +10, Jump +14; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Listen, +4 Spot  
**Languages** Gnoll

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary or pack (6-12)  
**Treasure** standard  
**Advancement** by character class; **Favored Class** barbarian  
**Level Adjustment** +3

**Special Abilities**

**Deathwatch (Ex)** The tampering with the unchosen’s brain has granted the creature the ability to “scent life.” As long as targets are in range of the unchosen’s scent ability, it instantly knows whether each creture in the area is dead, fragile (alive and wounded with 3 or fewer hit points left), fighting off death (alive with 4 or more hit points), undead, or neither alive nor dead (such as a construct).

**Ferocity (Ex)** An unchosen becomes enraged if mortally wounded. It continues to fight without penalty even while disabled or dying, and as long as it has less than 0 hit points, it gains a +2 bonus on attack rolls and all weapon damage rolls.

**Foresighted (Su)** An unchosen’s trepanation has partially unlocked an almost psychic ability to sense peril. It gains a +2 insight bonus on Initiative checks and to its armor class.
