## The Mourning One (CR 19)

**XP** 204,800  
Unique black-blooded sea serpent  
CE Colossal aberration (aquatic)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft.; Perception +8  
**Aura** blood rain (30 ft., 9 cold damage)

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 5, flat-footed 26 (+3 Dex, +24 natural, –8 size)  
**hp** 500 (25d10+250)  
**Fort** +23, **Ref** +19, **Will** +10  
**Defensive Abilities** elusive; **Immune** ability drain, cold, energy drain, poison; **Resist** fire 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +39 (6d6+17/19–20 plus 1d6 cold and grab), tail slap +33 (4d6+8 plus 1d6 cold and grab)  
**Space** 30 ft., **Reach** 20 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-ft. cone, 6d6 cold damage, Reflex DC 31 for half, usable every 1d4 rounds), capsize, constrict (3d6+18), swallow whole (4d8+18 bludgeoning damage, AC 21, 38 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 44, **Dex** 16, **Con** 29, **Int** 2, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +25; **CMB** +46 (+48 bull rush, +50 grapple); **CMD** 59 (61 vs. bull rush, can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), Improved Natural Attack (bite), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), [Skill Focus (Stealth)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Escape Artist +5, Perception +9, Stealth +19, Swim +25  
**SQ** amphibious

**Special Abilities**

**Blood Rain (Su)** Black-blooded creatures constantly leak and spray bursts of freezing black blood. Any creature within 15 feet of a black-blooded creature takes an amount of cold damage equal to the black-blooded creature’s Constitution modifier.

**Elusive (Su)** See page 244 of the *Pathfinder RPG Bestiary*.

**Tainted Life (Ex)** The blood of a black-blooded creature is antithetical to all life and consumes all but the heartiest hosts. Any creature with the black-blooded template must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save every day or take 1d4 points of Constitution damage.
