## Chuul, Lesser (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
CE Medium aberration (aquatic)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +11

**Defense**

**AC** 17, touch 12, flat-footed 15  (+2 Dex, +5 natural)  
**hp** 55 (5d8+15)  
**Fort** +4, **Ref** +3, **Will** +5

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 20 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +8 (1d8+5 plus grab), tendril +3 (1d4+2 plus staggering tendril)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with tendril)  
**Special Attacks** staggering tendril (DC 15)

**Statistics**

**Str** 21, **Dex** 14, **Con** 16, **Int** 8, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 5  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +8 (+12 grapple); **CMD** 20 (24 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md)  
**Skills** Perception +11, Sense Motive +8, Stealth +10, Swim +13  
**Languages** Common (Undercommon instead for many subterranean variants)  
**SQ** amphibious

**Special Abilities**

**Staggering Tendril (Ex)** A lesser chuul’s long, searching tendrils are coated in a mildly paralytic secretion. A creature struck by a lesser chuul’s tendrils must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or be staggered for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.
