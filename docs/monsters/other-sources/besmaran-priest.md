Priests of the pirate goddess are common fixtures on pirate vessels sailing the pirate isles, simultaneously serving as healers and spiritual guides for comrades who often have no time for religion until death is already grinning in their faces. While clerics of the pirate goddess often end up captaining ships of their own, they usually spend most of their pirating careers as first mates or ship’s surgeons.

When not needed to confront a more immediate peril, a cleric of the pirate goddess sees to the health of the crew, purifying spoiled food and water, healing the inevitable injuries sailors acquire in the course of their duties, or curing crew members of the diseases so widespread in the tropics and the sordid brothels that are a staple of every seaside town. If these duties spare her any time, a Besmaran priest goes out of her way to assist others in their tasks onboard and learn everything there is to know about running a ship and crew, for there is no telling what duties will be required aboard the pirate goddess’s own Seawraith should the cleric earn a coveted berth in the afterlife. In addition, every priest of the pirate goddess is expected to make a pilgrimage to the mysterious island called the pirate goddess’s Throne at least once in her life, and shipmates greeting a cleric upon her return are often struck by the renewed fervor with which she sets about her tasks.
