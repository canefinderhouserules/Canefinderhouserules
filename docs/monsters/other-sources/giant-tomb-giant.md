## Tomb Giant (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
NE Large humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +15

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 12, flat-footed 25 (+6 armor, +3 Dex, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 162 (13d8+104)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +7, **Will** +10  
**Defensive Abilities** negative energy affinity, rock catching; **Immune** death effects, paralysis

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft. (30 ft. in armor)  
**Melee** mwk scythe +21/+16 (2d6+16/19–20/×4 plus energy drain) or 2 slams +19 (1d6+11 plus energy drain)  
**Ranged**  rock +12 (1d8+11)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** energy drain (1 level, DC 18), rock throwing (120 ft.)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +14)  
Constant—*[deathwatch](../../magic/spells/deathwatch.md)*  
At will—*[detect undead](../../magic/spells/detect-undead.md)*  
3/day—*[make whole](../../magic/spells/make-whole.md)*, *[sculpt corpse](../../magic/spells/sculpt-corpse.md)*  
1/day—*[animate dead](../../magic/spells/animate-dead.md)*, *[control undead](../../magic/spells/control-undead.md)* (DC 19)

**Statistics**

**Str** 32, **Dex** 17, **Con** 26, **Int** 13, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +21 (+23 trip); **CMD** 34 (36 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Improved Critical (scythe)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Improved Trip](../../rules/feats/improved-trip.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Martial Weapon Proficiency (scythe)](../../rules/feats/martial-weapon-proficiency.md), [Weapon Focus (scythe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (scythe).*  
**Skills** Climb +12, Heal +12, Knowledge (religion) +14, Perception +15, Stealth +5, Survival +10  
**Languages** Common, Giant  
**SQ** corpse stitcher, sinister synergy

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land or underground  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cabal (3–13)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk breastplate, mwk scythe)

**Special Abilities**

**Corpse Stitcher (Sp)** Tomb giants can cast *make whole* as a spell-like ability, but only for the purposes of creating undead creatures. For example, a tomb giant can use this ability to aid in the creation of a necrocraft, to restore armor to be used for the creation of a phantom armor, or even to repair the armor of a graveknight.

**Energy Drain (Su)** A tomb giant can channel its energy drain attack through any melee weapon it wields.

**Sinister Synergy (Su)** Multiple tomb giants can combine their efforts to gain the ability to create undead. When two or more tomb giants are within 30 feet of each other, they can work together to use *create undead* as a spell-like ability (caster level 13th). Three or more tomb giants working in unison in this way can use *greater create undead* as a spell-like ability (caster level 15th). Every additional tomb giant beyond the third who participates in this synergy increases the caster level of this effect by 1.
