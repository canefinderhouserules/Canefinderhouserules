## Water Wraith (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
CN Large magical beast (aquatic)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 16, touch 11, flat-footed 14 (+1 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 19 (3d10+3)  
**Fort** +4, **Ref** +4, **Will** +2

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., swim 40 ft.  
**Melee** bite +4 (1d8+2 plus attach), 4 claws +4 (1d6+2)  
**Special Attacks** blood drain (1d2 Constitution), rend (2 claws, 1d3+3)

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 13, **Con** 13, **Int** 3, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 6  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6 (+10 when attached); **CMD** 18 (30 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Perception +5, Stealth +1, Swim +14  
**SQ** amphibious, expressive chameleon

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm rivers (the southern continent)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or swarm (3–12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Expressive Chameleon (Ex)** Once per round as a free action, a water wraith can change its color to any of its own choosing. A water wraith using this ability to camouflage itself gains a +8 bonus on Stealth checks.
