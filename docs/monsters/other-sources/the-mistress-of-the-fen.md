## The Mistress of the Fen (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Female unique water orm  
N Huge magical beast (aquatic)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., low-light vision; Perception +13

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 12, flat-footed 22 (+4 Dex, +14 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 225 (15d10+75); swamp healing 5  
**Fort** +14, **Ref** +13, **Will** +8  
**Defensive Abilities** elusive; **Immune** cold, poison; **Resist** electricity 20, fire 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., swim 50 ft.  
**Melee** bite +22 (4d6+8/19–20 plus grab and poison), tail slap +16 (2d8+4)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft. (20 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** swallow whole (3d6+12 bludgeoning damage, AC 17, 15 hp)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +15)  
At will—*[control water](../../magic/spells/control-water.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*  
5/day—*[diminish plants](../../magic/spells/diminish-plants.md)*, *[plant growth](../../magic/spells/plant-growth.md)*  
3/day—*[command plants](../../magic/spells/command-plants.md)*, *[thorn body](../../magic/spells/thorn-body.md)*, *[wall of thorns](../../magic/spells/wall-of-thorns.md)*  
1/day—*[hydraulic torrent](../../magic/spells/hydraulic-torrent.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 26, **Dex** 18, **Con** 21, **Int** 4, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 11  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +25 (+27 bull rush, +29 grapple); **CMD** 39 (41 vs. bull rush, 43 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Sickening Critical](../../rules/feats/sickening-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Perception +13, Stealth +9 (+17 in swamps), Swim +16; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception, +8 Stealth in swamps  
**Languages** Aquan (can’t speak)  
**SQ** amphibious, water travel

**Special Abilities**

**Elusive (Su)** See page 280 of *Pathfinder RPG Bestiary 2*.

**Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 22; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d3 Con and 1d3 Dex; cure 2 consecutive saves.

**Swamp Healing (Su)** As long as she is in a swamp, bog, or other wetland, the Mistress of the Fen gains fast healing 5.

**Water Travel (Su)** See page 280 of *Pathfinder RPG Bestiary 2*.
