## Clockwork Gunslinger (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
N Medium construct (clockwork)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +0

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 15, flat-footed 15 (+3 Dex, +2 dodge, +5 natural)  
**hp** 69 (9d10+20)  
**Fort** +3, **Ref** +8, **Will** +3  
**Immune** construct traits  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to electricity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** slam +14 (1d6+7)  
**Ranged** pistol +12 (1d8/x4)  
**Special Attacks** captive time

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 16, **Con** —, **Int** —, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 1  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 29  
**Feats** [Deadly AimB](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md), [Improved InitiativeB](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Lightning ReflexesB](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Point-Blank ShotB](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Precise ShotB](../../rules/feats/precise-shot.md)  
**SQ** difficult to create, integrated clockwork pistol, swift reactions, winding

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or squad (3-8)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Captive Time (Ex)** Once per hour, a clockwork gunslinger can rotate its built-in hourglass as a swift action to instantly reload its pistol and gain the effects of *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)* for 1 round. Additionally, the first time a clockwork gunslinger confirms a critical hit against an opponent, its hourglass shatters and each creature within a 30-foot radius of the clockwork gunslinger must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude save or be slowed as per the *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)* spell for 3 rounds. Once the hourglass is shattered, the clockwork gunslinger is unable to use this ability until a new hourglass is installed. This save DC is Constitution-based.

**Integrated Clockwork Pistol (Ex)** One of a clockwork gunslinger’s arms ends in an integrated clockwork pistol. This weapon cannot be disarmed, sundered, or removed. It is fed bullets automatically from an internal cache inside the clockwork’s body. Its programming grants [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), and [Precise Shot](../../rules/feats/precise-shot.md) as bonus feats.
