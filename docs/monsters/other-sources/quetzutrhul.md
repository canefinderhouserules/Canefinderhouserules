## Quetzutrhul (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
Rogue mutant intellect devourer  
CE Small aberration  
**Init** +17; **Senses** blindsight 60 ft., detect magic; Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 23, flat-footed 16 (+11 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 135 (9d8+63); fast healing 5  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +14, **Will** +8  
**Defensive Abilities** evasion, uncanny dodge; **DR** 10/adamantine, 10/magic; **Immune** fire, mind-affecting effects; **Resist** cold 20, electricity 20, sonic 20; **SR** 23  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to *protection from evil*

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 returning dagger* +19/+14 (1d3+12/19–20), 4 claws +16 (1d4+11)  
**Ranged**  *+1 returning dagger* +19 (1d3+1/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** body thief, sneak attack +7d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +9)  
Constant—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*  
At will—*[confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)* (single target only) (DC 15), *[daze monster](../../magic/spells/daze-monster.md)* (no HD limit) (DC 12), *[inflict serious wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-serious-wounds.md)* (DC 14), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *reduce size* (as per *[reduce person](../../magic/spells/reduce-person.md)* but self only)  
3/day—*[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[globe of invulnerability](../../magic/spells/globe-of-invulnerability.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 10, **Dex** 32, **Con** 22, **Int** 20, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +16; **CMD** 27 (31 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Multiattack, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +22 (+26 when jumping), Bluff +17, Climb +11, Disguise +9, Escape Artist +22, Knowledge (local) +16, Perception +19, Sense Motive +8, Stealth +34, Use Magic Device +10; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Bluff, +8 Perception, +8 Stealth  
**Languages** Common, Elven, Orc, Terran, Undercommon, Frontier (can’t speak); telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** deformities (poor ability [Charisma]), mutations (celerity, extra arm, fast healing), prehensile proboscis  
**Gear** *+1 dagger*

**Special Abilities**

**Prehensile Proboscis (Ex)** Quetzutrhul’s extra arm mutation manifests as a prehensile proboscis. It receives Multiattack as a bonus feat instead of Multiweapon Attack.
