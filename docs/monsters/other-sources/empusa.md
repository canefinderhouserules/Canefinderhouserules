## Empusa (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
CN Medium outsider (chaotic, extraplanar, shapechanger)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +21

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 18, flat-footed 20 (+8 Dex, +10 natural)  
**hp** 174 (12d10+108); fast healing 5  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +16, **Will** +10  
**DR** 10/lawful; **Immune** disease, electricity, poison; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** lash +22/+17/+12 (1d6+5/×2 plus poison)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with lash)  
**Special Attacks** lash, sneak attack +3d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th; concentration +19)  
Constant—*[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
At will—*[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[major image](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)* (DC 20), *[modify memory](../../magic/spells/modify-memory.md)* (DC 20), *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 19)  
3/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 20), *phantasmal vengeance* (DC 20), *[teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of gear only)  
1/day—*[mislead](../../magic/spells/mislead.md)* (DC 22), *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 26, **Con** 29, **Int** 18, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 23  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +20; **CMD** 33  
**Feats** [Agile Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/agile-maneuvers.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Deceitful](../../rules/feats/deceitful.md), [Improved Feint](../../rules/feats/improved-feint.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
**Skills** Acrobatics +23, Bluff +25, Disguise +25, Fly +27, Knowledge (planes) +13, Knowledge (religion) +10, Perception +21, Ride +23, Sense Motive +21, Sleight of Hand +23, Stealth +23  
**Languages** Common, Elven; *tongues*  
**SQ** change shape (elf, alter self)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Elysium)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or vengeance (3–6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Lash (Su)** As a move action, an empusa can manifest a whip-like weapon that appears to be a coiling serpent—once manifested, the lash exists as long as the empusa carries it. A lash vanishes if it is disarmed or the empusa drops it or is slain. An empusa’s lash has a reach of 10 feet and deals 1d6 points of slashing damage on a hit. In addition, a creature damaged by the lash is affected by its toxic venom.

 *Lash Venom*: Lash—injury; *save* Fort DC 25; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 Wis damage plus confused for 1 round; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Phantasmal Vengeance (Sp)** This spell-like ability functions as *phantasmal killer*, save that the image it creates is of a creature or person who the viewer has wronged in their past (or of the empusa herself if there are no other appropriate options). If the target of a phantasmal vengeance fails his Fortitude save, the empusa can opt to have the victim become paralyzed 1 for 1 hour rather than die from fear.
