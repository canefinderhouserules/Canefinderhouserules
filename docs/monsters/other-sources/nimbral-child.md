## Nimbral Child (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
CE Medium outsider (evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., see in darkness; Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 20, flat-footed 21 (+5 deflection, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +6 natural)  
**hp** 196 (14d10+56)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +15, **Will** +13  
**Defensive Abilities** dark nimbus; **Immune** blindness, cold, poison; **Resist** acid 10, electricity 10, fire 10  
**Weaknesses** light sensitivity

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +19 (2d8+3 plus 4d6 cold)  
**Special Attacks** breath of the void (30-ft. cone, 10d6 cold damage plus slowed, Reflex DC 21 for half, usable every 1d4 rounds), penumbral web (+18 ranged, DC 21, 14 hp)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +19)  
At will—*[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only)  
3/day—*[shadow conjuration](../../magic/spells/shadow-conjuration.md)* (DC 19), *[shadow evocation](../../magic/spells/shadow-evocation.md)* (DC 20), *[shadow walk](../../magic/spells/shadow-walk.md)* (DC 21), *[spell turning](../../magic/spells/spell-turning.md)*  
1/day—*[greater shadow evocation](../../magic/spells/shadow-evocation-greater.md)* (DC 23), *[power word blind](../../magic/spells/power-word-blind.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 19, **Con** 18, **Int** 13, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 37  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (claw)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (claw).*  
**Skills** Bluff +22, Climb +11, Intimidate +22, Knowledge (planes) +18, Perception +19, Spellcraft +18, Stealth +21, Use Magic Device +22  
**Languages** telepathy 120 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land (Shadow Plane)  
**Organization** solitary or gloaming (2-6)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Breath of the Void (Su)** A nimbral child’s breath weapon is a blast of howling, frigid winds drawn from the void. A nimbral child cannot use this ability while it is within an area of bright light. A creature that fails its saving throw against the nimbral child’s breath weapon is slowed (as per the *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)* spell) for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Dark Nimbus (Su)** Shadows swirl around a nimbral child, granting it concealment from attacks and a deflection bonus to its Armor Class equal to its Charisma modifier.

**Penumbral Web (Su)** Eight times per day as a standard action, a nimbral child can project gray, shadowy webs as per the web universal monster rule. In addition to being subject to the webs’ entangling effect, any creature not native to the Shadow Plane touching a penumbral web takes 1d6 points of Strength damage and is paralyzed 1 for 1d4 rounds (Fortitude DC 21 to halve the damage and negate the paralysis). A nimbral child can also create sheets of penumbral webbing. These sheets do not burn but can be pushed aside, although touching them affects a target as per a successful penumbral web attack. If a nimbral child is destroyed, all penumbral webs it has created dissolve in 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Constitution-based.
