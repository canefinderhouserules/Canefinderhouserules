## Karumzek (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
NE Medium outsider (evil, extraplanar, shapechanger)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, sense poison; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 17, touch 12, flat-footed 15 (+2 Dex, +5 natural)  
**hp** 60 (5d10+10)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +6, **Will** +2  
**DR** 5/good; **Immune** poison; **SR** 15

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 20 ft.  
**Melee** bite +8 (1d8+3 plus poison)  
**Special Attacks** poison, sneak attack +1d6, web (+7 ranged, DC 14, 5 hp)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th; concentration +6)  
Constant—*[negate aroma](../../magic/spells/negate-aroma.md)*  
At will—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*  
3/day—*[alchemical allocation](../../magic/spells/alchemical-allocation.md)*, *[death knell* ](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)(DC 12), *[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only), *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[vomit swarm](../../magic/spells/vomit-swarm.md)* (spiders only)

**Statistics**

**Str** 15, **Dex** 14, **Con** 15, **Int** 8, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +5; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 19 (31 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Climb +10, Craft (alchemy) +7, Knowledge (nature) +7, Knowledge (religion) +7, Perception +9, Stealth +14; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth  
**Languages** Abyssal, Infernal, Undercommon  
**SQ** change shape (Diminutive spider; *vermin shape II*), deft limbs

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Axis)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or swarm (3–12)  
**Treasure** standard (usually alchemical weapons)

**Special Abilities**

**Deft Limbs (Ex)** A karumzek’s front pair of feet are as nimble and dexterous as human hands. It takes a karumzek half the normal amount of time to create alchemical items.

**Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 14; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d3 Str, Dex, or Con (karumzek’s choice); *cure* 2 consecutive saves.

**Sense Poison (Ex)** As a free action, a karumzek can detect if a creature within 15 feet of it is poisoned. It can attempt to determine the exact type of poison, as if using *detect poison*.
