## Cave Wizard (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
Dwarf evoker 9  
NE Medium humanoid (dwarf)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +12

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 10, flat-footed 15 (+4 armor, +1 deflection, –1 Dex, +1 natural)  
**hp** 112 (9d6+58)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +2, **Will** +9; +2 vs. poison, spells, and spell-like abilities  
**Defensive Abilities** defensive training (+4 dodge bonus to AC vs. giants); **Immune** fire (108 points)

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** battleaxe +5 (1d8+1/×3)  
**Special Attacks** +1 on attack rolls against goblinoid and orc humanoids, intense spells (+4 damage)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +13)  
At will—elemental wall (9 rounds/day)  
7/day—force missile (1d4+4)  
**Evoker Spells Prepared** (CL 9th; concentration +13)  
5th—*[cone of cold* ](../../magic/spells/cone-of-cold.md)(DC 20), *[transmute rock to mud](../../magic/spells/transmute-rock-to-mud.md)*  
4th—empowered *[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*, *[resilient sphere* ](../../magic/spells/resilient-sphere.md)(DC 19), *[stone shape](../../magic/spells/stone-shape.md)*  
3rd—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fireball* ](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)(DC 18), *[gaseous form](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)*, *[ray of exhaustion* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-exhaustion.md)(DC 17), *[stinking cloud* ](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)(DC 17)  
2nd—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*, *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*, *[spider climb](../../magic/spells/spider-climb.md)*, *[summon swarm](../../magic/spells/summon-swarm.md)*  
1st—*[burning hands* ](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)(2, DC 16), *[color spray* ](../../magic/spells/color-spray.md)(DC 15), *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)(DC 15)  
0 (at will)—*[acid splash](../../magic/spells/acid-splash.md)*, *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*  
**Opposition Schools** enchantment, necromancy

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The wizard casts *bear’s endurance*, *mage armor*, and *protection from energy* (fire).  
**During Combat** The wizard casts *greater invisibility*, then uses *transmute rock to mud* to trap foes. He casts area damage spells at trapped targets and uses *stinking cloud* and *resilient sphere* to hinder those who escape the mud.  
**Base Statistics** Without *bear’s endurance*, *mage armor*, and *protection from energy* (fire), the wizard’s statistics are **AC** 11, touch 10, flat-footed 11; **hp** 74; **Fort** +6; **Immune** none; **Con** 16.

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 8, **Con** 20, **Int** 18, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +5; **CMD** 15 (19 vs. bull rush or trip)  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Empower Spell](../../rules/feats/empower-spell.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Scribe Scroll](../../rules/feats/scribe-scroll.md), [Spell Focus (evocation)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/spell-penetration.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**Skills** Appraise +15 (+17 to assess nonmagical metals or gemstones), Climb +4, Craft (alchemy) +12, Fly +6, Knowledge (arcana, dungeoneering) +16, Knowledge (engineering) +12, Perception +12 (+14 to notice unusual stonework), Sense Motive +4, Spellcraft +16  
**Languages** Common, Dwarven, Goblin, Terran, Undercommon  
**SQ** arcane bond (bat)  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*, *scroll of clairaudience/clairvoyance*, *scroll of solid fog*, *scroll of stinking cloud*, *scroll of stone shape*; **Other Gear** battleaxe, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *headband of vast intelligence +2*, *ring of protection +1*, spellbook, 665 gp

The cave wizard manipulates the energy of deep rock.
