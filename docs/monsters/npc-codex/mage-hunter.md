## Mage Hunter (CR 18)

**XP** 153,600  
Human ranger 19  
CE Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +24

**Defense**

**AC** 34, touch 19, flat-footed 28 (+10 armor, +3 deflection, +5 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural)  
**hp** 295 (19d10+105)  
**Fort** +21, **Ref** +24, **Will** +14  
**Defensive Abilities** improved evasion, *nondetection*; **Immune** electricity (120 points), fire (120 points), poison; **Resist** cold 30, electricity 30, fire 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 greataxe* +21/+16/+11/+6 (1d12+2/×3)  
**Ranged**  *+1 frost longbow* +27/+22/+17/+12 (1d8+1/19–20/×3 plus 1d6 cold)  
**Special Attacks** favored enemy (elves +6, gnomes +2, humans +4, magical beasts +2)  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 16th; concentration +18)  
4th—*[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[nondetection](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md)*  
3rd—*[darkvision](../../magic/spells/darkvision.md)*, *[neutralize poison](../../magic/spells/neutralize-poison.md)*, *[repel vermin](../../magic/spells/repel-vermin.md)*  
2nd—*[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[protection from energy* ](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)(2)  
1st—*[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(3)

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The ranger casts *[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[darkvision](../../magic/spells/darkvision.md)*, *[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, *[nondetection](../../magic/spells/nondetection.md)*, *[protection from energy* ](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)(electricity, fire), and *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(cold, electricity, fire).  
**During Combat** The ranger slays spellcasters with *bane* arrows. He rings his *chime of interruption* to hamper spellcasting.  
**Base Statistics** Without *barkskin*, *bear’s endurance*, *darkvision*, *longstrider*, *nondetection*, *protection from energy*, and *resist energy*, the ranger’s statistics are **Senses** normal; **hp** 157; **Fort** +17; **Defensive Abilities** improved evasion; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft.; **Con** 14; **Skills** Acrobatics +22.

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 24, **Con** 18, **Int** 10, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +19; **CMB** +20; **CMD** 41  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Critical (longbow)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Improved Precise Shot](../../rules/feats/improved-precise-shot.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Manyshot](../../rules/feats/manyshot.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Quick Draw](../../rules/feats/quick-draw.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Stunning Critical](../../rules/feats/stunning-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (greataxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Acrobatics +22 (+26 when jumping), Climb +14, Handle Animal +7, Heal +10, Knowledge (arcana, local) +10, Knowledge (dungeoneering, geography) +8, Knowledge (history) +5, Knowledge (nature) +7, Linguistics +1, Perception +24, Ride +14, Spellcraft +13, Stealth +29, Survival +15, Swim +9  
**Languages** Common, Draconic

**Combat Gear**  *+1 elf-bane arrows* (5), *+1 flaming arrows* (5), *+1 gnome-bane arrows* (5), *+1 human-bane arrows* (10), *+1 shock arrows* (5), *chime of interruption*, *potions of invisibility* (2), *wand of cure serious wounds* (20 charges); **Other Gear** *+4 mithral breastplate*, *+1 frost longbow* with 20 arrows, *+1 greataxe*, *bag of holding* (type I), *belt of incredible dexterity +4*, *cloak of resistance +4*, *ring of protection +3*, diamond dust for *nondetection* (worth 50 gp), 2,574 gp

A mage hunter thrills at killing sorcerers and wizards. Knowing how to circumvent their hated spells, he casts *nondetection* every day to deter pursuit and scrying.
