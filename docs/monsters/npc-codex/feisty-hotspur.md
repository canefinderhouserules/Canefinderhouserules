## Feisty Hotspur (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Halfling rogue 9/duelist 4  
NE Small humanoid (halfling)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** Perception +19

**Defense**

**AC** 26, touch 19, flat-footed 19 (+6 armor, +1 deflection, +6 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 160 (9d8+4d10+48)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +18, **Will** +10; +2 vs. fear  
**Defensive Abilities** canny defense +2, enhanced mobility, evasion, grace +2, improved uncanny dodge, parry, trap sense +3

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 rapier* +18/+13 (1d4+6/18–20)  
**Special Attacks** sneak attack +5d6, precise strike +4

**Tactics**

**During Combat** The duelist drinks her *potion of haste*. She attempts to disarm opponents, then makes sneak attacks even if she must feint to do so.

**Statistics**

**Str** 8, **Dex** 20, **Con** 15, **Int** 14, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +14 (+16 to disarm); **CMD** 25 (27 vs. disarm)  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Greater Feint](../../rules/feats/greater-feint.md), [Deft Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/deft-maneuvers.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Focus (rapier)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Agile Maneuvers and Weapon Finesse are one feat, Weapon Finesse; Improved Disarm, Improved Feint are covered by Deft Maneuvers; in their place: Improved Initiative, Toughness, Great Fortitude; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +23 (+19 when jumping), Bluff +17, Climb +1, Diplomacy +17, Disguise +17, Intimidate +17, Knowledge (dungeoneering, local) +10, Knowledge (nobility) +7, Perception +19, Perform (comedy) +5, Perform (sing) +6, Sleight of Hand +13, Stealth +25  
**Languages** Common, Halfling  
**SQ** improved reaction +2, rogue talents (combat trick, finesse rogue, surprise attack, weapon training), trapfinding +4  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of cure serious wounds* (2), *potion of haste*; **Other Gear** *+2 mithral chain shirt*, *+1 rapier*, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *belt of physical might +2* (Str, Con), *cloak of resistance +2*, *ring of protection +1*, 180 gp

Anyone who questions the martial prowess of halflings should keep quiet around feisty hotspurs, as these diminutive bravos excel at poking holes in large people who underestimate them.
