Human fighter 10/duelist 10

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 22, flat-footed 20 (+3 armor, +3 deflection, +7 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 insight, +3 natural)  
**hp** 310 (10d10+10d10+110)

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 human-bane shock rapier* +29/+24/+19/+14 (1d6+11/15–20 plus 1d6 electricity)
