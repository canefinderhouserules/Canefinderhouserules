## Castaway (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
Half-orc druid 12  
LN Medium humanoid (human, orc)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +17

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 15, flat-footed 17 (+3 armor, +1 deflection, +3 Dex, +1 dodge, +3 shield)  
**hp** 118 (12d8+22)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +9, **Will** +14; +4 vs. fey and plant-targeted effects  
**Defensive Abilities** orc ferocity; **Immune** poison

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 scimitar* +12/+7 (1d6+3/18–20) or mwk club +12/+7 (1d6+2)  
**Ranged**  dart +12/+7 (1d4+2)  
**Special Attacks** wild shape 5/day  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +16)  
12/day—lightning lord  
7/day—storm burst  
**Druid Spells Prepared** (CL 12th; concentration +16)  
6th—*[control winds](../../magic/spells/control-winds.md)*<sup>D</sup>(DC 20), *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[repel wood](../../magic/spells/repel-wood.md)*  
5th—*[call lightning storm* ](../../magic/spells/call-lightning-storm.md)(DC 19), *[cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds.md)*, *[ice storm](../../magic/spells/ice-storm.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[tree stride](../../magic/spells/tree-stride.md)*  
4th—*[cure serious wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md)*, *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[flame strike* ](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)(DC 18), *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[sleet storm](../../magic/spells/sleet-storm.md)*<sup>D</sup>  
3rd—*[call lightning](../../magic/spells/call-lightning.md)*<sup>D</sup>(DC 17), *[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[greater magic fang* ](../../magic/spells/magic-fang-greater.md)(3), *[protection from energy* ](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)(DC 17)  
2nd—*[barkskin* ](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)(2), *[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[bull’s strength](../../magic/spells/bulls-strength.md)*, *[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*<sup>D</sup>  
1st—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[entangle* ](../../magic/spells/entangle.md)(DC 15), *[faerie fire](../../magic/spells/faerie-fire.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[shillelagh](../../magic/spells/shillelagh.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*, *[virtue](../../magic/spells/virtue.md)*  
**D** Domain spell; **Domain** Weather

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The druid casts *liveoak* every 12 days and *endure elements* every morning.  
**During Combat** The druid commands his treant to protect him while he wild shapes into a Huge air elemental and casts *freedom of movement*, *barkskin*, and *greater magic fang*. He then spontaneously uses *summon nature’s ally VI* to summon a dire tiger, upon which he casts *animal growth*, *barkskin*, *greater magic fang*, and *cat’s grace*.

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 16, **Con** 13, **Int** 10, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 26  
**Feats** [Augment Summoning](../../rules/feats/augment-summoning.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Spell Focus (conjuration)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (scimitar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (scimitar).*  
**Skills** Climb +8, Fly +9, Handle Animal +5, Heal +11, Intimidate +1, Knowledge (nature) +13, Linguistics +3, Perception +17, Sense Motive +9, Survival +15, Swim +7  
**SQ** nature bond (Weather domain), nature sense, orc blood, trackless step, weapon familiarity, wild empathy +11, woodland stride  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of haste*, *scroll of plant growth*; **Other Gear** *+1 light fortification leather armor*, *+1 darkwood heavy steel shield*, *+1 scimitar*, darts (5), masterwork club, *brooch of shielding*, *cloak of resistance +2*, *headband of inspired wisdom +2*, *ring of protection +1*, healer’s kit, holly and mistletoe, spell component pouch, 273 gp
