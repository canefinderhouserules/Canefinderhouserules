## Mother of Beasts (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
Human cleric of Lamashtu 10  
CE Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 10, flat-footed 21 (+11 armor, +1 deflection, –1 Dex); +1 vs. good opponents  
**hp** 110 (10d8+30)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +3, **Will** +12; +2 vs. good opponents

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 falchion* +11/+6 (2d4+5/18–20)  
**Special Attacks** aura of madness (DC 19, 10 rounds/day), channel negative energy 4/day (DC 16, 5d6), might of the gods (+10, 10 rounds/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +14)  
7/day—strength surge (+5), vision of madness (+/–5)  
**Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 10th; concentration +14)  
5th—*[flame strike* ](../../magic/spells/flame-strike.md)(DC 19), *[righteous might](../../magic/spells/righteous-might.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[summon monster V](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-v.md)*  
4th—*[confusion](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)*<sup>D</sup>(DC 18), *[cure critical wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-critical-wounds.md)*, *[summon monster IV* ](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-iv.md)(2), *[unholy blight* ](../../magic/spells/unholy-blight.md)(DC 18)  
3rd—*[blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)*, *[magic circle against good](../../magic/spells/magic-circle-against-good.md)*, *[magic vestment](../../magic/spells/magic-vestment.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[summon monster III](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-iii.md)*, *[water breathing](../../magic/spells/water-breathing.md)*  
2nd—*[bull’s strength](../../magic/spells/bulls-strength.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[shield other](../../magic/spells/shield-other.md)*, *[silence* ](../../magic/spells/silence.md)(DC 16), *[summon monster II](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-ii.md)*  
1st—*[bane* ](../../magic/spells/bane.md)(DC 15), *[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[command* ](../../magic/spells/command.md)(DC 15), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[enlarge person](../../magic/spells/enlarge-person.md)*<sup>D</sup>(DC 15)  
0 (at will)—*[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*  
**D** Domain spell; **Domains** Madness, Strength

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The cleric casts *magic circle against good* and *magic vestment*.  
**During Combat** The cleric drinks a *potion of invisibility*, then uses *summon monster V* and *summon monster IV* to overwhelm opponents, and attacks with *flame strike*, casting *righteous might* before entering melee.  
**Base Statistics** Without *magic circle against good* and *magic vestment*, the cleric’s statistics are **AC** 20, touch 10, flat-footed 20.

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 8, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 20  
**Feats** [Augment Summoning](../../rules/feats/augment-summoning.md), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), Heavy Armor Proficiency, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Spell Focus (conjuration)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (falchion)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (falchion).*  
**Skills** Handle Animal +11, Heal +8, Knowledge (nature) +1, Knowledge (religion) +6, Perception +14, Spellcraft +8  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** aura  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of invisibility* (2); **Other Gear** *+1 full plate*, *+1 falchion*, *cloak of resistance +1*, *headband of inspired wisdom +2*, *ring of protection +1*, silver unholy symbol, 110 gp

The mother of beasts serves the goddess of madness and monsters. She looks after horrible creatures and summons extraplanar beings to defend herself and her pets.
