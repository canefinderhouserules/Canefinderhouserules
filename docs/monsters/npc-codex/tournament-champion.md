## Tournament Champion (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Half-elf bard 7/sorcerer 2/arcane archer 4  
N Medium humanoid (elf, human)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +25

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 16, flat-footed 17 (+1 dodge, +6 armor, +1 deflection, +4 Dex)  
**hp** 130 (7d8+2d6+4d10+22)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +15, **Will** +11; +2 vs. enchantments, +4 vs. bardic performance, language-dependent, and sonic

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk rapier +11/+6 (1d6/18–20)  
**Ranged**  *+2 longbow* +21/+16 (1d8+2/×3)  
**Special Attacks** bardic performance 19 rounds/day (move action; countersong, distraction, fascinate, inspire competence +3, inspire courage +2, suggestion), enhance arrows (elemental, magic), imbue arrow, seeker arrow (1/day).  
**Bard Spells Known** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  
4th (1/day)—*[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*, *[shout* ](../../magic/spells/shout.md)(DC 17)  
 3rd (4/day)—*[charm monster* ](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)(DC 16), *[crushing despair* ](../../magic/spells/crushing-despair.md)(DC 16), *[cure serious wounds* ](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds.md)(DC 16), *[deep slumber* ](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)(DC 16)  
 2nd (5/day)—*[cat's grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[eagle's splendor](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[shatter](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)*, *[silence* ](../../magic/spells/silence.md)(DC 15)  
 1st (6/day)—*[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 14), *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)*, *[lesser confusion* ](../../magic/spells/confusion-lesser.md)(DC 14), *[unseen servant](../../magic/spells/unseen-servant.md)*  
 0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[flare* ](../../magic/spells/flare.md)(DC 13), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*  
**Sorcerer Spells Known**(CL 2nd; concentration +5; arcane spell failure 20%)  
1st (5/day)—*[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[arcane mark](../../magic/spells/arcane-mark.md)*, *[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 13), *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 13), *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  
**Bloodline** arcane

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The arcane archer casts *cat’s grace* and drinks her *potion of haste*. She typically prepares *shock* arrows as her enhance arrows ability.  
**During Combat** The archer’s favorite tactic is to cast *greater invisibility*, then make shots from a distance using *true strike*.  
**Base Statistics** Without *cat’s grace*, the arcane archer’s statistics are **Init** +5; **Ref** +13; **Ranged** *+2 longbow* +19/+14 (1d8+2/×3); **Dex** 20; **CMD** 27.

**Statistics**

**Str** 10, **Dex** 24, **Con** 13, **Int** 8, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 28  
**Feats** [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Far Shot](../../rules/feats/far-shot.md), [Manyshot](../../rules/feats/manyshot.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (longbow)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility, Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Knowledge (geography) +6, Knowledge (local, nobility) +8, Perception +25, Perform (oratory, sing) +19, Spellcraft +3, Swim +0, Use Magic Device +7  
**Languages** Common, Elven  
**SQ** arcane bond (*+2 longbow*), bardic knowledge +3, bloodline arcana (+1 DC for spells with metamagic feats that increase spell level), elf blood, lore master 1/day, versatile performance (oratory, sing)  
**Combat Gear**  *+1 human-bane arrow* (2), *+1 magical beast-bane arrow* (4), *potion of haste*; **Other Gear** *+2 chain shirt*, *+2 longbow* with 40 arrows, masterwork rapier, *belt of incredible dexterity +2*, *cloak of resistance +1*, *lesser bracers of archery*, *ring of protection +1*, 309 gp

These half-elves travel from fair to fair, entertaining crowds with archery prowess, arcane flourishes, and epic ballads.
