## Orc Slayer (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Elf ranger 8  
CN Medium humanoid (elf)  
**Init** +10; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 23, touch 16, flat-footed 18 (+1 dodge, +5 armor, +1 deflection, +4 Dex, +2 natural)  
**hp** 102 (8d10+22)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +13, **Will** +4; +2 vs. enchantments  
**Immune** sleep; **Resist** fire 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk elven curve blade +16/+11 (1d10+6/18–20)  
**Ranged**  *+1 longbow* +15/+10 (1d8+1/×3)  
**Special Attacks** favored enemy (animals +2, orcs +4)  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +6)  
2nd—*[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*  
1st—*[entangle](../../magic/spells/entangle.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The ranger casts *[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, and *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(fire).  
**During Combat** If ranger acts in the surprise round, she casts *entangle*. She prefers to attack at range and from cover.  
**Base Statistics** Without *barkskin*, *cat’s grace*, and *resist energy*, the ranger’s statistics are **Init** +4; **AC** 20, touch 15, flat-footed 16; **Ref** +11; **Melee** mwk elven curve blade +13/+8 (1d10+1/18–20); **Ranged** *+1 longbow* +13/+8 (1d8+1/×3); **Dex** 19; **CMD** +25; **Skills** Acrobatics +11, Ride +10, Stealth +14.

**Statistics**

**Str** 13, **Dex** 23, **Con** 12, **Int** 12, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 26  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (curve blade)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Endurance is part of Toughness; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim, Power Attack need no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (curve blade), Dodgility, Improved Initiative; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +13, Climb +9, Heal +8, Knowledge (dungeoneering, geography) +6, Knowledge (nature) +8, Perception +14, Ride +12, Stealth +16, Survival +12, Swim +7  
**Languages** Common, Elven, Orc  
**SQ** elven magic, favored terrain (forest +4, plains +2), hunter’s bond (companions), swift tracker, track +4, weapon familiarity, wild empathy +7, woodland stride  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*; **Other Gear** *+1 chain shirt*, *+1 longbow* with 20 arrows, masterwork elven curve blade, *cloak of resistance +1*, *ring of protection +1*, 194 gp

The orc slayer is driven by vengeance to kill all orcs.
