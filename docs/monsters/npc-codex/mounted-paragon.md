## Mounted Paragon (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
Human paladin 16  
LG Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** Perception +15  
**Aura** courage (10 ft.), faith (10 ft.), justice (10 ft.), resolve (10 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 12, flat-footed 27 (+11 armor, +1 deflection, +1 Dex, +1 natural, +4 shield)  
**hp** 148 (16d10+56)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +10, **Will** +13  
**Immune** charm, disease, fear, poison; **Resist** fire 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 holy lance* +24/+19/+14/+9 (1d8+7/19–20/×3) or *+1 heavy mace* +23/+18/+13/+8 (1d8+7)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with lance)  
**Special Attacks** channel positive energy (DC 20, 8d6), smite evil 6/day (+2 attack and AC, +16 damage)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th; concentration +18)  
At will—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
**Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 13th; concentration +15)  
4th—*[holy sword](../../magic/spells/holy-sword.md)*  
3rd—*[heal mount](../../magic/spells/heal-mount.md)*, *[prayer](../../magic/spells/prayer.md)*  
2nd—*[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[eagle’s splendor](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md)*, *[remove paralysis](../../magic/spells/remove-paralysis.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*  
1st—*[bless* ](../../magic/spells/bless.md)(2), *[divine favor* ](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)(2)

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The paladin casts *[delay poison* ](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)and *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(fire).  
**During Combat** The paladin fights from horseback with his lance, positioning himself where he can attack the enemy leader using Spirited Charge. He uses Improved Overrun and Trample to pass and crush creatures in his way. If he is unhorsed and mounting again is dangerous or impractical, he casts holy sword on his mace and fights on foot.  
**Base Statistics** Without *delay poison* and *resist energy*, the paladin’s statistics are **Immune** charm, disease, fear; **Resist** none.

**Statistics**

**Str** 22, **Dex** 12, **Con** 16, **Int** 10, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 14  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +22 (+24 overrun); **CMD** 34 (36 vs. overrun)  
**Feats** [Improved Critical (lance)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Overrun](../../rules/feats/improved-overrun.md), [Mounted Combat](../../rules/feats/mounted-combat.md), [Ride-By Attack](../../rules/feats/ride-by-attack.md), [Spirited Charge](../../rules/feats/spirited-charge.md), [Trample](../../rules/feats/trample.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (lance)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Handle Animal +9, Heal +6, Intimidate +10, Perception +15, Ride +15 (+17 to stay in the saddle), Sense Motive +10  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** aura, code of conduct, divine bond (mount), lay on hands (8d6, 10/day), mercies (fatigued, frightened, paralyzed, shaken, staggered); **Other Gear** potion of haste, +2 full plate, +2 heavy steel shield, +1 heavy mace, +1 holy lance, amulet of natural armor +1, belt of physical might +2 (Str, Con), cloak of resistance +2, ring of protection +1, full plate barding, military saddle, 3,288 gp

This holy warrior is a skilled rider who dispenses harsh justice at the point of a lance.
