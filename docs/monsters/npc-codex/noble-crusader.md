## Noble Crusader (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
Human cleric of the goddess of valor 5  
LN Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** –1; **Senses** Perception +6

**Defense**

**AC** 18, touch 9, flat-footed 18 (+7 armor, –1 Dex, +2 shield)  
**hp** 55 (5d8+15)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +1, **Will** +7

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** mwk longsword +8 (1d8+3/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** channel positive energy 6/day (DC 13, 3d6)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +7)  
5/day—touch of law  
5/day—battle rage (+2 damage)  
**Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +7)  
3rd—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[magic vestment](../../magic/spells/magic-vestment.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[searing light](../../magic/spells/searing-light.md)*  
2nd—*[align weapon* ](../../magic/spells/align-weapon.md)(law only)<sup>D</sup>, *[enthrall* ](../../magic/spells/enthrall.md)(DC 14), *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(DC 14), *[sound burst* ](../../magic/spells/sound-burst.md)(DC 14)  
1st—*[command* ](../../magic/spells/command.md)(DC 13), *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[magic weapon](../../magic/spells/magic-weapon.md)*<sup>D</sup>, *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*, *[summon monster I](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-i.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[purify food and drink](../../magic/spells/purify-food-and-drink.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*  
**D** Domain spell; **Domains** Law, War

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The cleric casts *magic vestment*.  
**During Combat** The cleric attacks with her longsword, and casts *magic weapon* or *align weapon* as needed. When fighting undead, she channels positive energy. Otherwise, she uses ranged magical attacks only as a last resort.  
**Base Statistics** Without *magic vestment*, the cleric’s statistics are **AC** 18, touch 9, flat-footed 18.

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 8, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 15  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), Heavy Armor Proficiency, [Improved Shield Bash](../../rules/feats/improved-shield-bash.md), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Diplomacy +9, Heal +6, Knowledge (nobility) +6, Knowledge (religion) +5, Perception +6  
**Languages** Common  
**SQ** aura  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of bull’s strength*, thunderstone; **Other Gear** masterwork splint mail, *+1 light steel shield*, masterwork longsword, *cloak of resistance +1*, silver holy symbol, 271 gp

The noble crusader battles the forces of chaos, usually at the behest of a local monarch.
