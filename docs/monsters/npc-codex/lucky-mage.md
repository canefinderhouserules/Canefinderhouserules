## Lucky Mage (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
Halfling sorcerer 11  
N Small humanoid (halfling)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +13

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 16, flat-footed 17 (+4 armor, +1 deflection, +3 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 63 (11d6+22)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +11, **Will** +10; +2 vs. fear  
**Defensive Abilities** fated +3; **Immune** fire (120 points)

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** mwk longspear +6 (1d6–2/×3)  
**Ranged**  mwk light crossbow +10 (1d6/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** it was meant to be (1/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 11th; concentration +16)  
8/day—touch of destiny (+5)  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 11th; concentration +16)  
5th (5/day)—*[break enchantment](../../magic/spells/break-enchantment.md)*, *[interposing hand](../../magic/spells/interposing-hand.md)*, *[teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport.md)*  
4th (7/day)—*[bestow curse* ](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)(DC 19), *[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*  
3rd (7/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[fireball* ](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)(DC 18), *[hold person* ](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)(DC 18), *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*, *[ray of exhaustion* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-exhaustion.md)(DC 18)  
2nd (7/day)—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[blindness/deafness* ](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)(DC 17), *[blur](../../magic/spells/blur.md)*, *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*, *[spider climb](../../magic/spells/spider-climb.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)*, *[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 16), *[floating disk](../../magic/spells/floating-disk.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[arcane mark](../../magic/spells/arcane-mark.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*  
**Bloodline** destined

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The sorcerer casts *freedom of movement*, *mage armor*, and *protection from energy* (fire).  
**During Combat** The sorcerer uses *interposing hand* or *hold person* to keep opponents from reaching her while she uses ranged attack spells such as *fireball* and *ray of exhaustion*.  
**Base Statistics** Without *mage armor* and *protection from energy*, the sorcerer’s statistics are **AC** 17, touch 16, flat-footed 13; **Immune** none.

**Statistics**

**Str** 6, **Dex** 16, **Con** 12, **Int** 13, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +5; **CMB** +2; **CMD** 23  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Defensive Combat Training](../../rules/feats/defensive-combat-training.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Weapon Focus (longspear)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (longspear).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +5 (+1 when jumping), Bluff +13, Climb +0, Fly +9, Knowledge (arcana, history) +9, Perception +13, Spellcraft +10  
**Languages** Common, Gnome, Halfling  
**SQ** bloodline arcana (gains a luck bonus to saves when casting personal-range spells)  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*, *potion of cure serious wounds*, *potion of eagle’s splendor*, *potion of fly*, *potion of invisibility*, *wand of shield* (44 charges); **Other Gear** masterwork light crossbow with 10 bolts, masterwork longspear, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *cloak of resistance +2*, *headband of alluring charisma +2*, *ring of protection +1*, 649 gp

The lucky mage uses her magic, innate talents and good fortune to survive incredible odds with barely a scratch. Rather than depend on this strange fortune, however, the lucky mage constantly strives to understand and harness her unique abilities.
