## Tunnel Drummer (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
Dwarf bard 14  
LN Medium humanoid (dwarf)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** Perception +13

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 12, flat-footed 17 (+6 armor, +1 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 natural)  
**hp** 146 (14d8+34)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +10, **Will** +9; +2 vs. poison, spells, and spell-like abilities, +4 vs. bardic performance, language-dependent, and sonic  
**Defensive Abilities** defensive training (+4 dodge bonus to AC vs. giants)

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 thundering heavy mace* +14/+9 (1d8+3)  
**Ranged**  *+1 heavy crossbow* +12 (1d10+1/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** +1 on attack rolls against goblinoid and orc humanoids, bardic performance 35 rounds/day (swift action; countersong, dirge of doom, distraction, fascinate, frightening tune, inspire competence +4, inspire courage +3, inspire greatness, soothing performance, *suggestion*)  
**Bard Spells Known** (CL 14th; concentration +19)  
5th (2/day)—*[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[mind fog* ](../../magic/spells/mind-fog.md)(DC 20), *[song of discord* ](../../magic/spells/song-of-discord.md)(DC 20)  
4th (4/day)—*[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*, *[hold monster* ](../../magic/spells/hold-monster.md)(DC 19), *[shout* ](../../magic/spells/shout.md)(DC 19)  
3rd (5/day)—*[blink](../../magic/spells/blink.md)*, *[confusion* ](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)(DC 18), *[gaseous form](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)*, *[haste* ](../../magic/spells/haste.md)(DC 18), *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
2nd (6/day)—*[alter self](../../magic/spells/alter-self.md)*, *[glitterdust* ](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)(DC 17), *[shatter](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)*, *[sound burst* ](../../magic/spells/sound-burst.md)(DC 17), *[summon swarm](../../magic/spells/summon-swarm.md)*  
1st (6/day)—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)*, *[hideous laughter* ](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)(DC 16), *[magic mouth](../../magic/spells/magic-mouth.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[flare* ](../../magic/spells/flare.md)(DC 15), *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 15), *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The bard drinks a *potion of eagle’s splendor*.  
**During Combat** The bard uses *mind fog* and *dirge of doom*.  
**Base Statistics** Without *eagle’s splendor*, the bard’s statistics are **Bard Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Cha** 16; **Skills** Diplomacy +12, Perform (comedy, oratory) +16, Perform (percussion) +20, Perform (string) +12, Use Magic Device +14.

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 13, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +12; **CMD** 24 (28 vs. bull rush or trip)  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Reload](../../rules/feats/rapid-reload.md), [Weapon Focus (heavy mace)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +9 (+5 when jumping), Diplomacy +14, Knowledge (dungeoneering) +13, Knowledge (engineering, history) +12, Knowledge (geography, nobility, religion) +11, Perception +13 (+15 to notice unusual stonework), Perform (comedy, oratory) +18, Perform (percussion) +22, Perform (string) +14, Spellcraft +8, Stealth +10, Use Magic Device +16  
**Languages** Common, Dwarven  
**SQ** bardic knowledge +7, jack-of-all-trades (use any skill), lore master 2/day, versatile performance (comedy, dance, oratory, percussion)  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of eagle’s splendor*, *wand of cure moderate wounds* (50 charges); **Other Gear** *+2 chain shirt*, *+1 heavy crossbow* with 20 bolts, *+1 thundering heavy mace*, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *lyre of building*, drum, 88 gp

Tunnel drummers keep time for acts of work and war carried out in deep warrens and mine tunnels.
