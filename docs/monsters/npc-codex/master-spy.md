**Melee** 2 rapier +20/+15/+10 (1d6+5/18–20)  
**Ranged** +2 dagger +19/+14 (1d4+2/19-20)

**Before Combat** The bard keeps *[misdirection* ](../../magic/spells/misdirection.md)in place at all times.
