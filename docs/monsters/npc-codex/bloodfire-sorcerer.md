## Bloodfire Sorcerer (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
Half-orc sorcerer 7  
NE Medium humanoid (human, orc)  
**Init** +1; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +7

**Defense**

**AC** 17, touch 12, flat-footed 16 (+4 armor, +1 deflection, +1 Dex, +1 natural)  
**hp** 60 (7d6+33)  
**Fort** +3, **Ref** +3, **Will** +7  
**Defensive Abilities** orc ferocity; **Resist** fire 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** mwk falchion +7 (2d4+4/18–20)  
**Ranged**  mwk heavy crossbow +5 (1d10/19–20)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
6/day—elemental ray (1d6+3 fire)  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
3rd (5/day)—*[fireball* ](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)(DC 17), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[protection from energy](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[blur](../../magic/spells/blur.md)*, *[false life](../../magic/spells/false-life.md)*, *[glitterdust* ](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)(DC 15), *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[burning hands* ](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)(DC 15), *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[magic weapon](../../magic/spells/magic-weapon.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)(DC 14), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed* ](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)(DC 13), *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[flare* ](../../magic/spells/flare.md)(DC 14), *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[ray of frost* ](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)(fire), *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  
**Bloodline** elemental (fire)

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The sorcerer casts *false life* and *mage armor*.  
**During Combat** The sorcerer casts *haste* before targeting her foes with *fireball* or *scorching ray*. In melee combat, she casts *shield* on herself, and *magic weapon* on her falchion.  
**Base Statistics** Without *false life* and *mage armor*, the sorcerer’s statistics are **AC** 13, touch 11, flat-footed 12; **hp** 48.

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 12, **Con** 13, **Int** 8, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 18  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Spell Focus (evocation)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
**Skills** Intimidate +11, Knowledge (arcana) +3, Linguistics +0, Perception +7, Spellcraft +4  
**Languages** Common, Ignan, Orc  
**SQ** bloodline arcana (change energy damage spells to fire), orc blood, weapon familiarity  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*, *scroll of fly* (2); **Other Gear** masterwork falchion, masterwork heavy crossbow with 10 bolts, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *ring of protection +1*, 225 gp

The bloodfire sorcerer withers her enemies with flame and quickens her allies with hot-blooded ferocity.
