## Acid Terror (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
Human sorcerer 9/dragon disciple 4  
LE Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** Perception +12

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 15, flat-footed 21 (+4 armor, +2 deflection, +2 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural)  
**hp** 150 (9d6+4d12+48)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +10, **Will** +11  
**Resist** acid 10

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** 2 claws +10 (1d6+3 plus 1d6 acid), bite +10 (1d6+4) or quarterstaff +10/+5 (1d6+3)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-foot cone, 13d6 acid, DC 22, 2/day), claws (2, 1d6+3 plus 1d6 acid, magic, 9 rounds/day), dragon bite  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 12th; concentration +18)  
6th (4/day)—*[acid fog](../../magic/spells/acid-fog.md)*, *[form of the dragon I](../../magic/spells/form-of-the-dragon-i.md)*  
5th (6/day)—*[cloudkill* ](../../magic/spells/cloudkill.md)(DC 23), *[feeblemind* ](../../magic/spells/feeblemind.md)(DC 22), *[spell resistance](../../magic/spells/spell-resistance.md)*  
4th (7/day)—*[black tentacles](../../magic/spells/black-tentacles.md)*, *[confusion* ](../../magic/spells/confusion.md)(DC 21), *[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[fear* ](../../magic/spells/fear.md)(DC 20)  
3rd (7/day)—*[displacement](../../magic/spells/displacement.md)*, *[fly](../../magic/spells/fly.md)*, *[hold person* ](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)(DC 20), *[stinking cloud* ](../../magic/spells/stinking-cloud.md)(DC 21), *[suggestion* ](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)(DC 20)  
2nd (8/day)—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[glitterdust* ](../../magic/spells/glitterdust.md)(DC 20), *[hideous laughter* ](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)(DC 19), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*, *[web* ](../../magic/spells/web.md)(DC 20)  
1st (8/day)—*[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 18), *[color spray* ](../../magic/spells/color-spray.md)(DC 17), *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[reduce person* ](../../magic/spells/reduce-person.md)(DC 17), *[silent image* ](../../magic/spells/silent-image.md)(DC 17)  
0 (at will)—*[acid splash](../../magic/spells/acid-splash.md)*, *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 17), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  
**Bloodline** draconic (green)

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** As soon as she suspects combat is imminent, the dragon disciple casts *mage armor* and *spell resistance*.  
**During Combat** The dragon disciple prefers to keep her distance, and starts combat by casting quickened *web* followed by *acid fog*. She then casts *fly*, *black tentacles*, *acid arrow*, *feeblemind*, and *cloudkill*, with judicious uses of Quickened Spell.  
**Base Statistics** Without *mage armor*, the dragon disciple’s statistics are **AC** 20, touch 15, flat-footed 17.

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 14, **Con** 15, **Int** 10, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 25  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Greater Spell Focus (conjuration)](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Quicken Spell](../../rules/feats/quicken-spell.md), [Spell Focus (conjuration](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), enchantment), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Climb +4, Fly +6, Intimidate +19, Knowledge (arcana) +8, Linguistics +1, Perception +12, Spellcraft +7, Stealth +9  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**SQ** blood of dragons, bloodline arcana (acid spells deal +1 damage per die)  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure serious wounds*, *potion of delay poison*, *wand of detect thoughts* (15 charges); **Other Gear** quarterstaff, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *belt of mighty constitution +2*, *cloak of resistance +2*, *hat of disguise*, *headband of alluring charisma +2*, *ring of protection +2*, 800 gp

Cunning and manipulative, these dragon disciples trap their foes with schemes and spells before showering them with deadly acid.
