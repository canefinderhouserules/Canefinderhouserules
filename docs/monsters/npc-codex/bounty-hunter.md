## Bounty Hunter (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
Human ranger 12  
LN Medium humanoid (human)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** Perception +16

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 16, flat-footed 20 (+5 armor, +1 deflection, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +4 natural)  
**hp** 106 (12d10+36)  
**Fort** +11, **Ref** +13, **Will** +8  
**Defensive Abilities** evasion; **Immune** poison; **Resist** fire 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 kukri* +16/+11/+6 (1d4+5/18–20), *+1 kukri* +16/+11 (1d4+5/18–20)  
**Ranged**  mwk composite longbow +17/+12/+7 (1d8+4/×3)  
**Special Attacks** favored enemy (animals +2, humans +6, orcs +2)  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 9th; concentration +10)  
2nd—*[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*  
1st—*[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The ranger casts *[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, and *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(fire).  
**During Combat** The ranger attacks with his bow using Deadly Aim. In melee, he uses his kukris or attempts to grapple.  
**Base Statistics** Without *barkskin*, *cat’s grace*, *delay poison*, *longstrider*, and *resist energy*, the ranger’s statistics are **Init** +6; **Senses** normal; **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **Ref** +11; **Immune** none; **Resist** none; **Speed** 30 ft.; **Ranged** mwk longbow +15/+10/+5 (1d8+4/×3); **Dex** 14; **CMD** 30 (32 vs. grapple); **Skills** Acrobatics +12, Stealth +17.

**Statistics**

**Str** 19, **Dex** 18, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +16 (+18 grapple); **CMD** 32 (34 vs. grapple)  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Double Slice](../../rules/feats/double-slice.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Grapple](../../rules/feats/improved-grapple.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Focus (kukri)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Acrobatics +14 (+18 when jumping), Bluff +10, Climb +12, Disguise +7, Knowledge (geography, nature) +8, Knowledge (local) +5, Linguistics +2, Perception +16, Sense Motive +11, Stealth +19, Survival +16, Swim +10
