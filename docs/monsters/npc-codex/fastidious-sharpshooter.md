## Fastidious Sharpshooter (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Gnome fighter 8  
NE Small humanoid (gnome)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +10

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 18, flat-footed 20 (+1 dodge, +6 armor, +3 deflection, +3 Dex, +1 size)  
**hp** 76 (8d10+28)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +5, **Will** +4; +2 vs. illusions, +2 vs. fear  
**Defensive Abilities** bravery +2, defensive training (+4 dodge bonus to AC vs. giants)

**Offense**

**Speed** 25 ft.  
**Melee** mwk glaive +10/+5 (1d8/×3) or short sword +9/+4 (1d4/19–20)  
**Ranged**  mwk light crossbow +13/+13/+8 (1d6+3/17–20) or mwk light crossbow with +1 frost bolts +13/+13/+8 (1d6+4+1d6 cold/17–20)  
**Space** 5 ft., **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with glaive)  
**Special Attacks** +1 on attack rolls against goblinoid and reptilian humanoids, weapon training (crossbows +1)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +9)  
1/day—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 11), *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The fighter drinks her *potion of shield of faith*.  
**During Combat** The fighter drinks her *potion of haste* and launches full attacks with *screaming bolts*.  
**Base Statistics** Without *shield of faith*, the fighter’s statistics are **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **CMD** 21.

**Statistics**

**Str** 10, **Dex** 16, **Con** 16, **Int** 8, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 23  
**Feats** [Fleet](../../rules/feats/fleet.md), [Improved Critical (light crossbow)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Rapid Reload](../../rules/feats/rapid-reload.md), [Rapid Shot](../../rules/feats/rapid-shot.md), [Weapon Focus (light crossbow)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Specialization (light crossbow)](../../rules/feats/weapon-specialization.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility, Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Perception +10, Profession (soldier) +8, Sense Motive +7  
**Languages** Common, Gnome, Sylvan  
**SQ** armor training 2  
**Combat Gear**  *+1 frost bolts* (8), *potion of haste*, *potion of shield of faith* (CL 7th), *screaming bolts* (2); **Other Gear** mithral breastplate, masterwork glaive, masterwork light crossbow with 40 bolts, short sword, sunrods (2), 27 gp
