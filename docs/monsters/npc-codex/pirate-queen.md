## Pirate Queen (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
Halfling ranger 10  
NE Small humanoid (halfling)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** Perception +17

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 17, flat-footed 20 (+4 armor, +1 deflection, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +3 natural, +1 shield, +1 size)  
**hp** 120 (10d10+20)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +13, **Will** +7; +2 vs. fear  
**Defensive Abilities** evasion; **Resist** fire 20

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 rapier* +14/+9 (1d4+5/17–20), mwk handaxe +14/+9 (1d4+3/×3)  
**Ranged**  mwk light crossbow +16 (1d6/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** favored enemy (aquatic humanoids +4, humans +4, magical beasts +2)  
**Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +9)  
2nd—*[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*  
1st—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The ranger casts *[barkskin](../../magic/spells/barkskin.md)*, *[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[longstrider](../../magic/spells/longstrider.md)*, and *[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(fire).  
**During Combat** The ranger prefers melee, using Mobility and Spring Attack to engage the enemy leader while her pirate allies deal with underlings.  
**Base Statistics** Without *barkskin*, *longstrider*, and *resist energy*, the ranger’s statistics are **AC** 22, touch 17, flat-footed 17; **Resist** none; **Speed** 20 ft.

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 18, **Con** 12, **Int** 10, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +13; **CMD** 27  
**Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), [Spring Attack](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md), [Two-Weapon Defense](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-defense.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (rapier)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Endurance is part of Toughness; in their place: Weapon Focus (rapier); Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +11, Climb +17, Knowledge (geography, nature) +8, Perception +17, Profession (sailor) +10, Stealth +21, Survival +15, Swim +15  
**Languages** Common, Halfling  
**SQ** favored terrain (urban +2, water +4), hunter’s bond (companions), swift tracker, track +5, wild empathy +10, woodland stride  
**Combat Gear**  *potion of cure moderate wounds*; **Other Gear** *+1 studded leather*, *+1 rapier*, masterwork handaxe, masterwork light crossbow with 20 bolts, *belt of incredible dexterity +2*, *cloak of resistance +1*, *feather token* (swan boat), *ring of protection +1*, 863 gp

The pirate queen is a deadly foe, whether she’s encountered in a scummy port town or out on the high seas, flying the black flag. She keeps a full crew of unruly, vicious buccaneers in line, and rewards them with the treasure and carnage they crave.
