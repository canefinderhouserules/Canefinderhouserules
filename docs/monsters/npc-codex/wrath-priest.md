## Wrath Priest (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
Half-orc cleric of the god of destruction 8  
CE Medium humanoid (human, orc)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +5

**Defense**

**AC** 20, touch 10, flat-footed 20 (+9 armor, +1 natural)  
**hp** 71 (8d8+32)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +2, **Will** +8; +2 resistance vs. good  
**Defensive Abilities** orc ferocity; **Immune** fire (96 points)

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 greataxe* +12/+7 (1d12+9/×3)  
**Ranged**  javelin +6/+1 (1d6+6)  
**Special Attacks** channel negative energy 4/day (DC 15, 4d6), destructive aura (+4, 8 rounds/day), destructive smite (+4, 5/day), scythe of evil (4 rounds, 1/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +10)  
5/day—touch of evil (4 rounds)  
**Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +10)  
4th—*[chaos hammer* ](../../magic/spells/chaos-hammer.md)(DC 16), *[divine power](../../magic/spells/divine-power.md)*, *[unholy blight](../../magic/spells/unholy-blight.md)*<sup>D</sup>(DC 16)  
3rd—*[invisibility purge](../../magic/spells/invisibility-purge.md)*, *[prayer](../../magic/spells/prayer.md)*, *[protection from energy* ](../../magic/spells/protection-from-energy.md)(DC 15), *[rage](../../magic/spells/rage.md)*<sup>D</sup>  
2nd—*[align weapon](../../magic/spells/align-weapon.md)*<sup>D</sup>(evil weapons only), *[bull’s strength](../../magic/spells/bulls-strength.md)*, *[darkness](../../magic/spells/darkness.md)*, *[spiritual weapon* ](../../magic/spells/spiritual-weapon.md)(2)  
1st—*[bane* ](../../magic/spells/bane.md)(DC 13), *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[entropic shield](../../magic/spells/entropic-shield.md)*, *[protection from good](../../magic/spells/protection-from-good.md)*, *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*<sup>D</sup>  
0 (at will)—*[bleed* ](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)(DC 12), *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*  
**D** Domain spell; **Domains** Destruction, Evil

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The cleric casts *protection from energy* (fire).  
**During Combat** The cleric casts *divine power* and *shield of faith* at the start of combat, then closes to melee distance. If combat starts and he cannot reach any opponents on the first round, he casts *chaos hammer* or *unholy blight*. If fighting creatures he knows are good, he uses his scythe of evil ability on his greataxe. If he has allies, he casts *prayer*.

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 10, **Con** 14, **Int** 8, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +10; **CMD** 20  
**Feats** Heavy Armor Proficiency, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (greataxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Intimidate +7, Perception +5, Spellcraft +3  
**Languages** Common, Orc  
**SQ** aura, orc blood, weapon familiarity  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of cure serious wounds* (2), alchemist’s fire (2); **Other Gear** *+1 half-plate*, *+1 greataxe*, javelins (5), *amulet of natural armor +1*, wooden unholy symbol, 184 gp

The wrath priest serves the primordial god of disaster and destruction, striking down innocents and enemies with fire, blade, and hatred. He is a quintessential nihilist, and can not be bought or reasoned with except as it furthers his god’s horrific ends.
