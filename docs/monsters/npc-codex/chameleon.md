## Chameleon (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
Half-orc bard 11  
N Medium humanoid (human, orc)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 13, flat-footed 17 (+6 armor, +1 deflection, +1 Dex, +1 dodge)  
**hp** 110 (11d8+22)  
**Fort** +4, **Ref** +8, **Will** +7; +4 vs. bardic performance, language-dependent, and sonic  
**Defensive Abilities** orc ferocity

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** *+2 falchion* +15/+10 (2d4+8/18–20)  
**Ranged**  shortbow +9/+4 (1d6/×3)  
**Special Attacks** bardic performance 29 rounds/day (move action; countersong, dirge of doom, distraction, fascinate, inspire competence +4, inspire courage +3, inspire greatness, suggestion)  
**Bard Spells Known** (CL 11th; concentration +16)  
4th (2/day)—*[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*, *[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*  
3rd (5/day)—*[gaseous form](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)*, *[haste* ](../../magic/spells/haste.md)(DC 18), *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*, *[slow* ](../../magic/spells/slow.md)(DC 18)  
2nd (5/day)—*[alter self](../../magic/spells/alter-self.md)*, *[blindness/deafness* ](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)(DC 17), *[cure moderate wounds* ](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)(DC 17), *[detect thoughts* ](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)(DC 17), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*  
1st (6/day)—*[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 16), *[disguise self](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)*, *[expeditious retreat](../../magic/spells/expeditious-retreat.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[hideous laughter* ](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)(DC 16), *[undetectable alignment](../../magic/spells/undetectable-alignment.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 15), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[flare* ](../../magic/spells/flare.md)(DC 15), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Tactics**

**Before Combat** The bard drinks her *potion of bull’s strength* and *potion of eagle’s splendor*.  
**During Combat** The bard casts *greater invisibility*, then enters melee, using *haste* to augment her attacks or slow to hinder foes.  
**Base Statistics** Without *bull’s strength* and *eagle’s splendor*, the bard’s statistics are **Bard Melee** *+2 falchion* +13/+8 (2d4+5/18–20); **Spells Known** reduce spell DCs by 2; **Str** 14, **Cha** 17; **CMB** +10 (+12 sunder); **CMD** 23; **Skills** Climb +7, Intimidate +10, Perform (act) +17, Perform (dance) +17, Perform (oratory) +17.

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 13, **Con** 12, **Int** 10, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +12 (+14 sunder); **CMD** 25  
**Feats** [Arcane Strike](../../rules/feats/arcane-strike.md), [Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Weapon Focus (falchion)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Climb +9, Intimidate +12, Knowledge (arcana, nobility) +9, Knowledge (local) +12, Perception +9, Perform (act, dance, oratory) +19, Sense Motive +6, Spellcraft +6, Stealth +13  
**Languages** Common, Orc  
**SQ** bardic knowledge +5, jack-of-all-trades (use any skill), lore master 2/day, orc blood, versatile performance (act, dance, oratory), weapon familiarity  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of bull’s strength* (2), *potions of eagle’s splendor* (2), *potions of enlarge person* (2), *potions of reduce person* (2); **Other Gear** *+2 chain shirt*, *+2 falchion*, shortbow with 20 arrows, *ring of protection +1*, disguise kit, 295 gp

Chameleons are more comfortable portraying other people than appearing in their own natural forms.
