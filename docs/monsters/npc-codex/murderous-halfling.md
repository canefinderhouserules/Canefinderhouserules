## Murderous Halfling (CR 3)

**XP** 800  
Halfling fighter 4  
CE Small humanoid (halfling)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** Perception +6

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 16, flat-footed 16 (+1 dodge, +5 armor, +4 Dex, +1 size)  
**hp** 44 (4d10+4)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +6, **Will** +2; +3 vs. fear  
**Defensive Abilities** bravery +1

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft.  
**Melee** mwk dagger +9 (1d3+6/19–20), mwk dagger +9 (1d3+5/19–20)  
**Ranged**  dagger +10 (1d3+3/19–20)

**Tactics**

**During Combat** The fighter hurls daggers until her foes close with her. In melee, she fights defensively (and gains a +3 dodge bonus from her ranks in Acrobatics). She keeps her *feather token* in reserve for dangerous spellcasters or when battle turns against her.

**Statistics**

**Str** 12, **Dex** 18, **Con** 12, **Int** 13, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 19  
**Feats** [Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (dagger)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Specialization (dagger)](../../rules/feats/weapon-specialization.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +10 (+6 when jumping), Bluff +4, Climb +3, Perception +6, Sleight of Hand +5, Stealth +11  
**Languages** Common, Goblin, Halfling  
**SQ** armor training 1  
**Combat Gear**  *feather token* (whip), flask of acid; **Other Gear** *+1 chain shirt*, daggers (6), masterwork daggers (2), 24 gp

Small, sly, and seemingly innocuous, evil halflings make perfect murderers.
