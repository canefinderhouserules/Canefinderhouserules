## Giant Hunter (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
Gnome paladin 15  
LG Small humanoid (gnome)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +13  
**Aura** courage (10 ft.), faith (10 ft.), justice (10 ft.), resolve (10 ft.)

**Defense**

**AC** 24, touch 13, flat-footed 24 (+10 armor, +2 deflection, +1 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 137 (15d10+50)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +10, **Will** +15; +2 vs. illusions  
**Defensive Abilities** defensive training (+4 dodge bonus to AC vs. giants); **Immune** charm, disease, fear

**Offense**

**Speed** 15 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 shock gnome hooked hammer* +22/+17/+12 (1d6+7/×3 plus 1d6 electricity) or mwk gnome hooked hammer +22/+17/+12 (1d4+6/×4)  
**Ranged**  *+1 light crossbow* +17 (1d6+1/19–20)  
**Special Attacks** +1 on attack rolls against goblinoid and reptilian humanoids, channel positive energy (DC 20, 8d6), smite evil 5/day (+3 attack and AC, +15 damage)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +18)  
At will—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*  
1/day—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
**Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 12th; concentration +15)  
4th—*[holy sword](../../magic/spells/holy-sword.md)*  
3rd—*[daylight](../../magic/spells/daylight.md)*, *[prayer* ](../../magic/spells/prayer.md)(2)  
2nd—*[resist energy* ](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)(2), *[shield other](../../magic/spells/shield-other.md)*  
1st—*[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[divine favor* ](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)(2), *[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*

**Tactics**

**During Combat** The paladin uses divine bond to give her weapon a mix of the *defending*, *flaming*, and *holy* special abilities.

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 10, **Con** 15, **Int** 8, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 30  
**Feats** [Bleeding Critical](../../rules/feats/bleeding-critical.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Extra Lay on Hands](../../rules/feats/extra-lay-on-hands.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (gnome hooked hammer)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Knowledge (dungeoneering, local) +4, Perception +13, Stealth +8  
**Languages** Common, Gnome, Sylvan  
**SQ** aura, code of conduct, divine bond (weapon +4, 3/day), lay on hands (7d6, 12/day), mercies (cursed, diseased, nauseated, sickened, stunned)  
**Combat Gear**  *potions of haste* (2); **Other Gear** *+2 shadow half-plate*, *+1 light crossbow* with 20 bolts, *+1 shock/masterwork gnome hooked hammer*, *amulet of natural armor +1*, *belt of giant strength +2*, *boots of striding and springing*, *cloak of resistance +2*, *ring of protection +2*, 543 gp

This paladin’s lifelong mission is to eradicate evil giants.
