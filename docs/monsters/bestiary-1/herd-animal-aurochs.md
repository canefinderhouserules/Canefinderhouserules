## Aurochs (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
N Large animal  
**Init** +0; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +9

**Defense**

**AC** 13, touch 9, flat-footed 13 (+4 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 36 (3d8+12)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +3, **Will** +1

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** gore +7 (1d8+9)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** stampede, trample (2d6+9, DC 17)

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 10, **Con** 17, **Int** 2, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 4  
**Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +9; **CMD** 19 (23 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md)  
*House rules: Endurance is part of Toughness.*  
**Skills** Perception +9

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or herd (3–30)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Stampede (Ex)** A stampede occurs if three or more creatures with stampede make a trample attack while remaining adjacent to each other. While stampeding, the creatures can trample foes of their size or smaller, and the trample’s save DC increases by +2.
