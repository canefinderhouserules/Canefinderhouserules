## Adult Brass Dragon (CR 11)

**XP** 12,800  
CG Large dragon (fire)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** blindsense 60 ft., dragon senses; Perception +24  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 20)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 10, flat-footed 27 (+1 Dex, +18 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 161 (14d12+70)  
**Fort** +14, **Ref** +10, **Will** +12  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 22  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., burrow 30 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +21 (2d6+10), 2 claws +20 (1d8+7), 2 wings +15 (1d6+3), tail slap +15 (1d8+10)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (80-ft. line, DC 22, 12d4 fire), desert wind, sleep breath  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th)  
At will—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*, *[suggestion* ](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)(DC 18)  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th)  
3rd (5/day)—*[hold person* ](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)(DC 18), *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[alter self](../../magic/spells/alter-self.md)*, *[detect thoughts* ](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)(DC 15), *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)*, *[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 16), *[protection from evil](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[ventriloquism](../../magic/spells/ventriloquism.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[arcane mark](../../magic/spells/arcane-mark.md)*, *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 13), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 25, **Dex** 12, **Con** 21, **Int** 16, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +22; **CMD** 33 (37 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), Flyby Attack, Hover, [Greater Spell Focus (enchant)](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Spell Focus (enchant)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Bluff +20, Dip. +20, Fly +12, Heal +20, Ling. +20, Per. +24, S. Motive +24, Spellcraft +20, Survival +20  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, plus any 14 others  
**SQ** move sand

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm deserts

Consummate conversationalists, brass dragons prefer to talk instead of fight. Brass dragons lair near humanoid settlements, where they can hear the most recent news and gossip.
