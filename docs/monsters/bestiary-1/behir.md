## Behir (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
N Huge magical beast  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +8

**Defense**

**AC** 21, touch 9, flat-footed 20 (+1 Dex, +12 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 105 (10d10+50)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +8, **Will** +5  
**Immune** electricity

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., climb 20 ft.  
**Melee** bite +15 (2d6+9 plus grab)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (20-foot line, 7d6 electricity damage, Reflex DC 20 for half, usable every 1d4 rounds), constrict (2d6+9), rake (6 claws +14, 1d4+6), swallow whole (2d8+9 bludgeoning damage, AC 16, 10 hp)

**Statistics**

**Str** 23, **Dex** 12, **Con** 21, **Int** 7, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +18 (+22 grapple); **CMD** 29 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Climb +14, Perception +8, Stealth +5  
**Languages** Common

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm hills and deserts  
**Organization** solitary or pair  
**Treasure** double

**Special Abilities**

**Grab (Ex)** A behir’s grab attack works against creatures of any size category. It can constrict the same round it establishes a hold. On any round thereafter that it maintains its hold, the behir can choose to rake the grappled target or swallow it whole.
