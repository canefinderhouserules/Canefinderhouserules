## Astral Deva (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
NG Medium outsider (angel, extraplanar, good)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +26  
**Aura** protective aura

**Defense**

**AC** 30, touch 15, flat-footed 25 (+1 dodge, +4 Dex, +15 natural; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 172 (15d10+90)  
**Fort** +16, **Ref** +13, **Will** +11; +4 vs. poison, +4 resistance vs. evil  
**Defensive Abilities** uncanny dodge; **DR** 10/evil; **Immune** acid, cold, petrification; **Resist** electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., fly 100 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+2 disrupting warhammer* +26/+21/+16 (1d8+14/×3 plus stun) or slam +23 (1d8+12)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 13th)  
At Will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[continual flame](../../magic/spells/continual-flame.md)*, *[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[discern lies* ](../../magic/spells/discern-lies.md)(DC 20), *[dispel evil* ](../../magic/spells/dispel-evil.md)(DC 21), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[holy aura* ](../../magic/spells/holy-aura.md)(DC 24), *[holy smite* ](../../magic/spells/holy-smite.md)(DC 20), *[holy word* ](../../magic/spells/holy-word.md)(DC 23), *[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only), *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 23), *[remove curse](../../magic/spells/remove-curse.md)*, *[remove disease](../../magic/spells/remove-disease.md)*, *[remove fear](../../magic/spells/remove-fear.md)*  
7/day—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
1/day—*[blade barrier* ](../../magic/spells/blade-barrier.md)(DC 22), *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 26, **Dex** 19, **Con** 21, **Int** 18, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 23  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +23; **CMD** 38  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Focus (warhammer)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +22, Craft (any one) +22, Diplomacy +24, Fly +26, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (planes) +22, Knowledge (religion) +19, Perception +26, Sense Motive +26, Stealth +22  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech  
**SQ** change shape (*alter self*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any good-aligned plane  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or squad (3–6)  
**Treasure** double (*+2 disrupting warhammer*)

**Special Abilities**

**Stun (Su)** If an astral deva strikes an opponent twice in one round with its warhammer, that creature must succeed on a DC 25 Fortitude save or be stunned 1 for 1d6 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.

**Uncanny Dodge (Ex)** This ability functions identically to the rogue ability. If a deva gains uncanny dodge from a class level, he instead gains improved uncanny dodge.
