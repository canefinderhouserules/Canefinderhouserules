**Aura** fire, frightful presence (300 ft., DC 27)

**Defense**

**AC** 38, touch 5, flat-footed 38 (–1 Dex, +33 natural, –4 size)

**Melee** bite +36 (4d6+21/19–20), 2 claws +35 (2d8+14), 2 wings +33 (2d6+7), tail slap +33 (2d8+21)
