## Spirit Naga (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
CE Large aberration  
**Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +22

**Defense**

**AC** 23, touch 14, flat-footed 18 (+5 Dex, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 95 (10d8+50)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +10, **Will** +10

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., swim 20 ft.  
**Melee** bite +10 (2d6+6 plus poison)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** charming gaze  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th)  
3rd (5/day)—*[displacement](../../magic/spells/displacement.md)*, *[fireball* ](../../magic/spells/fireball.md)(DC 16)  
2nd (7/day)—*[cat’s grace](../../magic/spells/cats-grace.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[summon swarm](../../magic/spells/summon-swarm.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 14), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)*, *[daze* ](../../magic/spells/daze.md)(DC 13), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 20, **Con** 21, **Int** 12, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +12; **CMD** 27 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** Ability Focus (charming gaze), [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md), [Stealthy](../../rules/feats/stealthy.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +13, Escape Artist +13, Intimidate +9, Knowledge (arcana) +14, Perception +22, Spellcraft +11, Stealth +15, Swim +12  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate marshes  
**Organization** solitary or nest (2–4)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Charming Gaze (Su)** As *charm person*, 30 feet, Will DC 20 negates. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 20; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d4 Con damage; *cure* 1 save.

**Spells** A spirit naga casts spells as a 7th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the cleric list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a spirit naga.
