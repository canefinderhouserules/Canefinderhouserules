## Shoggoth (CR 19)

**XP** 204,800  
CN Huge ooze (aquatic)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** all-around vision, darkvision 120 ft., low-light vision, scent, tremorsense 60 ft.; Perception +33

**Defense**

**AC** 33, touch 15, flat-footed 26 (+7 Dex, +18 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 414 (23d8+230); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +14, **Will** +15  
**DR** 10/—; **Immune** blindness, charm effects, cold, deafness, ooze traits, sonic; **Resist** acid 20, electricity 20, fire 20; **SR** 30

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., climb 30 ft., swim 50 ft.  
**Melee** 4 slams +31 (3d6+15/19–20 plus grab)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 30 ft.  
**Special Attacks** constrict (3d6+15), maddening cacophony, engulf (4d6+22 bludgeoning damage plus 8d6 acid damage, AC 19, hp 33), trample (4d8+15, DC 36)

**Statistics**

**Str** 40, **Dex** 24, **Con** 31, **Int** 5, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 13  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +34 (+39 grapple); **CMD** 51 (cannot be tripped)  
**Feats** [Blind-Fight](../../rules/feats/blind-fight.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Improved Critical (slam)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (slam)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (slam).*  
**Skills** Climb +23, Perception +33, Swim +23; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Perception  
**Languages** Aklo  
**SQ** amphibious

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold aquatic or underground  
**Organization** solitary or tide (2–6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**All-Around Vision (Ex)** A shoggoth’s many sense organs grant a +4 racial bonus on Perception and immunity to flanking.

**Maddening Cacophony (Su)** As a free action, a shoggoth can give voice to sounds and words sane life was not meant to hear. All creatures in a 60-foot radius must make a DC 22 Will save or be confused for 1d6 rounds. Each round a creature is affected it takes 1d6 points of Wisdom damage. A creature that saves cannot be affected by this shoggoth’s maddening cacophony for 24 hours. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Engulf (Ex)** To use this ability, the shoggoth must begin its turn grappling a creature or must trample. A shoggoth may attempt to engulf as many creatures as it grapples or tramples in a round. This ability otherwise functions as swallow whole, save that a creature that cut its way out of a shoggoth leaves no hole in the protoplasmic creature’s body.
