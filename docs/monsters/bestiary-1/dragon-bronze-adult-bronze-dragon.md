## Adult Bronze Dragon (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
LG Huge dragon (water)  
**Init** +0; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +28  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 28, touch 8, flat-footed 28 (+20 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 184 (16d12+80)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +10, **Will** +15  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** electricity, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 24

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor), swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +23 (2d8+12), 2 claws +22 (2d6+8), 2 wings +20 (1d8+4), tail slap +20 (2d6+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (100-ft. line, DC 23, 12d6 electricity), crush, repulsion breath  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th)  
At will—*[create food and water](../../magic/spells/create-food-and-water.md)*, *[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*, *[speak with animals](../../magic/spells/speak-with-animals.md)*  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th)  
3rd (5/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[slow* ](../../magic/spells/slow.md)(DC 18)  
2nd (7/day)—*[blur](../../magic/spells/blur.md)*, *[gust of wind](../../magic/spells/gust-of-wind.md)*, *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*  
1st (8/day)—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*, 2 more

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 21, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +26; **CMD** 36 (40 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), Multiattack, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +24, Fly +11, Handle Animal +21, Intimidate +24, Knowledge (arcana, geography) +24, Perception +28, Sense Motive +28, Spellcraft +24, Stealth +11, Swim +35  
**Languages** Aquan, Common, Draconic, Elven, Gnome, 2 more  
**SQ** change shape, water breathing, wave mastery

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate coastlines

Bronze dragons have been known to ally with travelers and adventurers if the cause and reward is right and just.
