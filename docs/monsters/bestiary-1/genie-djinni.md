## Djinni (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
CG Large outsider (air, extraplanar)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +12

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 14, flat-footed 14 (+4 Dex, +1 dodge, +5 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 52 (7d10+14)  
**Fort** +4, **Ref** +9, **Will** +7  
**Immune** acid

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (perfect)  
**Melee** 2 slams +10 (1d8+4) or mwk scimitar +11/+6 (1d8+4/18–20)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** air mastery, whirlwind (1/10 minutes, 10–50 ft. tall, 1d8+4 damage, DC 17)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th)  
At will—*[invisibility* ](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)(self only), *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(willing targets to elemental planes, Astral Plane, or Material Plane only)  
1/day—*[create food and water](../../magic/spells/create-food-and-water.md)*, *create wine* (as *[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, but wine instead), *[gaseous form* ](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)(for up to 1 hour), *[major creation* ](../../magic/spells/major-creation.md)(created vegetable matter is permanent), *[persistent image* ](../../magic/spells/persistent-image.md)(DC 17), *[wind walk](../../magic/spells/wind-walk.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 19, **Con** 14, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +12; **CMD** 27  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodge](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Wind Stance](../../rules/feats/wind-stance.md)  
**Skills** Appraise +12, Craft (any one) +12, Fly +20, Knowledge (planes) +12, Perception +12, Sense Motive +12, Spellcraft +12, Stealth +10  
**Languages** Aquan, Auran, Common, Ignan, Terran; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Plane of Air)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, company (3–6), or band (7–10)  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork scimitar, other gear)

**Special Abilities**

**Air Mastery (Ex)** Airborne creatures take a –1 penalty on attack and damage rolls against a djinni.
