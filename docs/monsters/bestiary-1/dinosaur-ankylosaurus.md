## Ankylosaurus (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
N Huge animal  
**Init** +4; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 8, flat-footed 22 (+14 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 120 (10d8+40)  
**Fort** +12, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft.  
**Melee** tail +14 (3d6+12 plus stun)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 17, **Int** 2, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 8  
**Base Atk** +7; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 27 (31 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md), [Weapon Focus (tail)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Improved Bull Rush, Improved Overrun are covered by Powerful Maneuvers; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative, Toughness.*  
**Skills** Perception +14

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm forests and plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or herd (3–12)  
**Treasure** none

**Special Abilities**

**Stun (Ex)** The ankylosaurus’s tail can deliver a powerful, stunning blow. A creature struck by this attack must make a DC 23 save or be dazed for 1 round. If the strike is a critical hit and the target fails its save, it is instead stunned 1 for 1d4 rounds. The save DC is Strength-based.
