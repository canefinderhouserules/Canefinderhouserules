## Storm Giant (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
CG Huge humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +27

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 11, flat-footed 26 (+1 dodge, +6 armor, +2 Dex, +12 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 266 (19d8+114)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +8, **Will** +13  
**Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **Immune** electricity

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., swim 40 ft. (35 ft., swim 30 ft. in armor)  
**Melee** mwk greatsword +28/+23/+18 (4d6+21/17–20) or 2 slams +26 (2d6+14)  
**Ranged**  mwk composite longbow +15/+10/+5 (3d6+14/×3)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th)  
Constant—*[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*  
2/day—*[control weather](../../magic/spells/control-weather.md)*, *[levitate](../../magic/spells/levitate.md)*  
1/day—*[call lightning* ](../../magic/spells/call-lightning.md)(DC 15), *[chain lightning* ](../../magic/spells/chain-lightning.md)(DC 18)

**Statistics**

**Str** 39, **Dex** 14, **Con** 23, **Int** 16, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +30; **CMD** 43  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md), [Improved Critical (greatsword)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (greatsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Improved Bull Rush, Improved Sunder are covered by Powerful Maneuvers; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greatsword), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +18, Climb +17, Craft (any one) +13, Intimidate +20, Perception +27, Perform (sing) +12, Sense Motive +24, Swim +22  
**Languages** Auran, Common, Draconic, Giant  
**SQ** militant, water breathing

**Ecology**

**Environment** any warm  
**Organization** solitary or family (2–5 plus 1 sorcerer or cleric of 7th–10th level, 1–2 rocs, 2–6 griffons, and 2–8 sharks)  
**Treasure** standard (mwk breastplate, mwk composite longbow [+14 Str bonus] with 20 arrows, mwk greatsword, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Militant (Ex)** Storm giants are proficient with all simple and all martial weapons.

**Water Breathing (Ex)** Storm giants can breathe water as well as air.
