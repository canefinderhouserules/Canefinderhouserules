**Melee** bite +32 (2d8+16), 2 claws +31 (2d6+11), 2 wings +29 (1d8+5), tail slap +29 (2d6+16)

**Statistics**

**Str** 33, **Dex** 10, **Con** 25, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +22; **CMB** +35; **CMD** 45 (49 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), Flyby Attack, [Greater Spell Focus (enchant)](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-focus.md), Hover, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), Multiattack, [Quicken Spell](../../rules/feats/quicken-spell.md), [Spell Focus (enchant)](../../rules/feats/spell-focus.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Bluff +30, Diplomacy +30, Fly +17, Heal +30, Knowledge (local, history) +30, Linguistics +30, Perception +34, Sense Motive +34, Spellcraft +30, Survival +30  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, plus any 22 others  
**SQ** move sand

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm deserts

Consummate conversationalists, brass dragons prefer to talk instead of fight. Brass dragons lair near humanoid settlements, where they can hear the most recent news and gossip.
