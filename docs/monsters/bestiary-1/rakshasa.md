## Rakshasa (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
LE Medium outsider (native, shapechanger)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +14

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 16, flat-footed 19 (+5 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural)  
**hp** 160 (10d10+60)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +12, **Will** +8  
**DR** 15/good and piercing; **SR** 25

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 kukri* +16/+11 (1d4+4/15–20), claw +10 (1d4+1), bite +10 (1d6+1)  
**Special Attacks** detect thoughts  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th)  
3rd (5/day)—*[lightning bolt* ](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)(DC 16), *[suggestion* ](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)(DC 16)  
2nd (7/day)—*[acid arrow](../../magic/spells/acid-arrow.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[minor image](../../magic/spells/minor-image.md)*  
1st (7/day)—*[charm person* ](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)(DC 14), *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[silent image](../../magic/spells/silent-image.md)*  
0—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound* ](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)(DC 13), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 16, **Dex** 20, **Con** 22, **Int** 13, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +13; **CMD** 29  
**Feats** [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Critical (kukri)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Bluff +20, Diplomacy +16, Disguise +24, Perception +14, Perform +16, Sense Motive +14, Stealth +18; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Bluff, +8 Disguise  
**Languages** Common, Infernal, Undercommon  
**SQ** change shape (any humanoid, *alter self*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cult (3–12)  
**Treasure** double (*+1 kukri*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Detect Thoughts (Su)** A rakshasa can *detect thoughts* as per the spell of the same name (CL 18th). It can suppress or resume this ability as a free action. When a rakshasa uses this ability, it always functions as if it had spent three rounds concentrating and thus gains the maximum amount of information possible. A creature can resist this effect with a DC 18 Will save. The save DC is Charisma-based.
