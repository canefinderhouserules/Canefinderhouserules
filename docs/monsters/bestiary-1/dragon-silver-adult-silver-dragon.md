## Adult Silver Dragon (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
LG Huge dragon (cold)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses, fog vision; Perception +25  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 23)

**Defense**

**AC** 29, touch 8, flat-footed 29 (+21 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 306 (17d12+102)  
**Fort** +15, **Ref** +12, **Will** +17  
**DR** 5/magic; **Immune** acid, cold, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 25  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to fire

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (average); cloudwalking, graceful flight  
**Melee** bite +24 (2d8+12), 2 claws +23 (2d6+8), 2 wings +21 (1d8+4), tail slap +21 (2d6+12)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, DC 23, 12d8 cold), crush, paralyzing breath  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th)  
At will—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[fog cloud](../../magic/spells/fog-cloud.md)*  
**Spells Known** (CL 7th)  
3rd (5/day)—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[wind wall](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[augury](../../magic/spells/augury.md)*, *[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[web* ](../../magic/spells/web.md)(DC 17)  
1st (7/day)—*[alarm](../../magic/spells/alarm.md)*, *[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[flare](../../magic/spells/flare.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 21, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +27; **CMD** 37 (41 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Flyby Attack, Hover, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Lighting Reflexes, Multiattack, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)

**Skills** Acrobatics +17, Diplomacy +25, Fly +16, Heal +25, Intimidate +25, Knowledge (arcana, local, nobility) +25, Perception +25, Sense Motive +25, Spellcraft +25  
**Languages** Auran, Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Giant, Terran  
**SQ** change shape

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate mountains

Of all the dragons, silvers are the most courageous, holding themselves to a chivalrous code to help the weak, defeat evil, and behave in an honorable manner.
