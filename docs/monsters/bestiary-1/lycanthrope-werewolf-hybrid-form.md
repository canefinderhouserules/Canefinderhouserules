## Werewolf (Hybrid Form) (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
Human natural werewolf fighter 2  
CE Medium humanoid (human, shapechanger)  
**Init** +5; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +4

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 12, flat-footed 20 (+6 armor, +2 Dex, +4 natural)
