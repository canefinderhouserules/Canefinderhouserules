## Couatl (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
LG Large outsider (native)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect chaos/evil/good/law*; Perception +23

**Defense**

**AC** 22, touch 13, flat-footed 18 (+3 Dex, +1 dodge, +9 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 180 (12d10+60)  
**Fort** +9, **Ref** +13, **Will** +14

**Offense**

**Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +16 (1d8+7 plus grab and poison)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** constrict (1d8+7)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th)  
Constant—*[detect chaos](../../magic/spells/detect-chaos.md)*, *[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[detect good](../../magic/spells/detect-good.md)*, *[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*  
At will—*[detect thoughts* ](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)(DC 15), *[ethereal jaunt* ](../../magic/spells/ethereal-jaunt.md)(CL 16th), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[plane shift* ](../../magic/spells/plane-shift.md)(DC 20)  
**Spells Known** (CL 9th)  
4th (4/day)—*[charm monster* ](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)(DC 17), *[freedom of movement](../../magic/spells/freedom-of-movement.md)*  
3rd (7/day)—*[gaseous form](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)*, *[magic circle against evil](../../magic/spells/magic-circle-against-evil.md)*, *[summon monster III](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-iii.md)*  
2nd (7/day)—*[cure moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-moderate-wounds.md)*, *[eagle’s splendor](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md)*, *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*, *[silence* ](../../magic/spells/silence.md)(DC 15)  
1st (7/day)—*[endure elements](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[obscuring mist](../../magic/spells/obscuring-mist.md)*, *[protection from chaos](../../magic/spells/protection-from-chaos.md)*, *[true strike](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)*, *[disrupt undead](../../magic/spells/disrupt-undead.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[ray of frost](../../magic/spells/ray-of-frost.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*, *[stabilize](../../magic/spells/stabilize.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 20, **Dex** 16, **Con** 20, **Int** 17, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 17  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +18 (+22 grapple); **CMD** 32 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Empower Spell](../../rules/feats/empower-spell.md), [Eschew Materials](../../rules/feats/eschew-materials.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +18, Bluff +9, Diplomacy +18, Fly +20, Knowledge (arcana) +9, Knowledge (religion) +12, Perception +23, Sense Motive +15, Spellcraft +15, Survival +16, Use Magic Device +18  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** warm forests  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or flight (3–6)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Spells** A couatl casts spells as a 9th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the cleric list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a couatl, meaning that the creature does not need a divine focus to cast them.

**Poison (Ex)** Injury—bite; *save* Fortitude DC 16; *frequency* 1/minute for 10 minutes; *effect* 1d4 Str; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The DC is Constitution-based.
