**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect good*, *detect magic*, see in darkness; Perception +7

**Defense**

**hp** 16 (3d10); fast healing 2

**DR** 5/good or silver; **Immune** fire, poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10

**Statistics**

**Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +1; **CMD** 15
