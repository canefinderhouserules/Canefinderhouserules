## Hound Archon (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
LG Medium outsider (archon, extraplanar, good, lawful)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*, low-light vision, scent; Perception +10  
**Aura** aura of menace (DC 16), *magic circle against evil*

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 10, flat-footed 19 (+9 natural; +2 deflection vs. evil)  
**hp** 66 (6d10+6)  
**Fort** +6, **Ref** +5, **Will** +5; +4 vs. poison, +2 resistance vs. evil  
**DR** 10/evil; **Immune** electricity, petrification; **SR** 15

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft.  
**Melee** bite +9 (1d8+3), slam +8 (1d4+1) or mwk greatsword +9/+4 (2d6+3), bite +4 (1d8+2)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 6th)  
Constant—*[detect evil](../../magic/spells/detect-evil.md)*, *[magic circle against evil](../../magic/spells/magic-circle-against-evil.md)*  
At Will—*[aid](../../magic/spells/aid.md)*, *[continual flame](../../magic/spells/continual-flame.md)*, *[greater teleport* ](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)(self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 15, **Dex** 10, **Con** 13, **Int** 10, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 12  
**Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +8; **CMD** 18  
**Feats** [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Intimidate +10, Perception +10, Sense Motive +10, Stealth +13, Survival +14; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth, +4 Survival  
**Languages** Celestial, Draconic, Infernal; truespeech  
**SQ** change shape (*beast shape II*)

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Heaven)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or squad (3–5)  
**Treasure** standard (masterwork greatsword, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Change Shape (Su)** A hound archon can assume any canine form of Small to Large size, as if using beast shape II. While in canine form, the hound archon loses its bite, slam, and greatsword attacks, but gains the bite attack of the form it chooses. For the purposes of this ability, canines include any dog-like or wolf-like creature of the animal type.
