**Defense**

**AC** 37, touch 8, flat-footed 37 (+29 natural, –2 size)

**Weaknesses** vulnerability to fire

**Statistics**

**Str** 33, **Dex** 10, **Con** 25, **Int** 16, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +21; **CMB** +34; **CMD** 44 (48 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Alertness](../../rules/feats/alertness.md), Flyby Attack, [Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md), [Improved Critical (bite)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Sunder](../../rules/feats/improved-sunder.md), [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Toughness.*  
**Skills** Fly +16, Intimidate +27, Knowledge (arcane) +27, Knowledge (history) +27, Perception +32, Sense Motive +32, Spellcraft +27, Stealth +16, Swim +43  
**Languages** Common, Draconic  
**SQ** icewalking, ice shape

**Ecology**

**Environment** cold mountains

Although most consider it to be the weakest and most feral of the chromatic dragons, the white dragon makes up for its lack of cunning with sheer ferocity. White dragons dwell on remote, frozen mountaintops and in arctic lowlands, making their home in glittering caves full of ice and snow. They prefer their meals completely frozen.
