**XP** 600  
NE Medium humanoid (human, shapechanger)  
**Init** +8; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., tremorsense
60 ft.; Perception +7

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 14, flat-footed 15 (+3 armor, +4 Dex,
+2 natural)

**Ecology**

**Treasure** NPC gear (studded leather armor,
short sword, 2 doses of Medium spider
venom, other treasure)
