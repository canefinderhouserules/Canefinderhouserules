## the angel of vengeance (CR 26)

**XP** 2,457,600  
LG Huge outsider (angel, extraplanar, good, lawful)  
**Init** +13; **Senses** blindsense 60 ft., darkvision 60 ft., *detect evil*,
low-light vision, *true seeing*; Perception +40  
**Aura** *holy aura* (DC 27), primal aura (30 ft.), protective aura

**Defense**

**AC** 45, touch 31, flat-footed 35 (+1 dodge, +14 armor, +4 deflection,
+9 Dex, +9 sacred, –2 size)  
**hp** 744 (31d10+434); regeneration 20 (deific or mythic)  
**Fort** +35, **Ref** +23, **Will** +27; +4 vs. poison  
**Defensive Abilities** golden armor, righteous mantle; **DR** 20/epic
and evil; **Immune** ability damage, ability drain, acid, charm
effects, cold, compulsion effects, death effects, energy drain,
fire, petrification; **Resist** electricity 30; **SR** 37

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft., fly 90 ft. (good)  
**Melee** *+5 evil-outsider-bane flaming burst holy bastard sword*
+46/+41/+36/+31 (3d8+21/17–20), 5 wings +35 (1d8+5 plus
1d6 fire)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** burning wings, devil’s end, smite evil (7/day)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 26th; concentration +35)  
Constant—*detect evil, holy aura* (DC 27), *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
At will—*bestow grace, fire of judgment* (DC 22), *greater dispel magic, greater teleport, holy word*<sup>M</sup> (DC 26), *geas/ quest, mark of justice, shield other*<sup>M</sup>  
3/day—*[banishment](../../magic/spells/banishment.md)* (DC 26), quickened *[blade barrier](../../magic/spells/blade-barrier.md)*<sup>M</sup> (DC 25), *blessing of fervor, discern location, heal*<sup>M</sup>  
1/day—*gate, meteor swarm*<sup>M</sup> (DC 28), *[time stop](../../magic/spells/time-stop.md)*<sup>M</sup>  
<sup>M</sup> the angel of vengeance can use this ability’s mythic version in his realm.

**Statistics**

**Str** 32, **Dex** 28, **Con** 39, **Int** 23, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 29  
**Base Atk** +31; **CMB** +44 (+48 sunder); **CMD** 77 (79 vs. sunder)  
**Feats** [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Dazzling Display](../../rules/feats/dazzling-display.md), Greater

**Skills** Acrobatics +40, Bluff +43, Fly +43, Intimidate +54, Knowledge

Ride +40, Sense Motive +40, Stealth +35, Survival +40  
**Languages** Celestial, Infernal; telepathy 300 ft.; truespeech  
**SQ** lay on hands (17d6, 10/day), seed of life

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Heaven)  
**Organization** solitary (unique)  
**Treasure** triple (*+5 evil-outsider-bane flaming burst holy bastard
sword, +5 full plate*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Burning Wings (Su)** A creature damaged by the angel of vengeance’s burning wings must succeed at a DC 39 Reflex save or catch fire. The save DC is Constitution-based.

**Devils’ End (Ex)** the angel of vengeance gains a +4 bonus on caster level checks against devils and on saving throws against their spells, as well as to the DCs of his own supernatural abilities and spell-like abilities against devils. Against his father, Dispater, this bonus increases to +8.

**Golden Armor (Ex)** the angel of vengeance is never hindered by his +5 full plate, and is considered to be not wearing armor for the purposes of his maximum Dexterity bonus to Armor Class, his armor check penalty, and his speed.

**Lay on Hands (Su)** the angel of vengeance’s lay on hands works as a paladin’s. He can apply any paladin mercy to his lay on hands ability, and he can use this ability on himself even when a condition or effect would otherwise deny him actions on his turn.

**Primal Aura (Su)** As a free action with the angel of vengeance’s permission, any ally within the angel of vengeance’s primal aura can spend two uses of the angel of vengeance’s smite evil (or four uses of lay on hands) to instantly grant each ally within the aura the benefits of the angel of vengeance’s smite evil against an enemy of that ally’s choice.

**Righteous Mantle (Ex)** the angel of vengeance inherited an affinity for fire from his mother. Fire heals the angel of vengeance a number of hit points equal to the amount of damage it would have dealt. Any fire damage dealt by the angel of vengeance ignores evil outsiders’ fire resistance and immunity to fire, and deals double damage to devils. The angel of vengeance gains a sacred bonus to Armor Class equal to his Charisma bonus. Whenever the angel of vengeance receives magical healing, he has 2 additional hit points restored per die rolled.

**Smite Evil (Su)** the angel of vengeance can smite evil as a 20th-level paladin, but his smite deals extra damage only on the first hit against evil outsiders (not evil dragons or undead). He can trade two uses of lay on hands for an additional use of smite evil as many times per day as he wishes, provided he has enough uses of lay on hands available to do so.
