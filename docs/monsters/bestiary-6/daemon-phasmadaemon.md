## Phasmadaemon (CR 17)

**XP** 102,400  
NE Large outsider (daemon, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +11; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *deathwatch, true seeing*;
Perception +30  
**Aura** frightful presence (60 ft., DC 27)

**Defense**

**AC** 32, touch 17, flat-footed 24 (+7 Dex, +1 dodge, +15 natural,
–1 size)  
**hp** 264 (23d10+138)  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +14, **Will** +19  
**Immune** acid, death effects, disease, fear, poison; **Resist** cold 10,
electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 28

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (good)  
**Melee** bite +30 (3d8+8/19–20 plus
grab), 2 claws +30 (2d6+8), tail slap
+25 (2d6+4)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** consume fear, constrict
(2d6+8), rend (2 claws, 2d6+12)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 18th;
concentration +24)  
Constant—*deathwatch, true seeing*  
At will—*[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only),[ *persistent image](../../magic/spells/persistent-image.md)* (DC 21)  
3/day—*[greater shadow conjuration](../../magic/spells/shadow-conjuration-greater.md)* (DC 23), *[greater shadow evocation](../../magic/spells/shadow-evocation-greater.md)* (DC 24), *[mirage arcana](../../magic/spells/mirage-arcana.md)* (DC 21), *[nightmare](../../magic/spells/nightmare.md)* (DC 21), *[permanent image](../../magic/spells/permanent-image.md)* (DC 22), *[quickened phantasmal killer](../../magic/spells/phantasmal-killer.md)* (DC 20)  
1/day—*[mislead](../../magic/spells/mislead.md)* (DC 22), *summon* (level 8, 1 temerdaemon or 1d3 suspiridaemons 50%), *[symbol of fear](../../magic/spells/symbol-of-fear.md)* (DC 22), *[weird](../../magic/spells/weird.md)* (DC 25)

**Statistics**

**Str** 26, **Dex** 25, **Con** 23, **Int** 17, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +23; **CMB** +32 (+36 grapple); **CMD** 50 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Combat Casting](../../rules/feats/combat-casting.md), [Combat Reflexes](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Greater Spell Penetration](../../rules/feats/greater-spell-penetration.md), [Greater Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/greater-vital-strike.md), Improved
Critical (bite), Improved
Initiative, [Improved Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/improved-vital-strike.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), Quicken Spell-Like
Ability (*phantasmal killer*), Spell
Penetration, [Vital Strike](../../rules/feats/vital-strike.md)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Bluff +32, Fly +35, Intimidate +32, Knowledge (arcana,
planes) +29, Perception +30, Sense Motive +30, Spellcraft +29,
Stealth +29  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal;
telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** compression, tangible horror

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abaddon)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cabal (3–4)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Consume Fear (Su)** As a standard action, a phasmadaemon that begins its turn grappling an opponent can attempt to feed on the creature’s mortality and innate terror. Any creature that does not succeed at a DC 27 Will save takes 1d6 points of Charisma drain and becomes shaken for 2d4 rounds; in addition, the phasmadaemon gains 5 temporary hit points for every point of Charisma drain dealt this way. If the creature being grappled is already panicked at the beginning of the phasmadaemon’s turn, it must instead succeed at a DC 27 Fortitude save or be slain instantly by the phasmadaemon, which gains a +4 profane bonus on attack rolls, saving throws, and checks for 24 hours as a result of feeding on the death fears of its victim. The save DCs are Charisma-based.

**Tangible Horror (Su)** A phasmadaemon’s illusion abilities are partially real at a level above and beyond those normally conjured forth by similar illusion spells. If a creature succeeds at its Will save to disbelieve either a phasmadaemon’s *greater shadow conjuration* or *greater shadow evocation* spell-like ability, the conjured or evoked spell has 80% of the normal effect or is 80% likely to occur, rather than 60%.
