## Adult Infernal Dragon (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
LE Huge dragon (evil, extraplanar, lawful)  
**Init** +7; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +28  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 25)

**Defense**

**AC** 31, touch 7, flat-footed 31 (–1 Dex, +24 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 250 (20d12+120)  
**Fort** +18, **Ref** +11, **Will** +19; +6 vs. divination  
**Defensive Abilities** inscrutable; **DR** 5/chaotic; **Immune** fire,
hellfire, mind-affecting effects, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +29 (2d8+15/19–20), 2 claws +29 (2d6+10/19–20),
tail slap +23 (2d6+15), 2 wings +23 (1d8+5)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (100-ft. line, 12d10 hellfire damage,
Reflex DC 26 half), commanding voice, crush, oppressive heat  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +12)  
3rd (5)—*[blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)* (DC 18), *[vision of hell](../../magic/spells/vision-of-hell.md)* (DC 19)  
2nd (7)—*cure mod. wounds, darkness, hold person* (DC 17)  
1st (8)—*[bane](../../magic/spells/bane.md)* (DC 16), *[command](../../magic/spells/command.md)* (DC 18), *[forbid action](../../magic/spells/forbid-action.md)* (DC 18), *[sanctuary](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md)* (DC 16), *[shield of faith](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 15), *detect magic, guidance, mending, read magic, resistance, spark* (DC 15)

**Statistics**

**Str** 31, **Dex** 8, **Con** 23, **Int** 24, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +32; **CMD** 41 (45 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Bleeding Crit., [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), Crit. Focus, Imp. Crit. (bite, claw), Imp. Init., [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Weapon Focus (bite](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), claw), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
**Skills** Appraise +30, Bluff +28, Diplomacy +28, Fly +14,
Intimidate +28, Knowledge (history, local, nobility, planes) +30,
Linguistics +30, Perception +28, Sense Motive +28, Spellcraft +30  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Infernal, plus any 20 others  
**SQ** planar infusion (180 ft.)
