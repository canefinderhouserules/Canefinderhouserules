## Horla (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
CE Small aberration (extraplanar)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +18

**Defense**

**AC** 19, touch 17, flat-footed 13 (+1 dodge, +5 Dex, +2 natural, +1 size)  
**hp** 143 (11d8+55)  
**Fort** +8, **Ref** +8, **Will** +11  
**DR** 5/magic and slashing; **Immune** fear, fire, poison, sleep

**Offense**

**Speed** fly 30 ft. (good)  
**Melee** 3 tentacles +15 (1d4+5)  
**Special Attacks** command somnambulist, ennui, sneak attack +2d6  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 8th; concentration +12)  
Constant—*[greater invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility-greater.md)*  
At will—*[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)* (DC 16), *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 17)  
3/day—*[crushing despair](../../magic/spells/crushing-despair.md)* (DC 18)  
1/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 18), *[deep slumber](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)* (DC 17), *[dominate person](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md)* (DC 19), *[status](../../magic/spells/status.md)* (DC 16)

**Statistics**

**Str** 14, **Dex** 20, **Con** 21, **Int** 13, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 19  
**Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +12 (+14 disarm); **CMD** 25 (27 vs. disarm)  
**Feats** [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Combat Expertise](../../rules/feats/combat-expertise.md), [Improved Disarm](../../rules/feats/improved-disarm.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Weapon Focus (tentacle)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md)  
*House rules: Agile Maneuvers and Weapon Finesse are one feat, Weapon Finesse; in their place: Dodgility; Weapon Finesse adds Dex instead of Str to finesse damage and CMB.*  
**Skills** Bluff +15, Fly +25, Perception +18,
Sense Motive +15, Use Magic Device +15  
**Languages** Aklo; absorb language; telepathy 200 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Ethereal plane)  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or invasion (3–10)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Absorb Language (Ex)** When a creature answers a horla’s telepathy, the horla can speak any languages known by that creature for 24 hours.

**Command Somnambulist (Su)** Once per day when a horla controls a sleeping creature via *dominate person*, it can cause the creature to wake and obey it for a number of hours equal to the horla’s Charisma modifier (4 hours for the typical horla). If the creature is allowed to return to sleep before this duration expires, it retains no memories of any of the activities it performed during that time.

**Ennui (Su)** Once per day as a standard action, a horla can target a sleeping creature within 30 feet with an insidious curse. The target can resist the ennui with a successful DC 19 Will save, but regardless of the result of the save, the target is not aware that it has been targeted by this subtle effect. If the victim fails this save, it takes a –2 penalty on ability checks, saving throws, and skill checks for 24 hours. This penalty is doubled to –4 on saving throws against fear effects and for concentration checks. This is a mind-affecting curse effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.
