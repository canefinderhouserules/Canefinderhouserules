## Adult Rift Dragon (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
CE Huge dragon (chaotic, evil, extraplanar)  
**Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +28  
**Aura** frightful presence (180 ft., DC 26)

**Defense**

**AC** 31, touch 8, flat-footed 31 (+23 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 400 (20d12+160)  
**Fort** +19, **Ref** +14, **Will** +17  
**DR** 5/good; **Immune** acid, disease, nausea, paralysis, poison,
sleep, stun; **SR** 27

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft., burrow 30 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +30 (2d8+16/19–20 plus bleed), 2 claws +29 (2d6+11
plus bleed), tail slap +27 (2d6+16/19–20), 2 wings +27 (1d8+5)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** abyssal juggernaut, bleed 6, breath weapon
(50-ft. cone, 12d10 acid, Reflex DC 27 half), corrosion, crush,
foul acid, trample (2d8+16 plus bleed, DC 31)  
**Oracle Spells Known** (CL 7th; concentration +13)  
3rd (5)—*[contagion](../../magic/spells/contagion.md)* (DC 19), *[deeper darkness](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md)*  
2nd (8)—*[death knell](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)* (DC 18), *[hold person](../../magic/spells/hold-person.md)* (DC 18), *[shatter](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)* (DC 18)  
1st (8)—*[bane](../../magic/spells/bane.md)* (DC 17), *cure light wounds, murderous command* (DC 17), *obscuring mist, shield of faith*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 16), *detect magic, detect poison, guidance, read magic, resistance, spark* (DC 16)

**Statistics**

**Str** 33, **Dex** 10, **Con** 25, **Int** 18, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 22  
**Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +33; **CMD** 43 (47 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Critical Focus](../../rules/feats/critical-focus.md), [Improved Critical (bite](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), tail slap), Improved
Initiative, [Intimidating Prowess](../../rules/feats/intimidating-prowess.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), Multiattack, [Staggering Critical](../../rules/feats/staggering-critical.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Toughness.*  
**Skills** Bluff +29, Fly +15, Intimidate +40, Knowledge (arcana,
planes) +27, Perception +28, Sense Motive +28, Spellcraft +27,
Stealth +15, Use Magic Device +29  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Draconic  
**SQ** planar infusion (180 ft.)
