**XP** 135  
 NE Tiny undead  

**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +0  
 
  

**Defense**

 **AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 12 (+2 Dex, +2 size)  

**hp** 8 (1d8)  

**Fort** +0, **Ref** +2, **Will** +2  

**Immune** undead traits  
 
  

**Offense**

 **Speed** fly 40 ft. (perfect)  

**Melee** slam +1 (1d2)  

**Special Attacks** screaming  
 
  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 11, **Dex** 15, Con—, Int—, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 10  

**Base Atk** +0; **CMB** -1; **CMD** 11 (can’t be tripped)  
 
  

**Special Abilities**

 **Screaming (Ex)** Once every 1d4 rounds, a screaming beheaded can open its jaw and emit a bone-chilling scream. All creatures within 30 feet must make a Will save (DC 10) or be shaken for 1d4 rounds. This is a sonic mind-affecting fear effect. Whether or not the save is successful, an affected creature is immune to the same beheaded’s scream for 24 hours.
