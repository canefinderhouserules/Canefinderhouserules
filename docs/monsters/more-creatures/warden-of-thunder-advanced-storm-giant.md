## Warden of Thunder (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  

LE Huge humanoid (giant)  

**Init** +4; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +29  



**Defense**



**AC** 40, touch 13, flat-footed 38 (+1 dodge, +13 armor, +3 deflection, +2 Dex, +14 natural, –2 size)  

**hp** 304 (19d8+152)  

**Fort** +22, **Ref** +13, **Will** +18  

**Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **Immune** electricity  



**Offense**



**Speed** 35 ft., swim 30 ft.  

**Melee** mwk greatsword +30/+25/+20 (4d6+24/17–20) or 2 slams +28 (2d6+16)  

**Ranged** mwk composite longbow +17/+12/+7 (3d6+16/×3)  

**Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 15 ft.  

**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th, concentration +19)  


Constant—freedom of movement  

2/day—control weather, levitate  

1/day—*[call lightning](../../magic/spells/call-lightning.md)* (DC 17), *[chain lightning](../../magic/spells/chain-lightning.md)* (DC 20)  




**Statistics**



**Str** 43, **Dex** 18, **Con** 27, **Int** 20, **Wis** 24, **Cha** 19  

**Base Atk** +14; **CMB** +32 (+34 on bull rush and sunder); **CMD** 47 (49 vs bull rush and sunder)  

**Feats** Awesome Blow, Cleave, Combat Reflexes, [Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md), Improved Critical (greatsword), Improved Vital Strike, Iron Will, Vital Strike, [Weapon Focus (greatsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Improved Bull Rush, Improved Sunder are covered by Powerful Maneuvers; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greatsword), Dodgility.*  

**Skills** Acrobatics +23, Climb +33, Craft (any one) +21, Intimidate +26, Perception +29, Perform (sing) +19, Sense Motive +26, Swim +32  

**Languages** Auran, Common, Draconic, Giant  

**SQ** militant, water breathing  

**Gear** +4 full plate, star ring  



**Special Abilities**



**Militant (Ex)** Wardens of thunder are proficient with all simple and all martial weapons.  


**Water Breathing (Ex)** Wardens of thunder can breathe water as well as air.  



**Ecology**


**Environment** mountains  

**Organization** solitary, pair, patrol (2-4 plus several Wardens of Wind)  

**Treasure** standard
