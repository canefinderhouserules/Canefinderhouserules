## Illusionist (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
 Male human illusionist 3  
 CN Medium humanoid  

**Init** -2; **Senses** Perception +3  

**Defense**

 **AC** 10, touch 10, flat-footed 10  

**hp** 21 (3d6+3)  

**Fort** +2, **Ref** -1, **Will** +4  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft.  

**Melee** dagger +3 (1d4+2/19–20)  

**Special Attacks** blinding ray +1 touch (6/day, blindness for 1 round [or deafness for 1 round if target has more than 3 HD], 30-ft. range)  
 **Wizard Spells Prepared** (CL 3rd; concentration +6)  
  2nd—*[minor image](../../magic/spells/minor-image.md)* (DC 16), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[pyrotechnics](../../magic/spells/pyrotechnics.md)*  

1st—*[color spray](../../magic/spells/color-spray.md)* (DC 14), *[feather fall](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*, *[silent image](../../magic/spells/silent-image.md)* (DC 15)  

0—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 14), *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*  

**Prohibited Schools** conjuration, evocation  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 14, **Dex** 10, **Con** 13, **Int** 17, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 8  

**Base Atk** +1; **CMB** +3; **CMD** 13  

**Feats** Alertness, Craft Wondrous Item, Scribe Scroll, Skill Focus (Stealth), Spell Focus (illusion)  

**Skills** Climb +8, Craft (carpentry) +9, Knowledge (engineering) +9, Profession (stagehand) +7, Sense Motive +3, Spellcraft +9, Stealth +6  

**Languages** Common, Dwarven, Infernal, +1 additional  

**SQ** arcane bond (lizard familiar named Carbuncle), extended illusions, hunchback  

**Combat Gear** wand of *minor image* (12 charges), wand of *invisibility* (13 charges); **Other Gear** dagger, spell component pouch, spellbook (contains all prepared spells, all cantrips, and *charm person*, *disguise self*, magic aura, *ventriloquism*, *hypnotic pattern*, and *make whole*)  

**Special Abilities**

 **Hunchback (Ex)**  His hunched and twisted back forces him to walk with a clumsy, shuffling gait. This reduces his land speed by 10 feet and imparts a –2 penalty on Reflex saves and Initiative checks.
