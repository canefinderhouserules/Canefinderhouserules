**XP** **800**  
NE Large undead

**Melee** slams +4 (1d8+3)  
**Ranged** (ranged touch) belch +2 (1d6 [acid, cold, electricity or fire])  
**Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** Belching

 **Str** 16, **Dex** 13, Con—, Int—, **Wis** 9, **Cha** 15
