## very old Underworld Dragon (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
 LE Huge dragon (fire)  
 **Init** +3; **Senses** dragon senses, smoke vision; Perception +27; **Aura** frightful presence (270 ft., DC 24)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 36, touch 7, flat-footed 36 (-1 Dex, +29 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 250 (20d12+120)  
 **Fort** +18, **Ref** +11, **Will** +16  
 **DR** 15/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 26  
 **Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., burrow 50 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
 **Melee** bite +30 (2d8+16/19-20), 2 claws +30 (2d6+11/19-20), gore +29 (2d6+16), tail slap +24 (2d6+16)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite and gore)  
 **Special Attacks** adamantine claws, breath weapon (100-ft. line, 18d6 fire damage, DC 26), crush (DC 26, 2d8+16)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +24)  

  At will—soften earth and stone, spike stones (DC 18), stone shape, wall of stone  

 **Spells Known** (CL 11th; concentration +15)  

  5th (4/day)—transmute rock to mud, waves of fatigue  
 4th (7/day)—enervation, shout (DC 18), stone shape  
 3rd (7/day)—displacement, fireball (DC 17), protection from good, slow  
 2nd (7/day)—acid arrow, daze monster (DC 16), flaming sphere (DC 16), *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*, scorching ray  
 1st (7/day)—burning hands (DC 15), cause fear (DC 15), magic missile, ray of enfeeblement (DC 15), true strike  
 0 (at will)—acid splash, bleed (DC 14), detect magic, flare (DC 14), ghost sound, mage hand, read magic, resistance, touch of fatigue (DC 14)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 33, **Dex** 8, **Con** 23, **Int** 18, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 18  
 **Base Atk** +20; **CMB** +33 (+35 bull rush); **CMD** 42 (44 vs bull rush, 46 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Improved Bull Rush, Improved Critical (bite, claw), Improved Initiative, Improved Natural Armor, Lunge, Power Attack, Skill Focus (Stealth), Weapon Focus (bite, claw)  
 **Skills** Appraise +27, Bluff +27, Climb +34, Fly +14, Intimidate +27, Knowledge (dungeoneering, geography, planes) +27, Perception +27, Stealth +20  
 **Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Ignan, Terran  
 **SQ** change shape, underworld burrower  

**Special Abilities**

 **Adamantine Claws (Ex)** The claws of an underworld dragon are made of adamantine, and have the qualities of a weapon made from that material.  

 **Change Shape (Su)** A young or older underworld dragon can assume any humanoid form three times per day as if using polymorph.  

 **Smoke Vision (Ex)** A very young and older underworld dragon can see perfectly in smoky conditions (such as those created by pyrotechnics).  

 **Underworld Burrower (Ex)** An adult underworld dragon gains a 10-foot bonus to its burrow speed. When the underworld dragon becomes old and every two age categories thereafter, its burrow speed increases by an additional 10 feet.
