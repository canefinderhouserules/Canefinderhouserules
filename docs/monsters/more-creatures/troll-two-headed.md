## Two-Headed Troll (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
 Male two-headed troll  
 CE Large humanoid (giant)  
 **Init** +0; **Senses** darkvision 90 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +14  
 

**Defense**

 
 **AC** 21, touch 9, flat-footed 21 (+12 natural, –1 size)  
 **hp** 135 (9d8+63); regeneration 5 (acid or fire)  
 **Fort** +10, **Ref** +3, **Will** +5  
 

**Offense**

 
 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** 2 claws +13 (1d6+8), 2 bites +13 (1d8+8)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; Reach 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** rend (2 claws, 1d6+8)  
 

**Tactics**

 
  The beast tries to frighten and demoralize opponents on the first round of combat using Intimidate. He then moves to engage the largest looking foe in melee.
