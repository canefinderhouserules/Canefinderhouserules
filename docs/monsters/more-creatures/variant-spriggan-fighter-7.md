## Variant Spriggan Fighter 7 (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
 Male variant spriggan fighter 7  
 CE Small humanoid (gnome)  
 **Init** +8; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +10  

**Defense**

 **AC** 21, touch 16, flat-footed 16 (+5 armor, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +1 size)  
 **hp** 152 (11 HD; 4d8+7d10+39)  
 **Fort** +11, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4; +2 vs. fear  
 **Defensive Abilities** bravery +2  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 flail +17/+12 (1d6+6)  
 **Special Attacks** weapon Training (flails +1), sneak attack +2d6  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 4th; concentration +5)  

  At will—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 11), daze monster (DC 13), scare (DC 13)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 14, **Dex** 19, **Con** 14, **Int** 12, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 13  
 **Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 26  
 **Feats** Combat Reflexes, Dazzling Display, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Improved Initiative, Intimidating Prowess, Iron Will, Skill Focus (Intimidate), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Weapon Focus (flail), Weapon Specialization (flail)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  
 **Skills** Climb +4, Intimidate +24, Perception +10, Ride +20, Sleight of Hand +13, Stealth +17  
 **Languages** Common, Giant, Gnome  
 **SQ** armor training 2, giant rider, size alteration, spriggan magic, spriggan skills  
 **Gear** +1 chain shirt, +1 flail  

**Special Abilities**

 **Giant Rider (Ex)** He may ride any humanoid with the giant subtype with a Ride check as if it were a mount. Although giants are ill suited to be mounts, he does not take the –5 penalty on Ride checks for riding one.  

 **Size Alteration (Su)** At will as a standard action, a spriggan can change his size between Small and Large. Weapons, armor, and other objects on the spriggan’s person grow proportionally when he changes size (objects revert to normal size 1 round after a spriggan releases them). When a spriggan becomes Large, his speed increases to 30 feet, he gains +12 Strength, –2 Dexterity, and +6 Constitution, and he takes a –2 size penalty to his AC. While Large, a spriggan cannot use his sneak attack or his racial spell-like abilities (although if he possesses either from class levels or templates, he retains their use in both sizes).  

 **Spriggan Magic (Ex)** A spriggan gains a +1 racial bonus on concentration checks and to save DCs for all of its racial spell-like abilities.  

 **Spriggan Skills (Ex)** Climb, Disable Device, Perception, Sleight of Hand, and Stealth are class skills for spriggans.
