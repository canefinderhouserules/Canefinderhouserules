## Gargoyle Brute (CR 10)

**XP** 6,400  
 Advanced four-armed gargoyle  
 CE Large monstrous humanoid (earth)  
 **Init** +1; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +15  
 

**Defense**

 
 **AC** 24, touch 10, flat-footed 23 (+1 Dex, +14 natural, –1 size)  
 **hp** 195 (13d10+65)  
 **Fort** +14, **Ref** +14, **Will** +15  
 **DR** 10/magic  
 

**Offense**

 
 **Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (average)  
 **Melee** bite +19 (1d8+6), 4 claws +18 (1d8+6/19–20), gore +18 (1d4+6)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** rend (1d6+6)  
 

**Tactics**

 
 **During Combat** Gargoyle brutes wade into combat, focusing on a single foe if possible. They attempt to grapple anyone who proves to be particularly dangerous, allowing other gargoyles to gang up on them.  
 
 **Morale** These creatures do not retreat from combat.  
 

**Statistics**

 
 **Str** 23, **Dex** 12, **Con** 20, **Int** 8, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 11  
 **Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +20; **CMD** 31  
 **Feats** Flyby Attack, Great Fortitude, Hover, Improved Critical (claws), Improved Natural Attack (claws), Skill Focus (Fly), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
 **Skills** Fly +21, Perception +18, Stealth +13; **Racial Modifiers** +2 Stealth (+6 in stony environs)  
 **Languages** Common, Terran  
 **SQ** freeze  
 

**Special Abilities**

 
 **Freeze (Ex)** A gargoyle brute can hold itself so still it appears to be a statue. A gargoyle brute that uses freeze can take 20 on Stealth checks to hide in plain sight as a stone statue.  
 


**Ecology**

 
**Environment** any  
 **Organization** solitary, pair, or wing (3–12)  
 **Treasure** standard
