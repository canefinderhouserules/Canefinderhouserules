## Dusk Kamadan (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
 NE Large magical beast  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +10  

**Defense**

 **AC** 21, touch 14, flat-footed 16 (+4 Dex, +1 dodge, +7 natural, -1 size)  
 **hp** 52 (5d10+25)  
 **Fort** +9, **Ref** +8, **Will** +4  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft.  
 **Melee** bite +10 (1d6+5), 2 claws +9 (1d3+5), snakes +2 (1d4+1)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with snakes)  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-ft. cone, sleep, Fortitude DC 17 negates, usable every 1d4 rounds), pounce  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 21, **Dex** 19, **Con** 20, **Int** 9, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 13  
 **Base Atk** +5; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 26 (30 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Combat Reflexes, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +8 (+12 when jumping), Perception +10, Stealth +8; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth  
**Languages** Aklo  

**Special Abilities**

 **Breath Weapon (Su)** A dusk kamadan can exhale a cone of gas that makes living creatures fall asleep for 5 minutes (Fortitude DC 17 negates). Slapping or wounding awakens a creature put to sleep by this attack, but normal noise does not. This is a sleep effect. The save DC is Constitution-based.  

 **Snakes (Ex)** A dusk kamadan’s snakes attack simultaneously; this is always a secondary attack.  

 Snakes—injury; *save* Fort DC 17; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Con; *cure* 2 consecutive saves.
