## Pugwampi Gremlin Fighter 2 (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
 Male pugwampi fighter 2  
 NE Tiny fey  
 **Init** +5; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +5; **Aura** unluck (20-ft. radius)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 18 (+6 armor, +2 Dex, +2 size)  
 **hp** 25 (1d6+2d10+11)  
 **Fort** +5, **Ref** +4, **Will** +3 (+5 vs. fear)  
 **DR** 2/cold iron; **SR** 7  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 Small warhammer +2 (1d6–1/×3)  
 **Ranged** longbow +6 (1d4/×3)  
 **Space** 2-1/2 ft.; **Reach** 0 ft.  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 1st)  

  At will—prestidigitation, speak with animals  
 1/day—shatter (DC 10)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 7, **Dex** 14, **Con** 14, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 8  
 **Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +2; **CMD** 10  
 **Feats** [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Improved Initiative, [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus (warhammer), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Toughness.*  
 **Skills** Bluff +5, Craft (trapmaking) +4, Disable Device +5, Perception +5 (+1 sound-based), Ride +6, Stealth +18 **Racial Modifiers** +4 Stealth –4 Perception (sound-based)  
 **Languages** Gnoll, Terran  
 **SQ** bravery +1  
 **Gear** breastplate, *+1 Small warhammer*, longbow with 20 arrows  

**Special Abilities**

 **Unluck (Su)** A pugwampi radiates an aura of unluck to a radius of 20 feet. A creature in this area must roll 2d20 whenever a situation calls for a d20 roll (such as an attack roll, a skill check, or a saving throw) and must use the lowest of the two results generated. This is a mind-affecting effect that does not work on animals, other gremlins, and gnolls. Any character that gains any sort of luck bonus (such as that granted by a luckstone or divine favor) is immune to the pugwampi unluck aura.
