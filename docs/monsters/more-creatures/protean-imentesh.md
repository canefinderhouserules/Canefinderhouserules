## Imentesh Protean (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
 CN Large outsider (chaotic, extraplanar, protean, shapechanger)  
 **Init** +7; **Senses** blindsense 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +20  

**Defense**

 **AC** 25, touch 12, flat-footed 22 (+3 Dex, +13 natural, –1 size)  
 **hp** 182 (13d10+52); fast healing 5  
 **Fort** +12, **Ref** +7, **Will** +14  
 **Defensive Abilities** amorphous anatomy, *freedom of movement*; **DR** 10/lawful; **Immune** acid, *polymorph*; **Resist** electricity 10, sonic 10; **SR** 21  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft., fly 30 ft. (perfect), swim 30 ft.  
 **Melee** bite +19 (2d6+7), 2 claws +19 (1d8+7), tail +17 (1d8+3 plus grab)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** constrict (1d8+7), inflict warpwave, sneak attack +4d6  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +15)  

 Constant—*[detect law](../../magic/spells/detect-law.md)*, *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
 At will—*[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[make whole](../../magic/spells/make-whole.md)*, *[major creation](../../magic/spells/major-creation.md)*, *[shatter](../../magic/spells/shatter.md)* (DC 17), *[shrink item](../../magic/spells/shrink-item.md)*  
 3/day—*[chaos hammer](../../magic/spells/chaos-hammer.md)* (DC 19), *[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)* (DC 18)  
 1/day—*[break enchantment](../../magic/spells/break-enchantment.md)*, *[dispel law](../../magic/spells/dispel-law.md)* (DC 20), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[polymorph any object](../../magic/spells/polymorph-any-object.md)* (DC 23)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 24, **Dex** 17, **Con** 18, **Int** 23, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 21  
 **Base Atk** +13; **CMB** +21 (+25 grapple); **CMD** 34 (can’t be tripped)  
 **Feats** Combat Expertise, Combat Reflexes, Improved Feint, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Multiattack, Persuasive  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +19, Bluff +21, Diplomacy +25, Disable Device +16, Fly +9, Intimidate +25, Knowledge (arcana) +22, Knowledge (planes) +22, Knowledge (any two) +19, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Stealth +15, Swim +15; R**acial Modifiers** +8 Swim  
 **Languages** Abyssal, Protean; *tongues*  
 **SQ** change shape (*greater polymorph*)  

**Special Abilities**

 **Inflict Warpwave (Su)**  As a standard action, an imentesh can inflict a warpwave upon any corporeal creature within 100 feet. The target can resist the warpwave’s effects with a DC 20 Fortitude save. If the imentesh wishes, it can use this ability as a swift action, but if it does so, it is affected by the warpwave as well unless it resists the effects with its own Fortitude save. See Table below for a list of possible effects caused by a warpwave. The save DC is Constitution-based.
