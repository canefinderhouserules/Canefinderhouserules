## Red Dragon Ravener (CR 16)

**XP** 76,800  
 Female adult red dragon ravener  
 CE Huge undead (fire)  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** blindsense 120 ft., darkvision 240 ft., dragon senses, smoke vision; Perception +33; **Aura** cowering fear, fire (5 ft., 1d6), frightful presence (180 ft., DC 24)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 36, touch 11, flat-footed 36 (+3 deflection, +21 natural, +4 shield, –2 size)  
 **hp** 210 (17d8+134)  
 **Fort** +16, **Ref** +10, **Will** +17  
 **Defensive Abilities** channel resistance +4, soul ward (31 hp); **DR** 5/good; **Immune** fire, undead traits; **SR** 25  
 **Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
 **Melee** bite +27 (2d8+18/19–20), 2 claws +27 (2d6+12/19–20), tail slap +25 (2d6+18/19–20), 2 wings +25 (1d8+6/19–20)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite)  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon (50-ft. cone, 12d10 fire plus 2 negative levels, Reflex DC 24 half, usable every 1d4 rounds), crush (2d8+18, DC 24), soul consumption (DC 24), soul magic, tail sweep (DC 24)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 17th; concentration +23)  

  At will—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[pyrotechnics](../../magic/spells/pyrotechnics.md)* (DC 16), *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 17)  

 **Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 10th; concentration +16)  

  5th—*[waves of fatigue](../../magic/spells/waves-of-fatigue.md)*  
 4th—*[dimension door](../../magic/spells/dimension-door.md)*, *[lesser globe of invulnerability](../../magic/spells/globe-of-invulnerability-lesser.md)*  
 3rd—*[dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md)*, *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*, *[lightning bolt](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)* (DC 19)  
 2nd—*[command undead](../../magic/spells/command-undead.md)* (DC 18), *[false life](../../magic/spells/false-life.md)*, *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[see invisibility](../../magic/spells/see-invisibility.md)*  
 1st—*[grease* ](../../magic/spells/grease.md)(DC 17), *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)*, *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*,  
 *true strike*  
 0—*[acid splash](../../magic/spells/acid-splash.md)*, *[bleed* ](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)(DC 16), *[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 16), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  

**Tactics**

 **Before Combat** She casts *false life* and *shield* before she engages the PCs.  

 **During Combat** If forced into melee combat, she makes full attacks with all of her natural weapons, focusing her rage on individual combatants.  

 **Morale** Unwilling to give up her unnatural existence, she flees when reduced to 50 hit points or fewer, continuing to plague the land and consume souls until her health is restored.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 35, **Dex** 10, Con—, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 22  
 **Base Atk** +17; **CMB** +31; **CMD** 44 (48 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Cleave, Flyby Attack, Improved Initiative, Improved Vital Strike, Iron Will, Multiattack, Power Attack, Toughness, Vital Strike  
 **Skills** Appraise +25, Bluff +26, Fly +12, Intimidate +34, Knowledge (arcana) +25, Knowledge (history) +18, Knowledge (religion) +25, Perception +33, Sense Motive +25, Spellcraft +25, Stealth +20, Survival +15  
 **Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Ignan, Necril, Orc
