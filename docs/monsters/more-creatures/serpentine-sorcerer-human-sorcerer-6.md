## Serpentine Sorcerer (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
 Female human sorcerer 6  
CN Medium humanoid  
**Init** +2; **Senses** Perception +1  

**Defense**

 **AC** 14, touch 14, flat-footed 11 (+1 deflection, +2 Dex, +1 Dodge)  
 **hp** 54 (6d6+18)  
 **Fort** +3, **Ref** +4, **Will** +6  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** dagger +2 (1d4–1/19–20 plus poison)  
 **Ranged** light crossbow +5 (1d8/19–20 plus poison)  
 **Special Attacks** serpent’s fang* 7 rounds/day  

 **Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 6th; concentration +10)  

  3rd (4)—*[lightning bolt](../../magic/spells/lightning-bolt.md)* (DC 17)  
 2nd (6)—*[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, *[delay poison](../../magic/spells/delay-poison.md)*, *[touch of idiocy](../../magic/spells/touch-of-idiocy.md)*  
 1st (7)—*[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 16), *[hypnotism](../../magic/spells/hypnotism.md)* (DC 16), *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*, *[ray of enfeeblement](../../magic/spells/ray-of-enfeeblement.md)* (DC 15), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*

 **Bloodline** serpentine*  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 8, **Dex** 14, **Con** 13, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 18  
 **Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +2; **CMD** 16  
 **Feats** Arcane Strike, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Eschew Materials, Spell Focus (enchantment), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md)

 **Skills** Bluff +14, Diplomacy +10, Knowledge (arcana) +5, Spellcraft +9, Use Magic Device +10  
 **Languages** Common, Polyglot  
 **SQ** serpent’s friend*, viper familiar (named Veskavesh)  
 **Combat Gear** *scrolls of bull’s strength* (2), *scroll of expeditious retreat*, *scroll of glitterdust*, *scroll of mage armor*, *scroll of summon swarm*, black adder venom (3), blackfingers paste, blood sap (2), dreamtime tea (3), harlot sweets (2), slaver’s drops (4); **Other Gear** light crossbow with 20 bolts, dagger, *ring of protection +1*, emerald earrings (50 gp), emerald pendant (125 gp)  
 **PC Gear** +12,497 gp
