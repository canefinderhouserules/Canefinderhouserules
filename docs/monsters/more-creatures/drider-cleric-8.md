## Drider Cleric 8 (CR 15)

**XP** 51,200  
 Female drider cleric 8  
 CE Large aberration  

**Init**  +3; **Senses**  darkvision 120 ft.; Perception +18  

**Defense**

 **AC** 35, touch 13, flat-footed 31 (+9 armor, +3 Dex, +1 dodge, +8 natural, +5 shield, –1 size)  

**hp** 169 (17 HD; 9d8+8d8+89)  

**Fort**  +15, **Ref**  +11, **Will**  +19  

 **DR**  2/—; **Immune**  sleep; **SR**  26  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.  

**Melee** *+3 unholy heavy mace* +17/+12/+7 (1d8+5), bite +9 (1d4+1 plus poison)  

**Ranged** mwk javelin +15 (1d6+2)  

**Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft.  

**Special Attacks** channel negative energy 10/day (DC 22, 7d6), destructive smite (+7, 9/day), destructive aura (+7, 14 rounds/day), poison (DC 19), scythe of evil (7 rounds, 2/day), web (+15 ranged, DC 23, 17 hp)  

**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +14)  
  Constant—detect good, detect law, detect magic  

 At will—dancing lights, darkness, faerie fire  

 1/day—*[clairaudience/clairvoyance](../../magic/spells/clairaudience-clairvoyance.md)*, deeper darkness, dispel magic, levitate, suggestion (DC 18)  
 **Domain Spell-Like Abilities Known** (CL 14th; concentration +20)  
  9/day—touch of evil (7 rounds)  
 **Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 14th; concentration +20)  
  7th—blasphemy<sup>D</sup> (DC 23), destruction (DC 23), *[mass cure serious wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-serious-wounds-mass.md)*  

 6th—blade barrier (DC 22), harmD (DC 22), heal, *[mass inflict moderate wounds](../../magic/spells/inflict-moderate-wounds-mass.md)* (DC 22), word of recall  

 5th—breath of life, flame strike (DC 21), greater magic weapon (already cast), shoutD (DC 21), slay living (DC 21)  

 4th—chaos hammer (DC 20), divine power, magic vestment (both already cast), poison (DC 20), unholy blight<sup>D</sup> (DC 20)  

 3rd—bestow curse (DC 19), cure serious wounds, deeper darkness, invisibility purge, magic circle against good<sup>D</sup>, prayer  

 2nd—augury, *[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*, cure moderate wounds, death knell, shatter<sup>D</sup>, spiritual weapon  

 1st—bane (DC 17), cure light wounds, divine favor, doom (DC 17), entropic shield, protection from good<sup>D</sup>, shield of faith  

 0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 16), detect magic, guidance, read magic  

**<sup>D</sup>**  Domain spell; **Domains**  Destruction, Evil  

**Statistics**

 **Str**  15, **Dex** 17, **Con**  20, **Int**  13, **Wis**  22, **Cha**  20  

**Base Atk**  +12; **CMB**  +15; **CMD**  28 (40 vs. trip)  

**Feats** Blind-Fight, Channel Smite, Combat Casting, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Extra Channel, Lightning Reflexes, Selective Channeling, Weapon Focus (heavy mace), Weapon Focus (bite)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility.*  

**Skills** Climb +22, Diplomacy +18, Heal +13, Intimidate +17, Knowledge (religion) +17, Linguistics +5, Perception +18, Spellcraft +13, Stealth +15  

**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Elven, Undercommon  

**SQ** undersized weapons  

**Combat Gear** scroll of break enchantment, *scrolls of cure moderate wounds* (3), *scroll of heal*, *scroll of planar ally*, *scroll of scrying*, *wand of cure serious wounds* (15 charges); **Other Gear** *+1 adamantine breastplate*, *+1 light shield*, *+1 unholy heavy mace*, masterwork javelin (3), *cloak of resistance +1*, *handy haversack*, silver unholy symbol, 10 pp  

**PC Gear** +195,000 gp  

**Special Abilities**

 **Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 19; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Str; *cure* 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based.  
 **Undersized Weapons (Ex)** Although a drider is Large, its upper torso is the same size as that of a Medium humanoid‘s upper torso. As a result, it wields weapons as if it were one size category smaller than its actual size (Medium for most driders).
