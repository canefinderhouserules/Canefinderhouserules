## Werewolf Ranger 8 (hybrid form) (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
 Male human natural werewolf ranger 8  
 CE Medium humanoid (human, shapechanger)  
 **Init** +7 (+7 in forest); **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +12 (+16 in forest)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 23, touch 14, flat-footed 19 (+1 dodge, +5 armor, +3 Dex, +4 natural)  
 **hp** 136 (8d10+56)  
 **Fort** +12, **Ref** +10, **Will** +6  
 **DR** 10/silver  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 bastard sword +11/+6 (1d10+4/19–20), mwk dagger +10/+5 (1d4+1/19–20), bite +7 (1d6+2 plus trip and curse of lycanthropy)  
 **Special Attacks** curse of lycanthropy, favored enemy (humans +4, elves +2)  

 **Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 5th; concentration +6)  

  2nd—*[bear’s endurance](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md)*  
 1st—longstrider, magic fang  

**Tactics**

 **Before Combat** Once alerted by the guards, he casts *bear’s endurance*, longstrider, and magic fang.  

 **During Combat** He attempts to intimidate and demoralize anyone who dares to enter Highthrone. Then he draws his weapons and attacks any foes brave enough to face him.  

 **Morale** He must hold on to Highthrone at all costs, and fights to the death.  

 **Base Statistics** Without his spells, his statistics are:  

 **hp** 88 (8d10+40); **Fort** +10; **Speed** 30 ft.; **Melee** +1 bastard sword +11/+6 (1d10+4/19–20), mwk dagger +10/+5 (1d4+1/19–20), bite +6 (1d6+1 plus trip and curse of lycanthropy); **Con** 17  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 17, **Dex** 16, **Con** 21, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 10  
 **Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 25  
 **Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Exotic Weapon Proficiency (bastard sword), [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](../../rules/feats/improved-two-weapon-fighting.md), Iron Will, Two-Weapon Fighting, Weapon Focus (bastard sword), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Endurance and Toughness are one feat, Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility, Improved Initiative.*  
 **Skills** Climb +13, Intimidate +11, Knowledge (geography) +7 (+11 in forest), Knowledge (local) +4, Knowledge (nature) +7, Perception +12 (+16 in forest), Spellcraft +7, Stealth +13 (+17 in forest), Survival +12 (+16 in forest)  
 **Languages** Common  
 **SQ** change shape (human, hybrid, and wolf; polymorph), favored terrain (forest +4, mountain +2), hunter’s bond (companions), lycanthropic empathy (wolves and dire wolves), swift tracker, track +4, wild empathy +8, woodland stride  
 **Combat Gear** potion of cure serious wounds, silversheen (4); **Other Gear** +1 mithral chain shirt, +1 bastard sword, masterwork dagger, cloak of resistance +1, 163 gp  

**Special Abilities**

 **Change Shape (Su)** He has three forms—a humanoid form, an animal form, and a hybrid form. Equipment does not meld with the new form between humanoid and hybrid form, but does between those forms and animal form. He can shift to any of its three alternate forms as a move-equivalent action. If slain, he reverts to its humanoid form, but remains dead.  

 **Curse of Lycanthropy (Su)** His bite attack in animal or hybrid form infects a humanoid target with lycanthropy (Fortitude DC 15 negates). If the victim’s size is not within one size category, this ability has no effect.  

 **Lycanthropic Empathy (Ex)** In any form, he can communicate and empathize with wolves and dire wolves. He can use Diplomacy to alter such an animal‘s attitude, and when so doing gain a +4 racial bonus on the check.
