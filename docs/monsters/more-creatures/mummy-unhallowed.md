## Unhallowed Mummy (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
 Unhallowed mummy  
 LE Medium undead  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +16; **Aura** despair (30 ft., paralyzed for 1d4 rounds, Will DC 16 negates)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 20, touch 10, flat-footed 20 (+10 natural)(+2 vs good creatures)  
 **hp** 88 (8d8+24)  
 **Fort** +4, **Ref** +2, **Will** +8; +2 vs good creatures  
 **DR** 5/—; **Immune** undead traits  
 **Weaknesses** vulnerable to fire  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft.  
 **Melee** slam +14 (1d8+10 plus mummy rot)  

**Tactics**

 **During Combat** When released, each mummy activates its *coldfire wrappings* and charges intruders.  

 **Morale** The mummies fight until destroyed.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 24, **Dex** 10, Con—, **Int** 6, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 15  
 **Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +13; **CMD** 23  
 **Feats** [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Skill Focus (Perception), Weapon Focus, [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
 **Skills** Perception +16, Stealth +11  
 **Languages** Common  

**Special Abilities**

 **Despair (Su)** All creatures within a 30-foot radius that see a mummy must make a DC 16 Will save or be paralyzed 1 by fear for 1d4 rounds. Whether or not the save is successful, that creature cannot be affected again by the same mummy’s despair ability for 24 hours. This is a paralysis and a mind-affecting fear affect. The save DC is Charisma-based.  

 **Mummy Rot (Su)** *Mummy Rot*: curse and disease—slam; *save* Fort DC 16; *onset* 1 minute; *frequency* 1/day; *effect* 1d6 Con and 1d6 Cha; *cure* —.
