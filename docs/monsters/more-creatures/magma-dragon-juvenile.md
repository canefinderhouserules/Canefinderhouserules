## Magma Dragon, Juvenile (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
CN Medium dragon (fire)  
**Init** +6; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +17  


**Defense**


**AC** 25, touch 12, flat-footed 23 (+2 Dex, +13 natural)  
**hp** 115 (11d12+44)  
 **Fort** +13, **Ref** +9, **Will** +12  
**Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to cold  


**Offense**


**Speed** 40 ft., fly 150 ft. (average)  
**Melee** bite +18 (1d8+9/19-20 plus 4 fire), 2 claws +17 (1d6+6), 2 wings +12 (1d4+3)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-ft. cone, DC 19, 8d6 fire)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 11th; concentration +13)  

At will—*[burning hands](../../magic/spells/burning-hands.md)* (DC 13), *[scorching ray](../../magic/spells/scorching-ray.md)*  

**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 3rd; concentration +5)  

1st (6/day)—*[flare burst](../../magic/spells/flare-burst.md)** (DC 13), *[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)* (DC 13), *[shield](../../magic/spells/shield.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 12), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[open/close](../../magic/spells/open-close.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*, *[spark](../../magic/spells/spark.md)**  
* This spell is from the Pathfinder RPG Advanced Player’s Guide  


**Statistics**


**Str** 23, **Dex** 14, **Con** 19, **Int** 16, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +11; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 29 (33 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Great Fortitude, Improved Critical (bite), Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Vital Strike, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Acrobatics +13 (+20 jump), Climb +20, Escape Artist +13, Fly +16, Intimidate +16, Perception +17, Sense Motive +17, Stealth +16, Swim +20  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Ignan  
**SQ** superheated  


**Special Abilities**


**Superheated (Su)** At young age and older, a magma dragon’s bite attack deals additional fire damage equal to its age category.  


**Ecology**


**Environment** any mountains or underground (Plane of Fire)  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple
