## Gold Dragon Wyrm (CR 21)

**XP** 409,600  
LG Gargantuan dragon (fire)  
Init—2; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +42; **Aura** fire (10 ft., 1d6 fire), frightful presence (330 ft., DC 31)  


**Defense**


**AC** 41, touch 4, flat-footed 41 (-2 Dex, +37 natural, -4 size)  
**hp** 406 (28d12+224)  
**Fort** +24, **Ref** +14, **Will** +25  
**DR** 20/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 32  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold  


**Offense**


**Speed** 60 ft., fly 250 ft. (clumsy), swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +39 (4d6+22/19-20), 2 claws +39 (2d8+15/19-20), tail slap +37 (2d8+22/19-20), 2 wings +37 (2d6+7/19-20)  
**Space** 20 ft.; **Reach** 15 ft. (20 ft. with bite)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 22d10 fire, DC 32), crush (4d6+22, DC 32), tail sweep (2d6+22, DC 32), weakening breath  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 28th; concentration +35)  

At will—bless, daylight, detect evil, geas/quest, sunburst (DC 25)  

**Spells Known** (CL 17th; concentration +24)  

8th (4/day)—greater prying eyes, protection from spells  
7th (7/day)—greater teleport, resurrection, vision  
6th (7/day)—antimagic field, greater dispel magic, heal  
5th (7/day)—dispel evil (DC 24), plane shift, teleport, true seeing  
4th (7/day)—divination, restoration, spell immunity, stoneskin  
3rd (8/day)—dispel magic, haste, invisibility purge, prayer  
2nd (8/day)—aid, cure moderate wounds, lesser restoration, resist energy, silence  
1st (8/day)—alarm, divine favor, mage armor, shield, shield of faith  
0 (at will)—detect magic, detect poison, light, mage hand, mending, open/close, prestidigitation, read magic , stabilize  


**Statistics**


**Str** 41, **Dex** 6, **Con** 27, **Int** 24, **Wis** 25, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +28; **CMB** +47; **CMD** 55 (59 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Alertness, Critical Focus, Extend Spell, Hover, Improved Critical (bite, claw, wing, tail), Iron Will, Multiattack, Power Attack, Quicken Spell, Staggering Critical, Vital Strike  
**Skills** Diplomacy +38, Fly +15, Heal +38, Knowledge (arcana, history, local, nobility, planes, religion) +38, Perception +42, Sense Motive +42, Spellcraft +38, Swim +54; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Swim  
**Languages** Celestial, Common, Draconic, Elven, Halfling, 3 more  
**SQ** change shape, detect gems, fast flight, luck  


**Special Abilities**


**Change Shape (Su)** A very young or older gold dragon can assume any animal or humanoid form three times per day as if using polymorph.  

**Detect Gems (Sp)** A young or older gold dragon can detect gems three times per day. This functions as locate object, but can only be used to locate gemstones.  

**Fast Flight (Ex)** A young or older gold dragon is treated as one size category larger when determining his fly speed.  

**Fire Aura (Su)** An old or older gold dragon is surrounded by an aura of fire. All creatures within 5 feet of the dragon take 1d6 points of fire damage at the beginning of the dragon’s turn. An ancient gold dragon’s aura extends to 10 feet. A great wyrm’s damage increases to 2d6. The dragon can activate or suppress this aura as a free action.  

**Luck (Sp)** Once per day an adult or older gold dragon can touch a gem, usually one embedded in the dragon’s hide, and enspell it to bring good luck. As long as the dragon carries the gem, it and every good creature within a given radius of it (10 ft. per age category) receives a +1 luck bonus on all saving throws. If the dragon gives an enspelled gem to another creature, only that bearer gets the bonus. The effect lasts 1d3 hours plus 3 hours per age category of the dragon. This ability is the equivalent of a 2nd-level spell.  

**Weakening Breath (Su)** Instead of a cone of fire, a gold dragon can breathe a cone of weakening gas. Creatures within the cone must succeed on a Fortitude save or take 1 point of Strength damage per age category (Will save half).  



**Ecology**


**Environment** warm plains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple
