## Thanatotic Titan (CR 22)

**XP** 614,400  
 CE Colossal outsider (chaotic, evil, extraplanar)  

 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 120 ft., *true seeing*; Perception +31  

  

**Defense**

 **AC** 38, touch 2, flat-footed 38 (+12 armor, +24 natural, –8 size)  

 **hp** 471 (23d10+345)  

 **Fort** +22, **Ref** +15, **Will** +20; +8 resistance vs. mind-affecting  

 **DR** 15/lawful and epic; **Immune** aging, death effects, disease; **SR** 33  

  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 60 ft. (40 ft. in armor)  

 **Melee** *+3 unholy battleaxe* +38/+33/+28/+23 (6d6+22/19–20/×3), claw +29 (2d8+9) or 2 claws +34 (2d8+19)  

 **Ranged** rock +16/+11/+6/+1 (4d6+28)  

 **Space** 30 ft.; **Reach** 30 ft.  

 **Special Attacks** godslayer, rock throwing (100 ft.), trample (2d8+28, DC 40)  

 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +27)  
 Constant—*[air walk](../../magic/spells/air-walk.md)*, *[mind blank](../../magic/spells/mind-blank.md)*, *[spell turning](../../magic/spells/spell-turning.md)*, *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*  
 At will—*[bestow curse](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)* (DC 21), *[break enchantment](../../magic/spells/break-enchantment.md)*, divination, *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[sending](../../magic/spells/sending.md)*  
 3/day—*[disintegrate](../../magic/spells/disintegrate.md)* (DC 23), *greater* *[scrying](../../magic/spells/scrying.md)* (DC 24), *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*, *mass* *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 23)  
 1/day—*[greater planar ally](../../magic/spells/planar-ally-greater.md)*, *[imprisonment](../../magic/spells/imprisonment.md)* (DC 26), *[meteor swarm](../../magic/spells/meteor-swarm.md)* (DC 26), *[true resurrection](../../magic/spells/true-resurrection.md)*  

  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 49, **Dex** 10, **Con** 41, **Int** 27, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 24  

 **Base Atk** +23; **CMB** +50; **CMD** 60  

 **Feats** Awesome Blow, Bleeding Critical, Critical Focus, Greater Vital Strike, Improved Bull Rush, Improved Critical (battleaxe), Improved Initiative, Improved Vital Strike, Iron Will, Lightning Reflexes, Vital Strike, [Weapon Focus (battleaxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (battleaxe).*  

 **Skills** Bluff +33, Craft (any one) +34, Diplomacy +30, Intimidate +33, Knowledge (arcana, history, and planes) +34, Knowledge (religion) +31, Perception +31, Perform (any one) +30, Ride +18, Sense Motive +31, Spellcraft +34, Stealth +5, Use Magic Device +33  

 **Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Common; telepathy 300 ft.  

 **SQ** change shape (any humanoid, *alter self*)  

  

**Special Abilities**

 **Godslayer (Su)**  When a thanatotic titan damages a creature capable of casting divine spells, that creature must make a DC 28 Will save or be unable to cast any divine spells for 1d4 rounds and be shaken. If the save is successful, the creature struck is merely shaken for 1 round. A thanatotic titan’s attacks are treated as epic and evil for the purposes of overcoming damage reduction. The save DC is Charisma-based.  
  
  

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  

 **Organization** solitary, pair, or war band (3–6)  

 **Treasure** standard (*+3 full plate*, *+3 unholy battleaxe*, other treasure)
