**XP** 1,200  
 Human Fighter 2/Rogue 3  
 CN Medium humanoid (human)  
 **Init** +7; **Senses** Perception +9  

**Defense**

 **AC** 18, touch 14, flat-footed 14 (+4 armor, +3 Dex, +1 dodge)  
 **hp** 62 (5 HD; 2d10+3d8+13)  
 **Fort** +6, **Ref** +6 (+1 vs. traps), **Will** +2 (+1 vs. fear)

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.
