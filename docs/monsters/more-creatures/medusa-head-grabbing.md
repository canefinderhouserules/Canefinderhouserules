## Grabbing Medusa Head (CR 3)

**XP** 800  
 NE Small undead  
 **Init** +1; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +0  
 

**Defense**

 
 **AC** 15, touch 12, flat-footed 14 (+1 Dex, +3 natural, +1 size)  
 **hp** 44 (4d8+12)  
 **Fort** +3, **Ref** +2, **Will** +4  
 **Immune** undead traits  
 

**Offense**

 
 **Speed** fly 40 ft. (perfect)  
 **Melee** bite +6 (1d4+2 plus grab, petrifying bite)  
 **Special Attacks** grabbing  
 

**Statistics**

 
 **Str** 14, **Dex** 12, Con—, Int—, **Wis** 11, **Cha** 15  
 **Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +4 (+8 grapple); **CMD** 15 (can’t be tripped)  
 **Feats** Flyby Attack **Languages** common  
 

**Special Abilities**

 
 **Grabbing (Ex)** This type of beheaded has long tendrils of ragged hair. It gains the grab special ability when using its slam attack, and can attempt to grapple any creature of Medium size or smaller in this way.  
 
 **Petrifying Bite (Su)** Creatures bitten by a medusa head must make a DC 14 Fortitude save or turn to stone for 1d4 rounds. Targets immune to poison are immune to this effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.  
 


**Ecology**

 
**Environment** any  
 **Organization** solitary, pair, or trio  
 **Treasure** none
