## Draugr Captain Ranger 10 (CR 14)

**XP** 38,400  
 Male draugr captain ranger 10  
 CE Medium undead (water)  
 **Init** +7; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +19  
 

**Defense**

 
 **AC** 25, touch 14, flat-footed 21 (+7 armor, +3 Dex, +1 dodge, +4 natural)  
 **hp** 251 (13 HD; 3d8+10d10+114)  
 **Fort** +17, **Ref** +13, **Will** +13  
 **Defensive Abilities** evasion; **DR** 5/bludgeoning or slashing; **Immune** undead traits; **Resist** cold 10, fire 10  
 

**Offense**

 
 **Speed** 20 ft., swim 20 ft.  
 **Melee** +3 wounding dragon–bane greatsword +22/+17/+12 (2d6+12/17–20 plus 1 negative level) or slam +18 (1d10+9 plus 1 negative level)  
 **Ranged** +2 huntsman composite longbow +17/+12/+7 (1d8+8/×3)  
 **Special Attacks** combat style (two-handed weapon), favored enemy (dragons +6, fey +2, magical beasts +2)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +12)  
 
  3/day—obscuring mist  
 
 **Ranger Spells Prepared** (CL 7th; concentration +10)  
 
  3rd—water walk  
 2nd—barkskin, cat’s grace  
 1st—entangle (DC 14), lead blades, longstrider  
 

**Statistics**

 
 **Str** 22, **Dex** 16, Con—, **Int** 16, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 24  
 **Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +18; **CMD** 32  
 **Feats** Cleave, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Furious Focus, Improved Critical (greatsword), [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), Shield of Swings, Vital Strike, Weapon Focus (greatsword), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Endurance and Toughness are one feat, Toughness; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative, Iron Will.*  
 **Skills** Climb +13, Intimidate +23, Knowledge (arcana) +19, Knowledge (dungeoneering) +19, Knowledge (geography) +19, Knowledge (nature) +19, Perception +19, Stealth +17, Survival +19, Swim +16  
 **Languages** Common (can’t speak), Skald (can’t speak)  
 **SQ** favored terrain (cold +4, water +2), hunter’s bond (companions), swift tracker, track +5, wealth of adventuring, wild empathy +17, woodland stride  
 **Gear** +3 cold resistance fjord linnorm hide armor, +3 wounding dragon–bane greatsword, +2 huntsman composite longbow with 20 arrows, cloak of resistance +2, headband of alluring charisma +2, 170 gp  
 

**Special Abilities**

 
 **Negative Levels (Su)** Instead of causing nausea with a successful hit as an ordinary draugr does, draugr captains bestow 1 negative level on a hit. A draugr captain can even bestow a negative level via a weapon it wields, but if it gains multiple attacks with a weapon, it can only bestow 1 negative level per round in this manner.  
 
 **Wealth of Adventuring (Ex)** This creature was a famed adventurer in life and possessed a number of powerful magic items he collected along his travels, including his iconic linnorm hide armor, a mighty longbow, and his magical greatsword. His wealth is equivalent to that of a 13th-level PC, which increases his CR by 1.  
 
 **Magic Greatsword** **Price** 90,000 gp; **Slot** none; **CL** 10th; **Weight** 8 lbs.; **Aura** moderate conjuration and evocation
