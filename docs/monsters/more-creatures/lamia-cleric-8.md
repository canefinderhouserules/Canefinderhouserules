## Lamia Cleric 8 (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
 Female lamia cleric 8  
 CE Large Monstrous Humanoid  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +19 **Aura**: chaos, evil  

**Defense**

 **AC** 25, touch 16, flat-footed 20 (+2 armor, +2 deflection, +4 Dex, +1 dodge, +7 natural, -1 size)  
 **hp** 213 (9d10+8d8+8 favored class+51)  
 **Fort** +12, **Ref** +14, **Will** +19  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 60 ft.  
 **Melee** +2 flail +20/+15/+10 (1d8+5), touch +12 (1d4 Wis drain), and 2 claws +12 (1d4+1)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** Channel Negative Energy (4d6, DC 18, 7/day), Scythe of Evil (unholy effect on weapon, 4 rds, 1/day), Strength Surge (+4 Str., 10/day), Touch of Evil (sicken, 4 rds, 10/day), Wisdom Drain  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +17)  

 At will— *[disguise self](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)*, *[ventriloquism](../../magic/spells/ventriloquism.md)*

 3/day— *[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 15), *[major image](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)* (DC 14), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 14)

 1/day— *[deep slumber](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)* (DC 14)  

 **Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 8th; concentration +19)  

 **4** – *freedom of movement*, *poison* (DC 23), *sending*, *unholy blight* <sup>D</sup> (DC 21)

 **3** – *blindness/deafness* (DC 22), *cure serious wounds* (x2), *dispel magic*, *magic vestment* <sup>D</sup> (already cast), *stone shape*

 **2** – *bear’s endurance*, bull’s strength <sup>D</sup>, *cure moderate wounds* (x2), *death knell* (DC 21), *sound burst* (DC 19)

 **1** — *[command](../../magic/spells/command.md)* (DC 18), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)* (x3), *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[protection from good](../../magic/spells/protection-from-good.md)* <sup>D</sup>, *[sanctuary](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md)* (DC 18)

 **0** — (at will) *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[guidance](../../magic/spells/guidance.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[read magic](../../magic/spells/read-magic.md)*  

**Tactics**

  Both clerics cast *magic vestment* every day. This spell is figured into their statistics.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 16, **Dex** 18, **Con** 16, **Int** 13, **Wis** 24, **Cha** 18  
 **Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +19; **CMD** 33 (37 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Channel Smite, Combat Casting, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Greater Spell Focus (necromancy), Lightning Reflexes, Spell Focus (necromancy), Spring Attack, Wind Stance, [Weapon Focus (flail)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; in their place: Weapon Focus (flail).*  
 **Skills** Bluff +25, Knowledge (Religion) +21, Perception +19, Spellcraft +21, Stealth +16; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Bluff, +4 Stealth  
 **Languages** Common, Draconic, Giant, +1 additional  
 **SQ** Might of the gods (+8 Str. on checks, 8 rds/day), Spontaneous Casting (inflict spells), Undersized Weapons  
 **Gear** +2 flail, headband of wisdom +2, ring of protection +2  

**Special Abilities**

 **Undersized Weapons (Ex)**  Although a lamia is Large, its upper torso is the same size as that of a Medium humanoid. As a result, lamias wield weapons as if they were one size category smaller than their actual size (Medium for most lamias).  

 **Wisdom Drain (Su)**  A lamia drains 1d4 points of Wisdom each time it hits with its melee touch attack. (Unlike with other kinds of ability drain attacks, a lamia does not heal any damage when it uses its Wisdom drain.) Lamias try to use this power early in an encounter to make foes more susceptible to *charm monster* and *suggestion*.
