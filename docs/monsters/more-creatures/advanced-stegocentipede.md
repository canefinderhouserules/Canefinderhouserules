## Advanced Stegocentipede (CR 8)

**XP** 4,800  
 Advanced stegocentipede  
 N Gargantuan vermin  

**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft. Perception +4  
 
  

**Defense**

 **AC** 20, touch 9, flat-footed 17 (+3 Dex, +11 natural, -4 size)  

**hp** 156 (13d8+52)  

**Fort** +12, **Ref** +7, **Will** +4  

**Defensive Abilities** spines; **Immune** mind-affecting effects  
 
  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft.  

**Melee** bite +13 (3d6+8 plus poison) and tail sting +13 (3d6+8 plus poison)  

**Space** 20 ft.; **Reach** 20 ft.  
 
    
      Tactics 
     **During Combat** Although mindless, it does recognize gnolls as allies and won’t attack them-characters disguised as gnolls can move among the immense many-legged vermin safely as long as they don’t attack it. All other creatures are food, and it attacks the closest such target each round.  
 **Morale** It fights to the death.  
 
  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 27, **Dex** 16, **Con** 18, Int—, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 2  

**Base Attack** +9; **CMB** +21; **CMD** 34  

**Skills** Climb +12, Perception +4, Stealth -1; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Climb, +4 Perception, +8 Stealth  
 
  

**Special Abilities**

 **Poison (Ex)** Injury (bite or tail); *save* Fort DC 22; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds, *effect* 1d3 Dex, *cure* 1 save. The save DC is Constitution-based and includes a +2 racial bonus.  
 **Spines (Ex)** A stegocentipede raises its dorsal spine plates during combat and moves rapidly back and forth. Creatures adjacent to a stegocentipede must succeed on a DC 20 Reflex save each round or take 3d8+4 points of damage from the spine-plates each time they attack whether the attack is successful or not. The save DC is Constitution-based.  
   

**Ecology**

 
Environment—  

**Organization** solitary  

**Treasure** none
