## Swordlord (CR 5)

**XP** 1,600  
 Human Fighter (Swordlord archetype) 5/Swordlord Prestige Class 1  
 LN Medium humanoid (human)  
 **Init** +5; **Senses** Perception +0  

**Defense**

 **AC** 19, touch 13, flat-footed 16 (+4 armor, +3 Dex, +2 shield)  
 **hp** 77 (6d10+17)  
 **Fort** +7, **Ref** +6, **Will** +2 (+1 vs. fear)  
 **Defensive Abilities** bravery +1, defensive parry  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 dueling sword +11/+6 (1d8+6/19–20)  
 **Ranged** dagger +9 (1d4+1/19–20)  
 **Special Attacks** disarming strike  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 12, **Dex** 17, **Con** 14, **Int** 14, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 8  
 **Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +7 (+9 disarm); **CMD** 20 (22 vs. disarm)  
 **Feats** Dueling Mastery, Combat Expertise, Dazzling Display, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (dueling sword), Improved Disarm, Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (dueling sword), Weapon Specialization (dueling sword)  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +11, Bluff +7, Diplomacy +7, Intimidate +7, Knowledge (nobility) +10, Sense Motive +8  
 **Languages** Common, Orc, +1 additional  
 **SQ** deft strike  
 **Combat Gear** potions of cure light wounds (2); **Other Gear** +1 studded leather, +1 dueling sword, dagger, cloak of resistance +1, cold weather outfit, 45 gp  

**Special Abilities**

 **Dueling Mastery** The swordlord gains a +2 bonus on Initiative checks whenever she starts combat with a dueling sword in her hand. As long as she wields only a single dueling sword in one hand, she gains a +2 shield bonus to AC; if she wields the sword in two hands, this bonus decreases to +1.  

 **Defensive Parry (Ex)** When the swordlord makes a full attack with a dueling sword, she gains a +1 bonus to AC against melee attacks until the beginning of her next turn.  

 **Deft Strike (Ex)** The swordlord can add her Dexterity bonus on damage rolls made with a dueling sword instead of using her Strength bonus. This bonus on damage applies whether she is wielding a dueling sword one-handed or two-handed, though she does not apply 1-1/2 times her Dexterity bonus on damage rolls while fighting two-handed. She cannot use this ability if she is wielding a shield or an off-hand weapon, including armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons.  

 **Disarming Strike (Ex)** When the swordlord successfully disarms an opponent using a dueling sword, she also deals normal damage to the target, but without the normal Strength (or Dexterity) bonus to damage.
