The weak, mewling creatures of the world crumble before the rage worgs, elite hunters and scouts of monstrous armies. More terrifying and bestial than their common ilk and yet altogether more intelligent and plotting, Rage worgs stalk the borders and man the front lines of horrible armies to sate their addiction to sadism and death. Barely controlled at the best of times, they run like mad dogs through the countryside, destroying villages solely to vent their rage.
