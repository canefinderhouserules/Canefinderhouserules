## Warden of Wind (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
LE Huge humanoid (giant)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** low-light vision, scent; Perception +24  


**Defense**


**AC** 38, touch 13, flat-footed 36 (+1 dodge, +11 armor, +3 deflection, +1 Dex, +14 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 200 (16d8+128)  
**Fort** +21, **Ref** +11, **Will** +13  
**Defensive Abilities**  rock catching  


**Offense**


**Speed** 35 ft.  
**Melee** morningstar +25/+20/+15 (4d6+21) or 2 slams +24 (2d6+14)  
**Ranged** rock +14 (2d6+21)  
**Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** rock throwing (140 ft.)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 16th, concentration + 19)  

At will—levitate (self plus 2,000 lbs.), obscuring mist  
1/day—fog cloud  


**Statistics**


**Str** 39, **Dex** 17, **Con** 27, **Int** 16, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +28 (+30 on bull rush or overrun); **CMD** 41 (43 vs. bull rush or overrun)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, Cleave, Great Cleave, [Powerful Maneuvers](../../rules/feats/powerful-maneuvers.md), Intimidating Prowess, Iron Will, [Weapon Focus (morningstar)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Improved Bull Rush, Improved Overrun are covered by Powerful Maneuvers; Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (morningstar), Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Climb +28, Craft (any one) +12, Diplomacy +19, Intimidate +35, Perception +24, Perform (string instruments) +13  
**Languages** Common, Giant  
**SQ** oversized weapon  
**Gear** *+2 full plate*, star ring  


**Special Abilities**


**Oversized Weapon (Ex)** A warden of wind can wield Gargantuan weapons without penalty. Most favor the use of immense morningstars.
