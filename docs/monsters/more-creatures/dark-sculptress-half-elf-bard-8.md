## Dark Sculptress (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
 Female half-elf bard 8  
 CE Medium humanoid (elf, human)  
 **Init** +6; **Senses** low-light vision; Perception +12  

**Defense**

 **AC** 16, touch 12, flat-footed 14 (+4 armor, +2 Dex)  
 **hp** 80 (8d8+16)  
 **Fort** +3, **Ref** +8, **Will** +5; +2 vs. enchantment spells and effects; +4 vs. bardic performance, sonic, and language-dependent effects  
 **Immune** sleep  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** *+1 rapier* +9/+4 (1d6+1/18-20)  
 **Ranged** shortbow +8/+3 (1d6/x3)  
 **Special Attacks** bardic music 23 rounds/day (move action – countersong, dirge of doom, distraction, fascinate [DC 19], inspire competence +3, inspire courage +2, suggestion [DC 19])  

 **Bard Spells Known** (CL 8th; concentration +13 [+17 defensive])  

  **3rd** (3/day)—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 19), *[crushing despair](../../magic/spells/crushing-despair.md)* (DC 19), *[gaseous form](../../magic/spells/gaseous-form.md)*  
 2nd (5/day)—*[blindness/deafness](../../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md)* (DC 17), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*, *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 18), *[tongues](../../magic/spells/tongues.md)*  
 1st (6/day)—*[charm person](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md)* (DC 17), *[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[grease](../../magic/spells/grease.md)* (DC 16), *[hideous laughter](../../magic/spells/hideous-laughter.md)* (DC 17), *[undetectable alignment](../../magic/spells/undetectable-alignment.md)*  
 0 (at will)—*[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[light](../../magic/spells/light.md)*, *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[message](../../magic/spells/message.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*  

 **Tactics**  

 **Before Combat** She casts *undetectable alignment* on herself every morning. She also has a pair of cockatrices that she keeps loyal with weekly castings of *charm monster*.  

 **During Combat** She orders her cockatrices to attack, bolstering them with inspire courage and then joining them by casting spells or using her rapier. She’s fond of using *suggestion* to force foes to adopt dramatic poses and remain motionless so that when they’re petrified, the resulting statues are worth more.  

 **Morale** If brought below 10 hit points, she casts *gaseous form* and attempts to escape and alert allies to the situation.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 10, **Dex** 14, **Con** 12, **Int** 14, **Wis** 8, **Cha** 20  
 **Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 18  
 **Feats** Combat Casting, Improved Initiative, Skill Focus – Profession (merchant), Spell Focus (enchantment), Weapon Finesse  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +16*, Appraise +13, Bluff +16*, Diplomacy +16, Fly +16*, Knowledge (history, local) +17, Perception +12, Perform (dance) +16, Perform (sing) +16, Profession (merchant) +13, Sense Motive +16*; **Racial Modifiers** +2 Perception  
 **Languages** Common, Elven, two others  
 **SQ** lore master 1/day, bardic knowledge +4, *versatile performance (dance, sing)  
 **Combat Gear** *wand of cure moderate wounds* (45 charges); **Other Gear** chain shirt, *+1 rapier*, shortbow with 20 arrows, *Headband of Alluring Charisma +2*
