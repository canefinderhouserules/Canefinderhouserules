## Holy Knight (CR 7)

**XP** 3,200  
 Human Paladin 5/Celestial Knight 3  
 LG Medium humanoid (human)  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** Perception +6; **Aura** courage (10 ft.)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 24, touch 10, flat-footed 24 (+10 armor, +4 shield)  
 **hp** 101 (8d10+21)  
 **Fort** +12, **Ref** +6, **Will** +10  
 **Immune** disease  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 longsword +13/+8 (1d8+4/19–20)  
 **Ranged** javelin +8 (1d6+3)  
 **Special Attacks** channel positive energy (DC 15, 3d6), favored enemy (undead +2), smite evil 2/day (+3 attack and AC, +5 damage)  
 **Paladin Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 5th; concentration +8)  

  
  At will—detect evil  

  
 **Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 2nd; concentration +5)  

  
  1st—bless weapon, detect undead  

  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 16, **Dex** 10, **Con** 14, **Int** 8, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 16  
 **Base Atk** +8; **CMB** +11; **CMD** 21  
 **Feats** Extra Mercy, Improved Shield Bash, Shield Focus, Shield Wall, Vital Strike, Weapon Focus (longsword), [Improved Initiative](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Improved Initiative.*  
 **Skills** Diplomacy +14, Heal +5, Knowledge (religion) +7, Perception +6, Sense Motive +7  
 **Languages** Common  
 **SQ** aura, code of conduct, divine bond (weapon +1, 1/day), lay on hands (2d6, 5/day), mercy (shaken, sickened), sentinel against undeath +1  
 **Combat Gear** potion of eagle’s splendor, potion of lesser restoration, holy water; **Other Gear** +1 full plate, +1 heavy steel shield, +1 longsword, javelins (4), cloak of resistance +1, silver holy symbol, sunrods (2), 7 gp  

**Special Abilities**

 **Favored Enemy (Ex)** A Celestial Knight gains a favored enemy bonus of +2 against undead. This ability functions as the ranger class ability.  

 **Sentinel Against Undeath (Ex)** A Celestial Knight gains a bonus equal to 1/3 his Celestial Knight level on Perception and Sense Motive checks against undead. He also gains this bonus as a dodge bonus to his AC and CMD against attacks made by undead.
