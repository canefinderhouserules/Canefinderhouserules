## Human Rogue [swashbuckler] 4 (CR 4)

**XP** 1,200  
 Male human rogue (swashbuckler) 4  
 NG Medium humanoid (human)  
 **Init** +6; **Senses** Perception +7  

**Defense**

 **AC** 20, touch 13, flat-footed 17 (+1 dodge, +5 armor, +2 Dex, +2 shield)  
 **hp** 40 (4d8+8)  
 **Fort** +2, **Ref** +6, **Will** +3; +1 vs. fear  
 **Defensive Abilities** evasion, uncanny dodge  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** *+1 scimitar* +8 (1d6+4/18–20)  
 **Ranged** mwk shortbow +6 (1d6/×3)  
 **Special Attacks** sneak attack +2d6  

**Tactics**

 **During Combat** He stays mobile in combat, using Acrobatics to avoid attacks of opportunity as he flanks foes and to prevent enemies from taking full-attack actions on him. He uses Power Attack in all fights, but does so with a relatively unique style that makes him seem more graceful than brutal in nature.  

 **Morale** He is stubbornly brave and has a hard time envisioning his own demise. As a result of this attitude, he fights to the death.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 16, **Dex** 14, **Con** 12, **Int** 10, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 13  
 **Base Atk** +3; **CMB** +6; **CMD** 19  
 **Feats** Improved Initiative, Iron Will, [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Weapon Focus (scimitar), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Appraise +7, Bluff +8, Craft (carpentry) +7, Diplomacy +8, Disable Device +8, Handle Animal +5, Knowledge (history) +4, Perception +7, Profession (merchant) +7  
 **Languages** Common, +1 additional  
 **SQ** daring, martial training, PC gear, rogue talents (combat trick, weapon training)  
 **Combat Gear** *potions of cure light wounds* (3), *potion of cure moderate wounds*; **Other Gear** *+1 chain shirt*, *+1 buckler*, *+1 scimitar*, mwk shortbow with 20 arrows, belt pouch, bottle of fine brandy worth 50 gp (4), everburning torch, masterwork thieves’ tools, trail rations (4 days), wineskin, 76 gp
