## Cardinal Martyr (CR 28)

**XP** 4,915,200  
 LG Large outsider (angel, extraplanar, good, lawful)  
 **Init** +13; **Senses** blindsense 120 ft., darkvision 60 ft., detect evil, true seeing, zealous vision; Perception +42; **Aura** primal (30 ft.), protective  

**Defense**

 **AC** 44, touch 19, flat-footed 34 (+9 Dex, +1 dodge, +25 natural, –1 size; +4 deflection vs. evil)  
 **hp** 792 (33d10+462); regeneration 10 (evil artifacts, effects, and spells)  
 **Fort** +31, **Ref** +22, **Will** +26; +4 vs. poison, +4 resistance vs. evil  
 **DR** 15/epic and evil; **Immune** ability damage, ability drain, acid, bleed, charm effects, compulsion effects, cold, death effects, energy drain, petrification; **Resist** electricity 30, fire 30  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (average)  
 **Melee** Cicatrix +50/+45/+40/+35 (1d6+17/17–20 plus 1 bleed and 2d6 vicious)  
 **Ranged** Cicatrix +47 (1d6+17/17–20 plus 1 bleed)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** smite evil 7/day (+5 attack and AC, +20 damage, disintegrate evil outsiders)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 20th; concentration +25)  

  Constant—detect evil, true seeing  
 At will—command* (DC 16), greater teleport, haste*, paladin’s sacrifice, pain strike (DC 18)  
 3/day—discern location, *[geas/quest](../../magic/spells/geas-quest.md)* (DC 21), heal, holy word* (DC 22), mark of justice (DC 20), mass pain strike (DC 20), searing light*  

 * Cardinal Martyr can use the Mythic version of this ability in her realm.  

 **Paladin Spells Prepared** (CL 20th; concentration +25)  

  4th—blaze of glory (DC 19), break enchantment, death ward, king’s castle  
 3rd—dispel magic, *[fire of judgment](../../magic/spells/fire-of-judgment.md)* (DC 18), prayer, righteous vigor  
 2nd—bull’s strength, corruption resistance, litany of warding, remove paralysis, shield other  
 1st—divine favor (3), hero’s defiance, lesser restoration (2)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 35, **Dex** 29, **Con** 37, **Int** 18, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 21  
 **Base Atk** +33; **CMB** +46; **CMD** 66  
 **Feats** Blinding Critical, Combat Casting, Combat Reflexes, Critical Focus, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Flyby Attack, Improved Critical (dagger), Improved Initiative, Improved Iron Will, Improved Vital Strike, Iron Will, Spell Penetration, Step Up, Vital Strike, Weapon Focus (dagger), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed), [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge is part of Dodgility; Deadly Aim, Power Attack need no feat; in their place: Toughness, Lightning Reflexes.*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +45, Bluff +41, Fly +26, Heal +23, Intimidate +41, Knowledge (planes) +40, Knowledge (religion) +40, Perception +42, Sense Motive +42, Sleight of Hand +45, Stealth +41  
 **Languages** Celestial, Draconic; truespeech  
 **SQ** lay on hands (16d6, 25/day), seed of life  

**Special Abilities**

 **Primal Aura (Su)** Whenever Cardinal Martyr would be affected by a bleed effect, all enemies within 30 feet gain that bleeding condition instead, as though they were the effect’s original targets (no saving throw, creatures immune to bleeding are immune to this effect). The Heal DC to stop this bleeding is 25.  

 **Smite Evil (Su)** Cardinal Martyr can smite evil as a 20th-level paladin. Whenever she uses smite evil and successfully strikes an evil outsider, the outsider is also subject to disintegrate, using Cardinal Martyr’s paladin level as the caster level. After the disintegrate effect and the damage from the attack are resolved, the smite effect immediately ends.  

 **Spells** Cardinal Martyr casts spells as a 20th-level paladin.  

 **Zealous Vision (Su)** Cardinal Martyr automatically pinpoints the location of any evil creature within 1,000 feet of her.  

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Heaven)  
 **Organization** unique  
 **Treasure** standard (Cicatrix, other treasure)
