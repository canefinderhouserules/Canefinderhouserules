## Dog Warrior (CR 3)

**XP** 800  
 Halfling Barbarian 4  
 N Small humanoid (halfling)  
 **Init** +3; **Senses** Perception +10  

**Defense**

 **AC** 19, touch 13, flat-footed 15 (+1 dodge, +5 armor, +3 Dex, –2 rage, +1 shield, +1 size)  
 **hp** 60 (4d12+12)  
 **Fort** +8, **Ref** +5, **Will** +5; +3 vs. spells and spell-like or supernatural abilities, +2 vs. fear  
 **Defensive Abilities** trap sense +1, uncanny dodge  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** lance +9 (1d6+4/×3)  
 **Ranged** +1 halfling sling staff +10 (1d6+5/×3)  
 **Space** 5 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with lance)  
 **Special Attacks** rage (11 rounds/day), rage powers (superstition +3, surprise accuracy +2)  

 **TACTICS** **Before Combat** The barbarian applies oil of magic weapon.  

 **During Combat** The barbarian keeps his distance, pelting enemies with bullets. If ranged attacks fail, he charges with his lance.  

 Base Statistics  

 When not raging and without magic weapon, the barbarian’s statistics are **AC** 20, touch 14, flat-footed 17; **hp** 35; **Fort** +6, **Will**+3; **Melee** lance +7 (1d6+2/×3); **Ranged** mwk halfling sling staff +10 (1d6+2/×3); **Str** 14, **Con** 13; **CMB** +5; **Skills** Climb +9, Swim +5.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 18, **Dex** 16, **Con** 17, **Int** 10, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 10  
 **Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 19  
 **Feats** Weapon Focus (halfling sling staff), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +11, Handle Animal +6, Perception +10, Ride +9, Stealth +10, Survival +5  
 **SQ** fast movement  
 **Combat Gear** oils of magic weapon (2), potions of cure light wounds (2); **Other Gear** +1 chain shirt, masterwork buckler, lance, masterwork halfling sling staff with 20 bullets, riding dog with riding saddle and leather barding, 55 gp
