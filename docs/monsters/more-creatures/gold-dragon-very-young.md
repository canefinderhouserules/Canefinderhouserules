## Very Young Gold Dragon (CR 9)

**XP** 6,400  
LG Medium dragon (fire)  
**Init** +1; **Senses** dragon senses; Perception +18  


**Defense**


**AC** 21, touch 11, flat-footed 20 (+1 Dex, +10 natural)  
**hp** 95 (10d12+30)  
**Fort** +10, **Ref** +8, **Will** +12  
**Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep  
**Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold  


**Offense**


**Speed** 60 ft., fly 150 ft. (average), swim 60 ft.  
**Melee** bite +16 (1d8+7), 2 claws +15 (1d6+5/19-20), 2 wings +10 (1d4+2)  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (30-ft. cone, 4d10 fire, DC 18), weakening breath  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 10th; concentration +13)  

At will—detect evil  


**Statistics**


**Str** 21, **Dex** 12, **Con** 17, **Int** 16, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 16  
**Base Atk** +10; **CMB** +15; **CMD** 26 (30 vs. trip)  
**Feats** Alertness, Improved Critical (claw), Iron Will, Vital Strike, [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite).*  
**Skills** Diplomacy +16, Fly +14, Heal +16, Knowledge (local) +16, Knowledge (religion) +16, Perception +20, Sense Motive +20, Spellcraft +16, Swim +26; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Swim  
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Elven, Halfling  
**SQ** change shape 3/day (any animal or humanoid; polymorph)  


**Special Abilities**


**Weakening Breath (Su)** Instead of a cone of fire, a gold dragon can breathe a cone of weakening gas. Creatures within the cone must succeed on a Fortitude save or take 1 point of Strength damage per age category (Will save half).  



**Ecology**


**Environment** warm plains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** triple
