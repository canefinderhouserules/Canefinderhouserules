## Elite Bronze Giant (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
 Male elite bronze giant  
 N Huge humanoid (giant)  
 **Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +23  

**Defense**

 **AC** 24, touch 10, flat-footed 22 (+2 Dex, +14 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 240 (16d8+112)  
 **Fort** +17, **Ref** +9, **Will** +11  
 **Defensive Abilities** rock catching; **DR** 10/—; **Resist** fire 20  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 50 ft.  
 **Melee** +1 keen longsword +25/+20/+15 (3d6+20/17-20) or 2 slams +23 (1d8+13)  
 **Melee** (*Power Attack*) +1 keen longsword +20/+15/+10 (3d6+32/17-20) or 2 slams +19 (1d8+21)  
 **Ranged** bronze throwing sphere +13 (2d8+19)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 15 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** rock throwing (140 ft.), sardonic laugh  

**Tactics**

 **During Combat** He uses his sardonic laugh and hurls bronze shot before charging in for melee with his longsword, attacking with a Power Attack.  

 **Morale** He is disconsolate at his current circumstances and gladly fights to the death.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 36, **Dex** 15, **Con** 25, **Int** 10, **Wis** 18, **Cha** 12  
 **Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +27 (+29 bull rush); **CMD** 39 (41 vs. bull rush)  
 **Feats** Awesome Blow, Cleave, Improved Bull Rush, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Lightning Reflexes, Martial Weapon Proficiency (longsword), [Weapon Focus (longsword)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (longsword).*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +19 (+32 jump), Perception +23  
 **Languages** Giant, Ignan  
 **Gear** +1 keen longsword, bronze throwing spheres (14), boots of striding and springing  

**Special Abilities**

 **Sardonic Laugh (Ex)** Once per round as a standard action, a bronze giant can unleash a bellowing laugh that strikes fear into the hearts of any creature within 100 feet that hears it. Affected creatures that succeed on a DC 19 Will save are unaffected and immune to the sardonic laugh of that bronze giant for 24 hours. On a failed save, that creature is shaken for as long as it remains within 100 feet of the bronze giant and for 2d4 rounds thereafter. The save DC is Charisma-based.
