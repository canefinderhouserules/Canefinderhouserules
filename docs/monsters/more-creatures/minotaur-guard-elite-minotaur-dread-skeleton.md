## Minotaur Guard (CR 6)

**XP** 2,400  
 Dread skeleton elite minotaur  
 CE Large undead (augmented monstrous humanoid)  
 **Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., scent; Perception +11; **Aura** unnatural aura (30 ft)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 19, touch 11, flat-footed 17 (+2 Dex, +8 natural, -1 size)  
 **hp** 66 (6d10+6)  
 **Fort** +5, **Ref** +7, **Will** +6  
 **Defensive Abilities** +2 channel resistance, natural cunning; **DR** 10/bludgeoning; **Immune** cold, undead traits  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** mwk greataxe +13/+8 (3d6+9/x3)  
 **Ranged** mwk light crossbow +8/+3 (2d6/19-20×2)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** command skeletons, powerful charge (gore +11, 2d6+6)  

**Tactics**

 **During Combat** Minotaur guards open combat with a charge, then continue to fight with their melee weaponry. They only resort to crossbows if they simply can’t reach a victim.  

 **Morale** Minotaur guards fight until destroyed.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 23, **Dex** 14, Con—, **Int** 3, **Wis** 12, **Cha** 10  
 **Base Atk** +6; **CMB** +13; **CMD** 25  
 **Feats** Great Fortitude, Improved Bull Rush, Improved Initiative, [Weapon Focus (greataxe)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (greataxe).*  
 **Skills** Intimidate +7, Perception +11  
 **Languages** Giant  
 **SQ** natural cunning  
 **Gear** masterwork greataxe, masterwork light crossbow  

**Special Abilities**

 **Command Skeletons (Su)** A dread skeleton can command all normal skeletons within 30 feet as a free action. Normal skeletons never attack a dread skeleton unless compelled.  

 **Natural Cunning (Ex)** Although minotaurs are not especially intelligent, they possess innate cunning and logical ability. This gives them immunity to *maze* spells (which they are already immune to as undead creatures) but also prevents them from ever becoming lost or flat-footed.  

 **Unnatural Aura (Su)** Any animal within 30 feet of a dread skeleton becomes panicked and remains so until the distance between it and the dread skeleton is at least 30 feet. An animal can attempt a DC 12 Will save to resist panic for one round. The save DC is Charisma-based.  

**Ecology**

 **Environment** any  
 **Organization** solitary or gang (1 dread skeleton plus 1d10 skeletons).  
 **Treasure** standard
