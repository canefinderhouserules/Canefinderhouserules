## Unique Dragon Turtle (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
 Unique dragon turtle  
 NE Huge dragon (aquatic)  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +23  

**Defense**

 **AC** 23, touch 8, flat-footed 23 (+15 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 218 (19d12+95)  
 **Fort** +15, **Ref** +11, **Will** +14  
 **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft., swim 20 ft.  
 **Melee** bite +26 (3d6+9 plus snatch), 2 claws +26 (2d6+9 plus snatch)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon, capsize  

**Tactics**

 **During Combat** Kirhosk is confident in his battle abilities, but paranoid about magic. He ignores melee combatants in favor of attacking spellcasters, and uses his breath weapon whenever available, provided he can hit at least two targets. Otherwise he will only use it if the single target is gravely injured and likely to fall, or if he is gravely injured.  

 **Morale** Kirhosk is at the top of the food chain and knows it. He is loath to give up that place, even when attacked by surface dwellers, especially in his lair. This makes him less cautious and more likely to fight until severely injured. If reduced to less than a quarter of his total hit points, though, Kirhosk will use his breath weapon, trying to clear a path to the exit, and then flee.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 28, **Dex** 10, **Con** 19, **Int** 12, **Wis** 13, **Cha** 12  
 **Base Atk** +19; **CMB** +30 (+32 bull rush); **CMD** 40 (42 vs. bull rush)  
 **Feats** Awesome Blow, Blind-Fight, Cleave, Improved Bull Rush, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Power Attack, Snatch, Toughness, Vital Strike  
 **Skills** Diplomacy +23, Intimidate +23, Perception +23, Sense Motive +23, Stealth +14, Survival +23, Swim +39  
 **Languages** Aquan, Common, Draconic  

**Special Abilities**

 **Breath Weapon (Su)** Cloud of steam 20 ft. high, 25 feet wide, and 50 feet long, once every 1d4 rounds, damage 12d6 fire, Reflex DC 20 half; effective both on the surface and underwater. The save DC is Constitution-based.  

 **Capsize (Ex)** A dragon turtle can attempt to capsize a boat or ship by ramming it as a charge attack and making a CMB check. The DC of this check is 25, or the result of the boat captain’s Profession (sailor) check, whichever is higher. For each size category larger than the dragon turtle’s size, it takes a cumulative -10 penalty on this CMB check.  

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate aquatic  
 **Organization** solitary  
 **Treasure** double
