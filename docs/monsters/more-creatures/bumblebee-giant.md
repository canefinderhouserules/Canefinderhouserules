## Giant Bumblebee (CR 2)

**XP** 600  
 N Medium vermin  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +3  

**Defense**

 **AC** 17, touch 14, flat-footed 13 (+4 Dex, +3 natural)  
 **hp** 33 (3d8+9)  
 **Fort** +6, **Ref** +5, **Will** +4  
 **Immune** mind-affecting effects  
 **Weaknesses** vulnerable to smoke  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 20 ft., fly 60 ft. (good)  
 **Melee** sting +4 (1d4+3 plus poison)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 15, **Dex** 18, **Con** 17, Int—, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 13  
 **Base Atk** +2; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 18 (26 vs. trip)  
 **Skills** Fly +8  

**Special Abilities**

 **Poison (Ex)** Sting—injury; *save* Fort DC 14; *frequency* 1/round for 4 rounds; *effect* 1d2 Con; *cure* 1 save.  

 **Vulnerable to Smoke (Ex)** Smoke from particularly smoky fires or effects (such as that created by a pyrotechnics spell) causes a giant bee to become nauseated 1 if it fails a DC 14 Fortitude save. This condition persists as long as the giant bumblebee remains in the smoke, plus 1d4 rounds.  

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm plains  
 **Organization** solitary, group (2–5), or nest (6–19)  
 **Treasure** incidental
