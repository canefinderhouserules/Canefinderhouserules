## Shrine-Blessed Burning Skeletons (CR 1)

**XP** 400  
 NE Medium shrine-blessed undead  
 **Init** +6; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +0; **Aura** fiery aura (1d6)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 16, touch 12, flat-footed 14 (+2 armor, +2 Dex, +2 natural)(+2 deflection vs good creatures)  
 **hp** 9 (1d8+1)  
 **Fort** +3, **Ref** +4, **Will** +4; +2 vs good creatures  
 **Defensive Abilities** channel resistance +6; **DR** 5/bludgeoning; **Immune** fire, undead traits  
 **Vulnerability** cold  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft.  
 **Melee** broken scimitar +2 (1d6+2 plus 1d6 fire), claw –1 (1d4+3 plus 1d6 fire) or 2 claws +4 (1d4+4 plus 1d6 fire)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 15, **Dex** 14, Con—, Int—, **Wis** 10, **Cha** 12  
 **Base Atk** +0; **CMB** +4; **CMD** 14  
 **Feats** Improved Initiative  
 **Gear** broken chain shirt, broken scimitar  
 **SQ** fiery death (DC 11)  

**Special Abilities**

 **Fiery Aura (Ex)** Creatures adjacent to a burning skeleton take 1d6 points of fire damage at the start of their turn. Anyone striking a burning skeleton with an unarmed strike or natural attack takes 1d6 points of fire damage.  

 **Fiery Death (Su)** A burning skeleton explodes into a burst of flame when it dies. Anyone adjacent to the skeleton when it is destroyed takes 1d6 points of fire damage. A Reflex save (DC 11) halves this damage.
