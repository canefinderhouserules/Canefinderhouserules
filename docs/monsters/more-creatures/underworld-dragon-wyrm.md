## wyrm Underworld Dragon (CR 17)

**XP** 102,400  
 LE Huge dragon (fire)  
 **Init** +2; **Senses** dragon senses, smoke vision; Perception +32; **Aura** frightful presence (330 ft., DC 27)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 41, touch 6, flat-footed 41 (-2 Dex, +35 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 348 (24d12+192)  
 **Fort** +21, **Ref** +12, **Will** +19  
 **DR** 20/magic; **Immune** fire, paralysis, sleep; **SR** 28  
 **Weaknesses** vulnerability to cold  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., burrow 60 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
 **Melee** bite +36 (2d8+19/19-20), 2 claws +37 (2d6+13/19-20), gore +35 (2d6+19), tail slap +30 (2d6+19)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite and gore)  
 **Special Attacks** adamantine claws, breath weapon (100-ft. line, 22d6 fire damage, DC 29), crush (DC 29, 2d8+19)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 24th; concentration +29)  

  At will—repel metal or stone, soften earth and stone, spike stones (DC 19), stone shape, wall of stone  

 **Spells Known** (CL 15th; concentration +20)  

  7th (4/day)—*[delayed blast fireball](../../magic/spells/delayed-blast-fireball.md)* (DC 22), *[finger of death](../../magic/spells/finger-of-death.md)* (DC 22)  
 6th (6/day)—disintegrate (DC 21), flesh to stone (DC 21), *[wall of iron](../../magic/spells/wall-of-iron.md)*  
 5th (7/day)—*[acidic spray](../../magic/spells/acidic-spray.md)* (DC 20), passwall, transmute rock to mud, waves of fatigue  
 4th (7/day)—enervation, shout (DC 19), solid fog, stone shape  
 3rd (7/day)—displacement, fireball (DC 18), protection from good, slow  
 2nd (7/day)—acid arrow, daze monster (DC 17), flaming sphere (DC 17), *[resist energy](../../magic/spells/resist-energy.md)*, scorching ray  
 1st (8/day)—burning hands (DC 16), cause fear (DC 16), magic missile, ray of enfeeblement (DC 16), true strike  
 0 (at will)—acid splash, bleed (DC 15), detect magic, flare (DC 15), ghost sound, mage hand, read magic, resistance, touch of fatigue (DC 15)  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 37, **Dex** 6, **Con** 25, **Int** 20, **Wis** 21, **Cha** 20  
 **Base Atk** +24; **CMB** +39 (+41 bull rush); **CMD** 47 (49 vs bull rush, 51 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Critical Focus, Greater Weapon Focus (claw), Improved Bull Rush, Improved Critical (bite, claw), Improved Initiative, Improved Natural Armor, Lunge, Skill Focus (Stealth), Staggering Critical, Weapon Focus (bite, claw), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Toughness.*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +24 (+28 when jumping), Appraise +35, Bluff +35, Climb +43, Fly +13, Intimidate +35, Knowledge (dungeoneering, geography, planes) +35, Perception +35, Spellcraft + 35, Stealth +21  
 **Languages** Common, Draconic, Dwarven, Giant, Ignan, Terran  
 **SQ** change shape, underworld burrower  

**Special Abilities**

 **Adamantine Claws (Ex)** The claws of an underworld dragon are made of adamantine, and have the qualities of a weapon made from that material.  

 **Change Shape (Su)** A young or older underworld dragon can assume any humanoid form three times per day as if using polymorph.  

 **Smoke Vision (Ex)** A very young and older underworld dragon can see perfectly in smoky conditions (such as those created by pyrotechnics).  

 **Underworld Burrower (Ex)** An adult underworld dragon gains a 10-foot bonus to its burrow speed. When the underworld dragon becomes old and every two age categories thereafter, its burrow speed increases by an additional 10 feet.
