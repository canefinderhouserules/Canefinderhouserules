## Half-Fiend Medusa Cleric 11 (CR 17)

**XP** 102,400  
 Female half-fiend medusa cleric of Asmodeus 11  
 LE Medium monstrous humanoid  
 **Init** +10; **Senses** all-around vision, darkvision 60 ft.; Perception +28  

**Defense**

 **AC** 33 touch 22, flat-footed 25 (+7 armor, +4 deflection, +6 Dex, +2 dodge, +4 natural)  
 **hp** 256 (19 HD; 8d10+11d8+163)  
 **Fort** +18, **Ref** +17, **Will** +19  
 **DR** 10/magic; **Immune** poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10, electricity 10, fire 10; **SR** 28  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 30 ft., swim 30 ft.  
 **Melee** bite +23 (1d6+1), claws +22 (1d4+1), snake bite +23 (1d4+1 plus poison)  
 **Ranged** +3 axiomatic longbow +25/+20/+15/+10 (1d8+3/19–20/×3)  
 **Special Attacks** channel negative energy 8/day (DC 20, 6d6), petrifying gaze (DC 21*), smite good 1/day (as 19th level paladin, but vs. good targets)  
 **Half-Fiend Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 19th; concentration +24)  

  3/day—darkness, *[poison](../../magic/spells/poison.md)* (DC 19), unholy aura (DC 21)  
 1/day—desecrate, destruction (DC 22), displacement (DC 19), horrid wilting (DC 23), phantasmal killer (DC 19), scintillating pattern (DC 23), summon monster IX (fiends only), unhallow  

 **Domain Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 14th; concentration +20)  

  At will—master’s illusion (11 rounds/day)  
 9/day— copycat, touch of evil  
 1/day—scythe of evil  

 **Cleric Spells Prepared** (CL 14th; concentration +20)  

  6th—blade barrier (DC 22), heal, mislead D  
 5th—dispel good D, flame strike (2, DC 21), slay living (DC 21)  
 4th—air walk, cure serious wounds (2), spell immunity, unholy blight D (DC 20)  
 3rd—bestow curse (DC 19), create food and water, cure serious wounds, nondetection D, stone shape, water breathing  
 2nd—cure moderate wounds (5), darkness, invisibility D  
 1st—bane, cure light wounds (3), divine favor, protection from good D, sanctuary (DC 17)  
 0 (at will)—*[bleed](../../magic/spells/bleed.md)* (DC 16), guidance, read magic, *[resistance](../../magic/spells/resistance.md)*  

 **D** Domain spell; **Domains** Evil, Trickery  

**Tactics**

 **Before Combat** If she has time to prepare, she casts air walk, spell immunity (confusion and charm monster), and invisibility on herself.  

 **During Combat** She casts unholy aura on herself on the first round of combat, then uses summon monster IX to summon 1d3 barbed devils to attack the PCs. After this, she uses air walk or her swim speed to stay out of melee while she uses her spells and arrows.  

 **Morale** She fights to the death.  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 12, **Dex** 22, **Con** 24, **Int** 12, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 21  
 **Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +17; **CMD** 38 (can’t be tripped)  
 **Feats** Combat Casting, [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), Improved Critical (longbow), Improved Initiative, [Point-Blank Shot](../../rules/feats/point-blank-shot.md), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), [Weapon Finesse](../../rules/feats/weapon-finesse.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md); [Deadly Aim](../../rules/feats/deadly-aim.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Dodge and Mobility are one feat, Dodgility; Point-Blank Shot and Precise Shot are one feat, Point-Blank Shot; Deadly Aim needs no feat; in their place: Weapon Focus (bite), Lightning Reflexes, Great Fortitude.*  
 **Skills** Knowledge (engineering) +20, Knowledge (religion) +15, Perception +32, Profession (architect) +28, Spellcraft +17, Swim +20  
 **Languages** Common, Infernal  
 **Gear** +5 leather armor, +3 axiomatic longbow with 20 arrows, ring of protection +4  

**Special Abilities**

 **All-Around Vision (Ex)** A medusa’s snake-hair allows her to see in all directions. She gains a +4 racial bonus to Perception checks and she cannot be flanked.  

 **Petrifying Gaze (Su)** Turn to stone permanently, 30 feet, Fortitude DC 24 negates. The save DC is Charisma-based.  

 **Poison (Ex)** Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 25; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d3 Str; *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
