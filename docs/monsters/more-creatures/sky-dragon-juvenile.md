## Juvenile Sky Dragon (CR 10)

**XP** 9,600  
 LG Large dragon (air)  
 **Init** +5; **Senses** cloud sight, dragon senses; Perception +25; **Aura** frightful presence (120 ft., DC 20)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 25, touch 10, flat-footed 24 (Dex +1, natural +15, size -1)  
 **hp** 192 (12d12+48)  
 **Fort** +12, **Ref** +11, **Will** +12  
 **Immune** electricity, paralysis, sleep  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., fly 200 ft. (good)  
 **Melee** bite +17 (2d6+9), 2 claws +17 (1d8+6), gore +17 (1d8+9), tail slap +15 (1d8+9)  
 **Space** 10 ft.; **Reach** 5 ft. (10 ft. with bite and gore)  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon (40 ft. cone, 8d8 electricity, DC 20)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +16)  

  At will—detect evil, feather fall  

 **Spells Known** (CL 3th; concentration +7)  

  1st (6/day)—expeditious retreat, obscuring mist, shocking grasp  
 0 (at will)—detect magic, disrupt undead, mending, message, read magic  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 23, **Dex** 12, **Con** 19, **Int** 18, **Wis** 19, **Cha** 18  
 **Base Atk** +12; **CMB** +19; **CMD** 30 (34 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Flyby Attack, Hover, Improved Initiative, Lightning Reflexes, Multiattack, Skill Focus (Perception)  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +13, Diplomacy +19, Fly +18, Heal +19, Knowledge (arcana, religion) +19, Perception +25, Perform (sing) +16, Sense Motive +19, Spellcraft +19  
 **Languages** Auran, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal  
 **SQ** borne aloft, change shape  

**Special Abilities**

 **Borne Aloft (Su)** Old or younger sky dragons fly with good maneuverability while all older sky dragons have perfect.  

 **Change Shape (Su)** A young or older sky dragon can assume any humanoid form three times per day as if using polymorph.  

 **Cloud Sight (Su)** A very young or older sky dragon’s sight is not impeded by clouds or fog, or by spells that create areas of fog.  

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate or warm mountains  
 **Organization** solitary  
 **Treasure** triple
