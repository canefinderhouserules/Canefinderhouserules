## Young adult Forest Dragon (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
 CE Huge dragon (earth)  
 **Init** +4; **Senses** dragon senses, tremorsense 60 ft.; Perception +22; **Aura** frightful presence (150 ft., DC 19)  

**Defense**

 **AC** 27, touch 8, flat-footed 27 (+19 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 270 (15d12+90)  
 **Fort** +14, **Ref** +11, **Will** +13  
 **DR** 2/adamantine; **Immune** paralysis, poison, sleep; **SR** 24  

**Offense**

 **Speed** 40 ft., burrow 20 ft., climb 30 ft., fly 200 ft. (poor)  
 **Melee** bite +22 (2d8+12), 2 claws +21 (2d6+8), gore +21 (2d6+12), tail slap +19 (2d6+12)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft. (15 ft. with bite and gore)  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 10d6 piercing damage, DC 22) crush (DC 22, 2d8+12)  
 **Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 15th; concentration +17)  

  At will—entangle (DC 13), pass without trace  

 **Spells Known** (CL 5th; concentration +7)  

  2nd (5/day)—fog cloud, hideous laughter  
 1st (7/day)—obscuring mist, magic missile, ray of enfeeblement (DC 13), shield  
 0 (at-will)—detect magic, ghost sound, mage hand, read magic, resistance, touch of fatigue  

**Statistics**

 **Str** 27, **Dex** 10, **Con** 20, **Int** 14, **Wis** 15, **Cha** 14  
 **Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +25; **CMD** 35 (39 vs. trip)  
 **Feats** Improved Initiative, Improved Natural Armor, Iron Will, Multiattack, Skill Focus (Stealth), [Toughness](../../rules/feats/toughness.md), Weapon Focus (bite), [Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Lightning Reflexes.*  
 **Skills** Acrobatics +15 (+19 when jumping), Bluff +20, Climb +34, Fly -8, Intimidate +20, Knowledge (arcana) +13, Knowledge (nature) +13, Perception +20, Spellcraft +11, Stealth +16, Survival +13  
 **Languages** Common, Draconic, Elven, Sylvan  
 **SQ** sound imitation, woodland stride  

**Special Abilities**

 **Sound Imitation (Ex)** A very young or older forest dragon can mimic any voice or sound it has heard by making a successful Bluff check against the listener’s Sense Motive check.  

 **Woodland Stride (Ex)** As the druid ability of the same name.
