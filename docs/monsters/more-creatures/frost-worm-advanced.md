## Advanced Frost Worm (CR 13)

**XP** 25,600  
N Huge magical beast (cold)  
**Init** +9; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +19  


**Defense**


**AC** 32, touch 14, flat-footed 26 (+1 dodge, +5 Dex, +18 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 272 (16d10+112)  
**Fort** +17, **Ref** +15, **Will** +12  
**Immune** cold  
**Weaknesses** vulnerable to fire  


**Offense**


**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.  
**Melee** bite +27 (4d10+18 plus 4d6 cold)  
**Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
**Special Attacks** breath weapon (60-ft. cone, 15d6 cold damage, Reflex DC 25 half, usable once per hour), death throes, trill  


**Statistics**


**Str** 35, **Dex** 20, **Con** 25, **Int** 2, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +16; **CMB** +30; **CMD** 46 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** Cleave, Combat Reflexes, Improved Initiative, Iron Will, Skill Focus (Perception), Stand Still, Weapon Focus (bite), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md); [Power Attack](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md) (no feat needed)  
*House rules: Power Attack needs no feat; in their place: Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Perception +19, Stealth +8 (+16 in ice and snow); **Racial Modifiers** +8 Stealth in ice and snow  
**SQ** cold  


**Special Abilities**


**Cold (Su)** A frost worm’s body generates intense cold, allowing it to deal an additional 4d6 cold damage with its bite attack. Any creature that attacks a frost worm with an unarmed strike or a natural weapon takes 1d6 points of cold damage per successful hit. A creature that grapples or is grappled by a frost worm takes 4d6 points of cold damage per round the grapple is maintained.  

**Death Throes (Su)** When killed, a frost worm explodes in a 100-foot-radius burst that deals 12d6 cold damage and 8d6 piercing damage (DC 25 Reflex half). The save DC is Constitution-based.  

**Trill (Su)** As a full-round action, a frost worm can emit a strange trilling sound that affects all creatures within a 100-foot radius. Creatures must succeed on a DC 20 Will save or be fascinated for as long as the worm continues to trill (the frost worm can maintain this trill by concentrating). Once a creature has resisted or broken the effect, it cannot be affected again by that same frost worm’s trill for 24 hours. This is a sonic mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.  



**Ecology**


**Environment** cold plains or mountains  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** incidental
