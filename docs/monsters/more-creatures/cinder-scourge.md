## Argorth, Cinder scourge (CR 12)

**XP** 19,200  
 Always CE Huge aberration  
 **Init** +3; **Senses** blindsight 120 ft; Perception +18  
 


**Defense**

 

**AC** 26, touch 11, flat-footed 23 (+3 Dex, +15 natural, -2 size)  
 **hp** 150 (12d8+12 Toughness + 84)  
 **Fort** +15, **Ref** +7, **Will** +11  
 **DR** 10/cold iron; **Immune** cold, gaze attacks, mind-affecting effects, visual effects; **Resist** acid 10, fire 20; **SR** 21  
 


**Offense**

 

**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 20 ft., climb 20 ft., swim 40 ft.  
 **Melee** bite +19 (3d6+11+grab) and tail slam +18 (2d6+11+grab)  
 **Space** 15 ft.; **Reach** 10 ft.  
 **Special Attacks** breath weapon (60 ft. cone, 14d6 fire damage, Reflex DC 23 half, usable every 1d4 rounds), constrict (2d6+16), fearful shriek, grab, ground slam, swallow whole (2d8+8 dam. plus 8 acid dam., AC 15, 25 hp)  
 
 

**Tactics**



**Before Combat** Maddened engines of destruction, cinder scourges use their breath weapon and fearful shriek abilities and then rampage into battle.  

**During Combat** Once engaged, cinder scourges attempt to swallow as many creatures as they can, using their ground slam ability to devastate wide areas, all the while waiting for their breath weapon to recharge.  

**Morale** Cinder scourges know no fear and fight until destroyed.  
 


**Statistics**

 

**Str** 32, **Dex** 16, **Con** 24, **Int** 8, **Wis** 16, **Cha** 20  
 **Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +22 (+26 for grab); **CMD** 35 (can’t be tripped)  
 **Feats** Cleave, Combat Reflexes, Die Hard, Endurance, Toughness, Weapon Focus (bite)  
 **Skills** Climb +34, Perception +18, Swim +34; **Racial Modifiers** +8 on Climb and Swim  
 **Languages** Abyssal  
 **SQ** Death Throes, Skills
