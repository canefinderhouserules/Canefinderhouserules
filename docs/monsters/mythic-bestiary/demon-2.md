## Mythic Nalfeshnee (CR 17/MR 7)

**XP** 102,400  
CE Huge outsider (chaotic, demon, evil, extraplanar, mythic)  
**Init** +12<sup>M</sup>; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., *true seeing*; Perception +32  
**Aura** *unholy aura* (DC 25)

**Defense**

**AC** 36, touch 13, flat-footed 35 (+4 deflection, +1 Dex, +23 natural, –2 size)  
**hp** 355 (15d10+205); fast healing 5  
**Fort** +22, **Ref** +10, **Will** +21  
**DR** 10/epic and good; **Immune** fire, electricity, poison; **Resist** acid 10, cold 10; **SR** 28

**Offense**

**Speed** 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (poor)  
**Melee** bite +28 (3d8+15/18–20), 2 claws +28 (2d6+15/19–20 plus steal)  
**Space** 15 ft., **Reach** 15 ft.  
**Special Attacks** demonic knowledge, mythic power (7/day, surge +1d10), touch of chaos, unholy nimbus  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 12th; concentration +19)  
Constant—*[detect thoughts](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md)* (DC 19), *[true seeing](../../magic/spells/true-seeing.md)*, *[unholy aura](../../magic/spells/unholy-aura.md)* (DC 25)  
At will—*[call lightning](../../magic/spells/call-lightning.md)* (DC 20), *[feeblemind](../../magic/spells/feeblemind.md)* (DC 22), *[greater dispel magic](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic-greater.md)*, *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self plus 50 lbs. of objects only), *[move earth](../../magic/spells/move-earth.md)*, *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)* (DC 20), *[stone shape](../../magic/spells/stone-shape.md)*, *[stone tell](../../magic/spells/stone-tell.md)*  
3/day—*[scrying](../../magic/spells/scrying.md)*  
1/day—*[contact other plane](../../magic/spells/contact-other-plane.md)*, summon (level 5, 1 nalfeshnee 20%, 1d4 hezrous 40%, or 1d4 vrocks 50%)

**Statistics**

**Str** 40, **Dex** 13, **Con** 29, **Int** 23, **Wis** 22, **Cha** 24  
**Base Atk** +15; **CMB** +32 (+34 bull rush); **CMD** 47 (49 vs. bull rush)  
**Feats** Awesome Blow, [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Improved Bull Rush](../../rules/feats/improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Critical (bite)<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Critical (claws)](../../rules/feats/improved-critical.md), [Improved Initiative<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Iron Will<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Power Attack<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md)  
**Skills** Bluff +25, Diplomacy +25, Fly +11, Intimidate +22, Knowledge (arcana) +24, Knowledge (planes) +24, Knowledge (any one other) +21, Perception +32, Sense Motive +24, Spellcraft +24, Stealth +11, Use Magic Device +25; **Racial Modifiers** +8 Perception  
**Languages** Abyssal, Celestial, Draconic; telepathy 100 ft.

**Ecology**

**Environment** any (Abyss)  
**Organization** solitary or warband (1 nalfeshnee, 1 hezrou, and 2–5 vrocks)  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Demonic Knowledge (Su)** By expending one use of mythic power as a free action, the demon learns one weakness, vulnerability, or special defense of an opponent within reach.

**Touch of Chaos (Sp)** This functions like the Chaos domain granted power (*Core Rulebook* 42), except the demon can expend one use of mythic power as a free action to use this ability when it hits with a natural attack.

**Unholy Nimbus (Su)** Three times per day as a free action, the demon can create writhing colored lights on its body. One round later, the light bursts in a 60-foot radius. Any non-demon caught within this area must succeed at a DC 24 Will save or be dazed for 1d10 rounds as visions of madness hound it. The save DC is Charisma-based.
