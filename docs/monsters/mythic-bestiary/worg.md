## Mythic Worg (CR 3/MR 1)

**XP** 800  
NE Medium magical beast (mythic)  
**Init** +2; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent; Perception +11  
**Aura** darkness (30 ft., 1 step)

**Defense**

**AC** 15, touch 12, flat-footed 13 (+2 Dex, +3 natural)  
**hp** 54 (4d10+14)  
**Fort** +5, **Ref** +6, **Will** +3

**Offense**

**Speed** 50 ft.  
**Melee** bite +7 (1d8+4 plus trip)  
**Special Attacks** fear cone (30 ft., DC 12), mythic power (1/day, surge +1d6)

**Statistics**

**Str** 17, **Dex** 15, **Con** 13, **Int** 6, **Wis** 14, **Cha** 10  
**Base Atk** +4; **CMB** +7; **CMD** 19 (23 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Run](../../rules/feats/run.md), [Skill Focus (Perception)<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/skill-focus.md)  
**Skills** Perception +11, Stealth +9, Survival +5; **Racial Modifiers** +2 Perception, +2 Stealth, +2 Survival  
**Languages** Common, Goblin

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate forests and plains  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or pack (3–11)  
**Treasure** incidental

**Special Abilities**

**Darkness Aura (Su)** As a free action, a mythic worg can activate its darkness aura, which reduces the light level within 30 feet of it by one step. This never reduces the light level to supernatural darkness. Multiple worgs within range can reduce the light level multiple steps. Because a mythic worg has darkvision and low-light vision, this ability never interferes with its own vision. It can end this ability as a free action.
