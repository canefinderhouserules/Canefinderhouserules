## Mythic Lamia (CR 7/MR 3)

**XP** 3,200  
CE Large monstrous humanoid (mythic, shapechanger)  
**Init** +3; **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision; Perception +15  
**Aura** seduction (DC 16)

**Defense**

**AC** 25, touch 15, flat-footed 19 (+3 Dex, +3 dodge, +10 natural, –1 size)  
**hp** 138 (9d10+48)  
**Fort** +7, **Ref** +9, **Will** +11  
**DR** 5/epic

**Offense**

**Speed** 60 ft.  
**Melee** *+1 scimitar* +13/+8 (1d6+5/18–20), 2 claws +12 (1d4+2), touch +7 (1d4 Wisdom drain)  
**Space** 10 ft., **Reach** 5 ft.  
**Special Attacks** mythic power (3/day, surge +1d6), pounce, Wisdom drain  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 9th; concentration +11)  
At will—*[disguise self](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md)*, *[ventriloquism](../../magic/spells/ventriloquism.md)*  
3/day—*[charm monster](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md)* (DC 16), *[major image](../../magic/spells/major-image.md)* (DC 15), *[mirror image](../../magic/spells/mirror-image.md)*, *[suggestion](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md)* (DC 15)  
1/day—*[deep slumber](../../magic/spells/deep-slumber.md)* (DC 15)  
**Sorcerer Spells Known** (CL 6th; concentration +8)  
3rd (3)—*[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*  
2nd (6)—*[death knell](../../magic/spells/death-knell.md)* (DC 14), *[invisibility](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md)*  
1st (7)—*[cure light wounds](../../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds.md)*, *[divine favor](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md)*, *[mage armor](../../magic/spells/mage-armor.md)*, *[magic missile](../../magic/spells/magic-missile.md)*  
0 (at will)—*[dancing lights](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md)*, *[daze](../../magic/spells/daze.md)* (DC 12), *[detect magic](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md)*, *[ghost sound](../../magic/spells/ghost-sound.md)* (DC 12), *[mage hand](../../magic/spells/mage-hand.md)*, *[mending](../../magic/spells/mending.md)*, *[prestidigitation](../../magic/spells/prestidigitation.md)*

**Statistics**

**Str** 18, **Dex** 16, **Con** 14, **Int** 13, **Wis** 17, **Cha** 15  
**Base Atk** +9; **CMB** +14; **CMD** 29 (33 vs. trip)  
**Feats** [Dodge<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Great Fortitude](../../rules/feats/great-fortitude.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Dodgility](../../rules/feats/dodge.md), [Spring Attack<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/spring-attack.md)  
*House rules: Mobility is part of Dodgility.*  
**Skills** Bluff +10, Diplomacy +5, Disguise +7, Intimidate +11, Knowledge (religion) +4, Perception +15, Stealth +15, Survival +12; **Racial Modifiers** +4 Bluff, +4 Stealth  
**Languages** Abyssal, Common  
**SQ** change shape (giant constrictor snake, lamia matriarch, or lion; *polymorph*), undersized weapons

**Ecology**

**Environment** temperate desert  
**Organization** solitary, pair, or cult (3–12)  
**Treasure** double (*+1 scimitar*, other treasure)

**Special Abilities**

**Aura of Seduction (Su)** Any creature within 30 feet of a mythic lamia must succeed at a DC 16 Will save or become fascinated. A creature that succeeds at this save is immune to the lamia’s aura for 24 hours. This is a mind-affecting effect. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Spells** A mythic lamia casts spells as a 6th-level sorcerer, and can cast spells from the cleric list as well as those normally available to a sorcerer. Cleric spells are considered arcane spells for a mythic lamia.

**Wisdom Drain (Su)** A lamia drains 1d4 points of Wisdom each time she hits with her melee touch attack. (Unlike with other kinds of ability drain attacks, a lamia does not heal any damage when she uses her Wisdom drain.) Lamias try to use this power early in an encounter to make foes more susceptible to *charm monster* and *suggestion*.
