## Mythic Julunggali (CR 21/MR 8)

**XP** 409,600  
N Colossal magical beast (aquatic, extraplanar, mythic, shapechanger)  
**Init** +21<sup>M</sup>; **Senses** blindsense 60 ft., darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent, see in darkness; Perception +30  
**Aura** fascination (DC 26, 100 feet)

**Defense**

**AC** 38, touch 5, flat-footed 37 (+1 Dex, +2 insight, +33 natural, –8 size)  
**hp** 498 (22d10+278); fast healing 10  
**Fort** +22, **Ref** +18, **Will** +14  
**Defensive Abilities** mythic saving throws; **DR** 10/epic and magic; **Immune** acid, cold, electricity, poison; **Resist** fire 10; **SR** 32

**Offense**

**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 40 ft., fly 20 ft. (average), swim 90 ft.  
**Melee** bite +29 (4d8+14 plus grab and poison and pull or push), slam +29 (4d6+14)  
**Space** 30 ft., **Reach** 30 ft.  
**Special Attacks** amazing initiative, constrict (4d8+21), fast swallow, mythic power (8/ day, surge +1d10), pull (bite, 30 ft.), push (bite, 30 ft.), swallow whole (4d8 acid damage, AC 26, 39 hp)  
**Spell-Like Abilities** (CL 24th; concentration +29)  
Constant—*[foresight](../../magic/spells/foresight.md)* (self only)  
At will—*[bestow curse](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md)* (DC 18), *[bless](../../magic/spells/bless.md)*<sup>M</sup>, *[control water](../../magic/spells/control-water.md)*, *[create water](../../magic/spells/create-water.md)*, *[move earth](../../magic/spells/move-earth.md)*<sup>M</sup>, *[neutralize poison](../../magic/spells/neutralize-poison.md)*, *[prayer](../../magic/spells/prayer.md)*<sup>M</sup>, *[water breathing](../../magic/spells/water-breathing.md)*  
3/day—*[antipathy](../../magic/spells/antipathy.md)* (DC 24), *[baleful polymorph](../../magic/spells/baleful-polymorph.md)*<sup>M</sup> (DC 20), *[control weather](../../magic/spells/control-weather.md)*<sup>M</sup> (rain only), *[greater teleport](../../magic/spells/teleport-greater.md)* (self only), *[haste](../../magic/spells/haste.md)*<sup>M</sup>, *[heal](../../magic/spells/heal.md)*<sup>M</sup>, *[polymorph](../../magic/spells/polymorph.md)*, *[prismatic wall](../../magic/spells/prismatic-wall.md)* (DC 23), *[raise dead](../../magic/spells/raise-dead.md)*, *[slow](../../magic/spells/slow.md)*<sup>M</sup> (DC 18)

**Statistics**

**Str** 38, **Dex** 13, **Con** 28, **Int** 19, **Wis** 20, **Cha** 21  
**Base Atk** +22; **CMB** +44 (+48 grapple); **CMD** 57 (can’t be tripped)  
**Feats** [Cleave](../../rules/feats/cleave.md), [Combat Reflexes<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/combat-reflexes.md), [Great Cleave](../../rules/feats/great-cleave.md), [Improved Initiative<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/improved-initiative.md), [Improved Iron Will](../../rules/feats/improved-iron-will.md), [Improved Lightning Reflexes](../../rules/feats/improved-lightning-reflexes.md), [Iron Will](../../rules/feats/iron-will.md), [Lightning Reflexes<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/lightning-reflexes.md), [Power Attack<sup>M</sup>](../../rules/feats/power-attack.md), [Weapon Focus (bite)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md), [Weapon Focus (gore)](../../rules/feats/weapon-focus.md)  
**Skills** Fly –7, Handle Animal +16, Heal +27, Intimidate +27, Knowledge (nature) +26, Knowledge (religion) +15, Perception +30, Sense Motive +27, Swim +22  
**Languages** Celestial, Druidic, Sylvan; telepathy 100 ft.  
**SQ** amphibious, change shape (any snake; *polymorph*), compression

**Ecology**

**Environment** any land or water  
**Organization** solitary  
**Treasure** standard

**Special Abilities**

**Fascination Aura (Su)** Any creature within 100 feet of the julunggali who can see it must succeed at a Will save (DC 26) or be fascinated as long as it remains within range. A creature that saves against the julunggali’s aura is immune to it for 24 hours. The save DC is Charisma-based.

**Poison (Ex or Su)** A julunggali has three different poisons. Each time it bites, it decides which poison to inject.

*Julunggali Venom*: Bite—injury; *save* Fort DC 30; *frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *effect* 1d6 Con or 1d6 Dex or age one age category (julunggali’s choice); *cure* 2 consecutive saves. The save DC is Constitution-based.
