**Special Abilities**

**Exceptional Resources (Ex)** the betrayed goddess has the wealth of a 20th-level PC, rather than an NPC. This increases her CR by 1. She used some of this wealth to create and read the following books to gain inherent bonuses to her ability scores: *manual of gainful exercise +5*, *manual of quickness in action +4*, *tome of clear thought +5*, and *tome of leadership and influence +5*.

**Permanent Spells** Using *permanency*, the betrayed goddess gave herself the constant effects of *arcane sight*, *resistance*, and *see invisibility*. She is under the effect of a *contingency* that casts *harm* on her if she’s brought below 100 hit points.
