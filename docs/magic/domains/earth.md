***Granted Powers**: You have mastery over earth, metal, and stone, can fire darts of acid, and command earth creatures.*

*Acid Dart (Sp): You can unleash an acid dart targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch attack. This acid dart deals 1d6 points of acid damage + Your Wisdom. This counts as ranged attacks and can use a Full Round action to use more than one in the same turn. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier.*

*Damage Resistance (Ex): At 4th level, you gain DR 3/Adamantine. This DR increases to 5 at 6th level. At 20th level, you gain DR 20/-.*

***Domain Spells**: 1st—magic stone, 2nd—soften earth and stone, 3rd—stone shape, 4th—spike stones, 5th—wall of stone, 6th—stoneskin, 7th—elemental body IV (earth only), 8th—earthquake, 9th—elemental swarm (earth spell only).*

***Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the acid resistance power of the Earth domain.*

*Tunnel Runner (Su): At 4th level, you can move through tunnels and caves with ease. Activating this ability is a standard action. You can move across any stone surface as if under the effects of spider climb. You can also see very well in darkness, gaining darkvision out to a range of 60 feet. If you already possess darkvision, extend the range by 60 feet. While underground, you also gain an insight bonus equal to your cleric level on Stealth skill checks and an insight bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on initiative checks. You can use this ability for 1 minute per day per cleric level you possess. These minutes do not need to be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute increments.*

***Replacement Domain Spells**: 2nd—create pit, 3rd—spiked pit, 6th—hungry pit.*

*Metal Fist (Su): As a swift action, you can turn your fists into metal for 1 round, allowing you to make two unarmed strikes at full BAB that deal 1d8 points of bludgeoning damage plus your Strength modifier. These unarmed strikes do not provoke attacks of opportunity. In addition, these unarmed strikes ignore the hardness of items with a hardness of 10 or less. They also count as the Unarmed Weapon Special Ability. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.*

***Replacement Domain Spells**: 2nd—heat metal, 6th—wall of iron, 8th—iron body.*

###  *<span class="smallcaps">Petrification Subdomain</span>*

***Source** [Divine Anthology pg. 25](http://paizo.com/products/btpy9nvh?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Divine-Anthology)*

*This subdomain requires the [Acolyte of Apocrypha](https://www.aonprd.com/TraitDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Acolyte%20of%20Apocrypha) trait to access.*

***Associated Domain(s)**: Earth*

***Associated Deities**: [Ayrzul](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Ayrzul), [Minderhal](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Minderhal), [Soralyon](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Soralyon), [Xoveron](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Xoveron)*

***Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the acid dart power of the Earth domain.*

*Fossil Form (Su): At the beginning of your turn as a swift action, you can infuse your flesh with minerals, granting you cumbersome security. Until the beginning of your next turn, you have -2 to AC and attack, but gain DR 2 + your Cleric level/-, and gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against petrification. This saving throw bonus increases by 1 for every 4 cleric levels you have. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.*

***Replacement Domain Spells**: 4th—calcific touch<sup>APG</sup>, 5th—stoneskin, 6th—flesh to stone, 8th—statue.*

### *<span class="smallcaps">Radiation Subdomain</span>*

***Source** [Kobolds of Golarion pg. 13](http://paizo.com/products/btpy8yw0?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Kobolds-of-Golarion)*

***Associated Domain(s)**: Earth*

***Associated Deities**: N/A*

***Replacement Power**: Note: Only creatures with the [Kobold Faith](https://www.aonprd.com/TraitDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Kobold%20Faith) trait can take this subdomain.*

*The following granted power replaces the acid dart power of the Earth domain.*

*Radiating Touch (Su): As a move action, you can irradiate any object or creature for a number of rounds equal to your cleric level (If its an enemy, it requires a melee touch attack). While an object or creature is irradiated, it emits a faint aura of transmutation. Any creature that passes within 5 feet of the aura becomes sickened. You’re immune to this effect. You can spend 2 extra uses to grant immunity to your allies too. At 6th level it becomes a 10ft aura. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.*

***Replacement Domain Spells**: 2nd—defoliate, 4th—blight, 8th—horrid wilting.*
