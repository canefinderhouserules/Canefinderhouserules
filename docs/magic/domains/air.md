*Air Shroud (Sp)*: As a standard action, you can shroud yourself in gusts of wind, gaining 10% Concealment from enemy attacks. This improves by +10% at level 4 and another time at level 10 for a total of 30%.

*Fly (Su)*: At 6th level, you gain Fly speed equal to your normal speed.

###  <span class="smallcaps">Cloud Subdomain</span>

**Source** [*Advanced Player's Guide pg. 1*](http://paizo.com/pathfinderRPG/v5748btpy8fo1)

**Associated Domain(s)**: Air

**Associated Deities**: [Chupurvagasti](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Chupurvagasti), [Findeladlara](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Findeladlara), [Ghlaunder](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Ghlaunder), [Gozreh](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Gozreh), [Hathor](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Hathor), [Hei Feng](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Hei%20Feng), [Ithaqua](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Ithaqua), [Nyarlathotep (Faceless Sphinx)](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Nyarlathotep%20(Faceless%20Sphinx)), [Pazuzu](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Pazuzu), [Pulura](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Pulura), [Rull](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Rull), [Shelyn](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Shelyn), [Shelyn (pre-Earthfall)](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Shelyn%20(pre-Earthfall)), [Tjasse](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Tjasse)

**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the electricity resistance power of the Air domain.

*Thundercloud (Su)*: At 6th level, you can, as a standard action, summon a storm cloud. This power functions as *fog cloud* except that creatures inside the cloud are deafened and take 2d6 points of electricity damage each round from the flashes of thunder and lightning. It counts as a Storm source for Call Lightning/Storm. Once created, you can concentrate on the cloud to move it up to 30 feet each round. You can use this ability for a number of rounds per day equal to your cleric level. These rounds do not need to be consecutive.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 4th—*solid fog*, 9th—*storm of vengeance*.

###  <span class="smallcaps">Lightning Subdomain</span>

**Source** [*Divine Anthology pg. 24*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy9nvh?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Divine-Anthology)

*This subdomain requires the [Acolyte of Apocrypha](https://www.aonprd.com/TraitDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Acolyte%20of%20Apocrypha) trait to access.*

**Associated Domain(s)**: Air

**Associated Deities**: [Gozreh](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Gozreh), [Hei Feng](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Hei%20Feng), [Rovagug](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Rovagug), [Rull](https://www.aonprd.com/DeityDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Rull)

**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the electricity resistance power of the Air domain.

*Lightning Rod (Su)*: As a swift action when you cast a spell with the electricity descriptor, you can designate one creature within line of sight. The spell’s damage against that creature increases by 50%, as if affected by the Empower Spell feat. This additional damage results from divine power that is not subject to being reduced by electricity resistance, and you take an equal amount of electricity damage immediately after you cast the spell. The spell can deal this additional damage only once, even if it could affect the target multiple times. You can use this ability once per day at 8th level and one additional time per day for every 4 cleric levels you have beyond 8th.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 1st— *shocking grasp*, 2nd—*flame blade* (deals electricity damage and gains the electricity descriptor instead of fire), 3rd— *lightning bolt*.

**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the lightning arc power of the Air domain.

*Wind Blast (Su)*: As a standard action, you can unleash a blast of air in a 30-foot line. Make a combat maneuver check against each creature in the line, using your caster level as your base attack bonus and your Wisdom modifier in place of your Strength modifier. Treat the results as a Trip attempt. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 1st—*whispering wind*, 6th—*wind walk*, 9th—*winds of vengeance*.
