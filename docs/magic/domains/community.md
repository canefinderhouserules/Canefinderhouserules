# Community



**Granted Powers**: Your touch can heal wounds, and your presence instills unity and strengthens emotional bonds.

*Calming Touch (Sp)*: You can touch a creature as a move action (1 AP) to heal it of 1d6 points of nonlethal damage per cleric level. This touch also removes the [fatigued](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fatigued), [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken), and [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened) conditions (but has no effect on more severe conditions). You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

*Unity (Su)*: At 4th level, whenever a spell or effect targets you and one or more allies within 30 feet, you can use this ability to allow your allies to use your saving throw against the effect in place of their own. Each ally must decide individually. Using this ability is an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn). You can use this ability once per day at 4th level, and one additional time per day for every four cleric levels beyond 4th.

**Domain Spells**: 1st—[*bless*](../spells/bless.md), 2nd—[*shield other*](../spells/shield-other.md), 3rd—[*prayer*](../spells/prayer.md), 4th—[*imbue with spell ability*](../spells/imbue-with-spell-ability.md), 5th—[*telepathic bond*](../spells/telepathic-bond.md), 6th—[*heroes' feast*](../spells/heroes-feast.md), 7th—[*refuge*](../spells/refuge.md), 8th—*mass cure critical wounds*, 9th—[*miracle*](../spells/miracle.md).

###  Cooperation Subdomain


**Associated Domain(s)**: Community


**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the calming touch ability of the Community domain.

*Synergistic Touch (Sp)*: You can touch a creature as a standard action (1 AP) to confer upon it the benefits of any one Teamwork feat that you possess. You can use a Full Round Action (3 AP) and affect every adjacent ally. This effect persists for a number of rounds equal to your cleric level. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 1st—[*borrow skill*](../spells/borrow-skill.md), 2nd—[*share language*](../spells/share-language.md), 3rd—[*coordinated effort*](../spells/coordinated-effort.md).

###  Education Subdomain


**Associated Domain(s)**: Community, Knowledge


**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the unity power of the Community domain or the remote viewing power of the Knowledge domain.

*Teaching Moment (Su)*: At 4th level, when you or an ally within 30 feet rolls a natural 1 or a natural 20 on an attack roll, an ability check, a skill check, or a saving throw, as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) you can grant all allies within 30 feet special insights that help them overcome similar challenges. Once during the next minute, each affected creature can choose to roll twice and take the better result before attempting an attack roll, ability check, skill check, or saving throw. You can use this ability once per day at 4th level, and one additional time per day for every 4 cleric levels beyond 4th.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 1st—[*know the enemy*](../spells/know-the-enemy.md), 2nd—[*fox’s cunning*](../spells/foxs-cunning.md), 6th—[*battlemind link*](../spells/battlemind-link.md), 8th—[*circle of clarity*](../spells/circle-of-clarity.md).

###  Family Subdomain


**Associated Domain(s)**: Community


**Replacement Power**: The following power replaces the calming touch power of the Community domain.

*Binding Ties (Su)*: As a swift action (1 AP), you can touch an ally and remove one condition affecting the ally by transferring it to yourself. This transfer lasts a number of rounds equal to your cleric level, but you can end it as a free action on your turn. At the end of this effect, the condition reverts to the original creature, unless it has ended or is removed by another effect. While this power is in use, the target is immune to the transferred condition. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to 3 + your Wisdom modifier.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 2nd—[*calm emotions*](../spells/calm-emotions.md), 3rd—[*create food and water*](../spells/create-food-and-water.md).

###  Home Subdomain


**Associated Domain(s)**: Community


**Replacement Power**: The following granted power replaces the unity power of the Community domain.

*Guarded Hearth (Su)*: At 6th level, you can create a ward that protects a specified area. Creating this ward takes 10 minutes of uninterrupted work. This ward has a maximum radius of 5 feet per 2 cleric levels you possess. When the ward is completed, you designate any number of creatures inside its area. Should any other creature enter the warded area, all of the selected creatures are immediately alerted (and awoken if they were asleep). The designated creatures also receive a sacred bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier on all saving throws and attack rolls while inside the warded area. This ward immediately ends if you leave the area. The ward lasts for 1 hour per cleric level. You can use this ability once per day.

**Replacement Domain Spells**: 1st—[*alarm*](../spells/alarm.md), 3rd—[*glyph of warding*](../spells/glyph-of-warding.md), 7th—[*guards and wards*](../spells/guards-and-wards.md).
