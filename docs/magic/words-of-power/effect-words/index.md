# Effect Words

Effect words determine the overall outcome of a wordspell. A wordspell can contain more than one effect word, and it is possible for their effects to counteract one another. Each word belongs to a group of similar words; unless otherwise noted, an individual wordspell cannot contain two effect words from the same group.
