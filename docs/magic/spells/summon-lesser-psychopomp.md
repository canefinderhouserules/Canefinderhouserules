# Summon Lesser Psychopomp

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** cleric 5, sorcerer/wizard 5, summoner 4, witch 5  
**Casting Time** 1 round (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (a crocodile skull funerary mask)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or more summoned psychopomps (see text)  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like *summon monster*, except that it summons 1d3 esobok psychopomps or 1d4+1 nosoi psychopomps.

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any psychopomp, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
