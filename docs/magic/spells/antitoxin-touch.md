# Antitoxin Touch

**School** abjuration; **Level** alchemist 1, druid 1, ranger 1, shaman 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a small drop of antitoxin)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

The target's skin secretes a small amount of venom-resistant oils for the spell's duration. The target cannot accidentally poison itself while applying poison. Additionally, the target does not risk exposure to a creature's poison when attempting to milk venom.
