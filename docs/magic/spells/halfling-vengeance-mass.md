# Halfling Vengeance, Mass

**School** divination; **Level** antipaladin 4, cleric 7, inquisitor 6, oracle 7 Casting  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one Small or smaller creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell functions like [*halfling vengeance*](halfling-vengeance.md), except that it affects multiple creatures.
