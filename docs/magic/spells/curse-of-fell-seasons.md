# Curse of Fell Seasons

**School** transmutation [curse, darkness]; **Level** druid 9, shaman 9, sorcerer/wizard 9, witch 9  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, F (darkwood carving of a tree worth 15,000 gp)  
**Range** touch  
**Area** 2-mile radius emanating from the touched point  
**Duration** permanent (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

By touching the ground, you drastically change the weather in the area, as the unseasonable weather curse. The focus merges into the cursed area as part of the spell and can only be retrieved if the curse is lifted.
