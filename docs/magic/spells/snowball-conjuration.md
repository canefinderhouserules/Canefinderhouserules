# Snowball (Conjuration)

**School** conjuration (creation) [cold, water]; **Level** druid 1, magus 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, summoner 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one ball of ice and snow  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial (see text); **Spell Resistance** no

You conjure a ball of packed ice and snow that you can throw at a single target as a ranged touch attack. The snowball deals 1d6 points of cold damage per caster level (maximum 5d6) on a successful hit, and the target must make a successful Fortitude saving throw or be [staggered](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round.

!!! note "Two versions"
    This is the first version of this spell. A later version, [Snowball](snowball.md), is an evocation spell: it allows spell resistance and does not stagger.
