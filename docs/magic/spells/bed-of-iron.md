# Bed of Iron

**School** necromancy; **Level** inquisitor 1, magus 1, paladin 1, sorcerer/wizard 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a feather or swatch of soft fabric)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** one creature touched/level  
**Duration** 8 hours  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell makes even the clunkiest armor feel soft as silk to the wearer. The subjects are able to sleep comfortably in medium or heavy armor without suffering from fatigue the following day.
