# Alchemist Spells

New alchemist spells from *Ultimate Magic* and *Ultimate Combat*. Spells from other books will be added as they are transcribed. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

## 1st-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Abjuring Step](abjuring-step.md)**: You can move slowly and safely and still cast spells, until you move quickly, make an attack, or cast a harmful spell.
- **[Anticipate Peril](anticipate-peril.md)**: Target gains a bonus on one initiative check.
- **[Blend](blend.md)**: You draw upon your elven link to the wilderness to change the coloration of yourself and your equipment to.
- **[Fabricate Disguise](fabricate-disguise.md)**: Create a disguise in an instant.
- **[Illusion of Calm](illusion-of-calm.md)**: You appear to be standing still, even when you take some actions.
- **[Linebreaker](linebreaker.md)**: You gain a +20 foot bonus to your base speed when charging and a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks made to.
- **[Longshot](longshot.md)**: Grants a +10-foot bonus to the range increment for any ranged weapon fired.
- **[Obscure Poison](obscure-poison.md)**: Make it harder to detect a poison or a venomous creature.
- **[Polypurpose Panacea](polypurpose-panacea.md)**: Gain a relaxing or entertaining effect.
- **[Recharge Innate Magic](recharge-innate-magic.md)**: You channel magic energy into your own aura, recharging your innate magic abilities.
- **[See Alignment](see-alignment.md)**: Pick an alignment; in your sight, creatures and items with that alignment emit a ghostly radiance.
- **[Shock Shield](shock-shield.md)**: A shield of force protects you until you dismiss it in an explosion of electricity.
- **[Targeted Bomb Admixture](targeted-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw to deal more damage to a single creature.
- **[Urban Grace](urban-grace.md)**: You become one with the city around you, allowing you to move more easily through its crowds and buildings.
- **[Vocal Alteration](vocal-alteration.md)**: Disguise target’s voice.
- **[Wizened Appearance](wizened-appearance.md)**: Make a target appear as an older version of itself.
- **[Youthful Appearance](youthful-appearance.md)**: Target appears younger.

## Advanced Player's Guide Spells

New spells from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Ant Haul](ant-haul.md)**: Triples carrying capacity of a creature.
- **[Bomber's Eye](bombers-eye.md)**: Increases thrown weapon range; +1 attack.
- **[Crafter's Fortune](crafters-fortune.md)**: Subject gains +5 on next Craft check.
- **[Keen Senses](keen-senses.md)**: Gain +2 Perception and low-light vision.
- **[Negate Aroma](negate-aroma.md)**: Subject cannot be tracked by scent.
- **[Stone Fist](stone-fist.md)**: Your unarmed strikes are lethal.
- **[Touch of the Sea](touch-of-the-sea.md)**: Swim speed becomes 30 ft.

### 2nd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Alchemical Allocation](alchemical-allocation.md)**: Gain potion’s benefits without consuming it.
- **[Elemental Touch](elemental-touch.md)**: Gain energy damage touch attack.
- **[Fire Breath](fire-breath.md)**: Exhale a cone of flame at will.
- **[Heroic Fortune](heroic-fortune.md)**: Grants the target 1 temporary hero point.
- **[Perceive Cues](perceive-cues.md)**: +5 Perception and Sense Motive 10 min./level.
- **[Transmute Potion to Poison](transmute-potion-to-poison.md)**: Spit poison onto weapon after drinking potion.
- **[Vomit Swarm](vomit-swarm.md)**: Produces a spider swarm that fights for you.

### 3rd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Absorbing Touch](absorbing-touch.md)**: Caster absorbs an item for 1 day/level.
- **[Amplify Elixir](amplify-elixir.md)**: Empowers or extends the effects of any potion or elixir.
- **[Bloodhound](bloodhound.md)**: Enhances sense of smell and grants the caster the scent special ability.
- **[Draconic Reservoir](draconic-reservoir.md)**: Subject can absorb energy damage and enhance melee attacks with it.
- **[Elemental Aura](elemental-aura.md)**: Creates an aura of energy around you.
- **[Seek Thoughts](seek-thoughts.md)**: Detects thinking creatures’ thoughts.
- **[Thorn Body](thorn-body.md)**: Those attacking you take 1d6 +1 damage/level.

### 4th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Detonate](detonate.md)** (M): Inflicts 1d8/level energy damage to all creatures within 15 ft.
- **[Dragon's Breath](dragons-breath.md)**: Gives you a dragon’s breath weapon.
- **[Fluid Form](fluid-form.md)**: Gain DR 10/slashing, increases reach 10 ft., and breathe water.
- **[Universal Formula](universal-formula.md)** (M): Acts as any extract 3rd level or lower.

### 5th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Delayed Consumption](delayed-consumption.md)**: Extract doesn’t come into effect until you want it to.
- **[Elude Time](elude-time.md)** (M): Puts you in temporary suspended animation.
- **[Planar Adaptation](planar-adaptation.md)**: Resist harmful effects of one plane.
- **[Resurgent Transformation](resurgent-transformation.md)** (M): Gain second wind when close to death.

### 6th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Twin Form](twin-form.md)**: Creates controllable duplicate of you.
