# Alchemist Spells

New alchemist spells from *Ultimate Magic* and *Ultimate Combat*. Spells from other books will be added as they are transcribed. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

## 1st-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Abjuring Step](abjuring-step.md)**: You can move slowly and safely and still cast spells, until you move quickly, make an attack, or cast a harmful spell.
- **[Anticipate Peril](anticipate-peril.md)**: Target gains a bonus on one initiative check.
- **[Illusion of Calm](illusion-of-calm.md)**: You appear to be standing still, even when you take some actions.
- **[Longshot](longshot.md)**: Grants a +10-foot bonus to the range increment for any ranged weapon fired.
- **[Polypurpose Panacea](polypurpose-panacea.md)**: Gain a relaxing or entertaining effect.
- **[See Alignment](see-alignment.md)**: Pick an alignment; in your sight, creatures and items with that alignment emit a ghostly radiance.
- **[Shock Shield](shock-shield.md)**: A shield of force protects you until you dismiss it in an explosion of electricity.
- **[Targeted Bomb Admixture](targeted-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw to deal more damage to a single creature.
- **[Vocal Alteration](vocal-alteration.md)**: Disguise target’s voice.
- **[Youthful Appearance](youthful-appearance.md)**: Target appears younger.

## 2nd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Ablative Barrier](ablative-barrier.md)**: Surrounds the target with layers of force.
- **[Acute Senses](acute-senses.md)**: Subject gains a bonus on Perception checks.
- **[Animal Aspect](animal-aspect.md)**: You gain some of the beneficial qualities of an animal.
- **[Ant Haul, Communal](ant-haul-communal.md)**: As ant haul, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Bestow Weapon Proficiency](bestow-weapon-proficiency.md)**: Grants a creature proficiency in a single weapon for short period of time.
- **[Blistering Invective](blistering-invective.md)**: Make an Intimidate check against all foes within 30 feet; those demoralized also take fire damage.
- **[Blood Transcription](blood-transcription.md)**: Learn a spell from the target’s blood.
- **[Bullet Shield](bullet-shield.md)**: You gain a +4 deflection bonus to AC against firearm attacks.
- **[Catatonia](catatonia.md)**: Make a willing target appear to be dead.
- **[Certain Grip](certain-grip.md)**: You gain a +4 competence bonus on Acrobatics and Climb checks and to CMD.
- **[Defensive Shock](defensive-shock.md)**: Electricity damages your attackers.
- **[Enshroud Thoughts](enshroud-thoughts.md)**: Ward yourself against thought detection and memory alteration.
- **[Ghostly Disguise](ghostly-disguise.md)**: You look like a ghost of yourself.
- **[Grasping Vine](grasping-vine.md)**: A vine from your body manipulates objects within 20 ft.
- **[Kinetic Reverberation](kinetic-reverberation.md)**: Channels the force of an enemy’s attack back into its weapon.
- **[Pouncing Fury](pouncing-fury.md)**: Make a full attack with your claws after a charge.
- **[Shadow Bomb Admixture](shadow-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw to cover the target with a shadowy substance that grants it concealment and limits its vision.
- **[Skinsend](skinsend.md)**: Animate and possess your own skin as if it were a separate creature.
- **[Touch Injection](touch-injection.md)**: You can deliver an infusion, elixir, poison, or potion as a touch attack.
- **[Vine Strike](vine-strike.md)**: Enhance one of your natural or unarmed attacks with thorny vine growth.

## 3rd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Absorb Toxicity](absorb-toxicity.md)**: You become immune to diseases and toxins, absorb one, and then spread it to others.
- **[Age Resistance, Lesser](age-resistance-lesser.md)**: Ignore penalties from middle age.
- **[Animal Aspect, Greater](animal-aspect-greater.md)**: As animal aspect, but you gain two animal qualities.
- **[Burrow](burrow.md)**: Target gains a burrow speed of 15.
- **[Burst of Speed](burst-of-speed.md)**: You gain increased speed, and your movement ignores attacks of opportunity and allows you to move through the space of creatures larger than you are.
- **[Countless Eyes](countless-eyes.md)**: Extra eyes give all-around vision.
- **[Darkvision, Communal](darkvision-communal.md)**: As darkvision, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Delay Poison, Communal](delay-poison-communal.md)**: As delay poison, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Endure Elements, Communal](endure-elements-communal.md)**: As endure elements, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Eruptive Pustules](eruptive-pustules.md)**: Acid boils burst when you are attacked.
- **[Fey Form I](fey-form-i.md)**: Assume the form of a Small or Medium fey creature.
- **[Hypercognition](hypercognition.md)**: Rapidly recall everything you know about a subject.
- **[Lightning Lash Bomb Admixture](lightning-lash-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw with a ward that deals lightning damage each time those damaged by the bomb move more than 5 feet.
- **[Marionette Possession](marionette-possession.md)**: As magic jar, but limited to line of sight.
- **[Monstrous Physique I](monstrous-physique-i.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Small or Medium monstrous humanoid.
- **[Protection from Arrows, Communal](protection-from-arrows-communal.md)**: As protection from arrows, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Resinous Skin](resinous-skin.md)**: You gain DR 5/piercing and +4 to CMD against disarm attempts.
- **[Resist Energy, Communal](resist-energy-communal.md)**: As resist energy, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Spider Climb, Communal](spider-climb-communal.md)**: As spider climb, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Undead Anatomy I](undead-anatomy-i.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Small or Medium undead.
- **[Wing Thorns](wing-thorns.md)**: Wings sprout poisonous thorns you can attack with.

## 4th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Age Resistance](age-resistance.md)**: Ignore penalties from old age.
- **[Darkvision, Greater](darkvision-greater.md)**: See 120 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Echolocation](echolocation.md)**: Sonic sense gives you blindsight 40 ft.
- **[False Life, Greater](false-life-greater.md)**: Gain 2d10 temporary hp + 1/level.
- **[Monstrous Physique II](monstrous-physique-ii.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Tiny or Large monstrous humanoid.
- **[Mutagenic Touch](mutagenic-touch.md)**: You can pass a weaker version of your mutagen to another creature.
- **[Tongues, Communal](tongues-communal.md)**: As tongues, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Touch of Slime](touch-of-slime.md)**: Touch infests a target with green slime.
- **[Vermin Shape I](vermin-shape-i.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Small or Medium vermin.
- **[Viper Bomb Admixture](viper-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw to summon poisonous biting vipers that bite those hit or splashed by the bomb.
- **[Vitriolic Mist](vitriolic-mist.md)**: As fire shield, except acid damage.

## 5th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Age Resistance, Greater](age-resistance-greater.md)**: Ignore any penalties from venerable age.
- **[Air Walk, Communal](air-walk-communal.md)**: As air walk, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Dust Form](dust-form.md)**: You become an incorporeal creature of dust for a short period of time.
- **[Fey Form II](fey-form-ii.md)**: Assume the form of a Tiny or Large fey creature.
- **[Languid Bomb Admixture](languid-bomb-admixture.md)**: Empowers bombs you throw to cause fatigue in those directly hit or splashed by the bomb.
- **[Monstrous Physique III](monstrous-physique-iii.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Diminutive or Huge monstrous humanoid.
- **[Ooze Form I](ooze-form-i.md)**: Assume the form of a Small or Medium ooze creature.
- **[Shapechanger’s Gift](shapechangers-gift.md)**: Temporarily change a target into an animal, humanoid, or monstrous humanoid.
- **[Stoneskin, Communal](stoneskin-communal.md)**
- **[Undead Anatomy II](undead-anatomy-ii.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Tiny or Large undead.
- **[Vermin Shape II](vermin-shape-ii.md)**: As vermin shape, but Tiny or Large.

## 6th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Caging Bomb Admixture](caging-bomb-admixture.md)**: Your bombs create a cage of force that traps creatures inside the bomb’s splash radius.
- **[Fey Form III](fey-form-iii.md)**: Assume the form of a Diminutive or Huge fey creature.
- **[Monstrous Physique IV](monstrous-physique-iv.md)**: As monstrous physique III, with more abilities.
- **[Ooze Form II](ooze-form-ii.md)**: Assume the form of a Large ooze creature.
- **[Undead Anatomy III](undead-anatomy-iii.md)**: Take the form and some of the powers of a Diminutive or Huge undead.
- **[Walk Through Space](walk-through-space.md)**: You can spend a move action to teleport 30 feet or to stand while prone without provoking attacks of opportunity.

## Advanced Player's Guide Spells

New spells from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Ant Haul](ant-haul.md)**: Triples carrying capacity of a creature.
- **[Bomber's Eye](bombers-eye.md)**: Increases thrown weapon range; +1 attack.
- **[Crafter's Fortune](crafters-fortune.md)**: Subject gains +5 on next Craft check.
- **[Keen Senses](keen-senses.md)**: Gain +2 Perception and low-light vision.
- **[Negate Aroma](negate-aroma.md)**: Subject cannot be tracked by scent.
- **[Stone Fist](stone-fist.md)**: Your unarmed strikes are lethal.
- **[Touch of the Sea](touch-of-the-sea.md)**: Swim speed becomes 30 ft.

### 2nd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Alchemical Allocation](alchemical-allocation.md)**: Gain potion’s benefits without consuming it.
- **[Elemental Touch](elemental-touch.md)**: Gain energy damage touch attack.
- **[Fire Breath](fire-breath.md)**: Exhale a cone of flame at will.
- **[Heroic Fortune](heroic-fortune.md)**: Grants the target 1 temporary hero point.
- **[Perceive Cues](perceive-cues.md)**: +5 Perception and Sense Motive 10 min./level.
- **[Transmute Potion to Poison](transmute-potion-to-poison.md)**: Spit poison onto weapon after drinking potion.
- **[Vomit Swarm](vomit-swarm.md)**: Produces a spider swarm that fights for you.

### 3rd-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Absorbing Touch](absorbing-touch.md)**: Caster absorbs an item for 1 day/level.
- **[Amplify Elixir](amplify-elixir.md)**: Empowers or extends the effects of any potion or elixir.
- **[Bloodhound](bloodhound.md)**: Enhances sense of smell and grants the caster the scent special ability.
- **[Draconic Reservoir](draconic-reservoir.md)**: Subject can absorb energy damage and enhance melee attacks with it.
- **[Elemental Aura](elemental-aura.md)**: Creates an aura of energy around you.
- **[Seek Thoughts](seek-thoughts.md)**: Detects thinking creatures’ thoughts.
- **[Thorn Body](thorn-body.md)**: Those attacking you take 1d6 +1 damage/level.

### 4th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Detonate](detonate.md)** (M): Inflicts 1d8/level energy damage to all creatures within 15 ft.
- **[Dragon's Breath](dragons-breath.md)**: Gives you a dragon’s breath weapon.
- **[Fluid Form](fluid-form.md)**: Gain DR 10/slashing, increases reach 10 ft., and breathe water.
- **[Universal Formula](universal-formula.md)** (M): Acts as any extract 3rd level or lower.

### 5th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Delayed Consumption](delayed-consumption.md)**: Extract doesn’t come into effect until you want it to.
- **[Elude Time](elude-time.md)** (M): Puts you in temporary suspended animation.
- **[Planar Adaptation](planar-adaptation.md)**: Resist harmful effects of one plane.
- **[Resurgent Transformation](resurgent-transformation.md)** (M): Gain second wind when close to death.

### 6th-Level Alchemist Spells

- **[Twin Form](twin-form.md)**: Creates controllable duplicate of you.
