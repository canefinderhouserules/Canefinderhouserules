# Spell Immunity, Communal

**School** abjuration; **Level** cleric 5, inquisitor 5, occultist 5, psychic 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creatures touched  
**Duration** 10 min./level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell functions like [*spell immunity*](spell-immunity.md), except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
