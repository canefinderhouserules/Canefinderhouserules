# Rend Body III

**School** evocation; **Level** psychic 7  
**Casting Time** 1 full-round action  
**Components** S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial (see text); **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as *rend body I*, but the target takes 1d8 points of damage per caster level you have (maximum 15d8), and if a limb is ripped from its body, the target is sickened for as long as the bleed damage persists. This spell can be undercast.
