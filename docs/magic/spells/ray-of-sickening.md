# Ray of Sickening

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 1, druid 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, summoner 1, witch 1 This spell functions as ray of exhaustion, except the target is sickened if it fails its save and unaffected if it makes its save.  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a drop of sweat)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** ray  
**Duration** 1 min./level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial, see text; **Spell Resistance** yes
