# Karmic Blessing

**School** divination [good]; **Level** cleric 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Save** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless) The target treats one skill of your choice as a class skill.
