# Summon Monster 5

**School** conjuration (summoning) [see text]; **Level** bard 5, cleric 5, psychic 5, sorcerer/wizard 5, summoner 4, witch 5  
**Casting Time** 1 round (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or more summoned creatures  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md), except that the creature’s CR equals 10 (this spell’s level × 2), up to a maximum of your level.

    **Casting Time** 1 round  
    **Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)

    **Effect** one summoned creature
