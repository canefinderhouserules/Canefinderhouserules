# Necrotic Bolt

**School** evocation; **Level** cleric 1, oracle 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes

Make a ranged touch attack. On a hit, the target takes 1d5 points of negative energy damage per caster level (maximum 5d5). This spell cannot heal.
