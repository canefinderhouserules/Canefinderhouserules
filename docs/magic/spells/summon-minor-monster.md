# Summon Minor Monster

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** antipaladin 1, bard 1, cleric 1, medium 1, psychic 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, summoner 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 round  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** 1d3 summoned creatures  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions as summon monster I, except you can summon 1d3 Tiny or smaller animals, such as bats, lizards, monkeys, rats, ravens, toads, or weasels. The summoned animals must all be the same type of creature. As with animals summoned with summon monster I, you may apply one alignment-appropriate template to these animals.

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any Tiny or smaller animal, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
