# Umbral Infusion, Mass

**School** necromancy [shadow]; **Level** cleric 8, sorcerer/wizard 8, witch 8  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Targets** one mindless undead creature/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart  
**Duration** 1 minute/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [umbral infusion](umbral-infusion.md), except it can affect multiple mindless undead creatures.
