# Bestow Planar Infusion II

**School** evocation; **Level** cleric 4, inquisitor 4, magus 4, medium 4, occultist 4, psychic 4, sorcerer/wizard 4, summoner 4, witch 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 hour/level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes

This functions as [bestow planar infusion I](bestow-planar-infusion-i.md), except that the touched creature gains the improved infusion of the current plane.
