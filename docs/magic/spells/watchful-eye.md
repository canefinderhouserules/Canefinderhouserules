# Watchful Eye

**School** abjuration; **Level** cleric 1, paladin 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, F (a pair of silver or gold rings worth 10 gp, worn by both you and the target)  
**Duration** 1 minute/level

This spell emulates a parent's protection. It functions as [*shield other*](shield-other.md), except as noted above, and you must maintain line of effect to the target.
