# False Life, Greater

**School** necromancy; **Level** alchemist 4, sorcerer/  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a drop of blood)  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 hour/level or until discharged; see text

wizard 4, witch 4 This spell functions as false life, except you gain temporary hit points equal to 2d10 + 1 point per caster level (maximum +20). The effects of this spell do not stack with those of false life.
