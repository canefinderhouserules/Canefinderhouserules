# Occultist Spells

Occultists gain access to the following spells, determined by their chosen implement schools. A spell is not on the occultist’s spell list until he selects its implement school through the implements class feature. Once an implement school has been chosen, all spells on the lists from that school below are considered to be on the occultist’s spell list for the purpose of spell trigger and spell completion magic items. Spells without a link come from other sourcebooks that have not been added to this wiki yet. All spells are cast as psychic spells.
