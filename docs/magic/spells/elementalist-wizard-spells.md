# Elementalist Wizard Spells

Spells available to the elementalist wizard, who specializes in one of the four elemental schools (air, earth, fire, water) described in [Wizard Options and Archetypes](../../characters/classes/wizard-archetypes.md). Each level lists the spells of each elemental school, plus spells available to all elementalists, from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

## 0-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Message](message.md)**: Whisper conversation at distance.

### Earth

- **[Acid Splash](acid-splash.md)**: Orb deals 1d3 acid damage.

### Fire

- **[Spark](spark.md)**: Ignites flammable objects.

### Water

- **[Ray of Frost](ray-of-frost.md)**: Ray deals 1d3 cold damage.

## 1st-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Alter Winds](alter-winds.md)**: Increases/decreases natural wind strength.
- **[Feather Fall](feather-fall.md)**: Objects or creatures fall slowly.
- **[Shocking Grasp](shocking-grasp.md)**: Touch delivers 1d6/level electricity damage (max 5d6).

### Earth

- **[Expeditious Excavation](expeditious-excavation.md)**: Moves 5-ft. cubes of earth.
- **[Grease](grease.md)**: Makes 10-ft. square or one object slippery.
- **[Stone Fist](stone-fist.md)**: Your unarmed strikes are lethal.

### Fire

- **[Burning Hands](burning-hands.md)**: 1d4/level fire damage (max 5d4).
- **[Dancing Lantern](dancing-lantern.md)**: Animates a lantern that follows you.

### Water

- **[Hydraulic Push](hydraulic-push.md)**: Sudden and powerful wave of water bull rushes an enemy.
- **[Obscuring Mist](obscuring-mist.md)**: Fog surrounds you.
- **Touch of the Sea**: Swim speed becomes 30 ft.

## 2nd-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Glide](glide.md)**: No fall damage, move 60 ft./round while falling.
- **[Gust of Wind](gust-of-wind.md)**: Blast of wind blows away or knocks down creatures.
- **[Levitate](levitate.md)**: Subject moves up and down at your direction.
- **[Whispering Wind](whispering-wind.md)**: Sends a short message 1 mile/level.

### Earth

- **[Acid Arrow](acid-arrow.md)**: Ranged touch attack; 2d4 damage for 1 round + 1 round/3 levels.
- **[Create Pit](create-pit.md)** (F): Creates an extradimensional pit.
- **[Glitterdust](glitterdust.md)**: Blinds targets, outlines invisible things.
- **[Shatter](shatter.md)**: Sonic energy damages objects or creatures composed of crystal.
- **[Stone Call](stone-call.md)**: 2d6 damage to all creatures in area.

### Fire

- **[Burning Gaze](burning-gaze.md)**: 1d6 fire damage to target by looking at it.
- **[Fire Breath](fire-breath.md)**: Exhale a cone of flame at will.
- **[Flaming Sphere](flaming-sphere.md)**: Ball of fire deals 3d6 fire damage.
- **[Pyrotechnics](pyrotechnics.md)**: Turns fire into light or thick smoke.
- **[Scorching Ray](scorching-ray.md)**: Ranged touch attack deals 4d6 fire damage, +1 ray/four levels (max 3).

### Water

- **[Accelerate Poison](accelerate-poison.md)**: Hastens targeted poison’s onset.
- **[Fog Cloud](fog-cloud.md)**: Fog obscures vision.
- **[Slipstream](slipstream.md)**: Wave boosts creature’s speed.

### All

- **[Elemental Speech](elemental-speech.md)**: Enables you to speak to elementals and some creatures.
- **[Elemental Touch](elemental-touch.md)**: Gain energy damage touch attack.
- **[Resist Energy](resist-energy.md)**: Ignores first 10 (or more) points of damage per attack from specified energy type.
- **[Summon Monster II](summon-monster-ii.md)**: Summons elemental creature.

## 3rd-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Cloak of Winds](cloak-of-winds.md)**: A screen of strong wind around you.
- **[Fly](fly.md)**: Subject flies at a speed of 60 ft.
- **[Gaseous Form](gaseous-form.md)**: Become insubstantial and fly slowly.
- **[Lightning Bolt](lightning-bolt.md)**: Electricity deals 1d6/level damage.
- **[Wind Wall](wind-wall.md)**: Deflects arrows, smaller creatures, gasses.

### Earth

- **[Shifting Sand](shifting-sand.md)**: Creates difficult terrain, erases tracks.
- **[Spiked Pit](spiked-pit.md)**: As create pit, but filled with spikes.
- **[Stinking Cloud](stinking-cloud.md)**: Nauseating vapors, 1 round/level.

### Fire

- **[Campfire Wall](campfire-wall.md)**: Creates a shelter around a campfire.
- **[Fireball](fireball.md)**: 1d6 fire damage per level, 20-ft. radius.
- **[Flame Arrow](flame-arrow.md)**: Arrows deal +1d6 fire damage.

### Water

- **[Aqueous Orb](aqueous-orb.md)**: Creates rolling sphere of water.
- **[Hydraulic Torrent](hydraulic-torrent.md)**: Creates torrent of water that bull rushes any creature in its path.
- **[Sleet Storm](sleet-storm.md)**: Hampers vision and movement.
- **[Water Breathing](water-breathing.md)**: Subjects can breathe underwater.

### All

- **[Draconic Reservoir](draconic-reservoir.md)**: Subject can absorb energy damage and enhance melee attacks with it.
- **[Elemental Aura](elemental-aura.md)**: Creates an aura of energy around you.
- **[Protection From Energy](protection-from-energy.md)**: Absorbs 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy.

## 4th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Ball Lightning](ball-lightning.md)**: Flying balls of lightning deal 3d6 electricity damage each.
- **[River of Wind](river-of-wind.md)**: A stream of wind causes nonlethal damage and can knock down or push creatures.
- **[Shout](shout.md)**: Deafens all within cone, 5d6 sonic damage.

### Earth

- **[Acid Pit](acid-pit.md)** (M): Creates a pit with acid at the bottom.
- **[Calcific Touch](calcific-touch.md)**: Touch slows target, 1d4 Dex damage.
- **[Stone Shape](stone-shape.md)**: Sculpts stone into any shape.
- **[Stoneskin](stoneskin.md)** (M): Grants DR 10/adamantine.

### Fire

- **[Fire Shield](fire-shield.md)**: Creatures attacking you take fire damage; you’re protected from heat or cold.
- **[Fire Trap](fire-trap.md)** (M): Opened object deals 1d4 damage +1/level.
- **[Firefall](firefall.md)**: Fire bursts upward, dealing 2d6 fire damage.
- **[Wall of Fire](wall-of-fire.md)**: 2d4 fire damage to 10 ft. and 1d4 to 20 ft. Passing through wall deals 2d6 damage +1/level.

### Water

- **[Ice Storm](ice-storm.md)**: Hail deals 5d6 damage in area 40 ft. across.
- **[Solid Fog](solid-fog.md)**: Blocks vision and slows movement.
- **[Wall of Ice](wall-of-ice.md)**: Ice plane creates wall or hemisphere creates dome.

### All

- **[Detonate](detonate.md)**: 1d8/level energy damage to nearby creatures.
- **[Dragon’s Breath](dragons-breath.md)**: Gives you a dragon’s breath weapon.
- **[Elemental Body I](elemental-body-i.md)**: Turns you into a Small elemental.
- **[Summon Monster IV](summon-monster-iv.md)**: Summons elemental creature to fight for you.

## 5th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Overland Flight](overland-flight.md)**: You fly at a speed of 40 ft. and can hustle over long distances.
- **[Suffocation](suffocation.md)**: Target quickly suffocates to death.

### Earth

- **[Hungry Pit](hungry-pit.md)**: As create pit, but dealing 4d6 damage to those in it as it closes.
- **[Passwall](passwall.md)**: Creates passage through wood or stone wall.
- **[Transmute Mud to Rock](transmute-mud-to-rock.md)**: Two 10-ft. cubes per level.
- **[Transmute Rock to Mud](transmute-rock-to-mud.md)**: Two 10-ft. cubes per level.
- **[Wall of Stone](wall-of-stone.md)**: Creates a stone wall that can be shaped.

### Fire

- **[Fire Snake](fire-snake.md)**: Creates a serpentine path of fire 5 ft. long/ level that deals 1d6 fire damage/level.
- **[Geyser](geyser.md)**: Creates a geyser of boiling water.

### Water

- **[Cloudkill](cloudkill.md)**: Kills 3 HD or less; 4–6 HD save or die; 6+ HD take Con damage.
- **[Cone of Cold](cone-of-cold.md)**: 1d6/level cold damage.
- **[Geyser](geyser.md)**: Creates a geyser of boiling water.

### All

- **[Elemental Body II](elemental-body-ii.md)**: You become a Medium elemental.
- **[Planar Adaptation](planar-adaptation.md)**: Resist harmful effects of a plane.
- **[Planar Binding, Lesser](planar-binding-lesser.md)**: Traps elemental creature of 6 HD or less until it performs a task.
- **[Summon Monster V](summon-monster-v.md)**: Summons elemental creature to fight for you.

## 6th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Chain Lightning](chain-lightning.md)**: 1d6/level damage, 1 more bolt/level.
- **[Sirocco](sirocco.md)**: Hot wind does 4d6 damage, fatigues those damaged, and knocks creatures prone.

### Earth

- **[Acid Fog](acid-fog.md)**: Fog deals acid damage.
- **[Flesh to Stone](flesh-to-stone.md)**: Turns subject creature into statue.
- **[Move Earth](move-earth.md)**: Digs trenches and builds hills.
- **[Stone to Flesh](stone-to-flesh.md)**: Restores petrified creature.
- **[Wall of Iron](wall-of-iron.md)** (M): 30 hp/four levels; can topple onto foes.

### Fire

- **[Contagious Flame](contagious-flame.md)**: Rays do 4d6 fire damage, then move on to new targets.
- **[Sirocco](sirocco.md)**: Hot wind does 4d6 damage, fatigues those damaged, and knocks creatures prone.

### Water

- **[Fluid Form](fluid-form.md)**: Gain DR 10/slashing, increases reach 10 ft., and breathe water.
- **[Freezing Sphere](freezing-sphere.md)**: Freezes water or deals cold damage.

### All

- **[Elemental Body III](elemental-body-iii.md)**: Turns you into a Large elemental.
- **[Planar Binding](planar-binding.md)**: As lesser planar binding, but up to 12 HD.
- **[Summon Monster VI](summon-monster-vi.md)**: Summons elemental creature to fight for you.

## 7th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Control Weather](control-weather.md)**: Changes weather in local area.
- **[Fly, Mass](fly-mass.md)**: One creature/level gains ability to fly.

### Earth

- **[Rampart](rampart.md)**: Creates 5-ft. thick earthen barrier.
- **[Reverse Gravity](reverse-gravity.md)**: Objects and creatures fall upward.
- **[Statue](statue.md)**: Subject can become a statue at will.

### Fire

- **[Delayed Blast Fireball](delayed-blast-fireball.md)**: 1d6/level fire damage; you can postpone blast for up to 5 rounds.
- **[Firebrand](firebrand.md)**: Allies gain flaming weapons, immunity to your fire spells, and a one-use ray of fire attack.

### Water

- **[Control Weather](control-weather.md)**: Changes weather in local area.
- **Vortex**: Creates a whirlpool in water.

### All

- **[Elemental Body IV](elemental-body-iv.md)**: Turns you into a Huge elemental.
- **[Planar Adaptation, Mass](planar-adaptation-mass.md)**: As planar adaptation, but affects multiple creatures.
- **[Summon Monster VII](summon-monster-vii.md)**: Summons elemental creature to fight for you.

## 8th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Shout, Greater](shout-greater.md)**: Devastating yell deals 10d6 sonic damage; stuns creatures.
- **[Stormbolts](stormbolts.md)**: 1d8 damage/level (max 20d8) to targets.

### Earth

- **[Iron Body](iron-body.md)**: Your body becomes living iron.
- **Wall of Lava**: Wall damages foes that try to enter, periodically launches lava at nearby targets.

### Fire

- **[Incendiary Cloud](incendiary-cloud.md)**: Deals 6d6 fire damage/round.
- **Wall of Lava**: Wall damages foes that try to enter, periodically launches lava at nearby targets.

### Water

- **[Horrid Wilting](horrid-wilting.md)**: Deals 1d6/level damage within 30 ft.
- **[Polar Ray](polar-ray.md)**: Ranged touch attack deals 1d6/level cold damage and 1d4 points of Dexterity drain.
- **[Seamantle](seamantle.md)**: Sheathes you in protective water.

### All

- **[Planar Binding, Greater](planar-binding-greater.md)**: As lesser planar binding, but up to 18 HD.
- **[Summon Monster VIII](summon-monster-viii.md)**: Summons elemental creature to fight for you.

## 9th-Level Elementalist Wizard Spells

### Air

- **[Suffocation, Mass](suffocation-mass.md)**: One creature/level suffocates.
- **Winds of Vengeance**: Gives you the ability to fly and attack with wind.

### Earth

- **[Clashing Rocks](clashing-rocks.md)**: 20d6 damage to target creature.
- **World Wave**: Earth moves you across distances.

### Fire

- **[Fiery Body](fiery-body.md)**: You gain various fire-related powers.
- **[Meteor Swarm](meteor-swarm.md)**: Four meteors each deal 6d6 fire damage.

### Water

- **Tsunami**: Wave damages and sweeps up all in its path.
- **World Wave**: Earth moves you across distances.

### All

- **[Gate](gate.md)** (M): Connects two planes for travel or summoning.
