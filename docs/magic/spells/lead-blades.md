# Lead Blades

**School** transmutation; **Level** cleric 2, druid 1, oracle 2, ranger 1, warpriest 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal; touch for clerics and oracles  
**Target** you; creature touched for clerics and oracles  
**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)

Lead blades increases the momentum and density of the target’s melee weapons just as they strike a foe. All melee weapons the target is carrying when the spell is cast deal damage as if one size category larger than they actually are. For instance, a Medium longsword normally deals 1d8 points of damage, but it would instead deal 2d6 points of damage if benefiting from lead blades (see the Core Rulebook for more information). Only the target of the spell can benefit from it. If anyone else uses one of those weapons to make an attack it deals damage as normal for its size.
