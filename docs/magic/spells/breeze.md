# Breeze

**School** evocation [air]; **Level** sorcerer/wizard 0  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a miniature fan)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature or object  
**Duration** 1 hour (D)  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes

You create a light wind that blows against the target, from a direction of your choice. The breeze grants the subject a +2 bonus on saves against very hot conditions, severe heat, breath weapons, and saves against cloud vapors and gases (such as [*cloudkill*](cloudkill.md),* stinking cloud*, and inhaled poisons). This spell does not function without air or underwater.

You can only have one breeze active at any one time. If you cast this spell while another casting is still in effect, the previous casting is dispelled.

These cantrips are not legal in explorers’ society play. Note that [*penumbra*](penumbra.md) was changed into the higher-level spell* protective penumbra*. The cantrip version is similar, but does not replace its higher-level counterpart.
