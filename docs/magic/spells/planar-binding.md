# Planar Binding

**School** conjuration (calling) [see text for *lesser planar binding*];  
**Level** sorcerer/wizard 6

**Components**: V, S **Targets** up to three elementals or outsiders, totaling no more

than 12 HD, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart

when they appear This spell functions like *lesser planar binding,* except that you may call a single creature of 12 HD or less, or up to three creatures of the same kind whose Hit Dice total no more than 12. Each creature gets a saving throw, makes an independent attempt to escape, and must be individually persuaded to aid you.
