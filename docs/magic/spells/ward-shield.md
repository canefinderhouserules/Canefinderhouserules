# Ward Shield

**School** abjuration; **Level** cleric 4, inquisitor 4, magus 4, sorcerer/wizard 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** shield touched  
**Duration** 1 minute/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

With a touch, you enchant a shield and enable it to protect its wielder from hostile spells. The shield grants spell resistance 10 + your caster level against spells so long as the shield can be interposed between the wielder and the spell effect. A *ward shield* does not provide spell resistance against area effect spells such as *fireball*, but does provide a +5 bonus on Reflex saving throws against such spells.
