# Divine Favor

**School** evocation; **Level** cleric 1, inquisitor 1, paladin 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** personal or touch  
**Target** you or creature touched  
**Duration** 1 minute

Calling upon the strength and wisdom of a deity, the target gains a +1 luck bonus on attack and weapon damage rolls for every three caster levels you have (at least +1, maximum +3). The bonus doesn’t apply to spell damage.

You can cast this spell on another creature whose alignment falls within the range accepted by your deity (or whoever grants your powers). When you do, the spell has a range of touch.
