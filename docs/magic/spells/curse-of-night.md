# Curse of Night

**School** evocation [curse, darkness]; **Level** cleric 8, druid 8, sorcerer/wizard 8, witch 8  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, F (jet gemstones worth a total of 10,000 gp)  
**Range** touch  
**Area** 1-mile radius emanating from the touched point  
**Duration** permanent (D)  
**Saving Throw** none (see curse text); **Spell Resistance** no

By touching the ground, you curse an area to remain in bleak darkness, as the endless night curse. The focus merges into the cursed area as part of the spell and can only be retrieved if the curse is lifted.
