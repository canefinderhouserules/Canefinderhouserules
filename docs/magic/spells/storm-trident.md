# Storm Trident

**School** evocation [electricity]; **Level** cleric 2, druid 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** 0 ft.  
**Effect** trident-like bolt of electricity  
**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes

A 4-foot-long, blazing, forked bolt of electricity springs forth from your hand. You wield this spear-like bolt as if it were a trident (you are considered proficient with the bolt). Attacks with the storm trident are melee touch attacks. The bolt deals 1d8 points of electricity damage + 1 point per 2 caster levels (maximum +10). Since the bolt is immaterial, your Strength modifier does not apply to the damage. The bolt can ignite combustible materials such as parchment, straw, dry sticks, and cloth.
