# Sun’s Disdain

**School** transmutation [curse]; **Level** bloodrager 2, cleric 2, inquisitor 2, shaman 2, sorcerer/wizard 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (a piece of glass)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** permanent  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

You alter a creature so the touch of the sun is hostile to it. The cursed creature gains light blindness and is [blinded](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#blinded) for 1 round if exposed to bright light, such as sunlight or the [*daylight*](daylight.md) spell. The cursed creature is [dazzled](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazzled) as long as it remains in areas of bright light, and it is treated as being particularly susceptible to sunlight for the purposes of resolving spells like [*searing light*](searing-light.md), [*sunbeam*](sunbeam.md), and [*sunburst*](sunburst.md).
