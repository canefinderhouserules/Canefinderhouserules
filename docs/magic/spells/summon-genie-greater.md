# Summon Genie, Greater

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** cleric 6, sorcerer/wizard 6, summoner 5, witch 6  
**Casting Time** 1 round  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (an oil lamp worth at least 50 gp)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or more summoned genies  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like *summon genie, lesser*, except you can summon one shaitan, 1d3 djinn, or 1d4+1 janni.

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any genie, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
