# Heal

**School** conjuration (healing); **Level** cleric 6, druid 7  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

*Heal* enables you to channel positive energy into a creature to wipe away injury and afflictions. It immediately ends any and all of the following adverse conditions affecting the target: ability damage, [blinded](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#blinded), [confused](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#confused)*,* [dazed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazed), [dazzled](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazzled), [deafened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#deafened), diseased, [exhausted](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#exhausted), [fatigued](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fatigued), feebleminded*,* insanity, [nauseated](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#nauseated), poisoned, [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened), and [stunned](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#stunned). It also cures 10 hit points of damage per level of the caster, to a maximum of 150 points at 15th level.

*Heal* does not remove negative levels or restore permanently drained ability score points.

If used against an undead creature, *heal* instead acts like [*harm*](harm.md).
