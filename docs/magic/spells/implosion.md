**School** evocation; **Level** cleric 9

**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)

**Duration** concentration (up to 1 round per 2 levels)  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes
