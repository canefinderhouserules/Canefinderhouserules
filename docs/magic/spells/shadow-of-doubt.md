# Shadow of Doubt

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [darkness, emotion, evil, mind-affecting]; **Level** antipaladin 4, bard 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Targets** one creature/3 levels  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Will partial (see text); **Spell Resistance** yes

You create a shadowy aura of doubt fueled by a foe’s own inner flaws. Targets perceive all illumination as if it were one light level darker, to a minimum light level of darkness (not supernatural darkness), even on a successful saving throw.

Fleeting mental shadows of regrets and anxieties distract the spell’s targets; at the start of each of their turns, they must each succeed at a Will save or be [staggered](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round. A good-aligned target takes a –4 penalty on saving throws against this effect; an evil-aligned target gains a +4 bonus instead.
