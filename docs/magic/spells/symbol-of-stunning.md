# Symbol of Stunning

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** cleric 7, sorcerer/wizard 7  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 5,000 gp)  
**Range** 0 ft.; see text  
**Effect** one symbol  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like *symbol of death*, except that all creatures within 60 feet of a *symbol of stunning* instead become stunned for 1d6 rounds.

*Note*: Magic traps such as *symbol of stunning* are hard to detect and disable. A character with trapfinding can use the Perception skill to find a *symbol of stunning* and Disable Device to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 32 for *symbol of stunning*.
