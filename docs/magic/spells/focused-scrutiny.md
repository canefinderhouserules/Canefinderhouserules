# Focused Scrutiny

**School** divination; **Level** alchemist 2, bard 2, inquisitor 2, medium 2, mesmerist 2, psychic 2, shaman 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 10 minutes /level (D)

You gain heightened sensitivity to the mannerisms, reactions, and body language of one creature you can perceive, allowing you to sense its state of mind and easily influence it. You gain a +10 bonus on [Perception](../../rules/skills/perception.md), [Sense Motive](../../rules/skills/sense-motive.md), and [Survival](../../rules/skills/survival.md) checks attempted against the target creature, as well as a +5 bonus on [Diplomacy](../../rules/skills/diplomacy.md) and [Intimidate](../../rules/skills/intimidate.md) checks attempted against it.
