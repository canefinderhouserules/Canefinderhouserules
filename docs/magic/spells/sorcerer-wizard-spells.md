# Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

## 0-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells (Cantrips)

**Abjuration**

- **[Chameleon Scales](chameleon-scales.md)**: Change the color of your skin or scales.
- **[Detect Fiendish Presence](detect-fiendish-presence.md)**: Detects evil outsiders and their servants.
- **[Haunted Fey Aspect](haunted-fey-aspect.md)**: You surround yourself with disturbing illusions.
- **[Oath of Anonymity](oath-of-anonymity.md)**: Magically conceal your identity behind an alternate persona.
- **[Resistance](resistance.md)**: Subject gains +1 on saving throws.

**Conjuration**

- **[Acid Splash](acid-splash.md)**: Orb deals 1d3 acid damage.

**Divination**

- **[Detect Magic](detect-magic.md)**: Detects all spells and magic items within 60 ft.
- **[Detect Poison](detect-poison.md)**: Detects poison in one creature or small object.
- **[Read Magic](read-magic.md)**: Read scrolls and spellbooks.

**Enchantment**

- **[Daze](daze.md)**: A single humanoid creature with 4 HD or less loses its next action.

**Evocation**

- **[Dancing Lights](dancing-lights.md)**: Creates torches or other lights.
- **[Flare](flare.md)**: Dazzles one creature (–1 on attack rolls).
- **[Light](light.md)**: Object shines like a torch.
- **[Ray of Frost](ray-of-frost.md)**: Ray deals 1d3 cold damage.

**Illusion**

- **[Ghost Sound](ghost-sound.md)**: Figment sounds.

**Necromancy**

- **[Bleed](bleed.md)**: Cause a stabilized creature to resume dying.
- **[Disrupt Undead](disrupt-undead.md)**: Deals 1d6 damage to one undead.
- **[Touch of Fatigue](touch-of-fatigue.md)**: Touch attack fatigues target.

**Transmutation**

- **[Mage Hand](mage-hand.md)**: 5-pound telekinesis.
- **[Mending](mending.md)**: Makes minor repairs on an object.
- **[Message](message.md)**: Whisper conversation at distance.
- **[Open/Close](open-close.md)**: Opens or closes small or light things.

**Universal**

- **[Arcane Mark](arcane-mark.md)**: Inscribes a personal rune on an object or creature (visible or invisible).
- **[Prestidigitation](prestidigitation.md)**: Performs minor tricks.
- **[Vacuous Vessel](vacuous-vessel.md)**: A container looks and feels empty.
