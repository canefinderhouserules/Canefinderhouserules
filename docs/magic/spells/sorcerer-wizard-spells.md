# Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

## 0-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells (Cantrips)

**Abjuration**

- **Resistance**: Subject gains +1 on saving throws.

**Conjuration**

- **[Acid Splash](acid-splash.md)**: Orb deals 1d3 acid damage.

**Divination**

- **[Detect Magic](detect-magic.md)**: Detects all spells and magic items within 60 ft.
- **[Detect Poison](detect-poison.md)**: Detects poison in one creature or small object.
- **Read Magic**: Read scrolls and spellbooks.

**Enchantment**

- **[Daze](daze.md)**: A single humanoid creature with 4 HD or less loses its next action.

**Evocation**

- **[Dancing Lights](dancing-lights.md)**: Creates torches or other lights.
- **[Flare](flare.md)**: Dazzles one creature (–1 on attack rolls).
- **Light**: Object shines like a torch.
- **Ray of Frost**: Ray deals 1d3 cold damage.

**Illusion**

- **[Ghost Sound](ghost-sound.md)**: Figment sounds.

**Necromancy**

- **[Bleed](bleed.md)**: Cause a stabilized creature to resume dying.
- **[Disrupt Undead](disrupt-undead.md)**: Deals 1d6 damage to one undead.
- **Touch of Fatigue**: Touch attack fatigues target.

**Transmutation**

- **Mage Hand**: 5-pound telekinesis.
- **Mending**: Makes minor repairs on an object.
- **Message**: Whisper conversation at distance.
- **Open/Close**: Opens or closes small or light things.

**Universal**

- **[Arcane Mark](arcane-mark.md)**: Inscribes a personal rune on an object or creature (visible or invisible).
- **Prestidigitation**: Performs minor tricks.

## 1st-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

**Abjuration**

- **[Alarm](alarm.md)**: Wards an area for 2 hours/level.
- **[Endure Elements](endure-elements.md)**: Exist comfortably in hot or cold regions.
- **[Hold Portal](hold-portal.md)**: Holds door shut.
- **Protection from Chaos/Evil/Good/Law**: +2 to AC and saves, plus additional protection against selected alignment.
- **Shield**: Invisible disc gives +4 to AC, blocks *magic missiles.*

**Conjuration**

- **[Grease](grease.md)**: Makes 10-ft. square or one object slippery.
- **Mage Armor**: Gives subject +4 armor bonus.
- **Mount**: Summons riding horse for 2 hours/level.
- **Obscuring Mist**: Fog surrounds you.
- **Summon Monster I**: Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
- **Unseen Servant**: Invisible force obeys your commands.

**Divination**

- **[Comprehend Languages](comprehend-languages.md)**: You understand all spoken and written languages.
- **[Detect Secret Doors](detect-secret-doors.md)**: Reveals hidden doors within 60 ft.
- **[Detect Undead](detect-undead.md)**: Reveals undead within 60 ft.
- **[Identify](identify.md)**: Gives +10 bonus to identify magic items.
- **True Strike**: +20 on your next attack roll.

**Enchantment**

- **[Charm Person](charm-person.md)**: Makes one person your friend.
- **[Hypnotism](hypnotism.md)**: Fascinates 2d4 HD of creatures.
- **Sleep**: Puts 4 HD of creatures into magical slumber.

**Evocation**

- **[Burning Hands](burning-hands.md)**: 1d4/level fire damage (max 5d4).
- **[Floating Disk](floating-disk.md)**: Creates 3-ft.-diameter horizontal disk that holds 100 lbs./level.
- **Magic Missile**: 1d4+1 damage; +1 missile per two levels above 1st (max 5).
- **Shocking Grasp**: Touch delivers 1d6/level electricity damage (max 5d6).

**Illusion**

- **[Color Spray](color-spray.md)**: Knocks unconscious, blinds, and/or stuns weak creatures.
- **[Disguise Self](disguise-self.md)**: Changes your appearance.
- **Magic Aura**: Alters object’s magic aura.
- **Silent Image**: Creates minor illusion of your design.
- **Ventriloquism**: Throws voice for 1 min./level.

**Necromancy**

- **[Cause Fear](cause-fear.md)**: One creature of 5 HD or less flees for 1d4 rounds.
- **[Chill Touch](chill-touch.md)**: One touch/level deals 1d6 damage and possibly 1 Str damage.
- **Ray of Enfeeblement**: Ray causes 1d6 Str penalty + 1 per 2 levels.

**Transmutation**

- **[Animate Rope](animate-rope.md)**: Makes a rope move at your command.
- **[Enlarge Person](enlarge-person.md)**: Humanoid creature doubles in size.
- **[Erase](erase.md)**: Mundane or magical writing vanishes.
- **[Expeditious Retreat](expeditious-retreat.md)**: Your base speed increases by 30 ft.
- **[Feather Fall](feather-fall.md)**: Objects or creatures fall slowly.
- **Jump**: Subject gets bonus on Acrobatics checks.
- **Magic Weapon**: Weapon gains +1 bonus.
- **Reduce Person**: Humanoid creature halves in size.

**Enchantment**

- **[Hideous Laughter](hideous-laughter.md)**: Subject loses actions for 1 round/level.

**Evocation**

- **[Darkness](darkness.md)**: 20-ft. radius of supernatural shadow.
- **[Flaming Sphere](flaming-sphere.md)**: Rolling ball of fire deals 3d6 fire damage.
- **[Gust of Wind](gust-of-wind.md)**: Blows away or knocks down smaller creatures.
- **Scorching Ray**: Ranged touch attack deals 4d6 fire damage, + 1 ray/four levels (max 3).
- **Shatter**: Sonic energy damages objects or crystalline creatures.

**Illusion**

- **[Blur](blur.md)**: Attacks miss subject 20% of the time.
- **[Hypnotic Pattern](hypnotic-pattern.md)**: Fascinates 2d4 + level HD of creatures.
- **Invisibility**: Subject is invisible for 1 min./level or until it attacks.
- **Magic Mouth**: Object speaks once when triggered.
- **Minor Image**: As *silent image*, plus some sound.
- **Mirror Image**: Creates decoy duplicates of you.
- **Misdirection**: Misleads divinations for 1 creature or object.
- **Phantom Trap**: Makes item seem trapped.

**Necromancy**

- **[Blindness/Deafness](blindness-deafness.md)**: Makes subject blinded or deafened.
- **[Command Undead](command-undead.md)**: Undead creature obeys your commands.
- **[False Life](false-life.md)**: Gain 1d10 temporary hp + 1/level (max +10).
- **[Ghoul Touch](ghoul-touch.md)**: Paralyzes one subject, which exudes stench that makes those nearby sickened.
- **Scare**: Frightens creatures of less than 6 HD.
- **Spectral Hand**: Creates disembodied glowing hand to deliver touch attacks.

**Transmutation**

- **[Alter Self](alter-self.md)**: Assume form of a Small or Medium humanoid.
- **[Bear’s Endurance](bears-endurance.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Con for 1 min./ level.
- **[Bull’s Strength](bulls-strength.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Str for 1 min./level.
- **[Cat’s Grace](cats-grace.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Dex for 1 min./level.
- **[Darkvision](darkvision.md)**: See 60 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Eagle’s Splendor](eagles-splendor.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Cha for 1 min./level.
- **[Fox’s Cunning](foxs-cunning.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Int for 1 min./level.
- **Knock**: Opens locked or magically sealed door.
- **Levitate**: Subject moves up and down at your direction.
- **Make Whole**: Repairs an object.
- **Owl’s Wisdom**: Subject gains +4 to Wis for 1 min./level.
- **Pyrotechnics**: Turns fire into blinding light or thick smoke.
- **Rope Trick**: As many as eight creatures hide in extradimensional space.
- **Spider Climb**: Grants ability to walk on walls and ceilings.
- **Whispering Wind**: Sends a short message 1 mile/level.

## 3rd-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

**Abjuration**

- **[Dispel Magic](dispel-magic.md)**: Cancels one magical spell or effect.
- **[Explosive Runes](explosive-runes.md)**: Deals 6d6 damage when read.
- **Magic Circle against Chaos/Evil/Good/Law**: As *protection* spells, but 10-ft. radius and 10 min./level.
- **Nondetection**: Hides subject from divination, scrying.
- **Protection from Energy**: Absorbs 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy.

**Conjuration**

- **Phantom Steed**: Magic horse appears for 1 hour/level.
- **Sepia Snake Sigil**: Creates text symbol that immobilizes reader.
- **Sleet Storm**: Hampers vision and movement.
- **Stinking Cloud**: Nauseating vapors, 1 round/level.
- **Summon Monster III**: Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.

**Divination**

- **[Arcane Sight](arcane-sight.md)**: Magical auras become visible to you.
- **[Clairaudience/Clairvoyance](clairaudience-clairvoyance.md)**: Hear or see at a distance for 1 min./level.
- **Tongues**: Speak and understand any language.

**Enchantment**

- **[Deep Slumber](deep-slumber.md)**: Puts 10 HD of creatures to sleep.
- **[Heroism](heroism.md)**: Gives +2 bonus on attack rolls, saves, skill checks.
- **[Hold Person](hold-person.md)**: Paralyzes one humanoid for 1 round/level.
- **Rage**: Gives +2 to Str and Con, +1 on Will saves, –2 to AC.
- **Suggestion**: Compels a subject to follow stated course of action.

**Evocation**

- **[Daylight](daylight.md)**: 60-ft. radius of bright light.
- **[Fireball](fireball.md)**: 1d6 damage per level, 20-ft. radius.
- **Lightning Bolt**: Electricity deals 1d6/level damage.
- **Tiny Hut**: Creates shelter for 10 creatures.
- **Wind Wall**: Deflects arrows, smaller creatures, and gases.

**Illusion**

- **[Displacement](displacement.md)**: Attacks miss subject 50% of the time.
- **[Illusory Script](illusory-script.md)**: Only select creatures can read text.
- **Invisibility Sphere**: Makes everyone within 10 ft. invisible.
- **Major Image**: As *silent image*, plus sound, smell and thermal effects.

**Necromancy**

- **[Gentle Repose](gentle-repose.md)**: Preserves one corpse.
- **[Halt Undead](halt-undead.md)**: Immobilizes undead for 1 round/level.
- **Ray of Exhaustion**: Ray makes subject exhausted.
- **Vampiric Touch**: Touch deals 1d6 damage per two levels; caster gains damage as temporary hp.

**Transmutation**

- **[Beast Shape I](beast-shape-i.md)**: You take the form and some of the powers of a Small or Medium animal.
- **[Blink](blink.md)**: You randomly vanish and reappear for 1 round per level.
- **[Flame Arrow](flame-arrow.md)**: Arrows deal +1d6 fire damage.
- **[Fly](fly.md)**: Subject flies at speed of 60 ft.
- **[Gaseous Form](gaseous-form.md)**: Subject becomes insubstantial and can fly slowly.
- **[Haste](haste.md)**: One creature/level moves faster, +1 on attack rolls, AC, and Reflex saves.
- **Keen Edge**: Doubles normal weapon’s threat range.
- **Magic Weapon, Greater**: Weapon gains +1 bonus/four levels (max +5).
- **Secret Page**: Changes one page to hide its real content.
- **Shrink Item**: Object shrinks to one-sixteenth size.
- **Slow**: One subject/level takes only one action/round, –1 to AC, Reflex saves, and attack rolls.
- **Water Breathing**: Subjects can breathe underwater.

**Abjuration**

- **[Detect Scrying](detect-scrying.md)**: Alerts you to magical eavesdropping
- **Locate Creature**: Indicates direction to familiar creature.
- **Scrying**: Spies on subject from a distance.

**Enchantment**

- **[Charm Monster](charm-monster.md)**: Makes monster believe it is your ally.
- **[Confusion](confusion.md)**: Subjects behave oddly for 1 round/level.
- **[Crushing Despair](crushing-despair.md)**: Subjects take –2 on attack rolls, damage rolls, saves, and checks.

**Evocation**

- **Shout**: Deafens all within cone and deals 5d6 sonic damage.
- **Wall of Fire**: Deals 2d4 fire damage out to 10 ft. and 1d4 out to 20 ft. Passing through wall deals 2d6 damage + 1/level.

**Illusion**

- **[Hallucinatory Terrain](hallucinatory-terrain.md)**: Makes one type of terrain appear like another (field as forest, or the like).
- **[Illusory Wall](illusory-wall.md)**: Wall, floor, or ceiling looks real, but anything can pass through.
- **Invisibility, Greater**: As *invisibility,* but subject can attack and stay invisible.
- **Phantasmal Killer**: Fearsome illusion kills subject or deals 3d6 damage.
- **Rainbow Pattern**: Lights fascinate 24 HD of creatures.
- **Shadow Conjuration**: Mimics conjuration below 4th level, but only 20% real.

**Necromancy**

- **[Animate Dead](animate-dead.md)**: Creates undead skeletons and zombies out of corpses.
- **[Bestow Curse](bestow-curse.md)**: –6 to an ability score; –4 on attack rolls, saves, and checks; or 50% chance of losing each action.
- **[Contagion](contagion.md)**: Infects subject with chosen disease.
- **[Enervation](enervation.md)**: Subject gains 1d4 negative levels.
- **[Fear](fear.md)**: Subjects within cone flee for 1 round/level.

**Transmutation**

- **[Beast Shape II](beast-shape-ii.md)**: You take the form and some of the powers of a Tiny or Large animal.
- **[Elemental Body I](elemental-body-i.md)**: Turns you into a Small elemental.
- **[Enlarge Person, Mass](enlarge-person-mass.md)**: 1 humanoid creature/level doubles in size.
- **Mnemonic Enhancer**: *Wizard only.* Prepare extra spells or retain one just cast.
- **Reduce Person, Mass**: As *reduce person*, but affects 1 humanoid creature/level.
- **Stone Shape**: Sculpts stone into any shape.

**Transmutation**

- **Statue**: Subject can become a statue at will.

**Universal**

- **Limited Wish**: Alters reality (within limits).

## 8th-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

**Abjuration**

- **[Dimensional Lock](dimensional-lock.md)**: Teleportation and interplanar travel blocked for 1 day/level.
- **Mind Blank**: Subject is protected from mental/emotional magic and scrying.
- **Prismatic Wall**: Wall’s colors have array of effects.
- **Protection from Spells**: Confers +8 resistance bonus.

**Conjuration**

- **Incendiary Cloud**: Cloud deals 6d6 fire damage/round.
- **Maze**: Traps subject in extradimensional maze.
- **Planar Binding, Greater**: As *lesser planar binding,* but up to 18 HD.
- **Summon Monster VIII**: Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
- **Trap the Soul**: Imprisons subject within gem.

**Divination**

- **[Discern Location](discern-location.md)**: Reveals exact location of creature or object.
- **Moment of Prescience**: You gain +1/level insight bonus on single attack roll, check, or save.
- **Prying Eyes, Greater**: As *prying eyes*, but eyes have *true seeing*.

**Enchantment**

- **[Antipathy](antipathy.md)**: Object or location affected by spell repels certain creatures.
- **[Binding](binding.md)**: Utilizes an array of techniques to imprison a creature.
- **[Charm Monster, Mass](charm-monster-mass.md)**: As *charm monster,* but all within 30 ft.
- **[Demand](demand.md)**: As *sending,* plus you can send *suggestion.*
- **Irresistible Dance**: Forces subject to dance.
- **Power Word Stun**: Stuns creature with 150 hp or less.
- **Symbol of Insanity**: Triggered rune renders nearby creatures insane.
- **Sympathy**: Object or location attracts certain creatures.

**Evocation**

- **[Clenched Fist](clenched-fist.md)**: Large hand provides cover, pushes, or attacks your foes.
- **Polar Ray**: Ranged touch attack deals 1d6/level cold damage and 1d4 points of Dexterity drain.
- **Shout, Greater**: Devastating yell deals 10d6 sonic damage; stuns creatures.
- **Sunburst**: Blinds all within 10 ft., deals 6d6 damage.
- **Telekinetic Sphere**: As *resilient sphere,* but you move the sphere telekinetically.

**Illusion**

- **Scintillating Pattern**: Twisting colors *confuse,* stun, or render unconscious.
- **Screen**: Illusion hides area from vision and scrying.
- **Shadow Evocation, Greater**: As *shadow evocation,* but up to 7th level and 60% real.

**Necromancy**

- **[Clone](clone.md)**: Duplicate awakens when original dies.
- **[Create Greater Undead](create-greater-undead.md)**: Creates shadows, wraiths, spectres, or devourers.
- **[Horrid Wilting](horrid-wilting.md)**: Deals 1d6/level damage within 30 ft.
- **Symbol of Death**: Triggered rune kills nearby creatures.

**Transmutation**

- **[Form of the Dragon III](form-of-the-dragon-iii.md)**: Turns you into a Huge dragon.
- **[Giant Form II](giant-form-ii.md)**: Turns you into a Huge giant.
- **Iron Body**: Your body becomes living iron.
- **Polymorph Any Object**: Changes a subject into anything else.
- **Temporal Stasis**: Puts subject into suspended animation.

## 9th-Level Sorcerer/Wizard Spells

**Abjuration**

- **[Freedom](freedom.md)**: Releases creature from *imprisonment.*
- **Imprisonment**: Entombs subject beneath the earth.
- **Mage’s Disjunction**: Dispels magic, disenchants magic items.
- **Prismatic Sphere**: As *prismatic wall*, but surrounds on all sides.

**Conjuration**

- **[Gate](gate.md)**: Connects two planes for travel or summoning.
- **Refuge**: Alters item to transport its possessor to your abode.
- **Summon Monster IX**: Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
- **Teleportation Circle**: Teleports creatures inside circle.

**Divination**

- **[Foresight](foresight.md)**: “Sixth sense” warns of impending danger.

**Enchantment**

- **[Dominate Monster](dominate-monster.md)**: As *dominate person,* but any creature.
- **[Hold Monster, Mass](hold-monster-mass.md)**: As *hold monster,* but all within 30 ft.
- **Power Word Kill**: Kills one creature with 100 hp or less.

**Evocation**

- **[Crushing Hand](crushing-hand.md)**: Large hand provides cover, pushes, or crushes your foes.
- **Meteor Swarm**: Four exploding spheres each deal 6d6 fire damage.

**Illusion**

- **Shades**: As *shadow conjuration*, but up to 8th level and 80% real.
- **Weird**: As *phantasmal killer,* but affects all within 30 ft.

**Necromancy**

- **[Astral Projection](astral-projection.md)**: Projects you and companions onto Astral Plane.
- **[Energy Drain](energy-drain.md)**: Subject gains 2d4 negative levels.
- **Soul Bind**: Traps newly dead soul to prevent *resurrection*.
- **Wail of the Banshee**: Deals 10 damage/level to 1 creature/level.

**Transmutation**

- **[Etherealness](etherealness.md)**: Travel to Ethereal Plane with companions.
- **Shapechange**: Transforms you into certain creatures, and lets you change forms once per round.
- **Time Stop**: You act freely for 1d4+1 rounds.

**Universal**

- **Wish**: As *limited wish,* but with fewer limits.
