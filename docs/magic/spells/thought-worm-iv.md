# Thought Worm IV

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** psychic 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Will partial (see text); **Spell Resistance** yes

This functions as *thought worm III*, except that the penalty on Will saves is –6 and a target that succeeds at its Will save still takes a –3 penalty on Will saves against your mind-affecting spells and effects for the same duration. This spell can be undercast.
