# Rend Body IV

**School** evocation; **Level** psychic 8  
**Casting Time** 1 full-round action  
**Components** S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial (see text); **Spell Resistance** yes

This functions as *rend body III*, but the target takes 1d8 points of damage per caster level (maximum 20d8), and one of the target's limbs is ripped off if the amount of damage dealt is greater than 1/4 of its maximum hit point total. This spell can be undercast.
