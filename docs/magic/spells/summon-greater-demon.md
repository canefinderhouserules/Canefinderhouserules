# Summon Greater Demon

**School** conjuration (summoning) [chaotic, evil]; **Level** cleric 9, sorcerer/wizard 9, witch 9  
**Casting Time** 1 round (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one summoned creature  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like *summon monster*, except it allows you to summon a single coloxus, an omox demon, or 1d3 kalavakus demons.

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any demon, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
