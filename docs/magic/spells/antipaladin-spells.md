# Antipaladin Spells

New antipaladin spells from *Ultimate Magic*. Spells from other books will be added as they are transcribed. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

## 1st-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Demand Offering](demand-offering.md)**: Make a creature give you an object it’s holding.
- **[Litany of Sloth](litany-of-sloth.md)**: Single target cannot make attacks of opportunity for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Weakness](litany-of-weakness.md)**: Single target is fatigued for 1 round.
- **[Longshot](longshot.md)**: Grants a +10-foot bonus to the range increment for any ranged weapon fired.
- **[Murderous Command](murderous-command.md)**: Target is compelled to kill its ally.
- **[Summon Minor Monster](summon-minor-monster.md)**: Summon 1d3 Tiny animals.
- **[Wartrain Mount](wartrain-mount.md)**: Animal gains the combat training general purpose.

## 2nd-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Aquatic Cavalry](aquatic-cavalry.md)**: Summon hippocampi to serve as aquatic mounts.
- **[Bestow Weapon Proficiency](bestow-weapon-proficiency.md)**: Grants a creature proficiency in a single weapon for short period of time.
- **[Bleed for Your Master](bleed-for-your-master.md)**: Compel a companion to take damage for you.
- **[Inflict Pain](inflict-pain.md)**: Target takes a –4 penalty on attack rolls, skill checks, and ability checks.
- **[Litany of Defense](litany-of-defense.md)**: Doubles armor’s enhancement bonus.
- **[Litany of Eloquence](litany-of-eloquence.md)**: Fascinates a single creature for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Entanglement](litany-of-entanglement.md)**: Entangles a creature for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Warding](litany-of-warding.md)**: You gain two additional attacks of opportunity for 1 round.
- **[Magic Siege Engine](magic-siege-engine.md)**: Siege engine gains +1 on targeting and damage rolls.
- **[Pernicious Poison](pernicious-poison.md)**: Target takes a –4 penalty against poison.
- **[Protection from Good, Communal](protection-from-good-communal.md)**: As protection from good, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Protection from Law, Communal](protection-from-law-communal.md)**: As protection from law, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Toxic Gift](toxic-gift.md)**: Target suffers the effect of a poison in you.
- **[Unadulterated Loathing](unadulterated-loathing.md)**: Target is compelled to avoid another creature.
- **[Vestment of the Champion](vestment-of-the-champion.md)**: Armor or shield gains a +1 enhancement per four levels.
- **[Vine Strike](vine-strike.md)**: Enhance one of your natural or unarmed attacks with thorny vine growth.
- **[Widen Auras](widen-auras.md)**: Doubles the range of your antipaladin or paladin auras.

## 3rd-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Blade of Dark Triumph](blade-of-dark-triumph.md)**: Bonded weapon gains ghost touch.
- **[Burst of Speed](burst-of-speed.md)**: You gain increased speed, and your movement ignores attacks of opportunity and allows you to move through the space of creatures larger than you are.
- **[Darkvision, Communal](darkvision-communal.md)**: As darkvision, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Deadly Juggernaut](deadly-juggernaut.md)**: Your might increases with every kill you make.
- **[Litany of Escape](litany-of-escape.md)**: Teleports a friend out of a grapple.
- **[Litany of Sight](litany-of-sight.md)**: You can see invisible creatures and objects within 30 feet of you.
- **[Utter Contempt](utter-contempt.md)**: Target’s attitude worsens by two categories.
- **[Vigilant Rest](vigilant-rest.md)**: Cause a sleeping creature to retain some perception of its surroundings.
- **[Wing Thorns](wing-thorns.md)**: Wings sprout poisonous thorns you can attack with.

## 4th-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Companion Transposition](companion-transposition.md)**: Swap places with your animal companion, familiar, or spirit animal via teleportation.
- **[Darkvision, Greater](darkvision-greater.md)**: See 120 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Foster Hatred](foster-hatred.md)**: Cause creatures to hate one type of creature.
- **[Inflict Pain, Mass](inflict-pain-mass.md)**: As inflict pain, but affecting one creature per level.
- **[Litany of Madness](litany-of-madness.md)**: A single target is confused for at least 1 round.
- **[Litany of Thunder](litany-of-thunder.md)**: A single target is deafened until the condition is removed, and is confused for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Vengeance](litany-of-vengeance.md)**: Allies attacking the target of the spell gain a +5 bonus on damage rolls for 1 round.
- **[Magic Siege Engine, Greater](magic-siege-engine-greater.md)**: Siege engine gains +1 on targeting and damage rolls for every four caster levels.
- **[Nondetection, Communal](nondetection-communal.md)**
- **[Unholy Sword](unholy-sword.md)**: Weapon becomes +5, deals +2d6 damage vs. good.

## Advanced Player's Guide Antipaladin Spell List

The complete antipaladin spell list from the *Advanced Player's Guide*, including spells from the Core Rulebook. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Bane](bane.md)**: Enemies take –1 on attack rolls and saves vs. fear.
- **[Cause Fear](cause-fear.md)**: A creature of 5 HD or less flees for 1d4 rounds.
- **[Command](command.md)**: One subject obeys a command for 1 round.
- **[Curse Water](curse-water.md)** (M): Makes water unholy.
- **[Death Knell](death-knell.md)**: Kills dying creature; you gain 1d8 temporary hp, +2 to Str, and +1 caster level.
- **[Detect Poison](detect-poison.md)**: Detects poison in a creature or object.
- **[Disguise Self](disguise-self.md)**: Changes your appearance.
- **[Doom](doom.md)**: One subject takes –2 on attack rolls, damage rolls, saves, and checks.
- **[Inflict Light Wounds](inflict-light-wounds.md)**: Touch deals 1d8 damage +1/level (max +5).
- **[Magic Weapon](magic-weapon.md)**: Weapon gains +1 bonus.
- **Protection from Good/Law**: +2 to AC and saves, plus additional protection against selected alignments.
- **[Read Magic](read-magic.md)**: Read scrolls and spellbooks.
- **[Summon Monster I](summon-monster-i.md)**: (Evil creatures only.) Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.

### 2nd-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Blindness/Deafness](blindness-deafness.md)**: Makes subject blind or deaf.
- **[Bull’s Strength](bulls-strength.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Str for 1 min./level.
- **[Corruption Resistance](corruption-resistance.md)**: Protects creature against damage from alignment-based attacks.
- **[Darkness](darkness.md)**: 20-ft. radius of supernatural shadow.
- **[Darkvision](darkvision.md)**: See 60 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Desecrate](desecrate.md)** (M): Fills area with negative energy; aids undead.
- **[Eagle’s Splendor](eagles-splendor.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Cha for 1 min./ level.
- **[Hold Person](hold-person.md)**: Paralyzes one humanoid for 1 round/level.
- **[Invisibility](invisibility.md)**: Subject is invisible for 1 min./level or until it attacks.
- **[Scare](scare.md)**: Frightens creature of less than 6 HD.
- **[Silence](silence.md)**: Negates sound in 20-ft. radius.
- **[Summon Monster II](summon-monster-ii.md)**: (Evil creatures only.) Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
- **[Undetectable Alignment](undetectable-alignment.md)**: Conceals alignment for 24 hours.

### 3rd-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Animate Dead](animate-dead.md)** (M): Creates undead skeletons and zombies.
- **[Bestow Curse](bestow-curse.md)**: –6 to an ability score; –4 on attack rolls, saves, and checks; or 50% chance of losing each action.
- **[Contagion](contagion.md)**: Infects subject with chosen disease.
- **[Deeper Darkness](deeper-darkness.md)**: Object sheds shadows in 60-ft. radius.
- **[Defile Armor](defile-armor.md)**: As sanctify armor, but gain DR 5/good when using judgment or smite.
- **[Dispel Magic](dispel-magic.md)**: Cancels one magical spell or effect.
- **[Inflict Moderate Wounds](inflict-moderate-wounds.md)**: Touch attack, 2d8 damage +1/ level (max +10).
- **Magic Circle against Good/Law**: As protection spells, but 10-ft. radius and 10 min./level.
- **[Magic Weapon, Greater](magic-weapon-greater.md)**: Weapon gains +1 bonus/four levels (max +5).
- **[Nondetection](nondetection.md)** (M): Hides subject from divination, scrying.
- **[Summon Monster III](summon-monster-iii.md)**: (Evil creatures only.) Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
- **[Vampiric Touch](vampiric-touch.md)**: Touch deals 1d6 damage/2 levels; caster gains damage as temporary hp.

### 4th-Level Antipaladin Spells

- **[Dispel Good](dispel-good.md)**: +4 against attacks by good creatures.
- **[Dispel Law](dispel-law.md)**: +4 against attacks by lawful creatures.
- **[Fear](fear.md)**: Subjects within cone flee for 1 round/level.
- **[Inflict Serious Wounds](inflict-serious-wounds.md)**: Touch attack, 3d8 damage +1/ level (max +15).
- **[Invisibility, Greater](invisibility-greater.md)**: As invisibility, but subject can attack and stay invisible.
- **[Poison](poison.md)**: Touch deals 1d3 Con damage 1/round for 6 rounds.
- **[Resounding Blow](resounding-blow.md)**: Melee attack deals 1d6 more damage.
- **[Slay Living](slay-living.md)**: Touch attack deals 12d6 +1 per level.
- **[Summon Monster IV](summon-monster-iv.md)**: (Evil creatures only.) Summons extraplanar creature to fight for you.
