# Daze, Mass

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** bard 4, inquisitor 4, medium 3, mesmerist 4, occultist 4, psychic 3, sorcerer/wizard 4, summoner 4, witch 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Targets** one or more humanoid creatures, no two of which may be more than 30 ft. apart  
**Duration** 1 round  
**Saving Throw** will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [*daze*](daze.md), except as noted above.
