# Recentering Drone

**School** abjuration; **Level** cleric 2, druid 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Targets** one creature/2 levels (no two of which may be more than 30 ft. apart)  
**Duration** concentration  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

You emit a calming subsonic drone that allows the targets to temporarily refocus and restore their balance when they're suffering from debilitating conditions. A target with the [dazzled](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazzled), [fatigued](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fatigued), [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken), or [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened) condition ignores the penalties of those conditions for the duration of this spell. A target with one of the following conditions is treated as having the associated lesser condition for the duration of the spell: [blinded](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#blinded) (dazzled), [exhausted](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#exhausted) (fatigued), [frightened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#frightened) (shaken), or [nauseated](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#nauseated) (sickened). At the end of the spell's duration, the targets are again subject to the appropriate penalties if the original conditions affecting the target are still in effect.
