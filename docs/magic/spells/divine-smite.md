# Divine Smite

**Level** cleric 2, oracle 2, paladin 2; inquisitor 3, warpriest 3  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Saving Throw** Will half

This spell deals 1d5 points of sacred or profane damage per caster level (maximum 10d5). The damage type depends on the source of your power: good sources deal sacred damage, and evil sources deal profane damage.

You can divide the damage in half to affect two targets at once, as long as they are no more than 30 feet apart.
