# Mount

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** bloodrager 1, magus 1, occultist 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, summoner 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 round  
**Components** V, S, M (a bit of horse hair)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one mount  
**Duration** 2 hours/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

You summon a horse or a pony (your choice) to serve you as a mount (see the *Pathfinder RPG Bestiary*). The steed serves willingly and well. The mount comes with a bit and bridle and a riding saddle.
