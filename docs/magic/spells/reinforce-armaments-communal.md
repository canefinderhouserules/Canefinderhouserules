# Reinforce Armaments, Communal

**School** transmutation; **Level** cleric 2, magus 2, occultist 2, sorcerer/wizard 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (a metal pin)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** armor suits or weapons touched  
**Duration** 10 minutes/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless, object); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless, object)

This spell functions like [*reinforce armaments*](reinforce-armaments.md), except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the objects touched.
