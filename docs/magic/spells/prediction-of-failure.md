# Prediction of Failure

**School** divination [curse, fear, mind-affecting]; **Level** occultist 6, psychic 8, sorcerer/ wizard 8, witch 8  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** permanent or 1 round/level (see text)  
**Saving Throw** Will partial; **Spell Resistance** yes

You wrack the target’s body and mind with the anguish and suffering of every bitter failure it will ever experience, rendering it permanently [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken) and [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened). A successful Will save reduces the duration to 1 round per level. If the target is a spellcaster, failing this saving throw means it also gains a random minor spellblight.
