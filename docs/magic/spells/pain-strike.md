# Pain Strike

**School** evocation [evil]; **Level** bloodrager 3, psychic 2, sorcerer/wizard 3, spiritualist 3, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one living creature  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

Pain strike racks the targeted creature with agony, inflicting 1d6 points of nonlethal damage per round for 1 round per level (maximum 10 rounds). Additionally, the affected creature is [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened) for the spell’s duration, and the caster gains a +4 circumstance bonus on [Intimidate](../../rules/skills/intimidate.md) checks against the target.
