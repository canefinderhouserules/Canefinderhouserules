# Whip of Centipedes

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** bard 5, druid 5, sorcerer/wizard 5, summoner 5, witch 5

This spell functions as *whip of spiders*, except it summons centipedes, and any creature you strike with the whip takes swarm damage as if it were attacked by a centipede swarm (2d6 points of damage plus poison and distraction, Pathfinder RPG Bestiary). If you transform the whip into a centipede swarm, it has 10 hit points.
