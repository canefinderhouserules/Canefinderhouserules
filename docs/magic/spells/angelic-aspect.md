# Angelic Aspect

**School** transmutation [good]; **Level** cleric 5, paladin 3, sorcerer/wizard 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like *angelic aspect, lesser*, except you gain low-light vision, darkvision 60 feet, resistance to acid 10, resistance to cold 10, and DR 5/evil, and you sprout white feathered wings allowing you to fly at a speed of 30 feet with average maneuverability. In addition, your natural weapons and any weapons you wield are considered good-aligned for the purpose of overcoming damage reduction.
