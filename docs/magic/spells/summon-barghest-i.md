# Summon Barghest I

**School** conjuration (summoning) [evil]; **Level** cleric 4  
**Casting Time** 1 round  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one summoned barghest  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell summons a barghest from an Abyssal realm to serve the caster, functioning similarly to *summon monster* save that it may only be used to summon a single barghest. Although the summoned barghest does not have the feed ability, if you are at least caster level 9th the summoned barghest appears with 2 growth points (and with it the appropriate bonuses to its attacks, CMB, saves, skill checks, hit points, and caster level).

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any barghest, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
