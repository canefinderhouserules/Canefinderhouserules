# Symbol of Insanity

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** cleric 8, occultist 6, sorcerer/wizard 8, witch 8  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 5,000 gp)  
**Range** 0 ft.; see text  
**Effect** one symbol  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like [*symbol of death*](symbol-of-death.md), except that all creatures within the radius of the *symbol of insanity* instead become permanently insane (as the [*insanity*](insanity.md) spell).

Unlike *symbol of death*, symbol of insanity has no hit point limit; once triggered, a *symbol of insanity* simply remains active for 10 minutes per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as *symbol of insanity* are hard to detect and disable. A character with trapfinding can use the [Perception](../../rules/skills/perception.md) skill to find a *symbol of insanity* and Disable Device to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 33 for *symbol of insanity*.
