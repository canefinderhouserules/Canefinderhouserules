# Create Variant Mummy

**School** necromancy [evil]; **Level** cleric 7, sorcerer/wizard 7  
**Casting Time** 1 hour  
**Components** V, S, M (onyx gems worth 50 gp per HD of the undead to be created)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** 1 corpse steeped in a peat bog for at least 24 hours  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions as [*create undead*](create-undead.md), except it creates one of the following variant mummies: bog mummy, ice mummy, or desert tomb guardian.
