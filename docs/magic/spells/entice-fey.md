# Entice Fey

**School** conjuration (calling); **Level** bard 6, druid 7, shaman 7  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (offerings worth 1,250 gp plus payment), DF  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or two called fey, totaling no more than 12 Hit Dice, which can’t appear more than 30 ft. apart  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions as [*lesser entice fey*](entice-fey-lesser.md), except that the spell’s whimsical calling can produce a single fey of 12 Hit Dice or less, or two fey of the same kind whose Hit Dice total no more than 12.
