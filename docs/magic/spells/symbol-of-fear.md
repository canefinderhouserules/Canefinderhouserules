# Symbol of Fear

**School** necromancy [fear, mind-affecting]; **Level** cleric 6, occultist 4, sorcerer/wizard 6, witch 6  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (mercury and phosphorus, plus powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 1,000 gp)  
**Range** 0 ft.; see text  
**Effect** one symbol  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like [*symbol of death*](symbol-of-death.md), except that all creatures within 60 feet of the *symbol of fear* instead become [panicked](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#panicked) for 1 round per caster level.

*Note*: Magic traps such as *symbol of fear* are hard to detect and disable. A character with trapfinding can use the [Perception](../../rules/skills/perception.md) skill to find a *symbol of fear* and Disable Device to thwart it. The DC in each case is 25 + spell level, or 31 for *symbol of fear*.
