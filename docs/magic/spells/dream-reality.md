# Dream Reality

**School** illusion (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** alchemist 5, bard 5, cleric 6, mesmerist 5, occultist 5, psychic 6, sorcerer/wizard 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Will disbelieve; **Spell Resistance** yes

You cause the target to perceive the world as if in a dream. When the spell ends, the target forgets all incidental details of events that transpired while the spell lasted. The memories were not taken from it; rather, its mind never recorded the memories as real in the first place. If *modify memory* is cast upon it before the spell ends, the target remembers these events. A target under the effect of *lullaby* before this spell is cast applies its penalty to the saving throw against this spell.
