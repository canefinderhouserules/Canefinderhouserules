# Implanted Projection

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [emotion, mind-affecting]; **Level** mesmerist 3, psychic 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** 1 day/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as *telempathic projection*, except its mood-altering effects remain dormant in the target's mind. Upon casting this spell, the caster designates a command word or phrase, of which the target retains no memory. The next creature to speak the command word or phrase to the target triggers the implanted *telempathic projection*, treating the speaker as the original spellcaster.
