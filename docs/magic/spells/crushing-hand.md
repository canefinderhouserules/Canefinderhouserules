# Crushing Hand

**School** evocation [force]; **Level** psychic 9, sorcerer/wizard 9  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, F (a soft glove)  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Effect** 10-ft.-hand  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [*interposing hand*](interposing-hand.md), except that it can also grapple one opponent as [*grasping hand*](grasping-hand.md). Its CMB and CMD for grapple checks use your caster level in place of its base attack bonus, with a +12 bonus for its Strength score (35) and a +1 bonus for being Large (its Dexterity is 10, granting no bonus to the CMD). A *crushing hand* deals 2d6+12 points of damage on each successful grapple check against an opponent. The *crushing hand* can instead be directed to bull rush a target (as [*forceful hand*](forceful-hand.md)), using the same bonuses outlined above, or it can be directed to interpose itself, as [*interposing hand*](interposing-hand.md) does.
