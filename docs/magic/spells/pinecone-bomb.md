# Pinecone Bomb

**School** evocation; **Level** druid 2, ranger 3, shaman 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a pinecone)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Area** 10-ft.-radius spread  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Reflex half; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell infuses a small pinecone with power, causing it to detonate with explosive force when thrown. You throw the pinecone at a target or point within range and it detonates immediately upon landing, dealing 1d6 points of piercing damage per 2 caster levels you have (maximum 5d6) to each creature caught in the spread.
