# Soothing Word

**School** conjuration (healing); **Level** cleric 2, druid 2, inquisitor 2, paladin 2, ranger 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** 1 creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

The target of this spell causes several conditions that he suffers from to be lessened in severity by one step. If he is [nauseated](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#nauseated), he is instead [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened). If he is [stunned](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#stunned), he is instead [dazed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#dazed). If he is [exhausted](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#exhausted), he is [fatigued](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fatigued). If he is [frightened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#frightened), he is [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken). If he is [paralyzed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed), he is [staggered](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#staggered). The duration of the effect is unchanged; the effect is simply reduced to the lesser version. This spell has no effect on conditions not listed above.
