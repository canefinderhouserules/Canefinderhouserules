# Paladin Spells

## 1st-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Bless](bless.md)**: Allies gain +1 on attack rolls and saves against fear.
- **[Bless Water](bless-water.md)**: Makes holy water.
- **[Bless Weapon](bless-weapon.md)**: Weapon strikes true against evil foes.
- **[Bowstaff](bowstaff.md)**: A shortbow may double as a club, or a longbow as a quarterstaff.
- **[Compel Hostility](compel-hostility.md)**: Compels opponents to attack you instead of your allies.
- **[Create Water](create-water.md)**: Creates 2 gallons/level of pure water.
- **[Cure Light Wounds](cure-light-wounds.md)**: Cures 1d8 damage + 1/level (max +5).
- **[Detect Poison](detect-poison.md)**: Detects poison in one creature or small object.
- **[Detect Undead](detect-undead.md)**: Reveals undead within 60 ft.
- **[Divine Favor](divine-favor.md)**: You gain +1 per three levels on attack and damage rolls.
- **[Endure Elements](endure-elements.md)**: Exist comfortably in hot or cold regions.
- **[Ironbeard](ironbeard.md)**: School transmutation; Level antipaladin 1, cleric 1, magus 1, paladin 1, ranger 1 Casting Time 1 standard.
- **[Liberating Command](liberating-command.md)**: Target makes an Escape Artist check as an immediate action and gains a bonus on it.
- **[Linebreaker](linebreaker.md)**: You gain a +20 foot bonus to your base speed when charging and a +2 bonus on combat maneuver checks made to.
- **[Litany of Sloth](litany-of-sloth.md)**: Single target cannot make attacks of opportunity for 1 round.
- **[Longshot](longshot.md)**: Grants a +10-foot bonus to the range increment for any ranged weapon fired.
- **[Magic Weapon](magic-weapon.md)**: Weapon gains +1 bonus.
- **Protection from Chaos/Evil**: +2 to AC and saves, plus additional protection against selected alignment.
- **[Read Magic](read-magic.md)**: Read scrolls and spellbooks.
- **[Resistance](resistance.md)**: Subject gains +1 on saving throws.
- **[Restoration, Lesser](restoration-lesser.md)**: Dispels magical ability penalty or repairs 1d4 ability damage.
- **[Sun Metal](sun-metal.md)**: Weapon touched bursts into flames.
- **[Tactical Acumen](tactical-acumen.md)**: You gain an additional +1 on attack rolls or to AC due to battlefield positioning.
- **[Veil of Heaven](veil-of-heaven.md)**: You surround yourself with a veil of positive energy, making it harder for evil outsiders to harm you.
- **[Virtue](virtue.md)**: Subject gains 1 temporary hp.

## 2nd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Aquatic Cavalry](aquatic-cavalry.md)**: Summon hippocampi to serve as aquatic mounts.
- **[Bestow Weapon Proficiency](bestow-weapon-proficiency.md)**: Grants a creature proficiency in a single weapon for short period of time.
- **[Blessing of Luck and Resolve](blessing-of-luck-and-resolve.md)**: A favored blessing of halfling clerics, this spell grants its target a +2 morale bonus on saving throws.
- **[Blinding Ray](blinding-ray.md)**: School evocation [good, light]; Level cleric 2, inquisitor 3, paladin 2 Casting Time 1 standard action.
- **[Bull’s Strength](bulls-strength.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Str for 1 min./level.
- **[Delay Disease](delay-disease.md)**: The target becomes temporarily immune to disease.
- **[Delay Poison](delay-poison.md)**: Stops poison from harming subject for 1 hour/level.
- **[Divine Arrow](divine-arrow.md)**: Imbues a projectile with holy energy.
- **[Eagle’s Splendor](eagles-splendor.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Cha for 1 min./level.
- **[Effortless Armor](effortless-armor.md)**: Armor you wear no longer slows your speed.
- **[Endure Elements, Communal](endure-elements-communal.md)**: As endure elements, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Litany of Defense](litany-of-defense.md)**: Doubles armor’s enhancement bonus.
- **[Litany of Eloquence](litany-of-eloquence.md)**: Fascinates a single creature for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Entanglement](litany-of-entanglement.md)**: Entangles a creature for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Righteousness](litany-of-righteousness.md)**: A single evil creature takes more damage from creatures with a good aura.
- **[Litany of Warding](litany-of-warding.md)**: You gain two additional attacks of opportunity for 1 round.
- **[Magic Siege Engine](magic-siege-engine.md)**: Siege engine gains +1 on targeting and damage rolls.
- **[Owl’s Wisdom](owls-wisdom.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Wis for 1 min./level.
- **[Protection from Chaos, Communal](protection-from-chaos-communal.md)**: As protection from chaos, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Protection from Evil, Communal](protection-from-evil-communal.md)**: As protection from evil, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Remove Paralysis](remove-paralysis.md)**: Frees one or more creatures from paralysis or slow effect.
- **[Resist Energy](resist-energy.md)**: Ignores 10 or more points of damage per attack from specified energy type.
- **[Sacred Space](sacred-space.md)**: This spell sanctifies an area with heavenly power.
- **[Sea Steed](sea-steed.md)**: Your mount adapts to an aquatic environment.
- **[Shield Other](shield-other.md)**: You take half of subject’s damage.
- **[Undetectable Alignment](undetectable-alignment.md)**: Conceals alignment from magical detection for 24 hours.
- **[Widen Auras](widen-auras.md)**: Doubles the range of your antipaladin or paladin auras.
- **[Zone of Truth](zone-of-truth.md)**: Subjects within range cannot lie.

## 3rd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Burst of Speed](burst-of-speed.md)**: You gain increased speed, and your movement ignores attacks of opportunity and allows you to move through the space of creatures larger than you are.
- **[Cure Moderate Wounds](cure-moderate-wounds.md)**: Cures 2d8 damage +1/level (max. +10).
- **[Daybreak Arrow](daybreak-arrow.md)**: Targeted ammunition exudes radiant energy.
- **[Daylight](daylight.md)**: 60-ft. radius of bright light.
- **[Deadly Juggernaut](deadly-juggernaut.md)**: Your might increases with every kill you make.
- **[Delay Poison, Communal](delay-poison-communal.md)**: As delay poison, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Discern Lies](discern-lies.md)**: Reveals deliberate falsehoods.
- **[Dispel Magic](dispel-magic.md)**: Cancels one magical spell or effect.
- **[Heal Mount](heal-mount.md)**: As *heal* on horse or other special mount.
- **[Litany of Escape](litany-of-escape.md)**: Teleports a friend out of a grapple.
- **[Litany of Sight](litany-of-sight.md)**: You can see invisible creatures and objects within 30 feet of you.
- **Magic Circle against Chaos/Evil**: As *protection from chaos*, but 10-ft. radius and 10 min./level.
- **[Magic Weapon, Greater](magic-weapon-greater.md)**: Weapon gains +1 bonus/four levels (max +5).
- **[Prayer](prayer.md)**: Allies get +1 bonus on most rolls, enemies –1 penalty.
- **[Remove Blindness/Deafness](remove-blindness-deafness.md)**: Cures normal or magical blindness or deafness.
- **[Remove Curse](remove-curse.md)**: Frees object or person from curse.
- **[Resilient Reservoir](resilient-reservoir.md)**: School transmutation; Level magus 3, paladin 3, sorcerer/wizard 4, witch 4 Casting Time 1 standard action.
- **[Resist Energy, Communal](resist-energy-communal.md)**: As resist energy, but you may divide the duration among creatures touched.
- **[Sky Steed](sky-steed.md)**: Grant your mount angelic wings.
- **[Vigilant Rest](vigilant-rest.md)**: Cause a sleeping creature to retain some perception of its surroundings.

## 4th-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Break Enchantment](break-enchantment.md)**: Frees subjects from enchantments, transmutations, and curses.
- **[Companion Transposition](companion-transposition.md)**: Swap places with your animal companion, familiar, or spirit animal via teleportation.
- **[Cure Serious Wounds](cure-serious-wounds.md)**: Cures 3d8 damage + 1/level (max +15).
- **[Death Ward](death-ward.md)**: Grants bonuses against death spells and negative energy.
- **[Dispel Chaos](dispel-chaos.md)**: +4 bonus against attacks by chaotic creatures.
- **[Dispel Evil](dispel-evil.md)**: +4 bonus against attacks by evil creatures.
- **[Holy Sword](holy-sword.md)**: Weapon becomes +5, deals +2d6 damage against evil.
- **[Litany of Thunder](litany-of-thunder.md)**: A single target is deafened until the condition is removed, and is confused for 1 round.
- **[Litany of Vengeance](litany-of-vengeance.md)**: Allies attacking the target of the spell gain a +5 bonus on damage rolls for 1 round.
- **[Magic Siege Engine, Greater](magic-siege-engine-greater.md)**: Siege engine gains +1 on targeting and damage rolls for every four caster levels.
- **[Mark of Justice](mark-of-justice.md)**: Designates action that triggers *curse* on subject.
- **[Neutralize Poison](neutralize-poison.md)**: Immunizes subject against poison, detoxifies venom in or on subject.
- **[Paragon Surge](paragon-surge.md)**: You surge with ancestral power, temporarily embodying all the strengths of both elvenkind and humankind.
- **[Restoration](restoration.md)**: Restores level and ability score drains.
- **[Sea Stallion](sea-stallion.md)**: You and your mount adapt to an aquatic environment.

## Ultimate Magic Spells

New spells from *Ultimate Magic*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Diagnose Disease](diagnose-disease.md)**: Detect and identify diseases.
- **[Horn of Pursuit](horn-of-pursuit.md)**: Create three notes heard miles away.
- **[Know the Enemy](know-the-enemy.md)**: Gain +10 on a monster Knowledge check.
- **[Sanctify Corpse](sanctify-corpse.md)**: Prevent a corpse from becoming an undead.
- **[Wartrain Mount](wartrain-mount.md)**: Animal gains the combat training general purpose.
- **[Word of Resolve](word-of-resolve.md)**: Ally rerolls a save against charm or fear.

### 2nd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Arrow of Law](arrow-of-law.md)**: Harm and possibly daze chaotic creatures.
- **[Holy Shield](holy-shield.md)**: Lend your shield’s protection to another.
- **[Vestment of the Champion](vestment-of-the-champion.md)**: Armor or shield gains a +1 enhancement per four levels.

### 3rd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Archon’s Aura](archons-aura.md)**: Aura penalizes enemy attacks and AC.
- **[Blade of Bright Victory](blade-of-bright-victory.md)**: Bonded weapon gains ghost touch.
- **[Blessing of the Mole](blessing-of-the-mole.md)**: 1 ally/level gains darkvision and a +2 Stealth bonus.

### 4th-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Bestow Grace of the Champion](bestow-grace-of-the-champion.md)**: Target gains paladin abilities for 1 round/level.
- **[Raise Animal Companion](raise-animal-companion.md)**: As raise dead, but on an animal.
- **[Reprobation](reprobation.md)**: Marked target is shunned by your religion.
- **[Symbol of Healing](symbol-of-healing.md)**: Triggered rune heals living creatures.

## Advanced Player's Guide Spells

New spells from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Challenge Evil](challenge-evil.md)**: Sickens creature if it refuses to fight you.
- **[Ghostbane Dirge](ghostbane-dirge.md)**: Incorporeal creature takes half damage from nonmagical weapons.
- **[Grace](grace.md)**: Movement doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity.
- **[Hero's Defiance](heros-defiance.md)**: Allows the use of lay on hands while falling unconscious.
- **[Honeyed Tongue](honeyed-tongue.md)**: Roll 2 dice when using Diplomacy, take higher roll.
- **[Knight's Calling](knights-calling.md)**: Forces target to move toward you and fight you.
- **[Rally Point](rally-point.md)**: Square gives good creatures bonuses.
- **[Veil of Positive Energy](veil-of-positive-energy.md)**: +2 AC, +2 on saves vs. undead.

### 2nd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Aura of Greater Courage](aura-of-greater-courage.md)**: Increases strength of a paladin’s aura of courage.
- **[Bestow Grace](bestow-grace.md)**: Subject gains bonus on saving throws equal to Cha modifier.
- **[Blessing of Courage and Life](blessing-of-courage-and-life.md)**: Grants a +2 bonus on saves vs. fear and death.
- **[Corruption Resistance](corruption-resistance.md)**: Protects creature against damage from alignment-based attacks.
- **[Fire of Entanglement](fire-of-entanglement.md)**: Your ability to smite evil also entangles your foe.
- **[Instant Armor](instant-armor.md)**: Summon armor temporarily replacing your current attire.
- **[Light Lance](light-lance.md)**: Creates a soaring beacon of light.
- **[Paladin's Sacrifice](paladins-sacrifice.md)**: Take the damage and effects for another creature.
- **[Righteous Vigor](righteous-vigor.md)**: Boosts attack bonus with each hit.
- **[Sacred Bond](sacred-bond.md)** (F): Cast touch healing spells from a distance.
- **[Saddle Surge](saddle-surge.md)**: Bonus damage for moving on mount.
- **[Wake of Light](wake-of-light.md)**: Magical trail aids good creatures, hinders evil ones.
- **[Weapon of Awe](weapon-of-awe.md)**: Weapon gets +2 on damage rolls.

### 3rd-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Divine Transfer](divine-transfer.md)**: Transfer hit points and give DR/evil to target creature.
- **[Fire of Judgment](fire-of-judgment.md)**: Smited creature takes damage when it attacks.
- **[Ghostbane Dirge, Mass](ghostbane-dirge-mass.md)**: As ghostbane dirge, but affects multiple creatures.
- **[Heroic Fortune](heroic-fortune.md)**: Grants the target 1 temporary hero point.
- **[Holy Whisper](holy-whisper.md)**: Whisper sickens evil creatures, gives good creatures bonuses.
- **[Marks of Forbiddance](marks-of-forbiddance.md)**: Makes 2 creatures pass Will saves in order to attack each other.
- **[Sanctify Armor](sanctify-armor.md)**: +1 AC/four levels (max +5).
- **[Wrathful Mantle](wrathful-mantle.md)**: Subject shines and gets +1/four levels on all saves.

### 4th-Level Paladin Spells

- **[Blaze of Glory](blaze-of-glory.md)**: Last stand cures good creatures, hurts evil.
- **[Fire of Vengeance](fire-of-vengeance.md)**: Smited creature takes 3d8 damage.
- **[Forced Repentance](forced-repentance.md)**: Target falls prone and confesses sins.
- **[King’s Castle](kings-castle.md)**: Instantly switch places with a single ally.
- **[Oath of Peace](oath-of-peace.md)**: Grants +5 AC and DR 10/evil, can’t attack.
- **[Resounding Blow](resounding-blow.md)**: Melee attack deals 1d6 more damage.
- **[Sacrificial Oath](sacrificial-oath.md)**: Take damage for an ally for many rounds.
- **[Stay the Hand](stay-the-hand.md)**: Subject cannot attack with melee weapon.
