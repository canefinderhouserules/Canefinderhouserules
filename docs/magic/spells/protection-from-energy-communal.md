# Protection from Energy, Communal

**School** abjuration; **Level** cleric 4, druid 4, inquisitor 4, psychic 4, ranger 3, sorcerer/wizard 4, summoner 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creatures touched  
**Duration** 10 min./level or until discharged  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell functions like [*protection from energy*](protection-from-energy.md), except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched. Once the spell absorbs 12 points of energy damage per caster level (maximum 120 points) for a subject, the spell’s effects end for that subject.
