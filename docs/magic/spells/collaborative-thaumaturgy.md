# Collaborative Thaumaturgy

**School** universal; **Level** bard 3, cleric 3, druid 3, magus 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, summoner 3, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 immediate action (1 AP of your next turn)  
**Components** V, S, F (a bronze tuning fork)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous

You tap into the magical resonance between you and your allies to enhance one another’s magic. Cast this spell when an ally casts a spell at least 1 level lower than the highest-level spell she can cast. The ally casts her spell as if one of the following metamagic feats were applied to it without increasing the spell level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](../../rules/feats/enlarge-spell.md), [Extend Spell](../../rules/feats/extend-spell.md), [Silent Spell](../../rules/feats/silent-spell.md), or [Still Spell](../../rules/feats/still-spell.md). If the ally is the target of two *collaborative thaumaturgy* spells when casting a spell, the ally can instead apply the [Empower Spell](../../rules/feats/empower-spell.md) metamagic feat to a spell at least 2 levels lower than the highest-level spell she can cast. Finally, if the ally is the target of three *collaborative thaumaturgy* spells when casting a spell, she can instead apply the [Maximize Spell](../../rules/feats/maximize-spell.md) or [Widen Spell](../../rules/feats/widen-spell.md) metamagic feat to a spell at least 3 levels lower than the highest-level spell she can cast. You can’t target yourself with *collaborative thaumaturgy*.
