# Mydriatic Spontaneity, Mass

**School** evocation [darkness, light]; **Level** bard 5, mesmerist 5, psychic 6, sorcerer/wizard 7, witch 7  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Targets** one or more living creatures, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [mydriatic spontaneity](mydriatic-spontaneity.md), except it can affect multiple creatures.
