# Life Conduit, Greater

**School** conjuration (healing); **Level** summoner 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 round/level  

This spell functions like [*life conduit*](life-conduit.md), except you can transfer 3d6 hit points as a swift action.
