# Bloody Tears and Jagged Smile

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 2, sorcerer/wizard 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal and see text  
**Target** you and see text  
**Duration** 10 minutes/level  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes

Your eyes turn black and weep blood, and your teeth become jagged fangs. You gain a bite attack (1d3 points of damage if you are Small, 1d4 points of damage if you are Medium). You gain a +4 profane bonus on [Intimidate](../../rules/skills/intimidate.md) checks and on your spell DCs for spells with the fear descriptor. You perceive creatures as if using [*deathwatch*](deathwatch.md).
