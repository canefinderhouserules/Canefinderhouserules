# Arcane Sight, Greater

**School** divination; **Level** psychic 7, sorcerer/wizard 7, witch 7  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 min./level (D)

This spell functions like *arcane sight,* except that you automatically know which spells or magical effects are active upon any individual or object you see.

Unlike [*arcane sight*](arcane-sight.md), this spell cannot be made permanent with a [*permanency*](permanency.md) spell.
