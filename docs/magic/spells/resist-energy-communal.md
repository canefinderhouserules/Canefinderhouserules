# Resist Energy, Communal

**School** abjuration; **Level** alchemist 3, cleric 3, druid 3, inquisitor 3, occultist 3, paladin 3, psychic 3, ranger 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, summoner 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creatures touched  
**Duration** 10 min./level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell functions like [*resist energy*](resist-energy.md), except you divide the duration in 10-minute intervals among the creatures touched.
