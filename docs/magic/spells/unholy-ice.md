# Unholy Ice

**School** transmutation [cold, evil, water]; **Level** cleric 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a flask of unholy water or 5 pounds of powdered silver worth 25 gp) This spell functions as holy ice, except it is made of frozen unholy water rather than holy water.  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Effect** wall of ice or flying ice javelins (see text)  
**Duration** 1 minute/level, instantaneous, or until expended (see text)  
**Saving Throw** Reflex negates or none (see text); **Spell Resistance** yes
