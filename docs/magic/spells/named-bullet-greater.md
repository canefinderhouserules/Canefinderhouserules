# Named Bullet, Greater

**School** divination; **Level** inquisitor 6, psychic 6, ranger 4, sorcerer/wizard 6, witch 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (an item from the selected creature or creature type)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** one piece of ammunition or one thrown weapon  
**Duration** 10 minutes/level or until discharged  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless, object); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless, object)

This spell functions like [*named bullet*](named-bullet.md), except it deals 2 extra points of damage per caster level (maximum 40).
