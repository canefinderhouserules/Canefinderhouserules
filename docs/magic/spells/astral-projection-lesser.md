# Astral Projection, Lesser

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 5, sorcerer/wizard 5 This spell functions as astral projection, except you cannot leave the Astral Plane and explore other planes (though you can still return to the plane you were on when you cast this spell).  
**Casting Time** 30 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (1,000 gp jacinth)  
**Range** touch  
**Targets** you plus one additional willing creature touched per two caster levels  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes
