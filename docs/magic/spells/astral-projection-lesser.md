# Astral Projection, Lesser

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 5, psychic 5, sorcerer/wizard 5  
**Casting Time** 30 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M (1,000 gp jacinth)  
**Range** touch  
**Targets** you plus one additional willing creature touched per two caster levels  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [*astral projection*](astral-projection.md), except you cannot leave the Astral Plane and explore other planes (though you can still return to the plane you were on when you cast this spell).
