# Bless Water

**School** transmutation [good]; **Level** cleric 1, paladin 1  
**Casting Time** 1 minute  
**Components** V, S, M (5 pounds of powdered silver worth 25 gp)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** flask of water touched  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (object); **Spell Resistance** yes (object)

This transmutation imbues a flask (1 pint) of water with positive energy, turning it into holy water (see page 160).
