# Summoner Spells

New summoner spells from *Ultimate Magic*. Spells from other books will be added as they are transcribed. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

## 1st-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Compel Hostility](compel-hostility.md)**: Compels opponents to attack you instead of your allies.
- **[Corrosive Touch](corrosive-touch.md)**: Touch attack deals 1d4 acid/level.
- **[Icicle Dagger](icicle-dagger.md)**: Masterwork ice dagger deals +1 cold damage.
- **[Jury-Rig](jury-rig.md)**: Removes the broken condition from the targeted object.
- **[Life Conduit](life-conduit.md)**: You are bound even tighter to your eidolon than normal, and may share hit points with it through your life link ability.
- **[Ray of Sickening](ray-of-sickening.md)**: Ray makes the subject sickened.
- **[Summon Minor Monster](summon-minor-monster.md)**: Summon 1d3 Tiny animals.

## Advanced Player's Guide Spells

New spells from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Ant Haul](ant-haul.md)**: Triples carrying capacity of a creature.
- **[Rejuvenate Eidolon, Lesser](rejuvenate-eidolon-lesser.md)**: Eidolon cured 1d10 damage +1/level (max +5).
- **[Unfetter](unfetter.md)**: Remove distance restriction on eidolon.

### 2nd-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Create Pit](create-pit.md)** (F): Creates an extradimensional pit.
- **[Evolution Surge, Lesser](evolution-surge-lesser.md)**: Grants eidolon an evolution with 2 evolution points.
- **[Glide](glide.md)**: You take no falling damage and move 60 ft./round while falling.
- **[Summon Eidolon](summon-eidolon.md)**: Instantly summons your eidolon for duration of spell.

### 3rd-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Aqueous Orb](aqueous-orb.md)**: Creates rolling sphere of water.
- **[Devolution](devolution.md)**: An eidolon temporarily loses 1 evolution +1/ five levels.
- **[Evolution Surge](evolution-surge.md)**: Grants eidolon an evolution with 4 evolution points.
- **[Rejuvenate Eidolon](rejuvenate-eidolon.md)**: As lesser rejuvenate eidolon, but cures 3d10 damage +1/level (max +10).
- **[Seek Thoughts](seek-thoughts.md)**: Detects creatures in the area thinking about something.
- **[Spiked Pit](spiked-pit.md)**: As create pit, but filled with spikes.

### 4th-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Acid Pit](acid-pit.md)** (M): Creates a pit with acid at the bottom.
- **[Evolution Surge, Greater](evolution-surge-greater.md)**: Grants eidolon two evolutions with a total of 6 evolution points.
- **[Purified Calling](purified-calling.md)**: Eidolon summoned is fully healed.
- **[Transmogrify](transmogrify.md)** (M):  Change your eidolon's evolutions.

### 5th-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Hungry Pit](hungry-pit.md)**: As create pit, but dealing 4d6 damage to those in it as it closes.
- **[Planar Adaptation](planar-adaptation.md)**: Resist harmful effects of other plane.
- **[Rejuvenate Eidolon, Greater](rejuvenate-eidolon-greater.md)**: As lesser rejuvenate eidolon, but cures 5d10 damage +1/level (max +20).

### 6th-Level Summoner Spells

- **[Planar Adaptation, Mass](planar-adaptation-mass.md)**: As planar adaptation, but affects multiple creatures.
