# Brand, Greater

**School** transmutation; **Level** inquisitor 4  
**Casting Time** 1 round (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** permanent  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like [*brand*](brand.md), except that it inflicts 1d6 points of damage when applied and cannot be removed, even temporarily. In addition, a greater brand blazes as bright as a torch when brought within 30 feet of a visible symbol of your faith. While the symbol is blazing, the recipient is [sickened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#sickened). Like the effects of bestow curse, a greater brand cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed by any means that removes a mark of justice.
