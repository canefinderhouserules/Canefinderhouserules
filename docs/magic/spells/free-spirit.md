# Free Spirit

**School** abjuration; **Level** bard 3, cleric 3, druid 3, medium 3, shaman 3, spiritualist 3, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 minute/level

This spell allows you to channel the essence of a carefree philosophy, giving you the benefits of a *freedom of movement* spell and granting you a +4 bonus on saving throws against fear effects. The spell also makes you drunk. For the duration of the spell, you take a –2 penalty on attack rolls, ability checks, skill checks, and concentration checks. If you somehow remove or avoid these penalties, the spell's duration ends.
