# Ranger Spells

## 1st-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Alarm](alarm.md)**: Wards an area for 2 hours/level.
- **[Animal Messenger](animal-messenger.md)**: Sends a Tiny animal to a specific place.
- **[Calm Animals](calm-animals.md)**: Calms 2d4 + level HD of animals.
- **[Charm Animal](charm-animal.md)**: Makes one animal your friend.
- **[Delay Poison](delay-poison.md)**: Stops poison from harming subject for 1 hour/level.
- **[Detect Animals or Plants](detect-animals-or-plants.md)**: Detects kinds of animals or plants.
- **[Detect Poison](detect-poison.md)**: Detects poison in one creature or object.
- **[Detect Snares and Pits](detect-snares-and-pits.md)**: Reveals natural or primitive traps.
- **[Endure Elements](endure-elements.md)**: Exist comfortably in hot or cold regions.
- **[Entangle](entangle.md)**: Plants entangle everyone in 40-ft. radius.
- **[Hide from Animals](hide-from-animals.md)**: Animals can’t perceive one subject/level.
- **[Jump](jump.md)**: Subject gets bonus on Acrobatics checks.
- **[Longstrider](longstrider.md)**: Your base speed increases by 10 ft.
- **[Magic Fang](magic-fang.md)**: One natural weapon of subject creature gets +1 on attack and damage rolls.
- **[Pass without Trace](pass-without-trace.md)**: One subject/level leaves no tracks.
- **[Read Magic](read-magic.md)**: Read scrolls and spellbooks.
- **[Resist Energy](resist-energy.md)**: Ignores 10 (or more) points of damage/attack from specified energy type.
- **[Speak with Animals](speak-with-animals.md)**: You can communicate with animals.
- **[Summon Nature’s Ally I](summon-natures-ally-i.md)**: Summons creature to fight for you.

## 2nd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Barkskin](barkskin.md)**: Grants +2 (or higher) enhancement to natural armor.
- **[Bear’s Endurance](bears-endurance.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Con for 1 min./level.
- **[Cat’s Grace](cats-grace.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Dex for 1 min./level.
- **[Cure Light Wounds](cure-light-wounds.md)**: Cures 1d8 damage + 1/level (max +5).
- **[Hold Animal](hold-animal.md)**: Paralyzes one animal for 1 round/level.
- **[Owl’s Wisdom](owls-wisdom.md)**: Subject gains +4 to Wis for 1 min./level.
- **[Protection from Energy](protection-from-energy.md)**: Absorbs 12 points/level of damage from one kind of energy.
- **[Snare](snare.md)**: Creates a magic booby trap.
- **[Speak with Plants](speak-with-plants.md)**: You can talk to plants and plant creatures.
- **[Spike Growth](spike-growth.md)**: Creatures in area take 1d4 damage, may be slowed*.*
- **[Summon Nature’s Ally II](summon-natures-ally-ii.md)**: Summons creature to fight for you.
- **[Wind Wall](wind-wall.md)**: Deflects arrows, smaller creatures, and gases.

## 3rd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Command Plants](command-plants.md)**: Sway the actions of plant creatures.
- **[Cure Moderate Wounds](cure-moderate-wounds.md)**: Cures 2d8 damage +1/level (max. +10).
- **[Darkvision](darkvision.md)**: See 60 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Diminish Plants](diminish-plants.md)**: Reduces size or blights growth of normal plants.
- **[Magic Fang, Greater](magic-fang-greater.md)**: One natural weapon gets + 1/four levels (max. +5).
- **[Neutralize Poison](neutralize-poison.md)**: Immunizes subject against poison, detoxifies venom in or on subject.
- **[Plant Growth](plant-growth.md)**: Grows vegetation, improves crops.
- **[Reduce Animal](reduce-animal.md)**: Shrinks one willing animal.
- **[Remove Disease](remove-disease.md)**: Cures all diseases affecting subject.
- **[Repel Vermin](repel-vermin.md)**: Insects, spiders, and other vermin stay 10 ft. away.
- **[Summon Nature’s Ally III](summon-natures-ally-iii.md)**: Summons creature to fight for you.
- **[Tree Shape](tree-shape.md)**: You look exactly like a tree for 1 hour/level.
- **[Water Walk](water-walk.md)**: Subject treads on water as if solid.

## 4th-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Animal Growth](animal-growth.md)**: One animal doubles in size.
- **[Commune with Nature](commune-with-nature.md)**: Learn about terrain for 1 mile/level.
- **[Cure Serious Wounds](cure-serious-wounds.md)**: Cures 3d8 damage + 1/level (max +15).
- **[Freedom of Movement](freedom-of-movement.md)**: Subject moves normally despite impediments to movement.
- **[Nondetection](nondetection.md)**: Hides subject from divination, scrying.
- **[Summon Nature’s Ally IV](summon-natures-ally-iv.md)**: Summons creature to fight for you.
- **[Tree Stride](tree-stride.md)**: Step from one tree to another far away.

## Ultimate Magic Spells

New spells from *Ultimate Magic*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Anticipate Peril](anticipate-peril.md)**: Target gains a bonus on one initiative check.
- **[Diagnose Disease](diagnose-disease.md)**: Detect and identify diseases.
- **[Horn of Pursuit](horn-of-pursuit.md)**: Create three notes heard miles away.
- **[Know the Enemy](know-the-enemy.md)**: Gain +10 on a monster Knowledge check.
- **[Summon Minor Ally](summon-minor-ally.md)**: Summon 1d3 Tiny animals.
- **[Wartrain Mount](wartrain-mount.md)**: Animal gains combat training.

### 2nd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Acute Senses](acute-senses.md)**: Subject gains a bonus on Perception checks.
- **[Badger’s Ferocity](badgers-ferocity.md)**: Weapons are keen while you concentrate.
- **[Web Shelter](web-shelter.md)**: Create a comfortable shelter made of webbing.

### 3rd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Blessing of the Mole](blessing-of-the-mole.md)**: 1 ally/level gains darkvision and a +2 Stealth bonus.
- **[Burrow](burrow.md)**: Target gains a burrow speed of 15.
- **[Fickle Winds](fickle-winds.md)**: Wind walls selectively block attacks.

### 4th-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Darkvision, Greater](darkvision-greater.md)**: See 120 ft. in total darkness.
- **[Raise Animal Companion](raise-animal-companion.md)**: As raise dead, but on an animal.

## Advanced Player's Guide Spells

New spells from the *Advanced Player's Guide*. An M or F after a name marks a material or focus component not normally included in a spell component pouch.

### 1st-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Ant Haul](ant-haul.md)**: Triples carrying capacity of a creature.
- **[Aspect of the Falcon](aspect-of-the-falcon.md)**: Gives bonuses on Perception and ranged attacks.
- **[Call Animal](call-animal.md)**: Makes an animal come to you.
- **[Cloak of Shade](cloak-of-shade.md)**: Reduces effects of intense sun exposure and environmental heat.
- **[Dancing Lantern](dancing-lantern.md)**: Animates a lantern that follows you.
- **[Detect Aberration](detect-aberration.md)**: Detect presence of aberrations.
- **[Feather Step](feather-step.md)**: Subject ignores adverse movement effects in difficult terrain.
- **[Glide](glide.md)**: No fall damage, move 60 ft./round while falling.
- **[Gravity Bow](gravity-bow.md)**: Arrows do damage as though one size category bigger.
- **[Hunter's Howl](hunters-howl.md)**: Treat enemies as favored for 1 round/level.
- **[Keen Senses](keen-senses.md)**: Subject gains +2 Perception, low-light vision.
- **[Lead Blades](lead-blades.md)**: Melee weapons damage as if one size bigger.
- **[Negate Aroma](negate-aroma.md)**: Subject cannot be tracked by scent.
- **[Residual Tracking](residual-tracking.md)**: Tell creature's appearance by footprint.
- **[Tireless Pursuit](tireless-pursuit.md)**: Ignore fatigue while hustling.

### 2nd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Accelerate Poison](accelerate-poison.md)**: Hastens targeted poison's onset.
- **[Allfood](allfood.md)**: Turns item up to 5 lbs./level into food.
- **[Arrow Eruption](arrow-eruption.md)**: Creates duplicates of an arrow used to kill a creature in the previous round.
- **[Aspect of the Bear](aspect-of-the-bear.md)**: +2 AC and combat maneuver rolls.
- **[Bloodhound](bloodhound.md)**: Gives caster the scent special ability.
- **[Campfire Wall](campfire-wall.md)**: Creates a shelter around a campfire.
- **[Chameleon Stride](chameleon-stride.md)**: Gives a +4 bonus on Stealth checks and concealment.
- **[Create Treasure Map](create-treasure-map.md)** (M): Creates a treasure map out of a creature’s corpse.
- **[Eagle Eye](eagle-eye.md)**: Creates a magical sensor high above you.
- **[Guiding Star](guiding-star.md)**: Know approximate distance from where you cast this spell.
- **[Hide Campsite](hide-campsite.md)**: Hides all traces of your campsite.
- **[Hunter’s Eye](hunters-eye.md)**: +20 on Perception checks to locate a target.
- **[Lockjaw](lockjaw.md)**: Gives creature grab ability with a natural attack.
- **[Perceive Cues](perceive-cues.md)**: +5 Perception and Sense Motive 10 min./level.
- **[Protective Spirit](protective-spirit.md)**: Protects from attacks of opportunity.
- **[Slipstream](slipstream.md)**: Wave boosts creature’s speed.
- **[Stone Call](stone-call.md)**: 2d6 damage to all creatures in area.
- **Versatile Weapon**: Weapon bypasses some DR.

### 3rd-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Aspect of the Stag](aspect-of-the-stag.md)**: +2 AC against attacks of opportunity and increases speed.
- **[Bloody Claws](bloody-claws.md)**: Gives creature the ability to deal bleed damage with natural attacks.
- **[Cloak of Winds](cloak-of-winds.md)**: Creates a whirling screen of strong wind around you.
- **[Feather Step, Mass](feather-step-mass.md)**: As feather step, but affects many targets rather than one.
- **[Instant Enemy](instant-enemy.md)**: Target is treated as a favored enemy type of your choice.
- **[Life Bubble](life-bubble.md)**: Protects creatures from sustained environmental effects.
- **[Strong Jaw](strong-jaw.md)**: Natural attacks damage as two sizes bigger.
- **[Tireless Pursuers](tireless-pursuers.md)**: As tireless pursuit, but affects many targets rather than one.
- **Venomous Bolt**: Arrow or bolt poisons target.

### 4th-Level Ranger Spells

- **[Aspect of the Wolf](aspect-of-the-wolf.md)**: Subject gains +4 Str and Dex, and +2 bonus on trip attacks.
- **[Blessing of the Salamander](blessing-of-the-salamander.md)**: Subject gets fast healing 2, fire resistance 10, +2 CMD.
- **[Bow Spirit](bow-spirit.md)**: Summons an invisible spirit that fires your arrows for you as a swift action.
- **[Grove of Respite](grove-of-respite.md)**: Creates trees and a small spring.
