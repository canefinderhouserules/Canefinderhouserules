# Respectful Quiet

**School** illusion (glamer); **Level** bard 3, cleric 5, inquisitor 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Targets** any number of creatures in a 30-ft.-radius burst  
**Duration** 1 minute/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

All targets are rendered silent, as per [*silence*](silence.md). The silence affects the entirety of each creature but does not extend beyond. However, the silence is one-way—sounds from outside the creatures’ spaces can still enter. Quieted creatures gain a +10 circumstance bonus on [Stealth](../../rules/skills/stealth.md) checks.
