# Squeeze

**School** transmutation (polymorph); **Level** alchemist 2, sorcerer/wizard 2, witch 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 minute/level  
**Save** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless) The target becomes flexible regardless of its actual size and mass. It can move through areas at least half its size with no penalty for squeezing. It can move through a space at least one-quarter its width using the penalties for squeezing through a space at least half its width.
