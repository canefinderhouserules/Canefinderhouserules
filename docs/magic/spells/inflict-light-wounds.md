# Inflict Light Wounds

**School** necromancy; **Level** antipaladin 1, cleric 1, inquisitor 1, occultist 1, shaman 1, spiritualist 1, witch 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will half; **Spell Resistance** yes

When laying your hand upon a creature, you channel negative energy that deals 1d8 points of damage + 1 point per caster level (maximum +5).

Since undead are powered by negative energy, this spell cures such a creature of a like amount of damage, rather than harming it.

**Ranged casting**: You can cast this spell as if it were 1 spell level higher (for example, a level 1 spell becomes level 2). If you do, it requires a ranged touch attack instead of a touch, and has a range of close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels). A prepared caster with no way to cast it spontaneously must prepare it this way ahead of time (as if it had a metamagic feat applied, without needing the feat).
