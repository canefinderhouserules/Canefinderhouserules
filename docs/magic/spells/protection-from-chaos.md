# Protection from Chaos

**School** abjuration [lawful]; **Level** bloodrager 1, cleric 1, inquisitor 1, medium 1, paladin 1, shaman 1, sorcerer/wizard 1, spiritualist 1, summoner 1  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M/DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 min./level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** no; see text

This spell functions like [*protection from evil*](protection-from-evil.md), except that the deflection and resistance bonuses apply to attacks made by chaotic creatures. The target receives a new saving throw against control by chaotic creatures and chaotic summoned creatures cannot touch the target.
