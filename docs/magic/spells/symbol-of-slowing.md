# Symbol of Slowing

**School** transmutation; **Level** cleric 4, sorcerer/wizard 4, witch 4  
**Components** V, S, M (mercury and phosphorous, plus

powdered diamond and opal worth a total of 1,000 gp) Saving Throw Will negates; Spell Resistance yes This spell functions as symbol of death, except all creatures within 60 feet of a symbol of slowing are slowed (as the slow spell) for 1 round per caster level.

Symbol of slowing can be made permanent with a permanency spell by a caster of 11th level or higher for the cost of 10,000 gp.
