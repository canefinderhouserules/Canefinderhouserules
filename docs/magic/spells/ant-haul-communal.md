# Ant Haul, Communal

**School** transmutation; **Level** alchemist 2, cleric 2, druid 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, ranger 2, sorcerer/wizard 2, summoner 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (a small pulley)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creatures touched  
**Duration** 2 hours/level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

This spell functions like [*ant haul*](ant-haul.md), except you may divide the duration in 2-hour intervals among the creatures touched.
