# Reinvigorating Wind

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [air, mind-affecting]; **Level** bard 2, druid 3, shaman 2, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (a flower petal)  
**Range** 30 ft.  
**Area** cone-shaped burst  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

You exhale deeply, creating a gentle magical wind that invigorates any allies in the affected area, as follows. Any sleeping allies immediately wake up. [Fascinated](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#fascinated) allies are [shaken](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#shaken) free of the fascinate effect. [Flat-footed](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) allies no longer count as flat-footed even if they have not acted yet. The duration of effects that cause any allies to be [confused](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#confused), [frightened](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#frightened), [paralyzed 1](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed), slowed, or [stunned](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#stunned) is decreased by 1d4 rounds (roll separately for each target). If the duration of any such effect is reduced to 0 rounds or fewer, the effect ends for that ally. Finally, any allies lying [prone](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#prone) may stand up as an immediate action, provoking attacks of opportunity as normal.
