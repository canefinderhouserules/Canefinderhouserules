# Positive Pulse, Greater

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 4, inquisitor 4, paladin 3, shaman 4, sorcerer/wizard 4, spiritualist 4, summoner 3, witch 4  
**Area** 15-ft.-radius burst

This spell functions like [positive pulse](positive-pulse.md), except that the damage dealt increases to 3d6 plus your caster level, and the bonus on saving throws increases to +8.
