# Steal Years, Greater

**School** transmutation; **Level** cleric 5, druid 5, sorcerer/wizard 5, witch 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a handful of ash)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** creature touched  
**Duration** 1 day/level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like [steal years](steal-years.md), except you drain 1d6 years per two caster levels (maximum 10d6).
