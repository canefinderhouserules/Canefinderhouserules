# Lost Locale

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [mind-affecting]; **Level** sorcerer/wizard 9  
**Casting Time** 24 hours  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** 1 mile/level  
**Area** one 1-mile cube/level  
**Duration** 2 hours/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Will partial; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions as [*lost passage*](lost-passage.md) (see below), except that it affects a larger area.* Lost locale* can be made permanent with* permanency* at a cost of 90,000 gp.
