# Freedom

**School** abjuration; **Level** sorcerer/wizard 9  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels) or see text  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes

The subject is freed from spells and effects that restrict movement, including [*binding*](binding.md), [*entangle*](entangle.md), grappling, [*imprisonment*](imprisonment.md), [*maze*](maze.md), paralysis, petrification, pinning, [*sleep*](sleep.md), [*slow*](slow.md), stunning, [*temporal stasis*](temporal-stasis.md), and *web*. To free a creature from *imprisonment* or *maze,* you must know its name and background, and you must cast this spell at the spot where it was entombed or banished into the *maze*.
