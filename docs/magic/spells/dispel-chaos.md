# Dispel Chaos

**School** abjuration [lawful]; **Level** cleric 5, inquisitor 5, paladin 4, shaman 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (1 AP)  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Duration** 1 round/level or until discharged, whichever comes first  
**Saving Throw** see text; **Spell Resistance** see text

This spell functions like [*dispel evil*](dispel-evil.md), except that you are surrounded by constant, blue lawful energy, and the spell affects chaotic creatures and spells rather than evil ones.
