# Summon Monster 8

**School** conjuration (summoning) [see text]; **Level** cleric 8, psychic 8, sorcerer/wizard 8, summoner 6, witch 8  
**Casting Time** 1 round (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (a tiny bag and a small candle)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or more summoned creatures  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions like [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md), except that the creature’s CR equals 16 (this spell’s level × 2), up to a maximum of your level.
