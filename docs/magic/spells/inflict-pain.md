# Inflict Pain

**School** enchantment [mind-affecting, pain]; **Level** antipaladin 2, inquisitor 2, medium 2, mesmerist 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, sorcerer/wizard 3, spiritualist 2, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Will partial; see text; **Spell Resistance** yes

You telepathically wrack the target’s mind and body with agonizing pain that imposes a –4 penalty on attack rolls, skill checks, and ability checks. A successful Will save reduces the duration to 1 round.
