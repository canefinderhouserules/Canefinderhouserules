# Restful Cloak

**School** conjuration (healing); **Level** cleric 2, druid 2, occultist 2, paladin 2, ranger 2, shaman 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, F (cloak)  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 hour/level  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

A cloak you are wearing transforms into a canvas tent large enough to hold four Medium creatures, firmly pitched in a flat area adjacent to you (if there is no such area, this spell simply fails). A creature regains 1 hit point for each hour it rests in the tent, in addition to any other benefits of resting. Additionally, a fatigued creature resting within the tent for 1 hour loses the fatigued condition, but a creature cannot recover from being fatigued more than once per casting of *restful cloak*. At the end of the spell’s duration, the tent transforms back into a cloak. If the tent is moved prior to the expiration of the spell’s duration, the spell immediately ends.
