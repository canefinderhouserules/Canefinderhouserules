# Flesh to Stone

**School** transmutation; **Level** magus 6, shaman 6, sorcerer/wizard 6, witch 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (2 AP)  
**Components** V, S, M (lime, water, and earth)  
**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

The subject becomes [petrified 1](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#petrified): it loses its move action, keeps only its standard action, takes a –4 penalty on all rolls and damage, and has a 30% arcane spell failure chance on spells with somatic components. A subject that is already petrified becomes petrified 2: it turns, along with all its carried gear, into a mindless, inert statue. If the statue resulting from this spell is broken or damaged, the subject (if ever returned to its original state) has similar damage or deformities. The creature is not dead, but it does not seem to be alive either when viewed with spells such as [*deathwatch*](deathwatch.md).

Only creatures made of flesh are affected by this spell.
