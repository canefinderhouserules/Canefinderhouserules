# Chains of Light

**School** conjuration (creation) [good]; **Level** cleric 6, inquisitor 5, paladin 4, sorcerer/wizard 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, F (a length of fine golden chain)  
**Range** short (25 ft. + 5 ft./level)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** Reflex negates; **Spell Resistance** no

A creature targeted by this spell is held immobile by glowing golden chains composed of pure light. The creature is [paralyzed 1](../../rules/appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed) and held in place, but may attempt a new saving throw each round to end the effect. While held by the golden chains, a creature cannot use any sort of extradimensional travel, such as [*astral projection*](astral-projection.md), [*blink*](blink.md), [*dimension door*](dimension-door.md), [*ethereal jaunt*](ethereal-jaunt.md), [*etherealness*](etherealness.md), [*gate*](gate.md), [*maze*](maze.md), [*plane shift*](plane-shift.md), [*shadow walk*](shadow-walk.md), [*teleport*](teleport.md), and similar spells and spell-like abilities. The spell does not affect creatures that are already in ethereal or astral form when the spell is cast.
