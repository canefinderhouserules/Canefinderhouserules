# Binding Earth, Mass

**School** transmutation [earth]; **Level** druid 6, witch 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Target** one creature or object/level, no two of which can be more than 30 ft. apart This spell functions as *binding earth*, except as noted above.  
**Duration** 1 round/level  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes
