# Demon Dream

**School** illusion (phantasm) [evil, mind-affecting]; **Level** cleric 6  
**Casting Time** see text  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** unlimited  
**Target** one living creature  
**Duration** see text  
**Saving Throw** see text; **Spell Resistance** yes

When the target of *demon dream* is a worshiper of a demon lord or the goddess of monsters, the caster has the option of making the spell function instead as a [*dream*](dream.md) spell. Otherwise, this spell functions like [*nightmare*](nightmare.md), save that the subject matter of the dream always involves demons. Upon waking from a *demon dream*, the victim must make a saving throw against the spell to resist taking 1d6 points of Wisdom drain from the unsettling, madness- inducing dream—this is in addition to the damage and fatigue normally caused by the *nightmare*.
