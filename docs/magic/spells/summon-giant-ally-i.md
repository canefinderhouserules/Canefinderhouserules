# Summon Giant Ally I

**School** conjuration (summoning) [see text]; **Level** druid 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action (3 AP)  
**Components** V, S, F/DF (bit of cloth from a giant’s bag)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one or more giants  
**Duration** 1 round/level (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell functions as *summon nature’s ally VI*, except its casting time is shorter and you can summon one cave, hill, marsh, river, slag, stone, or wood giant.

**Summoning rules**: This spell follows the reworked [*summon monster I*](summon-monster-i.md) rules: the creature’s CR equals this spell’s level × 2, up to a maximum of your level. Instead of the creatures listed here, you can summon any giant, and this CR limit is 1 higher because the spell specializes in that type. This spell can’t summon any other kind of creature.
