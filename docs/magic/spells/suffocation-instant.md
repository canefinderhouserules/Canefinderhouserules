# Suffocation, Instant

**School** necromancy [death]; **Level** pesh adept 5  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S  
**Range** touch  
**Target** living creature touched  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude partial; **Spell Resistance** yes

This spell functions like [*slay living*](slay-living.md), and kills by immediate suffocation. Creatures that do not need to breathe are immune to this spell. The target is considered to have taken a dose of pesh for the purpose of addiction and withdrawal effects.

**Save-or-die**: The target rolls its saving throw twice and keeps the higher result, as explained in [Save-or-Die Spells](../casting-spells.md#save-or-die-spells).
