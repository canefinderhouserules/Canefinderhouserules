# Summon Totem Creature

**School** conjuration (summoning); **Level** cleric 3, druid 3, ranger 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, summoner 3  
**Casting Time** 10 minutes  
**Components** V, S, M/DF (a piece of bone from any one of your totem animals)  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Effect** one summoned creature  
**Duration** 1 hour (D)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

Some tribal peoples revere not only distant deities but also the animals and natural forces of the rugged lands they call home. Their spellcasters have learned ways to call upon the might of their clan's totem creatures in times of need. A character must have been raised in such a tribal culture and be considered part of a totem clan to be able to cast this spell. Characters with access to this spell can only summon creatures revered by the clan they are a part of; the GM assigns each clan a list of three creature types from the *summon nature's ally III* list (for example, small air elemental, 1d3 eagles, and small earth elemental). Except as noted above, this spell functions as *summon nature's ally III*.
