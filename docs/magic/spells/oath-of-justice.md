# Oath of Justice

**School** necromancy; **Level** cleric 3, paladin 2  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** touch  
**Target** two creatures touched  
**Duration** permanent (see text)  
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

This spell seals a solemn vow between two creatures. When this spell is cast, the targets must clasp hands and swear their oath in the name of the caster's deity. The spell functions like [*mark of justice*](mark-of-justice.md), except as noted above and rather than being cursed, the oath-breaker gains a mark on the face indicating to all dwarves who see it that the target has broken a sacred oath, which gives the oath-breaker a –4 penalty to influence dwarves. The mark can be removed as described in the *mark of justice* spell, or the other target can forgive the oath-breaker, which causes the mark to vanish.
