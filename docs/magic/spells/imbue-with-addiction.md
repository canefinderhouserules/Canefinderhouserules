# Imbue with Addiction

**School** transmutation; **Level** alchemist 2, sorcerer/wizard 3, witch 3  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, M (a dose of an addictive drug or substance)  
**Range** touch  
**Target** one living creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

The target immediately becomes addicted to the drug used during the casting of the spell (use the drug and addiction rules). If the target was ever addicted to the drug at any point in the past, it takes a –4 penalty on its saving throw.
