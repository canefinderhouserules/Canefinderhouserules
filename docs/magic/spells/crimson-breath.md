# Crimson Breath

**School** transmutation; **Level** alchemist 4, cleric 4, druid 4, sorcerer/wizard 4, witch 4  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** S  
**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)  
**Target** one creature  
**Duration** instantaneous  
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates; **Spell Resistance** no

When you cast this spell, your salivary glands transform and instantly fill with potent venom. Make a ranged touch attack against a single target in range; if you hit, the target is affected by a dose of [breath of the assassin](../../equipment/gear-ag.md). The Fortitude save DC for this poison is the same as the spell’s DC, not the standard save DC for the poison.
