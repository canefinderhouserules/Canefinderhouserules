# Shield of Dawn, Greater

**School** evocation [fire, good, light]; **Level** bard 6, cleric 6, magus 6  
**Casting Time** 1 standard action  
**Components** V, S, DF  
**Range** personal  
**Target** you  
**Duration** 1 round/level

You create a disk of sunlight on one arm. Any creature that strikes you with a melee attack deals normal damage, but also takes 1d6 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. Half of this damage is fire damage and half is holy damage (as the spell *flame strike*). An undead creature or a creature particularly vulnerable to light that hits you instead takes 2d6 points of damage + 1 point per caster level. Creatures with reach weapons are not subject to this damage if they attack you. The shield provides illumination equivalent to a *daylight* spell. You can have only one instance of this spell in effect at a time. It does not stack with similar damaging aura spells such as *fire shield*.
