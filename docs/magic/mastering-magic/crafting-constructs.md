# Crafting Constructs

Rules for building a construct step by step, plus new construct modifications and the automaton subtype. See [Building and Modifying Constructs](constructs.md) for the base rules, animated objects, and the original list of construct modifications.

To craft a construct, its creator must have the Craft Construct feat and meet any caster level requirement for that construct. The creator pays the cost listed in the construction requirements (part of it buys the raw materials for the body, the rest the oils and reagents that animate it) and spends 1 day of work per 1,000 gp of the construct's market price. At the end, the creator must succeed at a skill check (the skill and DC are listed in the construction requirements) and cast or otherwise replicate each listed spell. Each spell requirement the crafter cannot provide raises the DC by 5. As a general rule, player characters cannot craft constructs that have no construction requirements listed.

## Requirements

**Item Creation Feat**: You must have Craft Construct, which itself requires Craft Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Wondrous Item, and caster level 5th. Some constructs require additional item creation feats, and these are mandatory too.

**Spell Requirements**: Listed in the construction requirements. As with other magic items, you must have prepared the spells (or know them, if you cast spontaneously) but need not provide material components or focuses. Working on the construct expends the prepared spells each day of creation, just as if you had cast them. Each spell requirement you do not meet raises the crafting DC by 5.

**Caster Level**: More powerful constructs require a minimum caster level. Unlike other requirements, this one must be met; it cannot be ignored by raising the DC.

**Special Requirements**: Some constructs have special requirements. Robots, as a general rule, cannot be crafted by player characters because they require highly specialized technology. Unless the description says otherwise, any non-feat requirement can be bypassed by raising the DC by 5.

## Cost

You must gather the materials before beginning: the body itself, plus additional materials worth half the construct's base price. The cost listed in the construction requirements includes both and must be paid in full before work begins.

**Finding Materials**: Usually the body's materials are assumed to be available for purchase. If they are not (the GM decides), they must be collected by other means, which may involve an adventure. If a body is already found fully prepared, its cost need not be paid.

## Time

Animating a construct takes 1 day for every 1,000 gp of its market price (not the cost paid). A day of work is 8 hours of uninterrupted labor in a fairly quiet, comfortable, well-lit place. Some constructs require specialized equipment or laboratories.

**Accelerated Work**: You cannot work more than 8 hours a day on a construct. If you raise the DC to create it by 5 (decided before work starts), you need only 4 hours of work per 1,000 gp of base price, which lets you do 2 days' worth of work in one day.

**Dangerous Conditions**: A crafter working while adventuring or in similarly distracting or dangerous conditions can devote only 4 hours a day and works at half pace (completing only 2 hours of work that day).

**Intermittent Creation**: Each 8-hour (or 4-hour) block must be continuous, but you may stop between blocks, even for months, provided you do not start crafting something else.

**Simultaneous Creation**: You can work on only one item at a time. If you start a new item (of any kind), all materials and progress on the previous item are wasted.

## Skill Check

The check is attempted at the very end of the process.

**Skill Type**: Listed in the construction requirements. Most constructs allow a Craft skill, and some allow others (a flesh golem can be made with Heal). Most list more than one skill; you choose which to use.

**DC**: Listed in the construction requirements. Generally it equals 5 + the construct's default caster level, plus modifiers: +5 for accelerated crafting, and +5 for each optional prerequisite you fail to meet.

**Failure**: If you fail by less than 5, the attempt fails and the materials and time are wasted. Normally, failing a magic item check by 5 or more yields a cursed item. At the GM's discretion, a construct that fails by 5 or more may instead explode, or be uncontrolled and violent.

## The Finished Construct

A construct recognizes its creator intuitively and obeys all of the creator's commands. A new construct has the average hit points for its Hit Dice and is undamaged. Most constructs are mindless and have no feats or skills; the few with an Intelligence score have feats and skill ranks chosen by their creator at creation, which cannot later be changed.

## Other Methods of Gaining a Construct

**Golem Manuals**: A [golem manual](../../magic-items/construct-items.md) grants Craft Construct for the purpose of building one type of golem, increases your caster level for that purpose, grants a +5 competence bonus on the skill check, and holds all the needed spells (usable only to craft that golem, by anyone who has those spells on a class spell list). You still spend the normal time and pay for (or provide) the body, but not the rest of the cost. For example, with a clay golem manual you would provide a body worth 1,500 gp (instead of the full 21,500 gp), spend 42 days building it, need caster level 9th (the manual gives +2 against the normal 11th), have *animate objects*, *bless*, *commune*, *prayer*, and *resurrection* on a class spell list, and succeed at a DC 16 Craft (sculptures) or Craft (pottery) check with a +5 bonus.

**Purchasing a Construct**: Any craftable construct has a market price, usually close to twice the cost to craft. Buying follows the normal rules for purchasing magic items, so a very expensive construct may require traveling to a sufficiently large city.

**Stealing a Construct**: Constructs are large and heavy, and stealing one is not useful without a way to control it. The *control construct* spell can bring a construct to a desired location, where a final command can set it to guard the spot or wait to be controlled again. If the true owner tracks it down, or you lose control, the stolen construct might be turned against you.

## Other Kinds of Constructs

**Animated Objects**: Normally temporary through *animate objects*, they can be made permanent with *permanency*, and the rules in [Building and Modifying Constructs](constructs.md) allow permanent animated objects to be created with Craft Construct. They are relatively easy to hide in plain sight.

**Colossi**: Enormous mythic constructs built on a golem's principles at far larger scale. Only the greatest builders can craft one. Every colossus can transform between two forms, and many alternate golem abilities suit colossi at the GM's discretion.

**Homunculi**: A homunculus is empowered by a magical bond with an individual who gave their blood for its creation. It has an Intelligence score, making it a capable lab assistant, and a basic homunculus can be crafted by anyone with Craft Construct.

## New Construct Modifications

These modifications alter or enhance existing constructs; the base rules are in [Building and Modifying Constructs](constructs.md).

### Basic Modifications

**Movement Modification**: Improves existing movement. A land speed increases by 10 feet per purchase (2,000 gp each), up to double its original speed. Climb or swim speeds improve the same way at 3,000 gp per 10 feet, and fly speeds at 4,000 gp per 10 feet. A construct can also gain a new climb or swim speed of 15 feet for 6,000 gp, or a fly speed of 15 feet for 8,000 gp if it has at least 5 Hit Dice. A Tiny or smaller construct gains good maneuverability, Medium or Small average, Large or Huge poor, and Gargantuan or larger clumsy.

**Resistances**: The construct gains energy resistance 5 to acid, cold, electricity, or fire for 4,000 gp. Existing resistance can be improved up to double its value or 15, whichever is lower, for 6,000 gp for every increment of 5. A construct vulnerable to an energy type cannot gain resistance to it this way.

**Sense Modification**: Extends darkvision by 30 feet (to a maximum of 120 feet) for 2,500 gp per increment. Adds blindsense at 4,000 gp per 30 feet (maximum 60 feet). Adds blindsight to a construct with blindsense at 7,500 gp per 15 feet (maximum 30 feet).

**Spell-Like Ability Modification**: Grants a spell-like ability of any level that the creator can cast, usable once per day, for 3,000 gp per spell level (a 0-level spell counts as level 1/2). Paying again adds another daily use. The total spell levels gained this way cannot exceed the construct's Hit Dice (for example, *fireball* three times per day requires 9 Hit Dice).

### Complex Modifications

Each has requirements, a cost equal to the minimum level to cast the spell × the spell level × 250 gp, and sometimes a CR increase.

**Alarm Link** (Requirements Craft Construct, *alarm*; CR increase none; Cost 250 gp): The creator sets a password. Any creature the construct meets that does not speak it within 1 round triggers a mental alarm that alerts the creator within 1 mile, even waking the creator from nonmagical sleep without disturbing concentration. The creator can deactivate or reactivate it as a standard action. Each additional mile of range costs 250 gp.

**Construct Shelter** (Requirements Craft Construct, Craft Wondrous Item, *mage's private sanctum*, Small or larger construct; CR increase none; Cost 11,250 gp): The construct transforms (10 minutes) into a shelter housing its creator and allies. A Colossal construct holds one Colossal, two Gargantuan, four Huge, eight Large, 16 Medium, or 32 Small creatures; a Gargantuan construct holds one Gargantuan, two Huge, four Large, eight Medium, or 16 Small creatures, and so on down the sizes. There are no accommodations beyond the basics for rest. Occupants can see out; outsiders cannot see in. The construct can still receive orders and can eject creatures (Reflex DC 10 + the construct's CR avoids ejection) or refuse entry. A creature forcing entry must win a Strength check opposed by the construct's, or deal damage equal to one-quarter the construct's total hit points, which reduces its hit points by that amount. The shelter has AC 10 and keeps any damage reduction or hardness.

**Construct Storage** (Requirements Craft Construct, Craft Wondrous Item, *secret chest*, Small or larger construct; CR increase none; Cost 11,250 gp): An extradimensional space like a *bag of holding*. A Small construct holds 4 cubic feet and 25 pounds; capacity doubles for each size category larger, to 128 cubic feet and 800 pounds for a Colossal construct. The construct retrieves an object as a swift action; others retrieve one as a move action.

**Mind Link** (Requirements Craft Construct, *telepathy*; CR increase none; Cost 16,500 gp): The creator can issue orders mentally from within 100 feet. Unintelligent constructs can receive orders but cannot respond.

**Self-Repair** (Requirements Craft Construct, *make whole*; CR +1; Cost 1,500 gp): As a swift action, the construct gains fast healing 5 for a number of rounds per day equal to half its Hit Dice (minimum 1), not necessarily consecutive. It can be taken multiple times, each adding that many rounds per day up to double its Hit Dice. A construct that already has fast healing instead increases it by 2 per purchase, up to double its original value.

**Self-Destruct** (Requirements Craft Construct, *fireball*; CR +1; Cost 3,750 gp): When reduced to 0 or fewer hit points, the construct explodes in a 10-foot burst dealing 1d6 slashing damage per point of its CR (Reflex DC 10 + half its Hit Dice + its Constitution modifier halves). If it already has a similar ability, that ability's DC increases by 4 instead.

## Automatons

Automatons are intelligent constructs built long ago by a group of artificers who transplanted their own minds and life energy into advanced bodies. The secret of making them is lost, though rumors of new automatons or similar constructs persist.

**Automaton Subtype**: Automatons are lawful neutral constructs with the extraplanar subtype. Unless a creature's entry says otherwise, an automaton has:

- Darkvision 120 feet and low-light vision.
- Immunity to electricity.
- Resistance to cold 10 and sonic 10.
- Most are resistant to magic, with SR equal to 11 + its CR.
- Telepathy.
- Proficiency with its natural weapons, all simple weapons, and all weapons mentioned in its entry.
- Natural weapons and any wielded weapons are treated as lawful and magic for overcoming damage reduction.
- **Automaton Core (Ex)**: A destroyed automaton leaves behind an [automaton core](../../magic-items/construct-items.md), which can be harvested with a successful Spellcraft check.
- **Intelligent Construct (Ex)**: They can take class levels, gain feats, and gain class skills and extra skill points: 4 + Intelligence modifier (minimum 1) per Hit Die. Class skills are Climb, Craft, Diplomacy, Fly, Intimidate, Knowledge (all), Perception, Sense Motive, Spellcraft, Stealth, and Use Magic Device.
- **Vulnerable Mind (Ex)**: They are not immune to mind-affecting effects.

**Automaton Cores**: Each core holds a spark of consciousness from one individual combined with a large portion of planar energy. Removing a core from a destroyed or willing automaton takes a number of hours equal to half the automaton's CR, followed by a Spellcraft check (DC 20 + the automaton's CR). On a success, the core is removed and usable. On a failure, the attempt cannot be repeated for 24 hours; failing by more than 5 damages the core and reduces its spell points by 1. Returning a core to an inactive automaton's body takes another check with the same DC. If the consciousness in the body and the new core are incompatible or unwilling to meld, they make opposed Charisma checks; the winner becomes dominant, though it may be challenged again later. Powering an automaton with a different core can cause a mental struggle; it usually ends with one personality prevailing, but occasionally creates a new, shared consciousness.
