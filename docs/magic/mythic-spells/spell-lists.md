# Mythic Spell Lists

These lists show, by class, which spells have a mythic version. Spell names link to the entry in the mythic spell descriptions. Spells marked with an asterisk (*) are new spells with their own page in the main spell lists. See [Mythic Spells](index.md) for the rules on learning and casting mythic spells.

The spells are in alphabetical order by name, except that a spell whose name begins with "lesser," "greater," or "mass" is alphabetized under its second word.
