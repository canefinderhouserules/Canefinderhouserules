# Jutsu

Any of the 3 classes can build and cast essentially any jutsu as long as they have the required level; there are no spellbooks here. The number in brackets in the name is the spell level (that is, which sphere it belongs to).

Only non-elemental jutsu, or jutsu of an element your character has, can be used. You cannot use jutsu of an element your character has not mastered.

You can also make "physical techniques": something that in the game world is not strictly a jutsu but is still a technique that spends chakra, and therefore by the system is still a spell, and so a jutsu. Basically for melee things, or unique oddities such as the Hyuga clan's techniques.

There is a series of free jutsu, which do not spend a spell known; all shinobi have them.

## Free Jutsu

**Tree Walking [1]:** lets you walk, run and move in general on any hard surface, vertical or otherwise. Immediate action. Costs no chakra. Climb speed equal to your normal speed. If upcast to 2nd sphere, it becomes a free action (effectively permanent as soon as you can cast 2nd sphere, because it has no cost).

**Chakra Buff [1]:** you spend chakra to increase one of your physical faculties. You gain +2 to a physical ability score. Upcast to 2nd sphere it is +4, to 3rd sphere +6, and to the 5th and last sphere +8. 5 chakra. Swift action. Personal. Lasts 1 round.

**Illusory Clone [1]:** you create false clones of yourself to confuse the enemy. 2 chakra per clone. Basically *mirror image*, but you choose the number of clones. Standard action. Lasts 1 round/level. You can create up to a maximum set by the maximum cost of the jutsu's level (a first-sphere jutsu has a maximum cost of 10, so if you cannot upcast it to the second sphere, your maximum is 5 clones).

**Substitution Jutsu [1]:** you swap places with a nearby object to avoid being damaged. Immediate action. 5 chakra. +2 AC and saves when using it. Upcast to the third sphere, it lets you use an enemy instead of an object to take the attack, but the enemy must be adjacent and cannot be the one who made the attack (unless it is a ranged attack and somehow they threw it from afar and are adjacent). Using an enemy requires a touch attack from the user of this jutsu.

**Transformation Jutsu [1]:** 5 chakra, 1 chakra per turn. May require a Disguise check. Works like *alter self* but gives no abilities or ability score bonuses.

**Water Walking [2]:** lets you walk, run and move in general on water or any other liquid, avoiding contact with it and sinking. Immediate action. If upcast to 3rd, it becomes a free action (effectively permanent as soon as you can cast third sphere, because it has no cost).

**Body Flicker Jutsu [2]:** full-round action, 20 chakra. You instantly move your speed ×5. Provokes attacks of opportunity.

## Other Jutsu

The following jutsu, listed as they are built, do require that you spend a spell known to use them.

**Summoning Jutsu [2]:** (to be defined).

**Shadow Clone Jutsu [3]:** the user creates identical clones of themselves. They have their own personality, so they may be prone to confusion. They keep all the attributes of whoever creates them, but divide the chakra in half and have only 10% of the health. When they disappear they return half the chakra spent creating them to their user, along with their experiences. 50 chakra, returning half when dispelled. They have half the power of the original. Each clone has a fraction of the original's chakra proportional to the number of clones (one clone would have half the chakra, two clones would have 1/3 each). Full-round action.
