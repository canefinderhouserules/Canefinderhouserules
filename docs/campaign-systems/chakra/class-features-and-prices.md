# Class Features and Prices

## Class Features and Their Prices

Every class feature is priced as Iconic, OP, Common or Weak. Each time you choose a class feature you can take 1 Iconic, or 1 OP, or 2 Common, or 4 Weak (or 1 Common and 2 Weak). **From 12th level on, the class features gained at 12th, 14th, 16th and 18th level are smaller: each one gives you only 1 Common or 2 Weak class features (never OP or Iconic).**

**Limits:** you can have only 1 Iconic class feature, 1 OP class feature chosen while your character is level 1–10, and 1 OP class feature chosen while your character is level 11–20 (what counts is your level when you choose it, not the level of the feature). An Iconic class feature costs the same as an OP one; the category only exists to stop combinations of the strongest class features. Iconic class features are the ones that define a class or are far better than other OP class features.

**Prestige classes:** in this system, a prestige class feature counts as 3 levels higher than the level at which its prestige class gains it (a feature gained at 5th level counts as 8th level). The lists below already include this; the prestige classes themselves do not change.

The class features each class can copy are listed by level and price:

- [Shinobi Warrior Class Features](class-features-shinobi-warrior.md)
- [Shinobi Specialist Class Features](class-features-shinobi-specialist.md)
- [Jutsu Master Class Features](class-features-jutsu-master.md)

Class features that many classes repeat are unified in [Generic Class Features](generic-class-features.md): instead of copying one of them, you take the generic version and choose what it applies to. These exist only in this system.

## Feats and Class Features Unique to This System

**Extra Class Feature [Feat]:** Grants one extra class feature choice. If it is of the same level as the feat you are spending, it cannot be OP, and it only gives you 1 Common or 2 Weak features. If it is 4 levels lower, it can be OP (negotiable), and you can take 2 Common or 4 Weak features, or a combination of both.  
It can be taken multiple times. It is NOT a combat feat (so no abusing it with a full-BAB class).

**Accelerated Caster [Common Class Feature]:** Exclusive to the Jutsu Master. It gives you an accelerated spell progression similar to a wizard's: essentially you gain 1 spell known of the next spell level one class level earlier, and the rest stays the same (for example, at level 3 you gain 1 known 2nd-level spell, which stays at only 1 at level 4, and at level 5 you gain the second one and the first 3rd-level one).

**Level as BAB [OP Class Feature]:** Only for the Shinobi Specialist. Your level counts as your base attack bonus. You also use the Shinobi Warrior's chakra (6 per level instead of 8), and you lose 1 spell known of each spell level.

**Caster Specialist [OP Class Feature]:** Only for the Jutsu Master. Your base attack bonus advances as 3/4 instead of 1/2. Your chakra die drops to d8.

**Individual Blood Contract:** you have convinced and signed an official contract with a particular member of one of the many ninja animal groups. Reduces the cost of summoning it by 30%.

**Improved Chakra Control:** mandatory to be a medic. It lets you learn healing jutsu and activate some other particular techniques. Most shinobi end up learning it anyway; medics simply get it first, while the others get it through pure practice and years of work. [Toughness, but ×2 and for chakra.]

**Extra Elemental Affinity:** you gain one extra element. It has no level requirement the first time. This feat can be taken multiple times until you have all the elements. If your class grants you an element when you already have all of them, you retrain this feat for free. This feat can only be taken again at an interval of 6 levels (for example, if you take it at level 1, you cannot take it again until level 7).

**Aggression**

- **Friendly:** unless attacked, it does not attack, and even when attacking it tends to do so in non-lethal ways. It is easy to become its friend and control it.
- **Not aggressive:** unless attacked, it does not attack. It is sometimes inclined to help its host, though this still makes it somewhat hard to control.
- **Aggressive:** attacks anything nearby. Very hard to befriend.
- **Very aggressive:** attacks everything nearby and constantly tempts its host to let it out. Befriending it is legendary.

### Timeline

**Year 0:** taken as the baseline: the separation of the tailed beasts and the birth of ninjutsu at the hands of the Sage of the Six Paths.

**Year 250:** ninjutsu begins to become common across the world, though not very. The oldest clans form and start experimenting with ninjutsu, creating techniques and abusing the rarity of the tailed beasts (still without jinchuriki).

**Year 400:** most tailed beasts begin to despise the growing shinobi culture, which, lacking real knowledge, has experimented on them like mere lab rats.

**Year 500:** the daimyo realize the power of the shinobi and seek to hire them, paying exorbitant sums. This starts conflicts between the clans themselves over who should take which job, since accepting one was usually compared to surviving any winter without the slightest difficulty.

**Year 600:** some shinobi clans swear loyalty to different countries but remain disorganized. Many clans disappear, new ones are born constantly and the large ones keep growing thanks to the daimyo's money. The position of the daimyo's favorite becomes far too coveted. It is said that in this year the flames of the era of warring states began.

**Year 700:** the brutal wars between shinobi clans have only grown nonstop over the last hundred years. The daimyo did not know how to control them and no ordinary person could stand up to them, creating absolute chaos where every clan, large or small, constantly started and ended wars, aiming to steal other clans' techniques or simply ruin a rival's reputation to win the favor of the leaders of the world's countries.

**Years 710–725:** several clans decide the unthinkable and begin to join forces, seeking to unify clans under a single leader who swears loyalty to the Daimyo. What would years later be known as the five great shinobi villages are among the first to be unified and founded. News of these foundations motivates the other clans of the world to try to conquer and unify other clans in order to found what would later be known as the Hidden Shinobi Villages. By the end of those 15 years of seeking peace, the five most powerful nations had an organized shinobi village and knew peace for the first time after more than a hundred years of constant conflict. In the small, weak countries, however, conflicts and wars between clans persist; many clans refuse to abandon their lifestyle and join others, resisting the change of era.

**Year 741, First Shinobi World War:** after several years of peace between the five largest countries, a massive conflict breaks out in which the shinobi villages of those countries seek to gain power. The war was extremely devastating and almost no side could truly be called a winner. It ended only when the First Hokage managed to capture 8 of the 9 tailed beasts and offered them to the different shinobi villages to keep the balance of power.

**Year 761, Second Shinobi World War:** when the economies of the great countries began to diverge, some recovering much faster from the effects of the first war and reaching great wealth while others suffered enormously, those jealous of others' riches took the first step toward this war. With the First Hokage having died of old age ten years earlier and the Second Hokage leading the most prominent village, his death was sought by many until it was finally achieved, and Sarutobi was named Third Hokage after the sacrifice of Tobirama Senju. The war ended in a few years with the devastation of the Land of Rain and several others used as central battlefields. Konoha is considered its victor, although some other great nations managed to draw benefits from it.

**Year 768, Third Shinobi World War:** noticing a growing weakness in the most important shinobi villages, the small countries, desperate for more territory, began arming and expanding along the borders of the great nations. Soon all the great nations ended up joining the battles, seeking to avoid losing territory, and some even to conquer territory by exploiting the weakness.
