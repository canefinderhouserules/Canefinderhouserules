# Elements

Instead of just being another type of damage, each element has its own small distinction to make it more interesting. Every one has some unique effect.

## Common Elements

**Fire:** +1 caster level for damage and to overcome spell resistance. Leaves burn charges (1d6 per spell level per turn for 3 turns).

**Wind:** +1 range step (short becomes medium, and so on). Improvable with tools. +5 ft. to the area of area spells.

**Lightning:** +1 DC and attack (for spells that depend on an attack roll). Leaves –1 to all rolls per spell level, for 2 turns.

**Earth:** every defensive or enhancement jutsu works as if it were 2 levels better and affected by Extend.

**Water:** cannot be used without a nearby water source unless it is a level 6 or higher jutsu. Every Water jutsu costs 5 or 10 less chakra (minimum 1), depending on how large the nearby water source is. If there is a large water source (lakes, rivers, sea, and so on), they also gain the effects of Widen for free.

## Extra Elements

Each of these is taken as a feat.

**Ice:** cannot be used without a nearby water source unless it is a level 6 or higher jutsu. Every Water jutsu costs 5 or 10 less chakra (minimum 1), depending on the size of the nearby water source. With a large water source (lakes, rivers, sea, and so on) it also gains the effects of Widen for free. +1 DC and attack (for spells that depend on an attack roll). *Lore: bloodline technique from the Land of Water. The clan is extinct, with its last users scattered around the world.*

**Lava:** every defensive or enhancement jutsu works as if it were 1 level better and affected by Extend. +2 caster level for damage and to overcome spell resistance. Leaves burn charges (1d6 per spell level per turn for 3 turns). Every Lava jutsu costs 5 to 10 more chakra (single target or area, respectively). *Lore: a small clan from the "Haze Village" ninja village, destroyed in the second war, with very few survivors; they used to be at war with Konoha. A good share emigrated to the Land of Water only to fall into the bloodline hunt there.*

**Wood:** +2 DC and attack (for spells that depend on an attack roll). Every defensive or enhancement jutsu works as if it were 2 levels better and affected by Extend. *Lore: from the Senju clan, founders of Konoha. Many lost their lives during the conflicts caused by Madara Uchiha before the first war; it is rumored that only Hashirama survived and was the last Wood user.*

**Storm:** +1 caster level for damage, and +1 DC. Cannot be used without a nearby water source unless it is a level 6 or higher jutsu. Every Water jutsu costs 5 or 10 less chakra (minimum 1), depending on the size of the nearby water source. With a large water source it also gains the effects of Widen for free. *A clan of the village hidden in the clouds.*

**Explosion:** maximum range for jutsu of this element is Short. A single-target jutsu also affects one adjacent target. They gain the effects of Widen for free. Every Explosion jutsu costs 5 to 10 more chakra (single target or area, respectively). *A unique bloodline technique of the village hidden in the rocks.*

**Steam:** +2 caster level for damage. Ignores 50% of armor or damage reduction. *A bloodline technique of the Land of Water. Like all organized clans with bloodline techniques in the Land of Water, it was persecuted by people out of fear and ended up separated and lost, with a few survivors scattered around the world.*

**Crystal:** +2 attack and DC. Every defensive or enhancement jutsu works as if it were 1 level better and affected by Extend. *An unusual bloodline technique of a lost clan; little is known about it. Its clan possibly never stopped being nomadic and ended up dying out because of it when all the clans settled in hidden villages.*

**Mud:** cannot be used without a nearby water source unless it is a level 6 or higher jutsu. Every Water jutsu costs 5 or 10 less chakra (minimum 1), depending on the size of the nearby water source. With a large water source it also gains the effects of Widen for free. Every defensive or enhancement jutsu works as if it were 1 level better and affected by Extend. *A bloodline technique native to the village of Numagakure, of the Sakai clan, much appreciated there for the benefits it has brought. Formerly from the Land of Water, a good number emigrated and founded Numagakure during the bloodline hunt; others escaped on their own.*

**Scorch:** +1 range step (short becomes medium, and so on) and +2 caster level for damage and spell resistance. *A bloodline technique of the Land of Wind. The clan is half lost; not many survivors developed this element.*

**Magnetism:** *a bloodline technique of the village hidden in the sand, from the direct family of the third and fourth Kazekage. Not everyone inherits it, so it is unusual.*

Steel, Gold and others: to be defined.
