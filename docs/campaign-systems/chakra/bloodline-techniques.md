# Bloodline Techniques

**Byakugan** *(2 feats)*: activated as a standard action (a feat makes it a swift/immediate action). Costs 5 chakra per turn. While active it gives: +1 critical range (not multiplicable), +2 AC, +4 Will saves against jutsu, 360° vision, +10 on Perception checks, and immunity to flanking and to being flat-footed. Allows use of "Gentle Fist" techniques.

**Ketsuryūgan** *(feat)*: a dojutsu of the Chinoike clan of the Land of Lightning, completely wiped out by the Uchiha clan. When active it lets the user manipulate a new element, blood, and significantly improves their genjutsu. +3 genjutsu DC.

**Sharingan, 1 tomoe** *(feat)*: activating the Sharingan costs 5 chakra per turn and a swift/immediate action. +2 AC and Reflex, +2 genjutsu DC, +4 Perception, allows copying jutsu seen in the same battle.

**Sharingan, 2 tomoe** *(feat)*: costs 5 chakra per turn and a swift/immediate action. +3 AC and Reflex, +3 genjutsu DC, +5 Perception, allows copying jutsu seen in the same battle.

**Sharingan, 3 tomoe** *(feat)*: costs 5 chakra per turn and a swift/immediate action. +4 AC and Reflex, +4 genjutsu DC, +6 Perception, allows copying jutsu seen in the same battle.

**Mangekyo Sharingan** *(???)*: costs 5 chakra per turn and a swift/immediate action. +5 AC and Reflex, +5 genjutsu DC, +7 Perception, allows copying jutsu seen in the same battle. A unique and unmatched ability for each eye. Susanoo.

**Shikotsumyaku** *(feat, perhaps 2)*: this bloodline technique lets the ninja modify their bones almost freely, from their calcium to their density and a good variety of other things, chiefly regeneration, which happens almost immediately and subconsciously. Despite everything positive it may seem to have, the body is extremely strange because of the changes the technique causes, so any medic's healing is reduced to 1/4 effectiveness on them. 10 fast healing/regeneration. Access to the "bone" element. All healing other than fast healing is reduced to 1/4 effectiveness. Without chakra, the regeneration is nullified.

**Elemental bloodline** *(feat)*: you gain access to a unique element, or one combined from two others. It can only be taken with a level 1 feat.
