# Effects of Being a Vampire

!!! note "Vampire subsystem"
    This is a custom subsystem, separate from the normal Pathfinder rules.

## Vampire Type (racial traits applied to the race itself)

- Immunity to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, patterns and phantasms), but only if the vampire is of a higher generation (lower number) than the one casting them.
- Immunity to bleed, death effects, disease, paralysis, poison, sleep effects and stunning, with some limitation for cases where diseases or poisons are in the blood of someone you just fed on.
- Negative energy (such as an inflict spell) can heal undead creatures.
- Immunity to any effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless), but only if the vampire is of a higher generation than whoever is casting it.
- Not affected by raise dead and reincarnate spells or abilities.
- Undead do not breathe, eat or sleep.
- DR 10/slashing, resistance to cold 10, vulnerability to fire.
- Exposing any vampire to direct sunlight staggers it on the first round of exposure and destroys it utterly on the second consecutive round of exposure if it does not escape.
- Driving a wooden stake through a helpless vampire's heart instantly slays it (this is a full-round action). However, it returns to life if the stake is removed, unless the head is also severed.
- You can't rest and don't recover hit points.
- Disciplines.
- Blood points.
- **Fire sensitivity:** seeing fire in great quantity and/or up close, or directly the sun, causes effects ranging from panicked to shaken or paralyzed. Make a save (see the table below).
- **Clan curse:** see each [clan](clans.md).
- **Frenzy:** if they have less than 20% of their maximum blood (or for many other reasons that can call for a frenzy check), they must make a Will save or enter frenzy. The DC varies, but the most common is 20.
    - **Frenzy state:** depending on what causes it, the actions you are able to take are limited, ranging from killing and draining everything near you (cases of hunger or risk of death) to fleeing the place, going through anything necessary (red fear). Whatever it is, this state grants +4 to all three physical ability scores, but forbids all mental actions or actions that depend on mental ability scores.

| Fire sensitivity save result | Effect |
|---|---|
| 25 or higher | Nothing happens |
| 20–24 | Shaken |
| 15–19 | Frightened |
| 6–14 | Panicked |
| 1–5 | Paralyzed |

## Blood Pool

4 blood per level.

## Spell Cost

The cost equals the spell level, so a level 20 character with 80 points casts 8 spells of 9th level.

## Discipline Cost

Double the discipline level minus 1 (that is, 1-3-5-7-9).

## Discipline XP Cost

- +1 discipline point: 1000 XP (×2 if out of clan).
- +1 discipline power: 200 × level XP.

## Discipline Levels

- Only one discipline can exceed half your character level rounded up; the others round down.
- You start with all clan disciplines at 1.
- You gain 1 discipline point at level 3 and every 2 levels after that.
