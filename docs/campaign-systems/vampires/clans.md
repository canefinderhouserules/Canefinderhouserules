# Clans

!!! note "Vampire subsystem"
    This is a custom subsystem, separate from the normal Pathfinder rules. Costs are still to be decided.

| Clan | Curse | Disciplines |
|---|---|---|
| **Brujah** | +5 DC to resist frenzy. | Potence, Presence, Celerity |
| **Gangrel** | +1 DC to resist frenzy. Entering frenzy leaves a permanent animal mark that can make it impossible for them to socialize without breaking the Masquerade. | Animalism, Fortitude, Protean |
| **Lasombra** | They have no reflection. Light sensitivity. | Dominate, Obtenebration, Potence |
| **Malkavian** | They must take a mental drawback. | Auspex, Obfuscate, Dominate |
| **Giovanni** | Feeding causes severe pain and damage to their target, making it harder, noisier and more likely to end in death. | Potence, Dominate, Necromancy |
| **Nosferatu** | Their mere presence breaks the Masquerade because of their completely deformed appearance. | Animalism, Obfuscate, Potence |
| **Ravnos** | They suffer from a compulsion; resisting it penalizes them severely until they give in. It can range from negative things such as kleptomania to "positive" ones such as a hero complex. Completely individual to each kindred. | Animalism, Fortitude, Chimerstry |
| **Salubri** | A third eye that penalizes them if covered and that weeps blood when they use disciplines. Extra difficulty feeding from someone who is not willing to give their blood peacefully. | Auspex, Fortitude, Valeren |
| **Toreador** | They choose a kind of art that, when they see it, causes them to be fascinated with the same DC as their frenzy; to be decided by the GM. | Auspex, Celerity, Presence |
| **Tremere** | Depending on the era: weak to blood bonds (1 blood counts as 2), OR +10 DC to resist anything from a Tremere of a lower generation. | Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy |
| **Tzimisce** | They must rest near at least a kilo of earth from a place that was very significant to the vampire in question. Failing to do so leaves them exhausted until they do. | Vicissitude, Animalism, Auspex |
| **Ventrue** | They can only feed on one specific type of blood, chosen at character creation: from "specifically red-haired women" to "gay men," things so specific that they make feeding harder. Generally also people of quality, who have had a good, healthy life, not just any street bum, let alone animals. | Dominate, Fortitude, Presence |
| **Followers of Set (Setites)** | Light sensitivity. | Obfuscate, Presence, Protean |
| **Banu Haqim (Assamites)** | A curse that grows worse with the passing of time. Addicted to diablerie, and their aura is that of diablerie, which brings them many problems when moving through Camarilla territory. | Auspex, Celerity, Obfuscate |
