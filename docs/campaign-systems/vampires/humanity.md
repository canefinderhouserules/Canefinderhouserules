# Humanity

!!! note "Vampire subsystem"
    This is a custom subsystem, separate from the normal Pathfinder rules.

Humanity, or the Path of Enlightenment, is what keeps a vampire from falling to the Beast or being dominated by it. Many different paths exist, created as ideals (some almost religious) to cling to in order to hold the Beast at bay, but the Path of Humanity is the most common of all.

Paths of Enlightenment are divided into 10 levels, and as a general rule all vampires start at the 7th, effectively having 7/10 Humanity.

Different actions, depending on the path you choose, raise or lower it, in a way very similar to [stress/grace](../../characters/options/stress-and-grace.md) or willpower, only separate, much more esoteric, and strictly tied to the Beast every vampire carries inside.

Having more or less than 7 has its effects. On some paths they can vary, at least upward, although downward they rarely change. Here are the effects for the Path of Humanity, since it is the most common; if you want something unique, it can be discussed and researched.

| Humanity | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 | **Wight.** A monster, the Beast. You are completely lost, there is no way back; you are nothing but an empty shell entirely controlled by the Beast, and eating and sleeping are all you know how to do. Game over. |
| 1 | **Horrific.** You are on the edge of ruin, practically a monster, with a cadaverous appearance and an insane desire to feed almost constantly. Anything could make you fall forever. You have +18 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 2 | **Bestial.** +15 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 3 | +12 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 4 | +9 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 5 | +6 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 6 | +3 DC to resist frenzy. |
| 7 | Normal. |
| 8 | 3 DC lower to resist frenzy. |
| 9 | 5 DC lower to resist frenzy. |
| 10 | 7 DC lower to resist frenzy; possibility of reaching Golconda: enlightenment, transcendence, nirvana, or whatever you want to call it. |
