\- Blush of Life: Gastando 1 punto de sangre te permite ganar un poco de color en la piel para parecer vivo y tambien que se te pare el pitito pa coger y en general funcionar un poco como ser vivo. Dura 1 hora por nivel.

\- Blood Surge: Gastando 1 de sangre te otorgas un +2 al atributo fisico que quieras durante 1 ronda/nivel. Puede usarse multiples veces, cada vez que se pague su coste, afectando atributos diferentes.

**- Obtenebration:**

1a- Shadow Double: As a full round action, you create a single, shadowy duplicate. This shadow double remains in his square, mimicking his movements as a single mirror image, except that it lasts until it is destroyed or he chooses to dismiss it as a swift action. At 5th level, and every 5 levels after, you gain 1 extra double.

1b- Darkness.

2a- Mythic Darkness. In addition to using the shadow doubles as mirror images in his square, he can move his doubles as part of his own move action, dividing his movement between himself and his doubles. When outside his square, shadow doubles do not protect the Lasombra as mirror image and are limited in the actions they can take. Shadow doubles provide flanking for the vampire and his allies, but they do not possess teamwork feats or special abilities that alter the effects of flanking or aiding another. As a swift action, he can direct his shadow doubles to use the aid another action, using his own base attack bonus plus his Intelligence modifier for the roll.

2b- Black Tentacles pero single target.

3- Black Tentacles.

4a- The vampire can divide his actions between his actual body and his shadow doubles, using them as the origin point for attacks or abilities. For example, a lasombra making three attacks as a part of a full attack could make his primary attack from his own body and his other two attacks from two of his shadow doubles.

4b- Black Tentacles Mythic.

5- Shadow Form: The vampire and his shadows have no physical body. It can be harmed only by other incorporeal creatures, magic weapons or creatures that strike as magic weapons, and spells, spell-like abilities, or supernatural abilities. It is immune to all nonmagical attack forms except fire. Even when hit by spells or magic weapons, it takes only half damage from a corporeal source (except for channel energy). Although it is not a magical attack, holy water can affect incorporeal undead. Corporeal spells and effects that do not cause damage only have a 50% chance of affecting an incorporeal creature.
