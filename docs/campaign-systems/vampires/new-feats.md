# New Feats

!!! note "Vampire subsystem"
    This is a custom subsystem, separate from the normal Pathfinder rules.

**Extra Discipline Point:** +1 discipline point. It can only be taken once every 4 levels (levels 1, 5, 9, 13, 17).

**Extra Discipline:** You have access to a discipline outside your clan. It can only be taken once every 4 levels. You must define whether it comes from learning (requires a background showing a teacher), from a blood bond (requires a background showing someone you are bonded to), or from diablerie (requires nothing extra, but your aura looks foul, like a filthy diablerist's, and people will want to kill you for it).

**Blood Magic:** You have access to magic. You must take this before taking a magic class, unless you have a discipline that grants magic as an in-clan discipline (or at level 1 to be a magic class from level 1). Your clan determines the type of magic class you have access to; see the list in [Disciplines](disciplines.md).

**Extra Powers:** You obtain a second power from those available in one of your disciplines, one for each level, but the powers of level 2 and up are gained 1 level later. (Example: when you reach Obtenebration 3, you gain the option you did not choose at level 2.)
