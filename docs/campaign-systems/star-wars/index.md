# Star Wars Subsystem

!!! note "Custom subsystem"
    This is a free, fan-made campaign subsystem created for our table. It is completely separate from the normal Pathfinder rules and is not copied from any official system. It is written for the players to read.

This page holds the rules of the subsystem. The rest is on three pages: [Lightsabers and Blasters](lightsabers.md) (with the lightsaber forms), [Races](races.md), and [Force Powers](force-powers.md).

## Stamina

**Stamina:** a fixed number (by class type) × level, plus the casting ability modifier × level (like hit points with Constitution, but using an ability that suits the power).

**The fixed numbers are:**

| Class type | Fixed number |
|---|---|
| 9th-level caster class | 12 |
| 6th-level caster class | 8 |
| 4th-level caster class | 6 |

**Ability cost** (by spell level):

| Spell level | Stamina cost |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0–3 |
| 1 | 1–10 |
| 2 | 11–20 |
| 3 | 21–30 |
| 4 | 31–40 |
| 5 | 41–50 |
| 6 | 51–60 |
| 7 | 61–70 |
| 8 | 71–80 |
| 9 | 81–100 |

**Lower-level powers get cheaper:** each time you gain access to a new spell level, powers of the previous spell level cost half. This does not apply to cantrips. For example, a 2nd-level power that normally costs 20 costs 10 once you unlock 3rd-level powers.

**Casting ability:** the ability for casting with the Force is Wisdom; the ability for "casting" with technology is Intelligence. If you want to use another one, it can be negotiated. Charisma or Intelligence for the Force would not be entirely impossible, and I don't know whether anyone will come up with something other than Intelligence for technological things, so we will see.

- Charisma would be more common for Sith, or for classes with fewer spell levels: a power based on how strong your personality is, more intuitive, less technical and precise.
- Wisdom is the typical choice of the Jedi Order.
- Intelligence would be unusual among Jedi, but not that much; it would certainly be more common among Sith sorcerers, the really shady ancient alchemist types.

## Classes

Classes are extremely customizable and loosely defined, which is why there are only 3. They set in stone how many levels apart you gain a new class feature, how many hit points and how much stamina you gain per level, your BAB, your saves, and, to a degree, what kind of class features you can obtain (negotiable).

#### Class Features
The following are class features of the Warrior class.

##### Weapon and Armor Proficiency
A Warrior is proficient with all simple and martial weapons, one exotic weapon, and all armor.

##### Warrior Class Feature (Ex, Sp, Su)
At 1st level, 2nd level and every 2 levels thereafter you gain a class feature of your choice, ranging from a custom feature representing something very unique and different (to be systematized and balanced with the GM) to one borrowed from a Pathfinder class.

This is completely experimental, but for now the only tiny rules for keeping some degree of sense are:

- You can copy any class feature of the same level as the class feature you are gaining, or lower (you cannot copy the inquisitor's Bane with a level 2 class feature, since it is a level 5 feature for the inquisitor).
- You can copy class features only from similar classes: martial from martial (and from martial hybrids, negotiable and perhaps at a lower level). For the Warrior-type class, only from classes without spells or with 4th-level spells, and possibly from 6th-level casters with a weaker level or at the cost of a higher-level class feature.
- Each class feature has a different value, divided into OP, Common and Weak (see [Class Features and Their Prices](#class-features-and-their-prices)). Each time you choose a class feature you can take 1 OP, or 2 Common, or 4 Weak (or 1 Common and 2 Weak). **MAXIMUM 4 OP CLASS FEATURES.** Negotiable.

##### Powerless (Ex, Sp, Su)
An optional class feature for characters who have no powers or wild technological aptitudes, or who just want the most basic, passive, braindead option and not think about anything else. Instead of spells, they receive something passive and permanent, to be discussed with the GM, similar to a permanent 1st-level spell at 1st level, which improves to a 2nd-level spell at 4th level, a 3rd-level one at 7th, a 4th-level one at 10th and a 5th-level one at 13th.

##### Mutation (Ex, Sp, Su)
More than a class feature, this is a limit on the type of powers and abilities you can develop with the character. It is more narrative than anything else, but depending on what you choose it can be something cooler and more interesting, perhaps even OP through variety. I may put a price on it or make something stronger the more limiting it is (or give it an XP price the broader it is).

This is obviously decided at level 1 and the GM must know about it.

## Favored Class Bonuses

These favored class bonuses belong to this subsystem's three classes. Each time you gain a level in your favored class, choose one:

- +1 stamina.
- 1/8 of an extra Common class feature.
- 1/2 of an extra class skill.
- 1/3 of an extra Weak class feature.

## Feats and Class Features Unique to This Setting

**Extra Class Feature [Feat]:** Grants one extra class feature choice. If it is of the same level as the feat you are spending, it cannot be OP, and it only gives you 1 common or 2 weak features. If it is 4 levels lower, it can be OP (negotiable), and you can take 2 common or 4 weak features, or a combination of both.  
It can be taken multiple times. It is NOT a combat feat (so no abusing it with a full-BAB class).

**Accelerated Caster [Medium Class Feature]:** Exclusive to the 9th-level-spell class. It gives you an accelerated spell progression similar to a wizard's: essentially you gain 1 spell known of the next spell level one class level earlier, and the rest stays the same (for example, at level 3 you gain 1 known 2nd-level spell, which stays at only 1 at level 4, and at level 5 you gain the second one and the first 3rd-level one).

**Supreme Warrior [Medium Class Feature]:** Available to the 3/4 BAB class or the full BAB class. With 3/4 BAB, choose one lightsaber form and reduce its level requirement by 2. With full BAB, the level requirements of all forms are reduced by 2.

**Level as BAB [OP Class Feature]:** Only for the 3/4 BAB class. Your level counts as your base attack bonus. You also use the full BAB class's stamina (6 per level instead of 8), and you lose 1 spell known of each spell level.

**Caster Specialist [OP Class Feature]:** Only for the 1/2 BAB class. Your base attack bonus advances as 3/4 instead of 1/2. Your stamina die drops to d8.

## Force Sensitivity

This costs a feat, but it comes with benefits. The Consular gets it for free at level 1, because only someone with the Force can use that class, and being a full caster with full-caster hit points in a role where EVERYTHING fires 500 shots per second is, well, not the best idea.

**Force Sensitive** *(feat; prerequisite: can only be taken at level 1)*: you are Force sensitive. Your Perception roll can perceive much more than normal senses, sometimes even perceiving things before they happen, so it essentially ignores invisibility bonuses or drastically reduces them, and so on. You can block and deflect blasters with a lightsaber, and you have a free lightsaber (even 2, depending on the role; ask). You have +1 dodge AC, which rises at level 3 and every 3 levels after that (at level 6 it is +3, and so on). This AC is lost if you are caught flat-footed.

**Force Sensitive** *(trait; prerequisite: can only be taken at level 1)*: you are Force sensitive but have not had formal training, or are too lucky to have gone to be a temple guard. You can use Perception in an extrasensory way, but it does not always work: it only works if you roll more than 10 on the base die. You have no AC bonus and no lightsaber.

Being Force sensitive also enables the [Karma system](#dark-side-and-light-side).

Jedi characters can start anywhere from –20 to +20, as you prefer. A trait to start with more (±20 more?) is negotiable.

## Spells and Abilities

Any of the 3 classes can build and cast essentially any "spell" as long as they have the required level. There are no spellbooks: take whatever spell makes sense, according to the proper level (do not give me a *fireball* at level 1; it is still a 3rd-level spell). We sort it out as we go. You can invent spells, edit existing ones and adapt them, whatever.

You can also make "physical techniques": something that in the game world is not strictly a spell but is still a technique that spends stamina, and therefore by the system is still a "spell". Basically for melee things, or unique oddities.

Only Jedi can have 9th-level spells. Classes with 4th and 6th level spells are the technology/gadget type (or the Force too, of course).

Jedi use the mana/stamina system.

Non-Jedi use uses and are prepared casters (you prepare how many rounds of ammunition of each type you will carry for the day), using the ranger/magus slots per day.

**What if you want to use technology AND have the Force?** I hate you and I ban you, but in short you use a hybrid. You use stamina, but you "prepare your spells" by spending stamina, a sort of conserved-stamina system. Say you want a *fireball* that is a terrible, crazy chemical bomb because you are a Jedi but also an engineer, or you have a missile launcher on your wrist. You subtract the fireball's cost from your daily stamina. You can only prepare half of what a normal character could prepare, because of the oddity. (If a prepared caster at your level has only 2 third-level slots, you can only prepare 1 spell this way, so you only have 1 missile fireball; the rest you use the Force with stamina, as normal.)

## Skill Changes

- **Arcana** becomes **Science**.
- **Planes** is either ignored, or used for computer science knowledge.
- **Engineering** becomes engineering, but with robots.
- You can always ignore these and add new ones, since it costs little. This is mostly so that traits and other things that applied to the old skills now work for these; otherwise we will sort it out as we go.
- **Disable Device** is slicing.
- **Navigation** should be added, because technically Ride still works. It would be Intelligence-based, but could also use Dexterity, or Wisdom if force flying comes up.

## Deflecting Shots

Returning a shot is a ranged attack, but the damage is the same as the damage that was dealt to you. Defensive forms let you do it as a swift action and the like, but you can always spend a move action on your turn to stay alert so that if you receive a shot you can return it; otherwise, as a general rule, the shots just fly everywhere.
