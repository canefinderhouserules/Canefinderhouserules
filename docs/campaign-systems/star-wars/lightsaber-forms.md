# Lightsaber Forms

!!! note "Star Wars subsystem"
    This is a custom subsystem, separate from the normal Pathfinder rules.

The 7–8 Jedi forms are, you guessed it, style feats that only work with lightsabers. They are activated as a swift action, and you cannot have more than one active at a time unless you steal the effect of the monk's Master of Many Styles, you know the drill.

**Shii-cho Novice:**

Benefit: As a standard action, you can make a single lightsaber attack at your full base attack bonus against a foe within reach. If you hit, you deal damage normally and can make an additional attack (using your full base attack bonus) against up to two other foes that are also within reach. You take a –2 penalty to your Armor Class until the start of your next turn for each additional attack you make with this feat.

**Shii-cho Expert:**

Benefit: You can apply the benefits of Vital Strike, Improved Vital Strike, and Greater Vital Strike to all attacks made using Cerberus Style.

**Shii-cho Master:**

You can now attack up to 3 foes and make a 5-foot move in between if you have not used it, and the penalty drops to –1 AC for each attack instead of –2.

**Makashi Novice:**

Benefit: You can make a Bluff check to feint in combat as a move action. Counts as having Improved Feint. You also gain +1 to attack and AC with/against lightsabers whenever you are in a 1-on-1 fight.

**Makashi Expert:** You can feint as a swift action, or feint as a move action and gain +2 to your next attack. The +1 to attack and AC rises to +2, and now half of it still applies if you are not in a 1-on-1 fight.

**Makashi Master:** You can feint as a free action, but you take a –10 penalty on the Bluff check. If you feint as a swift action you gain +2 to attack and damage on the first hit; if you feint as a move action you gain +2 critical threat range (+1, not multiplicable, if you have Improved Critical). The bonus to attack and AC in a 1-on-1 fight is now +3.

**Soresu Novice:**

Benefit: You take only a –2 penalty on attack rolls for fighting defensively. While using this style and fighting defensively or using the total defense action, you gain an additional +1 dodge bonus to your Armor Class.

**Soresu Expert:**

Benefit: When fighting defensively you gain a +4 dodge bonus to AC against melee and blaster attacks. If a melee or blaster attack misses you by 4 or less, you lose this dodge bonus until the beginning of your next turn. If you are using the total defense action instead, you can deflect one melee or blaster attack that would normally hit you. An attack so deflected deals no damage and has no other effect (instead treat it as a miss). If it is a blaster bolt, it goes back to whoever shot you (this counts as an attack of opportunity). You do not expend an action when using this feat, but you must be aware of the attack and not flat-footed.

**Soresu Master:**

Benefit: You take only a –1 penalty on attack rolls for fighting defensively. Whenever you deflect an opponent’s attack using soresu experto or lose the dodge bonus from soresu experto because an attack missed you by 4 or less, you can make an attack of opportunity against the attacker after the attack misses. If your attack succeeds, you don't lose the dodge bonus and can do it again.

**Ataru Novice:**

Benefit: You gain a +2 bonus on acrobatics checks. While using the ataru Style feat, when an opponent targets you with a melee or ranged attack, you can spend an immediate action to make an acrobatics check. You can use the result as your AC or touch AC against that attack. You must be aware of the attack and not flat-footed.

**Ataru Expert:**

Benefit: Each time you hit with an unarmed or lightsaber strike while using ataru Style, you can move 5 feet without provoking an attack of opportunity as long as you move to a space adjacent to the opponent you hit with the attack. If you use this feat, you cannot take a 5-foot step during your next turn. You can now also use your Acrobatics result twice in place of your AC with a single immediate action (that is, to dodge 2 things in the same round).

**Ataru Master:**

You gain Pounce, and you can charge through difficult terrain (unless it is difficult terrain that also affects the air) and charge through obstacles as long as you can jump over them (that is, you cannot go through a wall, but you can jump over one).

**Shien Novice:**

Benefits: While using this style, when an opponent makes an attack of opportunity against you for moving through a threatened square, you can spend a swift action to make a retaliatory unarmed strike attack against that opponent. Your attack is resolved after the triggering attack of opportunity.

Also, if someone shoots at you and you successfully defend against it, you can spend an immediate action to send the bolt back.

**Shien Expert:**

Benefit: While using shien Style, you can spend a free action, instead of spending a immediate action, to make a retaliatory lightsaber strike or to return a bolt. You can make a number of retaliatory lightsaber strikes and bolt returns on your turn equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Shien Master:**

Benefit: While using Shien Style, your retaliatory lightsaber strikes are resolved before the triggering attacks. If your retaliatory lightsaber strike deals damage to an opponent, that opponent takes a –2 penalty on attack and damage rolls with the triggering attack of opportunity. You also gain +2 AC against blasters, and switching to Djem-so is a free action instead of a swift action.

**Djem-so Novice:**

Benefit: While using this style, when you use the charge action, the bonus on your attack roll increases by 1 and you deal 2d6 additional points of damage with one melee attack made as part of the charge.

**Djem-so Expert:**

Benefit: When you hit a target with a lightsaber strike you reduce their AC by 1 until the beginning of their turn. When you hit a target with a lightsaber strike and you have hit that target with a lightsaber strike previously that round, you deal an extra 1d6 points of damage.

**Djem-so Master:**

Now all the attacks you make reduce AC by 2, and each extra hit deals 2d6 instead of 1d6. Switching to Shien is a free action instead of a swift action.

**Niiman Novice:**

Benefit: While using Niiman Style, you can add your Intelligence modifier to the lightsabers damage (instead of any other ability bonus or modifier you can add to your weapon damage). The weapon must be one appropriate for your size.

**Niiman Expert:**

Benefits: You can add 1½ × your Intelligence modifier, or add your Intelligence modifier to your Strength modifier.

**Niiman Master:**

Reduce the two-weapon fighting penalty by 1 (only if you do not use spells), and you learn to cast force powers and wield your lightsaber at the same time. This functions much like two-weapon fighting, but the off-hand weapon is a force power that is being cast. To use this ability, the character must have one hand free (even if the spell being cast does not have somatic components), while wielding a light or one-handed lightsaber in the other hand. As a full-round action, he can make all of his attacks with his melee weapon at a –2 penalty and can also cast any force power with a casting time of 1 standard action (any attack roll made as part of this spell also takes this penalty). If he casts this spell defensively, he can decide to take an additional penalty on his attack rolls, up to his Intelligence bonus, and add the same amount as a circumstance bonus on his concentration check. If the check fails, the spell is wasted, but the attacks still take the penalty. A character can choose to cast the spell first or make the weapon attacks first, but if he has more than one attack, he cannot cast the spell between weapon attacks.

**Vapaad Novice:**

Benefit: While using this style, you gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against sleep effects, paralysis effects, and stunning effects. You ignore difficult terrain when you charge, run, or withdraw. You can also charge through squares that contain allies.

**Vapaad Expert:**

You can add 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus on the damage roll for your first lightsaber attack on a given round.

**Vapaad Master:**

Benefit: While using this Style, increase your Strength bonus on lightsaber strike damage rolls by an additional one-half your Strength bonus, to a total of double your Strength bonus on the first attack and 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus on the other attacks. When you score a critical hit or a successful Stunning Fist attempt against an opponent while using this style, that opponent is also shaken for a number of rounds equal to 1d4 + your Strength bonus.
