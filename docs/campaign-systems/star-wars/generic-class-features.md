# Generic Class Features

These class features exist only in this subsystem. Each one stands in for the many class features that did the same thing under different names, so instead of copying one of those you choose what the feature applies to. Warrior, Specialist and Consular can all take them.

## Skill Training

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one skill. You gain a bonus on checks with that skill equal to 1/2 your level (minimum +1). All Knowledge skills count as a single skill for this class feature. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different skill.

## Reliable Skill

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one skill. You can always take 10 with that skill, even when stress or distractions would normally prevent it. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different skill.

## Additional Class Skills

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose up to four skills. They become class skills for you. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose different skills.

## Feat Training

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

You gain one feat of your choice. You must meet its prerequisites. You can take this class feature more than once. You can take this class feature up to three times.

## Focused Resistance

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one of the following: fear and emotion effects; charm and compulsion effects; confusion and insanity effects; death effects, negative energy and energy drain; poison and disease; curses and hexes; traps; one school of magic; or one creature type. You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against it. This bonus increases to +4 at 10th level. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different option.

## Immunity

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 5th.

Choose one of the following: disease, fear, poison, sleep effects, negative energy and energy drain, or one of the blinded, confused, dazzled, deafened, fatigued or sickened conditions. You become immune to it. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different option.

## At-Will Detection

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one of detect magic, detect poison, detect undead, one detect alignment spell, or a detection of one creature type that works the same way. You can use it at will as a spell-like ability. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different spell.

## Spell Knack

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one spell of a level no higher than 1/2 your level, rounded down (minimum 1st, maximum 6th). You can cast it once per day as a spell-like ability, using your level as your caster level. Once chosen, the spell cannot be changed. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different spell.

## Terrain Stride

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 3rd.

You can move through nonmagical difficult terrain and undergrowth at your normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other impairment.

## Natural Movement

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose climb or swim. You gain that movement speed, equal to 1/2 your base speed. At 10th level, it increases to your base speed. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose the other movement.

## Weapon Familiarity

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one martial or exotic weapon. You become proficient with it. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different weapon.

## Energy Resistance

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose acid, cold, electricity, fire or sonic. You gain resistance 5 to that energy type. This increases to resistance 10 at 10th level. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different energy type.

## Keen Senses

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

Choose low-light vision, darkvision 60 feet, or scent. You gain that ability. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different ability.

## Water Adaptation

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

You can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to your Constitution score. At 6th level, you can breathe water as easily as air.

## Uncommon Speech

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 3rd.

Choose animals, plants, one creature type, or all languages. You can speak with that kind of creature at will. If you choose all languages, you can cast tongues on yourself three times per day as a spell-like ability instead. You can take this class feature more than once. Each time, choose a different option.

## Additional Skill Ranks

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

You gain 2 additional skill ranks at each level.

## Lucky Break

**Price:** Common. **Level:** 5th.

Once per day, you can reroll one d20 you just rolled, before the result is revealed. You must take the second result.

## Natural Weapon

**Price:** Common. **Level:** 1st.

Choose one bite or gore attack (1d6) or two claw attacks (1d4). You gain those primary natural attacks.

## Flight

**Price:** Common. **Level:** 12th.

You gain a fly speed equal to your base speed with average maneuverability. You can dismiss or manifest your means of flight as a free action.

## Quick Reactions

**Price:** Weak. **Level:** 1st.

You gain a +2 bonus on initiative checks. This bonus increases to +4 at 10th level.
