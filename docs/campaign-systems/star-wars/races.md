# Races

Use the race builder; there are too many to write up by hand. Do not go over 15 RP, and do not try to be too clever with the prices, since some things are very cheap for what they do.

The extra feat belongs only to humans or human variants. Do not try to sneak it into every race.

Built races: [Chiss](race-chiss.md), [Ithorian](race-ithorian.md), [Codru-ji](race-codru-ji.md), [Astromech](race-astromech.md).
