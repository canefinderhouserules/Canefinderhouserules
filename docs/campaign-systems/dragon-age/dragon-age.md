# Dragon Age Subsystem

!!! note "Custom subsystem"
    This is a free, fan-made campaign subsystem created for our table. It is separate from the normal Pathfinder rules and is not copied from any official system. It adds a templar prestige class, blood magic and Grey Warden feats, and notes on divine magic.

## Templar

**Requirements:**

Alignment: Any Lawful

Base Attack Bonus: +4

Skills: Intimidate 3 ranks, Knowledge (arcana) 2 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 2 ranks.

Special: Monthly consumption of lyrium.

**Class skills:**

Intimidate, Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge Planes, Perception, Sense Motive.

**Class features:**

| Level | BAB | Fort | Ref | Will | Special |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +1 | +1 | +1 | +1 | Smite Magic 1/day, Lyrium Addict |
| 2 | +2 | +1 | +1 | +1 | Disruptive, Dispel |
| 3 | +3 | +2 | +2 | +2 | Smite Magic 2/day, Master Dispeler, Immediate Dispel |
| 4 | +4 | +2 | +2 | +2 | Spell Resistance, Spellbreaker |

| 7 | +7 | +4 | +4 | +4 | Smite Magic 4/day, Greater Dispel |

Smite Magic (Su): This ability functions as the paladin’s smite evil class feature but against Magic Users (any creature that has a caster level).

Lyrium Addict (Su): They need to consume lyrium for their abilities to work. One dose per month, expensive and very hard to get without being part of the church. If they do not consume it, they have a permanent –4 on Will saves, and resting does not restore uses of their class abilities.

Disruptive (Feat): Your training makes it difficult for enemy spellcasters to safely cast spells near you.

Benefit: The DC to cast spells defensively increases by +4 for all enemies that are within your threatened area. This increase to casting spells defensively only applies if you are aware of the enemy's location and are capable of taking an attack of opportunity. If you can only take one attack of opportunity per round and have already used that attack, this increase does not apply.

Dispel (Su): As Dispel Magic but you use double your Templar lvl instead of your caster level.

Master Dispeler (Su): +1 to dispel check at lvl 3, and every 2 lvls after.

Immediate Dispel (Su): can use dispel as an immediate action, instead of a standard.

Spell Resistance (Su): You gain 5 + double your templar level as Spell Resistance.

Spellbreaker (feat): You can strike at enemy spellcasters who fail to cast defensively when you threaten them.

Benefit: Enemies in your threatened area that fail their checks to cast spells defensively provoke attacks of opportunity from you.

Will Evasion (Su): as evasion but for Will saves

Fort Evasion (Su): as evasion but for Fortitude saves

Greater Dispel (Su): As Greater Dispel Magic but you use double your Templar lvl instead of your caster level.

Greater Spell Resistance (Su): Your spell resistance goes up to 10 + double your templar level, instead of 5.

Greater will evasion (Su): as improved evasion but for Will saves

Greater fort evasion (Su): as improved evasion but for Fortitude saves

Mythic Dispel (Su): As mythic dispel but instead of healing yourself, you damage the caster of the spell if its nearby.

## Blood Mage

**Blood Magic Adept [Feat]** (Requirements: caster level 3): 1d2 Constitution damage per caster level or DC added, up to +3 caster levels to that spell (stacks with everything) or +3 DC. It causes psychological addiction, requiring possible Will saves (or determination rolls) to avoid wanting to use it again on the next turn. Failing many saves in a row in a desperate situation can lead to becoming an abomination, which is basically dying.

**Blood Magic Expert [Feat]** (Requirements: 3rd-level spells): Up to +4 caster levels maximum.

**Blood Magic Master [Feat]** (Requirements: 5th-level spells): Lets you trade 2 caster levels for +1 damage per die (which also stacks with everything). Constitution damage is always the minimum.

## Grey Warden

**Grey Warden [Feat]:** Congratulations, you are going to die in 2 or 3 decades. You gain nightmares that give you stress. But you have political immunity: they let you through everywhere, even if you are an apostate. +1 Perception to detect darkspawn; for each month you spend as a Grey Warden this grows, up to a maximum of +10. Darkspawn also have +5 Perception to detect you. This perception is not limited by anything, as if it were blindsense.

**Master Warden [Feat]:** If you do not have favored enemy, you gain it at half strength, based on your level, against darkspawn. You gain blindsense 15 ft. against darkspawn.

## Divine Magic

It exists, but it doesn't: it is just another way of doing magic. Magic is quite unique and open to reinterpretation in the Dragon Age universe, and divine magic would in most cases be tied to the spirits of the Fade. For example, you could be a cleric whose "god" is, say, a spirit of Justice, and that would give reasonable domains fitting that kind of spirit.

Andraste does not grant powers.

The Maker does not grant powers.

Being a divine caster is still bound by the same rules as any other caster: you are either an outcast or part of the Circle.
