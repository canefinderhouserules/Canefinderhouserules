# Class Features and Prices

**Strong:** AC bonus, flurry, spirit (medium), hexes, spell combat, sneak attack, wild shape, fervor, sacred weapon, mutagen, eidolon, challenge, bardic performance, bloodline, domains, companion, bane, kinetic blast, and any class feature of the form "take this talent or feat every 2 levels", meaning not just a single pick but gaining one every so many levels (rage powers, rogue talents, slayer talents, ki powers, and so on).

**Common:** favored enemy (depending on the role it can become Strong), smite evil (can become Strong depending on the role), channel energy, lay on hands, rage, weapon training, armor training, martial flexibility, bombs, discovery, tactician, blessings, animal focus, judgments, solo tactics, inspiration, fast movement, evasion, uncanny dodge, resolve, shifter claws, familiar/arcane bond, arcane discoveries, Invulnerable Rager's DR, phrenic pool and amplifications, hypnotic stare.

**Weak:** poisons, trackless step, wild empathy, woodland stride, unarmed strike, favored terrain, trapfinding, trap sense, dual identity, the barbarian's DR.

## Feats Unique to This System

**Extra Class Feature:** gives you one extra class feature choice. If it is of the same level as the feat you are spending, it cannot be Strong, and gives only 1 Common or 2 Weak. If it is 4 levels lower, it may possibly be Strong (negotiable), and can be 2 Common or 4 Weak or a combination of both. It can be taken multiple times. It is NOT a combat feat (so no abusing it with a full-BAB class).
