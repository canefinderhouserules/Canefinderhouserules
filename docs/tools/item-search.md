# Item Search

Every magic item and every piece of mundane equipment of the wiki in one list, grouped by what it is (armor, weapon, ring, rod, staff, wondrous item by slot, adventuring gear, tools...) and not by the book it comes from. Tick the kind, types, slots, schools or books to narrow it, set a price range in gp, or leave everything empty to see them all. Click a column name to sort by it.
