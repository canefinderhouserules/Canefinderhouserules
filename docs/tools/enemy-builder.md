# Enemy Builder

Make a quick enemy: choose its type, level and size, spread its ability scores and skill ranks, pick feats, weapons and spells, and the stat block works itself out. **Copy for Roll20** puts it on the clipboard ready to paste into a Roll20 character, with every attack, save, skill and spell as a button, as on the [monster pages](../monsters/roll20.md).

The enemy you are making stays in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to it.
