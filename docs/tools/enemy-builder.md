# Enemy Builder

Make a quick enemy: choose its type, level and size, pick a race (one of the wiki's or a custom one, whose ability modifiers add to the scores), spread its ability scores and skill ranks, pick feats, weapons and spells, and the stat block works itself out. Class features that are a list of choices (rogue talents, ninja tricks, slayer talents, rage powers, discoveries, infusions and utility wild talents) open a box to pick them, with as many picks as the class table gives at that level and only the options open at it. **Copy for Roll20** puts it on the clipboard ready to paste into a Roll20 character, with every attack, save, skill and spell as a button, as on the [monster pages](../monsters/roll20.md).

The enemy you are making stays in this browser, so you can close the page and come back to it.
