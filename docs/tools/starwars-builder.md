# Star Wars Enemy Builder

The [Enemy Builder](enemy-builder.md) for the [Star Wars subsystem](../campaign-systems/star-wars/index.md). Pick Warrior, Specialist or Consular and a level, then choose each class feature from that class's list ([Warrior](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-warrior.md), [Specialist](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-specialist.md), [Consular](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-consular.md) and the [generic ones](../campaign-systems/star-wars/generic-class-features.md)): it keeps count of the points of each choice and of the Iconic and OP limits, and each feature brings its text and, for the common ones that change the numbers (armor and weapon training, AC bonus, Nimble, sneak attack dice, bonus feats...), its effect at your level. Stamina, Force Sensitive, karma, lightsabers, blasters, lightsaber forms and [Force powers](../campaign-systems/star-wars/force-powers.md) are built in. **Copy for Roll20** works as on the monster pages.

The enemy you are making stays in this browser, apart from the ones of the normal Enemy Builder.
