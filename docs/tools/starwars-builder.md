# Star Wars Enemy Builder

The [Enemy Builder](enemy-builder.md) for the [Star Wars subsystem](../campaign-systems/star-wars/index.md). Pick Warrior, Specialist or Consular and a level, then choose each class feature from that class's list ([Warrior](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-warrior.md), [Specialist](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-specialist.md), [Consular](../campaign-systems/star-wars/class-features-consular.md) and the [generic ones](../campaign-systems/star-wars/generic-class-features.md)): it keeps count of the points of each choice and of the Iconic and OP limits, and each feature brings its text and, for the common ones that change the numbers (armor and weapon training, AC bonus, Nimble, sneak attack dice, bonus feats...), its effect at your level. A copied feature that is a list of choices (Rogue Talents, Ninja Tricks, Slayer Talents, Rage Powers, Discovery, Infusion, Wild Talents) opens a box to pick them, as many as its class gets at the level it scales with. Stamina, Force Sensitive, karma, lightsabers, blasters, lightsaber forms and [Force powers](../campaign-systems/star-wars/force-powers.md) are built in; a random enemy that uses the Force gets regular spells under Force names (Force Grip, Force Lightning…), and a specialist without it gets tech (Thermal Detonator, Net Launcher…), as the subsystem allows. The [Naruto](naruto-builder.md) and [Heroes](heroes-builder.md) Enemy Builders are this same tool for those subsystems. **Copy for Roll20** works as on the monster pages.

The enemy you are making stays in this browser, apart from the ones of the normal Enemy Builder.
