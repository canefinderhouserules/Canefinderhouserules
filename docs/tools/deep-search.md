# Deep Search

Finds every section of the wiki whose text holds the words you search, not only the names, and lists them by category with the sentence where they appear. Use it to find keywords such as a condition, a bonus type or a rule term. The quick search box at the top of every page is still the fastest way to open a page by its name.

The first search loads the whole text of the wiki, which takes a few seconds; the searches after it are immediate.
