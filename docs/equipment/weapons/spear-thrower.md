## Description

This is little more than a handle with a cup, loop, or spur to hold the butt of a dart, javelin, or shortspear. Using a spear-thrower to throw such a weapon doubles the projectile’s range increment. Setting a weapon into a spear-thrower is a move action (1 AP); by taking the [Rapid Reload](../../rules/feats/rapid-reload.md) (spear-thrower) feat, you reduce this to a free action.
