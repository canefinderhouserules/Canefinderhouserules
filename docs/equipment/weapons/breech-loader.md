## Description

This double-barreled pistol variant has its barrel attached to its stock by a hinge, enabling ammunition to be loaded quickly and easily. Reloading a breech-loader is a standard action (2 AP) that reloads both barrels; the Rapid Reload feat reduces this to a move action (1 AP).
