## Description

Seaxes are straight, single-edged blades used since ancient times. The broken-back seax is the size of a short sword, and has a broad blade with a distinctive trapezoidal shape. If your Strength score is less than 15, you take a –2 penalty on attack rolls with a broken-back seax, as you're unable to put enough force into the blows to land them effectively.

**Weapon group:** Light Blades
