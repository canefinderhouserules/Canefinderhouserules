## Description

Also known as a “monk’s staff,” the khakkhara is often used as a ceremonial walking stick for scholars and priests. It consists of a wooden shaft 5 feet long with a large metal loop or cagelike structure on the top end. Multiple small rings hang from the cage, chiming with the movement of the staff.
