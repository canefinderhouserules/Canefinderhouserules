# Evil Poisons

The following new types of poisons are popular concoctions among the unscrupulous individuals drawn to underhanded tactics.

| Name | Type | Fort DC | Onset | Frequency | Effect | Cure | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peasant’s woe | Injury | 15 | — | 1/rd. for 4 rds. | –10 ft. speed, 1 Dex | 1 save | 400 gp |
| Brain blot | Inhaled | 13 | 1 min. | 1/min. for 6 min. | –5 concentration/1 Int | 1 save | 800 gp |
| Everwake serum | Ingested | 16 | — | 1/day for 5 days | No sleep; see text | 2 saves | 2,500 gp |

### Brain Blot

*Type* poison, inhaled; *Save* Fortitude DC 13  
*Onset* 1 minute; *Frequency* 1/minute for 6 minutes  
*Initial Effect* –5 on concentration checks for 1 hour; *Secondary Effect* 1 Int damage; *Cure* 1 save

### Everwake Serum

*Type* poison, ingested; *Save* Fortitude DC 16  
*Frequency* 1/day for 5 days  
*Effect* cannot voluntarily rest or sleep (spells such as *sleep* still function as normal), preventing spellcasters who need to rest to regain or prepare spells from doing so; *Cure* 2 saves

### Peasant’s Woe

*Type* poison, injury; *Save* Fortitude DC 15  
*Frequency* 1/round for 4 rounds  
*Initial Effect* –10 ft. to base speed for 1d6 minutes; *Secondary Effect* 1 Dex damage; *Cure* 1 save
