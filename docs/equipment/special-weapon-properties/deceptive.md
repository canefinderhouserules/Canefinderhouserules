# Deceptive

**Source** [*Pathfinder Society Primer pg. 14*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy8yw6?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Pathfinder-Society-Primer)

**Aura** moderate illusion **CL** 9th

**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

A *deceptive* weapon’s shape blurs and appears to bend unpredictably. When the weapon is in hand, the wielder gains a bonus on Bluff checks to feint equal to the enhancement bonus of the weapon. In addition, when the wielder successfully confirms a critical hit with a *deceptive* weapon against an opponent, she can attempt to feint against that opponent as an immediate action.

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *dazzling blade*<sup>RG</sup>; **Price** —

<h3 id="description-25"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>A <em>defending</em> weapon allows the wielder to transfer some or all of the weapon’s enhancement bonus to his AC as a bonus that stacks with all others. As a free action, the wielder chooses how to allocate the weapon’s enhancement bonus at the start of his turn before using the weapon, and the bonus to AC lasts until his next turn. This ability can only be placed on melee weapons.</p>
