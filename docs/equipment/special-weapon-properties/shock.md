# Shock

**Source** [*Ultimate Equipment pg. 147*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy8tmc?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Equipment), [*PRPG Core Rulebook pg. 469*](http://paizo.com/pathfinderRPG/v5748btpy88yj)

**Aura** moderate evocation **CL** 8th

**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

Upon command, a *shock* weapon is sheathed in crackling electricity that deals an extra 1d6 points of electricity damage on a successful hit. The electricity does not harm the wielder. The effect remains until another command is given.

#### <span class="smallcaps">Construction</span>

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *call lightning* or *lightning bolt*; **Price** —

<p>This special ability can be placed only on a melee weapon. A <em>slithering</em> weapon can bend and flex when swung at a target, allowing it to fit through narrow gaps and around difficult angles with greater ease and making the weapon difficult to strike. When a <em>slithering</em> weapon is used to attack a creature with cover, the target’s cover bonus to Armor Class is halved. In addition, the wielder adds the weapon’s enhancement bonus to her CMD against attempts to disarm or sunder her <em>slithering</em> weapon. If the wielder is squeezing in a narrow space, she takes only half the normal penalty on attack rolls with her <em>slithering</em> weapon.</p>
