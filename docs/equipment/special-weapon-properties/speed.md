# Speed

**Aura** moderate transmutation **CL** 7th

**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +3 bonus; **Weight** —

## Description

When making a full-attack action, the wielder of a speed weapon may make one extra attack with it. The attack uses the wielder’s full base attack bonus, plus any modifiers appropriate to the situation. (This benefit is not cumulative with similar effects, such as a *haste* spell.)

## Construction

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *haste*; **Price** —

??? tip apr "Action Points (optional rule)"
    A *speed* weapon gives its wielder 1 extra AP every turn that can only be spent on an attack with that weapon, and that attack takes no multiple attack penalty.
