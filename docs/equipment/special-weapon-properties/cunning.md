# Cunning

**Source** [*Ultimate Equipment pg. 138*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy8tmc?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Equipment), [*Advanced Player's Guide pg. 287*](http://paizo.com/pathfinderRPG/v5748btpy8fo1)

**Aura** moderate divination **CL** 6th

**Slot** weapon quality; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

This special ability allows a weapon to find chinks in a foe’s defenses using the wielder’s knowledge of the target. Whenever the weapon's attack is a critical threat, the wielder gains a +4 bonus on the confirmation roll if she has 5 or more ranks in a Knowledge skill that would be used to identify the target's creature type, or a +6 bonus instead if she has 15 or more ranks.

<h3 id="description-21"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>This special weapon ability can be placed on only melee weapons. When a character wielding a <em>dazzling radiance</em> weapon uses the Dazzling Display feat, the weapon flashes in a burst of bright color. All creatures within 15 feet of the wielder who can see the display must attempt a DC 17 Fortitude save. Those who fail are blinded for 2 rounds and dazzled for an additional 1d4 rounds; those who succeed are instead dazzled for 1d4 rounds.</p>
