# Kinslayer


**Aura** moderate conjuration **CL** 8th

**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### Description

A *kinslayer* weapon is enhanced with enmity against all those born into the wielder’s familial line. Against creatures related to the wielder, this weapon’s enhancement bonus is +2 better than its actual bonus. It also deals an extra 2d6 points of damage against such creatures. This extra damage is not multiplied on a critical hit.

#### Construction

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *detect relations*; **Price** —


## Leveraging

**Aura** faint transmutation **CL** 6th

**Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

### Description

This special ability can be placed only on melee weapons. The wielder of a *leveraging* weapon gains a bonus equal to the weapon’s enhancement bonus to her Combat Maneuver Defense against bull rush, drag, reposition, and trip combat maneuvers. The *leveraging* weapon’s enhancement bonus is doubled when applied to combat maneuver checks to attempt bull rush, drag, reposition, and trip combat maneuvers.

### Construction

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *enlarge person*; **Price** —
