# Spirit-Hunting

**Source** [*Advanced Class Guide pg. 215*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy978v?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Advanced-Class-Guide)

**Aura** moderate conjuration **CL** 8th

**Slot** none; **Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

A *spirit-hunting* weapon excels against enemies with either the mystery or spirit class feature (but has no effect against a shaman who has a temporary bond to the appropriate spirit by way of the wandering spirit class feature or similar effect). Against a designated foe, the weapon’s enhancement bonus is 2 better than its actual bonus. It also deals 2d6 additional points of damage against such foes. When combined with abilities like *bane* or *blood-hunting*, the enhancement bonus increases granted by these abilities do not stack, but an additional 1d6 points of damage are added to the additional damage for each special ability beyond the first. (For example, a *bane spirit-hunting* weapon would deal 3d6 additional points of damage, and a *bane blood-hunting spirit-hunting* weapon would deal 4d6 additional points of damage.) To randomly determine a weapon’s designated foe, roll on the following table. Other designated foes are possible, but most of these weapons are tuned against creatures with the following mysteries or spirits.

<th><h1 id="unaligned"><span class="smallcaps">Unaligned</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpya1am"><em>Planar Adventures pg. 48</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> faint abjuration <strong>CL</strong> 5th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> none; <strong>Price</strong> +1 bonus; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-82"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>Typically forged by aeons or other creatures that eschew commonly held structures of morality, <em>unaligned</em> weapons quell the effects of extreme alignments on the wielder. The wielder counts her Hit Dice as 4 lower than her actual total HD when someone attempts to detect her alignment using <em>detect chaos</em>, <em>detect evil</em>, <em>detect good</em>, <em>detect law</em>, or similar spells or effects. Furthermore, if the wielder is an outsider and is targeted by a spell or effect with an alignment descriptor that deals additional damage to or has an additional effect on outsiders (such as <em>holy smite</em>), she adds a bonus equal to the unaligned weapon’s enhancement bonus on saving throws she attempts against that spell or effect. Finally, the wielder takes no penalties on her Charisma-, Intelligence-, or Wisdom-based checks from a plane’s mildly aligned or strongly aligned traits, regardless of her actual alignment.</p>
<h3 id="construction-79"><span class="smallcaps">Construction</span></h3>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong> Craft Magic Arms and Armor, <em>undetectable alignment</em>; <strong>Price</strong> —</p></th>
