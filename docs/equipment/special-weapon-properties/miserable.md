# Miserable


**Aura** moderate conjuration **CL** 8th

**Price** +1 bonus; **Weight** —

#### Description

A *miserable* weapon cuts through the energy resistance of certain foes. *Miserable* weapons must be crafted with a designated creature type (just like [*bane*](../../magic/spells/bane.md) weapons), and each time they deal damage to their designated foes, their magic halves the creatures’ natural energy resistances for 1 round.

#### Construction

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*summon monster I*](../../magic/spells/summon-monster-i.md); **Price** —
