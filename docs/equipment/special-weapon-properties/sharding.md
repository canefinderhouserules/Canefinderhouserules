# Sharding

**Source** [*Weapon Master's Handbook pg. 30*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy9hdy?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Weapon-Masters-Handbook)

**Aura** moderate conjuration **CL** 10th

**Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

This special ability can be placed only on melee or thrown weapons. The wielder of a *sharding* weapon can make a special ranged attack with the weapon in place of any melee attack. To do this, the wielder goes through the motion of throwing the weapon without releasing it. The weapon splits off a duplicate of itself that flies as if thrown by the wielder at the intended target. The duplicate gains a range increment of 10 feet for this purpose, but uses the same proficiency and otherwise functions the same as the original weapon. The duplicate vanishes after hitting or missing its target.

<th><h1 id="shattering"><span class="smallcaps">Shattering</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy8wfn?Pathfinder-Player-Companion-Dungeoneers-Handbook"><em>Dungeoneer's Handbook pg. 28</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> moderate evocation <strong>CL</strong> 8th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> weapon quality; <strong>Price</strong> +2 bonus; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-133"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>A <em>shattering</em> weapon functions as a <em>breaking</em> weapon that also explodes with a resounding crash upon striking a successful critical hit. In addition to the extra damage from the <em>breaking</em> ability, a <em>shattering</em> weapon deals an extra 1d10 points of damage to objects and crystalline creatures on a successful critical hit. If the weapon’s critical multiplier is ×3, it deals an extra 2d10 points of damage instead, and if the multiplier is ×4, it deals an extra 3d10 points instead. When used against a crystalline creature, this damage stacks with <em>bane</em> and similar weapon special abilities (though the damage from the <em>breaking</em> ability still doesn’t).</p>
<p>Even if the <em>breaking</em> ability is not active, the weapon still deals its extra damage on a successful critical hit. A <em>shattering</em> weapon ignores the hardness of objects with a hardness of 10 or lower.</p>

<th><h1 id="silencing"> <span class="smallcaps">Silencing</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy9j6p?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Intrigue"><em>Ultimate Intrigue pg. 238</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> moderate illusion <strong>CL</strong> 8th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> none; <strong>Price</strong> +2 bonus; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-135"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>A <em>silencing</em> weapon makes no noise when drawn or when used to attack. In addition, after hitting a creature, the wielder can surround that creature with an aura that muffles all sounds coming from it until the beginning of the wielder’s next turn. The target can still hear and speak (including uttering command words or casting spells with verbal components), but is much harder to hear. The DC of Perception checks to hear the sound of battle increases from –10 to 10, as long as the only combatants are the wielder of the <em>silencing</em> weapon and targets affected by the weapon’s muffling ability.</p>
<p>If the wielder confirms a critical hit with a <em>silencing</em> weapon, the target is also encased in a mobile aura of magical <em>silence</em> (Will DC 13 negates), which lasts a number of rounds equal to the critical multiplier of the weapon. The save DC increases by 2 for each critical multiplier of the weapon higher than ×2. This <em>silence</em> fills the target’s space but doesn’t extend beyond it.</p>

<p><strong>Requirements</strong> Craft Magic Arms and Armor, <em>silence</em> ; <strong>Price</strong> —</p></th>

<th><h1 id="stalking"><span class="smallcaps">Stalking</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy8tmc?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Equipment"><em>Ultimate Equipment pg. 148</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> moderate divination <strong>CL</strong> 10th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> weapon quality; <strong>Price</strong> +2 bonus; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-136"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>The longer the wielder of a <em>stalking</em> weapon studies a target, the more effective her strike. As a standard action, a character wielding a <em>stalking</em> weapon can command it to study a creature within 60 feet. The wielder must have line of effect and line of sight to that target. When the wielder attacks the studied creature, on a successful hit the <em>stalking</em> weapon deals +1d6 points of bonus damage per consecutive round spent studying a target, up to a number of bonus dice equal to the <em>stalking</em> weapon’s enhancement bonus. This bonus damage is precision damage and only applies to the first successful hit against that creature.</p>
<p>If the wielder attacks a creature other than the studied creature, commands the weapon to study a different creature, or ends her turn more than 60 feet from the studied creature, the weapon loses all bonus damage dice against the previously studied creature.</p>
<h3 id="construction-133"><span class="smallcaps">Construction</span></h3>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong> Craft Magic Arms and Armor, <em>hunter’s eye</em>, <em>true strike</em>; <strong>Price</strong> —</p></th>

<h3 id="description-137"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>Weapons with the <em>toxic</em> special ability reinforce the effects of applied poisons. The save DC of a poison applied to the weapon and delivered via an attack increases by 2. In addition, each time such a weapon strikes a target, there is a 25% chance that the poison dose is not expended in the attack and can be used to make an additional attack. A single dose of poison can be preserved by this property only once.</p>
