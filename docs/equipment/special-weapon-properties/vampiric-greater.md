# Vampiric, Greater

**Source** [*Horror Adventures pg. 214*](http://paizo.com/products/btpy9n5a?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Horror-Adventures)

**Aura** moderate necromancy **CL** 8th

**Price** +2 bonus; **Weight** —

#### <span class="smallcaps">Description</span>

This special ability functions as the *vampiric* special ability, except that the daily limit of healing from *greater vampiric* weapons is 5 hit points per Hit Die of the owner. A wielder can be attuned to only one *vampiric* or *greater vampiric* weapon at a time.

#### <span class="smallcaps">Construction</span>

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *cure moderate wounds*, *inflict moderate wounds*, *vampiric touch*; **Price** —

<h3 id="description-143"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A <em>wounding</em> weapon deals 1 point of bleed damage when it hits a creature. <th><h1 id="transformative"> <span class="smallcaps">Transformative</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy8tmc?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Equipment"><em>Ultimate Equipment pg. 149</em></a>, <a href="http://paizo.com/pathfinderRPG/v5748btpy8fo1"><em>Advanced Player's Guide pg. 289</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> moderate transmutation <strong>CL</strong> 10th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> weapon quality; <strong>Price</strong> +10,000 gp; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-144"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A <em>transformative</em> weapon alters its shape at its wielder’s command, becoming any other melee weapon of the same general shape and handedness; the weapon’s categorization as simple, martial, or exotic is irrelevant. For example, a Medium <em>transformative longsword</em> can take the shape of any other Medium one-handed melee weapon, such as a scimitar, flail, or trident, but not a Medium light or two-handed melee weapon (such as a Medium short sword or a Medium greatsword). It can even take the shape of comparable weapons of different size categories. For example, a Small greatsword is a two-handed slashing weapon for a Small character, but is a one-handed slashing weapon for a Medium character, which is very similar to a Medium longsword; a Small <em>transformative greatsword</em> can thus become an actual Medium longsword, usable by a Medium creature without the –2 penalty for using a weapon of the wrong size. The weapon retains all of its abilities, including enhancement bonuses and weapon special abilities, except those prohibited by its current shape. For example, a <em>keen transformative weapon</em> functions normally in the form of a piercing or slashing weapon, but cannot use the keen special ability when in the shape of a bludgeoning weapon. When unattended, the weapon reverts to its true shape.</p>

<th><h1 id="transformative-greater"><span class="smallcaps">Transformative, Greater</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy9l37/discuss&amp;page=5?Pathfinder-Campaign-Setting-Inner-Sea-Intrigue"><em>Inner Sea Intrigue pg. 52</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> strong transmutation <strong>CL</strong> 15th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> none; <strong>Price</strong> +15,000 gp; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-146"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>A <em>greater transformative</em> weapon becomes any other weapon the wielder desires when a command word is spoken. The weapon cannot become ammunition, but can freely change between simple, martial, exotic, light, one-handed, twohanded, melee, and ranged. The weapon retains all of its enhancement bonuses and weapon special abilities. However, abilities prohibited by its current shape do not function. For example, a <em>keen greater transformative</em> weapon functions normally in the form of a piercing or slashing weapon, but cannot use the <em>keen</em> special ability when in the shape of a bludgeoning weapon. A double weapon that loses the double quality cannot use the abilities on one of its ends (wielder’s choice), whereas a non-double weapon that gains the double quality applies all its abilities to only one end. When unattended for 1 day, the weapon reverts to its true shape.</p>
<h3 id="construction-143"><span class="smallcaps">Construction</span></h3>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong> Craft Magic Arms and Armor, <em>polymorph any object</em>; <strong>Price</strong> 7,500 gp</p></th>

<th><h1 id="spellstealing"> <span class="smallcaps">Spellstealing</span></h1>
<p><strong>Source</strong> <a href="http://paizo.com/products/btpy8tmc?Pathfinder-Roleplaying-Game-Ultimate-Equipment"><em>Ultimate Equipment pg. 148</em></a></p>
<p><strong>Aura</strong> strong divination <strong>CL</strong> 13th</p>
<p><strong>Slot</strong> weapon quality; <strong>Price</strong> +3 bonus; <strong>Weight</strong> —</p>
<h3 id="description-154"><span class="smallcaps">Description</span></h3>
<p>This special ability can only be placed on melee weapons. A <em>spellstealing</em> weapon allows its wielder to siphon protective magic from a target and transfer it to herself. When the wielder rolls a critical threat against a target, she can forgo confirming the critical hit and instead automatically learn which spells or magical effects are active upon the target. The wielder may then make a caster level check to steal her choice of one of those effects, using the <em>spellstealing</em> weapon’s caster level plus its enhancement bonus, against a DC of 11 + the caster level of the effect. If the check succeeds, the target immediately loses the benefits of that effect and the wielder gains the effect for 1 minute (or until the effect expires, whichever comes first). If the <em>spellstealing</em> weapon has a critical multiplier greater than ×2, the wielder may attempt to steal one additional spell effect per additional multiple beyond ×2 (two effects for ×3, and so on).</p>
<h3 id="construction-151"><span class="smallcaps">Construction</span></h3>
<p><strong>Requirements</strong> Craft Magic Arms and Armor, <em>greater arcane sight</em>, <em>limited wish</em>; <strong>Price</strong> —</p></th>
