# Extended Weapon List

An extended list of weapons for this table, with the special properties used here (see [Special Weapon Properties](special-weapon-properties/index.md)). The weapons in the Core Rulebook are in [Core Weapons](core-weapons.md).

!!! warning "House rules"
    Weapons are divided by proficiency and use weapon properties in the Special column instead of the old scattered rules. Where a weapon says "see text", the weapon's own description applies. Weapons that grant bonuses of the form "+X damage per die" follow the [Damage-Per-Die Bonuses](../characters/classes/damage-per-die-bonuses.md) rule.

- [Simple Weapons](weapons-simple.md)
- [Martial Weapons](weapons-martial.md)
- [Exotic Weapons](weapons-exotic.md)
- [Eastern Weapons](weapons-eastern.md)
- [Firearms](weapons-firearms.md)

*Weight figures are for Medium weapons. A Small weapon weighs half as much, and a Large weapon weighs twice as much. A weapon with two types is both types if the entry specifies "and".*
