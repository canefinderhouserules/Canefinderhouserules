# Horror Adventures Wondrous Items

Wondrous items are varied and diverse. Some must be worn in a specific magic item slot in order to work, while others must merely be kept on hand and used. “Wondrous item” is a catchall category for anything that doesn’t fall into other groups, such as armor, weapons, staves, and so on. Anyone can use a wondrous item unless the item specifies otherwise. There are two main categories of wondrous items: slotted and slotless. A slotted item takes up a magic item slot and must be worn by someone who wants to employ the item or benefit from its abilities.
