# Weapon Master Items

Magic weapons that allow weapon masters to make better use of their training are especially prized.

## Weapon Special Abilities

See [Driving](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/driving.md) (+1 bonus), [Heart-Piercing](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/heart-piercing.md) (+5 bonus), [Leveraging](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/leveraging.md) (+1 bonus), [Liberating](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/liberating.md) (+7,000 gp), and [Quaking](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/quaking.md) (+2 bonus). The [Sharding](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/sharding.md) and [Vampiric](../equipment/special-weapon-properties/vampiric.md) abilities also appear in this wiki.

## Specific Magic Weapons

### Merrymaker's Tankard

*Aura* moderate conjuration; *CL* 10th; *Slot* none; *Price* 10,301 gp; *Weight* 2 lbs.

This steel tankard functions as a +1 light hammer. The interior of the tankard contains six identical extradimensional spaces. Each space can hold 1 dose of a different potable liquid of up to 1/2 cubic foot in volume (including potions, but not poisons). Liquid never sloshes out of these spaces accidentally (though it often appears ready to do so), and when the wielder drinks from the tankard, it provides the drink from the desired extradimensional pocket (or a random pocket, if the wielder has no preference).

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Wondrous Item, *secret chest*; Cost 5,301 gp.

### Crusader's Longsword

*Aura* moderate evocation; *CL* 10th; *Slot* none; *Price* 47,315 gp; *Weight* 4 lbs.

This +1 cold iron holy demon-bane longsword is marked with the symbol of a deity of valor. Whenever the wielder uses the divine bond ability or sacred weapon ability to enhance it, a crusader's longsword increases the points of enhancement bonus she has to spend on its abilities by 1.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *holy smite*, *summon monster I*, creator must be a paladin or a warpriest; Cost 23,815 gp.

### Lawman's Pistol

*Aura* moderate divination; *CL* 9th; *Slot* none; *Price* 20,050 gp; *Weight* 5 lbs.

This +1 double-barreled pistol contains a single grit feat from the following list: Deft Shootist Deed, Leaping Shot Deed, No Name, and Ricochet Shot Deed. Anyone who wields or carries the lawman's pistol and meets the feat's prerequisites gains the feat's benefit. The wielder can't use the pistol's feat to meet prerequisites.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, chosen grit feat, *share memory*; Cost 11,050 gp.

### Spear of Ancestral Spirits

*Aura* moderate conjuration; *CL* 9th; *Slot* none; *Price* 15,302 gp; *Weight* 6 lbs.

When the wielder of this glimmering +1 *ghost touch* spear enters a rage, she can drum the spear against a shield or the ground as a swift action. If she does, colorful lights dance around the spear and the wielder can choose to gain the benefit of *guidance*, grant the touched shield the *ghost touch* special ability, or grant the spear the *returning* special ability. The chosen benefit lasts for the duration of the rage.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *guidance*, *faerie fire*, *plane shift*; Cost 7,802 gp.

### Valorous Blade

*Aura* moderate enchantment; *CL* 9th; *Slot* none; *Price* 20,315 gp; *Weight* 5 lbs.

Once per day within 1 minute of using this +1 *valiant* scimitar to kill or knock unconscious a foe, as a standard action, the wielder can inspire all allies within 60 feet who can see her. Those allies gain a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws for 10 minutes.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *good hope*, *true strike*; Cost 10,315 gp.

### Windreader's Bow

*Aura* moderate divination; *CL* 8th; *Slot* none; *Price* 54,400 gp; *Weight* 3 lbs.

Whenever an arrow fired from this +1 *adaptive seeking* composite longbow damages the wielder's favored enemy or studied target (as per the ranger and slayer class features, respectively), in addition to its normal effects, it also deals 2 points of bleed damage. As long as the bleed effect continues, the wielder can sense that foe's approximate direction and distance twice per day by smelling the wind as a standard action.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *bleed*, *locate creature*, *warp wood*; Cost 27,400 gp.
