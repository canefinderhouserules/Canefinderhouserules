## A

### Arbalest Cloak

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 9th

**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

Images of ballistae ornament this metallic cloak, which fits easily over armor and other bulky clothing. The wearer can activate this cloak as a swift action (1 AP), causing the garment to splay outward for 1 round, its jagged hem protruding around the wearer in stiff, sharp points. While the cloak is active, the wearer counts as wearing masterwork armor spikes with which she is proficient, and she can walk on air (as though using the [*air walk*](../../magic/spells/air-walk.md) spell). If she’s riding a mount, the *air walk* effect instead affects the mount while the cloak is active, and the mount can move with the item’s help even if it’s not specially trained to move with the aid of *air walk*. If the wearer (or her mount) isn’t standing on a solid surface by the end of the round, she immediately falls, taking any applicable falling damage.

In addition, on her first successful charge attack during the round the cloak is active, the wearer deals an additional 1d6 points of piercing damage (1d4 if she is Small), plus another 1d6 (or 1d4 if she is Small) for every 20 feet the wearer traveled in a straight line during the charge (maximum 3d6).

This cloak can be activated up to three times per day, but not more often than once per minute.

**Construction**

**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item, [*air walk*](../../magic/spells/air-walk.md),* bull’s strength*; **Cost** 4,500 gp

*Source: Adventure Items.*

### Aspergillum Cloak

**Slot** shoulders; **CL** 7th; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

**Aura** moderate transmutation

This drab brown, hooded cloak acts as a *cloak of resistance +1* but also quickly absorbs any liquids poured onto it, drying in moments. Three times per day, the wearer can pour a vial of holy water onto the interior lining of an *aspergillum cloak*, causing its outer surface to glisten and drip. The holy water cannot be recovered once absorbed. For 7 minutes, the garment is soaked in holy water, dealing 2d4 points of damage to any undead or evil outsider that strikes the wearer with an unarmed strike or a natural weapon. An *aspergillum cloak* can also absorb unholy water instead, dealing damage to good outsiders instead. An *aspergillum cloak* gains no special abilities from absorbing any other liquid.

**Construction Requirements:** Craft Wondrous Item, [*bless water*](../../magic/spells/bless-water.md); **Cost** 4,000 gp

*Source: Player Companion Items, Secret Faith Items.*

## B

### Bear Pelt of the Bonebreaker

**Slot** shoulders; **CL** 7th; **Price** 3,300 gp; **Weight** 10 lbs.

**Aura** moderate transmutation

This large bearskin cloak is almost too big for a human to wear comfortably. The wearer gains a +2 competence bonus on Intimidate checks. Once per day, the wearer can use [*bless*](../../magic/spells/bless.md); using this ability causes the hair on the cloak to bristle menacingly.

If the stone giant deity of strength is the wearer’s patron, the wearer can use [*beast shape II*](../../magic/spells/beast-shape-ii.md) once per day to transform into a black or brown bear for up to 7 minutes. Some champions of this deity claim to have more powerful pelts that allow them to take the form of polar bears or even dire bears.

**Construction Requirements:** Craft Wondrous Item, [*beast shape II*](../../magic/spells/beast-shape-ii.md), [*bless*](../../magic/spells/bless.md), creator must have 5 ranks in Intimidate; **Cost** 1,650 gp

*Source: Patron Items, Items.*

### Cloak of Human Guise

**Aura** faint illusion; **CL** 1st  
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 900 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This plain cloak only has any effect when worn by a member of a half-human race such as a half-orc or half-elf. It alters the wearer’s appearance similarly to a *hat of disguise*, but only to the extent that it conceals or alters the wearer’s non-human physical traits so the creature appears fully human. Any feature that is plausibly human remains. For example, a half-orc wearing the cloak loses his green or gray skin color and pointed ears, has no visible tusks, and is otherwise completely able to pass as a human version of his normal self. Likewise, a half-elf wearing the cloak has round ears, humanlike eyes, and no other traits indicating elf ancestry. The wearer has no control over the specific guise, and those familiar with his normal appearance can recognize him in his human guise.

**Construction** Craft Wondrous Item, disguise self; **Cost** 450 gp

*Source: Racial Magic Items (Advanced Race Guide), Half-Orc Items.*

### Cloak of the Diplomat

**Aura** moderate enchantment; **CL** 7th  
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 20,000 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Half-elves are often called upon to mediate disputes when elves and humans come into conflict. This is due in part to their blood, but also to their natural skills as facilitators and diplomats. The *cloak of the diplomat* greatly aids them in this task. The cloak grants a +5 competence bonus on Diplomacy and Sense Motive checks. In addition, once per day, before making a Diplomacy or Sense Motive check, the wearer can decide to call upon the powers of the cloak to roll twice and take the better result. These cloth shoulder straps have many pebbles stitched into them. The wearer of the straps can command them to unfurl a pair of wings made from a hard gray stone. These wings grant her a fly speed of 60 feet and average maneuverability. When these wings are present her skin also takes on a noticeable stone-like appearance and she gains DR 10/ adamantine. The wearer can furl and unfurl these wings as a swift action (1 AP). The wings can be used for up to 5 minutes each day. The duration need not be continuous, but it must be used in 1-minute increments.

**Construction** Craft Wondrous Item, [*fly*](../../magic/spells/fly.md), stoneskin; **Cost** 36,000 gp

*Source: Ultimate Equipment Shoulders Wondrous Items.*

### Winter Guard Pauldrons

*Aura* faint abjuration; *Slot* shoulders; *Price* 5,750 gp; *Weight* 6 lbs.

These simple, leather-strapped iron pauldrons are engraved with intricate images of icy winds and howling winter wolves, and are worn by senior members of a military order that serves a winter queen. The pauldrons grant their wearer a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against fire effects. If the wearer succeeds at a saving throw against an enemy's fire effect, the pauldrons shimmer with blue light for 5 rounds. The wearer also gains a +2 competence bonus on Intimidate checks. This bonus increases to +5 while the pauldrons cast blue light. The wearer gains the benefits of [*endure elements*](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md), but only for cold temperatures.

*Construction* *Requirements* Craft Wondrous Item, [*endure elements*](../../magic/spells/endure-elements.md), [*resistance*](../../magic/spells/resistance.md); *Cost* 2,875 gp

*Source: Regional Combat Items, Rings, Rods, and Wondrous Items.*

### Wyvern Cloak

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th  
**Slot** shoulders; **Price** 78,600 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

This heavy cloak is made of blue scaled leather and has a pointed hem that hangs near the ground. The cloak provides the wearer a +4 resistance bonus on Will saves, a continuous [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md) effect (as the spell), and the ability to use [*fly*](../../magic/spells/fly.md) once per day. At will as a swift action (1 AP), the wearer can form the hem into a stinger, granting her a sting natural weapon. This sting deals 1d6 points of damage plus [*poison*](../../magic/spells/poison.md) (as the spell; DC 16). Reverting the stinger to its normal, inconspicuous shape is a free action.

**Construction** Craft Wondrous Item, [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md), [*fly*](../../magic/spells/fly.md), [*poison*](../../magic/spells/poison.md), [*resistance*](../../magic/spells/resistance.md), creator must be at least caster level 12; **Cost** 39,300 gp

*Source: Ultimate Equipment Shoulders Wondrous Items.*
