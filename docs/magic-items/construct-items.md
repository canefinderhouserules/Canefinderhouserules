# Construct Items

Magic items for those who create, command, or destroy constructs. See [Crafting Constructs](../magic/mastering-magic/crafting-constructs.md) for the rules on building them.

## Automaton Core

*Aura* strong transmutation; *CL* 20th; *Slot* none; *Weight* 1 lb.; *Minor artifact*

These glowing metal cylinders are often plated in gold or silver and decorated with tiny, glowing arcane symbols. They primarily power an automaton, but one harvested from an automaton's body can also enhance spellcasting. A spellcaster holding an automaton core treats her caster level as 4 higher for all purposes.

A core contains 1 spell point per CR of the automaton it came from. As a swift action, a spellcaster can expend spell points to cast a spell she has prepared without expending that spell or spell slot; the cost is twice the spell's level. Once all spell points are spent, the core can no longer power an automaton, and there is no known method to restore it.

A core can also offset the cost of building a construct by 2,500 gp per spell point; a core used this way can still be removed under the normal rules. Automatons covet these cores and have been known to ruthlessly hunt down creatures who carry them.

**Destruction**: An automaton core can be destroyed only by placing it in a ritual circle, surrounded by six other automaton cores, which must then expend a number of spell points equal to the remaining points in the central core. This overloads the central core, destroying it forever.

## Chirurgeon Cube

*Aura* moderate transmutation; *CL* 11th; *Slot* none; *Price* 9,800 gp; *Weight* —

This 1-inch steel cube bears intricate carvings on each face. On command, it sprouts wings and arms and becomes a Diminutive cube-shaped mechanical construct (treat as a thrush with hardness 10) that serves whoever activated it. It understands Common but cannot speak. As a standard action, it can attach itself to a construct and stitch together torn and broken pieces, giving that construct fast healing 2 for as long as it remains in contact. It can be activated for up to 10 minutes each day, in 1-minute increments. If it is broken or destroyed in either form, it is forever ruined.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *animate objects*, *fly*, *make whole*; Cost 4,900 gp.

## Diadem of Control

*Aura* strong enchantment; *CL* 15th; *Slot* headband; *Price* 189,000 gp; *Weight* —

This elegant headband has an intricately carved crystal in its center. The wearer can remove or replace the crystal as a swift action. As a standard action, the wearer can try to force the gem onto a construct with a touch attack that provokes an attack of opportunity. Once attached, the construct must succeed at a DC 23 Will save or become dominated by the crystal and follow the orders of whoever wears the headband. The wearer can assume direct control as a full-round action, controlling all of the construct's actions as *possession*, and can end this control at any time as a free action. The wearer can remove the crystal from a construct as a standard action, returning control to its original master and freeing the crystal for another construct. If the construct is destroyed while the crystal is attached, there is a 10% chance the crystal is destroyed with it.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *dominate monster*, *possession*; Cost 94,500 gp.

## Energizing Salve

*Aura* faint transmutation; *CL* 5th; *Slot* none; *Price* 3,000 gp; *Weight* 1/2 lb.

When smeared onto a construct as a standard action, this foul-smelling paste grants the construct a +2 dodge bonus to AC and a +2 insight bonus on attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks. In addition, the construct can make an additional attack at its full base attack bonus when making a full-attack action. The salve remains effective for 5 minutes. It can be wiped off with an alcohol-based solution such as ale or wine, as a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *haste*; Cost 1,500 gp.

## Golem Manual

*Aura* varies; *CL* varies; *Slot* none; *Price* see table; *Weight* 5 lbs.

A golem manual contains information, incantations, and magical powers that help a character craft a golem. It grants a +5 competence bonus on skill checks to craft the golem's body, holds all prerequisite spells for one golem type (usable only to create that golem and not copyable), effectively grants Craft Construct for that golem, and increases the reader's caster level for crafting it. The spells require spell trigger activation. The price does not include the cost of the golem's body. When the golem is finished, the writing fades and the book is consumed in flames; sprinkling the ashes on the golem fully animates it.

## Machinebane Oil

*Aura* faint transmutation; *CL* 5th; *Slot* none; *Price* 750 gp; *Weight* 1/2 lb.

This shimmering violet unguent is an effective weapon against constructs made of metal. A creature can throw the vial at a construct as a ranged touch attack with a range of 20 feet. On a hit, the construct must succeed at a DC 15 Reflex save or become staggered for 5 rounds. If it fails the initial save, it must attempt an additional save at the start of its turn for the next 5 rounds, taking 2 points of Dexterity damage on each failure. A construct struck by machinebane oil immediately loses the benefits of *energizing salve*.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *disable construct*; Cost 750 gp.

## Necklace of Construct Aversion

*Aura* moderate divination and enchantment; *CL* 11th; *Slot* neck; *Price* 132,000 gp; *Weight* 1 lb.

The wearer is automatically aware of all constructs within 60 feet, as per the lifesense ability, but senses only constructs. Constructs cannot see, hear, or otherwise detect the wearer, even with extraordinary or supernatural senses such as blindsense, blindsight, scent, or tremorsense. If the wearer touches a construct or attacks any creature, this concealment ceases for 1 minute. A construct with an Intelligence of 3 or higher can attempt a DC 18 Will save to detect the wearer.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *invisibility*, *locate object*; Cost 66,000 gp.

## Oculus of Magnetic Fury

*Aura* moderate transmutation; *CL* 10th; *Slot* none; *Price* 90,000 gp; *Weight* 1 lb.

Fashioned from the eye of a robot, this device lets its holder, once per hour as a standard action, target a single robot within 60 feet with an electromagnetic pulse. The robot takes 10d6 electricity damage and becomes helpless for 1 minute; a successful DC 18 Will save halves the damage and negates the helpless effect. A robot rendered helpless gets a new Will save at the end of each of its turns to shake off the effect.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *disable construct*, *lightning bolt*; Cost 45,000 gp.

## Suppression Gem

*Aura* moderate enchantment; *CL* 12th; *Slot* none; *Price* 65,000 gp; *Weight* —

This small gem crackles with arcing electricity. As a standard action, a creature can try to place it on a construct with a touch attack that provokes an attack of opportunity. Once placed, the creature that placed it can issue orders to the construct as if she were its original creator until the gem is destroyed. The construct can resist self-destructive commands with a successful DC 14 Will save. The original owner can use a standard action to try to regain control with a Charisma check opposed by the gem user's Charisma check, and the gem's user can do the same to retake control. A suppression gem has 30 hit points and hardness 10.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *suggestion*; Cost 32,500 gp.
