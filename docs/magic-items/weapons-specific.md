## A

### Alchemist’s Bullet

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 3rd  
**Price** 330 gp; **Weight** 1/2 lb.

This *+1 sling bullet* contains a magical reservoir that can store a single vial of any alchemical substance capable of being used as a splash weapon. If the *alchemist bullet* hits, the splash weapon also strikes the target and causes splash damage as normal. If the *alchemist’s bullet* misses but is not destroyed, the alchemical substance remains stored within, and the bullet does not explode and can be reused. If it misses and is destroyed, the bullet is destroyed as well without effect.

**Construction** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*shrink item*](../magic/spells/shrink-item.md); **Cost** 180 gp

*Source: Ultimate Equipment Magic Weapons.*

### Angelfall Bow

**Aura** moderate evocation **CL** 7th

**Slot** none; **Price** 26,900 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

Black feathers adorn the tips of this ebony *+1 composite longbow* (+5 Strength bonus). The bow gives off the faint scent of brimstone when the string is drawn back.

Three times per day as a swift action (1 AP), the wielder of an *angelfall bow* can imbue the next arrow she fires that round with the bow’s inherent raging energy. On a hit, the attack deals damage as normal and acts as a trip combat maneuver to knock the target prone, treating the result of the wielder’s attack roll as the combat maneuver check result. If the target is flying, it must immediately succeed at a DC 25 Fly check to stay aloft or plummet to the ground.

Once per day when the wielder of an *angelfall bow* would be affected by a spell or spell-like ability with the light descriptor, she can attempt an immediate dispel check (using the caster level of the bow) to counter the effect. This manifests as a pulsating darkness emerging from the bow and swallowing the light.

**Construction**

**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item, *dust of twilight*APG,* thundering drums*APG; **Price** 13,750 gp

*Source: Adventure Items.*

### Ash Hammer

**Aura** moderate conjuration and evocation **CL**

**Slot** none; **Price** 12,312 gp; **Weight** 5 lbs.

This pitch-black *+1 flaming warhammer* is bereft of any ornamentation beyond an engraving on its face of a bull’s head with an open flame, the unholy symbol of the archdevil the archdevil of fire and sacrifice. Whenever the wielder of *ash hammer* successfully strikes a creature with the hammer, he can create a cloud of ash around the target as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn). This ash shares the target’s space and persists for 1 round before dissipating. The ash does not obscure sight, but the target and any creatures that enter the cloud must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude save or take a –2 penalty on attack rolls for 1 round as they cough and choke on the fiery ash. Holding one’s breath provides no benefit, but creatures that don’t breathe or that have any kind of fire immunity or resistance are immune to the cloud’s effects.

If the archdevil of fire and sacrifice is the wielder’s patron, the wielder can forge masterwork armor and weapons with *ash hammer*, which reduces the DC of the Craft check for items’ masterwork components to 10 (the Craft DC for the items’ standard components are unchanged). Items crafted in this way have a faint evil aura for the purposes of detect evil or similar effects.

**Construction**

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *ash storm*UM,* fireball*, creator must worship the archdevil of fire and sacrifice and have 5 ranks in Craft (armor) or Craft (weapons); **Price** 6,812 gp

*Source: Adventure Items.*

### Assassin's Mirage

*Aura* moderate illusion and transmutation; *Slot* none; *Price* 65,575 gp; *Weight* 2 lbs.

With a blade crafted entirely out of black metal, this +2 *speed sawtooth sabre* has only one hint of color—a red hilt. Once per day as a standard action (1 AP), the wielder of the blade can create 1d4+3 illusory assassins that last for 1 hour. These illusions confound the wielder's enemies, allowing the wielder to flank an enemy with an appropriately positioned figment. The wielder can direct the movement of all figments as a single swift action (1 AP), moving each of them up to 30 feet. Any creature interacting with (or being flanked by) an illusion can attempt a DC 16 Will save to disbelieve the effect. If an illusory assassin is hit by an attack, it disappears. (Use the wielder's AC as the illusion's AC.) Three times per day as a swift action (1 AP), when the wielder is the target of an enemy's melee attack, she can swap places with an illusory assassin that is adjacent to her.

*Construction* *Requirements* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*haste*](../magic/spells/haste.md), [*mirror image*](../magic/spells/mirror-image.md), [*mislead*](../magic/spells/mislead.md); *Cost* 32,955 gp

### Axe of the Imperative

**Aura** moderate necromancy **CL** 9th

**Slot** none; **Price** 5,310 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

An *axe of the imperative* is a *+1 battleaxe* that cuts through willpower and resolve as surely as it hews flesh and bone. On a successful hit against a creature, the wielder can utter a short command as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) to prohibit the struck creature from a specific course of action. This command must consist five words or fewer, such as, “Never attack me again!” or “Never cast an enchantment spell!” or “Don’t set foot in the capital!” Once the imperative is uttered, the wound caused by the hit glows with faint golden light, as does the blade of the axe. This glow persists even after the damage is healed. The imperative lasts until one of two things occurs. It ends if the wielder utters another imperative (only one imperative can be in effect at a time), or if the target creature attempts an action that would break the imperative. When the target takes that action, it takes damage as if struck with a successful critical hit by the wielder of the *axe of the imperative*. A successful DC 15 Fortitude save negates this damage. When this occurs, the wielder immediately knows that the target broke the imperative. A lingering imperative can be removed without harming the target as if it were a curse effect. The imperative is a sonic language-dependent effect.

**Construction**

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*mark of justice*](../magic/spells/mark-of-justice.md); **Price** 2,830 gp

*Source: Adventure Items.*

### Beaststrike Club

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 6th  
**Slot** none; **Price** 7,300 gp; **Weight** 3 lbs.

This +1 club is often decorated with large teeth and carved with stylized images of different animals. As a standard action (1 AP), the wielder may transform the club into the head or limb of an animal; the weapon’s damage does not change but the type of damage changes to suit the shape of the club: bite (piercing), claw (slashing), gore (piercing), slam (bludgeoning), sting (piercing), or talon (slashing). When transformed, the club counts as a natural weapon and a manufactured weapon for the purpose of spells and effects that enhance or improve either manufactured or natural weapons. The club also counts as the natural weapon of its current shape for the purpose of feats and spells that rely on specific natural weapons, such as Weapon Focus (bite) or the bloody claws spell. If the wielder has the wild shape ability, she can expend one use of wild shape to increase the weapon’s damage by one step (see Pathfinder RPG Core Rulebook) for the duration of that wild shape as long as the druid remains in contact with the weapon; if the wielder can wild shape at will, the club’s damage is always increased in this fashion. If unattended, the club reverts to its normal shape.

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, shillelagh; **Cost** 3,800 gp

*Source: Advanced Player's Guide Magic Weapons.*

### Blade of Binding

**Aura** faint conjuration; **CL** 3rd  
**Slot** none; **Price** 12,350 gp; **Weight** 8 lbs.

This +1 greatsword is decorated with a chain motif. On a successful hit with the sword against a creature of the wielder’s size or smaller creature, the wielder can attempt to start a grapple as a free action that doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity. If the wielder succeeds at the grapple, the sword blade transforms into a metal chain and wraps around the target, giving the wielder a +5 bonus on further grapple checks against the target. If the target escapes the grapple or the wielder decides to release the target from the grapple, the chain reverts to sword form (with the wielder holding the handle). The wielder can also let go of the chain’s handle while grappling or pinning the target; if so, the chain remains in place and the target gains the entangled condition (Escape Artist DC 20 to slip free, break DC 28, hardness 10, 10 hit points, concentration DC 17 to cast spells while entangled). Another creature can grab the chain’s handle as a standard action (1 AP) and will it to revert to sword form as a swift action (1 AP), which releases the bound creature. ### Blade of the Leopard

**Aura** moderate transmutation **CL** 9th

**Price** 10,302 gp; **Weight** 1 lb.

Crafted some 300 years ago, This blade is a katar some 15 inches in length. An embossed image of a ferocious desert leopard adorns the weapon’s hilt, while strange shadowy patterns writhe across its damascened blade, even in strong light.

Easily twice the size of common leopards, desert leopards are infamous for their savagery. Now extremely rare, these massive creatures once ranged across the northern deserts (same stats as tigers). Treat wereleopard lycanthropes as chaotic evil weretigers (MM 172).

### Blade of the Rising Sun

**Aura** strong evocation and necromancy; **CL** 15th  
**Price** 51,850 gp; **Weight** 6 lbs.

This golden-bladed *+1 glorious undead bane katana* shines like the first rays of the morning sun. Whenever the wielder of a *blade of the rising sun* threatens a critical threat (regardless of whether the critical is confirmed or whether the target is subject to critical hits), the target is surrounded by a halo of golden sunlight equivalent to [*faerie fire*](../magic/spells/faerie-fire.md) until the beginning of the wielder’s next turn. Undead surrounded by this glow take 1d6 points of damage at the beginning of their turn.

The wielder of a *blade of the rising sun* may declare a sacrificial strike (similar to the retributive strike of a *staff of power*) as a standard action (1 AP), or as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) before losing consciousness upon gaining the dying or dead condition. A sacrificial strike converts the wielder’s body and spirit into raw energy equivalent to a [*sunburst*](../magic/spells/sunburst.md) (DC 22). Allies of the wielder within the *sunburst* are not harmed, and the wielder may choose to affect all allies within the *sunburst* as [*mass cure light wounds*](../magic/spells/cure-light-wounds-mass.md) or all allies within 10 feet as [*breath of life*](../magic/spells/breath-of-life.md). The caster level for the effects of the sacrificial strike is equal to the sword’s caster level plus 1/2 the wielder’s level (maximum 25th level).

A sacrificial strike destroys the wielder’s body (as the spell [*disintegrate*](../magic/spells/disintegrate.md)), but she has a 50% chance of being reincarnated (as the [*reincarnate*](../magic/spells/reincarnate.md) spell) 24 hours after performing a sacrificial strike in a random safe location within 1 mile. The wielder’s gear is left behind unharmed after the sacrificial strike; however, the *blade of the rising sun* is teleported to a random location at least 100 miles distant.

**Construction** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*blindness/deafness*](../magic/spells/blindness-deafness.md), [*daylight*](../magic/spells/daylight.md), [*flare*](../magic/spells/flare.md), [*miracle*](../magic/spells/miracle.md), [*sunburst*](../magic/spells/sunburst.md); **Cost** 28,100 gp

### Blade of the Willing Martyr

The *blade of the willing martyr* is a *+3 keen vicious dagger*, but its greatest power can only be invoked outside of battle. Once you forge a link, your soul and the target’s soul are intertwined. Should you die, your target dies, suffering the same apparent injuries and symptoms; likewise, you die if your target dies. The link may only be severed by [*break enchantment*](../magic/spells/break-enchantment.md) or [*remove curse*](../magic/spells/remove-curse.md).

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, [*enervation*](../magic/spells/enervation.md), [*keen edge*](../magic/spells/keen-edge.md), [*symbol of death*](../magic/spells/symbol-of-death.md); Cost 75,000 gp.
