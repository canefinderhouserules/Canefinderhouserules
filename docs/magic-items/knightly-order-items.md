# Knightly Order Items

Items used by the knightly orders of infernal knights and their foes. See [Infernal Knight](../characters/prestige-classes/infernal-knight.md).

## Conquering Brand

*Aura* varies; *CL* varies; *Slot* none; *Price* varies (see below); *Weight* 5 lbs.

Each of these 3-foot-long branding irons ends in a distinctive, cruel-looking symbol. Once per day on command, the wielder can cause the tip to grow red-hot for 1 hour, and during this time one creature can be branded. The recipient is burned as normal for a branding iron and is also affected as per *brand* or another spell the iron carries, as the wielder chooses. A conquering brand can create only one magical brand per day.

## Pyre Salt

*Aura* faint evocation; *CL* 2nd; *Slot* none; *Price* 700 gp; *Weight* —

Composed of glistening black-and-white granules that smell of smoke, pyre salt typically comes in a narrow glass vial containing one use. When it is cast into any magical or mundane flame whose area is at least 10 feet by 10 feet, the fire in that area deals double the normal damage to any objects or creatures in that space for 1 round. It cannot be activated by fire spells or effects cast onto it.

Additionally, objects or creatures destroyed by the affected fire are exceptionally difficult to restore. Anyone casting *mending*, *make whole*, or a similar spell on an object destroyed by the fire must first succeed at a DC 20 caster level check or the spell fails. Likewise, anyone casting *raise dead*, *resurrection*, or a similar spell on a creature killed by the fire must succeed at a DC 20 caster level check or the spell fails.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *produce flame*; Cost 350 gp.

## Pyre Bead

*Aura* moderate transmutation; *CL* 7th; *Slot* none; *Price* 1,440 gp; *Weight* —

This tiny glass bead transforms into a 10-foot-by-10-foot pile of logs and kindling when its command word is spoken, with a sturdy 8-foot-tall post jutting from the center. The heap does not light on its own, but anyone who touches it and says a second command word causes it to burst into flame (this also automatically activates any pyre salt in the flame's area). The fire burns for 4 hours or until extinguished, at which point it turns back into a bead. The owner must wait twice as long as the fire burned before the bead can be ordered to become a pile of logs again.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *death knell*, *pyrotechnics*; Cost 720 gp.

## Mindrust

*Type* poison (inhaled); *Price* 1,600 gp; *Save* Fortitude DC 20; *Frequency* 1/round for 6 rounds; *Effect* 1 Wis damage and suggestible for 1 minute per point of Wisdom damage taken; *Cure* 2 consecutive saves

A brownish, flaky concoction of condensed nightmare vapor, striped toadstools, and howler milk. Any creature that fails its Fortitude saving throw takes 1 point of Wisdom damage on each subsequent failed save. For every point of Wisdom damage a sentient creature takes, it becomes skittish and susceptible to *suggestion* for 1 minute; these minutes are cumulative. During that time, anyone can use the Intimidate skill as if it were Diplomacy to make a request of the creature, and the creature takes a –4 penalty on saving throws against spells from the compulsion subschool. If the poison is neutralized, the susceptibility to *suggestion* immediately ends.
