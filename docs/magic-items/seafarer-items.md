# Seafarer Items

Magic items found aboard ships and among seafaring raiders.

## Scrying Aviary

*Aura* moderate divination; *Slot* none; *Price* 16,000 gp; *Weight* 5 lbs.

This reinforced wicker box is decorated with images of owls, ravens, or other cunning birds and has room for up to three Tiny birds. Any bird of the animal type stored in the aviary for 24 hours becomes attuned to its magic, radiating the same aura as the aviary itself. By issuing a command word and concentrating on the bond between their spirits, a user can direct a released bird to fly either to a known location (as with *animal messenger*) or in a specific direction. The bird flies in a direct route at its normal speed. For the next hour, by maintaining physical contact with the aviary, the user can scry through the bird's senses, making the bird the sensor for a *clairaudience/clairvoyance* spell. While scrying, the user cannot sense her own surroundings, but she can switch between her senses and the bird's as a move action.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *animal messenger*, *clairaudience/clairvoyance*, crafter must have 5 ranks in Handle Animal; Cost 8,000 gp.

## Amulet of the Frozen Heart

*Aura* strong abjuration; *Slot* neck; *Price* 40,000 gp; *Weight* —

Crafted by evil spellcasters who walk the first steps of a dark path of winter transformation, this talisman is a tiny human figure formed from unmelting ice that rests on the wearer's chest. When worn, it freezes the wearer's heart solid, chilling body and soul. The wearer immediately develops hypothermia, becoming permanently fatigued for as long as he wears the amulet, but is otherwise protected against cold weather by a constant *endure elements* effect. The amulet also grants cold resistance 10, immunity to bleed effects, and a +4 resistance bonus on saving throws against spells with the emotion descriptor. Once per day as a standard action, the wearer can use *frigid touch* as a spell-like ability. Removing the amulet thaws the wearer's heart but leaves him fatigued until he spends 8 hours warming up.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *calm emotions*, *endure elements*, *frigid touch*; Cost 21,600 gp.

## Traps and Haunts

### Thief's Guilt Trap

**CR** 9; **Type** magic; **Perception** DC 33; **Disable Device** DC 33  
**Trigger** touch; **Reset** none; **Bypass** key  
**Effect** spell effect (heightened *geas/quest* [CL 15th]); the target is commanded to leave all contents of the protected chest untouched and to turn himself over to the owner for disciplinary action for accessing her personal effects.

### Shocking Lock Trap

**CR** 13; **Type** magic; **Perception** DC 31; **Disable Device** DC 31  
**Trigger** touch; **Reset** none  
**Effect** a magical trap on a lock meant to shock any thief attempting to access the container. If the floor of the room is soggy or wet, the discharge arcs to affect all creatures touching the floor.
