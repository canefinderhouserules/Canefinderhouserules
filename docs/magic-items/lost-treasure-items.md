# Lost Treasure Items

Unique and legendary relics. Each is a single one-of-a-kind item; names of places, peoples and deities have been made neutral.

## Astrolabe

*Aura* strong divination; *Slot* none; *Price* 16,000 gp; *Weight* 3 lbs.

An astrolabe is essentially an extraplanar compass, a device that planar explorers use to orient themselves in realms without shared cardinal directions or physical laws. It creates an abstraction of north, east, south and west, as well as up, down, in, out, back, forward, past, future and other "directions" useful for navigating the planes. All astrolabes share a uniform internal design and give consistent, shared directions, so planar cartographers use them to make maps. The device keeps a constant orientation, but it does not adjust for or circumvent hazards and does not show particular paths. Any creature not on the Material Plane who uses an astrolabe while attempting a Knowledge, Survival or similar skill check to navigate gains a +4 bonus on that check.

An astrolabe can also help a traveler prepare to cross to another plane. It reads emanations from portals, gates and other connections between planes and displays them on its surface. A successful Knowledge (planes) check interprets the display (usually DC 25; demiplanes and stranger realms may require a higher DC or yield inconclusive results). Those who succeed learn the name of the plane beyond the portal, but not the exact location on that plane; it does not reveal tiers, layers or sub-realms of divided planes. Those who fail cannot tell what lies beyond, and those who fail by 10 or more incorrectly identify the plane, with the result decided by the GM.

An astrolabe does not function on the Material Plane, shutting down and giving off only a faint divination aura. If it is brought within 20 feet of a portal or similar passage to another plane, it activates for as long as it remains nearby. On any other plane it is active continuously.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *know direction*, cannot be created on the Material Plane; Cost 8,000 gp.

## Atavistic Splinter

*Aura* moderate transmutation; *Slot* none; *Price* 37,000 gp

This sheet of charred bark is 2 feet long, 10 inches wide and 1 inch thick. It gives its owner primal authority over beasts, granting a +4 insight bonus on wild empathy checks.

Once each day, the owner can touch the Splinter to a humanoid to force an animalistic transformation (a melee touch attack against an unwilling target). The target must succeed at a DC 16 Fortitude save or acquire the **bestial** simple template (see below) for 24 hours. After that period, the target must succeed at a DC 14 Fortitude save or keep the template for another 24 hours, and the Intelligence and Charisma penalties from the template each increase to –4. Each day the target keeps the template it attempts another Fortitude save; each successive day the save DC decreases by 2 and the Intelligence and Charisma penalties increase by 2 (to a minimum score of 2 in each). The effect cannot be dispelled, but it can be removed with *break enchantment*, *limited wish*, *miracle*, *remove curse* or *wish*. If both Intelligence and Charisma reach 2, the template becomes permanent and can be removed only by *miracle* or *wish*. A target that succeeds at a saving throw is immune to the Splinter for 1 year.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *dominate animal*, *polymorph*; Cost 18,500 gp.

### Bestial (Simple Template)

Bestial is an acquired simple template that can be applied to any humanoid that does not have the wild empathy class feature. Afflicted humanoids gain traits of a random wild animal from the waist up, appearing much like a lycanthrope in hybrid form (bears, boars, rats, weasels and wolves are common). The transformation makes them resilient and gives them rudimentary natural attacks, but dulls the mind and imparts a brutish demeanor.

*Rebuild Rules* **AC** natural armor bonus increases by 1; **Attacks** gain 2 claw attacks and 1 bite attack (all primary natural attacks), dealing damage as though the creature were one size category smaller; **Ability Scores** +2 Con, –2 Int, –2 Cha.

## Bamboo Palace

*Price* 1,000 gp (8,000 gp when restored); *Weight* 10 lbs.

A shallow celadon pot holds a miniaturized stand of bamboo trained into the shape of an ornate royal palace. The bonsai itself is nonmagical, and in its pristine state is a magnificent work of the art. Currently overgrown, its price reflects its neglect. If an experienced bonsai gardener tends it once a week for 1 year (a successful DC 15 Knowledge [nature] check and DC 15 Knowledge [engineering] check each week), it returns to its manicured form and its value to a collector rises to 8,000 gp.

If the bonsai is fully restored, a character who spends 15 minutes tending it and succeeds at DC 20 Knowledge (nature) and Knowledge (engineering) checks gains the favor of the guardian spirit (a kami of ornamental plants) that lives within it. For 24 hours, whenever the favored character begins a round within 15 feet of the bonsai, the kami's aura of luck grants a +2 luck bonus on all skill checks. Only one character can gain this favor each day.

## Beacon of the North

*Aura* strong conjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 90,000 gp; *Weight* 4 lbs.

This heavy cast-iron bullseye lantern has dull blue glass and frost-themed scrollwork. Using any of its powers requires a special lamp oil made only by a coven of winter witches; it holds enough oil to burn for 8 hours. When lit it burns with a cold light, and if the burning oil is used as a weapon it deals cold damage instead of fire damage.

As a standard action the wielder can focus the beam on a single living creature as a ranged touch attack. This consumes 1 minute's worth of oil and opens a small portal from the frozen north to the inside of the target's body. The target takes 1d6 cold damage and suffers frostbite and hypothermia (treated as fatigued until all nonlethal damage is healed). Any creature that dies from this cold damage rises the following midnight as a draugr.

Also as a standard action, the user can focus the light on a depiction of a specific ship, creating a 5-foot-wide portal connecting the depiction to the ship's main mast. Any creature that touches one side of the portal appears at the other. The portals look like shimmering blue-white patches of light and you cannot see through them. They stay open as long as the lantern is lit, even if it no longer shines on the depiction.

Finally, if the lit beacon hangs on the prow of a ship, a ship-sized portal opens before the vessel and lets it sail through. This portal always leads to the harbor of the witches' frozen capital, and immediately closes any other portals the lantern has opened. Creating a portal of any size consumes 10 minutes' worth of oil.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *chill touch*, *teleportation circle*; Cost 45,000 gp.

## Bell of Obedience

*Aura* strong enchantment; *Slot* none; *Price* 157,500 gp; *Weight* 2,000 lbs.

This 4-foot-tall bell is cast from high-tin bronze and transmits a powerful compulsion to all who hear it. To function, it must be suspended from a yoke and sounded with a mallet or clapper. All creatures within 1,000 feet other than the user must succeed at a DC 20 Will save or be affected by a compulsion effect for 1 hour. The effect depends on the material of the yoke:

| Yoke Material | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bronze | *good hope* |
| Iron | *rage* |
| Oak | *calm emotions* |
| Stone | *crushing despair* |

The bell can be heard up to 1 mile away in most conditions, but has no effect on listeners more than 1,000 feet away. Humanoids with the human subtype are especially vulnerable and take a –2 penalty on saving throws against it. Normally the compulsion carries no special instructions, so enraged listeners might commit wanton violence and hopeful listeners simply become cheerful. If the user succeeds at a DC 25 Perform (percussion) check when sounding the bell, simple secondary instructions can be transmitted with the compulsion, such as directing enraged listeners to hate a particular object or inspiring the hopeful to credit their feelings to a particular cause. The bell can transmit a compulsion once every 8 hours.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, Widen Spell, *calm emotions*, *crushing despair*, *good hope*, *rage*; Cost 78,750 gp.

## Assassin Lord's Mask

*Aura* moderate divination, enchantment and illusion; *Slot* head; *Price* 52,500 gp; *Weight* 1 lb.
*Alignment* lawful evil; *Senses* 30 ft.; *Int* 12, *Wis* 15, *Cha* 10; *Ego* 12; *Language* speech (Common, Infernal)

This crimson mask has two enormous insectile eyes, a mouth with short mandibles and two thick, shorn-off antennae. It has three charges per day, which replenish every 24 hours. Each charge grants the wearer two of the following abilities for 30 minutes: darkvision to 60 feet, the effects of *see invisibility*, the effects of *death watch*, or a +5 competence bonus on Perception checks. Three times per day the wearer can also use *disguise self*, though the mask's stubby antennae always remain and must be hidden with hair or a hood. If the wearer strikes a target with a melee attack during a surprise round, that target cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components for 1 round.

The mask contains the spirit of a haughty assassin lord and has the special purpose of slaying insurrectionists, traitors and other challengers to rightful monarchs. It can use *geas/quest* once per day, but rarely does so at the wearer's behest; instead it targets the wearer whenever it dominates him in a personality conflict, tasking him to assassinate a target suited to its purpose. While the wearer pursues the goal, the mask uses its abilities to aid him. It can spend its charges to improve its own senses rather than the wearer's, and is likely to waste charges this way if the wearer resists its will.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *darkvision*, *death watch*, *disguise self*, *geas/quest*, *see invisibility*; Cost 26,250 gp.

## Dossal of Salt and Tears

*Aura* moderate abjuration and transmutation (lawful); *Slot* none; *CL* 10th; *Price* 50,000 gp; *Weight* 10 lbs.

This damp, ornately embroidered tapestry depicts a dead god performing a series of miracles and drips water as if weeping. Unrolled it measures 3 yards by 1-1/2 yards. It has the properties of a magic altar of that dead god. Praying at it grants a +2 competence bonus on all Knowledge checks, or one of the following weapon special abilities (the worshiper's choice) on one magic weapon carried or wielded by the worshiper: *axiomatic* or *bone*. The effect is suppressed if the weapon leaves the worshiper's grasp and resumes if it returns. A day's worth of the water that drips from the dossal acts as a vial of holy water. These abilities work only for characters who worship the dead god or the god who inherited his mantle, and last for 24 hours. They come from the magic of the altar itself, not from the dead god.

Because of the successor deity's blessing, a cleric of that deity who prepares spells in the dossal's presence can prepare domain spells from one of the Community, Knowledge, Law or Protection domains (the four domains the dead god once granted that the successor does not), gaining none of the other domain abilities. Although most altars lose their abilities when moved, the dossal regains its power once it has been securely hung for 24 hours. It has hardness 30, 100 hit points and regains 5 hit points each round.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *commune*, *hollow*, *order's wrath*, *summon monster I*, creator must worship the dead god; Cost 25,000 gp.

## Strategist's Warhammer

*Aura* moderate conjuration and transmutation; *Slot* none; *Price* 58,312 gp; *Weight* 5 lbs.
*Alignment* lawful neutral; *Senses* 120 ft.; *Int* 12, *Wis* 14, *Cha* 10; *Ego* 14; *Language* empathy

This +1 *defiant* *transformative* warhammer houses the mind of a cunning schemer and brilliant military tactician. It has a deep sense of patriotism for its homeland and seeks to help its owner rise through that nation's military ranks. It never willingly reveals its name until it feels its job as a military tutor is nearly complete. It enjoys hearing military strategies and empathically communicates its feelings on tactics to its owner. It evaluates the owner's underlings and despises incompetent or untrustworthy comrades, generals and commanders, urging the owner to dismiss them. With a well-meaning but tactically inept owner, it tries to assert its ego during military matters.

The weapon has a +12 bonus on Profession (soldier) checks and a +7 bonus on Sense Motive checks. It can cast *magic aura* on itself at will (Will DC 13 disbelieves) and *teleport* itself once per day. It can read and understand Common and one regional language. Owners who think too highly of their homeland's rivals, or who argue with the weapon often, find it missing at critical moments: it uses *transformative*, *magic aura* and *teleport* to evade service to unsuitable owners and to leave its current owner when it feels its purpose is complete.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *magic aura*, *major creation*, *summon monster I*, *teleport*, creator must have 10 ranks in Profession (soldier) and 5 ranks in Sense Motive; Cost 25,562 gp.

## Thornlord's Plate

*Aura* moderate enchantment and necromancy; *Slot* armor; *Price* 60,500 gp; *Weight* 50 lbs.

This suit of +4 *bolstering* *grinding* adamantine full plate bristles with long thorns and barbs along the edges of its heavy plates. The thorns come from the *grinding* special ability and also act as +1 *cruel* armor spikes. ## Eternal Egg

*Aura* strong enchantment and transmutation; *Slot* none; *Price* 240,000 gp; *Weight* 100 lbs.

Intricate runes cover this large gleaming ovoid case, and humming gold wires connect it to a circle inscribed into the floor. It is an arcane engine that draws on the spell-like abilities of the guardian creature's egg trapped within to power a beacon that calls across the dimensions to a particular lost archmage, trying to draw him home.

Once per day the user can activate the device to teleport herself, the Eternal Egg and up to 8 willing creatures holding hands to or from the maker's private extradimensional retreat (a permanent demiplane, as *lesser create demiplane*). This functions as *plane shift*. When teleporting out of the retreat, the item and its passengers return to a location 5 to 500 miles (determined randomly) from the last place the Eternal Egg occupied on the Material Plane.

If the lost archmage ever answers the beacon, the egg can transport the user and anyone standing in the 10-foot-radius magic circle inscribed on the floor to his location. Twenty-four hours later the user and companions can follow the beacon back. This use exhausts the Eternal Egg's powers.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *plane shift*, *temporal stasis*; Cost 120,000 gp.

## Demonbane Axe

*Aura* moderate conjuration and evocation; *Slot* none; *Price* 28,715 gp; *Weight* 6 lbs.
*Alignment* lawful good; *Senses* 60 ft.; *Int* 10, *Wis* 14, *Cha* 12; *Ego* 10; *Language* empathy

This well-made +1 battleaxe has the *bane* ability against demons and constantly emits a susurrus of faint, overlapping voices that swells to a cacophony of angry cries in the presence of demons. The voices are individually unintelligible; the axe communicates by empathy because its intelligence is a collection of several dozen essences rather than one personality. It has the destruction of demons as its special purpose and the dedicated power to detect demons within 60 feet, even when hidden or in disguise. Three times per day it can cast *bless weapon* on itself, and three times per day *lesser restoration* on its wielder. If its bearer dawdles in taking the fight to demonkind, its muttering grows into surly grumbles; after weeks without demonic activity, it tries to assert control or find a more suitable wielder.

*Construction* Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *bless weapon*, *lesser restoration*, *summon monster I*; Cost 14,357 gp.

## Genie Seal

*Aura* strong abjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 56,000 gp; *Weight* —

This magical metal imprint must be crafted onto a receptacle (a bottle, lamp or similar) that holds a genie via the minimus containment effect of a *binding* spell. *Binding* usually has an ending trigger condition set by the binder, such as services the genie must perform.

When applied to a receptacle holding a bound genie, the seal gives the genie a measure of freedom in exchange for a greater demand from the binder. The genie can freely leave and return to its receptacle whenever it is not physically sealed. It cannot move the receptacle on its own and is held within 300 feet of it by indestructible golden chains of force. While crafting the seal, the crafter can edit the ending trigger condition of the *binding* spell. The new condition must be a task the genie could feasibly accomplish (a genie's near-immortal lifespan allows for far-reaching demands). Each year the genie fails to complete the task, it must grant one wish to the binder if able. A genie under the seal cannot otherwise grant wishes until its task is complete.

If the genie is restrained or prevented from working on its task, there is a cumulative 1% chance each round that it goes berserk because it cannot engage in its all-consuming task. A creature actively working against the task adds 1% each round it remains within the genie's sight. A berserk genie attacks any creature within 300 feet of its receptacle; when it has no further targets it leaves the berserk state after 1 minute and returns to its task, resetting the chance to 0%.

Only one genie seal can be crafted onto a container; a second seal immediately frees the genie. If a seal is destroyed or removed, a new one can be crafted onto the receptacle.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *binding*, *geas/quest*, creator must have personally bound the genie in the receptacle; Cost 28,000 gp.

## Ghoul Stone

*Aura* faint transmutation; *Slot* neck; *Price* 5,400 gp; *Weight* 1 lb.

This bronze amulet set with mica is perpetually bloodied no matter how it is cleaned. Once per day, if the wearer eats a pound of flesh from a creature of its own race, the amulet grants a +2 enhancement bonus to Strength for 24 hours. If the wearer goes 24 hours without eating such flesh, the bonus ends and he takes 2 points of Strength damage, and 2 additional points for every further day without a meal. This damage can be healed normally. Eating such flesh again temporarily negates the Strength damage and restores the bonus, but all damage not yet healed returns if the amulet is removed or a cannibalistic meal is missed. Removing the amulet stops further damage but does not undo previous damage. A creature reduced to 0 Strength by the amulet dies and rises 24 hours later as a ghoul (10% chance of a ghast).

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *undead anatomy I*; Cost 2,700 gp.

## Golden Carriage

*Aura* strong conjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 114,000 gp; *Weight* 1,200 lbs.

A vehicle fit for royalty, larger than an ordinary carriage, seating six inside plus two drivers and a footman outside. The interior is lavish and magically maintained: occupants are protected from extreme heat and cold as if under *endure elements*, and are treated as acclimated for altitudes up to 15,000 feet. Both doors are secured with *alarm* and *arcane lock*, and an invisible driver can handle the reins as an *unseen servant* if no living driver is at the reins.

Four spectral gray horses pull the carriage tirelessly over nearly any terrain. They function as *phantom steed* cast by a 12th-level caster, and grant their *air walk*, *water walk* and other abilities to the carriage.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *phantom steed*, *secure shelter*; Cost 57,000 gp.

## Iron Road

*Aura* strong conjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 50,000 gp

This spherical iron talisman is etched with pictographs of chains winding in and out of mouths and eyes. It is a melancholic talisman: a magic item containing a bound hellmouth devil (a portal to Hell crafted from a devil). Once per day the wearer can activate it to summon the hellmouth, which takes the form of an eyeless, amphisbaenic underworld dragon with corroded iron scales. Each of its two mouths forms a 10-foot portal to Hell: one to the first layer, the other to a city of infernal commerce on the second. Only one portal can be used per activation, and it remains open for 10 minutes (or until the wearer dismisses it), functioning as the spell *gate*. The portal cannot connect anywhere else. If the wearer passes through and lingers in Hell for more than 10 minutes, the hellmouth closes behind him and traps him there. The hellmouth is only a portal, not an independent creature with its own desires.

The mouth to the second layer always opens at an infernal auction house run by a contract devil, who acts as a fence for stolen goods and will search the markets for rare treasures for a commission (typically 10% of the item's cost), usually taking at least a day. For more immediate boons she will tailor an infernal contract. The other mouth opens outside a fortress of the first layer commanded by an ambitious horned devil, who sells the services of his garrison as mercenaries at a fair price, as though the wearer had cast *planar ally*; for particularly glorious assignments he may halve or waive his price. Devils delight in contracts whose small print traps clients in Hell until they agree to altered terms.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *gate*, *greater planar binding*, ability to conjure and bind a greater devil; Cost 25,000 gp.

## Kiss of the Succubus

*Aura* strong conjuration (evil); *Slot* none; *Price* 56,000 gp; *Weight* —

This flap of leathery skin was flayed from the face of a succubus assassin. To attach the graft, the new user must make several lacerations around his own mouth, cheeks and chin, dealing 1d6 points of Constitution damage to himself. The skin is placed over the area and the user's blood revivifies the flesh, fusing it to his face. His face looks the same as before except that his lips constantly appear plump and lush.

Whenever the wearer kisses another creature he imposes 1 negative level on it (an unwilling victim must be grappled first). The negative level can be removed with a successful DC 16 Fortitude save after 24 hours. A creature that already has a negative level from the Kiss takes no additional negative levels from later kisses. The wearer can also communicate telepathically with any creature he has kissed in the past 24 hours, regardless of distance or planes; the wearer can end the link at any time, and an unwilling creature can prevent it with a Will save (DC 10 + the wearer's Charisma modifier).

The Kiss can be removed by cutting the skin away, which deals a further 1d6 points of Constitution damage and leaves the face permanently scarred.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *enervation*, *regenerate*, creator must have a Charisma score of at least 18; Cost 28,000 gp.

## Guardian Doll

*Aura* moderate transmutation; *Slot* none; *Price* 28,500 gp; *Weight* 2 lbs.
*Alignment* neutral; *Senses* 60 ft., blindsense; *Int* 10, *Wis* 16, *Cha* 12; *Ego* 10; *Language* empathy

This well-worn rag doll is 18 inches tall, with floppy limbs and fine clothing patched in several places. Pressing the left button-eye makes the doll preternaturally rigid and fixes it in space as an *immovable rod*, with hardness 20 while in this state. It can hold this state for 1 minute before returning to plush; pressing the right eye returns it to its original state immediately. Pressing either eye is a move action.

The doll has the protection of children as its special purpose and prioritizes protecting its owner if the owner is a child. It does not impose a negative level on an owner who is a child, regardless of the child's alignment. It is content to lie inert most of the time, watching its owner's surroundings for threats (Perception +13), and rarely tries to communicate other than to encourage feelings of calm and safety. If danger presents itself, it can move up to 10 feet under its own power, become immobile as above, or take the aid another action to grant its owner an AC bonus. Once per day it can act as if it had the In Harm's Way feat. Aware that its immobility is temporary, it empathically urges its owner to escape while it holds the menace at bay. It can move under its own power only to block a perceived threat.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *shield other*, *telekinesis*; Cost 14,250 gp.

## Lorestone

*Aura* moderate divination; *Slot* none; *Price* varies; *Weight* 15 lbs.

Weathered carvings of constellations, suns and planets cover the surface of this ancient melon-sized stone sphere. A lorestone is a dense encyclopedia of natural lore carved in stone. Its surface shows constellations and celestial markers; creatures who study it and succeed at a DC 20 Knowledge (nature) check notice inconsistencies in the sky map and realize it conceals weather patterns and seasonal changes like a visual almanac. A reader who identifies the hidden information can attempt a DC 25 Knowledge (nature) check to spot omissions and emphases that point to further concealed information. A lorestone may have several successive layers of knowledge (plant reproduction, aquatic landscapes, druidic rituals and so on), but the Knowledge (nature) DC to unlock each subsequent layer increases by 5.

A successful check of DC 30 or higher (set by each stone) lets a reader who holds the stone identify a unique path along the sphere. By tracing it with a finger she harmonizes with the stone. After 24 hours of harmonization the stone grants her special benefits, which last until a new creature harmonizes with the stone (ending all previous harmonizations). Reharmonizing requires possessing the stone and succeeding at the check again, with a +5 bonus for familiarity.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *commune with nature*, creator must speak Druidic and have 15 ranks in Knowledge (nature); Cost varies.

Example lorestones:

- **Granite Sphere** (90,000 gp): its cosmological layer emphasizes the moon; deeper layers cover forest animals, secret tunnels, lycanthropes and shapechangers, and rituals that force a creature's inner beast to the surface. A harmonized possessor (DC 40) can use *moonstruck* at will (DC 16).
- **Grave Sphere** (14,000 gp): a clammy obsidian sphere whose layers detail rot, decay, final resting places of ancient druids and rituals to exhume the ancient dead. A harmonized possessor (DC 35) can use *expeditious excavation* five times per day. If she is evil, she can also use *animate dead* once per day on a Medium or smaller humanoid; after 24 hours the creature collapses into carrion that cannot be animated again.
- **Piscine Stone** (17,500 gp): a constantly damp gray stone whose deeper levels cover aquatic biology and rituals to thwart aquatic aberrations. A harmonized possessor (DC 30) can breathe underwater and use *detect aberration* at will.

## Mandala Vial

*Aura* moderate conjuration and enchantment; *Slot* none; *Price* 25,000 gp

This crystal vial holds a small amount of silver and gold sand and is stoppered with an elaborate silver cap bearing a rebus. When the user opens it while chanting prayers to the master of masters, the sand pours out into an elaborate multicolored mandala. The vial never runs empty, but pours enough sand for only one mandala at a time.

These ki points are temporary and vanish if the user rests or meditates for 8 hours to recover; the vial cannot restore ki beyond the owner's total ki pool. Once per week, on speaking a command word, the owner can pour out the contents, which coalesce into a guardian spirit marked with the rebus that fights beside the owner and protects her to the best of its ability.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *calm emotions*, *owl's wisdom*, *summon monster IV*, creator must have the ki pool class feature; Cost 12,500 gp.

## Mistcaller

*Aura* moderate conjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 16,000 gp; *Weight* 1 lb.

Carved from a mammoth tusk, this ivory flute is covered with delicate carvings of wind blowing over waves inlaid with silver. It is a masterwork musical instrument, but its notes are pitched much lower than most flutes and any tune carries a mournful quality.

Once per day the user can attempt a DC 15 Perform (wind) check to summon a bank of spectral fog. This functions as *obscuring mist* centered on the user, except that it moves with the user and lasts as long as the user continues to play, to a maximum of 5 minutes. Spectral faces appear to anyone in the mist other than the user, whispering for them to lay down their burdens and rest. Anyone within the mist other than the user can choose to fall asleep as a free action. Creatures asleep within the fog heal 1 hit point per minute spent sleeping and recover 1 point of ability damage if they sleep at least 1 full minute. Sleepers can be awakened normally.

*Construction* Craft Wondrous Item, *fog cloud*, *restoration*; Cost 8,000 gp.

## Shadow Mirror

*Aura* strong conjuration; *Slot* none; *Price* 126,000 gp; *Weight* 5 lbs.

This thin sheet of opaque black stone is polished to a reflective sheen, but its center drinks all light, becoming a pool of total darkness. Exact powers vary between mirrors; this is a typical example.

Three times per day it can summon a creature from the Plane of Shadow. Each attempt requires a successful DC 15 Will save. On a failed save the user takes 1d4 points of Strength damage and must attempt another save on her next turn. On a success, a shadow is summoned (as *summon monster IV*, except it can summon only a single shadow, which remains for 1 hour or until destroyed). If the user took Strength damage during the attempt, the shadow remains for 1 additional hour per point of Strength lost (restored Strength no longer adds to the duration).
