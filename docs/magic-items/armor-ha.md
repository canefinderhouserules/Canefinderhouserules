# Horror Adventures Armor

The following section includes armor with unusual powers and magical abilities. Magic armor can have either abilities that need to be activated to function or powers that work passively and constantly.

If you’re running a horror adventure or just want to add a splash of horror to the treasure you roll, consider using the magic items on the Horror Adventures pages before rolling randomly on the treasure generator, or add these items to the treasure generator.

### Lupine Rageskin

**Aura** faint transmutation; **CL** 5th  
**Slot** armor; **Price** 12,160 gp; **Weight** 15 lbs.

This +1 leather armor consists of wolf skins sewn together with sinew. When the wearer rages, he automatically turns into a Medium wolf, as if using change shape (*beast shape I*). The armor melds with his new form as normal, but the armor’s enhancement bonus changes into a +1 enhancement bonus to her natural armor. When the rage ends, the wearer reverts to his own form.

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *beast shape I*; **Cost** 6,160 gp

### Ursine Rageskin

**Aura** moderate transmutation; **CL** 7th  
**Slot** armor; **Price** 20,165 gp; **Weight** 25 lbs.

This +1 hide armor is stitched together from grizzly bear hides. When the wearer rages, she automatically turns into a Large brown bear, as if using change shape (*beast shape II*). The armor melds with her new form as normal, but the armor’s enhancement bonus changes into a +1 enhancement bonus to her natural armor. When the rage ends, the wearer reverts to her own form.

**Requirements** Craft Magic Arms and Armor, *beast shape II*; **Cost** 10,165 gp
