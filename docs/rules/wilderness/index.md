# Wilderness Rules

Rules for exploring, surviving in and mastering the wilds, from *Pathfinder Roleplaying Game: Ultimate Wilderness*. Companion rules for herbs, poisons, weather and wilderness hazards sit alongside the exploration system.

- [Aquatic Rules](aquatic-rules.md): swimming, buoyancy, underwater combat, drowning, pressure, currents and whirlpools (from *Aquatic Adventures*).
- [Aquatic Archetypes, Gear, Feats and Spells](aquatic-options.md): underwater class options, gear, feats and spells.
- [Discovery and Exploration](discovery-and-exploration.md): Discovery Points, exploration checks, way signs and creating territories.
- [The First World](first-world.md): the fey realm, its planar traits, hazards and echoes.
- [Foraging and Salvaging](foraging-and-salvaging.md): gathering and recycling supplies in the wild.
- [The Green Faith](green-faith.md): the druidic philosophy, its orders and archetypes.
- [Harvesting Poisons](harvesting-poisons.md): gathering venom, antivenom and natural poisons.
- [Hazards and Disasters](hazards-and-disasters.md): natural hazards, disasters and their damage tables.
- [Herbalism](herbalism.md): gathering, preparing and using herbs.
- [Spells of the Wild](spells-of-the-wild.md): advice on spells for wilderness play.
- [Trophies and Treasures](trophies-and-treasures.md): harvesting, valuing and using creature trophies.
- [Weather](weather.md): baselines, temperature, precipitation, wind and severe events.
- [Wilderness Traps](wilderness-traps.md): ten simple traps built from natural materials.
