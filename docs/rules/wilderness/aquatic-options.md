# Aquatic Archetypes, Gear, Feats and Spells

!!! note "Source"
    Options from *Pathfinder Campaign Setting: Aquatic Adventures*, for use with the [Aquatic Rules](aquatic-rules.md). Deity-specific feats and setting-specific lore have been omitted. Magic items are in [Aquatic Items](../../magic-items/aquatic-items.md).

## Archetypes and Class Options

Other archetypes suited to aquatic campaigns: aquatic druid, sea singer bard, sea witch, merfolk wave warden ranger, undine adept druid and undine watersinger bard.

### Aquachymist (Alchemist Archetype)

**Class Skills.** Swim instead of Fly.

**Sureseal Alchemy (Su).** Extracts and mutagens self-generate a flexible outer shell of sureseal (see Sureseal bladder below), keeping the supply waterproof. *Alters alchemy.*

**Underwater Bombs (Su).** Fire bombs deal steam damage, working underwater or on land. When creating a bomb the aquachymist weights it or includes excess air, giving it swiftly rising or swiftly sinking buoyancy and allowing him to target foes directly above or below. A missed bomb deals no splash damage but continues up or down until it loses potency at the end of his turn (a swiftly sinking bomb close to the bottom may explode there). Above water the range increment is only 5 feet. *Alters bombs.*

**Amphibious Mutagen (Su).** At 2nd level his mutagen also lets him breathe air and water for its duration. *Replaces the 2nd-level discovery.*

### Aquakineticist (Kineticist Archetype)

**Aquatic Focus.** Must choose water as primary element and both expanded elements, and water blast as first simple blast. *Alters expanded element.*

**Basic Aquakinesis (Sp).** As basic hydrokinesis (mild currents), and as a standard action she can set her buoyancy to any level, lasting until she uses it again. *Replaces basic hydrokinesis, alters elemental focus.*

**Call of the Deep.** At 2nd level gains *waterdancer* as a bonus utility talent. At 6th level gains *watersense*, and while waterdancer is active can ignore currents up to 60 feet. *Replaces the 2nd- and 6th-level utility talents.*

**Ocean's Caress (Su).** At 2nd level she breathes underwater and gains *cold adaptation* as a bonus utility talent, adding half her kineticist level to its cold resistance. Accepting 1 burn adds 3 more cold resistance; at 40 or higher she gains cold immunity. This resistance and immunity also apply to pressure damage, and she adjusts to a new pressure all at once after five Fortitude saves. Whenever she accepts burn using a water talent, her cold resistance doubles for 1 round. She can dismiss or restore the effect as an immediate action. *Replaces the defense talent from elemental defense.*

**Ice Propagation (Su).** At 7th level, her cold blast underwater (which freezes water and deals half piercing damage) can be a physical blast to which she can apply any form or substance infusion usable with ice composite blasts; it still deals damage as a simple physical blast. *Alters cold simple blast.*

### Aquanaut (Fighter Archetype)

**Armor Proficiency.** Light and medium armor, no shields.

**Piercing Focus.** A fighter bonus feat that normally requires choosing a single weapon (except weapon proficiency feats) can instead apply to all melee piercing weapons he is proficient with that meet the prerequisites; this counts as having the feat for each weapon for prerequisites and weapon mastery. *Alters bonus feats and weapon mastery.*

**Tidal Celerity.** At 2nd level, +1 on Reflex saves and saves against effects that would immobilize or paralyze, increasing by 1 every 4 levels beyond 2nd. *Replaces bravery.*

**Aquadynamic Paragon.** *Replaces armor training and armor mastery.*

- **3rd:** a swim speed equal to unmodified base land speed (a racial swim speed instead increases by 10 feet), and neutral buoyancy once per turn as a free action.
- **7th:** may choose any of the five buoyancy states once per turn as a free action, taking his buoyancy movement as part of it without becoming off-balance or needing a Swim check; may double or halve how far a current moves him.
- **11th:** when he moves, double moves, withdraws or runs underwater, he can leave a current (speed 10 feet in his movement direction) in the squares along his path, lasting until his next turn.
- **15th:** as a full-round action he spins to create a whirlpool, dragging all creatures within 120 feet 30 feet closer (creatures within 30 feet circle around him in a direction of his choice). It deals no damage, he is unaffected, and he can make attacks of opportunity against creatures moving in it.
- **19th:** DR 5/— while underwater.

**Underwater Training.** At 5th level choose bludgeoning, slashing or piercing melee weapons. Piercing: +1 on attack and damage rolls with them underwater. Bludgeoning or slashing: ignore the usual underwater penalties. At 9th and 13th level he chooses another type (and gains +1 attack and damage with the earlier types); at 17th level +1 more with all three. *Replaces weapon training.*

### Aquatic (Bloodrager Bloodline)

**Bonus Feats:** Aquadynamic Focus, Dodge, Lightning Reflexes, Mobility, Skill Focus (Fly), Steam Spell, Toughness.

**Bonus Spells:** *hydraulic push* (7th), *slipstream* (10th), *hydraulic torrent* (13th), *control water* (16th).

- **Underwater Attacks (Su, 1st):** bludgeoning and slashing melee attacks underwater ignore the usual penalties.
- **Aquatic Adaptation (Ex, 4th):** swim speed 30 feet and breathe water and air. 8th: cold resistance 5. 12th: swim speed 60 feet, and the 4th-level benefits are constant, even outside bloodrage.
- **Watersense (Ex, 8th):** tremorsense 30 feet in water; 60 feet at 12th.
- **Wavedarter (Sp, Su, 12th):** while underwater, reach +5 feet and the effects of *haste*.
- **Currentcaller (Su, 16th):** three times per day underwater, as a swift action at the start of your turn, call up a current (speed up to 20 feet, direction of your choice) in your space, moving with you until the end of your turn.
- **Deep Fury (Su, 20th):** tremorsense 120 feet in water, evasion, and immunity to cold and pressure damage, constantly.

### Deep Shaman (Shaman Archetype)

**Deep Spirit.** Must choose the waves spirit and an aquatic-subtype spirit animal, which gains amphibious instead of breathing water. Swim is a class skill.

**Aquatic Hexes (Su).** Beckoning chill on a creature in water makes it fatigued from hypothermia for the duration, and entangled if it takes damage from her spirit abilities or from water-descriptor spells. Her spirit abilities that deal damage count as water-descriptor spells for *crashing waves*. Instead of *mist's shroud* and *watersight*, she can select:

- **Buoyancy Control (Sp).** Touch a willing creature to change its buoyancy to any of the five states for 1 minute (once per 24 hours per creature). She has neutral buoyancy regardless, and can end or resume this as a standard action.
- **Deep Heart (Su).** Breathe underwater and swim speed equal to base land speed. At 8th level she takes half pressure damage and adjusts to a new pressure all at once after five saves.

**Aquatic Spirit Abilities (Su).** Underwater, *wave strike* deals lethal bludgeoning damage and pushes the target 5 feet + 5 feet per 6 levels. At 11th level, underwater all weapons she holds have *impact* instead of *quenching*. Instead of a swim speed and water breathing, *fluid mastery* grants tremorsense 30 feet in water, and underwater its torrent deals 1d6 per shaman level in a 30-foot cone.

**Brine Dragon Form (Su).** The true waves spirit lets her assume the form of a Large or smaller brine dragon (*form of the dragon II*) with an acid line breath weapon, acid resistance 20 and swim speed 60 feet, for 1 hour per level per day in 1-hour increments (breath weapon limit applies across the day). Underwater time doesn't count against the limit, but each minute spent flying expends 1 hour. At 18th level, Huge or smaller as *form of the dragon III*. *Replaces the elemental form of the true spirit.*

### Drowned Channeler (Spiritualist Archetype)

**Class Skills.** Swim instead of Fly.

**Drowned Phantom.** The phantom has a swim speed of 30 feet in both forms and the water subtype, so its slam attacks take no underwater penalty. The etheric tether extends twice as far underwater but half as far above water. The phantom grants no benefits while in the channeler's consciousness unless she is within 25 feet of a large body of water. Both bonded manifestation forms grant the channeler the swim speed, and ectoplasmic tendril attacks take no bludgeoning penalty underwater. *Alters phantom, shared consciousness, etheric tether and bonded manifestation.*

**Drowned Spells (2nd).** Adds *control water*, *fluid form*, *hydraulic push*, *hydraulic torrent*, *life current*, *pressure adaptation*, *ride the waves*, *slipstream*, *stabilize pressure*, *unlife current*, *water breathing* and *wave form* to her spell list at the lowest level they appear on the cleric or sorcerer/wizard list; she must still select them as spells known. *Replaces bonded senses.*

**Drowned Powers (Sp).** 5th: *hydraulic push* 1/day (+1/day at 9th and every 4 levels). 7th: *slipstream* 1/day (+1/day at 11th and every 4 levels). 9th: *ride the waves* 1/day. 16th: *fluid form* 1/day. *Replaces detect undead, calm spirit, see invisibility and call spirit.*

### Oceanrider (Cavalier Archetype)

**Armor Proficiency.** Light and medium armor and shields (not tower shields).

**Aquatic Mount.** A Medium oceanrider can ride a seahorse or an orca (Large from the start, without level 7 abilities until level 7); a Small one can ride a dolphin. *Alters mount, replaces expert trainer.*

### Order of the Waves (Cavalier Order)

**Edicts.** Perpetually seek and explore new places and secrets under the sea, stopping only to protect newfound treasures and people from undersea threats.

**Challenge.** +1 morale bonus on saves while underwater, increasing by 1 every 4 levels.

**Skills.** Adds Knowledge (geography) and Perception; can use Knowledge (geography) untrained, and adds half his level (minimum 1) to checks to find an underwater location.

- **Waverider (Ex, 2nd):** swim speed equal to unmodified base land speed (+10 feet if he already has one; if both he and his mount have one, the mount's increases by 10 feet too). +1 morale bonus on damage rolls underwater.
- **Current's Rush (Ex, 8th):** +2 on attack rolls when charging in the same direction as a current pushing him, and +1 damage per 10 feet of the current's speed (maximum +6).
- **Explore the Seas (Ex, 15th):** gains Pressure Adept as a bonus feat and adds two oceanic zones to his native range instead of one. When exploring a new underwater location, +2 morale bonus on initiative and Perception, and he can always act in the surprise round.

### Pearl Seeker (Paladin Archetype)

Some paladins are called by empathic visions and dreams that beckon them to the sea to right aquatic injustice.

**Armor Proficiency and Class Skills.** Light and medium armor, no shields; Swim is a class skill.

**Seek Impressions (Ex, Sp).** Gains Psychic Sensitivity as a bonus feat and can use *detect psychic significance* at will. *Replaces detect evil.*

**Aquatic Domain (Su).** At 3rd level, the 1st-level domain power of the Aquatic terrain domain (paladin level as druid level); at 8th level the 6th-level power. *Replaces aura of courage and aura of resolve.*

**Vision Magic.** Casts divine spells spontaneously, using the bloodrager's spells known and per day progression. Bonus spells known: *slipstream* (7th), *ride the waves* (10th), *fluid form* (13th), *sea mantle* (16th). Adds *hydraulic push* (1st) and *hydraulic torrent* (3rd) to her list.

**Divine Hippocampus (5th).** Her divine bond must be a hippocampus: Size Large; Speed 5 ft., swim 80 ft.; AC +4 natural armor; Attack bite (1d6), tail slap (1d4, secondary); Str 18, Dex 13, Con 15, Int 2, Wis 12, Cha 11; SQ darkvision 60 ft., low-light vision, scent, water dependency. *Alters divine bond, replaces channel positive energy.*

### Pelagic Hunter (Hunter Archetype)

**Pelagic Companion.** Must select an aquatic animal companion.

**Pelagic Focus (Su).** Instead of animal focus, the hunter contacts magical deep sea creatures. Each option gives the following benefits:

| Aspect | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Aboleth | +1 morale bonus on saves against divine spells and spell-like abilities (+2 at 8th, +3 at 15th) |
| Cetus | Lunge as a bonus feat; at 12th level instead +5 feet reach |
| Charybdis | Attacks ignore the first 5 points of hardness (7 at 8th, 10 at 15th) |
| Deep One | Reduces pressure damage by 10; immune to changing-pressure damage at 12th |
| Hippocampus | +4 competence bonus on Swim (+6 at 8th, +8 at 15th) |
| Kraken | Jet ability with speed equal to the higher of base land speed or four times swim speed; five times at 8th, seven times at 15th |
| Reefclaw | Diehard as a bonus feat; at 12th level instead ferocity |
| Scylla | Attacks deal 1 bleed damage (1d3 at 8th, 1d6 at 15th) |
| Sea Serpent | Under a *nondetection* spell, caster level equal to the hunter's class level |
| Selkie | +4 competence bonus on Bluff (+6 at 8th, +8 at 15th) |
| Shoggoth | +2 on Perception and flanking bonuses against it reduced by 1 (Perception +3 at 8th, +4 at 15th); can't be flanked at 12th |
| Siyokoy | Attacks deal 1 extra electricity damage (1d3 at 8th, 1d6 at 15th) |

**Blood to Water (Su, 5th).** When the hunter or her companion bleeds into water, the blood becomes ordinary water within 1 round, so predators with keen scent aren't drawn and tracking is harder. *Replaces woodland stride.*

### Tidal Trickster (Rogue Archetype)

**Wisdom of the Waves (Ex).** Swim speed equal to unmodified base land speed (+10 feet if she has a racial one). Instead of the +8 bonus, she gains a racial bonus on Swim equal to 4 + half her rogue level (or her better racial bonus). Bluff bonus equal to half her rogue level. At 3rd level +1 on Will saves while underwater, increasing by 1 at 6th and every 3 levels thereafter. *Replaces trapfinding and trap sense.*

**Liquid Disruption (Ex, 4th).** Can sneak attack oozes and water-subtype creatures normally immune to precision damage, and ignores a hydrokineticist's elemental overflow chance to negate precision damage. *Alters sneak attack, replaces uncanny dodge and the 4th-level talent.*

**Tidal Distortion (Ex, 8th).** While making a full attack underwater, she can give up an attack to move up to 10 feet. If the movement includes a square adjacent to foes, she can choose one and make a Bluff check (DC 11 + the foe's Sense Motive or Swim modifier, whichever is higher), with any penalty the sacrificed attack would have had (–5/–10/–15 for the second to fourth iterative attacks). On a success the movement doesn't provoke from that foe and the foe is off-balance until its next turn. She can give up any number of attacks but not two consecutive ones. *Replaces improved uncanny dodge and the 8th-level talent.*

### Underwater (Ranger Combat Style)

When gaining a combat style feat, the ranger may choose Aquadynamic Focus, Aquadynamic Shot, Dolphin Style or Shark Style. At 6th level he adds Dolphin Dart and Shark Tear; at 10th level Dolphin Circle and Shark Leap. He ignores prerequisites other than the style feat.

## Underwater Gear

| Item | Price | Weight | Craft DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air tank (gallon) | 5 sp | ½ lb. | — |
| Air tank, pressurized | 25 gp | 30 lbs. | — |
| Bottom-walker's anchor | 5 gp | 50 lbs. | — |
| Hippocampus, combat-trained | 750 gp | 1,300 lbs. | — |
| Hippocampus, common | 500 gp | 1,000 lbs. | — |
| Jellyfish, luminous | 2 gp | 1 lb. | — |
| Masterwork flippers | 50 gp | 1 lb. | — |
| Poison sealant | 20 gp | — | 20 |
| Pressure pill | 75 gp | — | 25 |
| Scent salts | 1 sp | — | — |
| Sureseal bladder, mundane | 1 sp | 1 lb. | — |
| Sureseal bladder, potion | 8 gp | — | — |
| Underwater goggles | 5 gp | — | — |
| Wet suit | 8 gp | 4 lbs. | — |

**Air tank.** A large air tank is excellent for achieving swiftly rising buoyancy, and can be adjusted to give neutral buoyancy. Every 6 gallons of air lasts roughly 1 minute underwater.

**Air tank, pressurized.** A pressurized tank stores enough air to breathe for 1 hour (the air weighs 7 pounds; an empty tank weighs 23). A small crank releases a breath as a standard action, breathed through a tube with a semipermeable end that lets air out but not water in. Refilling takes 5 gp of materials and a DC 20 Craft (alchemy) check (failure destroys the tank) or a kineticist with basic aerokinesis.

**Bottom-walker's anchor.** A dense anchor on a detachable chain (removal is a move action) that all but guarantees swiftly sinking buoyancy. It increases the armor check penalty by 1 and lowers the maximum Dexterity bonus by 1 (or sets it at +6 if there was none).

**Hippocampus.** Roughly as easy to train as horses. Even combat-trained ones rarely wear barding heavier than leather, which interferes with swimming.

**Jellyfish, luminous.** A 4-inch jellyfish that glows as bright as a candle. Kept in a container with holes for plankton it serves as a living lantern; it lives up to 1 year in this stage.

**Masterwork flippers.** +2 circumstance bonus on Swim checks to perform complex maneuvers underwater or swim through rough waters; doesn't stack with the racial bonus from a swim speed.

**Poison sealant.** Applied poison washes off underwater in 1d10 rounds unless inside the weapon. Applying the sealant takes the same effort as applying a poison; it keeps the weapon poisoned underwater. It dissolves in blood, so it doesn't prevent accidental poisoning.

**Pressure pill.** Swallowed as a full-round action, it instantly adjusts you to the surrounding pressure, but you take 3 nonlethal damage per 100 feet of adjustment.

**Scent salts.** Dissolve slowly, scenting the nearby water for about 1 hour.

**Sureseal bladder.** A thin membrane that lets you fill and drink liquids underwater without seawater entering. The mundane bladder holds about 2 cups. The potion bladder is pressurized to expel an entire potion, drunk as a standard action like any potion. Each can be used only once.

**Underwater goggles.** Protect a land-dweller's eyes; the wearer can use visual Perception like an aquatic native.

**Wet suit.** Designed for surface dwellers exploring the deeps, with waterproof pockets.

|---|---|---|

| **Aquadynamic Shot** (Combat) | Far Shot, Point-Blank Shot, base attack bonus +4 | Projectile attacks take –1 per 5 feet of water instead of –2. Thrown weapons still don't work well. |
| **Child of Two Worlds** (Story) | One parent aquatic, the other a land-dweller; 1st level only | See below. |
| **Deep Breath** | Con 15 | Hold your breath four times your Constitution score in rounds instead of two. |
| **Dolphin Style** (Combat, Style) | Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 3 ranks, BAB +3 or monk 3rd | +1 on melee attack rolls against a creature adjacent to at least two of its allies if you aren't flanking it; your melee attacks ignore AC bonuses from aid another. |
| **Dolphin Dart** (Combat) | Dolphin Style, Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 6 ranks, BAB +6 or monk 6th | In Dolphin Style, as a standard action underwater, move up to half swim speed, make one melee attack against a creature within reach, then retreat to your previous position; no attacks of opportunity. |
| **Dolphin Circle** (Combat) | Dolphin Dart, Dolphin Style, Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 10 ranks, BAB +10 or monk 10th | See below. |
| **Master Swimmer** | Skill Focus (Swim), Swim 10 ranks | Swim speed of 30 feet or base land speed, whichever is slower; your racial bonus for a swim speed is +2 instead of +8. |
| **Murky Spell** (Metamagic) | — | See below. |
| **Pressure Adept** | Swim 5 ranks | Add one oceanic zone to your native range for pressure; surface dwellers must choose the sunlight zone, aquatic creatures a zone directly above or below their native range. |
| **Shark Style** (Combat, Style) | Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 3 ranks, BAB +3 or monk 3rd | Unarmed strikes can deal piercing or bludgeoning damage. Piercing unarmed strikes and bite attacks deal +1d6 bleed. |
| **Shark Tear** (Combat) | Shark Style, Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 6 ranks, BAB +6 or monk 6th | In Shark Style, scent in water against bleeding creatures only, and +1 on attack and damage rolls against bleeding creatures. |
| **Shark Leap** (Combat) | Shark Style, Shark Tear, Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 10 ranks, BAB +10 or monk 10th | See below. |
| **Steam Spell** (Metamagic) | — | A spell with the fire descriptor works underwater without a caster level check, but needs a caster level check to function above water. Uses a slot of the spell's actual level. |

**Child of Two Worlds.** You can't have other feats, traits or abilities that specify your bloodline. *Benefit:* an aquatic creature gains a land speed of 10 feet; a non-aquatic creature gains +4 on Swim checks (doesn't stack with a swim speed's bonus). *Goal:* you must live among the race of your other parent, performing significant rites of passage and forming real bonds with its members until accepted. *Completion Benefit:* you gain the amphibious subtype (and aquatic if you lack it) and breathe water and air equally. As a swift action you can turn your feet into a tail fin (swim speed 40 feet) or your tail fin into feet (land speed 30 feet).

**Dolphin Circle.** In Dolphin Style, as a full-round action underwater, choose a circular path starting and ending in your space, no longer than one move action of swimming. You circle until your next turn without provoking attacks of opportunity. You count as being in every space along the path for threatening, flanking (for allies) and area effects; other creatures can attack you and touch you with beneficial touch spells as if you were in every space. You don't count as occupying them for moving through occupied spaces. At the start of your next turn, stop at any space along the path.

**Murky Spell.** Apply only to a mist or fog spell that doesn't normally work underwater (*cloudkill*, *fog cloud*, *mind fog*, *obscuring mist*, *solid fog*). Cast underwater, it clouds the water with sediment to the same effect as the normal spell, except the duration is one-tenth normal (generally 1 minute per level; *mind fog* 3 minutes), not affecting the duration of secondary effects. Abilities that see through mist and fog don't see through it unless they also see through murky water. A current of speed 10 feet or more disperses it in 1 round. No effect above water. Uses a slot of the spell's actual level.

**Shark Leap.** In Shark Style, underwater with neutral, rising or swiftly rising buoyancy, you can leap at opponents directly above you as a charge that moves only straight up and makes a single piercing unarmed strike or bite. A hit deals double damage; if the roll also exceeds 11 + the target's Swim modifier, it is off-balance until its next turn. Limited to a standard action (such as the surprise round), you move half as far and double only the damage dice (as Vital Strike).

## Aquatic Spells

See also *buoyancy* as an emergency spell granting several targets swiftly rising buoyancy.

### Aquatic Trail

**School** divination [water]; **Level** druid 3, inquisitor 3, ranger 2, shaman 3
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S
**Range** medium; **Area** circle centered on you, radius 100 ft. + 10 ft./level; **Duration** 1 hour/level
**Saving Throw** none; **Spell Resistance** no

You track creatures through the water as if across soft ground as long as they passed through the area less than 1 week ago. The area moves with you, and others can follow the trail as long as they move with you. Trails may be covered by those of other creatures in the busy upper zones; the spell is more useful in the deep zones.

### Extreme Buoyancy

**School** evocation; **Level** alchemist 2, bard 2, druid 2, medium 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, ranger 2, shaman 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a bag of air), DF
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** 10 minutes/level
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

The target's buoyancy becomes and stays swiftly rising regardless of how dense it and its gear are.

### Free Swim

**School** abjuration; **Level** alchemist 3, bard 3, cleric 3, druid 3, inquisitor 3, medium 3, mesmerist 3, occultist 3, psychic 3, ranger 3, spiritualist 3
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a strip of kelp bound to the target), DF
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** 10 minutes/level
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

The target moves and attacks underwater normally without the usual penalties, as if under *freedom of movement*, but gains no protection against grapple, paralysis and magical effects that impede movement (*solid fog*, *slow*, *web*).

### Invisibility Bubble

**School** illusion (glamer); **Level** alchemist 2, antipaladin 2, bard 2, inquisitor 2, magus 2, medium 2, mesmerist 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, sorcerer/wizard 2, spiritualist 2, summoner (unchained) 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a needle from a sea urchin)
**Range** touch; **Area** touched creature's space, up to one 5-foot cube; **Duration** 1 minute/level (D)
**Saving Throw** Will negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

A glamered bubble surrounds the target's space, making her completely invisible underwater by hiding both her and the water she displaces. Works only on Medium or smaller targets. Other objects and creatures within the space are invisible while they stay there, which might reveal the spell to an observer who notices fish disappearing into the bubble. Creatures inside can see each other, so foes who can pinpoint the bubble can stick their heads in to see the target. Any creature inside that takes an action that would break invisibility pops the bubble and ends the spell. On land the bubble is clearly visible. The inside blocks light from escaping, so light sources inside neither illuminate past the edge nor reveal a telltale glow.

**Giant** (bard 3, mesmerist 3, occultist 3, psychic 3, sorcerer/wizard 3, summoner (unchained) 3): area 10-foot-radius emanation centered on the touched creature's space, allowing a creature up to Gargantuan or more creatures.

**Mass** (mesmerist 6, psychic 7, sorcerer/wizard 7, summoner (unchained) 6): range long; any number of targets, no two more than 180 feet apart, affecting each creature's area regardless of size. The effect moves with the group and breaks when anyone attacks. Individuals in separate bubbles can't see each other. The spell is broken for any individual who moves more than 180 feet from the nearest member of the group.

### Lead Anchor

**School** evocation; **Level** alchemist 2, bard 2, druid 2, medium 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, ranger 2, shaman 2, witch 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a bag of air), DF
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** 10 minutes/level
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

The target's buoyancy becomes and stays swiftly sinking regardless of density. It can walk on the bottom, but needs a DC 20 Swim check to swim toward the surface and stay above the bottom. Useful for exploring the sea floor and for drowning an air-breathing foe.

### Life Current

**School** conjuration (healing) [water]; **Level** cleric 3, druid 3, paladin 3, ranger 3, shaman 3
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, DF
**Range** medium; **Area** current or portion of current up to 20 feet wide and 100 feet long; **Duration** 1 round
**Saving Throw** Will half; **Spell Resistance** yes

You infuse the current with positive energy. At the start of their turn when the current moves them, living creatures in the area heal 1d6 hit points per 10 feet of the current's speed (maximum 6d6). Undead take an equal amount of damage instead.

### Neutral Buoyancy

**School** evocation; **Level** alchemist 2, bard 2, druid 2, medium 2, occultist 2, psychic 2, ranger 2, shaman 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a bag of air), DF
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** 10 minutes/level
**Saving Throw** Will negates; **Spell Resistance** yes

The target's buoyancy becomes and stays neutral regardless of density.

### Pressure Adaptation

**School** abjuration; **Level** alchemist 3, bloodrager 3, cleric 3, druid 3, paladin 3, psychic 3, ranger 3, shaman 3, summoner (unchained) 3
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (slime from a cephalopod)
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** 1 hour/level
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

The target adjusts to 100 feet of new pressure (or the entire shift, for an instantaneous shift within its native range) after just one successful Fortitude save instead of five.

### Stabilize Pressure

**School** abjuration; **Level** alchemist 2, bloodrager 2, cleric 2, druid 2, paladin 2, psychic 2, ranger 2, shaman 2, summoner (unchained) 2
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a piece of shell)
**Range** touch; **Target** creature touched; **Duration** instantaneous
**Saving Throw** Fortitude negates (harmless); **Spell Resistance** yes (harmless)

The target instantly adjusts to its current pressure all at once. This doesn't help it readjust to future changes.

### Steam Ray Fusillade

**School** evocation [fire, water]; **Level** sorcerer/wizard 7
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S, M (a scraping from a hydrothermal vent)
**Range** personal; **Target** you; **Duration** 1 round/level

For the duration you can fire three rays of steam once per round as a standard action (and three as part of casting). They function like *scorching ray* (close range, ranged touch attacks, 4d6 fire damage each, spell resistance applies), but work underwater and above water without a caster level check for being a fire spell.

### Unlife Current

**School** conjuration (healing) [water]; **Level** antipaladin 3, cleric 3, druid 3, ranger 3, shaman 3

As *life current*, but with negative energy: it heals undead and harms living creatures.

### Wave Form

**School** transmutation [water]; **Level** bloodrager 4, cleric 4, druid 4, shaman 4, sorcerer/wizard 4
**Casting Time** 1 standard action; **Components** V, S
**Range** personal; **Duration** instantaneous; **Saving Throw** see text

You become a wave of frothing water up to 15 feet wide and 15 feet tall, as space permits, and surge in a straight line up to twice your base speed before returning to normal form. This movement doesn't provoke attacks of opportunity. While in wave form you are immune to weapon attacks and attacks that target a specific number of creatures. All creatures and unattended objects in your path take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and are knocked prone; a successful Reflex save prevents being knocked prone.
