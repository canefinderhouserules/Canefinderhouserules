# Horror Adventures

This section collects the rules from Horror Adventures. It covers new options and advice for players in a horror game, the corruption subsystem, expanded fear and sanity rules, and the rules for curses, diseases, environments, fleshwarping, haunts and madness.

This chapter contains new options and advice for players in a horror game. The majority of the chapter is focused on corruptions, a new subsystem that allows players to take on the role of a character slowly transforming into a monster and gaining strange new powers and drawbacks. Additionally, there are new racial abilities, more detailed and nuanced rules for fear, and a sanity system that tracks the stresses that weigh upon a character’s mind and the terrible scars they leave.
