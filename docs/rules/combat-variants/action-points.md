# Action Points

!!! note "Optional house rule"
    Action points (AP) are an optional way to run turns, close to the three-action turn of other games but adapted to these rules. Everything on the wiki keeps its normal action, and the AP cost appears next to it in another color, like this: standard action (1 AP). The table chooses whether to use it. The **AP** switch at the top of every page hides or shows these costs.

## The Turn

Each character has **3 AP** on its turn. Standard, move and swift actions stop being different kinds of action: each one costs **1 AP**, and you can spend your AP in any order and on anything (three attacks, three moves, a spell and two attacks…).

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Standard, move or swift action | 1 AP |
| Full-round action | 3 AP |
| Anything that takes "1 round" | 3 AP, and it happens on the same turn |
| Immediate action | 1 AP of your next turn |
| Free action | Free, as now |
| 5-foot step | Free, once per turn, if you did not otherwise move |

**Things that cost more or less than 1 AP:**

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Charge (move up to double your speed and attack once) | 2 AP |
| Pounce (charge and attack with everything you have) | 3 AP |
| Run (move four times your speed) | 3 AP |
| Withdraw | 3 AP |
| Coup de grace | 3 AP |
| Total defense | 2 AP |
| Aid another | 1 AP |
| Start a grapple | 2 AP |
| Pin a grappled foe | 2 AP |
| Maintain a grapple | 1 AP |
| Stand up from prone, draw a weapon, drink a potion, open a door, retrieve an item | 1 AP |
| Move and draw a weapon (with a base attack bonus of +1 or more) | 1 AP together |
| Ready an action | What the readied action costs |
| Delay | Nothing |
| Fight defensively | Nothing; it changes every attack you make that turn |

## Attacks

**Iterative attacks no longer exist.** Every attack costs 1 AP and uses your full attack bonus, with a **multiple attack penalty (MAP)**: the second attack you make in a turn takes –5 and the third –10. Combat maneuvers count as attacks for the MAP. Attacks of opportunity do not use AP and never take the MAP.

**Two-Weapon Fighting:** 2 AP for one attack with each weapon, both at –2. A third attack that turn takes –10. With Improved Two-Weapon Fighting the third attack takes –5 instead, and once your base attack bonus is high enough for Greater Two-Weapon Fighting all three attacks take –2.

**Rapid Shot:** works like Two-Weapon Fighting: 2 AP for two shots at –2, and a third shot that turn at –10. **Manyshot** costs 2 AP for one bow attack that fires two arrows; with Rapid Shot, all your bow attacks that turn take –2 (Manyshot plus one more shot: three arrows, all at –2).

**Flurry of blows:** the unchained monk's flurry gives two attacks with no penalty for 2 AP, and a third attack that turn takes –10. The monk (core) works like Two-Weapon Fighting (–2) but uses his monk level as his base attack bonus, as now. **Ki extra attack:** spending 1 ki point makes the third attack of the turn take no penalty instead of –10; it still costs 1 AP.

**Other attack feats:** Vital Strike (any version) costs 2 AP. Cleave, Whirlwind Attack and Spring Attack cost 2 AP; the attacks of Cleave and Whirlwind Attack take no multiple attack penalty among themselves.

**Natural attacks:** each different natural attack you have is one attack with no MAP, up to three (a bite and two claws: three attacks with no penalty). Repeated ones do not avoid the MAP (two claws: two attacks with no penalty and a third at –10; a single bite takes the MAP like any weapon). Secondary natural attacks keep their –5 and half Strength to damage. A creature with more than three natural attacks has a special 3 AP action to attack with all of them.

**Weapons and natural attacks together:** the weapon attack is made with no penalty and the natural attacks become secondary (–5, half Strength). A hand that holds a weapon cannot also make a claw attack.

**Haste** gives 1 extra AP for anything while it lasts, together with its speed, attack, AC and Reflex bonuses. **Speed** weapons give 1 extra AP that can only be spent on an attack with that weapon, and that attack takes no MAP.

## Reloading

Reloading as a standard action costs 2 AP. Each improvement that makes it faster (Rapid Reload, class features) lowers it by 1 AP, and once it is a free action you can make three shots a turn, with the MAP.

## Spells

| Spell | Cost |
|---|---|
| Harmless spells and buffs on one target | 1 AP |
| Very strong buffs, or buffs on many targets or an area (*haste*, mass spells) | 2 AP |
| Control spells (they disable, hinder or move creatures, or create walls and hazards) | 2 AP |
| Offensive spells, especially area ones | 2 AP |
| Weak offensive spells (fixed damage that barely grows, small penalties) | 1 AP |
| Cantrips and orisons | 1 AP |
| Casting time of 1 round or a full round | 3 AP, and the spell takes effect that turn |
| Swift action spells | 1 AP |
| Immediate action spells | 1 AP of your next turn |
| Concentrating on or maintaining a spell | 1 AP |

**Touch spells** follow the same categories: a touch spell is not weak because it needs a touch, so one whose damage grows with your level (*shocking grasp*, *vampiric touch*) or that curses, disables or poisons (*bestow curse*, *contagion*) costs 2 AP. With spell combat, a magus pays 1 AP for it, never less.

There is no limit on how many spells you cast in a turn, other than your AP. Each spell's cost appears next to its casting time. **Spontaneous casters** applying a metamagic feat pay 1 extra AP instead of taking a full-round action. **Quicken Spell** lowers the cost by 1 AP (minimum 1 AP).

**Haste:** a 3rd-level spell on one target and a 4th-level spell as an area (mass) version; for the medium, 2nd and 3rd level.

## Class Features

- **Anything a feat or class feature does as a standard, move or swift action costs 1 AP.**
- **Supernatural and spell-like abilities that work like a spell** (hexes, channel energy, wild shape, lay on hands…) cost what that kind of spell costs: 2 AP to control or to deal damage in an area, 1 AP for buffs and weak effects. Each hex shows its cost at the start of its text. Abilities that are attacks (kinetic blast, bombs) cost 1 AP each and take the MAP.
- **Faster actions:** when a class feature makes an action faster, the cost drops 1 AP if it started at 2 AP or more. An action that already costs 1 AP stays at 1 AP when it becomes a swift action. Anything that becomes a free action is free.
- **Bardic performance:** starting a performance costs 2 AP, and 1 AP once the bard can start it as a move or swift action.
- **Fervor:** gives 1 free extra AP each turn that can only be spent casting spells on yourself.
- **Spell combat:** the damage spell costs 1 AP instead of 2, so a magus can cast it and attack twice. If the spell needs an attack roll, or is delivered with spellstrike, it is made at –2, and a third attack that turn takes –10, as with Two-Weapon Fighting.

## Conditions

Conditions keep their [numbered levels](../appendices/conditions.md). At their worst, they take AP away:

| Condition | Effect on AP |
|---|---|
| Staggered | –1 AP |
| Slowed (*slow*) | –1 AP |
| Nauseated 2 | Only 1 AP, which can also be spent on an attack |
| Stunned 1 | Only 1 AP |
| Stunned 2, dazed, paralyzed, helpless | 0 AP |

There is no maximum number of AP: *haste*, fervor and similar effects add up.

## Mounts and Companions

Mounted combat works as now. Mounts, animal companions, eidolons, familiars and summoned creatures have their own turn and their own 3 AP. Giving one an order costs 1 AP when it would normally take more than a free action, and is free otherwise.
