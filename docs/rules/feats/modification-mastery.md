# Modification Mastery

*(Combat)*

You can create inconceivable bastardizations of the smith’s art.

**Prerequisites**: [Creative Armorsmith](creative-armorsmith.md), [Armor Adept](armor-adept.md), and Craft (armor) 7 ranks, or [Creative Weaponsmith](creative-weaponsmith.md), [Weapon Adept](weapon-adept.md), and Craft (weapons) 7 ranks; Knowledge (engineering) 7 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can use [Creative Armorsmith](creative-armorsmith.md) or Creative Weaponsmith to add a modification to a weapon or suit of armor that already has a modification. The modification you are adding must be one that you have selected with [Armor Adept](armor-adept.md) or [Weapon Adept](weapon-adept.md). In addition, others can use armor or weapons with your temporary modifications.
