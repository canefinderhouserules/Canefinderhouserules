# Merciless Precision

*(General)*

You delight and excel in bringing down hobbled targets.

**Prerequisites**: Sneak attack class feature, kobold.

**Benefit**: Your sneak attack damage increases by 1d6 against targets that are already afflicted with any of the following conditions: [blinded](../appendices/conditions.md#blinded), dying, [entangled](../appendices/conditions.md#entangled), [exhausted](../appendices/conditions.md#exhausted), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), [helpless](../appendices/conditions.md#helpless), [nauseated](../appendices/conditions.md#nauseated), [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked), [paralyzed](../appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed), or [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned).
