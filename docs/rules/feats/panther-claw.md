# Panther Claw

*(Combat)*

You unleash a rapid series of blows on foes that attempt to attack you when you move.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Panther Style](panther-style.md).

**Benefit**: While using [Panther Style](panther-style.md), you can spend a free action, instead of spending a swift action, to make a retaliatory unarmed strike. You can make a number of retaliatory unarmed strikes on your turn equal to your Wisdom modifier.
