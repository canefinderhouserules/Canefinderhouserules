# Terrifying Mask

Your fiendish visage has made you a truly terrifying spectacle to behold, and you can force others to betray their feelings with a single look.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [Monstrous Mask](monstrous-mask.md), tiefling.

**Benefit**: Anytime you can make a [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) check to get a hunch or detect whether someone is trustworthy or not, you can choose to instead make an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check at a –2 penalty. This ability only works on creatures of the humanoid type.
