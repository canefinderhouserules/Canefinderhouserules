# Quick Draw (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You can retrieve any item quickly, not just weapons.

**Prerequisite**: [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md).

**Benefit**: You can use [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md) to draw items of any kind, not just weapons, provided they are stored or concealed on your person. As a move action (1 AP), you can expend one use of mythic power to retrieve up to two hidden items. You must have two hands free to do so.
