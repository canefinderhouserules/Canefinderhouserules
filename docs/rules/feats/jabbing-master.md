# Jabbing Master

*(Combat)*

Your quick punches become even more lethal.

**Prerequisites**: Dodge, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Jabbing Dancer](jabbing-dancer.md), [Jabbing Style](jabbing-style.md), [Mobility](mobility.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md); base attack bonus +12, brawler level 8th, or monk level 8th.

**Benefit**: While using [Jabbing Style](jabbing-style.md), the extra damage you deal when you hit a single target with two unarmed strikes increases to 2d6, and the extra damage when you hit a single target with three or more unarmed strikes increases to 4d6.
