# Destroy Identity

*(Combat, Critical)*

You can use your weapons to mar your opponents’ most distinctive characteristics.

**Prerequisites**: [Critical Focus](critical-focus.md), base attack bonus +11, worshiper of a deity of monsters and madness.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an opponent, in addition to dealing critical damage as normal, you maim your target so much that it takes 2 points of Charisma damage and becomes [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round.
