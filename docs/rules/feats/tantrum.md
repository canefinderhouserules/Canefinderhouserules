# Tantrum

*(Combat)*

Your crying, sputtering, and even frothing at the mouth makes you so laughably incoherent that opponents are apt to dismiss you as a threat.

**Prerequisites**: Bluff 1 rank, rage class feature, gnome.

**Benefit**: You may use the Bluff skill to feint while raging. The effect of the feint lasts only until the first time you hit the target. This is a mind-affecting, language-dependent effect that uses your gnome flair for conversation; magical effects that temporarily improve Bluff checks (such as *glibness*) cannot modify these checks.
