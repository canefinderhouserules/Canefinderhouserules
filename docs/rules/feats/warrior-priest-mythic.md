# Warrior Priest (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your faith speeds you in battle and further strengthens your mind and confidence.

**Prerequisite**: [Warrior Priest](warrior-priest.md).

**Benefit**: You gain a bonus equal to half your tier both on initiative checks and on concentration checks to cast a spell or use a spell-like ability when casting defensively or while [grappled](../appendices/conditions.md#grappled). These bonuses stack with the bonuses from [Warrior Priest](warrior-priest.md).
