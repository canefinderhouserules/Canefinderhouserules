# Improved Drow Nobility

Your magical heritage is more potent than that of your peers, as demonstrated by your superior spell-like abilities.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [Drow Nobility](drow-nobility.md), able to use drow spell-like abilities, drow.

**Benefit**: You may use your [*dancing lights*](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md), [*faerie fire*](../../magic/spells/faerie-fire.md), [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md), and [*levitate*](../../magic/spells/levitate.md) spell-like abilities twice per day. Your [*darkness*](../../magic/spells/darkness.md) spell-like ability instead becomes [*deeper darkness*](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md), which you may use twice per day.
