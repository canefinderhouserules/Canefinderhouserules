# Deft Hands (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your hands are astonishingly nimble.

**Prerequisite**: [Deft Hands](deft-hands.md).

**Benefit**: The bonus on [Disable Device](../skills/disable-device.md) and [Sleight of Hand](../skills/sleight-of-hand.md) skill checks from [Deft Hands](deft-hands.md) increases by 2. In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Disable Device or Sleight of Hand check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
