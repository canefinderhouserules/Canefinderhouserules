# Raging Hurler

An opponent can do little to evade your wrathful pitching of weapons and objects.

**Prerequisites**: Rage class feature, [Throw Anything](throw-anything.md).

**Benefit**: While raging, you can throw a two-handed weapon as a standard action (1 AP), and you double the range increment for weapons you throw. If you also have the [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md) feat, you can throw two-handed weapons at your full normal rate of attacks. Further, you can pick up an unattended object that you can use as a improvised weapon within your reach as part of the attack action to throw that item.
