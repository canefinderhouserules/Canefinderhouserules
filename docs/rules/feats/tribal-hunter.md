# Tribal Hunter

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

From watching pack animals hunt, you learned to take down large prey as part of a group.

**Prerequisite**: [Animal Affinity](animal-affinity.md).

**Benefit**: +1 to attack and damage rolls against animals whenever an ally within 30 feet also has this feat.

**Normal**: You must be positioned opposite an ally to flank an opponent.

**Category**: Trait
