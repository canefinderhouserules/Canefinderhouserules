# Armored Rider

You are accustomed to riding while wearing armor, and your armor doesn’t hinder your riding skills.

**Prerequisites**: [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md), Ride 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You don’t take the usual armor check penalty on [Ride](../skills/ride.md) checks. If you are knocked [unconscious](../appendices/conditions.md#unconscious) while in a saddle, you always remain in the saddle.

**Normal**: If you are knocked unconscious while riding, you have a 50% chance to stay in the saddle (75% if you’re in a military saddle).
