# Heroic Recovery (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You can shrug off the effects of harmful conditions after they've taken effect.

**Prerequisites**: [Heroic Recovery](heroic-recovery.md).

**Benefit**: As a swift action (1 AP), you can expend one use of mythic power to use [Heroic Recovery](heroic-recovery.md) additional times. Whenever you do, add your tier to the result of the Fortitude save.
