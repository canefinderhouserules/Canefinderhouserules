# Beast Speaker

You have trained in the ways that ancient beast speakers once used to ally themselves with magical beasts.

**Prerequisites**: Handle Animal 7 ranks, animal companion with effective druid level 7.

**Benefit**: You can select a creature from the list of [beast companions](../../characters/companions/beast-companions.md) to serve as your animal companion. You acquire and advance this beast companion in the same way as an animal companion detailed in the class feature that grants you access to your animal companion. You can also dismiss the creature, as dictated by your class feature. You must meet additional prerequisites to select a companion with an Intelligence score of 3 or higher, as described in each creature's entry.
