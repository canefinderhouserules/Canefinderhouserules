# Grabbing Master

*(Combat)*

You can grapple two foes as easily as one.

**Prerequisites**: [Grabbing Drag](grabbing-drag.md), [Grabbing Style](grabbing-style.md), [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md); base attack bonus +12, brawler level 8th, or monk level 8th.

**Benefit**: When you are grabbing two opponents while using [Grabbing Style](grabbing-style.md), you can use your grapple to move or damage one or both opponents you are grappling, instead of just one.
