# Fey-Guarded

You have trained your mind to resist fey magic—both that cast by fey creatures and that cast by other denizens of the First World.

**Prerequisite**: Knowledge (nature) or Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 morale bonus on saving throws against enchantment and illusion spells and spell-like abilities cast by creatures of the fey type.
