# Wolf Trip

*(Combat)*

You have studied the manner in which wolves bring down their prey.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Wolf Style](wolf-style.md), Knowledge (nature) 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: While using [Wolf Style](wolf-style.md), you gain a +2 bonus when you attempt a trip combat maneuver as part of an attack of opportunity. Whenever you successfully trip a creature, as a free action you can choose an available space that is both adjacent to you and the creature’s original space for the tripped creature to land prone in.
