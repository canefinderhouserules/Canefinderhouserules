# Kindred Pretense

*(General)*

Pointing out your shared kinship with someone gives you the chance to win over creatures associated with your bloodline.

**Prerequisites**: Bluff 1 rank, bloodline class feature, gnome.

**Benefit**: When attempting a [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) check to influence a creature with a plausible connection to your sorcerous bloodline, you may instead make a [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) check. For example, a gnome sorcerer with the draconic bloodline may use this feat against dragons and their kin, but no other creatures. This is a mind-affecting, language-dependent effect that uses your gnome flair for conversation; magical effects that temporarily improve Bluff checks (such as [*glibness*](../../magic/spells/glibness.md)) cannot modify these checks.
