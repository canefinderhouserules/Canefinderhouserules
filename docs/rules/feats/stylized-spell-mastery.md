# Stylized Spell Mastery

You have altered your understanding of a select number of spells.

**Prerequisites**: [Spell Mastery](spell-mastery.md), [Stylized Spell](stylized-spell.md), Bluff 5 ranks, Spellcraft 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You do not modify the spell slot used when applying the [Stylized Spell](stylized-spell.md) metamagic feat to any spell you have mastered with [Spell Mastery](spell-mastery.md).

**Normal**: A stylized spell uses up a spell slot 1 higher than the spell’s actual level.
