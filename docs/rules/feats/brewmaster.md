# Brewmaster

You can concoct potent brews.

**Prerequisites**: Craft (alchemy) 1 rank, Profession (brewer) 1 rank, dwarf.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [Craft](../skills/craft.md) (alchemy) and [Profession](../skills/profession.md) (brewer) checks, and you add +1 to the DC of any ingested poison you create.
