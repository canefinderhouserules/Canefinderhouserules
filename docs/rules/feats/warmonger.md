# Warmonger

You are adept at instigating wars among those you advise or antagonize.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, hobgoblin.

**Benefit**: You gain [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) and Diplomacy as class skills. In addition, you receive a +4 competence bonus on Bluff and [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) skill checks when using either skill to convince another character or group to go to war against or attack another creature or group.
