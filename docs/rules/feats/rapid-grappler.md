# Rapid Grappler

*(Combat)*

You are a quick hand at grappling.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Greater Grapple](greater-grapple.md), [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use [Greater Grapple](greater-grapple.md) to successfully maintain a grapple as a move action (1 AP), you can then spend a swift action (1 AP) to make a grapple combat maneuver check at a –5 penalty.
