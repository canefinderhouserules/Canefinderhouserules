# Reflexive Caster

You are ready to defend yourself with a spell at a moment's notice.

**Prerequisites**: [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), caster level 5th.

**Benefit**: If you fail a [Perception](../skills/perception.md) check that results in you being unable to act in a surprise round, you still roll initiative normally. You can act on your initiative count in the surprise round, but can only take a standard action (1 AP) to cast an abjuration spell that targets only yourself.
