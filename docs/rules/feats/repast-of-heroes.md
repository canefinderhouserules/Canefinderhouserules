# Repast of Heroes

Your natural spells are significantly increased, enabling you to feed and heal others.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, character level 8th, ghoran, natural magic racial trait.

**Benefit**: Once per week, you can cast *heroes' feast* as a spell-like ability with a caster level equal to your character level. If you do so, you lose access to the spells normally granted by the natural magic racial trait for the next 48 hours.
