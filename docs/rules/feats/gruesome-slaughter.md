# Gruesome Slaughter

*(Combat)*

You can inspire extreme revulsion when you slaughter your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [Intimidating Prowess](intimidating-prowess.md), [Killing Flourish](killing-flourish.md), Intimidate 11 ranks, slayer level 11th.

**Benefit**: Creatures you demoralize by using the [Killing Flourish](killing-flourish.md) feat must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + 1/2 your character level + the higher of your Strength or Dexterity modifier) or become sickened for 1 minute.
