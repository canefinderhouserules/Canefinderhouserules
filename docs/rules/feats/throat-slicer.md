# Throat Slicer

*(Combat)*

Sometimes the only way to deal with those who see your illicit activities is to get rid of them.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When using a 1-handed, light, or natural weapon, you can deliver a coup de grace to an [unconscious](../appendices/conditions.md#unconscious), bound, or [pinned](../appendices/conditions.md#pinned) target (though not other kinds of [helpless](../appendices/conditions.md#helpless) targets) as a standard action (1 AP).
