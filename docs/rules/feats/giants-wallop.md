# Giant’s Wallop

*(Combat)*

When you strike, you knock the sense out of smaller foes.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Power Attack](power-attack.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), base attack bonus +6, giant subtype.

**Benefit**:  If you hit a creature at least one size category smaller than you with a melee attack while using both [Power Attack](power-attack.md) and [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), the creature you attacked is also [dazed](../appendices/conditions.md#dazed) for 1 round. A successful Fortitude save negates this effect (DC = 10 + your base attack bonus). You must choose to use this feat before making the attack roll.

**Special**:  This effect stacks with the effect of [Giant’s Crush](giants-crush.md).
