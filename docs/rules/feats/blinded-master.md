# Blinded Master

*(Combat)*

Your skill at arms while unable to see is astounding.

**Prerequisites**: [Blind-Fight](blind-fight.md), [Blinded Blade Style](blinded-blade-style.md), [Blinded Competence](blinded-competence.md), [Greater Blind-Fight](greater-blind-fight.md), [Improved Blind-Fight](improved-blind-fight.md), Perception 15 ranks.

**Benefit**: While you are using [Blinded Blade Style](blinded-blade-style.md) and you are [blinded](../appendices/conditions.md#blinded) or unable to see, your ability to pinpoint creatures' locations using [Blinded Competence](blinded-competence.md) improves to function like blindsight rather than blindsense, and the range increases to 30 feet. In addition, you add half your character level to the DCs of [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) checks to feint you in combat.
