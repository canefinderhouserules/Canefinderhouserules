# Self-Sufficient (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Independence comes naturally to you, and you have mastered taking care of yourself

**Prerequisite**: [Self-Sufficient](self-sufficient.md).

**Benefit**: The bonus on [Heal](../skills/heal.md) and [Survival](../skills/survival.md) skill checks from Self- Sufficient increases by 2. In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Heal or Survival check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
