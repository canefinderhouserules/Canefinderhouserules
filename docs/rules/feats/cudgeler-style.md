# Cudgeler Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You have learned how to rush your foe and deliver an incapacitating blow. This style focuses on dealing only nonlethal damage to incapacitate opponents. Variants are practiced worldwide by bounty hunters, prison guards, and others who aim to subdue rather than kill their quarry.

**Prerequisites**: Bludgeoner.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a charge attack to deal nonlethal damage while using this style, your weapon deals damage as if it were one size category larger than it actually is. If you have the ability to make more than one attack on a charge, this increase applies only to your first attack.
