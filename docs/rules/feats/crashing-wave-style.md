# Crashing Wave Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You relentlessly push your enemies around, as the sea moves those within and upon it against their will.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [Improved Drag](improved-drag.md), [Improved Reposition](improved-reposition.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md).

**Benefit**: When you successfully drag or reposition an opponent while using this style, at any point during the dragging or repositioning of the opponent, you can move 5 feet as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn), including moving into a square previously occupied by the opponent, even if you have already taken a move action (1 AP) this round. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.
