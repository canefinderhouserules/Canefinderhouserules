# Uncanny Alertness

Your research into arcana and the nature of reality has given you heightened senses.

**Prerequisite**: [Alertness](alertness.md).

**Benefit**: This feat gives you an additional +1 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) and [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) checks, and you gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against sleep and charm effects.
