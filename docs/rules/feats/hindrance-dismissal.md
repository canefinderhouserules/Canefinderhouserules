# Hindrance Dismissal

You've mastered the art of dismissing certain spells when they become a problem for you or your allies.

**Benefit**: You can dismiss any pit spell (such as [*create pit*](../../magic/spells/create-pit.md), [*hungry pit*](../../magic/spells/hungry-pit.md), [*spiked pit*](../../magic/spells/spiked-pit.md), and similar spells with "pit" in their names) or wall spell (such as [*wall of fire*](../../magic/spells/wall-of-fire.md), [*wall of ice*](../../magic/spells/wall-of-ice.md), and similar spells with "wall" in their names) you cast. The spells must have a duration of at least 1 round. This ability follows the normal rules for dismissing spells, allowing you to dismiss conjured pits and walls as a standard action (1 AP).

**Normal**: Several pit and wall spells cannot be dismissed without the use of this feat.
