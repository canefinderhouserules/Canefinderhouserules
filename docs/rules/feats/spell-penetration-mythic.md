# Spell Penetration (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your mythic power breaches your enemies' defenses.

**Prerequisite**: [Spell Penetration](spell-penetration.md).

**Benefit**: Add half your tier to caster level checks to overcome [spell resistance](../../magic/spell-descriptions.md#spell-resistance). If you have [Greater Spell Penetration](greater-spell-penetration.md), add your full tier instead.
