# Blighted Critical

*(Critical)*

With a critical hit from a spell or spell-like ability, you give the target a minor spellblight (see [Spellblights](../../magic/mastering-magic/spellblights.md)).

**Prerequisites**: Caster level 5th.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a critical hit with a touch spell, ranged touch spell, or spell-like ability against an opponent, the victim gains a random minor spellblight (see [Spellblights](../../magic/mastering-magic/spellblights.md)).

**Special**: You can apply the effects of only one critical feat to a given critical hit unless you possess Critical Mastery.
