# Nameless Servitor

The fact that you have given up your real name makes you resistant to certain types of magic.

**Prerequisites**: member of the sun-king’s guard.

**Benefit**: Anyone who attempts to use magic in order to learn personal information about you, such as through [*divination*](../../magic/spells/divination.md), [*scrying*](../../magic/spells/scrying.md), or similar magic, must make a caster level check with a DC equal to 10 plus your level to succeed. You are immune to spells that require the use of your name in order to function (such as [*trap the soul*](../../magic/spells/trap-the-soul.md)).

Most members of the sun-king’s guard with this feat are addressed by their rank.

**Special**: If you ever choose a real name, you lose all benefits of this feat.
