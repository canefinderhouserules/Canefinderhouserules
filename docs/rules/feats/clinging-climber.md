# Clinging Climber

*(Combat)*

Using leverage and pure brawn, you can cling to a cliff face, ladder, or rope with your legs to free both of your hands to take other actions.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Climb 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a move action (1 AP) while climbing, you can cling to your climbing surface with your legs by succeeding at a [Climb](../skills/climb.md) check with the same DC as that of climbing the surface as a move action (1 AP). If successful, you can make attacks with a two-handed ranged weapon and reload ranged weapons until you resume climbing. The GM can rule that this feat doesn’t work on certain climbing surfaces.
