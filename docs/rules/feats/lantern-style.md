# Lantern Style

*(Combat, Style)*

Your thrown weapons shine with a divine light that dazzles their targets.

**Prerequisites**: [Point-Blank Shot](point-blank-shot.md), good or lawful alignment, caster level 1st or monk level 1st.

**Benefit**: Your thrown weapons glow with holy light, illuminating a 20-foot-radius area where they fall for 1 round. A target damaged by one of these weapons becomes dazzled for 1 round.
