# Intimidate Animals

*(Combat)*

You are skilled at intimidating animals with your bluster and fury.

**Prerequisites**: [Greater Wild Empathy](greater-wild-empathy.md), Intimidate 5 ranks, Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, wild empathy class feature.

**Benefit**: You add your Wisdom modifier on [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks against animals in addition to your Charisma modifier. An animal with 4 or fewer Hit Dice is [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened) instead of [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) on a successful Intimidate check to demoralize it.
