# Infernal Knight Obsession

You equate pain with discipline and can inflict damage upon yourself to unlock greater power.

**Prerequisites**: [Infernal Knight Obedience](infernal-knight-obedience.md), Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks, must swear allegiance to a single [knightly order](../../characters/prestige-classes/knightly-orders.md).

**Benefit**: During your hour-long reckoning, if you choose to deal lethal damage to yourself, you reach a state of fantastic focus. Once during the next 24 hours, you can draw upon this focus to gain the benefits of any one of the following effects. To activate it, you must deal 1 point of lethal damage to yourself as a swift action (unless otherwise noted). A member of the Order of the Spike with a sharp barb installed in his armor takes this damage when struck by a critical hit; if struck by a critical hit while wearing such armor, using this ability doesn't require a swift action.

- **Fervor**: The DCs to resist the boons granted by your Infernal Knight Obedience feat increase by 1 for 1 hour.
- **Indomitability**: You can reroll a single failed Will save and must take the second result, even if worse. A swift action isn't required, but you must still take 1 point of damage.
- **Menacing**: +3 bonus on Intimidate and Sense Motive checks for 1 hour.
- **Mercilessness**: The critical threat range of your order's favored weapon doubles for 1 hour (even if it is not a piercing or slashing weapon). This doesn't stack with other effects that increase a weapon's critical threat range.
