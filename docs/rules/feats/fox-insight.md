# Fox Insight

*(Combat)*

Your foxlike movements protect you from your opponents’ attempts to manipulate you.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [Fox Style](fox-style.md).

**Benefit**: While using [Fox Style](fox-style.md), you can use your base attack bonus in place of your ranks in Sense Motive to determine your [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) skill bonus when foes attempt to feint against you, and creatures attempting to demoralize you don’t gain a bonus for being bigger than you (though they still take a penalty for being smaller). If your Intelligence score is at least 19, the DC to demoralize or feint you increases by 4.
