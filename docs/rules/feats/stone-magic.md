# Stone Magic

Knowledgeable even among stone giant elders, you have unlocked greater mastery over earth and stone.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 17, stone giant elder (age 700 years or older).

**Benefit**: In addition to the usual spell-like abilities of a stone giant elder, you gain the ability to use [*passwall*](../../magic/spells/passwall.md),* spike stones*,* statue*,* stoneskin*, and* wall of stone *once per day as spell-like abilities (caster level 10th, the save DCs are Charisma-based).
