# Saving Shield

*(Combat)*

You deflect attacks that could mean your ally’s death.

**Prerequisite**: [Shield Proficiency](shield-proficiency.md).

**Benefit**: Whenever an adjacent ally is the target of an attack, you can, as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn), grant that adjacent ally a +2 shield bonus to AC. You must be wielding a light shield, heavy shield, or tower shield to use this feat.
