# Cockatrice Strike

*(Combat)*

With a single strike, you transmute flesh to stone.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Gorgon’s Fist](gorgons-fist.md), [Medusa’s Wrath](medusas-wrath.md), base attack bonus +14.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action (3 AP), you can make a single unarmed strike against a [dazed](../appendices/conditions.md#dazed), [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed), [paralyzed](../appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed), [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered), [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned), or [unconscious](../appendices/conditions.md#unconscious) foe. If that attack is a critical hit, the target is [petrified 1](../appendices/conditions.md#petrified) unless it succeeds on a Fortitude saving throw with a DC of 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier. This is a supernatural polymorph effect.
