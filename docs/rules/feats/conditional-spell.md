# Conditional Spell

*(Metamagic)*

**Benefit**: Create a condition for the receiver to follow. If the spell’s recipient violates the oath or prohibition while the conditional Spell remains in effect, the spell is undone as if never cast. If the spell was a healing spell, the hit point damage or condition you removed returns immediately, even if the subject has enjoyed subsequent rest or healing. Poisons, diseases, curses, restored ability damage, and negative levels removed by the paired spell return as well.

**Level Increase**: +0

**Category**: Trait
