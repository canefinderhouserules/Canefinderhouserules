# Greater Drow Nobility

You have mastered the lesser spell-like abilities of the drow, demonstrating true nobility.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [Drow Nobility](drow-nobility.md), [Improved Drow Nobility](improved-drow-nobility.md), able to use drow spell-like abilities, drow.

**Benefit**: Your [*detect magic*](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md) spell-like ability is now constant. You may use your [*dancing lights*](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md), [*deeper darkness*](../../magic/spells/deeper-darkness.md), [*faerie fire*](../../magic/spells/faerie-fire.md), [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md), and [*levitate*](../../magic/spells/levitate.md) spell-like abilities at will.
