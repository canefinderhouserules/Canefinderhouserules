# Bestow Luck

You are extremely lucky and sometimes your allies are as well.

**Prerequisites**: [Defiant Luck](defiant-luck.md), [Inexplicable Luck](inexplicable-luck.md), human.

**Benefit**: You gain an extra use per day of your [Defiant Luck](defiant-luck.md) ability. You can also use your [Inexplicable Luck](inexplicable-luck.md) ability to grant an ally that can see and hear its benefit as an immediate action.
