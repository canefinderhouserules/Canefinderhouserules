# Shatter Defenses

*(Combat)*

Your skill with your chosen weapon leaves opponents unable to defend themselves if you strike them when their defenses are already compromised.

**Prerequisites**: [Weapon Focus](weapon-focus.md), [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with weapon.

**Benefit**: Any [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked) opponent hit by you this round is [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) to your attacks until the end of your next turn. This includes any additional attacks you make this round.
