# Stone Read

Faint vibrations in stone can alert you to trouble.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (dungeoneering) 4 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +3 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks in underground or mountainous environments. If you have 10 or more ranks in [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (dungeoneering), this bonus increases to +6.
