# Night Stalker

You excel at hunting humans in the dead of night and murdering them quietly.

**Prerequisites**: Skill Focus (Stealth), darkvision, half-ogre or ogre, size Large or larger.

**Benefit**: While in areas of dim lighting or natural darkness, you ignore all size penalties on [Stealth](../skills/stealth.md) checks and gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls against [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) enemies.
