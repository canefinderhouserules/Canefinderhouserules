# Canticle of Agony

*(Masterpiece, Sing)*

Your haunting melody is capable of shattering the very minds of those who hear it.

**Prerequisites**: Perform (sing) 7 ranks.

**Benefit**: This agonizing eulogy gets into a listener's blood, and bestows on him the phantom sensation of all the agonies suffered by the most pious worshipers of a god of pain. One target within range is cursed as though affected by *bestow curse* as long as the bard maintains the performance. Although this lullaby does have words, it is not a language-dependent effect.

**Cost**: Feat or 3rd-level bard spell known. **Use**: 1 bardic performance round per round. **Action**: 1 round.
