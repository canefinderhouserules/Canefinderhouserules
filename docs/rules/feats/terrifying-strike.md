# Terrifying Strike

Your touch spreads fear.

**Prerequisites**: Despair special ability, mummy.

**Benefit**:  Any creature struck by your slam attack must succeed at a Will save at the same DC as your despair ability or be [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) for 1d4 rounds. If the creature is already shaken, [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked), it is [paralyzed](../appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed) for 1 round instead. This is a mind-affecting fear effect.
