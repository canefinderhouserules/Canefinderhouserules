# Electric Eel Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You have learned how to harness electrical energy to momentarily disorient your foes.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md) attempt per day. While you are in this style, your Elemental Fist attack deals electricity damage. When a creature takes electricity damage from your Elemental Fist attack, you gain a +4 bonus for 1 round on combat maneuver checks to grapple the creature.
