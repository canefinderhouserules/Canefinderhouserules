# Improved Umbral Scion

You are a master of drow noble magic.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Wis 13, [Drow Nobility](drow-nobility.md), [Greater Drow Nobility](greater-drow-nobility.md), [Improved Drow Nobility](improved-drow-nobility.md), [Umbral Scion](umbral-scion.md), able to use drow spell-like abilities, drow.

**Benefit**: You may use [*dispel magic*](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md), [*divine favor*](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md), and [*suggestion*](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md) once per day as spell-like abilities. Your caster level is equal to your character level.
