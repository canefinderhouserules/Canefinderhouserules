# Ki Stand

If an opponent knocks you down, you swiftly rebound with an attack.

**Prerequisite**: Ki pool.

**Benefit**: While you have at least 1 ki point in your ki pool, you can stand up as a swift action (1 AP) that provokes attacks of opportunity.

You may spend 1 point of your ki pool to stand up as a swift action (1 AP) without provoking attacks of opportunity.
