# Undead Master (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Through your fearsome mythic nature, you have matchless power over the undead.

**Prerequisite**: [Undead Master](undead-master.md).

**Benefit**: When you cast animate dead or use the [Command Undead](command-undead.md) feat, add your tier to your caster level when determining the number of Hit Dice of undead you animate. This stacks with the increased caster level of [Undead Master](undead-master.md). When you cast command undead, your duration is tripled rather than doubled.
