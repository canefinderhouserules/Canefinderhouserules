# Read the Room

You can gain some insight into the mood of a crowd with just a look.

**Prerequisites**: [Drunken Sing-Along](drunken-sing-along.md), Diplomacy 1 rank, Perform (sing) 1 rank, Sense Motive 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Before you use the [Drunken Sing-Along](drunken-sing-along.md) feat, you can attempt a DC 20 [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) check as a free action to pick the exact right song for that crowd. If you are successful, you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on your next [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) check to gather information in that tavern.
