# Pinpoint Mastery

*(Combat)*

**Prerequisites**: [Pinpoint Attack](pinpoint-attack.md), base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: You count as 4 base attack bonus higher, only for the positive effects of pinpoint attack (or the negative effects on the enemy). This removes the –1 attack on ranged attacks. It increases the duration of the penalties by 1 (so they last 2 rounds).
