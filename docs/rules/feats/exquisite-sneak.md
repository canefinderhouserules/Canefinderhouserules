# Exquisite Sneak

You know how to move silently even while wearing some forms of armor.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Careful Sneak](careful-sneak.md), Stealth 6 ranks.

**Benefit**: You do not apply the armor check penalty for light and medium armor on [Stealth](../skills/stealth.md) skill checks no matter how fast you move. You still cannot use Stealth while running or charging.
