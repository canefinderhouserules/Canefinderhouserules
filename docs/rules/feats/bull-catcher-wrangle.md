# Bull-Catcher Wrangle

*(Combat)*

You can wrestle a charging foe to the ground.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Bull-Catcher Style](bull-catcher-style.md), [Bull-Catcher Toss](bull-catcher-toss.md), [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md); base attack bonus +12, brawler level 8th, or monk level 8th.

**Benefit**: When a creature is charging you, and you successfully grapple it with the [Bull-Catcher Style](bull-catcher-style.md) feat, you can immediately move it as per the [Bull-Catcher Toss](bull-catcher-toss.md) feat. Once the creature is moved, you can break your grapple as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn). If you do, select another creature in the repositioned target’s threatened area. That creature becomes the new target of the initial charge attack.
