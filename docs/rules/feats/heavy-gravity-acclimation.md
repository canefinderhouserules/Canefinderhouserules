# Heavy Gravity Acclimation

Your muscles work overtime in areas with heavy gravity.

**Prerequisites**: Str 17 or Endurance.

**Benefit**: You do not take the normal penalties for being on heavy gravity planes (see [Planar Traits](../campaign/planar-traits.md)). At the GM's discretion, this effect may apply to certain environments on the Material Plane, such as high-gravity planets. In addition, your Strength is considered to be 4 higher for the purpose of determining your carrying capacity.
