# Gorgon’s Fist

*(Combat)*

With one well-placed blow, you leave your target reeling.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Scorpion Style](scorpion-style.md), base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: As a standard action (1 AP), make a single unarmed melee attack against a foe whose speed is reduced (such as from [Scorpion Style](scorpion-style.md)). If the attack hits, you deal damage normally and the target is [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) until the end of your next turn unless it makes a Fortitude saving throw (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wis modifier). This feat has no effect on targets that are staggered.
