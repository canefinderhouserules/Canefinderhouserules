# Crane Riposte

*(Combat)*

You use your defensive abilities to make overpowering counterattacks.

**Prerequisites**: [Crane Style](crane-style.md), [Crane Wing](crane-wing.md), Dodge, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: You take only a –1 penalty on attack rolls for fighting defensively. Whenever you deflect an opponent's attack using [Crane Wing](crane-wing.md) or lose the doge bonus from Crane Wing because an attack missed you by 4 or less, you can make an attack of opportunity against the attacker after the attack misses.
