# Two-Weapon Fighting

*(Combat)*

You can fight with a weapon wielded in each of your hands. You can make one extra attack each round with the secondary weapon.

**Prerequisite**: Dex 14.

**Benefit**: Your penalties on attack rolls for fighting with two weapons are reduced. The penalty for your primary hand lessens by 2 and the one for your off hand lessens by 6. See Two-Weapon Fighting in Chapter 8.

**Normal**: If you wield a second weapon in your off hand, you can get one extra attack per round with that weapon. When fighting in this way you suffer a –6 penalty with your regular attack or attacks with your primary hand and a –10 penalty to the attack with your off hand. If your off-hand weapon is light, the penalties are reduced by 2 each. An unarmed strike is always considered light.

??? tip apr "Action Points (optional rule)"
    Spend 2 AP to attack once with each weapon, both at –2. A third attack that turn takes –10 ([multiple attack penalty](../combat-variants/action-points.md#attacks)). With [Improved Two-Weapon Fighting](improved-two-weapon-fighting.md) the third attack takes –5, and once your base attack bonus reaches +11 all three attacks take –2. The off-hand weapon still adds only half your Strength bonus to damage.
