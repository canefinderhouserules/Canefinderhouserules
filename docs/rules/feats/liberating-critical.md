# Liberating Critical

*(Technique)*

Your jarring blows can cleanse your enemies’ minds of supernatural control.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: When you score a critical hit against an opponent, you can use a swift action to cast *dispel magic* (using your total Hit Dice as your caster level) to target mind-affecting effects currently affecting that opponent. You do not need to be aware of whether or not your opponent is currently being affected by such an effect. While the intention of this effect is to liberate mentally dominated individuals from their masters, all mind-affecting effects can be dispelled, including beneficial ones opponents might have cast on themselves. This effect functions like *dispel magic* in that it targets all mind-affecting effects influencing the target, starting with the highest caster level, and ends when you successfully dispel one or fail to dispel them all.
