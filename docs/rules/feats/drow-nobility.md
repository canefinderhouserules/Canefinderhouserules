# Drow Nobility

Your blood courses with power, granting you greater spell-like abilities.

**Prerequisites**: Able to use drow spell-like abilities, drow.

**Benefit**: You may use [*detect magic*](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md) as a spell-like ability at will, and add [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md) and [*levitate*](../../magic/spells/levitate.md) to the spell-like abilities that you may use once each per day. Your caster level is equal to your character level.
