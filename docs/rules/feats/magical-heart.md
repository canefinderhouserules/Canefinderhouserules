# Magical Heart

*(Combat)*

Through intense focus, your magical strikes reinforce your ioun heart.

**Prerequisites**: [Arcane Strike](arcane-strike.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), wyrwood.

**Benefit**: While your unarmed strikes are imbued with power from the [Arcane Strike](arcane-strike.md) feat, a successful hit with an unarmed strike grants you a number of temporary hit points equal to the bonus damage granted by Arcane Strike.
