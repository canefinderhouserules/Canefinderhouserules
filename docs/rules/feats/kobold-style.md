# Kobold Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You're able to manipulate foes that are caught off guard. Quick, powerful movements mark this style, which focuses on rushing and upending an opponent, then climbing on top of it to keep the creature prone.

**Prerequisites**: Combat Expertise, Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on combat maneuver checks against enemies denied their Dexterity bonus to AC.

**Special**: Feat path: Kobold Style, Kobold Groundling, Kobold Flood.
