# Merchant Combine Partner

You have earned the attention of a vast merchant combine and can call in favors when necessary.

**Prerequisites**: Associate (a merchant combine).

**Benefit**: Whenever you are in a settlement that is the size of a small town or larger, you can purchase (but not sell) items through your combine contacts. Treat your current settlement as though it were a metropolis to determine its base value and available magic items. You pay a 5% markup for such items, and orders take 3d6 days to be delivered through the combine's channels. You can pay an additional 1,000 gp to have your item magically delivered in 1d3 days instead.
