# Stag Submission

*(Combat, Style)*

You can quickly subdue an opponent you've charged using a special submission hold.

**Prerequisites**: [Charging Stag Style](charging-stag-style.md), Dodge, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Mobility](mobility.md), [Stag Horns](stag-horns.md).

**Benefit**: When you are in [Charging Stag Style](charging-stag-style.md) and succeed at a combat maneuver check to pin a foe, you can choose to also knock the foe prone or to deal your unarmed attack damage to the foe.

**Normal**: After successfully maintaining a grapple on a target, you can choose to move, damage, or pin the target.
