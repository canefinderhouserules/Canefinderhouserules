# Quicken Spell

*(Metamagic)*

You can cast spells in a fraction of the normal time.

**Benefit**: Cast spell as a swift action. This does not allow two damaging spells to be used in the same turn.

**Special**: You can apply the effects of this feat to a spell cast spontaneously, so long as it has a casting time that is not more than 1 full-round action (3 AP), without increasing the spell’s casting time.

**Level Increase**: +3

**Category**: Powerful

??? tip apr "Action Points (optional rule)"
    A quickened spell costs 1 AP less (minimum 1 AP). There is no limit on how many spells, damaging or not, you cast in a turn: only your AP limit you.

??? note "Original rule"

    *(Metamagic)*

    You can cast spells in a fraction of the normal time.

    **Benefit**: Casting a quickened spell is a swift action (1 AP). You can perform another action, even casting another spell, in the same round as you cast a quickened spell. A spell whose casting time is more than 1 round or 1 full-round action (3 AP) cannot be quickened.

    A quickened spell uses up a spell slot four levels higher than the spell’s actual level. Casting a quickened spell doesn’t provoke an attack of opportunity.

    **Special**: You can apply the effects of this feat to a spell cast spontaneously, so long as it has a casting time that is not more than 1 full-round action (3 AP), without increasing the spell’s casting time.
