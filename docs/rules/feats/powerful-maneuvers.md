# Powerful Maneuvers

*(Combat)*

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You do not provoke an attack of opportunity when performing a grapple, bull rush, drag, overrun, or sunder combat maneuver. In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on checks with these combat maneuvers.

**Special**: This feat is a prerequisite for the relevant greater combat maneuver feats.

**Note**: This feat combines [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md), [Improved Bull Rush](improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Drag](improved-drag.md), [Improved Overrun](improved-overrun.md), [Improved Sunder](improved-sunder.md), which remain available on their own.
