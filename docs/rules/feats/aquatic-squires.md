# Aquatic Squires

When you call for aid from the sea, your summoned creatures remain to serve you longer.

**Prerequisites**: Triton, [*summon nature's ally II*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-ii.md) spell-like ability, character level 5th.

**Benefit**: The duration of your [*summon nature's ally II*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-ii.md) spell-like ability is 1 minute per level.

**Normal**: [*Summon nature's ally II*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-ii.md) has a duration of 1 round per level.
