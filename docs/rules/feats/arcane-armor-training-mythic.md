# Arcane Armor Training (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You've adapted your spellcasting to overcome limitations of wearing armor.

**Prerequisite**: [Arcane Armor Training](arcane-armor-training.md).

**Benefit**: You don't have to spend a swift action (1 AP) to gain the reduction to arcane spell failure chance from [Arcane Armor Training](arcane-armor-training.md). Furthermore, if you're wearing light armor, reduce your arcane spell failure chance by 20%.
