# Black Powder Spectacle

*(Combat, Grit, Performance)*

You can sacrifice ammunition to make a grand spectacle.

**Prerequisites**: [Amateur Gunslinger](amateur-gunslinger.md) or the grit class feature, base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: When you spend a swift action to attempt a performance combat check, you can sacrifice one cartridge or an amount of black powder required for a single shot to make either a loud sound or an impressive flash. If you choose to make your weapon’s report louder, you gain a +4 bonus on all [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks you attempt for 1 round. If you choose to create an impressive flash, you gain a +4 bonus on any [Perform](../skills/perform.md) checks you attempt for 1 round.
