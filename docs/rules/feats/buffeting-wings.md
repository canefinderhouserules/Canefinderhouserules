# Buffeting Wings

*(Combat)*

You can create a gust of wind with your wings.

**Prerequisites**: Hover, [Powerful Wings](powerful-wings.md).

**Benefit**: As a full-round action (3 AP), you can beat your wings to create a blast of air as the [*gust of wind*](../../magic/spells/gust-of-wind.md) spell. This is an extraordinary ability.
