# Second Wind

*(Combat, Performance)*

The roar of the crowd strengthens your resolve.

**Prerequisites**: [Endurance](endurance.md).

**Benefit**: When you spend a swift action (1 AP) to attempt a performance combat check, you gain a +2 bonus on your performance combat check, and if you are [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [sickened](../appendices/conditions.md#sickened), or [fatigued](../appendices/conditions.md#fatigued), these conditions are suppressed until the end of your next turn.
