# New Ancient Imperial Magic

You use ancient imperial magic in a nontraditional way that preserves the ancient traditions while lifting some of its restrictions.

**Prerequisites**: ancient imperial specialization.

**Benefit**: You choose one of your two opposition schools. You also gain ancient imperial as a bonus language. If you already speak ancient imperial, you gain one of the following languages as a bonus language instead: Aklo, drowned imperial, Giant, highland tribal, or wandering nomad.

**Normal**: An ancient imperial specialist cannot customize his choice for opposition schools.
