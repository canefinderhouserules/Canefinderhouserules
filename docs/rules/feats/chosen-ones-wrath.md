# Chosen One’s Wrath

You can draw upon your god’s power to defeat your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Chosen One, Chosen One’s Might, character level 10th, must worship a god.

**Benefit**: Once per day as a swift action, you can gain the benefits of the omdura’s divine might class feature, treating your omdura level as your character level – 5.

**Special**: You can take this feat a second time at 19th level, allowing you to use the ability a second time per day.
