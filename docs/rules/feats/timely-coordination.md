# Timely Coordination

*(Teamwork)*

You can precisely time your teamwork.

**Benefit**: Whenever you delay your turn to act at the same time as an ally who also has this feat, you both gain +3 to attack rolls or spell DCs, but only if your base rolls (casters roll a d20 for this purpose) are less than 4 apart. If they differ by 4 or more but less than 8, the bonus becomes +2; if they differ by 8 or more, it becomes +1. If you roll the same number, the bonus is +4.

If one of you rolls a critical hit, he can pass the critical to the other if you are adjacent (one stops critting so the other does in his place).

If one fails and the other succeeds, the successful one can transfer part of his surplus to the other if you are adjacent. For example, if your ally missed by 1 and you exceeded the target number by 2, and you both had +2 from this feat, you give him +1 so that he hits as well.

**Category**: Powerful
