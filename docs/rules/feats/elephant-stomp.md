# Elephant Stomp

*(Combat)*

You deliver a crushing blow to downed enemies.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Power Attack](power-attack.md), [Improved Overrun](improved-overrun.md), base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When you overrun an opponent and your maneuver check exceeds your opponent’s CMD by 5 or more, instead of moving through your opponent’s space and knocking her [prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone), you may stop in the space directly in front of the opponent (or the nearest adjacent space) and make one attack with an unarmed strike or a natural weapon against that opponent as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn).

**Normal**: When your overrun maneuver check exceeds your opponent’s CMD by 5 or more, you move through the target’s space and she is knocked prone.
