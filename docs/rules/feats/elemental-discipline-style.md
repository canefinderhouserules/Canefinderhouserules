# Elemental Discipline Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You have trained at a monastic school and have an innate connection to the school’s associated element.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, base attack bonus +5 or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: When you take this feat, choose one of three elemental disciplines (flame, wind, or iron); once this decision has been made it cannot be changed. While using this style, you gain a type of energy resistance based on the discipline you have chosen. Students of the iron discipline gain acid resistance 5. Students of the flame discipline gain fire resistance 5. Students of the wind discipline gain electricity resistance 5. If you have energy resistance from a racial trait or class feature, the energy resistance gained from this style stacks.

Additionally, you gain a ki pool that you can use to activate abilities of your chosen discipline’s style. Your pool contains 1 ki point at 1st level and increases by 1 point every 4 levels thereafter. If you have a ki pool from another source, you do not gain additional ki points from this feat.

**Special**: You may select Elemental Discipline Style multiple times. Each time you do so, you must select a different discipline.
