# Know Master

*(Technique)*

You recognize the telltale signs of undead overlords among their slaves.

**Benefit**: You can spend 1 minute examining the remains of an undead creature you destroyed within the last hour. Upon doing so, you can attempt a [Perception](../skills/perception.md) check with a DC equal to 15 + the undead creature’s Hit Dice. If you succeed, you know how the undead was created—through a spell, the create spawn ability, spontaneous generation, or another origin.

**Special**: If you have the [Vampire Scholar](vampire-scholar.md) feat, you might also recognize some sign of a destroyed undead being’s creator. If you determine that the undead creature was created by a vampire, you can immediately make use of your Vampire Scholar feat to determine who created it.
