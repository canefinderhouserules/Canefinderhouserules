# Orc Snarl

*(Combat)*

Your attacks leave your opponent quivering in fear.

**Prerequisites**: [Bullying Blow](bullying-blow.md), [Intimidating Prowess](intimidating-prowess.md), [Orc Fury Style](orc-fury-style.md), [Orc Rampage](orc-rampage.md), Intimidate 7 ranks, weapon familiarity racial trait.

**Benefit**: While using [Orc Fury Style](orc-fury-style.md), you gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against effects created by shaken foes.
