# Swordplay Upset

*(Combat)*

You catch opponents off guard with your attacks.

**Prerequisites**: [Combat Expertise](combat-expertise.md), [Improved Feint](improved-feint.md), [Swordplay Style](swordplay-style.md), Weapon Focus with the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: While using [Swordplay Style](swordplay-style.md), as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn), you can attempt a feint against an opponent that makes a melee attack against you and misses.
