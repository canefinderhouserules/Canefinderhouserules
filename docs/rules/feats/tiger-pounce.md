# Tiger Pounce

*(Combat)*

Your unarmed strikes are as precise as they are powerful, but they leave you open and you can pursue foes with blinding speed.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md), [Tiger Claws](tiger-claws.md), [Tiger Style](tiger-style.md), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 8th.

**Benefit**: While using the [Tiger Style](tiger-style.md) feat, you can apply the penalty from [Power Attack](power-attack.md) to your AC instead of attack rolls. Additionally, once per round as a swift action, you can move up to half your speed closer to a target you hit with an unarmed strike or made a successful combat maneuver against on this turn or your last turn.
