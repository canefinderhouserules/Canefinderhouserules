# Duelist of the Shrouded Lake

You have embraced the Shrouded Lake style of Noble swordplay, known for its circuitous footwork.

**Prerequisites**: Noble Dueling Disciple, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Noble dueling sword), Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (Noble dueling sword), base attack bonus +5.

**Benefit**: When attempting to bull rush or reposition a foe, you gain a +1 insight bonus on the combat maneuver check and a +4 dodge bonus to your AC against attacks of opportunity you provoke. You also gain a +1 insight bonus to your CMD against bull rush and reposition attempts. When you attempt a dueling dodge during a duel, your bonus to AC increases to +6. To gain these benefits, you must be wielding only an Noble dueling sword (not using a shield, an off-hand weapon, armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).
