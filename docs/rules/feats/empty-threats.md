# Empty Threats

You have a gift for making authentic-sounding threats you never intend to follow through on.

**Prerequisites**: Bluff 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can use the [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) skill to demoralize opponents in combat instead of [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md). You can attempt a Bluff check in place of an Intimidate check when using the [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md) feat and feats that list Dazzling Display as a prerequisite. If you use the Bluff skill to demoralize, you cannot use it to feint until the beginning of your next turn, and vice versa.
