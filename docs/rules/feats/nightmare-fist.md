# Nightmare Fist

*(Combat)*

You are even more deadly in magical darkness.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), Intimidate 1 rank, ability to create magical darkness.

**Benefit**: While fighting within an area of magical darkness, you gain a +2 bonus on damage rolls with unarmed strikes, or a +4 bonus against opponents that are [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked). You also gain a +2 morale bonus on [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) and [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks.
