# Greater Ray Shield

*(Shield Mastery)*

You can turn your shield to deflect rays and other ranged attacks without harming it.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 17, [Missile Shield](missile-shield.md), [Ray Shield](ray-shield.md), [Spellbreaker](spellbreaker.md).

**Benefit**: When you use the [Ray Shield](ray-shield.md) feat, your shield is not affected by the deflected ranged touch attack.
