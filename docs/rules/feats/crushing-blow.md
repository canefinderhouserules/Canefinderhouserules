# Crushing Blow

*(Combat)*

Your focus allows you to smash your enemy’s defenses.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md).

**Benefit**: You can make a [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md) attempt as a full-round action (3 AP). If successful, instead of stunning your target, you reduce the target’s AC by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier for 1 minute. This penalty does not stack with other penalties applied due to Crushing Blow.
