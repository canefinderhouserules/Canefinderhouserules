# Hurtful

*(Combat)*

You know how to add injury to insult.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Power Attack](power-attack.md).

**Benefit**: When you successfully demoralize an opponent within your melee reach with an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check, you can make a single melee attack against that creature as a swift action. If your attack fails to damage the target, its [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) condition from being demoralized immediately ends.
