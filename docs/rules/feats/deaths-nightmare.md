# Death’s Nightmare

*(Technique)*

Even the mindless dead fear your coming.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 14.

**Benefit**: Mindless undead with total Hit Dice equal to or less than your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier are vulnerable to your class abilities, skills, and spells that cause the [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked), and [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) conditions.

This includes the [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) skill, which you can use to demoralize affected mindless undead.
