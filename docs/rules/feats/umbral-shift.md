# Umbral Shift

*(Teamwork)*

Your shadows dance in the darkness, carrying you with them swiftly and silently so that you’re able to swap places with your kindred.

**Prerequisites**: Darkness racial spell-like ability.

**Benefit**: Whenever you and an ally with the darkness racial spell-like ability who also has this feat are each in an area of darkness within 20 feet of an object radiating darkness from a [*darkness*](../../magic/spells/darkness.md) spell, you can swap places with that ally as a swift action (1 AP). Your ally must be willing and must spend an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) to complete the swap.
