# Enfilading Fire

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

Your ranged attacks take advantage of the flanking maneuvers of allies.

**Benefit**: When ally flanks opponent, you gain +2 on ranged attack rolls against opponent.

**Category**: Trait

??? note "Original rule"
