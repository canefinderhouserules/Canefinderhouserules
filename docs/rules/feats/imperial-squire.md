# Imperial Squire

You spent time as a squire to a knight and understand how to fight alongside him.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, proficient in a martial weapon.

**Benefit**: When within 50 feet of a mounted ally with the Mounted Combat feat, you get a +2 bonus on initiative. If this ally is injured, you gain a +1 dodge bonus to AC as long as you are within 10 feet of him.
