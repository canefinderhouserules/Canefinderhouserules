# Improved Devastating Strike

*(Combat)*

The fury and power channeled through your attack is enough to kill a lesser being outright.

**Prerequisites**: [Devastating Strike](devastating-strike.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), base attack bonus +13.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md), or [Greater Vital Strike](greater-vital-strike.md), you gain a bonus on attack rolls to confirm a critical hit equal to the bonus on damage rolls you gain from [Devastating Strike](devastating-strike.md).
