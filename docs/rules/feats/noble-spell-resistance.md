# Noble Spell Resistance

Your ascension is complete; you have the spell resistance approaching that of a demon.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Wis 13, [Greater Drow Nobility](greater-drow-nobility.md), character level 13th, drow.

**Benefit**: Your spell resistance is equal to 11 + your character level.

**Special**: You receive a +1 circumstance bonus on [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) and [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks made against any drow.
