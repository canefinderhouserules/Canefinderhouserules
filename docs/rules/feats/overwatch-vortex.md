# Overwatch Vortex

*(Combat)*

You are ready to respond to many more of your foes’ actions.

**Prerequisites**: [Overwatch Style](overwatch-style.md), [Overwatch Tactician](overwatch-tactician.md), [Precise Shot](precise-shot.md), [Rapid Shot](rapid-shot.md), Weapon Focus with the chosen weapon, base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: While using the [Overwatch Style](overwatch-style.md), as a full-round action (3 AP) you can ready up to four ranged attacks, each with its own triggering event. You take a –2 penalty on attack rolls made with these readied actions.
