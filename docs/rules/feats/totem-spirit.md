# Totem Spirit

You are mystically tied to your tribe’s sacred totem.

**Prerequisites**: Member of a highland tribal tribe.

**Benefit**: The benefit granted by this feat depends on which highland tribal tribe you belong to:

*Lyrune-Quah (Moon Clan)*: You gain a +1 bonus on Will saves and a +2 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks.

*Shade-Quah (Axe Clan)*: If you have the rage ability, you can rage for 3 additional rounds per day. You also gain a +2 bonus on [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks.

*Shriikirri-Quah (Hawk Clan)*: You gain a +2 bonus on Initiative checks and a +2 bonus on [Ride](../skills/ride.md) checks.

*Shundar-Quah (Spire Clan)*: You gain a +1 bonus on Fortitude saves and a +2 bonus on Perception checks.

*Sklar-Quah (Sun Clan)*: You gain a +1 bonus on Reflex saves and a +2 bonus on [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) checks.

*Skoan-Quah (Skull Clan)*: You gain a +2 bonus on weapon damage against undead and a +2 bonus on [Heal](../skills/heal.md) checks.

*Tamiir-Quah (Wind Clan)*: Your base land speed increases by 5 feet. You also gain a +2 bonus on Acrobatics checks.
