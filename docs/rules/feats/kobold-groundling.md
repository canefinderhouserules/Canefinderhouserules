# Kobold Groundling

*(Combat, Style)*

Your small size grants you superiority over grounded foes.

**Prerequisites**: [Kobold Style](kobold-style.md), Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: [Prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone) creatures are denied their Dexterity bonus to AC against your attacks.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Combat Expertise, Kobold Style, Small size or smaller.
