# Infernal Knight Obedience

Performing daily reckonings grants you special boons.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks, must swear allegiance to a single [knightly order](../../characters/prestige-classes/knightly-orders.md).

**Benefit**: Upon taking this feat, you gain proficiency in all of your order's favored weapons. By meditating and inflicting strenuous reckonings upon yourself, you unlock the power behind your order's doctrine. Each order has its own reckoning, a trial of obedience and pain; coupled with reflection and meditation it takes 1 hour per day. Once you have performed your order's reckoning, you gain the benefit of its special ability. If you have at least 12 Hit Dice, you also gain the order's first boon; at 16 Hit Dice, the second boon; at 20 Hit Dice or more, the third. Unless a boon lists a duration or number of uses per day, its effects are constant.

If you ever fail to perform a daily reckoning, you lose all access to the benefits and boons of this feat until you next perform it.

You can take this feat only if you are associated with a knightly order, whether in training, already a member, or otherwise aligned with it; the specifics are for you and the GM to determine. Once per year you can change allegiance, with the approval of the order you wish to join. If you are cast out of your order, you lose the benefits of this feat until you restore yourself in its esteem (details at the GM's discretion).
