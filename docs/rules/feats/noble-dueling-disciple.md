# Noble Dueling Disciple

Your prowess in Noble swordplay grants you bravado.

**Prerequisites**: Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Noble dueling sword), [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus (Noble dueling sword).

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 morale bonus on [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks to demoralize opponents, and the DC of any attempt to demoralize you increases by 2. When you’re participating in a duel, these bonuses increase to +4.
