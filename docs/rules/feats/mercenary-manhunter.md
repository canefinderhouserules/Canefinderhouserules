# Mercenary Manhunter

You are skilled at fighting criminal humans such as bandits.

**Prerequisites**: member of a mercenary company.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 bonus on [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md), [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md), [Perception](../skills/perception.md), [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md), and [Survival](../skills/survival.md) checks against humans, and a +1 bonus on weapon attack and damage rolls against humans. If you have a favored enemy bonus against humans, this feat does not stack with that bonus.
