# Maddening Style

*(Combat, Style)*

When you clobber an opponent with your fists, you inundate its mind with visions of madness and horror.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, Cha 11, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md), Knowledge (arcana) 4 ranks, ki pool class feature, nongood alignment.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional use of [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md) per day. When using this style, if you have at least 1 point in your ki pool, you increase the DCs of your confusion, madness, and insanity effects (including [Maddening Strike](maddening-strike.md)) by 1. Additionally, when you successfully stun an opponent with your Stunning Fist attack, your target takes 1 point of Wisdom damage.

**Special**: If you are using the sanity system, when you successfully stun an opponent with your Stunning Fist attack, the target takes 1d4 points of sanity damage instead of 1 point of Wisdom damage.
