# Escape Route

*(Teamwork)*

You have trained to watch your allies’ backs, covering them as they make tactical withdraws.

**Benefit**: You do not provoke attacks of opportunity when moving through spaces adjacent to allies.

**Category**: Trait

??? note "Original rule"
