# Slipslinger Bombardment

*(Combat)*

You can sling a rain of splash weapons at your enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md), [Slipslinger Grenadier](slipslinger-grenadier.md), [Slipslinger Style](slipslinger-style.md), [Throw Anything](throw-anything.md), Weapon Focus (sling) or weapon training (thrown) class feature, warslinger racial trait.

**Benefit**: You can use a sling to hurl alchemical splash weapons as ammunition with [Slipslinger Grenadier](slipslinger-grenadier.md) during an attack action or a full-attack action, loading your sling with such weapons as a free action.

**Special**: This feat can’t be used with alchemists’ bombs.
