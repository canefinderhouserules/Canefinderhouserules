# Treant's Call

You have learned some words of the mystic language treants use to call trees to their defense.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 17, ability to cast [*speak with plants*](../../magic/spells/speak-with-plants.md), character level 13th, gathlain.

**Benefit**: You can cast [*liveoak*](../../magic/spells/liveoak.md) once per day as a spell-like ability, but its duration is only 10 minutes.
