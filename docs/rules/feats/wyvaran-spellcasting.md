# Wyvaran Spellcasting

Your unique physiology makes you particularly adept at wielding draconic magic.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (arcana) 6 ranks, wyvaran.

**Benefit**: Select one spell you can cast that you gained through your draconic bloodline or a domain or subdomain with a draconic theme (such as the Scalykind domain or the Dragon subdomain). It must be at least 1 spell level lower than the highest-level spell you can cast. You can cast this spell as a spell-like ability twice per day.
