# Cultivate Magic Plants

*(Item Creation)*

You combine a natural green thumb and knowledge of magic in order to grow magic plants.

**Prerequisites**: Brew Potion, Craft Wondrous Item, Knowledge (nature) 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You can cultivate magic plants—see Chapter 7 for several example types of magic plants. Cultivating a magic plant takes 1 week per 1,000 gp in its base price. When you create a magic plant, you make the same choices that you would normally make when casting the spell. Whoever consumes the fruit of the magic plant is the target of the spell.
