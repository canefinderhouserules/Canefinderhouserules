# Indomitable Mountain Style

*(Combat, Style)*

Like a rugged mountain, you are impassable and unmovable.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: As long as you do not take a move action (1 AP), you gain a +4 morale bonus to your CMD and to the DC of [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) checks used for feint attempts against you. Additionally, you are considered to be one size category larger for the purpose of calculating your CMD when creatures attempt [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) checks to move through spaces you threaten without provoking attacks of opportunity.
