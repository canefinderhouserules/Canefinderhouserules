# Falling Water Gambit

You have integrated both the Roaring Falls and Shrouded Lake techniques of Noble swordplay into a seamless union.

**Prerequisites**: [Noble Dueling Disciple](noble-dueling-disciple.md), [Duelist of the Roaring Falls](duelist-of-the-roaring-falls.md), [Duelist of the Shrouded Lake](duelist-of-the-shrouded-lake.md), Exotic Weapon Proficiency (Noble dueling sword), [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus (Noble dueling sword), base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: When attacking a creature denied its Dexterity bonus to AC because of your successful feint, you increase the threat range of your Noble dueling sword by 1 (typically to 18–20/×2), and gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls to confirm critical hits. After you make a dueling dodge or succeed at a dueling parry during a duel, you can make an attack of opportunity against the foe who attacked you if that foe is [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked). To gain these benefits, you must be wielding only an Noble dueling sword (not using a shield, an off-hand weapon, armor spikes, unarmed strikes, or natural weapons).
