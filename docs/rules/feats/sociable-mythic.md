# Sociable (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your easy nature flows from you without effort, putting others at ease.

**Prerequisite**: [Sociable](sociable.md).

**Benefit**: You grant and benefit from the +2 bonus on [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) checks from Sociable constantly without having to spend a move action (1 AP). You can spend a move action (1 AP) to increase the bonus to +4 for a number of rounds equal to your tier.
