# Rapid Shot

*(Combat)*

You can make an additional ranged attack.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Point-Blank Shot](point-blank-shot.md).

**Benefit**: When making a full-attack action with a ranged weapon, you can fire one additional time this round at your highest bonus. All of your attack rolls take a –2 penalty when using Rapid Shot.

??? tip apr "Action Points (optional rule)"
    Spend 2 AP to make two ranged attacks, both at –2. A third ranged attack that turn takes –10 ([multiple attack penalty](../combat-variants/action-points.md#attacks)).
