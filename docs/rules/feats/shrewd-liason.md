# Shrewd Liason

Your dealings with both pirates and the Scarlet have taught you to be exceedingly careful.

**Prerequisites**: Mediogalti affinity.

**Benefit**: Opponents do not gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls for flanking you, although they can still sneak attack you. You also gain a +2 bonus on [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) checks made to resist a foe’s feinting in combat [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) checks.
