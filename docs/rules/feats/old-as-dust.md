# Old as Dust

You have been dead for so long you have become quite difficult to kill.

**Prerequisites**: [Sleeper](sleeper.md), [Warren Digger](warren-digger.md), creature has been a ghoul for at least 500 years.

**Benefit**: When you are reduced to 0 hit points, you are not destroyed—you cannot be killed unless your body is completely destroyed with fire or doused in holy water, or you take damage from a cure spell or channeled positive energy after you've been reduced to 0 hit points. Anytime you are reduced to 0 hit points, you become unconscious until you are healed or until the following midnight, at which time you regain 1 hit point per Hit Die and awaken.
