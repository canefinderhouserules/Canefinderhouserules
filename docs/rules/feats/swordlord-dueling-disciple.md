# Swordlord Dueling Disciple

*(Combat)*

Your pride and prowess in the art of formal swordplay grants you both bravery and bravado.

**Prerequisites**: Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (a light or one-handed slashing or piercing melee weapon that can be used with Weapon Finesse).

**Benefit**: Choose one weapon that meets the prerequisites; it is your dueling blade for all feats in this chain. You gain a +2 morale bonus on all Intimidate checks to demoralize opponents, and the DC of any attempt to demoralize you increases by 2. When you’re participating in a duel, these bonuses increase to +4.
