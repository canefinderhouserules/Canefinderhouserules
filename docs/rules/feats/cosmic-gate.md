# Cosmic Gate

You know that the stars shining in a certain demiplane's pools aren't reflections, but are gateways, and you can use this knowledge in your travels.

**Prerequisites**: [Starlit Guidance](starlit-guidance.md), Knowledge (planes) 10 ranks.

**Benefit**: Once per day, you can briefly enter a demiplane of stars and use the pools located there to instantly travel to a nearby location on the Material Plane; this functions as *teleport* except you are always considered very familiar with your destination.
