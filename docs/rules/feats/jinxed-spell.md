# Jinxed Spell

*(Metamagic)*

**Benefit**: Any creature that fails its saving throw against a jinxed spell also suffers the effects of your jinx. This feat has no effect on spells that do not allow a saving throw. If the spell affects an area or multiple creatures, you must select one creature to take the jinx, unless you have the [Area Jinx](area-jinx.md) feat; if you have the Area Jinx feat, choose a jinx burst area within the spell’s area or among its targets and apply the jinx to those in the burst that failed their save.

**Level Increase**: +0

**Category**: Trait
