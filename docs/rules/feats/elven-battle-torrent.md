# Elven Battle Torrent

*(Combat)*

You rapidly counter enemy attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, [Elven Battle Focus](elven-battle-focus.md), [Elven Battle Style](elven-battle-style.md), [Elven Battle Training](elven-battle-training.md), [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), base attack bonus +10, weapon familiarity racial trait.

**Benefit**: While using [Elven Battle Style](elven-battle-style.md), any opponent that makes an attack against you and misses while you are fighting defensively or using total defense or the [Combat Expertise](combat-expertise.md) feat provokes an attack of opportunity from you. Even if you have [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), you can’t benefit from this feat more than once per round.
