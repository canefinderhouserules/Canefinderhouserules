# Surprise Maneuver

Your maneuvers are devastating against disadvantaged foes.

**Prerequisites**: Combat Expertise; sneak attack +3d6 or studied strike +3d6.

**Benefit**: If you have sneak attack, when you attempt a combat maneuver check against a creature that you are flanking or that is denied its Dexterity bonus to AC against your attack, you gain a bonus on the combat maneuver check that's equal to your number of sneak attack dice. If you have the studied combat class feature, you can use studied strike on a combat maneuver check. When you do so, you gain a bonus on the combat maneuver check equal to your number of studied strike dice.
