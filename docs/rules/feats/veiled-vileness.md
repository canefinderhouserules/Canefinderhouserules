# Veiled Vileness

Your human blood shows itself dominant.

**Prerequisites**: Half-orc.

**Benefit**: You appear outwardly human, with no obvious signs of your orcish heritage (no [Disguise](../skills/disguise.md) check required). Your subtly menacing presence and strength grant a +1 bonus on [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) and [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks and Will saves.
