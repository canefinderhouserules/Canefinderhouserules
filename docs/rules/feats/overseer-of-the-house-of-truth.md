# Overseer of the House of Truth

*(Origin)*

Your creator was a politician and administrator, and your memories are filled with the sound of clinking coins.

**Prerequisites**: Shabti.

**Benefit**: You gain a second daily use of your [*suggestion*](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md) spell-like ability. You gain a third daily use at 8th level, a fourth at 13th level, and a fifth at 16th level.
