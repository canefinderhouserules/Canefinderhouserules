# Vast Spell

*(Metamagic)*

Your spells cover targets across a larger area.

**Prerequisites**: Caster level 3rd.

**Benefit**: You can alter a spell that targets a number of creatures no more than 30 feet apart, such as [*haste*](../../magic/spells/haste.md). The maximum distance apart of those you target with the spell increases to 60 feet.

**Level Increase**: +0

**Category**: Common
