# Dolphin Circle

*(Combat)*

You circle your foes like dolphins, forcing them to play into your attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Dolphin Dart, Dolphin Style, Improved Unarmed Strike, Swim 10 ranks, base attack bonus +10 or monk level 10th.

**Benefit**: While in Dolphin Style, as a full-round action underwater, choose a circular path starting and ending in your current space whose length is no longer than you could swim with a single move action. You circle that path until your next turn without provoking attacks of opportunity. You count as being in every space along the path for threatening squares, providing flanking for allies (but not yourself) and being in an area effect. Other creatures can attack you (and touch you with beneficial touch spells) as if you were in every space along the path, but you don't count as being in any of them for other creatures moving through occupied spaces. At the start of your next turn, choose any space along the path to stop circling.
