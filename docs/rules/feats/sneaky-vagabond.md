# Sneaky Vagabond

You have a well-practiced ability at going unnoticed.

**Benefit**: You get a +2 bonus on [Stealth](../skills/stealth.md) checks when others are actively trying to find you. In crowds, this bonus increases to +4. Additionally, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) (to gather information) and [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (local) checks when trying to find a place to hide.
