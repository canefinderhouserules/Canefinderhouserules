# Coven-Touched

You have a natural affinity for coven magic.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 11, changeling.

**Benefit**: You can join a changeling coven even if you are not a witch with the coven hex. Your effective witch level is equal to half your character level. A changeling coven must still include at least one changeling witch who has the coven hex. Additionally, select a single 0-level spell drawn from the witch's spell list. You gain the ability to cast this spell three times per day as a spell-like ability with a caster level equal to your character level. The save DC for this spell is 10 + your Intelligence modifier.
