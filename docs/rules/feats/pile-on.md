# Pile On

*(Combat)*

You can keep a foe shuddering in fear.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Hurtful](hurtful.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md).

**Benefit**: Once per round when you damage a creature that is [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked), you can choose to deal half your normal damage in order to extend the duration of its fear condition by 1 round.
