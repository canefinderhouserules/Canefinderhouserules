# Cleaver

*(Combat)*

You are ferocious at hewing your foes, even those a step apart.

**Prerequisites**: [Cleave](cleave.md), base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: One of the squares you choose to attack with Cleave may be non-adjacent to the others you choose, as long as it is no more than 1 square away from them.

You gain an extra +2 on attack rolls if you have a racial hatred ability against a specific race matching the race you attack with Cleave (for example, +2 against orcs if you have the dwarven hatred ability against orcs).

??? note "Original rule"

    *(Combat)*

    You are ferocious at hewing smaller opponents.

    **Prerequisites**: Str 13, Cleave, Power Attack, dwarf.

    **Benefit**: When using Cleave or Great Cleave, if your initial attack hits, you may take your additional attacks against any creature smaller than you that you threaten; your targets need not be adjacent to one another. Additional attacks you make against humanoids (goblinoid) gain a +2 circumstance bonus on attack rolls.
