# Favor of the Torrent Queen

You can unleash powerful watery torrents to push back your foes underwater.

**Prerequisites**: Worshiper of a deity of waters or pearl seeker paladin, character level 5th.

**Benefit**: Once per day while underwater, you can use [*hydraulic torrent*](../../magic/spells/hydraulic-torrent.md) as a spell-like ability. At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, you can use this ability an additional time each day.
