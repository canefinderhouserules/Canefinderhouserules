# Street Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You use the urban environment to your advantage, thrashing and bashing your foe and driving it back.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [Improved Bull Rush](improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md), base attack bonus +4 or monk level 3rd.

**Benefit**: While using this style, once per round as a swift action (1 AP) when you hit a target with an unarmed strike, you can deal an extra 1d6 points of damage and attempt a bull rush combat maneuver against that target.

**Special**: You can enter the style stance for street style only while in urban terrain, and the stance ends immediately if you cease to be in urban terrain.
