# Ready for Anything

*(Combat)*

You never let down your guard.

**Prerequisites**: [Alertness](alertness.md), [Improved Initiative](improved-initiative.md), [Lightning Reflexes](lightning-reflexes.md), [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md), base attack bonus +6 or uncanny dodge class feature.

**Benefit**: You can always act in the surprise round even if you fail a [Perception](../skills/perception.md) check to notice foes, but you are still considered [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) until you take an action.
