# Sly Draw

*(Combat)*

You can draw your weapon with such speed and finesse that your opponents don’t realize they’re threatened.

**Prerequisites**: [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md).

**Benefit**: When you draw a light weapon, you may make a [Sleight of Hand](../skills/sleight-of-hand.md) check instead of a [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) check to feint in combat. Other feats and abilities that affect a feint still apply to your feint.
