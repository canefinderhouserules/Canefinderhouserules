# Psychovore Master

*(Combat)*

When your unarmed attacks strike true, you also damage your target's mind and gain power from it.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Psychovore Strike](psychovore-strike.md), [Psychovore Style](psychovore-style.md), Sense Motive 15 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you score a critical hit against the creature you are gaining a dodge bonus to AC against from [Psychovore Style](psychovore-style.md) with your unarmed strike, that target also takes 1 point of Intelligence and Wisdom damage and you gain 2d8 temporary hit points. These temporary hit points last for 1 hour and don't stack with other temporary hit points you might gain with this feat.
