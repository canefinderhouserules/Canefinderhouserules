# Break Guard

*(Combat)*

You can use one of your two weapons to occupy your opponent’s defenses while attacking with the other.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Improved Disarm](improved-disarm.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md).

**Benefit**: While wielding two weapons, whenever you successfully use one weapon to disarm an opponent, you can spend a swift action (1 AP) to attack the opponent you attempted to disarm using your other weapon.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Dex 15, Int 13, Combat Expertise, Improved Disarm, Two-Weapon Fighting.
