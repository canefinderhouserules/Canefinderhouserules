# Anti-Divine Assault

*(Combat)*

You have trained to apply your knowledge of religions to predicting and defeating divine servitors.

**Prerequisites**: Bluff 1 rank, Knowledge (religion) 1 rank, must have been trained by an organization that opposes divine servitors.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on saving throws against divine spells and effects and abilities from outsiders. If you must attempt a saving throw against a divine spell, you gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls against the creature that cast the spell.
