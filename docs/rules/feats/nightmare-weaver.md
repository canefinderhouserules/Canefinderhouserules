# Nightmare Weaver

*(Combat)*

You can use your ability to create magical darkness to terrorize enemies.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Nightmare Fist](nightmare-fist.md), Intimidate 2 ranks, ability to cast darkness.

**Benefit**: By spending a full-round action to cast darkness, you can also make [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks to demoralize all foes in the spell’s initial area.

**Special**: This feat counts as [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md) for purposes of qualifying for [Deadly Stroke](deadly-stroke.md) and [Shatter Defenses](shatter-defenses.md).
