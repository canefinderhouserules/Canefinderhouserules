# Death Cult Disciple

Your research has gained the attention of a necromantic cult, which rewards you with access to its libraries.

**Prerequisites**: Associate (a necromantic death cult).

**Benefit**: You can exploit lore acquired from the cult once per day to combine your own blood and a vial of unholy water to create a *potion of cure light wounds* or a *potion of inflict light wounds* as a full-round action (3 AP). The blood loss from this transformation deals 1 point of Constitution damage to you. In addition, in any settlement that is the size of a small town or larger, you can always find scrolls of the following spells available for purchase regardless of their legality: [*animate dead*](../../magic/spells/animate-dead.md), [*control undead*](../../magic/spells/control-undead.md), [*create undead*](../../magic/spells/create-undead.md), [*detect undead*](../../magic/spells/detect-undead.md), [*disrupt undead*](../../magic/spells/disrupt-undead.md), [*false life*](../../magic/spells/false-life.md), *greater false life*, [*ghoul touch*](../../magic/spells/ghoul-touch.md), *lesser animate dead*, [*repair undead*](../../magic/spells/repair-undead.md), and *undead anatomy I–IV*.
