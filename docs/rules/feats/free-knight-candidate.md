# Free Knight Candidate

*(Achievement)*

You have proven your courage and skill to both the free knights and the leaders of your republic.

**Prerequisites**: Character level 5th, convince four different free knights and either a member of the republic’s ruling council or a town mayor to write letters of commendation on your behalf.

**Benefit**: Choose one branch of the free knights you hope to join. You gain a specific benefit based on this choice.

*Legion*: Each round as a move action, you may issue commands to all allies within 30 feet who can see and hear you. Each affected ally that obeys your orders gains a +1 bonus on attack rolls, Armor Class, and saving throws during that round.

*Envoys*: You deal +1 damage with weapon attacks directed at any creature against which you have concealment. If you have total concealment against the creature, this bonus increases to +2.

*Spies*: During the surprise round, the threat range of your weapons doubles for any attack made against unaware targets. This does not stack with any other effect that expands the threat range of a weapon.
