# Bestow Hope

You instill hope in the creatures you heal.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Channel](improved-channel.md), channel energy class feature, worshiper of a deity of healing and redemption.

**Benefit**: When you heal a creature by channeling positive energy, you also relieve its fear. If a creature you heal is [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), that condition ends. If the creature is [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), it becomes shaken instead. If the creature is [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked), it becomes frightened instead.
