# One Eye Open

Your senses seek out threats even while you sleep.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, [Alertness](alertness.md).

**Benefit**: The DCs of your [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks don’t increase when you are asleep. If you succeed at a Perception check to notice something dangerous while asleep, you can wake up to confront the danger.

**Normal**: The DC for a Perception check attempted by a sleeping creature increases by 10.
