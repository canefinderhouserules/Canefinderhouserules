# Deadly Stroke (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You can kill with a single stroke.

**Prerequisite**: Deadly Stroke.

**Benefit**: When making a Deadly Stroke attack, you can expend one use of mythic power to instantly kill a nonmythic living creature. A Fortitude save (DC 10 + your base attack bonus + your tier) negates this effect, though the attack still deals double damage and Con bleed as Deadly Stroke. Constructs, oozes, and creatures immune to critical hits or precision-based damage can't be killed with this feat.
