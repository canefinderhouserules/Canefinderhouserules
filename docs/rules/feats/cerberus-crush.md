# Cerberus Crush

*(Combat)*

When you strike from three directions, you channel deadly force into each blow.

**Prerequisites**: [Cerberus Snare](cerberus-snare.md), [Cerberus Style](cerberus-style.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.

**Benefit**: You can apply the benefits of [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md), and [Greater Vital Strike](greater-vital-strike.md) to all attacks made using [Cerberus Style](cerberus-style.md).
