# Scion of the Lost Empire

Though you might not be able to trace your lineage back to the drowned empire, you can feel the ancient power in your blood whenever you’re near others like yourself.

**Prerequisites**: Human (infernal imperial or old imperial).

**Benefit**: While you’re adjacent to an ethnically infernal imperial or old imperial ally who also has this feat, you gain a +2 competence bonus on a single attack roll, saving throw, ability check, or skill check as an immediate action. You must choose to use this ability before making the roll.
