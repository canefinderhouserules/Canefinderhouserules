# Enforcer

*(Combat)*

You are skilled at causing fear in those you brutalize.

**Prerequisite**: Intimidate 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Whenever you deal nonlethal damage with a melee weapon, you can make an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check to demoralize your target as a free action. If you are successful, the target is [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) for a number of rounds equal to the damage dealt. If your attack was a critical hit, your target is [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened) for 1 round with a successful Intimidate check, as well as being shaken for a number of rounds equal to the damage dealt.
