# Careful Reader

Your experience deciphering ancient scrolls and inscriptions has taught you proper caution and wariness.

**Prerequisites**: Linguistics 3 ranks, Spellcraft 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws against written magical effects, including [*explosive runes*](../../magic/spells/explosive-runes.md), [*glyph of warding*](../../magic/spells/glyph-of-warding.md), [*illusory script*](../../magic/spells/illusory-script.md), [*symbol of death*](../../magic/spells/symbol-of-death.md), and similar magic, as well as against any form of curse, madness, possession, or similar effect—magical or not—that might occur from reading a book, inscription, scroll, or any other form of writing.
