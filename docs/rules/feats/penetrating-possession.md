# Penetrating Possession

Your ability to possess foes can bypass magical protections.

**Prerequisites**: *Magic jar* as a known spell or spell-like ability, Cha 21.

**Benefit**:  The first time you attempt to possess a host under the effect of [*protection from evil*](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md) spell or any similar spell, you have a chance to dispel that spell as if you had cast [*dispel magic*](../../magic/spells/dispel-magic.md) on the spell.
