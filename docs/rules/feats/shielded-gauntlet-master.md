# Shielded Gauntlet Master

*(Combat)*

You can effectively attack and defend with your gauntlet.

**Prerequisites**: [Shielded Gauntlet Attack](shielded-gauntlet-attack.md), [Shield Gauntlet Style](shield-gauntlet-style.md), Weapon Focus (gauntlet or spiked gauntlet), proficiency with bucklers and light shields.

**Benefit**: While using [Shield Gauntlet Style](shield-gauntlet-style.md), you no longer lose your shield bonus to AC when you attack with your gauntlet (or spiked gauntlet) or use it to hold a weapon. In addition, you add your gauntlet’s enhancement bonus to the shield bonus to AC granted by this feat as if it were a shield enhancement bonus.
