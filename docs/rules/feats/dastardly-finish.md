# Dastardly Finish

*(Combat)*

You can take advantage of an enemy’s debilitated state to attempt a coup de grace.

**Prerequisite**: Sneak attack +5d6.

**Benefit**: You can deliver a coup de grace to [cowering](../appendices/conditions.md#cowering) or [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned) targets.

**Normal**: You can only coup de grace [helpless](../appendices/conditions.md#helpless) targets.
