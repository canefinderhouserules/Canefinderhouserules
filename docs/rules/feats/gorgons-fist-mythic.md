# Gorgon’s Fist (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your strikes debilitate and disorient your foes.

**Prerequisite**: Gorgon's Fist.

**Benefit**: When you hit a creature while using Gorgon's Fist, if the target fails its Fortitude saving throw, it is [dazed](../appendices/conditions.md#dazed) for 1 round instead of [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered). You can expend one use of mythic power when you hit with a Gorgon's Fist attack, but before the saving throw is attempted, to increase its DC by half your tier.
