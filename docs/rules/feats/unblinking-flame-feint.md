# Unblinking Flame Feint

You move as swiftly and unpredictably as a flickering flame.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13; Perfect Style; base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: While using Perfect Style, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Charisma modifier when attempting Bluff checks to feint. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action to gain the benefits of [Improved Feint](improved-feint.md) for 1 round.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Int 13; Wis 13; Combat Expertise; Perfect Style; base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.
