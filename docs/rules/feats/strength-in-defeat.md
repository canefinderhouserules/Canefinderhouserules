# Strength in Defeat

*(Combat)*

You tap into inner reserves, fighting even more fiercely when losing.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, half-elf of drow descent.

**Benefit**: When reduced to half of your maximum hit points or fewer, you gain a +1 morale bonus on attack and damage rolls. This bonus increases to +2 at 8th level and to +3 at 15th level.
