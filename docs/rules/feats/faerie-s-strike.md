# Faerie's Strike

*(Combat)*

Ephemeral wisps of nature spirits dance about those you strike.

**Prerequisites**: [Nature Magic](nature-magic.md) or the ability to cast druid or ranger spells; [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you use Vital Strike (or [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md) or [Greater Vital Strike](greater-vital-strike.md)), the creature you hit with the attack must succeed at a Will save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be illuminated as if by faerie fire for 1 minute. You can use this benefit a number of times per day equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1).
