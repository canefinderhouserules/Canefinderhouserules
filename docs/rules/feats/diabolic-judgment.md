# Diabolic Judgment

*(Combat)*

You punish your foes for every opportunity they present.

**Prerequisites**: [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), [Diabolic Humiliation](diabolic-humiliation.md), [Diabolic Style](diabolic-style.md), [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md) or Weapon Focus (unarmed strike), base attack bonus +12 or monk level 11th.

**Benefit**: While using [Diabolic Style](diabolic-style.md), the first time each round that you successfully hit with an attack of opportunity, you can apply the effects of any [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md) feat you have to that attack.

**Special**: A monk of at least 14th level with this feat can select [Greater Vital Strike](greater-vital-strike.md) as a monk bonus feat.
