# Smell Fear

You can catch the sour scent of fear on the wind.

**Prerequisites**: [Keen Scent](keen-scent.md), half-orc or orc.

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks to detect creatures with the [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked) conditions using scent. You can substitute your Perception skill for [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) skill checks if the subject has one of the previous conditions or is attempting to cover up their fear in some way.
