# Bolster Jinx

*(General)*

Your ability to resist certain effects somehow makes it even harder for creatures you jinx to do the same.

**Prerequisites**: [Great Fortitude](great-fortitude.md), [Iron Will](iron-will.md), or [Lightning Reflexes](lightning-reflexes.md); Halfling Jinx racial trait.

**Benefit**: A jinxed target's penalty on Fortitude, Reflex, or Will saves increases by 2 if you have the [Great Fortitude](great-fortitude.md), Lightning Reflexes, or [Iron Will](iron-will.md) feat, respectively. If you have more than one of these feats, the additional penalties apply to all of the corresponding saves.
