# Mantis Style

*(Combat, Style)*

You have learned to target vital areas with crippling accuracy.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md), Heal 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md) attempt per day. While using this style, you gain a +2 bonus to the DC of effects you deliver with your Stunning Fist.
