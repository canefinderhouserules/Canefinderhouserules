# Detect Expertise (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You can sense a creature's magical abilities with just a mere glance.

**Prerequisite**: [Detect Expertise](detect-expertise.md).

**Benefit**: You gain the benefit of [Detect Expertise](detect-expertise.md) immediately (without needing to observe a creature for 3 rounds) when using the divinations listed in Detect Expertise. Non-mythic creatures cannot attempt a Will save to resist this effect.
