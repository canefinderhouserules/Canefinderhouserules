# Beastmaster Salvation

*(Combat)*

Your presence grants your companion the toughness and will to resist all manner of threats.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, [Alertness](alertness.md), [Beastmaster Style](beastmaster-style.md), Handle Animal 5 ranks, Sense Motive 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: If you are adjacent to your animal companion when it attempts a saving throw, you can attempt a [Handle Animal](../skills/handle-animal.md) check as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn). Your animal companion does not attempt the saving throw, but instead uses your Handle Animal result as its result for the save.
