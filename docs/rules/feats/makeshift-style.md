# Makeshift Style

*(Combat, Style)*

Your skill with improvised weapons makes them more dangerous than they would otherwise be.

**Prerequisites**: Catch Off-Guard or Throw Anything.

**Benefit**: While in this style, you deal damage with improvised weapons as if they were one size category larger. For every style feat you have that lists Makeshift Style as a prerequisite, treat the improvised weapon as an additional size category larger, to a maximum of three sizes larger than its actual size.
