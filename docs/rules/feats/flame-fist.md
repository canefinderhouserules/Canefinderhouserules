# Flame Fist

*(Combat, Style)*

You can exploit the weaknesses in your opponents’ defenses, like a flame seeking fuel to consume.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md), [Flame Feint](flame-feint.md), base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.

**Benefit**: When using [Elemental Discipline Style](elemental-discipline-style.md), the save DC of your stunning fist attack increases by 2 against [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) targets. When you spend 1 ki point to gain the benefits of [Improved Feint](improved-feint.md) for 1 round, you may attempt a feint as part of your movement when charging.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Int 13, Wis 13, Combat Expertise, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), Stunning Fist, Flame Feint, base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.
