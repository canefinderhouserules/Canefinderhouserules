# Flame Fist

*(Combat, Style)*

You can exploit the weaknesses in your opponents’ defenses, like a flame seeking fuel to consume.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, Wis 13, Combat Expertise, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), Stunning Fist, Flame Feint, base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.

**Benefit**: When using Elemental Discipline Style, the save DC of your stunning fist attack increases by 2 against flat-footed targets. When you spend 1 ki point to gain the benefits of Improved Feint for 1 round, you may attempt a feint as part of your movement when charging.
