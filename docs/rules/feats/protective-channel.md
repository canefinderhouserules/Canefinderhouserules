# Protective Channel

When you channel energy, your deity grants protection against dark forces.

**Prerequisites**: Channel energy 7d6, worshiper of a deity of valor and justice.

**Benefit**: When you channel positive energy to heal living creatures, you can choose to affect healed creatures with a [*protection from evil*](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md) spell (caster level equal to your effective cleric level).
