# Osiriontologist

You are well schooled in the traditions, culture, and history of the desert kingdom, especially the broad expanse of its long history and ancient relics.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (history) 4 ranks, Knowledge (local) 4 ranks, Speak Language (the desert tongue, Ancient the desert tongue).

**Benefit**: When in the desert kingdom, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md), [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md), [Disguise](../skills/disguise.md), Gather Information, [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md), and [Perform](../skills/perform.md) checks. You gain a +5 bonus when using [Appraise](../skills/appraise.md), Decipher Script, [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (all skills), and Search to learn about a person, place, or item of ancient the desert kingdom.
