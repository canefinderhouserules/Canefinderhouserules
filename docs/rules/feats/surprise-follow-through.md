# Surprise Follow-Through

*(Combat)*

When striking one opponent, you catch its ally off guard.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Cleave](cleave.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md), base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: When using Cleave or [Great Cleave](great-cleave.md), the second foe you attack on your turn is denied its Dexterity bonus against you.
