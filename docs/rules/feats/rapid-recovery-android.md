# Rapid Recovery

*(Android)*

When you heal damage with your nanite surge, you also remove harmful effects.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13, [Rapid Repair](rapid-repair.md), nanite surge racial trait.

**Benefit**: When you heal damage using [Rapid Repair](rapid-repair.md), you remove any and all of the following conditions: [blinded](../appendices/conditions.md#blinded), [confused](../appendices/conditions.md#confused), [dazzled](../appendices/conditions.md#dazzled), [deafened](../appendices/conditions.md#deafened), [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken), [sickened](../appendices/conditions.md#sickened), and [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered). You cannot cure blindness or deafness caused by loss of sensory organs.
