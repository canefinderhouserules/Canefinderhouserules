# Relic Breaker

*(Combat)*

You are furious when destroying dangerous objects.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, Gate Breaker, Improved Sunder, Power Attack, base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: Whenever you sunder a held object, its bearer must succeed at a Reflex saving throw or catch on fire (taking 1d6 points of fire damage immediately, and an additional 1d6 points of fire damage each subsequent round the creature fails a Reflex saving throw). When your total Hit Dice reach 5, this damage increases by 1d6, and it increases by 1d6 every 5 levels thereafter, to a maximum of 5d6 at 20th level. The saving throw's DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your Hit Dice + your Charisma modifier.
