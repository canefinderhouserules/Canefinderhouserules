# Deadly Aim (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your aim is truly devastating.

**Prerequisite**: [Deadly Aim](deadly-aim.md).

**Benefit**: When you use [Deadly Aim](deadly-aim.md), you gain a +3 bonus on ranged damage rolls instead of +2. When your base attack bonus reaches +4, and every +4 thereafter, the bonus on ranged damage rolls increases by +3 rather than +2.
