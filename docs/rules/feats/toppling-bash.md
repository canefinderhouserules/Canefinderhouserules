# Toppling Bash

*(Shield Mastery)*

Your shield bash throws your enemies off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Shield Bash](improved-shield-bash.md), [Shield Focus](shield-focus.md), [Stumbling Bash](stumbling-bash.md), base attack bonus +11 or fighter level 8th.

**Benefit**: As a swift action when you hit a creature with a shield bash, you can attempt a trip combat maneuver against that creature at a –5 penalty. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity, and if your attempt fails by 10 or more, you are not knocked [prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone).
