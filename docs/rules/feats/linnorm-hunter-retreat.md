# Linnorm Hunter Retreat

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

You and your animal companion have learned to cover each other in combat.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Dex 13, [Dodge](dodge.md), [Linnorm Hunter Style](linnorm-hunter-style.md), base attack bonus +4, must have or be an animal companion.

**Benefit**: When an ally with this feat uses Linnorm Hunter Style and hits a target with a melee attack, you can move 5 feet as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) without provoking an attack of opportunity from that target.

**Special**: Animal companions with an Intelligence score of 1 or 2 can take this feat.
