# Close Call

Your nimble fingers are able to catch small slips before they become big disasters.

**Prerequisites**: [Deft Hands](deft-hands.md).

**Benefit**: Once per day, you may reroll either a [Disable Device](../skills/disable-device.md) or [Sleight of Hand](../skills/sleight-of-hand.md) check. You must decide to use this ability after the first attempt but before the results are revealed by the GM. You must take the second roll, even if it’s worse.
