# Leapfrog

*(Combat, Performance)*

You can use an opponent as a springboard.

**Prerequisites**: Acrobatics 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: When you spend a swift action (1 AP) to attempt a performance combat check after a successful charge attack or combat maneuver, you gain a +2 bonus on your performance combat check and gain a +4 bonus on your next [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md), [Climb](../skills/climb.md), [Fly](../skills/fly.md), [Ride](../skills/ride.md), or [Swim](../skills/swim.md) check attempted before the end of your next turn.
