# Persuasive Bribery

You know how to get the most out of bribes.

**Prerequisite**: [Persuasive](persuasive.md).

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 bonus on [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) checks when attempting to bribe someone and on Diplomacy or Charisma checks to bargain with a conjured creature (such as with [*planar ally*](../../magic/spells/planar-ally.md) or [*planar binding*](../../magic/spells/planar-binding.md)), in addition to any bonus granted by the bribe itself. The first time someone refuses a bribe you offer, that person’s attitude toward you doesn’t worsen, even if the offer would normally offend the person.
