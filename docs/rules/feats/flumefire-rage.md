# Flumefire Rage

Your meditation before a rune-engraved stone unlocks the secret of its blazing flame.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 15; bloodrage class feature or elemental focus class feature.

**Benefit**: When casting an evocation spell that deals fire damage, you can gain a +1 enhancement bonus to fire damage per die rolled. If you do, you must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 15 + the spell or blast’s level) or become [fatigued](../appendices/conditions.md#fatigued). You cannot use this feat while fatigued or if you cannot become fatigued.

If you are raging (such as when using bloodrage or affected by the [*rage*](../../magic/spells/rage.md) spell), this bonus increases to +2 per die rolled and you roll twice when attempting the Fortitude save and take the better result. This feat does not otherwise grant you the ability to cast spells while raging.
