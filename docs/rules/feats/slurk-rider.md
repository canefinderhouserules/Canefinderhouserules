# Slurk Rider

*(Combat)*

You are skilled at riding slurks into battle.

**Prerequisites**: Kobold.

**Benefit**: You are able to control, guide, and communicate with slurks as if they understood Draconic. You can also spend a standard action to manipulate the nodules on the slurk's back to activate its slime ability. Both you and the slurk can activate this ability during the same round.
