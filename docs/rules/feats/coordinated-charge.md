# Coordinated Charge

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

You are an expert at leading your allies into the fray.

**Prerequisites**: At least two other teamwork feats, base attack bonus +8.

**Benefit**: When an ally with this feat charges a creature that is no further away from you than your speed, you can, as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn), charge that creature. You must be able to follow all of the normal charge rules.

**Category**: Powerful
