# Medusa’s Wrath

*(Combat)*

You can take advantage of your opponent’s confusion, delivering multiple blows.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Gorgon’s Fist](gorgons-fist.md), [Scorpion Style](scorpion-style.md), base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use the full-attack action and make at least one unarmed strike, you can make two additional unarmed strikes at your highest base attack bonus. These bonus attacks must be made against a [dazed](../appendices/conditions.md#dazed), [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed), [paralyzed](../appendices/conditions.md#paralyzed), [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered), [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned), or [unconscious](../appendices/conditions.md#unconscious) foe.
