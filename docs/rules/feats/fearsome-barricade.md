# Fearsome Barricade

*(Combat)*

Your wrath is as potent a barrier as your blades.

**Prerequisites**: Intimidate 5 ranks, member of the northern royal guard.

**Benefit**: Choose an adjacent ally as a free action on your turn. Once per round, when an opponent attacks that ally, you may make an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check to demoralize the opponent as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn). This action counts as an attack of opportunity.
