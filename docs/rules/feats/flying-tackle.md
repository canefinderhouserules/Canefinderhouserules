# Flying Tackle

You quickly bring down an off-balanced opponent.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Overrun](improved-overrun.md).

**Benefit**: When attempting an overrun combat maneuver check against a [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) opponent, if the attempt knocks the creature [prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone), you can also fall prone in order to attempt a grapple combat maneuver check against the creature as a free action. Doing so does not provoke an attack of opportunity from the target of your grapple.
