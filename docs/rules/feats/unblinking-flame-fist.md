# Unblinking Flame Fist

You can exploit the weaknesses in your opponents’ defenses, like a flame seeking fuel to consume.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13; Perfect Style; [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md); [Unblinking Flame Feint](unblinking-flame-feint.md); base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.

**Benefit**: When using Perfect Style, the save DC of your stunning fist attack increases by 2 against [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) targets. When you spend 1 ki point to gain the benefits of [Improved Feint](improved-feint.md) for 1 round, you can attempt a feint as part of your movement when charging.
