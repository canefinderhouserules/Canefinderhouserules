# Sap Master

*(Combat)*

You knock the sense out of foes with a well-timed surprise attack.

**Prerequisites**: Sneak attack +3d6, Sap Adept.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use a bludgeoning weapon to deal nonlethal sneak attack damage to a f lat-footed opponent, roll your sneak attack dice twice, totaling the results as your nonlethal sneak attack damage for that attack.
