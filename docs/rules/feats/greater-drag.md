# Greater Drag

*(Combat)*

Foes that you drag are thrown out of balance.

**Prerequisites**: [Powerful Maneuvers](powerful-maneuvers.md), Str 13, [Improved Drag](improved-drag.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md), base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You receive a +2 bonus on checks made to drag a foe. This bonus stacks with the bonus granted by [Improved Drag](improved-drag.md). Whenever you drag a foe, his movement provokes attacks of opportunity from all of your allies (but not you).

**Normal**: Creatures moved by drag do not provoke attacks of opportunity.
