# Steam Spell

*(Metamagic)*

You use the closely guarded secrets of steam casters, allowing you to use fire magic underwater easily.

**Prerequisites**: None.

**Benefit**: You can apply Steam Spell only to a spell with the fire descriptor. A steam spell works underwater without a caster level check, but requires a caster level check to function above water. A steam spell uses up a spell slot of the spell's actual level.
