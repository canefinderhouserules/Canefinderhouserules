# Greater Weapon of the Chosen

*(Combat)*

Your deity guides your hand when you fight with her favored weapon.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Weapon of the Chosen](improved-weapon-of-the-chosen.md), Weapon Focus with deity's favored weapon, [Weapon of the Chosen](weapon-of-the-chosen.md), worship and receive spells from a deity.

**Benefit**: When you use your deity's favored weapon to attempt a single attack with the attack action, you roll two dice for your attack roll and take the higher result. You do not need to use your [Weapon of the Chosen](weapon-of-the-chosen.md) feat to gain this feat's benefit.
