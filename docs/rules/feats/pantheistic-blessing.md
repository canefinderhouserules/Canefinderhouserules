# Pantheistic Blessing

You are granted a small bit of divine power as a result of your veneration of a pantheon of deities.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, must worship a pantheon of deities.

**Benefit**: When taking this feat, select a pantheon from the list below. You gain the listed spell-like ability for your selected pantheon, usable once per day. If the granted spell allows a saving throw, the DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Charisma modifier.

| Pantheon | Granted spell-like ability |
|---|---|
| Archdevils (a group of lawful evil rulers of Hell) | *infernal healing* |
| Ascended mortals (heroes who attained godhood) | *divine favor* |
| Demon lords | *lesser confusion* |
| Dwarven pantheon | *crofter's fortune* |
| Elven pantheon | *identify* |
| Empyreal lords | *protection from evil* |
| First World lords (fey rulers) | *silent image* |
| Lawful pantheon (lawful deities united in one order) | *protection from chaos* |
| Small-town cultural pantheon | *sanctuary* |
| National cultural pantheon | *shield of faith* |
| Vast distant-land pantheon (thousands of deities) | *true strike* |

**Special**: Single-class clerics gain their powers through allegiance to a single deity, and therefore cannot select this feat. If you act out of line with the tenets of your pantheon's beliefs, you lose the ability to cast the granted spell-like ability until you have received an *atonement* for your transgression (as a cleric restoring lost class abilities).
