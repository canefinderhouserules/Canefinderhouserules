# Pantheistic Blessing

You are granted a small bit of divine power as a result of your veneration of a pantheon of deities.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, must worship a pantheon of deities.

**Benefit**: When taking this feat, select a pantheon from the list below. You gain the listed spell-like ability for your selected pantheon, usable once per day. If the granted spell allows a saving throw, the DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Charisma modifier.

| Pantheon | Granted spell-like ability |
|---|---|
| Archdevils (a group of lawful evil rulers of Hell) | [*infernal healing*](../../magic/spells/infernal-healing.md) |
| Ascended mortals (heroes who attained godhood) | [*divine favor*](../../magic/spells/divine-favor.md) |
| Demon lords | [*lesser confusion*](../../magic/spells/confusion-lesser.md) |
| Dwarven pantheon | *crofter's fortune* |
| Elven pantheon | [*identify*](../../magic/spells/identify.md) |
| Empyreal lords | [*protection from evil*](../../magic/spells/protection-from-evil.md) |
| First World lords (fey rulers) | [*silent image*](../../magic/spells/silent-image.md) |
| Lawful pantheon (lawful deities united in one order) | [*protection from chaos*](../../magic/spells/protection-from-chaos.md) |
| Small-town cultural pantheon | [*sanctuary*](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md) |
| National cultural pantheon | [*shield of faith*](../../magic/spells/shield-of-faith.md) |
| Vast distant-land pantheon (thousands of deities) | [*true strike*](../../magic/spells/true-strike.md) |

**Special**: Single-class clerics gain their powers through allegiance to a single deity, and therefore cannot select this feat. If you act out of line with the tenets of your pantheon's beliefs, you lose the ability to cast the granted spell-like ability until you have received an [*atonement*](../../magic/spells/atonement.md) for your transgression (as a cleric restoring lost class abilities).
