# Greater Serpent Lash

*(Combat)*

Your ability to disarm and pull foes off balance with your whip is impressive.

**Prerequisites**: [Serpent Lash](serpent-lash.md), base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: When you make a successful disarm or trip maneuver using the [Serpent Lash](serpent-lash.md) feat, you can make your additional disarm or trip attempt against any target within your whip’s reach. With a reposition maneuver, you can move the target creature to any point within your whip’s reach.
