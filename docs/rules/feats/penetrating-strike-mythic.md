# Penetrating Strike (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You know just how to strike to bypass even the most heavily armored foe's defenses.

**Prerequisite**: [Penetrating Strike](penetrating-strike.md).

**Benefit**: When using [Penetrating Strike](penetrating-strike.md) or [Greater Penetrating Strike](greater-penetrating-strike.md), you can ignore an additional point of damage reduction for every 3 tiers you posses. Additionally, the effects apply to damage reduction without a type (such as DR 10/-).
