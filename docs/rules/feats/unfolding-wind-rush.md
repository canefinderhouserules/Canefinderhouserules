# Unfolding Wind Rush

Thrown weapons fly from your hands as if blown by a gale, and you leave behind a wake of wind as you move.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13; Wis 13; Dodge; Mobility; Perfect Style; [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md); [Unfolding Wind Strike](unfolding-wind-strike.md); base attack bonus +13 or monk level 13th.

**Benefit**: When using Perfect Style and wielding thrown weapons, you can combine a full-attack action with a single move action (1 AP). You must forgo the attack at your highest bonus but can take the remaining attacks as normal at any point during your movement. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action (1 AP) to create a path of wind along your path of movement that functions as [*wind wall*](../../magic/spells/wind-wall.md) for 1 round; this effect does not affect your ranged attacks.
