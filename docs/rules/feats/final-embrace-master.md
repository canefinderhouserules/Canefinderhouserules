# Final Embrace Master

*(Combat)*

Few creatures can survive the crushing horror of your [Final Embrace](final-embrace.md).

**Prerequisites**: Str 17, Int 3; naga, serpentfolk, or creature that has the constrict special attack; Final Embrace; [Final Embrace Horror](final-embrace-horror.md); base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Double the number of damage dice for your constrict special attack.
