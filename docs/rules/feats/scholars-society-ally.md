# Scholars' Society Ally

A society of explorers and scholars grants you access to its archives in thanks for services previously rendered.

**Prerequisites**: Associate (a scholars' society).

**Benefit**: The society's vast archives are available for you to exploit. In any settlement that is the size of a small town or larger, you can spend 1d4 hours researching notes from available scholars to gain a +4 circumstance bonus on a single Knowledge check.
