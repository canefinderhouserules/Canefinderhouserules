# Wind Strike

*(Combat, Style)*

The wind through which your thrown weapons fly directs them to their target or back to your hand.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Wis 13, Elemental Discipline Style (wind), [Quick Draw](quick-draw.md), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: When using [Elemental Discipline Style](elemental-discipline-style.md), you double the range increment of any thrown weapon you wield. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action (1 AP) to imbue a number of thrown weapons equal to your Wisdom bonus with the returning or seeking weapon special ability for 1 round.
