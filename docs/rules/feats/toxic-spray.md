# Toxic Spray

*(Combat)*

You produce enough venom to cast a web of poison over your hapless victims.

**Prerequisites**: [Spit Venom](spit-venom.md), [Viscous Venom](viscous-venom.md), nagaji.

**Benefit**: When you spit poison using the ability granted by the [Spit Venom](spit-venom.md) feat, you can envenom a 5-foot-radius area within 30 feet for 1 round. Any creature that enters or begins its turn in this area is both exposed to the poison granted by Spit Venom and [entangled](../appendices/conditions.md#entangled) as if you had targeted that creature with the ability granted by the [Viscous Venom](viscous-venom.md) feat. In addition, you gain one additional use per day of the ability to spit poison granted by the Spit Venom feat.
