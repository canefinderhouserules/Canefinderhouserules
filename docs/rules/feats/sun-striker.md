# Sun Striker

*(Combat, Critical)*

Called by the dawn to strike down the undead, your weapon becomes an agent of burning will.

**Prerequisites**: Critical Focus, Weapon Focus (deity’s favored weapon), character level 7th, worshiper of a deity of the sun.

**Benefit**: Whenever you confirm a critical hit against an undead creature, your attack bypasses all DR, deals full damage to incorporeal creatures, and acts as though it has the *flaming burst* weapon special ability.
