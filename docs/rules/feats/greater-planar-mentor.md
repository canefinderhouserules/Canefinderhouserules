# Greater Planar Mentor

Your mentor begins to look upon you as an equal and can even answer your call.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Planar Mentor](improved-planar-mentor.md), [Planar Mentor](planar-mentor.md), character level 13th.

**Benefit**: You can benefit from your [Planar Mentor](planar-mentor.md) feat one additional time per day. You add the following to the list of spell-like abilities you can cast using [Improved Planar Mentor](improved-planar-mentor.md) for their respective alignments, each of which affects only a single target within 30 feet: [*blasphemy*](../../magic/spells/blasphemy.md) (evil), [*dictum*](../../magic/spells/dictum.md) (lawful), [*holy word*](../../magic/spells/holy-word.md) (good), [*slay living*](../../magic/spells/slay-living.md) (neutral), and [*word of chaos*](../../magic/spells/word-of-chaos.md) (chaotic). Once per week you can cast *greater planar ally* as a spell-like ability, but only to conjure your planar mentor. Your mentor requires only half the normal payment for its services.
