# Peacemaker

Your words of peace ring true and are much more difficult for others to resist.

**Prerequisites**: Charisma 13, good alignment.

**Benefit**: The DC to resist spells you cast to ensure peace or force aggressive creatures to become peaceful increases by +2. This affects spells that dissuade creatures from aggressive actions without exerting long-term or absolute control over them, and without leaving them defenseless. These spells include, but are not limited to, [*calm animals*](../../magic/spells/calm-animals.md), [*calm emotions*](../../magic/spells/calm-emotions.md), [*command*](../../magic/spells/command.md), [*compassionate ally*](../../magic/spells/compassionate-ally.md), [*enthrall*](../../magic/spells/enthrall.md), [*euphoric tranquility*](../../magic/spells/euphoric-tranquility.md), [*sanctuary*](../../magic/spells/sanctuary.md), and [*serenity*](../../magic/spells/serenity.md).
