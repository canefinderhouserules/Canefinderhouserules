# Insightful Gaze

In your personal interactions, you notice what others don’t. It’s hard to get anything past you.

**Prerequisites**: Stern gaze class feature, Sense Motive 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: Whenever you make a [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) check to oppose someone's [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) check, you can roll two dice and take the higher result.
