# Shifter’s Edge

You use your shapechanging powers to make your natural attacks especially lethal.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), shifter claws class feature.

**Benefit**: Whenever you use [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md) to make a melee attack with your claws or a natural attack augmented by your claws, you also add your shifter level to the damage.
