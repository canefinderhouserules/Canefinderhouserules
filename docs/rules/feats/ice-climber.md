# Ice Climber

You can move or clamber across slippery surfaces with great skill.

**Prerequisites**: [Arctic Adaptation](arctic-adaptation.md) or Mountaineer; Climb 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: When moving across a slippery surface (including but not limited to icy surfaces), you gain a +5 bonus on [Climb](../skills/climb.md) checks and on [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) checks to maintain your balance. If you fail a Climb check, you fall only if you fail the check by 10 or more. You also gain a +5 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks to notice an avalanche and a +2 circumstance bonus on Reflex saves to avoid an avalanche.
