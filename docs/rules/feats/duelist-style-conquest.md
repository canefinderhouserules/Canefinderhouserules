# Duelist Style Conquest

*(Combat, Style)*

Defense is now second nature to you, and your blade punishes those who try to attack and fail.

**Prerequisites**: [Duelist Style](duelist-style.md), [Duelist Style Aegis](duelist-style-aegis.md), [Alertness](alertness.md), [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), Exotic Weapon Proficiency (dueling sword), [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus (dueling sword).

**Benefit**: When you attempt to parry an attack using [Duelist Style Aegis](duelist-style-aegis.md), you do not take any penalties on the attack roll incurred from using [Combat Expertise](combat-expertise.md) or fighting defensively.
