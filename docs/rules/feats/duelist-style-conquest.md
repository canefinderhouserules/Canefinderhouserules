# Duelist Style Conquest

*(Combat, Style)*

Defense is now second nature to you, and your blade punishes those who try to attack and fail.

**Prerequisites**: Duelist Style, Duelist Style Aegis, Alertness, Combat Reflexes, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (dueling sword), Weapon Finesse, Weapon Focus (dueling sword).

**Benefit**: When you attempt to parry an attack using Duelist Style Aegis, you do not take any penalties on the attack roll incurred from using Combat Expertise or fighting defensively.
