# Improved Uncivilized Tactics

*(Combat)*

You are skilled at twisting your foe's limbs out of joint.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Power Attack](power-attack.md), [Uncivilized Tactics](uncivilized-tactics.md).

**Benefit**: You don't provoke an attack of opportunity when attempting a combat maneuver check to use [Uncivilized Tactics](uncivilized-tactics.md). In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on such checks.

**Normal**: You provoke an attack of opportunity when using the Uncivilized Tactics feat.
