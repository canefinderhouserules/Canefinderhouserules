# Pinch Time

Often having one parent with a human lifespan and one with an elven lifespan, many half-elves have a skewed perception of time, and none more so than half-elves of tower-elf descent, who also study the subject of time academically.

**Prerequisites**: Character level 5th, half-elf of tower-elf descent.

**Benefit**: Once per day, as a standard action, you can gain the benefits of *haste* until the end of your next turn.
