# Spell Focus (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your magical specialties are particularly effective.

**Prerequisite**: Spell Focus.

**Benefit**: Choose a school of magic you already have Spell Focus in. The bonus to save DCs provided by Spell Focus and Greater Spell Focus for that school increases by L You can expend one use of mythic power as part of casting a spell from your chosen school to force any of the spell's targets to roll their saving throws twice, taking the lower result.

**Special**: You can gain this feat multiple times. The effects do not stack. Each time you take this feat, it applies to a different non-mythic Spell Focus feat.
