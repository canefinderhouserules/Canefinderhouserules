# Spell Mastery (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You're so familiar with certain spells you can prepare them with the greatest of ease.

**Prerequisite**: [Spell Mastery](spell-mastery.md).

**Benefit**: You can prepare spells you have selected with [Spell Mastery](spell-mastery.md) as a full-round action (3 AP). You must spend the appropriate amount of time preparing other spells you have not mastered. As a full-round action (3 AP), you can expend one use of mythic power to prepare all your spells that you have selected with Spell Mastery.
