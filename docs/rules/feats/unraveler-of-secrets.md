# Unraveler of Secrets

You have a knack for learning what others would like to remain hidden.

**Prerequisites**: Int 15, Knowledge (history) 1 rank, half-orc of underworld descent.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (history) checks. In addition, you can cast [*object reading*](../../magic/spells/object-reading.md) once per day as a spell-like ability, attempting a Knowledge (history) check in place of an [Appraise](../skills/appraise.md) check.
