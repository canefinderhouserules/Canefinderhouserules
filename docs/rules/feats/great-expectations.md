# Great Expectations

Burdened with heavy responsibility at a young age, you must mature faster than most.

**Prerequisites**: Half-orc of jungle descent, skilled alternate racial trait.

**Benefit**: Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma when you take this feat. This choice cannot be changed. As an immediate action a number of times per day equal to your modifier in the chosen ability score, you can gain a +2 circumstance bonus on any skill check that uses the chosen ability score. You can use this ability after rolling, but must use it before the results of the roll are declared.
