# Cudgeler Takedown

*(Combat, Style)*

Opponents you batter to the ground stay down.

**Prerequisites**: [Bludgeoner](bludgeoner.md), [Cudgeler Style](cudgeler-style.md), [Cudgeler Sweep](cudgeler-sweep.md), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: When you hit with a charge attack using the [Cudgeler Style](cudgeler-style.md) feat, your weapon deals damage as though it were one size category larger for 1 round or until it leaves your hand. When you successfully trip an opponent with the [Cudgeler Sweep](cudgeler-sweep.md) feat, your opponent must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your character level + your Strength modifier) or be [stunned 1](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned) for 1 round.
