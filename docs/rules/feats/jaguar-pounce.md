# Jaguar Pounce

*(Combat)*

Your ambushes are especially lethal.

**Prerequisite**: Base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: When making a charge or using [Spring Attack](spring-attack.md) against an opponent who is [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) or [helpless](../appendices/conditions.md#helpless), you treat your first melee attack against that target as if you had the [Improved Critical](improved-critical.md) feat.
