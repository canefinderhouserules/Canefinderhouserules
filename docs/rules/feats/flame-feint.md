# Flame Feint

*(Combat, Style)*

You move as swiftly and unpredictably as a flickering flame, throwing your opponents off guard.

**Prerequisites**: Int 13, Wis 13, Combat Expertise, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: When using Elemental Discipline Style, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Charisma modifier when attempting Bluff checks to feint. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action to gain the benefits of Improved Feint for 1 round.
