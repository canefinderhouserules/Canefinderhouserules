# Flame Feint

*(Combat, Style)*

You move as swiftly and unpredictably as a flickering flame, throwing your opponents off guard.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.

**Benefit**: When using [Elemental Discipline Style](elemental-discipline-style.md), you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Charisma modifier when attempting [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) checks to feint. You can spend 1 ki point as a swift action (1 AP) to gain the benefits of [Improved Feint](improved-feint.md) for 1 round.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Int 13, Wis 13, Combat Expertise, Elemental Discipline Style (flame), base attack bonus +9 or monk level 9th.
