# Monster Spotter

Your knowledge of strange life forms makes you adept at piercing their disguises and detecting their hiding places.

**Prerequisites**: Skill Focus (Knowledge [any]), Knowledge (any) 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can substitute a [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) skill for opposed [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks against creatures’ [Disguise](../skills/disguise.md) and [Stealth](../skills/stealth.md) checks, and for [Survival](../skills/survival.md) checks to track creatures. You must have 3 ranks in the Knowledge skill that corresponds to the creature’s type.
