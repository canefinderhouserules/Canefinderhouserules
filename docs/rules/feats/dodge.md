# Dodgility

*(Combat)*

Your training and reflexes let you avoid attacks and move safely through a dangerous melee. This feat combines Dodge and Mobility.

**Prerequisite**: Dex 13.

**Benefit**: You gain a +1 dodge bonus to your AC, and a +4 dodge bonus to AC against attacks of opportunity caused when you move out of or within a threatened area. A condition that makes you lose your Dex bonus to AC also makes you lose the benefits of this feat.
