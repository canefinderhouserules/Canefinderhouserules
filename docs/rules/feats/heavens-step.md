# Heaven’s Step

*(Combat)*

Like a daring offensive championed by a prince of an old empire, you confidently take the fight to your enemy and strike an unexpected blow.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Following Step](following-step.md), [Step Up](step-up.md), [Step Up and Strike](step-up-and-strike.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md), base attack bonus +10.

**Benefit**: When you make an attack using [Step Up and Strike](step-up-and-strike.md), you can also make an extra attack with your offhand weapon using your highest base attack bonus. Both attacks take the normal penalties for two-weapon fighting.
