# Rending Summons

You have learned to make your summons destructive to the fabric of the planes, bringing black flame in the wake of your magic.

**Prerequisites**: [Sacred Summons](sacred-summons.md).

**Benefit**: When you summon a single evil outsider, it appears in a flash of black flame, harming all adjacent nonevil creatures. This deals an amount of damage equal to twice the CR of the summoned creature. An affected creature can attempt a Will save (DC = the DC of the spell used to summon the creature) to halve this damage. A spell augmented with Rending Summons uses up a spell slot 1 level higher than the spell's actual level.
