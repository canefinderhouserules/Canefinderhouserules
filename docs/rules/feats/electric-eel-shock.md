# Electric Eel Shock

*(Combat, Style)*

You can stagger your opponents with jolts of electricity.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, [Electric Eel Style](electric-eel-style.md), [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +11 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md) attempt per day. Creatures that take electricity damage from your Elemental Fist attack must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC 10 + half your character level + your Wisdom modifier) or be [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round. This also applies to creatures that take electricity damage from your [Electric Eel Conduit](electric-eel-conduit.md) feat while [grappled](../appendices/conditions.md#grappled).
