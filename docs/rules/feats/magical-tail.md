# Magical Tail

You grow an extra tail that represents your growing magical powers.

**Prerequisite**: Kitsune.

**Benefit**: You gain a new spell-like ability, each usable twice per day, from the following list, in order: [*disguise self*](../../magic/spells/disguise-self.md), [*charm person*](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md), [*misdirection*](../../magic/spells/misdirection.md), [*invisibility*](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md), [*suggestion*](../../magic/spells/suggestion.md), [*displacement*](../../magic/spells/displacement.md), [*confusion*](../../magic/spells/confusion.md), [*dominate person*](../../magic/spells/dominate-person.md). For example, the first time you select this feat, you gain *disguise self* 2/day; the second time you select this feat, you gain *charm person* 2/day. Your caster level for these spells is equal to your Hit Dice. The DCs for these abilities are Charisma-based.

**Special**: You may select this feat up to eight times. Each time you take it, you gain an additional ability as described above.
