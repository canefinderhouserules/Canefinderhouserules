# Blazing Channel

Your holy power burns the undead like fire.

**Prerequisites**: Turn Undead, channel positive energy class feature.

**Benefit**: When you use your Turn Undead feat, undead failing their saving throw are engulfed in holy fire. This functions identically to catching on fire, but the damage dealt is divine (like the divine damage from *flame strike*) rather than fire. Water can't extinguish this holy fire, but any negative energy effect that heals the creature of at least 5 points of damage extinguishes the flames instantly.
