# Ghost Whisperer

Your words soothe the dead and undead alike, and can even affect haunts.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast 3rd-level spells.

**Benefit**: You gain a language of the dead (as Necril) as a bonus language, and gain a +2 bonus on [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md), [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md), [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md), and [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) checks against undead creatures or when you speak that language. Add [*speak with dead*](../../magic/spells/speak-with-dead.md) to your list of spells known. As soon as you gain the ability to cast 4th-level spells, add [*speak with haunt*](../../magic/spells/speak-with-haunt.md) to your list of spells known. Whenever you cast *speak with dead* or *speak with haunt*, treat your alignment as identical to that of the dead creature or haunt with which you are speaking, and increase the effective caster level of the spell by 2.
