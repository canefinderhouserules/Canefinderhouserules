# Sneaking Precision

Your knowledge of your enemies’ vulnerable spots is especially punishing.

**Prerequisites**: Sneak attack +6d6, [Critical Focus](critical-focus.md), any critical feat, base attack bonus +9.

**Benefit**: Whenever you successfully sneak attack an opponent for a second time on your turn, you can spend a swift action (1 AP) to apply the effects of one critical feat you know to that opponent.
