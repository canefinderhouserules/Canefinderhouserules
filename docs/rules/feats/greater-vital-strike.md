# Greater Vital Strike

*(Combat)*

You can make a single attack that deals incredible damage.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), base attack bonus +16.

**Benefit**: When you use the attack action, you can make one attack at your highest base attack bonus that deals additional damage. Choose one of these options:

- **Weapon dice**: Roll the weapon’s damage dice for the attack five times and add the results together before adding bonuses from Strength, weapon abilities (such as *flaming*), precision-based damage, and other damage bonuses.
- **Static damage**: Multiply the attack’s static damage bonuses (the flat numbers added to the dice, such as Strength or enhancement) by 4.

Neither kind of extra damage is multiplied on a critical hit.

**Example**: A greatsword attack that normally deals 2d6+10 deals either 10d6+10 (weapon dice) or 2d6+40 (static damage).
