# Ironguts

You have an especially strong stomach.

**Prerequisites**: Con 13; dwarf, half-orc, or orc.

**Benefit**: You gain a +2 racial bonus on saving throws against any effect causing the [nauseated](../appendices/conditions.md#nauseated) or [sickened](../appendices/conditions.md#sickened) conditions and against all ingested poisons (but not other poisons). In addition, you receive a +2 bonus on [Survival](../skills/survival.md) skill checks to find food for yourself (and only yourself ).
