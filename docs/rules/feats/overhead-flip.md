# Overhead Flip

*(Combat)*

You hoist your opponent above your head before slamming him to the ground, temporarily staggering the foe.

**Prerequisites**: Str 13, [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), Savage Slam*, base attack bonus +9 or monk level 6th.

**Benefit**: While you are using [Savage Slam](savage-slam.md), if you succeed at your grapple combat maneuver check to knock your opponent [prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone), the opponent must succeed at a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your base attack bonus) or be [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) for 1 round. Oozes, incorporeal creatures, and creatures that are immune to critical hits are immune to this effect.
