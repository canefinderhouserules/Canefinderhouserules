# Missile Shield (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your remarkable awareness allows you to deflect multiple ranged attacks and even ray spells.

**Prerequisites**: [Missile Shield](missile-shield.md).

**Benefit**: When using [Missile Shield](missile-shield.md), you can deflect an additional number of ranged attacks per round equal to half your tier. You can expend one use of mythic power as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) to deflect a single ray from a ray spell or effect targeting you.
