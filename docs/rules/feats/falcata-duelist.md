# Falcata Duelist

*(Combat)*

You trained at one of an old empire’s elite fighting schools and are skilled at the art of fighting with the falcata and buckler.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (falcata), Shield Proficiency (buckler), Weapon Focus (falcata).

**Benefit**: When fighting with the falcata and buckler, your shield bonus to AC increases by +1 and you gain a +2 bonus on [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) checks.
