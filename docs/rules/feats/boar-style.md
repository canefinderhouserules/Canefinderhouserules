# Boar Style

*(Combat, Style)*

Your sharp teeth and nails rip your foes open.

**Prerequisites**: Improved Unarmed Strike, Intimidate 3 ranks.

**Benefit**: You can deal bludgeoning damage or slashing damage with your unarmed strikes—changing damage type is a free action. While using this style, once per round when you hit a single foe with two or more unarmed strikes, you can tear f lesh. When you do, you deal 2d6 extra points of damage with the attack.
