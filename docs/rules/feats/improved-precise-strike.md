# Improved Precise Strike

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

Your precise timing and smooth teamwork allow you to strike a foe where it hurts.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Precise Strike](precise-strike.md), base attack bonus +4.

**Benefit**: Whenever you deal additional damage with [Precise Strike](precise-strike.md), you also deal 1 point of bleed damage. This bleed damage does not stack with itself.
