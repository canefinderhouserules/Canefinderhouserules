# Surface Scout

You were trained from youth to infiltrate and ambush aboveground settlements.

**Prerequisites**: Drow, spell-like abilities drow racial trait.

**Benefit**: You can use [*fear the sun*](../../magic/spells/fear-the-sun.md) and [*ignoble form*](../../magic/spells/ignoble-form.md) each once per day as a spell-like ability. Your caster level is equal to your character level.
