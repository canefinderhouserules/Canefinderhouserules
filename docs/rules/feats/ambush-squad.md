# Ambush Squad

*(Combat, Teamwork)*

You know that the keys to victory are sudden attacks and even more sudden retreats.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1, kobold.

**Benefit**: When you’re adjacent to an ally who also has this feat at the beginning of a surprise round, and both you and that ally can act during that surprise round, you can take both a standard action (1 AP) and a move action (1 AP) during that surprise round.

**Normal**: You can take only a standard action (1 AP) or a move action (1 AP) during a surprise round.
