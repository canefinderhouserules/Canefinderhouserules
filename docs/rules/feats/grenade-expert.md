# Grenade Expert

You are skilled at both manufacturing and using alchemical grenades.

**Prerequisites**: [Throw Anything](throw-anything.md).

**Benefit**:  When you light an alchemical grenade (such as a fuse grenade, pellet grenade, or sting grenade) you select how many rounds later the grenade explodes (minimum 1 round, maximum 3 rounds). In addition, you gain a +4 bonus on [Craft](../skills/craft.md) (alchemy) checks to craft grenades.

**Normal**:  Alchemical grenades explode 1d3 rounds after the fuse is lit.
