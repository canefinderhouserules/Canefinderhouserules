# Ancestral Scorn

The fury you harbor for your fiendish ancestors gives evil outsiders great reason to fear you.

**Prerequisites**: Intimidate 5 ranks, tiefling.

**Benefit**: Whenever you successfully demoralize an outsider of the evil subtype with an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check, it becomes [sickened](../appendices/conditions.md#sickened) for 1 round in addition to being affected by the normal effects of being demoralized. If you beat the DC by 5 or more, the creature is [nauseated 1](../appendices/conditions.md#nauseated) for 1 round instead.

**Normal**: Demoralizing a foe with a successful Intimidate check causes it to become [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) for 1 round, +1 round for every 5 by which you beat the DC.
