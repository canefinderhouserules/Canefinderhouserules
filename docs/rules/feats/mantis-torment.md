# Mantis Torment

*(Combat)*

Your knowledge of the mysteries of anatomy allows you to cause debilitating pain with a simple touch.

**Prerequisites**: Heal 9 ranks, [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Mantis Style](mantis-style.md), [Mantis Wisdom](mantis-wisdom.md), [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md).

**Benefit**: You gain one additional [Stunning Fist](stunning-fist.md) attempt per day. While using [Mantis Style](mantis-style.md), you make an unarmed attack that expends two daily attempts of your Stunning Fist. If you hit, your opponent must succeed at a saving throw against your Stunning Fist or become [dazzled](../appendices/conditions.md#dazzled) and [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) with crippling pain until the start of your next turn, and at that point the opponent becomes [fatigued](../appendices/conditions.md#fatigued).
