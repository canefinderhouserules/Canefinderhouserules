# Fey Obedience

Your devotion to a fey lord is so great that daily prayer and offerings grant you special boons.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (planes) 3 ranks, must worship a fey lord.

**Benefit**: Each fey lord requires a different obedience, but each obedience takes only 1 hour to perform. Once you’ve performed the obedience, you gain the benefit of a special ability or resistance as indicated in the “Obedience” entry for that fey lord (see [Fey Obediences](../../characters/options/fey-obediences.md)).

If you are at least 6th level, you also gain the first boon granted by your fey lord upon undertaking your obedience. If you are at least 10th level, you also gain the fey lord’s second boon. If you are at least 14th level, you also gain the fey lord’s third boon. You choose any one boon list your patron offers. Unless a specific duration or number of uses per day is listed, a boon’s effects are constant.

If you ever fail to perform a daily obedience, you lose all access to the benefits and boons granted by this feat until you next perform the obedience.
