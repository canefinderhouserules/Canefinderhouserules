# Terrorizing Display

*(Combat)*

Your battle prowess frightens friend and foe alike.

**Prerequisites**: [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md), [Demoralizing Lash](demoralizing-lash.md), [Taskmaster](taskmaster.md), [Weapon Focus](weapon-focus.md), Intimidate 10 ranks, hobgoblin, proficiency with selected weapon.

**Benefits**: When you use [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md), in addition to its normal effects, you can use it to spur allies within 30 feet as if you had used the Taskmaster feat. Use the same [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check to determine the effects of Dazzling Display and this feat.
