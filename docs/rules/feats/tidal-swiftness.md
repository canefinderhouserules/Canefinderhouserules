# Tidal Swiftness

*(Conduit)*

A surge of churning, rushing water from the Plane of Water drives you swiftly across the ground, even in the most arid or frigid of regions.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: As a standard action, you can call forth a tide of water that flows around your feet and enhances your speed, as per [slipstream](../../magic/spells/slipstream.md).

You can use this feat's benefit for a number of minutes per day equal to your ranks in Knowledge (planes). These minutes need not be consecutive, but they must be spent in 1-minute increments.
