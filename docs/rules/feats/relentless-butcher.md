# Relentless Butcher

You revel in the glory of dismemberment and maiming.

**Prerequisites**: Confirm at least 50 critical hits.

**Benefit**: When you confirm a critical hit, that opponent must make a Fortitude save (DC = 10 + your Strength or Dexterity modifier) or be [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned) for 1 round.
