# Perturbing Stare

*(Combat, Stare)*

Your stare dulls your foe's reactions.

**Prerequisites**: Mesmerist level 5th, painful stare class feature.

**Benefit**: When you trigger your painful stare, the target must succeed at a Will save (DC = 10 + half your mesmerist level + your Charisma modifier) or lose its Dexterity modifier to AC for 1 round.

**Special**: Characters without the Compounded Pain feat can apply the effects of only one stare feat to an individual attack; a mesmerist with multiple stare feats must choose which to apply before the damage roll is made.
