# Deflect Arrows (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You deflect projectiles and ray spells with uncanny skill.

**Prerequisite**: [Deflect Arrows](deflect-arrows.md).

**Benefit**: When using [Deflect Arrows](deflect-arrows.md), you can deflect an additional number of ranged attacks per round equal to half your tier. You can expend one use of mythic power as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) to deflect a single ray from a ray spell or effect targeting you.
