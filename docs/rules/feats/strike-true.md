# Strike True

*(Combat)*

Rather than attempting a series of wild blows, you line up a perfect attack.

**Prerequisite**: base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: You can focus yourself as a move action (1 AP). When focused, you gain a +4 bonus on your next melee attack roll before the end of your turn.

??? note "Original prerequisites"

    **Prerequisites**: Combat Expertise, base attack bonus +6.
