# Shared Insight

You deftly direct others’ attentions where you wish.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 13, half-elf.

**Benefit**: As a move action (1 AP), you can grant all friendly creatures within 30 feet who can see or hear you a +2 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks for a number of rounds equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round).
