# Star-Chart Initiate

You follow ancient teachings that adopt a multidisciplinary approach to charting and predicting astrological events. This insight allows you to easily locate and apply the effects of celestial phenomena.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (arcana) 1 rank, Knowledge (nature) 1 rank, Knowledge (planes) 1 rank.

**Benefit**: Gain a +3 bonus on skill checks to detect an astrological event. All bonuses you gain from astrological events are increased by 1, and any penalties are decreased by 1 (to a minimum penalty of –1).
