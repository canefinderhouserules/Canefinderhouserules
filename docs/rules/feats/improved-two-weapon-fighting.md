# Improved Two-Weapon Fighting

*(Combat)*

You are skilled at fighting with two weapons.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md), base attack bonus +6.

**Benefit**: In addition to the standard single extra attack you get with an off-hand weapon, you get a second attack with it, at a –5 penalty. Once your base attack bonus reaches +11, you also gain a third attack with your off-hand weapon, at a –10 penalty.

**Normal**: Without this feat, you can only get a single extra attack with an off-hand weapon.

??? tip apr "Action Points (optional rule)"
    With [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md) you spend 2 AP for two attacks at –2. This feat makes a third attack that turn take –5 instead of –10, and once your base attack bonus reaches +11 all three attacks take –2.
