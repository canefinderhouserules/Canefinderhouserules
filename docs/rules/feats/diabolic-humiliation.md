# Diabolic Humiliation

*(Combat)*

Your humiliating strikes decrease a foe's morale.

**Prerequisites**: [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), [Diabolic Style](diabolic-style.md), [Vital Strike](vital-strike.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md) or Weapon Focus (unarmed strike), base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: When you successfully stagger a foe with an unarmed strike using [Diabolic Style](diabolic-style.md), the target becomes further humiliated. The target cannot gain the benefit of any morale bonus for 1 minute after being [staggered](../appendices/conditions.md#staggered) in this way, and for the duration of that minute, any additional staggered effects it suffers from your attacks of opportunity last for 2 rounds rather than 1. The durations of multiple staggered effects administered in this way stack.

**Special**: A monk of at least 10th level with this feat can select [Improved Vital Strike](improved-vital-strike.md) as a monk bonus feat.
