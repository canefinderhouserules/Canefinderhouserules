# Fate-Deck Summoning

Your summoned creatures are empowered by the magic of the fate deck.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast conjuration (summoning) spells or use summoning spell-like abilities, a complete fate deck you own.

**Benefit**: When you cast a conjuration (summoning) spell or use a spell-like ability to summon one or more creatures, you can draw two random cards from a complete [fate deck](../../equipment/fate-deck.md) you own. Doing so adds both a somatic component (if the spell does not already have one) and a focus component (the deck) to the spell, but does not increase the spell's casting time. For the duration of the spell, each summoned creature gains a +4 enhancement bonus to the ability scores corresponding to the drawn cards' suits. If both cards are the same suit, each summoned creature gains a +6 bonus to that ability score instead. If either card's alignment is a true match for the summoned creatures' alignment, the duration of the spell is doubled, while drawing an opposite match halves the duration of the spell. If both a true match and an opposite match are drawn, the spell's duration is unchanged.

A creature summoned in this way is distinctly altered by the cards drawn. Its physical appearance reflects the visual elements of the cards, and its personality is influenced by them, though not so much as to alter its alignment.
