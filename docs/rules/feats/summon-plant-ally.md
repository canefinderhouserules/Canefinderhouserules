# Summon Plant Ally

You can call plant creatures to your aid in battle.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (nature) 1 rank, ability to cast *summon nature's ally* spells.

**Benefit**: When casting a *summon nature's ally* spell, you gain access to the list of plant creatures below.

- [*Summon nature's ally I*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-i.md): leaf leshy.
- [*Summon nature's ally II*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-ii.md): gourd leshy, leaf ray (without seed ability).
- [*Summon nature's ally III*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-iii.md): fungus leshy, weedwhip.
- [*Summon nature's ally IV*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-iv.md): assassin vine, seaweed leshy (without air cyst ability).
- [*Summon nature's ally V*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-v.md): mandragora, shambling mound.
- [*Summon nature's ally VI*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-vi.md): tendriculos, treant.
- [*Summon nature's ally VII*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-vii.md): giant flytrap.
- [*Summon nature's ally VIII*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-viii.md): jimenju.
- [*Summon nature's ally IX*](../../magic/spells/summon-natures-ally-ix.md): alraune.
