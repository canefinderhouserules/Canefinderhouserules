# Collector’s Argument

The collectors of the lost dead prefer to use complex theology or blunt warnings to gather wayward spirits, but sometimes a more direct approach is necessary. One of them shared a trade secret with you.

**Prerequisites**: Ability to cast [*disrupt undead*](../../magic/spells/disrupt-undead.md), character level 3rd, duskwalker.

**Benefit**: The damage dealt by your *disrupt undead* spell increases by 1d6. It increases by an additional 1d6 at 6th level and every 3 levels thereafter (to a maximum of 7d6 at 18th level).
