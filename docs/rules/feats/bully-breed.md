# Bully Breed

Your animal companion can terrify as well as fight.

**Prerequisites**: Handle Animal 4 ranks, Intimidate 4 ranks, animal companion or mount class feature.

**Benefit**: Your animal companion or mount gains Intimidate as a class skill. It can attempt to demoralize an opponent using the [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) skill as a move action (1 AP) on the same round that it damages an opponent with a natural attack.

**Special**: Your animal companion can select the [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md) and [Shatter Defenses](shatter-defenses.md) feats if it meets the prerequisites. This feat does not allow your animal companion to use Intimidate to make a target friendly or gain their assistance.

**Normal**: Animal companions and mounts with an Intelligence score of 2 or less must select feats from a limited list.
