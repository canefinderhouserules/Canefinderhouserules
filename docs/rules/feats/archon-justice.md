# Archon Justice

*(Combat)*

Attacking you during your period of vigilance opens foes up to allies' strikes.

**Prerequisites**: [Archon Diversion](archon-diversion.md), [Archon Style](archon-style.md), [Combat Expertise](combat-expertise.md), [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: You no longer take a penalty to AC for using [Archon Style](archon-style.md) to grant nearby allies a bonus to AC, and you can activate this effect as a swift action (1 AP) or a move action (1 AP). Whenever you take damage from an attack you diverted to yourself using [Archon Diversion](archon-diversion.md), each ally threatening the attacking creature can make an attack of opportunity against that creature.
