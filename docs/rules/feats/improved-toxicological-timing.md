# Improved Toxicological Timing

You know how to adjust the amount of time required for a poison to take effect.

**Prerequisites**: Craft (alchemy) 11 ranks, [Toxicological Timing](toxicological-timing.md).

**Benefit**: Whenever you craft a poison with the [Craft](../skills/craft.md) (alchemy) skill, you can add or subtract up to 1 day from its onset time (to a minimum onset period of 1 round).
