# Drunken God’s Blessings

The god of freedom and ale smiles upon you when you visit his most favored temples: taverns.

**Prerequisites**: Worshiper of the god of freedom and ale.

**Benefit**: When you have had at least a number of drinks equal to your Constitution modifier (or get drunk or soused) in a tavern, once within the following 24 hours, you can gain the benefits of [*remove paralysis*](../../magic/spells/remove-paralysis.md) as a free action. Alternatively, you can choose one of the following benefits depending on the type of bar you patronized. At the GM’s discretion, other types of taverns might grant other benefits.

- *Gambling Den*: You can gain the benefits of [*detect thoughts*](../../magic/spells/detect-thoughts.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
- *Illegal Tavern*: You can gain the benefits of [*invisibility*](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
- *Posh Tavern*: You can gain the benefits of [*eagle’s splendor*](../../magic/spells/eagles-splendor.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
- *Private Club*: You can gain the benefits of [*undetectable alignment*](../../magic/spells/undetectable-alignment.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
- *Rowdy Bar*: You can gain the benefits of [*bear’s endurance*](../../magic/spells/bears-endurance.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
- *Tavern of Intrigue*: You can gain the benefits of [*fox’s cunning*](../../magic/spells/foxs-cunning.md) for 1 minute as a free action.
