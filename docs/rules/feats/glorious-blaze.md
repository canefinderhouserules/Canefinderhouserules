# Glorious Blaze

Some goblins are drawn to a deity of healing and the sun, in part due to that deity's incarnation as a giant ball of fire, and celebrate with a wild dance.

**Prerequisites**: Goblin, worshipper of a deity whose portfolio includes the sun.

**Benefit**: If the sun is visible, you can spend 1 round performing a wild dance in worship of your deity. Starting at the beginning of your next turn, allies who can see you gain a +1 morale bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws. This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to half your character level + your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1 round). At 9th level, this bonus increases to +2.
