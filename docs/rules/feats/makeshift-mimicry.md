# Makeshift Mimicry

*(Combat)*

Your unique understanding of improvised tools enables you to turn the intrinsic form of all manner of objects to your advantage in combat.

**Prerequisites**: Catch Off-Guard or Throw Anything, Makeshift Style*.

**Benefit**: While in Makeshift Style, you can take a –2 penalty on attack rolls to treat an improvised weapon as if it had one of the following weapon special qualities until the start of your next turn: blocking, brace, disarm, distracting, nonlethal, performance, or trip. If the weapon has the fragile special quality or broken condition, you can treat it as if it had the jagged special quality.
