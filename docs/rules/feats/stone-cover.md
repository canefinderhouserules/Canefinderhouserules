# Stone Cover

You can use stone around you to help you avoid attacks.

**Prerequisites**: Wis 15, Stone Shroud, Stealth 6 ranks, dwarf, stonecunning racial trait.

**Benefit**: While in areas of rocky terrain or while underground, you can gain 10% miss chance as a swift action. Alternatively, you can take a moment to better position yourself and gain concealment as a move action. The miss chance or concealment you gain from this feat does not stack with similar effects and remains until the start of your next turn.
