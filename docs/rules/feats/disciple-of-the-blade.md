# Disciple of the Blade

Your dedication to mastering your deity’s favored weapon puts others to shame.

**Prerequisites**: Weapon Focus (deity’s favored weapon), cleric or inquisitor level 4th, worshiper of a deity.

**Benefit**: You gain the benefits of Weapon Specialization with your deity’s favored weapon, and you can use your cleric or inquisitor level in place of your fighter level for the purpose of qualifying for Greater Weapon Focus and Greater Weapon Specialization with that weapon. You can use this feat instead of Weapon Specialization to qualify for Greater Weapon Specialization with that weapon.
