# Pummeling Bully

*(Combat)*

Your punch has the chance to trip or move your target.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Reposition](improved-reposition.md), [Improved Trip](improved-trip.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Pummeling Style](pummeling-style.md); base attack bonus +9, brawler level 5th, or monk level 5th.

**Benefit**: When you use [Pummeling Style](pummeling-style.md) to make an entire full attack or flurry of blows against a single target, if you hit with any of your attacks, you can attempt a reposition or trip combat maneuver check as a free action.
