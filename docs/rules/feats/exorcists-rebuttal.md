# Exorcist’s Rebuttal

You've experienced the suffocating grip of demonic possession. The event left you emotionally scarred, but it also tempered your mind against the prying magic of evil enchantments.

**Prerequisites**: [Improved Iron Will](improved-iron-will.md), [Iron Will](iron-will.md).

**Benefit**: If you use your [Improved Iron Will](improved-iron-will.md) feat to reroll a Will save against a compulsion effect and successfully save against the effect, the creature that created the effect (if it originated from a creature) takes 1d4 points of Wisdom damage.
