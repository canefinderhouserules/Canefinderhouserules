# Shambling Monolith

You are able to draw up available vegetation into your body mass and increase your size and strength.

**Prerequisites**: Shambling mound.

**Benefit**: As a full-round action (3 AP), you may draw additional vegetable matter into yourself and increase your size, strength, and durability as if using an [*animal growth*](../../magic/spells/animal-growth.md) spell. At the beginning of each turn, you must succeed at a Fortitude save to maintain your increased size. The save DC is 10 if in forest or jungle and 15 if in a swamp or underground, and increases by 1 for each round that passes. If you fail the save, you collapse back to your normal size and are [fatigued](../appendices/conditions.md#fatigued) for 8 hours.

**Special**: You must be in forest, jungle, swamp, or underground terrain to use this feat. You cannot use this feat when you are fatigued or [exhausted](../appendices/conditions.md#exhausted).
