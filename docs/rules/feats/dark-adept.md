# Dark Adept

You gain several additional spell-like abilities from your dark elven heritage.

**Prerequisites**: Drow, character level 3rd

**Benefit**: You gain three new spell-like abilities, each usable once per day. These spell-like abilities are [*detect magic*](../../magic/spells/detect-magic.md), [*feather fall*](../../magic/spells/feather-fall.md), and [*levitate*](../../magic/spells/levitate.md). Your caster level for these spell-like abilities equals your total character level.
