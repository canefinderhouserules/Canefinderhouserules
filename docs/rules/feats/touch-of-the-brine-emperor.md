# Touch of the Brine Emperor

Your touch can suppress spells that aid in breathing water.

**Prerequisites**: Worshiper of a deity of the sea's depths, character level 5th.

**Benefit**: Once per day while underwater, you can touch a creature to suppress any spells that allow it to breathe underwater (*air bubble*, *life bubble*, *water breathing* and so on) for 5 minutes. Suppressing a spell requires you to roll 1d20 + your character level against a DC of 11 + the spell's caster level (if the spells affecting the creature have differing caster levels, you might dispel some but not others). This is a spell-like ability. At 10th level and every 5 levels thereafter, you can use this touch an additional time each day.
