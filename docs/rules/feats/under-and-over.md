# Under and Over

*(Combat)*

You can slip under a foe trying to grab you, knocking him off balance.

**Prerequisites**: [Agile Maneuvers](agile-maneuvers.md), Small size or smaller.

**Benefit**: If an opponent larger than you attempts to grapple you and fails, you may make a trip attack as an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) against that opponent with a +2 bonus. This does not provoke an attack of opportunity.
