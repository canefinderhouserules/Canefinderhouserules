# Merciless Butchery

*(Combat)*

Your talent for slaughter lets you dispatch [helpless](../appendices/conditions.md#helpless) opponents before they can recover.

**Prerequisites**: [Dastardly Finish](dastardly-finish.md), sneak attack +5d6, studied target class feature.

**Benefit**: As a swift action (1 AP) that does not provoke attacks of opportunity, you can attempt a coup de grace against a [cowering](../appendices/conditions.md#cowering), helpless, or [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned) opponent that you have designated as your studied target.
