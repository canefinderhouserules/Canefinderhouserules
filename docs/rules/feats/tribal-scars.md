# Tribal Scars

You endured the grueling coming-of-age rituals of your tribe or following, and proudly bear the scars that grant you the blessings of your tribe’s ancestors or totem.

**Benefit**: You gain one of the following benefits, depending on which tribal following you belong to. (At the GM’s discretion, other options of her own design could exist for different tribes.)

- **Bearpelt**: You gain a +1 bonus on Fortitude saves and a +2 bonus on [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) checks.
- **Greattusk**: You gain a +2 bonus on checks to perform bull rush or overrun combat maneuvers and a +2 bonus on [Ride](../skills/ride.md) checks.
- **Ice Chasm**: You gain a +1 bonus on Reflex saves and a +2 bonus on [Climb](../skills/climb.md) checks.
- **Night Hunt**: You gain a +2 bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) and [Survival](../skills/survival.md) checks.
- **Raptorscale**: Your base land speed increases by 5 feet, and you gain a +2 bonus on [Acrobatics](../skills/acrobatics.md) checks.
- **Slothjaw**: You gain a +1 bonus on Will saves and a +2 bonus on [Handle Animal](../skills/handle-animal.md) checks.
