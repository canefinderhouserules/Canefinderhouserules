# Magical Aptitude (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your understanding of the principles and intricacies of magic goes far beyond your training.

**Prerequisite**: [Magical Aptitude](magical-aptitude.md).

**Benefit**: The bonus on [Spellcraft](../skills/spellcraft.md) and [Use Magic Device](../skills/use-magic-device.md) skill checks from [Magical Aptitude](magical-aptitude.md) increases by 2. In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Spellcraft or Use Magic Device check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
