# Disheartening Display

*(Combat)*

Your show of prowess further demoralizes others.

**Prerequisites**: [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md), [Weapon Focus](weapon-focus.md), base attack bonus +6, proficiency with chosen weapon.

**Benefit**: When you successfully use [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md) against any shaken, frightened, or panicked opponents, their fear increases by one step. An already panicked creature demoralized by this feat cowers. Once affected by this feat, a creature cannot be affected by it again (by you or anyone else) for 24 hours.
