# Potion Glutton

You gulp down potions with unsettling speed.

**Prerequisites**: Worshiper of a deity of gluttony or undeath.

**Benefit**: You can drink potions, elixirs, or other potables as a swift action (1 AP) without provoking attacks of opportunity.

**Normal**: Drinking potions is a move action (1 AP) that provokes attacks of opportunity.
