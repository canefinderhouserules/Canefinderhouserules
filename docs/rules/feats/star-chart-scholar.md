# Star-Chart Scholar

You have learned to draw additional power from celestial phenomena, harnessing their influence for longer than their natural duration.

**Prerequisites**: Star-Chart Initiate, Knowledge (arcana) 5 ranks, Knowledge (nature) 5 ranks, Knowledge (planes) 5 ranks.

**Benefit**: You increase the duration of effects caused by astrological events. The duration of effects that last less than a day is increased by 50%, while those that last 24 hours or longer last 1 additional day.
