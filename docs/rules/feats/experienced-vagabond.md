# Experienced Vagabond

You spent significant time as a vagabond and know your way around their society.

**Prerequisites**: Bluff 2 ranks, Knowledge (local) 2 ranks, member of an underclass.

**Benefit**: When dealing with criminals, vagabonds, unsavory characters, or other members of a country’s underclass, you gain a +1 bonus on [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md), [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) (to gather information), [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (local), and [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) checks. When traveling with at least one other person who has this feat, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on [Perception](../skills/perception.md) checks to avoid being surprised at the start of combat.
