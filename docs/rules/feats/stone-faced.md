# Stone-Faced

Rocks show more expression than you.

**Prerequisite**: Dwarf.

**Benefit**: You receive a +4 bonus on [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) checks to lie or conceal your feelings or true motives, but not to feint in combat or deliver secret messages. In addition, the DC for [Sense Motive](../skills/sense-motive.md) checks to get a hunch about you is 25 rather than 20.
