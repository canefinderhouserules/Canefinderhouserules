# Ominous Mien

Your walk, stance, and voice all amplify your status as an infernal knight when you cast spells or use spell-like abilities against opponents.

**Prerequisites**: member of the infernal knights.

**Benefit**: Whenever you cast a spell or use a spell-like ability from the compulsion, fear, phantasm, or shadow sub-schools, add +1 to the DC of the saving throw against that spell or effect.
