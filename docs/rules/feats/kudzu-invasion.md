# Kudzu Invasion

*(Combat)*

In addition to obscuring creatures' vision, your vines crush their bodies.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, [Greater Grapple](greater-grapple.md), [Improved Grapple](improved-grapple.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Kudzu Grappler](kudzu-grappler.md), base attack bonus +6 or monk level 6th, vine leshy.

**Benefit**: When you blind a creature using your Kudzu Grappler feat, you can also deal your unarmed strike damage to it.
