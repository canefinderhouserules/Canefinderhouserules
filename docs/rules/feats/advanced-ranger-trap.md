# Advanced Ranger Trap

Your ranger traps are especially difficult to notice and avoid.

**Prerequisites**: Trap class feature, ranger level 5th.

**Benefit**: Add +1 to the Difficulty Class on all [Perception](../skills/perception.md) and [Disable Device](../skills/disable-device.md) skill checks to find or disable the traps you make with your trap class feature. Add a +1 to the Difficulty Class on all saving throws against the effects of the trap you make with your trap class feature.
