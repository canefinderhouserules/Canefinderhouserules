# Street Carnage

*(Combat)*

For you, the urban environment becomes another brutal weapon you can use to shred flesh and break bones.

**Prerequisites**: Str 15, [Improved Bull Rush](improved-bull-rush.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), [Power Attack](power-attack.md), [Street Style](street-style.md), [Street Sweep](street-sweep.md), base attack bonus +8 or monk level 7th.

**Benefit**: While using Street Style, the critical multiplier of your unarmed strikes becomes ×3, instead of ×2.
