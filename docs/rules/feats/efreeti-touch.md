# Efreeti Touch

*(Combat)*

Your knowledge of the secrets of the burning wind and blazing sun allows you to collect flames into your hands and unleash them in a gout of elemental fire.

**Prerequisites**: Con 15, Wis 17, [Efreeti Style](efreeti-style.md), [Efreeti Stance](efreeti-stance.md), [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md), [Improved Unarmed Strike](improved-unarmed-strike.md), base attack bonus +13 or monk level 11th.

**Benefit**: While using [Efreeti Style](efreeti-style.md), as a standard action, you can spend two [Elemental Fist](elemental-fist.md) attempts to unleash a 15-foot cone-shaped burst of flame. Creatures caught in the cone take your unarmed strike damage plus the fire damage from your Elemental Fist and catch on fire. A successful Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wis modifier) reduces the damage by half and prevents a target from catching on fire.
