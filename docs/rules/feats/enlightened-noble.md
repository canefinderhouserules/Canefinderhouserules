# Enlightened Noble

You excel in a particular area thanks to your noble family's field of expertise.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Noble Scion, Knowledge (nobility) 1 rank.

**Benefit**: You gain an additional benefit based on the flavor of your noble family chosen with the Noble Scion feat.

**Scion of the Arts**: A number of times per day equal to your Charisma modifier, you can roll 2d20 for a Perform check and take the better result.

**Scion of Lore**: You can attempt Knowledge skill checks untrained. If you have 10 or more ranks in a Knowledge skill, the bonus from Scion of Lore increases to +2 for that skill.

**Scion of Magic**: Once per day, you can gain a +2 bonus to caster level on a concentration check, a Use Magic Device check, or a check to overcome spell resistance.

**Scion of Peace**: Choose one Wisdom-based skill. Once per day when you take 10 on this skill, you treat the result as if you had rolled an 18 instead of a 10.

**Scion of War**: After rolling initiative, you can attempt a Bluff check as an immediate action to feint against a creature or an Intimidate check to demoralize a creature. This can only affect creatures that act after you in the initiative count.

## Noble Scion Benefits by Court

Noble houses train their courts to focus on the ideals and skills most appropriate for their region. The following benefits can be taken in place of the standard selection of benefits provided by the Noble Scion feat.

**Scion of the Crossroads City**: You gain one of the following languages as a bonus language: Dwarven, Elven, Giant, Gnome, Halfling, or Orc. You gain a +1 bonus on Diplomacy checks when interacting with a creature that shares this bonus language.

**Scion of Rival Houses**: You gain a +2 bonus on Bluff checks against other nobles or members of a noble court.

**Scion of the Infernal Court**: You gain a +1 bonus on Knowledge (planes) checks and treat your caster level as 1 higher when attempting to bind or call a devil.

**Scion of the Back Alleys**: Choose either Sleight of Hand or Stealth. While you are in a small city or larger settlement, you can take 10 with the chosen skill, even while rushed or threatened.

**Scion of the Mountain Hold**: You use your Constitution modifier instead of your Charisma modifier for the purpose of the Leadership feat.

**Scion of the Frozen Court**: You reduce the amount of nonlethal damage you take from exposure to cold by 4 (minimum 0).

**Scion of the Island Court**: Once per day, you can reroll a save against a psychic spell and gain a +2 bonus on that reroll.

**Scion of the Merchant Court**: Once per day, you can attempt a Diplomacy check to reduce the cost of an item by 10% (DC = 15, + 1 for every 500 gp of the item's base cost). The item must have a market value of 5,000 gp or less.

**Scion of the Forest Court**: You use your Dexterity modifier instead of your Charisma modifier for the purpose of the Leadership feat.

**Scion of the Shadowed Court**: You gain low-light vision. If you already have low-light vision, you instead gain a +2 bonus on Perception checks in areas of dim light.

**Scion of the Scrapland Court**: You gain a +1 bonus on skill checks when interacting with technology and a +2 bonus on damage rolls against creatures of the robot subtype.

**Scion of the Desert Court**: You reduce the amount of nonlethal damage you take from heat exposure by 2 (minimum 0). Additionally, you require only a gallon of water per day to avoid dehydration in hot climates.

**Scion of the Cavalry Court**: Once per day, when attempting to negate an attack with Mounted Combat, you can roll twice and take the better result. Ride is a class skill for you.

**Scion of the Free Frontier**: Whenever you take 10 on Handle Animal, Knowledge (nature), Perception, Ride, or Survival checks, treat the result as if you had rolled a 13 instead of a 10.

**Scion of the Old Empire**: You gain a +2 bonus on Charisma-based checks when interacting with nobles or members of a noble court.

**Scion of the Gothic Court**: Once per day as a free action, you can suppress any fear effects affecting you for a number of rounds equal to half your Hit Dice (minimum 1) even if you could not normally take actions due to your fear. Knowledge (religion) is a class skill for you.
