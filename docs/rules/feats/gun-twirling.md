# Gun Twirling

*(Grit)*

You can spin and juggle a small firearm, making it difficult to predict where you will shoot.

**Prerequisites**: Amateur Gunslinger feat or grit class feature, Dazzling Display, Weapon Focus.

**Benefit**: You can spend 1 grit point to make a feint attempt using a one-handed firearm for which you have Weapon Focus (instead of a melee weapon). The target of this feint must be within 30 feet of you and be able to see you. If you have the Quick Draw feat, you can holster a one-handed firearm as a free action as long as you have at least 1 grit point.
