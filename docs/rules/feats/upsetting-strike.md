# Upsetting Strike

*(Combat)*

You can turn the tables on disoriented opponents.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Combat Reflexes](combat-reflexes.md), [Improved Shield Bash](improved-shield-bash.md), [Upsetting Shield Style](upsetting-shield-style.md), proficiency with bucklers.

**Benefit**: While you are using [Upsetting Shield Style](upsetting-shield-style.md), if a foe is taking a penalty on attack rolls against you as a result of Upsetting Shield Style, and that foe makes a melee attack roll against you that misses your AC by 5 or more, that foe provokes an attack of opportunity from you. You can only make one attack of opportunity against an opponent in this manner each round.
