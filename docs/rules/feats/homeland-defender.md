# Homeland Defender

For your kingdom and your general, you may strike down enemies who threaten your homeland.

**Prerequisites**: Born or sworn in a small military state.

**Benefit**: Once per day, you may attempt to smite an enemy of your homeland with one normal melee attack. You gain a +4 morale bonus on your attack roll and deal 1 extra point of damage per level. For the purpose of this feat, an enemy of your homeland is any person or creature that is not one of its citizens. You can only use this ability within your homeland’s borders.
