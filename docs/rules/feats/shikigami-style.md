# Shikigami Style

*(Combat, Style)*

Your skill with improvised weapons makes them more dangerous than they would otherwise be.

**Prerequisites**: [Throw Anything](throw-anything.md).

**Benefit**: While in this style, you deal damage with improvised weapons as if they were one size category larger. [Shikigami Mimicry](shikigami-mimicry.md) also treats the improvised weapon as one more size category larger. [Shikigami Manipulation](shikigami-manipulation.md) does not add another size category. This feat stacks with [Improvised Weapon Mastery](improvised-weapon-mastery.md).

??? note "Original rule"

    *(Combat, Style)*

    Your skill with improvised weapons makes them more dangerous than they would otherwise be.

    **Prerequisites**: Catch Off-Guard or Throw Anything.

    **Benefit**: While in this style, you deal damage with improvised weapons as if they were one size category larger. For every style feat you have that lists Shikigami Style as a prerequisite, treat the improvised weapon as an additional size category larger, to a maximum of three sizes larger than its actual size.
