# Spear Dancing Spiral

*(Combat)*

You wield spears with poise and grace.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 15, [Spear Dancing Style](spear-dancing-style.md), [Two-Weapon Fighting](two-weapon-fighting.md), [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md), Weapon Focus with the chosen weapon.

**Benefit**: While using [Spear Dancing Style](spear-dancing-style.md), you gain the benefit of [Weapon Finesse](weapon-finesse.md) with the chosen weapon if it is appropriately sized for a creature of your size category. In addition, you can use any feat or ability that functions with a quarterstaff with your chosen weapon.
