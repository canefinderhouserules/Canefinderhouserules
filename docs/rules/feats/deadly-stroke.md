# Deadly Stroke

*(Combat)*

With a well-placed strike, you can bring a swift and painful end to most foes.

**Prerequisites**: [Dazzling Display](dazzling-display.md), [Greater Weapon Focus](greater-weapon-focus.md), [Shatter Defenses](shatter-defenses.md), [Weapon Focus](weapon-focus.md), proficiency with the selected weapon, base attack bonus +11.

**Benefit**: As a standard action (1 AP), make a single attack with the weapon for which you have [Greater Weapon Focus](greater-weapon-focus.md) against a [stunned](../appendices/conditions.md#stunned) or [flat-footed](../appendices/conditions.md#flat-footed) opponent. If you hit, you deal double the normal damage and the target takes 1 point of Constitution bleed (see Appendix 2). The additional damage and bleed is not multiplied on a critical hit.
