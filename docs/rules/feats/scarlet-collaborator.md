# Scarlet Collaborator

You pass along private details to the Scarlet, and they protect you in return.

**Prerequisites**: Associate (Scarlet).

**Benefit**: The Scarlet can help you hide from trouble in one of their secret safe houses. Anyone attempting [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) or [Survival](../skills/survival.md) checks to locate you during this time takes a –10 penalty, and anyone attempting to scry upon you or magically divine your location must first succeed at a caster level check (DC = 15 + your character level). You can stay in a Mantis safe house for up to 1 day per character level before you are forced to vacate.
