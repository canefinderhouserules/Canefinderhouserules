# Armor Trick

*(Combat)*

When used cleverly, armor becomes far more than a defensive implement.

**Prerequisites**: Base attack bonus +1.

**Benefit**: You can use any [armor tricks](../combat/armor-tricks.md) related to the chosen armor option if you meet the trick’s prerequisites and are proficient with any armor that you use as part of the trick. Choose one armor trick option (light, medium, heavy, or shield). You can perform armor tricks in combat when wearing the appropriate armor option.

**Special**: You can gain Armor Trick multiple times. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a new armor trick option.
