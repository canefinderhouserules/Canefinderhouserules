# Mounted Shield

*(Combat)*

Your defensive tactics defend both you and your mount.

**Prerequisites**: [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md), [Shield Focus](shield-focus.md).

**Benefit**: You may add your base shield bonus (including the bonus from [Shield Focus](shield-focus.md) but not including enhancement bonuses) to your mount’s AC. In addition, you may add this bonus when making a Ride check to negate a hit against your mount using the [Mounted Combat](mounted-combat.md) feat.
