# Fey Spell Lore

You have learned the treacherous spells employed by the fey.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Spellcraft 1 rank, ability to cast druid spells.

**Benefit**: Add the following spells to your druid spell list at the indicated levels: 0—[*dancing lights*](../../magic/spells/dancing-lights.md), 1st—*lesser confusion*, 2nd—[*charm person*](../../magic/spells/charm-person.md), 3rd—[*invisibility*](../../magic/spells/invisibility.md), 4th—[*bestow curse*](../../magic/spells/bestow-curse.md), 5th—[*charm monster*](../../magic/spells/charm-monster.md), 6th—*major curse**,* 7th—[*cloak of dreams*](../../magic/spells/cloak-of-dreams.md), 8th—[*insanity*](../../magic/spells/insanity.md), 9th—[*irresistible dance*](../../magic/spells/irresistible-dance.md).
