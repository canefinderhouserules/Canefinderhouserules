# Fey Spell Lore

You have learned the treacherous spells employed by the fey.

**Prerequisites**: Cha 13, Spellcraft 1 rank, ability to cast druid spells.

**Benefit**: Add the following spells to your druid spell list at the indicated levels: 0—*dancing lights*, 1st—*lesser confusion*, 2nd—*charm person*, 3rd—*invisibility*, 4th—*bestow curse*, 5th—*charm monster*, 6th—*major curse**,* 7th—*cloak of dreams*, 8th—*insanity*, 9th—*irresistible dance*.
