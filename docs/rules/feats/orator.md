# Orator

You're able to construct statements and commands that are much more convincing than their content would otherwise suggest.

**Prerequisite**: Skill Focus (Linguistics).

**Benefit**: You can use a [Linguistics](../skills/linguistics.md) check in place of a [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md) check to tell a falsehood or conceal information, in place of a [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md) check to change the attitude of a creature, or in place of an [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md) check to force a creature to cooperate. You must deliver your attempt in a language the target understands.
