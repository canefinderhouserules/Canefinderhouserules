# Ancient Kingdom Scholar

You are well schooled in the traditions, culture, and history of an ancient desert kingdom, especially the broad expanse of its long history and ancient relics.

**Prerequisites**: Knowledge (history) 4 ranks, Knowledge (local) 4 ranks, speaks the kingdom’s modern and ancient languages.

**Benefit**: When in the desert kingdom, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus on [Bluff](../skills/bluff.md), [Diplomacy](../skills/diplomacy.md), [Disguise](../skills/disguise.md), [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md), and [Perform](../skills/perform.md) checks. You gain a +5 bonus when using [Appraise](../skills/appraise.md), [Linguistics](../skills/linguistics.md), [Knowledge](../skills/knowledge.md) (all skills), and Perception to learn about a person, place, or item of the ancient kingdom.
