# Expert Salvager

You are adept at foraging and salvaging raw materials from even the most seemingly sparse of environments.

**Prerequisites**: Craft (any) 2 ranks, Spellcraft 2 ranks.

**Benefit**: You gain a +4 bonus on [Craft](../skills/craft.md) checks for Craft skills in which you have at least 2 ranks and [Spellcraft](../skills/spellcraft.md) checks when crafting items by foraging alchemical supplies and material components, salvaging raw crafting materials, and salvaging raw magical item materials.
