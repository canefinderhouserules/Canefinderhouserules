# Athletic (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

You excel far beyond others at acts of physical prowess.

**Prerequisite**: [Athletic](athletic.md).

**Benefit**: The bonus on [Climb](../skills/climb.md) and [Swim](../skills/swim.md) skill checks from Athletic increases by 2. In addition, you can expend one use of mythic power to treat a Climb or Swim check as if you had rolled a natural 20. You must decide to use this ability before making the roll.
