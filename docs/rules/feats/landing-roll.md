# Landing Roll

*(Combat)*

You have learned the technique of rolling safely away when an enemy trips you.

**Prerequisites**: Dex 13, Dodge, [Mobility](mobility.md).

**Benefit**: If you are tripped, you can spend an immediate action (1 AP of your next turn) to move 5 feet without provoking an attack of opportunity. This does not count as taking a 5-foot step. You fall [prone](../appendices/conditions.md#prone) after this movement.
