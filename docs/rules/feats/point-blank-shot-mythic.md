# Point-Blank Shot (Mythic)

*(Mythic)*

Your accuracy with ranged attacks is unmatched at pointblank range.

**Prerequisite**: [Point-Blank Shot](point-blank-shot.md).

**Benefit**: The bonus on attack and damage rolls granted by [Point-Blank Shot](point-blank-shot.md) increases to +2. As a swift action, you can expend one use of mythic power to gain an additional bonus on these rolls equal to half your tier.
