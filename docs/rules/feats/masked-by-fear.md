# Masked by Fear

You embody all that you fear while wearing your mask.

**Prerequisites**: [Nameless One](nameless-one.md); base Will save bonus +6 or bravery class feature.

**Benefit**: By donning a mask etched with your greatest terrors, you are able to eschew fear completely. While benefiting from the Nameless One feat, when you suffer the [cowering](../appendices/conditions.md#cowering), [frightened](../appendices/conditions.md#frightened), or [panicked](../appendices/conditions.md#panicked) condition, that condition causes you to act as if [shaken](../appendices/conditions.md#shaken) instead of imposing its normal effects. In addition, whenever you attempt a skill check (normally [Intimidate](../skills/intimidate.md)) to demoralize an opponent, you gain a +3 bonus on the check.
