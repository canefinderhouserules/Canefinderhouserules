# Coup de Grace

!!! warning "House rule"
    A coup de grace no longer forces a death save. It cannot kill a helpless target outright.

You can use a full-round action to deliver a coup de grace against a helpless opponent. As normal, the attack automatically hits and is automatically a critical hit. Sneak attack damage applies as normal.

**The target does not make a Fortitude save to avoid death.** The attack simply deals its critical-hit damage. A coup de grace only kills if that damage is enough to reduce the target to death by itself.
