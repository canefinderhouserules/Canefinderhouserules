# Ranger Fighting Styles by Creed

Rangers often take combat styles that let them walk the path laid out by their patron or creed. If a ranger selects one of the styles below, he can choose from the listed feats whenever he gains a combat style feat; at 6th level and 10th level, he adds the additional feats to the list. Each style is keyed to the theme of a patron power rather than a specific faith, so a GM can attach any appropriate deity or tradition.

## Divine Styles

| Style | Starting list | 6th level adds | 10th level adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seduction and revenge (whip) | Combat Reflexes, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (whip), Weapon Finesse, Whip Mastery | Improved Trip, Improved Whip Mastery | Greater Trip, Greater Whip Mastery |
| Bravery and luck (rapier) | Catch Off-Guard, Combat Expertise, Combat Reflexes, Weapon Finesse | Improved Disarm, Lunge | Disarming Strike, Improved Critical (rapier) |
| Travel and dreams (thrown) | Quick Draw, Point-Blank Shot, Rapid Shot, Weapon Finesse | Distance Thrower, Opening Volley | Close-Quarters Thrower, Shot on the Run |
| Hunting and farming (bow) | Deadly Aim, Far Shot, Point-Blank Shot, Precise Shot | Clustered Shots, Snap Shot | Improved Snap Shot, Pinpoint Targeting |
| War and battle (power) | Cleave, Improved Bull Rush, Power Attack, Shield of Swings | Cleaving Finish, Vital Strike | Death or Glory, Improved Vital Strike |
| Sea and storm (thrown, trident) | Deadly Aim, Distance Thrower, Point-Blank Shot, Two-Handed Thrower | Lunge, Precise Shot | Impaling Critical (trident), Strike Back |
| Valor and protection (shield) | Bodyguard, Improved Shield Bash, Step Up, Two-Weapon Fighting | In Harm's Way, Shield Slam | Bashing Finish, Shield Master |
| Perfection and discipline (unarmed) | Improved Grapple, Improved Unarmed Strike, Monastic Legacy, Two-Weapon Fighting | Improved Trip, Spring Attack | Ki Throw, Stunning Fist |
| Savagery and madness | Cleave, Furious Focus, Power Attack, Pushing Assault | Bloody Assault, Vital Strike | Dreadful Carnage, Improved Vital Strike |
| Secrets and murder (short sword) | Blind-Fight, Quick Draw, Wave Strike, Weapon Finesse | Improved Critical (short sword), Improved Feint | Deadly Finish, Greater Blind-Fight |
| Sun and healing (dance) | Improved Initiative, Mobility, Nimble Moves, Sidestep | Whirlwind Attack, Wind Stance | Lightning Stance, Spring Attack |
| Forge and defense (hammer) | Bludgeoner, Improved Sunder, Power Attack, Shield Focus | Felling Smash, Greater Sunder | Greater Shield Focus, Sundering Strike |
| Pain and torment (spiked chain) | Agile Maneuvers, Disruptive, Exotic Weapon Proficiency (spiked chain), Weapon Finesse | Bloody Assault, Improved Trip | Bleeding Critical, Pin Down |
