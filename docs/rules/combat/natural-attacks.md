# Natural Attacks

!!! warning "House rule"
    Player characters have a cap on natural attacks based on character level, and a single natural attack can be upgraded to iterative attacks with a feat.

## Attack limit

The same limit that applies to summoner eidolons applies to player characters: the number of natural attacks you can make is capped according to your character level.

## Single natural attack

A creature or form that has only **one** natural attack for good (for example, an animal form) can spend **one feat** to gain iterative attacks with it, like the shifter class feature. These are extra attacks at the usual iterative progression, each at the usual –5 penalty step.
