# Shooting into Melee

!!! warning "House rule"
    The penalty for shooting into melee is **–2** instead of –4.

If you make a ranged attack against a target that is engaged in melee with one of your allies, you take a **–2 penalty** on the attack roll (instead of the normal –4). All other rules for shooting into melee work as normal.
