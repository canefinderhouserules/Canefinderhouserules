# Planar Exploration

Traits and exploration rules for the Transitive and Inner Planes. See [Planar Traits](planar-traits.md) for the trait definitions and [Planar Infusions](../../rules/campaign/planar-infusions.md) for the character options gained by spending time on a plane.

## Ethereal Plane

**Category**: Inner Plane (Transitive). **Traits**: no gravity; normal time; immeasurable; lasting structure (little to alter); mixed essence; mildly neutral-aligned; normal magic. **Qualities**: natives are immune to death effects, disease, and poison.

The Ethereal Plane is a ghostly place a hair's breadth from material existence, a realm of displaced dreams, potent emotion, and wandering souls. Ethereal creatures can perceive objects, terrain, and creatures on the Material Plane as wispy, ghostlike images, but are themselves invisible to all on the Material Plane save for the supernaturally gifted. While traveling on the Ethereal Plane, a creature is insubstantial, invisible, and utterly silent to someone on the Material Plane. Its threats are subtle: the difficulty Material Plane natives have moving through it can make combat challenging and escape nearly impossible, and since the geography is three-dimensional, threats can come from any direction. There are no celestial bodies and no analogue to north.

**Ectoplasm**: Where the mist interacts with a soul traveling from a death into the River of Souls, it can partially solidify into a semiliquescent ooze known as ectoplasm. Magic (particularly necromancy), powerful emotional upheavals, and certain rare undead can also manifest it. Free-floating ectoplasm forms tangled, pale-green, weblike sheets; it is odorless and tasteless, hinders movement as difficult terrain, and provides concealment as if it were dense fog.

**Vision**: The ever-present mist gives the realm an eerie gray-green glow, so true darkness is rare, but it distorts and obscures line of sight beyond 300 feet. From 300 to 900 feet, creatures and objects are visible as blurry, indistinct shapes and gain concealment. Beyond 900 feet, vision is obscured completely. Abilities that allow sight through mist or fog grant the ability to see normally.

**Magic**: Spells function normally on the Ethereal Plane, though their effects do not cross onto the Material Plane. The reverse is also true, save for spells and spell-like abilities with the force descriptor and abjuration spells that affect ethereal beings; these can cross from the Material Plane to the Ethereal Plane. Spellcasters on the Material Plane must have some way to detect foes on the Ethereal Plane to target them with force-based spells. It is possible to hit ethereal enemies with a force spell cast on the Material Plane, but the reverse is not: no magical attacks cross from the Ethereal Plane to the Material Plane, including force attacks.

**Movement**: The lack of gravity and near-total absence of solid matter would normally make movement close to impossible; however, as a result of its psychic resonance, creatures can move through the plane via sustained force of will. This requires the same action as regular movement, but the creature moves at only half its normal base speed (one-quarter if it lacks an Intelligence score). A creature with a swim or burrow speed can use that speed to determine its halved movement rate instead. Creatures capable of flight, or whose movement is based on nonterrestrial means such as ectoplasmic gliding, move at their full speed. Movement in this fashion is a purely mental action, and a paralyzed creature can still move this way. Any effect that prevents thought components from functioning also prevents movement on the Ethereal Plane.

## Shadow Plane

**Category**: Inner Plane (Transitive). The Shadow Plane is a dim, monochrome echo of the Material Plane that is both coterminous to and coexistent with it, so a planar traveler can use it to cover great distances quickly.

**Magic**: Spells with the shadow descriptor are enhanced. *Shadow conjuration* and *shadow evocation* are 30% as powerful as the conjurations and evocations they mimic (instead of 20%); *greater shadow conjuration* and *greater shadow evocation* are 70% as powerful (instead of 60%); and *shades* conjures at 90% of the power of the original (instead of 80%). Spells that produce, use, or manipulate darkness are unaffected. Spells with the light descriptor or that use or generate light or fire are impeded; all light sources have their ranges halved.

**Shifting Shadows**: Parts of the plane continually flow into other planes, so creating a precise map is next to impossible. Spells such as *shadow conjuration* and *shadow evocation* modify the base material of the plane.

**Vision**: The ambient lighting is dim illumination regardless of whether one is outside or indoors; the source is never apparent and seems to shift. Light sources, mundane and magical alike, can brighten this illumination but their ranges are halved. Magical darkness effects reduce illumination levels normally.

**Voidboils (CR 2, 5, or 12)**: Due to the plane's proximity to the Negative Energy Plane, portions occasionally erupt with gouts of black lightning and fire that tear through the planar fabric. Sometimes a voidboil can be predicted by tumorlike growths that rapidly buckle the land and vent small sprays of negative energy (a DC 20 Knowledge [planes] check gives 1d4 rounds to prepare), but just as often they are sudden outbursts. A typical voidboil sprays negative energy in a 30-foot-radius burst (CR 5): a DC 16 Reflex save avoids it; those caught take 6d6 points of negative energy damage. Smaller voidboils (CR 2) deal 2d6 points of damage in a 20-foot-radius burst (Reflex DC 12 negates). Particularly large voidboils (CR 12) deal up to 10d6 points of damage in a 60-foot-radius burst (Reflex DC 22 half) and persist for 1d6 rounds.

## Negative Energy Plane

**Category**: Inner Plane. **Traits**: subjective directional gravity; normal time; unbounded; lasting structure; major negative-dominant (isolated islands have the minor trait and tend to be inhabited); mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced and impeded magic. Exploration beyond the fringes, where islands and precipices form somewhat stable and sheltered regions, should be attempted only by the most well-prepared groups. There are no celestial bodies and no analogue to north.

**Magic**: Spells and spell-like abilities that use negative energy are enhanced; class abilities that use negative energy, such as channel negative energy, gain a +4 bonus to the save DC to resist the ability. Spells and spell-like abilities that use positive energy (including *cure* spells) are impeded. Characters take a –10 penalty on saving throws made to remove negative levels imposed by an energy drain attack.

**Blight Quartz**: Deposits of solidified negative energy appear as black or smoky gray gemstones that periodically crackle with dark purple energy. The majority are no larger than a human fist. As long as a creature carries a pound or more of blight quartz or is within 5 feet of a deposit of at least 10 pounds, it incurs 1 negative level for the duration of the proximity. This negative level never results in actual level loss, but cannot be overcome in any way (including via *restoration*) while the proximity continues. This is a negative energy effect.

Blight quartz cannot be worked into armor, but can augment melee weapons and ammunition for an additional 200 gp per piece of ammunition or 2,500 gp for a weapon. A creature hit with a blight quartz weapon must succeed at a DC 15 Fortitude save or take 1 negative level, which lasts 1 minute and never results in permanent level loss. Negative levels from multiple hits reset the duration but do not otherwise stack, nor stack with other negative levels the weapon may impart or the creature may already have. Blight quartz has 10 hit points per inch of thickness and hardness 10, but decays rapidly when taken from the Negative Energy Plane, taking 2d6 points of damage that bypasses hardness each round until it crumbles into nothingness.

**Void-Ravaged Creatures**: A mortal creature who survives for months or years on the plane without dying, or is exposed to potent pulses of negative energy, can become a [void-ravaged creature](../../monsters/templates/void-ravaged.md).

## Positive Energy Plane

**Category**: Inner Plane. **Traits**: subjective directional gravity; normal time; unbounded; lasting structure; major positive-dominant (rare isolated pockets of minor positive-dominant); mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced and impeded magic. The greatest danger is the nature of the plane itself. There are no celestial bodies and no analogue to north.

**Magic**: Spells and spell-like abilities that use positive energy (including *cure* spells) are enhanced; class abilities that use positive energy, such as channel energy, gain a +4 bonus to the save DC to resist the ability. Spells and spell-like abilities that use negative energy are impeded.

**Pre-Incarnate Souls**: These souls have no sentience and are more akin to a form of energy than to distinct creatures. At most, they act like natural forces with a minor tendency to mirror the actions of living creatures. New ones constantly manifest, and they are almost impossible to destroy: powerful blasts of negative energy, *wish*, or *miracle* could wipe one out, but not effects that directly target and damage souls. A pre-incarnate soul is a quivering, floating mass of quintessence, spiritual potential, and positive energy. A character who comes into contact with one increases the effects of positive-dominant essence traits currently affecting the character by one step (or doubles the effects of major positive-dominant effects) for as long as the contact persists (minimum 1 round). In addition, each round of contact removes 1 negative level from a creature.

**Radiant Creatures**: Mortal creatures destroyed by the plane's energies are occasionally reincarnated as [radiant creatures](../../monsters/templates/radiant.md).

## Plane of Air

**Traits**: subjective directional gravity; normal time; immeasurable; lasting structure; air-dominant; mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced magic for the air descriptor and spells that use, manipulate, or create air (such as those of the Air domain and the elemental air bloodline), and impeded magic for earth. There is no day-night cycle; the skies are deep blue and strewn with clouds, and light comes from unknown sources. Travelers navigate by magic or visual landmarks; there is no analogue to north. Natives usually have innate means of reaching a destination; visitors need airships, flying mounts, or magic.

**Dead-Air Pockets**: Spaces where the air becomes inexplicably dense, heavy, and still; travelers cannot breathe it or navigate through it. They are invisible and nearly undetectable, and a sail-driven airship loses propulsion as soon as it enters one. Most are unstable and dissipate over the years; some have existed for millennia.

**Sky Streams**: Currents of air dense enough to carry ships, from a few feet to a dozen miles in width, invisible and ever-changing. Their routes usually connect most major locations. Unprepared travelers can easily become stranded or swept away.

**Fire Orbs**: Distant pinpoints of light, which are stationary spheres of intense heat and light. Most airships cannot get close for fear of igniting. The plane's subjective gravity turns the rising hot air into a chaotic rush of air currents, so navigation is almost impossible for hundreds of miles around them.

## Plane of Earth

**Traits**: normal gravity; normal time; immeasurable; lasting structure; earth-dominant; mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced magic for the earth descriptor and spells that use, manipulate, or create earth or stone, and impeded magic for air. Tunnels and caverns are both the primary means of travel and a great hazard: cave-ins are common, and victims lucky enough not to suffocate instantly can find themselves entombed alive. Caustic and poisonous gases are also a threat. There is no day-night cycle; travelers navigate by signs etched on tunnel walls or other landmarks. Strong magnetic fields allow traditional compasses to aid navigation, provided a traveler can distinguish one specific field from the others.

## Plane of Fire

**Traits**: normal gravity; normal time; immeasurable; lasting structure; fire-dominant; mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced magic for the fire descriptor and spells that use, manipulate, or create fire, and impeded magic for water. Outside the few settlements tailored for visitors, travel can quickly become deadly; even for those with immunity to fire, it is perilous. Flying is the most direct way to travel, though it is far from easy.

## Plane of Water

**Traits**: subjective directional gravity (sinking or rising is slower and less dangerous than on the Plane of Air); normal time; water-dominant; enhanced magic for the water descriptor and spells that use or create water, and impeded magic for fire. **Qualities**: natives have the aquatic subtype, immunity to water and cold effects, and a swim speed of 40 feet.

The simplest way to traverse is to swim, but this is often not viable for nonaquatic visitors and the vastness makes self-propelled locomotion nearly impossible. Longer journeys use the currents, which vary in intensity, temperature, and direction; some double back upon themselves, others are well-traveled routes. Ferries float through the sea, many with magically sustained air bubbles for nonaquatic passengers. There is no day-night cycle; some regions are in continual darkness, others lit by sunken stars, luminescent fish, or diffuse light. There is no analogue to north.

## Astral Plane

**Category**: Outer Plane (Transitive). **Traits**: subjective directional gravity; timeless; mildly neutral-aligned; enhanced magic. **Qualities**: natives have the incorporeal subtype and a fly speed of 20 feet (perfect).

The Astral Plane is a disorienting realm of silver mist that connects all realities and provides the souls of the dead a route to the afterlife. Each creature can "fall" at great speed in the direction of its choice. There is no day-night cycle. The flow of souls from life to death, or of recycled quintessence back to the Positive Energy Plane, can aid navigation if detected, but there is no workable analogue to north.

**Magic**: All spells and spell-like abilities used within the Astral Plane can be employed as if improved by the Quicken Spell or Quicken Spell-Like Ability feats, though this has no effect on spells and abilities that have already been quickened or on spells from magic items. Spells thus quickened are prepared and cast at their unmodified level. As with Quicken Spell, only one quickened spell or ability can be cast per round.

**Silver Squalls (CR 10)**: Weather is virtually nonexistent, but drifts of aggregating thoughts can turn violent when sparked by competing ideas, triggering chain reactions that erupt into clouds of obfuscating chatter punctuated by arcing bolts of psychic energy. A squall's effects target all creatures in the area and vary by the debate it represents, dealing 2d6 points of ability damage to Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma (depending on the debate), causing the confused condition for 1d10 rounds, or striking victims permanently blind, deaf, or mute. A squall generally fills a 300-foot-diameter sphere and lasts 2d6 rounds. Each round a creature is exposed, it must succeed at a DC 20 Will save to resist the effect; once a creature has been affected by a particular squall, it cannot be affected by it again. Sentient beings caught within a squall can calm it as a full-round action by discerning its constituent ideas (typically a DC 30 Knowledge [planes] check). Larger squalls can last for days. Silver squalls are mind-affecting effects.
