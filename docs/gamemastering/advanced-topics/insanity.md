# Insanity

Insanity is an affliction inflicted upon those who suffer from extraordinary physical, mental, or spiritual anguish and trials. It can also be caused by exposure to particularly potent sources of unhinging horror, madness, or alien natures. Insanity is a mind-affecting effect. This is a simple alternative to the madness rules in the [Horror section](../../rules/horror/madness.md).

## Going Insane

A person has a chance of going insane every time he suffers a tremendous shock to one of his mental ability scores (Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma). Every time a creature is reduced to a score of 0 in one of these scores, there is a chance he goes insane. (Wisdom damage is particularly likely to cause insanity, since a 0 Wisdom score imparts a –5 penalty on all Will saves.) Either roll on the table below or select an insanity appropriate to the cause. Make the victim's saving throw in secret: he should not know the result, nor the type of insanity that might afflict him. Some insanities (like phobias) take days or even months to trigger, while others (such as paranoia) are immediately obvious.

At your option, a creature can risk going insane under extremely unusual situations even when his mental ability scores are unharmed. A character suffering long imprisonment might have to save against developing agoraphobia or claustrophobia. Someone repeatedly betrayed by allies might have to save against paranoia. A mind possessed by a powerful demon might have to save upon being exorcized to keep from becoming psychotic.

Insanity can also be inflicted via magic. Consider allowing the spell *insanity* to inflict 1 randomly determined insanity per 5 caster levels rather than causing permanent confusion. *Bestow curse* can also inflict a single insanity on a foe, in which case the insanity is also a curse.

It is possible to suffer from multiple forms of insanity. If you become afflicted with a form of insanity you already have, the current DC of that insanity increases by 5.

## Curing Insanity

All insanities have a DC that represents the insanity's strength. It is the Will save needed to resist contracting the insanity when first exposed, and also the DC needed to recover. Recovering naturally is lengthy: once per week, make a Will save against the insanity's current DC. On a success, the insanity's DC is reduced by your Charisma bonus (minimum 1). You continue to suffer the full effects until its DC is reduced to 0, at which point you are cured.

*Lesser restoration* has no effect on insanity, but *restoration* reduces the current DC of one insanity affecting a target by an amount equal to the caster's level. *Greater restoration*, *heal*, *limited wish*, *miracle*, or *wish* immediately cures a target of all insanity.

## Types of Insanity

| d% | Insanity |
|---|---|
| 1–11 | Amnesia |
| 12–48 | Mania/Phobia |
| 49–68 | Multiple Personality Disorder |
| 69–78 | Paranoia |
| 79–84 | Psychosis |
| 85–100 | Schizophrenia |

### Amnesia

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 20; **Onset** immediate  
**Effect** –4 penalty on Will saving throws and all skill checks; loss of memory

A character with amnesia cannot remember his name, his skills, or his past. He can build new memories, but any memories that existed before are suppressed. The amnesiac loses all class abilities, feats, and skill ranks for as long as the amnesia lasts. He retains his base attack bonus, base saving throw bonuses, combat maneuver bonus, combat maneuver defense, total experience points, and Hit Dice (and hit points). If a character gains a class level while suffering from amnesia, he may use any abilities gained by that class level normally. If the class level was of a class he already possesses levels in, he gains the abilities of a 1st-level character of that class. If his amnesia is later cured, he regains the full abilities of this class, including those gained from any levels taken while amnesiac.

### Mania/Phobia

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 14; **Onset** 1 day  
**Effect** target is sickened (if manic) or shaken (if phobic) as long as the source of the mania or phobia is obvious; chance of becoming fascinated or frightened

A mania is an irrational obsession with a (usually inappropriate) particular object or situation, while a phobia is an irrational fear of a (usually commonplace) object or situation. If a manic or phobic character is directly confronted by his obsession (requiring a standard action), he must make a Will save against the insanity or become fascinated (if manic) or frightened (if phobic) by the object for 1d6 rounds.

### Multiple Personality Disorder

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 19; **Onset** 2d6 days  
**Effect** –6 penalty on Will saving throws and Wisdom-based skill checks; multiple personalities

This disorder manifests as one or more distinct personalities within the same body and mind. The number of additional personalities equals the DC of the insanity divided by 10 (round down, minimum 1). If the insanity worsens (such as by the save DC increasing), the number increases as well; it decreases as the sufferer recovers. The GM develops these additional personalities.

Every morning, and each time the afflicted character is rendered unconscious, he must make a Will save against his insanity's DC. Failure indicates that a different personality takes over. The character's memories and skills remain unchanged, but the various personalities have no knowledge of each other and will deny, often violently, that the others exist.

### Paranoia

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 17; **Onset** 2d6 days  
**Effect** –4 penalty on Will saves and Charisma-based skill checks; cannot receive benefit from or attempt the aid another action; cannot willingly accept aid (including healing) from another creature unless he makes a Will save against his insanity's DC

The paranoid character is convinced that the world and all that dwell within it are out to get him. Paranoid characters are typically argumentative or introverted.

### Psychosis

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 20; **Onset** 3d6 days  
**Effect** character becomes chaotic evil; gains a +10 competence bonus on Bluff checks to hide the insanity

This complex insanity fills the victim with hate for the world. He may suppress his psychosis for 1 day by making a Will save against the DC of his insanity; otherwise he cannot help but plot and plan the death and destruction of his friends and enemies alike. The impact of psychosis must mostly be roleplayed, although not all players enjoy playing a lunatic trying to do in his friends. In such cases, the GM should assume control of the character whenever his psychosis is in control.

### Schizophrenia

**Type** insanity; **Save** Will DC 16; **Onset** 1d6 days  
**Effect** –4 penalty on all Wisdom- and Charisma-based skill checks; cannot take 10 or take 20; chance of becoming confused

A schizophrenic character has lost his grip on reality and can no longer tell the difference between what is real and what is not. These constant hallucinations make him appear erratic, chaotic, and unpredictable. Each time he finds himself in a stressful situation (such as combat), he must make a Will save against his insanity's DC. Failure indicates that the character becomes confused for 1d6 rounds.
