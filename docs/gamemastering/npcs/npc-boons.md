# NPC Boons

A boon is a quantifiable, non-monetary way an NPC might help the PCs: a discount on goods or services, a one-time bonus on a specific skill check, or a simple benefit that only that character can provide. Boons are intended to be a simple way for GMs to give PCs a minor rules benefit in reward for developing bonds with NPCs. They are never monetary, though they often have a monetary value, and should feel like favors between friends, not something that would change the life of either the characters or the NPC.

The nature of a boon depends more on an NPC's role in the world than on any statistic. A young prince who is merely a 1st-level aristocrat might grant a far more favorable boon (granting a pardon, financing a voyage, decreeing a law) than a baker statted as an 11th-level commoner.

Boons are not wantonly granted. Only NPCs with an attitude of helpful grant such benefits, and usually only to PCs they have come to trust over a significant period of time or who have done them meaningful personal services. NPCs are more likely to favor an individual than an entire party. Regardless of the effect, PCs should never have direct control over the granting of boons: they cannot force even their closest allies to grant benefits against their will.

## Kinds of Boons

**Favor**: Any character might aid friends with favors: a shopkeeper granting a 10% discount, a nobleman setting up a meeting with a local lord, or a retired adventurer loaning a masterwork longbow.

**Skill**: Minor bonuses on skill checks that an NPC passes on to a favored PC. As a guideline, skill boons grant either a +2 bonus on a skill in a very specific situation (never on all uses of a skill) or a one-time +4 bonus on a specific skill check. For example, a famous merchant might give a character his signet ring, providing a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks made with other merchants in his home city's marketplace.

**Unique**: The rarest boons, special powers an NPC might grant that are exclusive to that character and fall outside the purview of his class's typical abilities: a blessing that causes an ally's weapons to deal an additional +1 point of damage against a particular local menace, or an alchemist who can concoct a potion making the drinker immune to brown mold for 24 hours. Because such boons have the most flexibility and potential for exploitation, limit them to being useful only once or relevant for a single adventure.

## Sample Boons by NPC Class

Any NPC of any class can grant a boon; these lists are a guide for creating your own.

### Adept

- **Favor**: Free healing on a single occasion.
- **Favor**: Letter of recommendation to lower-ranking priests, ordering them to help the PCs as required (granting the aid of a 1st-level adept hireling for 3 days).
- **Skill**: Favorable introductions to contacts in a local church, providing a PC a +2 bonus on Diplomacy checks made to influence members of that specific church.
- **Skill**: Proves especially knowledgeable in mysterious alchemical techniques, granting a PC a +4 bonus on one Craft (alchemy) check made to create an alchemical item.
- **Unique**: Can brew four unique potions that instantly heal the disease filth fever.
- **Unique**: Allows the PC to commune with the spirit animal of his tribe, granting the ability to *speak with animals* of a specific regional species once per day.

### Aristocrat

- **Favor**: Provides an invitation to an aristocratic event, such as an estate party, royal gala, or public celebration.
- **Favor**: Uses influence to save the PCs from prosecution for a crime.
- **Skill**: Offers a day-long primer on local courtesy, granting the PC a +2 bonus on a Knowledge (nobility) check for the city or region.
- **Skill**: Attends a character on his visit to the royal court, granting the PC a +4 bonus on Sense Motive checks during interactions with the court's members during that outing.
- **Unique**: Loans a ship and provides a crew for a voyage to a distant land.
- **Unique**: Grants a PC a minor, landless title that affords him access to certain local rights.

### Commoner

- **Favor**: Provides room and prepares an elaborate feast in a PC's honor.
- **Favor**: Provides a 50% discount on a high-quality, nonmagical item made using one of his Craft skills.
- **Skill**: Freely uses his highest Craft or Profession skill for the PC for a month of service (perhaps crewing a vehicle or ship, tending a rare plant, or training an animal).
- **Skill**: Shares rural remedies, granting the PC a +2 bonus when using Heal to treat diseases.
- **Unique**: Creates a map or leads a PC through the local wilderness to a secret location only he knows about.
- **Unique**: Competently manages a home or business for an absentee PC.

### Expert

- **Favor**: Provides material for a PC, cutting the price to create a nonmagical item in half.
- **Favor**: Can find a seller to buy any nonmagic item or a buyer for any magic item.
- **Skill**: Grants access to an exceptionally well-outfitted workspace, granting a PC a +4 bonus on a specific Craft or Profession check.
- **Skill**: Teaches a PC a trick of the trade, granting a perpetual +1 bonus on one Craft or Profession check that the PC and expert share.
- **Unique**: Obtains membership in a regional guild, providing a PC with a 10% discount on a certain kind of goods in a wide region.
- **Unique**: Can create a special tool that opens an ancient lock, circumvents an impassable trap, or replaces a part of a fabulous broken mechanism.

### Warrior

- **Favor**: Gifts a PC one nonmagical weapon, piece of armor, or adventuring gear.
- **Favor**: Can guard a precious object or hide it where none will find it.
- **Skill**: Relates his experience patrolling the local sewers, granting a PC a +2 bonus on Knowledge (dungeoneering) checks in the city sewers.
- **Skill**: Provides information with which to blackmail a local criminal, granting a PC a +4 bonus on Intimidate checks against local street thugs.
- **Unique**: Can form a posse, bringing together 2d4 low-level warriors to aid in one specific plan.
- **Unique**: Grants the secret of a specialized fighting style, providing a PC with a +1 bonus on initiative.
