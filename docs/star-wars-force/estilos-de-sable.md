you learn to cast force powers and wield your lightsaber at the same time. This functions much like two-weapon fighting, but the off-hand weapon is a force power that is being cast. To use this ability, the character must have one hand free (even if the spell being cast does not have somatic components), while wielding a light or one-handed lightsaber in the other hand. If he casts this spell defensively, he can decide to take an additional penalty on his attack rolls, up to his Intelligence bonus, and add the same amount as a circumstance bonus on his concentration check. A magus can choose to cast the spell first or make the weapon attacks first, but if he has more than one attack, he cannot cast the spell between weapon attacks.

**Vapaad Maestro:**

Benefit: While using this Style, increase your Strength bonus on lightsaber strike damage rolls by an additional one-half your Strength bonus, to a total of double your Strength bonus on the first attack and 1-1/2 times your Strength bonus on the other attacks.
